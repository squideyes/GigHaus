FAQs 
 Are you dog friendly?
Yes, we love dogs. We do require any dogs that are visiting to be leashed and controlled by their owners at all times. Please not we have a house dog that wanders about but minds her business
 Are you 21+?
Most shows are 21+ unless you are accompanied by a parent. Please see individual show descriptions for specifics. During the day we are 100% family friendly but require children to be watched at all times. 
 Are kids free? 
Kids 12 and under do not need a ticket for music. 
 Do you offer to-go beer?
We will happily pour you a growler but we do not package at this time (nor do we plan to). 
 Where else can I find your beer?
We distribute locally. You can find our beer at Morning Social, The Hub, Cedar Mountain Canteen, Cedar Mountain Outpost, Headwater’s Outfitters
 Where can we park?
We unfortunately do not have a private parking lot for our business. There is street parking available and the city-owned lot at the Railroad Depot just a short walk down the road at: 390 Railroad Ave, Brevard, NC 28712
 When are doors/when does ticketing begin
On the day of a show we are generally open before the show starts. If you are coming to hang out but do not plan to stay for the show we do last call about a ½ hour before the show starts. We check for hand stamps at the beginning of the show and ask that you decide if you are going to stay or not at that point in time. We do have a small seating area in the front of our business where you can grab a beer and hang out if you are not wanting to pay for live music.

185 King Street 
185 King Street opened their doors in 2011 with the mission of providing a space to support local musicians. At the time all the surrounding commercial real estate was empty, a skeleton left from the closure of Brevard Lumber in 2008. The concept of opening a music venue on King Street was met with a decent amount of skepticism and opposition, but founder Jackson Wine and supporters from the community worked tirelessly to figure out a way to make it work. Over a decade later, 185 is thriving and serves as a pillar in the regional music scene.

Bringing The Art of Music back to SETX 
If you’re looking for a unique concert experience, look no further than 7 Oaks Event Garden & Listening Room in Southeast Texas. This hidden gem offers intimate dinner shows for 100 listeners on a beautifully curated 90-year old homestead in rural LaBelle, Tx., with the added bonus of being able to bring your own food on their Sunday and Tuesday shows. Whether you’re a music lover or just looking for an escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life, 7 Oaks is the perfect venue for you. Come enjoy amazing live music in a truly one-of-a-kind setting!

We Would Love To Have You Visit Soon! 
Whether you are getting tickets for an upcoming concert, or booking 7 Oaks for your next private event, we know you’ll love our venue! This is a little slice of Heaven in the middle of beautiful Southeast Texas. Check out the venue and see what makes us different. 
 About 7 Oaks
 How do I buy tickets?
Click this link to the event you want to attend and check out online. You can also call me if you are having any issues with online checkout!
 Do I need to print tickets to get in? 
No! We have you on our list and the link via your phone will suffice when needed(larger events).
 Do y’all have assigned seats?
Yes! Early ticket purchases are placed closer to stage and so forth! There isn’t a bad seat in the house . Email me for seating notes or accommodations.
 Do y’all have ADA access?
Yes we do! 
Wave your blue pass to the parking attendant and he will park you upfront! Working on a poured slabbed sidewalk from parking to venue this winter! 
Bathrooms are ADA accessible. 
 Can I pay at the door?
Most events do not allow this! As we need to prepare the room for our guest. Call or text me to see if a pay at the door option is possible for the event in reference.

Watching a live performance at Alvas Showroom is always a unique experience due to its great atmosphere and intimate staging. Set up like a small amphitheater, the Showroom only seats about 99 people. Being so close to the artists, the viewer literally feels like a participant in the live performance.
At Alvas Showroom guests instantly feel as if they’re partying with friends and are usually joined by the performers as well. Bring your own food and drink, no alcohol! Coffee, tea, and purified water is provided. There is also plenty of free parking!
Alvas Showroom has an excellent sound system and great lighting, contributing to a memorable live performing arts experience.
Seeing the artists so up close and in such a relaxed and intimate venue gives guests the opportunity not only to hear and see the performers but also to get to know them.
Many artists have said that Alvas Showroom is their favorite place to perform in the LA area.

At Alvas Showroom, we give a F.A.Q. about your questions.
Here are the most common ones:
Q: Is there assigned seating?
A: Most seats are General Admission and are first come, first serve. We do have table seating that can be reserved (only after purchasing your admission) by submitting a reserved seating request. Check it out!
Q: Is there wheelchair access?
A: We have 2 wheelchair accessible spaces as well as wheelchair accessible bathrooms. Wheelchair accessible spaces are also first come, first served, so please send an email to booking@alvas.com with your request to confirm availability for special wheelchair spaces.
Q: Do you need to provide a printed email confirmation for entry?
A: No physical printout necessary. Purchaser’s name will be on a list at the door. Simply provide the purchaser’s name at the door for entry.
Q: Can I buy a admission for other people?
A: Yes. The other people will simply provide the purchaser’s name at the door for entry.
Q: Can admission be purchased at the venue and is there a service fee?
A: Advanced admission purchases can only be done on our website. In-person admission purchases can only be done at the front desk on the night of the show IF seats are still available. There is no service fee for in-person admission purchases. We always recommend purchasing admission in advance on our website to guarantee admission. No refunds or exchanges.
Q: When do doors open and shows start/stop?
A: Because we allow the artist to decide that, we cannot give a definite answer. Generally, doors open approximately 30 minutes before showtime, and shows are approximately 1 to 2 hours long.
Q: Is there alcohol?
A: NO ALCOHOL is allowed or sold at our venue.

Avondale Brewing Co. is an outdoor venue. All Avondale Brewing Co. concerts are rain or shine. We do not allow umbrellas.
SEVERE WEATHER POLICY
In the event of inclement weather and/or lightning within an 8-mile radius, we will evacuate the venue. Security will guide guests out of the venue to find shelter. Please seek shelter in one of the neighboring businesses and stay tuned to social media for updates every 30 minutes. We will be in constant contact with the National Weather Service. When we get the all-clear, we will announce this on Avondale Brewing Co.'s social media. @AVONDALEBREWING on FB & IG 
Age restrictions
ABCo. is all ages unless otherwise specified. If an event has an age restriction, it will be listed on Ticketmaster. Any person over the age of 2 requires a ticket to enter. Anyone over 18 must present a valid ID. 
Clear Bag Policy
To reduce staff contact with guest belongings, we have implemented the following bag policy:
We will allow clear plastic, vinyl or PVC tote bags no larger than 12” x 6” x 12” and/or small clutch bags (4.5”x 6.5”). Small clutch bags do NOT have to be clear.

The Story Behind The Back Room
Laurie Lewis & Friends on Opening Night, April 16, 2016.
Contact Sam Rudin about bookings or other inquiries:
sam@backroommusic.com
(510) 381-1997
samrudin.com
On April 16, 2016, local musician Sam Rudin opened a new music venue in downtown Berkeley called The Back Room, which honored his vision to recreate the cozy ambience of the original Freight & Salvage, a beloved Berkeley music venue dedicated to acoustic music.
Every East Bay music lover of a certain age knows that the beloved 420 seat concert hall started out as a tiny 87 seat room, stuffed with thrift-store couches and great music. The Freight’s overwhelming success and resulting expansion have been celebrated by all, but there are some who miss the living room intimacy that allowed lesser-known musicians to make their mark, and better-known players to get up close and personal with their fans.
Rudin, known for his rip-roaring solo piano wizardry as well as his band of jazz and blues veterans (Hurricane Sam & the Hotshots), describes his venture as a small space (100 people max), with great acoustics and comfortable seating, that showcases the musicians with respect and appreciation.
The Back Room welcomes all acoustically-based genres, including jazz, blues, folk, bluegrass, Americana, and more, and is an all-ages venue. Children under 12 are free. The space is wheelchair-friendly, with a lift just inside the front door.

A Live Music Venue Should Make You Want To Listen
There are live music venues, and then there are places where you actually feel like you're in the music.
The difference isn't always the size of the stage or the number of lights hanging from the ceiling. Sometimes it's a musician being close enough that you can see their hands. A singer taking a breath before the next line. A drummer leaning into a groove. The little things you miss when the room is mostly interested in spectacle.
The Back Room is built around those little things.
Located in downtown Berkeley, The Back Room is an intimate music venue with room for about 100 people, comfortable seating, great acoustics, brick walls, a vaulted wooden ceiling, and a Steinway grand piano on stage. It's a room designed to put the musicians where they belong: at the center of the experience.
A Berkeley Music Venue With A Living Room Feel
The Back Room was opened in 2016 by local musician Sam Rudin, with the idea of bringing back some of the cozy, living-room intimacy of Berkeley's original Freight & Salvage. That history matters because it explains why the room feels different from a conventional concert hall.
The seating is comfortable. The room is small. The acoustics do the heavy lifting.
You can settle into a chair, get yourself situated, and forget about everything else for a couple of hours.
That's a pretty good way to spend an evening.

History
The beautiful stained-glass windows and charming little bell tower of The Befry, a 100+ year old church on Main Street in Sisters, are hard to miss. Built in 1914, during the early days of the small mountain town, the Belfry served as the gathering place for the Sisters Christian Church. Like many old buildings, it was sadly forgotten for a time. Over the past decades, the doors were rarely opened, and the community moved on to other pursuits. That is, until the summer of 2012 when Angeline Rhett took a look inside.
Through community action and Rhett’s creative thinking, this little old abandoned church was transformed into a retro concert venue. With the help of many through the Kickstarter campaign, Rhett transformed this gem on the corner of Main & Spruce into a diverse entertainment center and community event space.

About Us
Nestled along the banks of the majestic Mississippi River, Big River Theatre is an intimate arts venue that fosters a culture of exceptional music, film and creative community. The venue showcases a diverse lineup of talented artists, both local and nationally touring musicians and film makers. Big River Theatre is dedicated to promoting and preserving independent music and film, and makes big efforts to platform highly skilled artists and create a comfortable and high quality listening environment. 
​
Big River Theatre, (originally called "The Tscharner Building" ) built in 1895, has gone through a number of identities, but has been a live music and arts venue since 2002. Notably, it was the anchor point for the Big River Radio Wave, which aired on Wisconsin Public Radio from 2010-2019, hosted by Mac Cherry & Al Ross.

 Bird's Nest Listening Room of NC
Welcome to The Bird's Nest, Dunn
Dunn, NC's premier listening room, where the power of music comes alive in an intimate and enchanting setting. Nestled in the heart of the city and expertly managed by Sean and Carla, The Bird's Nest is a haven for music enthusiasts seeking a one-of-a-kind auditory experience. With a passion for curating unforgettable performances and a commitment to creating an inviting atmosphere, Sean and Carla have crafted a space where artists and audiences connect on a deeply personal level. Sit back, relax, and indulge in the captivating sounds while enjoying a selection of carefully curated beer, wine, and bourbon available on-site. The combination of exceptional talent and a well-stocked bar ensures that your evening at The Bird's Nest will be a harmonious symphony for the senses.
We often are asked where the idea for opening a listening room came from. The idea was born in March 2020, when music performed in front of live audiences could not happen. It was at that time that we decided to host a house show on our screened in back porch, out in the country in Sampson County. We invited a close circle of friends, and got a talented friend of ours to play for us. During that time, we were expecting to only need to host a few shows to support some local artists. But then a couple of months became a couple of years of hosting house shows. With a desire to keep the music and the relationships that were cultivated going, a listening room seemed the logical next step. After much prayer, planning, & hard work, we are excited to bring our back porch experience to the Bird’s Nest Listening Room.

Let’s talk about what our Listening Room is….
Our Listening Room is a ticketed live music venue where the patrons are respectful of the artists and fellow attendees by refraining from loud irrelevant conversations while artists are performing. Does not matter what style of music is being performed-singer/songwriters or rock bands.
We have several genres of music represented by the artists we are booking….so every show won’t be seated. We will be flexible and take into account our ticket holders when we set up the space. There will always be some chairs for folks who don’t want to (or can’t) stand the entire time, with the floor in front of the stage open.…for rock bands, for example. We want folks to be comfortable and enjoy the show!
There is no smoking or vaping inside our room. There is a designated area out back for smoking.
No outside food or beverages are allowed.
There is no re-entry. Once you are checked in, you cannot leave (except to smoke) and expect to re-enter the room.
Our shows are all-ages. We simply ask you to use discretion when bringing your kids.
We are NOT a bar with background music.
We ARE a live music venue that has a bar.
The music is the focus.

Welcome
Welcome to the historical Blue Door — The Best Listening Room in Oklahoma. 
Housed in a space steeped in local music history, The Blue Door has built its reputation on one simple idea: give great songwriters a room where the audience actually listens. Every week, we bring you live performances from the best local, regional, and national singer-songwriters and musicians, in a setting built for the music, not distractions.
​
Come pull up a chair and settle in for a night that feels different from your typical concert venue — intimate, unplugged in spirit, and centered entirely on the songs. Whether you're a longtime regular or walking through our doors for the first time, you'll find a listening room experience that's become a beloved part of Oklahoma's music scene.

Where can I purchase tickets?
Tickets can be purchased through TicketStorm prior to the show. This is the only authorized ticket seller for The Blue Door — please avoid third-party sites or resellers, as there are many ticketing scams and we are unable to honor tickets purchased elsewhere.
​
Tickets are handled electronically; you will not receive a paper ticket. Instead, your name will be added to our Will Call list at the door.
​
After purchasing: Check your email (including spam/junk folders) for a confirmation of your order. If you don't receive a confirmation email, check your bank or card statement to verify the purchase went through — if it did, your name will still be on the list at the door.
Can I buy tickets at the door?
Yes, tickets are available at the door — CASH ONLY. We do not accept credit or debit cards for door sales, so please come prepared with cash.
What time do the doors open?
The doors open 30 minutes before showtime. Most shows start at 8:00 pm, so doors generally open at 7:30 pm.
Is there a student discount?
In an effort to encourage young musicians and songwriters to visit The Blue Door and learn from master players and writers, we offer a discount for high school students with a valid student ID, for tickets purchased at the door.
Is there an age limit to attend The Blue Door?
There is no age limit to attend Blue Door shows, and we occasionally host shows specifically for children. Minors must be accompanied by an adult.
Is seating reserved or general admission?
Seating is general admission and available on a first-come, first-served basis. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime, so arriving early is recommended for the best seats.

Q: What is a listening room?
A: A listening room is a space to come, silence your cell phone and enjoy an intimate live show of storytelling and music. It's a place to listen and connect with the band or artist in a unique way, unlike your usual concert, bar or restaurant gig. 
Q What is the "PBQ Policy"?
A: P-PLEASE, B-BE, Q-QUIET. 
Blue Jay is a listening room and while the artists love the audience to applaud, laugh and even sing along, too much conversation during the show is a distraction to other listeners and can be disrespectful to the band or artist who is there to share their stories, inspirations and music with us. 
We encourage talking before the show, during set break and after the show, but strongly encourage audience members NOT TO TALK during the show. 
***If you are warned multiple times, you will politely be asked to leave. 
Q: Where is Blue Jay located?
A: We are located in the Costa Verde Plaza, on the corner of 25th Ave South and 3rd Street in South Jax Beach, UPSTAIRS from Casa Maria Mexican Restaurant, across the street from Bold Bean Coffee and Roy's. 
***There are small, blue arrow signs within the plaza to lead the way!
Q: What are your hours?
A: Our schedule is a little willy nilly as we are only open during shows. We host an every Tuesday jazz jam and the rest of the days can be found on our schedule page in the main menu. Doors open at 7pm and shows begin at 8pm unless otherwise noted. 
Q: Do you serve alcohol?
A: Yes. BEER & WINE ONLY. We have a lovely selection of wines as well as a wonderful craft beer list. We also have domestic beers, local beers on tap and plenty of non-alcoholic choices. 
Q: Do you serve food?
A: No. We do not serve food however we highly recommend grabbing a pre-show dinner across the street at SoNapa Grille!
Q: May I bring outside food or drink in?
A: No. 
Q: Do you allow smoking/vaping inside?
A: No.

About
Music is a deep passion of mine. It runs through my veins and I'm not sure if there's anything that quite fulfills me more. It has gotten me through the highest of highs and the lowest of lows. Writing, playing and listening to music has been the one constant in my life and since I first picked up a guitar at age 13, it’s been the only outlet I could truly lean on no matter the time, place or instance. When you can’t find a friend, you’ve still got the radio, ya know?
For years, I've wanted a place where I could go and listen to live music. Not just any place. An artist centric place intended solely for listening. I wanted to sit on a comfy couch and without distraction, listen to artists share their stories and inspirations. I wanted to be able to clearly hear their lyrics and see the expressions on their faces. I wanted to meet them and have the opportunity to thank them. I grew tired of going to see my favorite local artists at bars and restaurants where they’re shoved in a corner, forced to sing covers and sneak in only an original or two. I wanted more than being one of thousands of people in an arena or theater, where the band is only able to really be seen on the screen behind them. I wanted to be up close and personal. I wanted interaction. I wanted connection. I wanted to create the ideal listening environment for show-goers and the ideal storytelling environment for artists. And thus, the dream of Blue Jay Listening Room became a reality.

I know what a special thing it is to be able to bare your heart and soul and share those stories with people who actually want to hear them. About
Who We Are...
Driven By Our Love of 
Craft Beer, Culinary Arts, &
Community Growth...
 Brues Alehouse Brewing Company was founded in 2015, with a powerful common goal: Create a brewpub unlike any other. Located on the Historic Arkansas Riverwalk in Pueblo, Colorado, Brues brings forth fresh craft beer, from-scratch food, and live music from around the world.
Our company is comprised of creative and driven people who continually work to foster an exceptional experience. From classic European beer styles, to locally sourced dishes, to delicate desserts and espresso, to packed-house concerts, we have you covered for an amazing experience. See you here. 
News
The Brewery
Our Love of Beer
Our brewing philosophy is decidedly German. We obsess over process details with a commitment to both science and art. We strive to brew elegant European and Continental Lagers, fresh IPAs, and complex Belgian Ales. Our ingredients are always top quality: Malts from Bamberg, Germany. Hops from across the globe. Reverse-Osmosis Water. Yeast sourced from world-class breweries. Fresh fruits and whole spices.

A lot of brewpubs are just that. Not us. Our knowledgeable bar staff works together to come up with an array of cocktails, blending old world classics with new-age twists. If you're not a beer drinker, we still have a whole bar devoted to serving you.

The Kitchen
Culinary Experts Working as one...
Pueblo, Colorado is known for its unique cultural cuisine: a blend Mexican, Italian, German, Native American, and American styles. Here at Brues Alehouse, not only do we understand the local palate for rich, flavorful dishes, but we also have a fierce creative drive to push the boundaries with modern recipes and culinary techniques. The result? A menu beaming with possibilities. Want one of the best burgers you've ever had? We've got you. You're Vegan or Gluten-Free? We have multiple Vegan and Gluten-Free options. Brunch? Every Sunday from 10 to 2. Fresh vegetable dishes? We source our produce from the source (Musso Farms) when in season. Check out the menu and see what gets you excited!
The Bakery
Excitingly Fresh...
Our bakery is both elegance and technique. From traditional Macarons to delicate tarts, our bakery evolves with the seasons to provide our guests with some of the finest specialty desserts in southern Colorado. With a constant rotation of baked goods, you should plan to arrive early in the day before the case sells out. We can also do custom orders for cakes and specialty items for any event or gathering you may have!

This incredible museum brings the genius, creativity, and curiosity of Leonardo da Vinci to life through hands-on exhibits, art, science, and innovation. Whether you're visiting for the first time or bringing the family back for another adventure, it's a great way to spend some time exploring Pueblo's Riverwalk.
