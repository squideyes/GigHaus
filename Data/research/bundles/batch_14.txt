Tastes, Tonics, & Troubadours
"A Little Off the Rails" in Downtown Edmond
If these walls could talk, they’d tell you about clinking bottles, butter churns, and the steady rhythm of horse hooves headed out on ice delivery routes. Long before live music and cocktails filled the air, this corner of 2nd Street was home to the Edmond Ice Company, built in 1909.
Today, those same brick walls and sturdy foundations are alive again. The creamery, bottling works, and icehouse building now welcome guests for thoughtfully crafted food, cocktails, and live music, while The Stables serves a new purpose as a gathering space for celebrations and special events. Our kitchen is led by Executive Chef Zach Hutton, a James Beard Award finalist (Best Chef Southwest) and Food Network Chopped Champion, whose menus celebrate Oklahoma ingredients with creative, approachable dishes designed to be shared around the table.
This space has always been about bringing Edmond together, and we're honored to carry that tradition on ... one great meal, one great show, and one memorable evening at a time.

About The Old Icehouse
Rooted in History, Alive with Community
Our Mission
At The Old Icehouse, our mission is simple: to bring people together. We believe food tastes better with friends, music feels richer in community, and a historic space comes alive when it’s filled with laughter, stories, and song.
By blending Edmond’s past with a vibrant present, we strive to create a place where families feel welcome, artists are celebrated, and neighbors connect all over great meals, crafted drinks, and unforgettable curated experiences.
Keeping Edmond Cool Since 1909
The Story of The Edmond Ice Company
If these walls could talk, they’d tell you about clinking bottles, butter churns, and the steady rhythm of horse hooves headed out on ice delivery routes. Long before live music and cocktails filled the air, this corner of Second Street was home to the Edmond Ice Company, built in 1909.
Back then, ice wasn’t just a convenience, it was a lifeline. Families left cards in their windows to show how many pounds they needed, and wagons rolled out from the stables (now our very own The Stables Event Space) to keep Edmond cool. As the town grew, so did the business. The plant added a creamery and turned out thousands of pounds of Shelden’s “Golden Glow” butter each week. Soon after came ice cream at all fountain shoppes in town and even a soda bottling works, where grape, orange, and a rainbow of flavors fizzed to life right here on site.
For decades, the Edmond Ice Company was part of the daily rhythm of this community; until electric refrigeration and changing times brought its operations to a close in the 1940s. The buildings lingered, shifting from production to storage, and finally to a quiet pause.

Full-Service Private Events
The Old Icehouse is more than a restaurant and music venue - it’s also one of Edmond’s most unique event spaces. Housed in a revitalized piece of history, our 3,500 square feet of combined bar and event space offers flexible layouts for everything from intimate dinners to weddings and milestone celebrations. With the patio and lawn activated, we can comfortably host up to 300 guests.

Old Quarter Songwriter Festival
Get ready for three unforgettable days of storytelling, soul, and song! From November 12th to 14th, the heart of Downton Galveston will come alive with the voices of songwriters from near and far, sharing original music in its purest form.
The Old Quarter Songwriter Festival celebrates the craft of songwriting in an intimate listening-room setting where lyrics take center stage. Whether you're a longtime fan of the Texas folk tradition or discovering the magic of songwriter showcases for the first time, this weekend promises heartfelt performances, acoustic brilliance, and a gathering of kindred spirits.
Join us as we honor the voices behind the music — raw, real, and rooted in truth. This is more than just a music festival; it’s a tribute to the stories that connect us all.

Concerts, Music Lessons & Events in Ferndale, CA
Ferndale Music Company brings concerts and creative instruction to the Victorian Village of Ferndale, California from our location in a beautiful former church and Humboldt’s premier historic concert venue, The Old Steeple! We offer music lessons and other learning opportunities for all ages in a vibrant, supportive environment. Make sure to check out our calendar for upcoming shows and tune in to KEET-TV’s “Live from The Old Steeple” (available on PBS stations nationwide!) to watch live recordings of past shows. The Old Steeple is also available to rent for weddings and other private events. NEW: Book our Airbnb guest suite, The Green Room at The Old Steeple!

ABOUT US
Ferndale Music Company began in 2012 as a lesson studio and music store on Main Street in Ferndale, a small historic town near the California Redwoods. In 2015, we acquired and moved into Ferndale’s majestic 115+ year-old former Methodist Church, opening it up to the public as a concert venue, The Old Steeple. We’re thrilled to be able to share this one-of-a-kind building with the Humboldt County community through concerts, special events, and music lessons in guitar, bass, ukulele, piano, drums, fiddle, mandolin and more - and to share our unique venue with the nation via “Live from The Old Steeple”!
Located next to the town cemetery at 246 Berding Street, Ferndale, CA - The Old Steeple is a gorgeous example of turn-of-the-century Victorian architecture featuring intricate stained glass windows, ADA accessibility, 225 seats and professional sound. Contact Ferndale Music Company at (707) 786-7030 with rental inquiries. Thinking of getting married in the historic heart of the Redwoods? Find info on The Old Steeple and other Ferndale wedding venues here: Ferndale Wedding Information

The Old Steeple — Ferndale Music Company
Concerts & Events
Humboldt County's new old venue: intimate, cozy, beautiful.
Located in the Victorian Village of Ferndale, California, The Old Steeple resides in the 115+ year-old former Methodist church alongside Ferndale Music Company. This gorgeous example of turn-of-the-century Victorian architecture in the historic heart of the Redwoods sits next to the town cemetery at 246 Berding Street.
The facility features gorgeous stained glass windows, ADA accessibility, 225 seats and professional sound, creating an ideal setting for intimate concerts and beautiful weddings. Contact Ferndale Music Company at 707-786-7030 with rental inquiries.

Announcing Orange! A Coffee, Art, and Music experience! Tucked into the restored historic landmark Green Lantern Building in a bucolic village on the banks of the mighty Delaware River, on the Appalachian Trail, a new and fresh arts venue is born. Orange is the first e-conscious, ethical, and carbon neutral arts adventure in the region. Coffee is slow at Orange. Conversations are gentle; friendships are warm.
The Coffee: We proudly serve ethically-sourced, small farm / big impact roasts of Passenger Coffee. At Orange, every organic espresso is measured carefully, our specialty coffees are dripped one cup at a time, our signature sweets and savories are baked daily in small batches.
The Art: With fast paced art openings and rotating exhibits of New York, Philly and local artists, Orange is a gallery that promises engagement, enlightenment, and a celebration of diversity and inclusion, proud to shine a light in a gloomy world. 
The Music: Professional touring folk acts will take the stage at Orange: an intimate space with original tin ceiling, maple floors, clear sight-lines, pro sound and lighting. Following the traditions of Godfrey Daniels, Club Passim, Caffe Lena, and New York City’s famed Gerdes, Orange offers a first class listening and gathering room energy.

Our Story
Our love of craft beer started many years ago, but the story of Ore Dock Brewing Company started in the summer of 2009 during a 4,000 mile road trip across the western United States. Up to this point, whenever we found ourselves traveling to a new place, we had formed a habit of visiting the local brewery. Local breweries always seemed to exude native culture and never failed to provide an eclectic mix of friendly people, not to mention the fact that each region had its own unique beer styles and local favorites. We especially enjoyed those breweries that exhibited a clear link to their community, utilized locally produced ingredients and products, and fostered local art and music.
The trip out West was no exception; in fact we planned our route based on the breweries we wanted to try. Somewhere between the stunning western landscape and the amazing breweries that we visited, we were surprised to find ourselves homesick for the Upper Peninsula. It was then that we realized we wanted to take the atmosphere at these breweries and infuse it with all the things we love about Marquette, and the dream to start a microbrewery was born. We began working on a business plan and found that we were truly passionate about our idea; passionate enough to get through more than two years of working, planning, and saving. Ore Dock Brewing Company officially opened its doors and taps in May of 2012.
We are so very grateful to the many wonderful friends and family who have been supportive and helpful in so many ways along our journey. We strive to make Ore Dock Brewing Company a true reflection of all the things we love about Marquette and the Upper Peninsula. Cheers!

Our Story
 Oxbow is a small farmhouse brewery located in rural Newcastle, Maine. The brewery is nestled in an enchanted forest amongst rolling farmlands and winding tidal rivers, of which the ebb and flow provide a constant reminder that the coast is near. 
The name Oxbow is inspired by the bends in these rivers, the Dyer in particular, which features stunning horseshoe-shaped oxbows where the river covers tremendous ground to travel what could otherwise be a very short linear distance. We employ a similar approach when crafting our beers, taking months or years to create a beverage that is commonly produced in a matter of days.
Oxbow Brewing Co.
There is a small sign with the original Oxbow owl logo that marks the dirt (or more frequently, mud) driveway to 274 Jones Woods Road, which leads to the brewery and tasting room. The brewery is in a converted barn and has been dedicated to the pursuit of crafting unique farmhouse ales of distinctively European influence. All Oxbow beers are brewed here and utilize the soft spring water that is drawn from an artesian well that sits adjacent to the brewery. The tasting room next door has a rotating selection of the brewery’s finest offerings, both on tap and in bottle, as well as light snacks and merchandise. Customers are encouraged to relax inside amongst the barrels and the bar’s rough-cut pine interior, or outside at one of the many picnic tables. The pond behind the brewery and tasting room provides a lovely backdrop for a beer tasting session or a game of bag toss.
The Farmhouse at Oxbow Brewing Co.
Overlooking the brewery, tasting room, and pond sits the Farmhouse at Oxbow Brewing Co. It is a three-bedroom passive-solar house. It is great year-round and has easy access to fresh or saltwater swimming holes in the summer and plenty of leaf-peeping in the fall

In 2026, the hall entered a new chapter under the stewardship of Katie and Danny of Easy Wind Farm. Their approach to music has always centered around community: welcoming audiences, supporting artists, and creating the kind of atmosphere where strangers become regulars and regulars become friends. At Easy Wind Farm, the focus has never been spectacle — it’s connection, creativity, and memorable moments shared between musicians and listeners.
That same philosophy now guides Oyster Shoals.

Whether it’s a seated listening show, a high-energy Saturday night, or your first time discovering a new favorite band, Oyster Shoals Music Hall exists for one simple reason:
music sounds better when experienced together.
We’re proud to be a hometown venue, and even prouder to help keep live music thriving in the Northern Neck.

Booking
Thanks for your interest in playing our club! Before contacting us about booking your band, PLEASE read through this list of guidelines to determine if your act is a good fit for Petra’s. It will save us both time! Then email us at petrasbooking@gmail.com.
When do you usually host bands at Petra's?
We can host local and touring bands typically on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays, and occasionally Tuesdays.
Weekend shows start at 9, school night shows start at 8. Please include this info when you are promoting your show! Load-in for bands is 6-7.
Is my band appropriate for Petra's?
We book acts who play ORIGINAL material, with rare exceptions for tribute acts who stray from the typical cover band fodder (classic rock, top 40, etc.). If it’s got heart and you’re creating your art we’ll most likely book you. If you’ve got a good thing going at coffeeshops, bars, and breweries around town it might be best to stick with what’s paying your bills. We do not have a built in crowd so we depend on you to bring your fans, friends, and family to make it a good night for all involved.
Petra’s is a 21 and over club. Sorry, no exceptions.
If you are touring as a package (2+ bands who aren’t from Charlotte), we probably can’t help you with a show. Our ideal lineups will have 3 acts, at least 2 of which are local.

TICKETED EVENTS (Dinner + Show)
 Go to our Events Calendar, find your event, scroll to the end of the description, and click Get Tickets.
Your ticket includes entrance to the show and reserved seating.
If you are using the Resy App, make sure you click the Event Reservation - not the Dinner-Only button. You don’t need to make a separate dinner reservation if you’re attending an event!
Event Timing
Seating for events begins 1 hour before showtime so we can serve food and drinks at a comfortable pace. You may also order food and drinks during the show.
Where 
Events take place upstairs and downstairs — check the event details. Please note that we do not have handicapped access for upstairs. 
We (almost) always offer dining in the opposite room from the music, so feel free to book “Dinner Only” even on music nights.
📞 Call or 📧 email us anytime if you have questions.

About Poe Mill Music Studio 
At Poe Mill Music, we believe live events should feel electric—whether you’re here for a concert, a community showcase, or an unexpected pop-up set. Our space was built to bring people together around sound, expression, and the kind of nights you’ll be talking about the next day. 
From up-and-coming bands to creative collaborations, our calendar features a mix of performances and gatherings in an environment that’s as comfortable as it is acoustically dialed-in. The venue layout invites movement, connection, and spontaneity—with food and drink on hand and room to let the music take over. 
Whether you’re a regular or it’s your first time through the doors, every event is designed to feel welcoming, high-energy, and unmistakably local.

About us 
Welcome to poe mill music.
the place where we build community 
The Artist Comes First
At Poe Mill Music, we believe your music deserves to be the center of attention. Every song you write is a piece of your heart and soul, and our goal is to give you the space it deserves.
Spaces Built for Music
•The Listening Room: A room designed to be acoustically sound, ensuring your audience hears every note as intended.
•The Recording Studio: A professional environment to capture your work and make it a permanent part of your legacy. 
FOR MUSIC LOVERS
A Space for Original Music
Poe Mill Music was built for people who truly love music. We focus on original music by local, regional and touring artist. Our listening room is an intimate, professional setting for musicians to share their art with music fans.
Feel the Energy
There is a specific kind of magic in the connection between a musician and an audience. Whether you’re here to listen intently, dance, or cheer, you’re part of the experience. For us, music is medicine for the soul—come join the community. 
Founders of Poe mill music 
Brice Smith Co-founder of Poe Mill Music
Brice Smith, part-time drummer and the spirited co-founder of Poe Mill Music, seamlessly blends his executive acumen as the founder and CEO of InvestiNet for the past 13 years with his love for music. At the core of Poe Mill Music, Brice's passion for beats and melodies converges with his entrepreneurial spirit, creating a space where music goes beyond entertainment to become a force for community engagement and uplift.
This visionary's commitment extends beyond the boardroom into the very fabric of the community, embodying Poe Mill Music's ethos: a symphony of artistic passion and social responsibility. Brice's journey with InvestiNet underscores his skill in leadership and innovation, qualities he channels into making Poe Mill Music a beacon of artistic excellence and local impact. 
Jason Nodine Co-founder of Poe Mill Music

Rental Space
Over 4,500 square feet of space. Rich interior colors, soft lighting, 15′ ceilings and beautiful hardwood floors define the spacious concert hall to create a warm and charming environment. The entire space is equipped with central heating and air conditioning and is ADA accessible.
Your rental of Polaris Hall is for the full building and includes:
The Concert Hall, which includes the stage, full PA, screen and projector and theatrical lighting
The Main Hall Bar on the second floor
The Parlor Bar on the first floor
The Parlor lounge which includes a pool table, shuffleboard and cozy seating areas
A patio with 2 fire pits
Two dressing room areas
Access to in-house tables and chairs
A fully staffed Rock 'n Roll events team who will be on hand during your event.

History
This charming building, 'Star Ballroom,' was originally built in 1930 by a culturally diverse, working class fraternal organization in Portland. Men from “every rank and station” did work in the name of “friendship, love and truth” here, as did the great women who subsequently bought the building and operated it as the North Star Ballroom for several decades.

History 
The old DePollo store
It was June of 2001 when (former) partner Kate and I first peered into the vacant DePollo Store on Front Street in historic Thomas. We immediately had a common vision for the space— a varied and healthy deli menu, a community gathering space, and live Appalachian-based acoustic music. We also had our first spat in this instantly created business partnership.
“And it must have a good beer selection,” I said.
“I am NOT raising my kids in a bar!” Kate cried.
And so our first disagreement led to that Purple Fiddle attribute of which we are most proud – a smoke-free, mostly-acoustic music venue where families can also enjoy tasting their favorite crafted beverage in a responsible and non-threatening environment.
Another thing for which we strived, was for consistent high-quality professional entertainment every weekend, but we never anticipated the overwhelming success we have had. Since opening 13 years ago, we have presented a performance every Friday and Saturday night at 8:30 pm! Sure there has been some wicked weather up here at 3000 feet, resulting in some last-minute cancellations, but somehow we are blessed by amazing and responsive musical creatives who have always responded to our call at the eleventh hour.
The Avett Brothers’ last show at the Purple Fiddle!
These days we are thrilled to find that our music schedule practically fills itself six months in advance with nationally-touring professional acts eager to play our great-sounding, intimate space. For this we also give kudos to you, our customers, for always being attentive and respectful of the performances. Almost all of the bands mention this.

Live music venue
in the Quad Cities
The Raccoon Motel is a cozy, 200-cap independent music venue in downtown Davenport, Iowa. First opened in 2017, it has hosted thousands and thousands of artists, including Colter Wall, Tyler Childers, Sierra Ferrell, Orville Peck, Dawes, Black Flag and The Melvins. Walking in the front doors of the Motel, you're transported into the belly of a time capsule, in true dive bar style. There's a velvet painting of Willie Nelson, a chain-smoking fox, Farrah Fawcett poster and a pull-tab machine to greet you in the front bar, before you go through the green-shag-carpeted doors to enter the GA venue area. The full bar features all your domestic favorites, along with signature cocktails on draft. To make matters even better, you don't even have to steal yourself away after the show as the Motel has three rooms to rent above the club -- a spacious 3-bedroom and two single-bedroom units with full kitchens, baths and laundry services. Come stay and play at The Raccoon Motel.

 About Randy
Randy Now's Man Cave, a record store owned by Randy Ellis and his wife Mary in Hightstown, New Jersey, buys and sells a wide variety of merchandise, including records, cassettes, and DVDs. The store also carries T-shirts, snacks, and sodas and hosts in-store concerts.
Randy Ellis has been involved in the music scene for over 40 years. He began his career as a DJ at City Gardens in Trenton, New Jersey. He eventually became the promoter for the club, booking bands such as The Ramones, The Replacements, and Green Day. After City Gardens closed, Ellis continued to book bands at various clubs in the Trenton, NJ, area. Still working a day job, Randy delivered newspapers for over 14 years (365 days a year!).
Randy was offered the opportunity to book The New Hope Winery; basically setting them up with numerous music agencies, booking theartists there, and also getting their presence onto the web in the early days and helped set up email weekly mailings. Also arranged the room's seating for optimum sight lines and comfortable seating.
Randy opened his "Man Cave" store in Bordentown, NJ, in 2013 and spent 10 years in that location. The time was approaching for expansion in 2022. Luckily, we found an ideal new space in Hightstown, NJ, going from 500 square feet to our current 1750 sq. ft. location. The new Man Cave is butted up between The Hightstown Diner (94 years strong) and The Old Hights Brewpub (3 years old).
