WHAT IS THE REFINERY?
The Refinery is a mixed use development located in the "Neck" region of the Charleston Peninsula. It features over 40,000sf of office space, a 2,000 person capacity outdoor amphitheater, various private event spaces available for rent, and two independent food and beverage establishments: Cleats and The Whale. 
Built for a work and play lifestyle, The Refinery is where office hours turn into happy hours!
CONTACT INFORMATION
The Refinery is located at 1640 Meeting Street Rd. Charleston, SC 29405
If you need to contact The Refinery please do so via the email below and we'll be in touch as soon as possible: 
info@therefinerychs.com 
WHERE ARE THE CONCERTS HELD?
Concerts at The Refinery are held in the outdoor amphitheater on the east side of The Refinery building. This area is partially paved and there is also a lawn area to spread out on.
The entry and exit gate is located at the southeastern end of the property. Check out our venue map for more detail!
There are no dedicated seats in either General Admission or VIP. All seating options are available on a first-come, first-served basis. 
REFUNDS, CANCELLATIONS, + INCLEMENT WEATHER
All ticket sales are final and non-refundable.
The Refinery is an outdoor venue and shows will be held in all elements of Charleston weather. All events are rain or shine. 
Please be prepared for a wide range of weather conditions. This may include full exposure to the sun, heat, and rain. We recommend that patrons bring rain gear (no umbrellas), sunscreen, and bug spray. Event staff will monitor weather conditions and prioritize guest and staff safety. 
The Refinery reserves the right to cancel any event at any time for any reason. 
AMPHITHEATER HOURS
On event days, The Refinery amphitheater will be closed to the public for sound checks and show operations.

Located in the historic downtown Arts & Entertainment district, Live at Hub City Vinyl is a new and exciting hub for musical artists and art performers. Founded by husband and wife owners Lloyd and Sheree Thoburn, Live at Hub City Vinyl is one of the best venues when it comes to location, square footage, and state-of-the-art amenities in Hagerstown, Maryland.
Ready to Rock:
For full details on Artist Accommodations, Sound, and Lighting Equipment please review the main Venue Specs on this page. We are fully equipped with the ability to adapt for your performance needs. We want to showcase your talent and allow you to be the life of the party, so if you have a special stage need that is not listed, indicate on the form below the needs that you have and we’ll see what we can do. Ticket links are live through our website and ticket portal. Ticket sales are a shared responsibility of the venue and the artist(s). We want your show to be a HUGE success!
Capacity:
Live at Hub City Vinyl can host artists and guests in two performance spaces: The Sidebar can accommodate 150 seated and 220 standing cap; The Main Stage can accommodate 400 seated and 650 standing cap. Interested in booking a show at our venue? Fill out the form below.

About 
Live At Ted’s is an intimate, 62-seat listening room nestled beneath the Cape Fear Memorial Bridge in downtown Wilmington, North Carolina. Designed for true music lovers, our space invites you to fully experience live performance—where every note matters, every lyric resonates, and every song comes with a story.
Housed in a lovingly restored early-1900s bungalow, Ted’s blends historic charm with a warm, living-room feel—like discovering your next favorite artist right at home. To preserve this special atmosphere, we ask guests to keep conversation to a minimum during performances, ensuring an exceptional experience for both artists and audience.
If music moves you, you belong here. Welcome home to Ted’s! 
The Ted’s Experience
 Here are a few FAQ’s and additional info about our venue! 
 What is a listening room?
A listening room is an intimate space designed with one purpose in mind: to fully experience the music. Unlike a typical bar or brewery filled with background chatter, this setting invites you to slow down, be present, and truly listen. It’s a place where music lovers can connect more deeply—not only with the songs, but with the stories and artistry behind them.
To preserve this experience, we kindly ask that conversations be kept to a minimum during performances. This shared attentiveness creates something special—a rare kind of magic that happens when artists and audience are fully engaged with one another.
 Why is there a ticket charge?
We are a very small and intimate space and unlike a bar, our patrons are not there primarily to drink. We charge a ticket price so that we can afford to pay the musicians that play a fair fee. Also, we are truly hosting a performance at Ted’s. The musicians will tell stories behind their music and engage with the audience unlike most bar/brewery gigs.

 Ayreheart 
December 11, 2026
Lute-Driven Music for Modern Folk from Grammy-Nominated Lutenist & Composer, Ronn Mcfarlane. The members of Ayreheart wield some of the most ancient instruments used in modern music. The band centers around the versatile lute textures created by Ronn McFarlane. Ronn conceived and founded the group in the wake of his Grammy-nominated album, Indigo Road.
Renaissance meets traditional roots meets contemporary folk, this is lute-driven music for modern folk, in a unique blend of lute with vocals, bass, colascione (a kind of bass lute), violin, guitar and percussion.
The members of Ayreheart wield some of the most ancient instruments used in modern music. The band centers around the versatile lute textures created by Ronn McFarlane. Ronn conceived and founded the group in the wake of his Grammy nomination for the album Indigo Road, a compilation of his original works for lute. This was followed by the album One Morning, which formally introduced Ayreheart to the musical world.
Now filled out by bass and drums team, this staple rhythm section shows subtle surprises—fretless bass providing expressive foundation paired with percussion instruments from around the world. About Loft 129
Loft 129 is a non-profit corporation that operates Cumberland’s premier live entertainment and special events venue. The venue is located on the second floor of 129 Baltimore Street in the Historic City Center district in Downtown Cumberland, Maryland. 
Loft 129 focuses primarily on:
· Providing regular live musical entertainment in a variety of genres including rock, pop, blues, jazz, soul, bluegrass, folk, classical music and more.
· Providing a live performance venue in the arts and entertainment district.
· Providing a venue that can be rented for meetings, private social gatherings, lectures, and small conferences.

Main Street Crossing = Up-Close Concert Venue + Sunday Home for Multiple Small
Churches + Non Profit Organization + Funding Resource for Charities +
Davis Family Operated
MINISTRY AND CHARITY
Funded, built and founded in 2004 by the Davis Family of Tomball, the original idea was as follows: To co-locate and share this 150 seat live-music-concert venue with small church communities who may not be able to afford such a beautiful and functional venue for their Sunday church services. We have had 4 different churches gather at MSC through the years: Journey, Sondays, Well Spring, and Freedom House. We currently host Sondays Community Church on Sunday mornings at 10:30 A.M. For more information about this church please visit Tomball Renewal Center. 
In recent years, Main Street Crossing has also begun offering the use of this beautiful venue, with its stage, it’s sound system and its kitchen to other charities and ministries, not directly affiliated with Main Street Crossing for fund raising events to fulfill their ministry and charitable purposes.
UP-CLOSE CONCERTS (ALL SEATS WITHIN 45 FEET OF THE STAGE OR CLOSER)
We present Country, TexasCountry, Americana, Folk, Blues, Oldies 60s/70s, Tribute Bands, and more in an “Up Close” setting. For most concerts, there is an opportunity to meet the artist after the show, get a photo together, etc. We also provide food and beverage table service before and during the concert.

Manor Mill is a restored pre-revolutionary grist mill that houses a fine arts gallery, classes, a live music venue, ceramics studio and workshops, just over the hill from the Monkton Hotel in Baltimore County, MD.
Voted Best of Baltimore 2024 for "Cornucopia of Classes," Manor Mill is a community built upon creativity and learning, rooted in history and situated in the most beautiful area of rural Baltimore County in Monkton, Maryland.

McCabe's has always been a place for droppers-by and itinerant musicians to just hang out and poke around. Or, if you just feel like chatting, you can always give us a call.
Instrument Sales 
We are proud to have a fantastic selection of instruments. Ask about our in-house, 3-year warranty on almost every instrument we sell. That includes set-ups, adjustments, and anything else your instrument might do to itself (i.e. not broken strings). You’ll also get our 3-year 70% trade-up option.
Repairs & Maintenance 
McCabe’s has the best luthiers west of the Mississippi (probably east too). Has your guitar (uke, dulcimer, etc.) suffered injury or neglect? Buzzing? Bring it on by. Appointments aren't necessary, but feel free to call ahead...
Lessons 
Here at McCabe’s, we’ve taught tens of thousands of aspiring (and inspiring) musicians how to play, and we can teach you, too! We are currently offering both remote and in-person private lessons and the occasional remote group lessons. Check out our Lessons tab for details!
Live Music 
Since our first impromptu show back in 1968, McCabe's has been hosting the finest in folk, bluegrass, Americana, world music, and more. Our intimate, 150-seat venue is one of the best listening rooms in the world. We look forward to welcoming everyone back to the back room.

About
McCabe's® is a musical instrument store, concert venue, repair shop, and music school that opened in 1958. We have guitars, ukuleles, banjos, mandolins, dulcimers (hammered and plucked), fiddles, bouzoukis, sitars, ouds, ethnic percussion... you get the idea. We also carry electrics, amps, and accessories.
 Open daily 11:00 am -7:00 pm except New Year's Day, Easter Sunday, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and global pandemics, rain or shine.

About the event
Beat Root Revival is a dynamic multi-instrumentalist roots duo blending Folk, Blues, Country, and Rock ’n’ Roll into a rich, melodic sound driven by powerful, soul-stirring harmonies. The band is made up of powerhouse vocalists Andrea Magee and Ben Jones, whose voices intertwine with striking intensity and warmth.
Originally hailing from England and Ireland, Ben and Andrea journeyed to the United States four years ago—following in the footsteps of their ancestors—in search of a new life and a wider audience for their music. Armed with little more than a guitar, a bodhrán, and a fierce passion for storytelling through song, they found their adopted home in Austin, Texas.
Since then, they’ve built a steadily growing fan base, performing regularly across the live music capital while writing prolifically and honing a sound that is both timeless and fresh. With hunger in their harmonies and grit in their grooves, Beat Root Revival delivers music that feels at once deeply rooted and unmistakably alive.

About the event
Nathan Quick spent years working 190 miles off the shore of Louisiana. Getting to and from the oil rig required a helicopter ride, so personal effects had to be kept to a minimum. That meant no guitar for the young singer-songwriter. But Quick found the far-flung locale helped him fill up lyric books.
“The night is kind of beautiful, man,” he says. “The stars light up in the sky and they don’t look the same as they do at home. You can sit up and write without distractions. It was three weeks on, three weeks off, so it gives you stories and it gives you time to think. You go out on the helideck and look at nothing but water. What is MilkBoy Philly? 
MilkBoy Philly is a restaurant, bar, cafe and live-music venue all in one, located in Center City Philadelphia at 11th and Chestnut Sts. — the brainchild of music industry’s Tommy Joyner and Jamie Lokoff of MilkBoy Recording Studio.
MilkBoy Philly offers an upstairs music venue that hosts an impressive lineup of local and nationally recognized musicians, two full bars and a stepped up food and beverage program. MilkBoy Philly does triple time with early AM crowds opening weekdays at 7 a.m. offering the full drink and food menu thanks to neighboring hospitals, as well as a happy hour following and the late night scene.
MilkBoy Philly’s food menu reflects a dedication to quality product and a devotion to the Philadelphia dining and bar scene with options for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and every snack in between. General Manager Anna Reed and her team of some of Philly’s most skilled bartenders offer an array of creative signature cocktails & classics. Ever-rotating craft beer on tap, fun drink specials, your favorite canned brews, AND home of the famous MilkBoy Espresso Martini.

FAQ 
 Do I have to be 21 to see a show at MilkBoy?
MilkBoy Philly is a 21+ bar, restaurant & venue (with valid ID!). Anyone under the age of 21 is permitted only if they are accompanied by a parent or legal guardian for the duration of their stay at MilkBoy. Parents must remain with their children at all times & underage persons are not permitted near either of our bar areas. 
 Are refunds or exchanges available if I can’t make the show I bought tickets for?
Ticket purchases are non-refundable. Ticket insurance is available at checkout. 
 Is your venue ADA accessible and is seating available?
MilkBoy is not a seated venue, and the venue is located on the 2nd floor. There is no elevator access to the 2nd floor, and concertgoers must be able to walk up a flight of stairs to access MilkBoy shows. Chairs are available upon request. Please feel free to contact us at milkboytickets@gmail.com if you’d like more information and we will try to be as accommodating as possible. 
 Can I go to MilkBoy without buying show tickets/paying a cover?
Of course! There is almost never a cover to hang out downstairs. 
 Where can I park?
There are parking lots and a garage nearby on Sansom street, or metered street parking. 
 Can I take pictures at your venue?
Cameras without detachable lenses are permitted- so take ‘em, post ‘em, and tag us @milkboyphilly! Those who wish to take professional photos must receive permission from the band/artist or venue. 
 Can I host a private party at MilkBoy?
Yes! Email us at milkboy.events@gmail.com or call us at (215)925-6455. Our upstairs venue space is beautiful, totally private, has a large bar & full PA system! Our rates are low and our hospitality is generous and fun. We will give you the complete run-down in person, you’ll see that hosting your event at MilkBoy will give your guests something to talk about all year long.

About Us
We LOVE bringing people together! It’s what we do. Creating the magical blend of food, music, and laughter. All of the touchstones that bring life to a community. 
We understand the power of the live event and the healing energy of simply gathering. 
If you have been to Miller’s, you know what a gorgeous venue it is, warm and inviting to all who enter. Together, we always dreamed of having a venue on the main street of our sweet little town of Carnation, where we have lived for 20 years. Our deepest gratitude to beautiful soul’s, Jules Hughes and Lee Grumman, along with the support of our friends and neighbors who have made this dream possible. Here we go! 
Nancy Colton:
Nancy spent years as an accomplished, award-winning singer-songwriter. She has performed locally and nationally in venues including The Gorge, Tractor Tavern, 5th Avenue Theatre, Harvard Square and Bumbershoot. Nancy is also a passionate baker, curator of flavors, and alchemist of apothecary magic! Her zest for life and adventure shines brightly in her love of family, nature, hiking, skiing and paragliding.

Eppy
My name is Michael “Eppy” Epstein
In 1971, I opened My Father’s Place at 19 Bryant Avenue in Roslyn with Richie Havens as our first performer. I operated at that location until 1987. It was fun and dangerous. Read the book! Since then I have operated My Father’s Place in various locations in Roslyn, Queens, Westbury and recently in Glen Cove. Now, we are back in Roslyn and back in business as a Supper Club inside The Roslyn H0tel.

The Fine Print
Welcome to My Father’s Place at The Roslyn Hotel. We are here to provide a friendly, musical and gastronomically pleasing environment for your edification. However, there are a few things to keep in mind:​​
​
The venue is a Supper Club.
All shows, dates, times and venue locations are subject to change without notice.
All ticket holders will be seated on a first come first serve basis. 
Tickets must be purchased prior to seating - no reservations.
Doors open at 6 pm for dinner and drinks
Shows start at 8 pm unless otherwise stated
We are here to have fun - but not at the cost of our fellow attendees. 
Drink responsibly.
Remember - smoking and vaping anywhere in the hotel is STRICTLY prohibited. A designated smoking area is available outside.
Refund Policy - If an event is cancelled, no action is required. We will refund the ticket price to the original method of payment used at the time of purchase within 30 days. Service fees are not refundable.
If an event is postponed and rescheduled, the original ticket will be honored on the new date.
Events may be cancelled or postponed by venue, promoter, or performer for a variety of reasons.

Frequently Asked Questions
HOW DO I GET TO MY FATHER’S PLACE?
First, DO NOT follow GOOGLE. We are located in the Roslyn Hilton Tapestry Hotel at 1221 Old Northern Blvd. in Roslyn, NY. 
WHAT ABOUT PARKING?
We have a 3 level parking garage in the rear of the building. When you pull in the top level, you will find a ramp leading down in the far left corner. The club is located on the second level of the garage. PARKING IS FREE.
WHAT TIME DO THE DOORS OPEN FOR A SHOW?
Typically the doors will open TWO HOURS prior to show time.
HOW LONG DOES THE SHOW LAST?
That is totally up to the artists. Typically the shows last 1 ½ - 2 hours after the artist takes the stage, but that is at the artists discretion.
​
CAN I RESERVE A TABLE?
Sorry, we DO NOT take reservations. Seating is on a FIRST COME, FIRST SEATED basis. The earlier you arrive, the better seats you will have. 
WHAT AM I ENTITLED TO FOR MY TICKET PURCHASE?
You purchase allows you entry to the venue to see the performance. As long as you arrival is timely (NOT late), it also entitles you to a seat.
CAN I GET FOOD AND A DRINK?
YES! We are a Supper Club. At each table you will find a menu. We have full wait staff service. A sample menu can be found at the bottom of our HOME page on this Web site.
You will enjoy the show from the table you are seated at – no need to move!
​
IS THERE A MINIMUM/COVER CHARGE?
We currently do NOT have a minimum or cover charge. That being said, we DO expect patrons to dine. We couldn’t survive has a music venue without folks enjoying the offerings.
HOW MANY CAN FIT AT A TABLE?
Most of our tables are set up for 2 and 4 people. Please note that tables can NOT be pushed together. (A few reasons for this. It causes an undue burden on the wait staff, it causes problems with the Fire Marshall. As well, many of the professionals who play the club comment on the amount of conversation coming from larger groups that makes it difficult for them and other patrons to enjoy the show.)

Our Musical Journey
Established in 1971, My Father's Place was founded on the principle of presenting original music by original artists to a discerning audience of Long Island music fans. The live music scene developed and evolved over the years to include live concerts broadcast on 92.7 WLIR FM in association with record companies, agents and artist management. The musical journey of Eppy and My Father's Place was memorialized in the book, FUN+DANGEROUS by photographer Steve Rosnefield and Michael "Eppy" Epstein. More adventures were covered in the documentary film Dare To Be Different by film maker ...And coming soon, an independent documentary film about My Father's Place and Eppy will be released sometime in the near future.
The film by Meshakai ... has a working title - Names Don't Mean Shit.

ABOUT US
The Narrows Center for the Arts was founded in 1995 for the promotion and enjoyment of the visual and performing arts. The Narrows, a fully accessible venue, is comprised of two visual art galleries, a performance theater, visual artist studios, and rental space for events, classes, and meetings.
FROM HUMBLE BEGINNINGS
The Narrows Center for the Arts started as a small gallery exhibition space devoted to local artists and craftsmen with an open mic for local musicians. The initial location, where the North and South Watuppa Ponds come together, was near a waterway the locals refer to as the Narrows.
When a development project took over the initial space, the property owner offered rental space on the third floor of the former American Printing Company (APC) mill building on Anawan Street on the historic Fall River Massachusetts waterfront, our current home. In the early 20th century, APC was one of the largest textile operations in the world. 
Thanks to generous community support and strong stewardship, the Narrows Center grew from 1,000 to 15,000 square feet and with the help of dedicated volunteers the 3rd floor space was redesigned. It took almost four years to complete the transformation as we embraced the elegant architectural design, high ceilings and spectacular views of Mount Hope Bay and Battleship Cove. The 20-foot ceilings provide the ideal acoustic and visual backdrop for what has come to be known as a regional destination for the visual and performing arts. In 2017, the Narrows purchased our gorgeous mill building and tripled the area in which we present live music, visual art, artists’ studios, classroom instruction, and arts-related businesses.

The founding mission of the Narrows Center for the Arts was to promote and develop an appreciation of the visual and the performing arts. Artist studios are one component of our ever-growing and successful cultural arts center.
Over the years several artists have used studio workspace at the Narrows to create their artwork in the company of like-minded artists. An open studio door is a welcome invitation to all the arts enthusiasts who attend live performances and/or visit our galleries to view exhibitions.
