The Shepherd Center for the Performing Arts is a nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting all forms of the arts and providing educational programs that highlight the diverse culture of Fairhaven and surrounding communities.
It is “Community-Focused” and strives to showcase the musical, theatrical, dance, and cinematic efforts of local performers.
The Shepherd Center presents a wide variety of events and clinic/master classes that appeal to all generations and cultures within our region. We also aspire to provide an economic boost to historic Fairhaven’s active tourism industry as well as to become a cornerstone for the performing arts and serve as a point of pride for area residents.

Our Mission
The Shepherd Center for the Performing Arts was founded in 2022 to educate, enrich and support the performing arts community in Fairhaven, MA and the surrounding communities. We provide clinics, rehearsal space, lectures, and outreach programs that give emerging artists opportunities to perform and grow. We distribute materials, instruments and instruction that are designed to entertain, educate, support, and inspire artists, patrons and the community.

About Us
This is a safe, creative space where everyone is welcome!
Our volunteer staff works endlessly to create an enjoyable and welcoming experience from the moment you walk through our doors. Our quality sound engineering and venue acoustics help us to provide countless unforgettable performances.
We are here to share with you the wonderful array of talent that your friends and neighbors have to offer, along with some regional artists! But our mission goes beyond entertainment: We want YOU to take part in the experience…not only as audience members, but also as creators and we can provide that education here!

MUSIC
Local, regional and national artists fill the center with their talents!
Our admission price are always of nominal cost or funded by sponsor donations.
Music clinics, lectures, exhibitions, open mics and jam sessions held at little to no cost.

DANCE
Ballet, Ballroom, Square Dancing & more!
There are open areas for rehearsals and performances in various styles.
Programs and recitals are presented in the main hall by and for local artists.
Regional and national troupes are hosted for clinics and master classes.
CINEMA
Aaannd, ACTION!
Regional digital and film producers present their programs with a Q&A afterward.
Classes are offered to budding filmmakers in pre-production, production, and post-production standards and editing techniques.
Lectures from film and digital greats…would be great!
THEATER
Dramatic performances!
Plays, poetry, storytelling, comedy, and musicals are rehearsed and/or presented.
Educational presentations on Stage Crafts such as Theatrical Lighting/Sound techniques, Set design/fabrication, Stage Management, Costume Fabrication, Stage Combat, etc. are offered – we are always looking for more teachers!

 Music Center at St. Petersburg College Gibbs Campus 
The 2026-27 Palladium Chamber Series continues at the Music Center on SPC’s Gibbs Campus while the Palladium at SPC undergoes its historic renovation. This welcoming 300-seat recital hall offers wonderful acoustics, two Steinway pianos, an intimate audience experience perfect for chamber music, and ample free parking.
Location & Address
Music Center on SPC’s Gibbs Campus
6605 Fifth Avenue North
St. Petersburg, FL 33710
Music Center Seating Chart
View the seating layout for Palladium Chamber Series performances at the Music Center. Seating is reserved, and availability will vary by performance and subscription package. 
On a mobile device? Click or tap image below to view the full-size seating chart.
Planning Your Visit
Arrival and Doors
For all Palladium Chamber Series performances, the Music Center lobby, remote box office, and doors to the concert hall will open at 6 p.m. Performances begin at 7 p.m.
Remote Box Office and Will Call
A Palladium remote Box Office will be set up in the northwest corner of the Music Center lobby. Tickets held at Will Call will be available for pickup there.
Tickets may be purchased in advance online or by calling our box office at 727.822.3590. Tickets may also be purchased at our remote box office on the evening of the performance, subject to availability.
Parking
Free parking can be found in the SPC campus lots surrounding the Music Center. Accessible parking spaces are also available. Please see the campus map below for parking locations and additional details.

WHY YOUR GIFT MATTERS
Your gift helps sustain the performances, artists, and programs that bring our community together. 
For more than 25 years, the Palladium at St. Petersburg College has been a home for live music, dance, and cultural experiences in the heart of St. Petersburg. 
Each year, the Palladium:
Presents more than 250 performances and events
Welcomes over 67,000 patrons
Supports more than 2,000 artists
Contributes an estimated $14 million to the local economy
MAKE YOUR GIFT 
Please select how you would like your gift to support the Palladium:
Friends of the Palladium: Our annual fund to support ongoing programming, artists, and operations through the Palladium’s annual fund.
Transform the Palladium (Palladium Capital Improvement Fund): Help renovate and reimagine our historic venue.
Palladium Chamber Series: Support world-class chamber music performances. 
BEACON Dance Performances: Support innovative contemporary dance and new work.
Palladium Creative Fellowships: Support local artists developing new work through our Creative Fellowship program (select “Other” in the form and type “Palladium Creative Fellowships” in the designation field)

The Concept of Singacoustic
The Artists. The Audience. The Exchange.
Singacoustic gives artists room to be heard and audiences a chance to get closer to their work. Established performers, local musicians, and first-time open mic participants all contribute to what happens here. People come to hear someone they know, discover someone they don’t, and spend time with others who care about live music. The bar and restaurant give those encounters time to continue, over a meal, between sets, or after the show.
The Heart of the Session: Our Mission to the Artist
Some rooms have music. At Singacoustic, the whole room listens. With just 52 seats and exceptional sound, a performance feels close enough to be yours, a breath between lyrics, the brush of fingers across strings, a story shared a few feet from your table.
That intimacy carries beyond the stage. Warm lighting, inviting surroundings, and a personal welcome make Singacoustic a place to settle into. Come for a show, linger over dinner, or meet a friend at the bar. Our kitchen and full bar are part of the experience, whether there’s a show that evening or you simply want good food, a drink, and good company.
Singacoustic brings the care of a listening room to the pleasures of a neighborhood bar and restaurant. Music, well served—and a warm welcome every time.
The Architect of Discovery: Empowering the Songwriter’s Voice
Our mission is to serve as a bridge between a songwriter’s private craft and a public audience that truly listens. We recognize that for many aspiring artists, the challenge isn't just finding a stage, but finding one that respects the nuance of their work. By providing a professional environment designed for intentional performance, we empower musicians to present their music in its purest form.

Where Education
Meets Entertainment
Singacoustic was built on a simple belief: that every songwriter deserves to be heard in a room that actually knows how to listen.
The venue is the culmination of 35 years on the road. After three decades as a touring sound engineer and 20 years teaching the mechanics of concert production, founder Hani Gadallah set out to create an intimate space where the audio quality matches the caliber of the performance.
From Virtual to Physical
Our story began in the quiet of the 2020 Covid 19 shutdown. What started as a virtual production, featuring local, aspiring singer-songwriters in live-streamed recording sessions was born out of a necessity to keep the craft alive. When the world reopened, that mission transitioned from a screen to a physical stage. Singacoustic moved from the recording studio to our current home: a dedicated, intimate listening room designed for the ``hidden`` talents of the world.
The Listening Room Experience
At Singacoustic, we strip away the distractions. This isn't a noisy bar or a background-music lounge; it is a space intentionally designed for indie-folk, jazz, and acoustic artists.
Every technical detail from the signal path to the room's atmosphere—is informed by decades of professional audio experience. Singacoustic provides a professional platform for artists to share their stories and a quiet, immersive environment for the community to discover them.
Born as a virtual concept during the 2021 pandemic, Singacoustic is now a vibrant reality in Portland, Oregon.

Private Events at Singacoustic
Looking for a unique space for your next gathering? Singacoustic is available for select private events, offering an intimate atmosphere centered around music, conversation, cocktails, beer, food, and community.
Our listening-room environment is ideal for:
Birthday celebrations
Anniversary celebrations
Corporate gatherings
Album release parties
Songwriter showcases
Community events
Small receptions and creative meetups
Singacoustic provides a warm, welcoming setting that feels personal and memorable, far from the typical event venue experience. Guests enjoy an authentic atmosphere designed for enjoyment.
Because our calendar fills quickly, we encourage hosts to contact us early to discuss availability, event size, and special requests.
Can I rent Singacoustic without live music?
Yes. While music is central to our identity, the venue can also accommodate private gatherings, meetings, and creative events without performances.
Can we bring our own performers?
In most cases, yes. We can also help connect you with local artists that fit your event style and budget.
Is food and beverage service available?
Yes. We provide a full kitchen and Bar menu.
How many guests can the venue accommodate?
Capacity depends on the style of the event and room setup. Contact us with your event details so we can discuss the best configuration.
Can private events be livestreamed or recorded?
Not at the moment. Our live stream installation in underway and should be ready shortly.
How do I inquire about booking a private event?
Please contact us through the website with your preferred date, event type, estimated attendance, and any special requests.

Society Hall Welcomes the Beautiful Celtic and World Music Virtuosity of Michael Mandrell and Anam Cara!
11/15/26, 7:00 PM
Anam Cara, which means “soul friend” in Irish, is a trio based in Taos, NM featuring Celtic, New Age, and original music. Julie Hawley on Celtic harp and lead vocals and Jimmie Killingsworth on bouzouki, tenor banjo, and mandolin have been mainstays in the northern New Mexico folk scene for over a decade and have recently teamed with the touring and recording guitar ace and composer, Michael Mandrell, to produce a rich interplay of soulful vocals and intricate instrumental arrangements. The music ranges from rousing to meditative and is designed to move your heart and free your mind.

Society Hall Welcomes Back Junior and Mickie Vasquez for "Chapter 23" CD Release Concert!
11/21/26, 7:00 PM
From the wind-carved plains of Lubbock, Texas, where music is woven into the land, Calistro Junior and Mickie Vasquez step into the spotlight together with their long-awaited duo debut “Chapter 23”. Released on Alamosa's own Howlin’ Dog Records, this self-titled album is a heartfelt collection of songs born from life, love, and years of making music side by side. Featured tracks on the new album include “Little Things” — a haunting reminder of how little things can overwhelm; “It’ll Turn Around” — a hopeful anthem in uncertain times; “Time and Again” — the echoes of love that keep returning; “1,4,3” — a timeless expression of devotion; and “No Tom Petty” — a nostalgic nod to youth, loss, and musical roots.
Calistro “Junior” Vasquez is a singer and songwriter, born in Lubbock and raised in West Texas. He has been playing professionally for over 50 years. Junior began playing guitar at the age of ten and fronted his first band at age 11. From the early 70's to the early 80's, Junior fronted several bands, including Peyote, Texas Rain, Junior's Farm, and Rhema, and traveled throughout Texas, New Mexico and Oklahoma. They opened for several national acts including David Alan Coe, Styx, Batdorf and Rodney and many more. "Music is the universal language - it knows no boundaries”, says Vasquez. He continues, “It reaches down to the soul and makes a person laugh, cry, reminisce, dance…. My hope is that my music will do just that."
Society Hall board member and area musician and music producer Don Richmond adds, “Junior and Mickie's gorgeous vocals and great songs just transport you to a wonderful place, where life is amazing and the world is full of beauty. Can't help but love them!”
Born and raised in Lubbock, Tx. Junior has been an integral part of the music scene in Lubbock and the surrounding area since 1973. On June 1, 2015, we officially received our 501(C)(3) status for the Society Hall Foundation. On August 3, 2015, with a little help from a lot of people, we were able to close on the property. Yay!

SOFIA RENTAL PACKET
The Sofia Tsakopoulos Center for the Arts is Sacramento’s premier rental venue — a state-of-the-art facility designed for performance, presentations, and private events of all kinds. We offer a range of flexible spaces, each with professional production values, excellent acoustics, and a committed support staff.
Whether you’re hosting a concert, dance performance, graduation ceremony, corporate meeting, film screening, or private celebration, the Sofia has a space that fits your event and your budget.
The building is open Tuesday through Sunday, 10am–10pm. Monday access is available for an additional fee.
Parking is easily accessible in the adjacent parking structure.
Please fill out the form below and a representative will contact you as soon as possible!
Sutter Theatre 
Capacity – 380
The Sutter Theatre is a fully equipped proscenium theatre with professional lighting, sound, and projection systems. It is ideal for dance, music, theatre, lectures, film screenings, graduations, and large assemblies.

Lighting Control console is ETC Ion Xe.
ETC ColorSource Spot and Pars
2 1117C ALEX FOLLOW SPOTS E-vision Laser 10K with the ultra- short throw lens (0.38:1).
Rear Projection Screen 50′-0″ x 21’6″
Stage Dimensions
Proscenium opening is 44′-0″ wide x 18′-0″ high
Rough square footage of stage plus wing space is 2300 sq. feet.
Audience floor to stage floor is 3′-0″

(1) basic pre-set stage lighting look
(1) assigned chorus dressing room
(1) Lighting and Sound Technician
Setup for two microphones Basic Sound Playback

The Spot on Kirk
About The Spot on Kirk
Nestled in the heart of Roanoke, The Spot on Kirk is a lively hub for music enthusiasts. It offers a unique space where local and touring artists can showcase their talents.
The Story
Mission Statement: To serve as an intimate music venue and gathering place in Downtown Roanoke, fostering a vibrant community through diverse live entertainment experiences.
In 2016, The Spot on Kirk started under the non-profit umbrella of Down By Downtown Music (DxDT), which is an annual celebration of Roanoke's ever-growing music scene which takes place every April.
As a very special, much needed music venue, The Spot on Kirk has hosted over 500 shows within nearly a decade — and that's not even including all the history from back when the joint was known as Kirk Avenue Music Hall! Attracting an eclectic cast of artists from around the world, and helping launch the likes of Morgan Wade and Palmyra, The Spot on Kirk is both a listening room and community center for all music lovers. For an exhaustive list of the artists who have played on our stage, visit our archive page, HERE!
This charming room also serves as an event rental space for special events including private parties, meetings, screenings, and performances. To learn more about rentals, visit our rental tab HERE!

Welcome
The humble, yet nationally known, Stone Mountain Arts Center
in Brownfield, Maine is turning twenty years old.
Two decades ago, singer-songwriter Carol Noonan and her husband Jeff Flagg opened an unlikely music venue up a mountain road in what to most seems to be the middle of nowhere. Artists continue to profess their love about playing at what is now a nationally coveted tour stop.
Come see music in a special way.

Meet the Owners
Meet the owners of the Stone Mountain Arts Center. Carol Noonan and Jeff Flagg.
Noonan the Musician
Noonan, being a musician herself and having knowledge of touring first hand, wanted to do it better than what she found was a growingly careless circuit. There are and were fewer owner-operated venues, and the lack of attention to detail for the artist as well as the audience was, she felt, fostering lackluster results. Noonan’s music life has been a journey from studying voice at the New England Conservagtory, to adult contemporary/ singer songwrter genre with indy and major label signings (Island and Rounder)resulting in multiple recordings under her belt. These days she is the chief cook and bottle washer and on occasion hangs up her apron and puts on her guitar for a show with the Stone Mountain band. The band features her two good friends, Duke Levine and Kevin Barry who have played with her for over 30 years and enjoy coming up to the Maine woods to do shows when she can fit one in. 
Jeff the Net Builder
Jeff had a successful commercial fishing net business, building ground fish nets for a large fleet of boats out of the New England coast harbors. Based out of Portland originally, when they got the house in Brownfield, he decided to downsize, and build a barn on their property to work from home. Little did he know years later that very barn would be the Stone Mountain Arts Center. Jeff is a self-made carpenter who learned to do whatever job had to be done. He would learn how to do it rather than hiring out. Carol jokes, his “thriftiness” made him learn how to do everything we needed done.

Our Performers
Our Performers are like family to us. We have always wanted SMAC to feel like a respite.. a bit of a home away from home … complete with bountiful food, a personalized cake, and country hospitality. Uncommon Ground is a sustainable restaurant, urban farm, coffee house, brewery and home to an iconic music venue! Our Listening Room helps support the emerging performers who are rooted in Chicago! We’re able to provide performers our intimate sit down listening room complete with full light and sound engineering so long as you bring out your audience to eat, drink and listen with us during your show! Live music, comedy, open mics, screenings, variety shows, and intimate theater thrives in our spaces. The Upper Jay Art Center (UJAC) was founded by Scott and Byron Renderer in 2005 as a
place to enjoy music, theater, and visual art. 
​
In 2008, it incorporated as a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization and, with a wink and a nod the brothers, upholsterers by trade, named the performance space the Recovery Lounge. Over the past decade, the Lounge has become an essential gathering place in the Ausable River Valley, bringing an eclectic array of professional musicians to this rural corner of the northeast Adirondacks throughout the year and staging plays by classic and contemporary playwrights, acted by an ever expanding troupe of local talent.

Goldpine is powerhouse Nashville duo Benjamin and Kassie Wilson, and they’re releasing a new album this year, their fourth: “FOUR”! Characterized by Kassie’s powerful and lavish vocals, the songs take the formidable approach to grab your attention and tell it like it is. It’s empowered without showboating or pushiness. There’s no frailty here but there’s a consistent depth of authenticity and honesty. 
Bold + gold harmonies straight from Nashville. Winner of the Rocky Mountain Songwriter Contest and seen on NPR's 'Mountain Stage with Kathy Mattea' and the KC Chiefs National Anthem, Goldpine's brand of “aggressive Americana” is a flawless channel for their bold and reminiscent compositions. Husband and wife of 16 years, Goldpine has just released their third album, appropriately titled Three, which collects stories of sorrow, tested-love and space-trips.

Cutting-edge Blues singer, songwriter and bottleneck slide guitarist, Catfish Keith, has established himself as one of the most exciting Country Blues performers of our time. Catfish’s innovative style of foot-stomping, deep Delta Blues and American Roots music has spellbound audiences the world over.

The 30-year veteran has toured the USA, UK and Europe dozens of times to wide acclaim, headlining major music festivals, and appearing with legends John Lee Hooker, Ray Charles, Robert Cray, Koko Taylor, Taj Mahal, Leo Kottke, and many others.

Rock, Blues, New Orleans Funk, R&B and Southern Soul!
Often transcending genres, Bywater Call is known for their dynamic, emotional live shows, featuring powerful vocals and exciting interplay between all 7 band members. Meghan Parnell confirms her status as one of the most impressive vocalists in the modern blues and soul scene, while Dave Barnes and the rest of the band demonstrate just how tight they have become.
The band’s music is an exploration of life’s highs and lows. From intimate ballads that lay bare the complexities of human emotion to electrifying anthems that demand to be played at full volume, their catalogue is a journey through the human experience, incorporating influences from the likes of The Band, The Wood Brothers, Tedeschi Trucks Band, Otis Redding, Little Feat, Sly and the Family Stone, among others.

Hello Art Lover...
Vangarde Arts is the result of years of passion and sharing ideas within a group of innovative thinkers! Led by local promoter Brent Stockton, a dedicated group of volunteers have invested time and energy into creating a non-profit center that magically blends visual art with performing art in an open and inviting atmosphere. Appropriately named "Vangarde Arts", this group is leading the way and exploring new boundaries for the arts, adding to the already growing cultural district in downtown Sioux City. The gallery lounge and intimate listening room create a wonderful space for patrons to enjoy the social and educational value of original art forms.
The Vangarde Arts Partner program has been a valuable part of the experience for over a decade! We have recently added some great new perks to membership, including FREE guest passes, a Birthday Gift Card and other money saving benefits!

Entertainment Info 
Please Note: All shows are General Admission unless otherwise noted in the Calendar. 
Admission is Cash Only.
We produce shows from October to April each year. We book mainly well-known performers and groups, reoccurring shows, and benefit concerts. As always, check out our online calendar or newsletter for more info.
The Bean has been the forum for many types of entertainment. It started at our first “Java Jive”, or Open Mic, in 1990. Over the years, we have hosted many benefits and provided space for many different genres of artistic presentation. It seemed that this type of entertainment, which is all based in the Folk Tradition, just wanted to be here.
The entertainment has ranged from Celtic Folk to Cajun Zydeco, from storytelling to poetry reading to even an Off Broadway Play. Over the years we have booked many talented people to help create awareness for this type of entertainment, and continue to do so, including here on the internet.

Venue of Merging Arts
Our mission is to rescue a historically significant former church building by transforming it into a unique venue in which various forms of artistic expression can merge together to promote, educate, entertain and involve the community in the arts. 
About 
VOMA is an all-volunteer 501 (c) (3) non-profit community arts center located in the historic Cambria City section of Johnstown.
VOMA is housed in a former church that was constructed in 1911 as St. Mary’s Syrian Orthodox Church. In 1977, a newly formed congregation, St. George Serbian Orthodox Church acquired the property. By 2008 the property was no longer home to an active parish and had fallen into disrepair. A former Cambria City couple acquired the property that same year with a vision of re-purposing it into a unique community arts center. In 2012 the former church and parsonage house were donated to VOMA.

Willingham Theater
We want every citizen and visitor of Yadkin County to be moved, inspired, informed, and challenged by the transformational power of the art they consume. But art capable of inspiring, educating, and exploring is not limited to pen to paper, or paint to canvas.
The staple of our theater arts program is the Willingham Theater.
Throughout history, creatives have documented, questioned, and reconstructed society based on the power of their music and stories.
Our mission is to transform and enrich the lives of every citizen, and visitor, of Yadkin County through a broad variety of arts programs, including live theater.
The staple of our theater arts program is the Willingham Theater.
About the Theater
The Willingham Theater hosted its first performance in December 2012, just two short years after the Yadkin Cultural Arts Center opened its doors to the citizens of Yadkin County. While many might dismiss the power of a small city theater, we like to prove that we can indeed bring the big city excitement and experience to our small town. Our state-of-the-art theater seats 193 patrons and showcases a variety of engaging performances ranging from music, dance, drama, comedy, and even film. In addition to ample seating, the Willingham Theater has a remarkable state-of-the-art-technology.

The Yadkin Arts Council
The Yadkin Arts Council is a nonprofit 501(C)3 organization dedicated to enriching the lives of Yadkin County citizens through the arts. The Yadkin Arts Council has been a creative beacon in the community since 1975 and has shared space all over Yadkinville, including in the Yadkinville High School auditorium, and even a retired freezer locker! Finally, we found our permanent home when the Yadkin Cultural Arts Center opened its doors in September 2011. Click HERE to learn More About the History of the Yadkin Arts Council. 
“Transforming lives in our community through the arts by offering opportunities to encounter, create, and participate.”
Yadkin Cultural Arts Center
While we started small in those shared spaces, we have expanded rapidly in our new space, bringing big city excitement to our small town!

OUR GREATEST PASSION
LIVE MUSIC
The Word Barn serves as a unique and intimate glorified house concert setting and concert experience unlike any other. With impeccable acoustics, welcoming environment, unique interaction with performers, and a combination of seats, stools, and balcony, it provides a superb community gathering centered around good music.
The Word Barn plays host to Bright & Lyon Productions—a passion project of music fanatics who are dedicated to bringing and sharing the best offerings of the music scene today. All concerts have limited seating, require tickets (first come, first serve). Concerts usually sell out—advance ticket purchase is strongly recommended.

 Onstage at the ZACC
The Show Room at the ZACC is a performance venue that hosts live music, comedy, theatre, film screenings, dance performances, community gatherings and more. Throughout the year, we welcome local and regional artists alongside nationally touring acts, offering an ever-changing lineup of performances and experiences.

 INDIGENIZING THE ZACC 2026 - FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 6
Explore artwork by Indigenous artists, shop from Indigenous makers, enjoy live music and food, and come together in recognition of the strength, resilience, and continued presence of Indigenous communities. The evening begins with an MMIR Awareness Walk and smudging at 4:15 PM, starting at the library, followed by festivities at the ZACC from 5–9 PM.   Featured Artwork: Rebirth from the Flames by Lauryn Gordon (Blackfeet) This year's featured poster artwork, Rebirth from the Flames, by Lauryn Gordon, reflects the themes of resistance, resilience, and renewal through the imagery of a butterfly dancer rising like a phoenix. Inspired by the phoenix's symbolism of rebirth and transformation, Gordon's work depicts a dancer whose shawl becomes wings of flame, representing the strength to rise, overcome restrictions, and emerge renewed.
