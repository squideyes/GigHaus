My family and I set out to create a place in the Texas Hill Country where people could gather to truly listen to songwriters sing their songs and tell their stories. This room has been inspired by other storied establishments but mainly by Kent Finlay’s Songwriter Circle at the Cheatham Street Warehouse where I and many others cut their teeth.

Watch our documentary!
Want to learn more about how the Reeves was renovated and reopened? Enjoy the documentary Reeves: A Home for Music by filmmaker Michael Lippert about the renovation and reopening process.
​Most people who grew up near Elkin, NC have fond memories of the Reeves. The beautiful Art -Deco movie house was originally opened in 1941, showing films and news reels of the day. Later, it was converted into the Elk Twin, also a movie theater, before eventually falling into disrepair. The people of Elkin knew this place was too special to lose, but times were tough and funds were low. Years passed and the Reeves sat empty, slowly being improved and preserved by dedicated citizens.
Finally, in 2013, the Reeves was up for sale, and three long-time Elkin residents decided to buy it and turn it into a live music venue. Employing and partnering with as many local craftspeople and manufacturers as possible, they fully restored the original building facade, and rebuilt the sign and marquee to the specifications of the original architectural plans. The inside was converted into a modern industrial music hall, with great acoustics and superior sound quality as the primary goal. 
In 2020, like so many small businesses, the Reeves was forced to close due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Reeves patrons rallied behind the venue and its staff to raise money and keep up an interest until a safe reopening was possible in the summer of 2021.

A Cultural Cornerstone Since 1940
Historically a center for furniture manufacturing, Galax, Virginia is also known as a center of traditional “old-time” music and musicians.
In 1940, the town of Galax was excited when the historic Rex Theater first opened it’s doors and the public rushed in. Operating as the only movie theater in the twin county area, many area youngsters and families grew up going to the Rex to see the latest Hollywood blockbusters.
Since 1940, the Rex has served as many things to the Galax community. Today, it is owned by the City of Galax, who has renovated it and made it an important part of the officially designated Historical Downtown area.
80 YEARS OF LIVE Music 
Besides being a beautiful venue for a variety of local, regional, and national music acts, the Rex is also home to “Blue Ridge Backroads Live,” a weekly radio show featuring Old Time and Bluegrass bands broadcast live by a local radio station, Classic Country 98.1 FM. This broadcast is heard in five states. It can also be listened to “live” on the internet.
For the last 80 years the Rex has entertained both the citizens of Galax and visitor’s from all over the world. Recently receiving extensive renovations, it is now poised for even bigger things in the next 80 years.

Our History
Originally a printing press, the Rex served as a movie theatre from the 1940s to 1980s. 1989 saw the end of the Rex’s life as a movie house. The property then changed hands several times, functioning as a series of nightclubs. In 2016, the building was purchased by the Manchester Development Corporation (MDC). The Palace Theatre Trust entered into a private-public partnership with MDC in October 2018 with the shared goal of reviving the Rex and opening the space as a theatre once again.
On October 30, 2019 we opened the doors of the Rex Theatre, a state-of-the-art, 300-seat performance venue. In the four months the Rex was open before temporarily shutting down due to COVID-19, we began to see the possibilities of this new cultural resource. This venue has added immensely to our existing performance capabilities. We look forward to experiencing the growth of the Palace Theatre Trust’s newest addition.

Procedure for parking meters:
1. Find available space and remember your space number
2. Walk to street level corner stairwell or front of office location and find pay by space meter

About Us
The Rose Bowl Tavern, established in 1948, is a music venue, honky tonk, dive bar, watering hole and gathering place in downtown Urbana, Illinois. With a cozy no frills environment, lots of seating, dim lighting, live music (or some other fun event) everyday, and a great bar menu, there is something for everyone! 
Live music and events
From country to jazz to blues to rock to hiphop to bluegrass to experimental noise to songwriter circles to punk throwdowns to goth DJ nights to acoustic open jams and trivia and comedy and bingo and so on and so forth and you name it…. We are proud to feature national and regional touring artists as well as our amazing local performers in all genres and styles. Stop by and expect the unexpected, or check out our events calendar to learn more and plan your night out.
The Bar
12 draft beer taps, a top notch whiskey and tequila selection, a seasonal cocktail menu of special treats, THEE locally infamous watermelon shot, a healthy choice of all the staple American beers and boozes, and a decent selection of non-alcoholic options including craft CBD seltzers & sodas, drip coffee and a small selection of teas. Check out the full menu here. There is almost always a seat available at the bar, the booths, or a table to accommodate you and your party. The Rose Bowl, with its dimly lit, watering hole, 21st century honky-tonk vibe is a great place to have a drink. 
What else to know?
We do not have food, but you are welcome to bring take out. We highly recommend any of the nearby restaurants in downtown Urbana, their are many and they are all delicious! There are packaged bar snacks available for purchase
Most concerts and events are FREE with no cover charge. While we encourage guests to tip the artists via cash in the bucket, on your bar tab or with digital payments, it is not required. Please note that some concerts do have a cover charge and/or tickets for sale. Please check our events calendar

About the Series
The Roseburg Fall Concert Series brings twelve nights of touring talent to The Rosebud Theatre, an intimate, mostly all-ages music venue in the heart of downtown Roseburg. Indie favorites, outlaw country, bluegrass, blues, a Halloween weekend double header and a Tom Petty tribute to close it out. Touring artists tell us The Rosebud is their favorite room in Oregon, and once you’re inside, you’ll see why. It’s intimate and comfortable, every spot is close to the stage, and the sound is dialed in for every show.
Come for the music. Stay for the wine country, the river and a downtown full of great places to eat.

Easy to get to
Right off I-5. About an hour from Eugene, about 90 minutes from Medford, and under three hours from Portland.
Up close
The Rosebud holds about 100 people. No nosebleeds, no watching the band on a screen.
Easy on the wallet
Tickets run $15 to $25, and presale saves you a few bucks.
Walkable
Dinner, drinks and the show are all within a few blocks of each other downtown. Parking is untimed and free after 5pm.
Wine country next door
The Umpqua Valley is home to more than 20 wineries, most within a short drive of town.

If you need to purchase a ticket for the show, they will be available at the door when doors open until we reach our capacity.

South Seattle’s premier live music venue since 2011. 
We have live music every night of the week! Check out our calendar to see what’s coming up. 
With permanent backline, recording and video, a Steinway B grand piano and a generous stage, the Royal Room was designed to serve musicians and artists. Go to our booking page if you’re interested in performing at the Royal Room. 
Advance tickets for all ticketed shows can be purchased through Stranger Tickets before 3:00 PM day of show. Advance tickets can only be purchased online: we do not sell advance tickets at the venue. Refunds are not available within 48 hours of the event. 
Tickets do not guarantee seating during shows at the Royal Room. After purchasing tickets, please visit the Reservations page to book a table. Table reservations require advance tickets, and are only for guests who plan to dine at the Royal Room. We do not take reservations over the phone. 
Seating for non-diners is first come, first served. Please arrive early to guarantee a seat! 
The Royal Room is All Ages until 10pm; however, minors must remain outside the bar area at all times.

About 
In 2009 musician and composer Wayne Horvitz approached longtime Seattle bar, restaurant and venue owners Tia Matthies and Steve Freeborn about partnering on a new project in Seattle. After two years of planning and development, the Royal Room opened in historic Columbia City in December 2011.
With permanent backline, recording and video, a grand piano and a generous stage, the venue has been designed to serve the musicians and artists who will be performing. The restaurant and bar has a comfortable neighborhood feel that welcomes patrons of all ages and varied tastes, from an after work happy hour to late in the evening.
The music featured at the Royal Room covers a wide range of styles, with an emphasis on creative programming and development. This allows artists to work on projects in a collaborative community space.
For more information on the Royal Room please visit our home page, or follow us on Facebook and Twitter.
South Hudson Music Project was conceived in 2016, as a non profit partner to sustain music presentation at the Royal Room and support deepening connections with the surrounding neighborhood and like-minded musical community.
For more information on the South Hudson Music Project please visit www.shmproject.org.

Frequently Asked Questions 
Q: Is the Royal Room ADA accessible?
A: Yes! There are no stairs at our main entrance and most of our seating is accessible by wheelchair.
Q: Do you take reservations?
A: Yes! We are now accepting reservations for diners. After purchasing tickets, please visit the Reservations page to book a table. Table reservations require advance tickets, and are only for guests who plan to dine at the Royal Room. We do not take reservations over the phone.
Q: Is the Royal Room an all-ages venue? 
A: Minors are permitted at the Royal Room until 10 PM daily. The bar area is 21+ at all times.
Q: What time do you open? Google says you are open at 5PM. 
A: Our opening time depends on the show. Doors are usually one hour prior to showtime. Please check the specific event page on the website for details.
Q: Can I get a refund for my tickets?
A: Refunds are available until 48 hours prior to the day of the show. Please send a screenshot of your ticket confirmation to support@boldtypetickets.com.

Welcome to Sanctuary 
Sanctuary is a 5,000 square foot concert and event space. With high ceilings,
 historic non-denominational stained-glass windows, grand chandeliers and a
 state-of-the-art sound and lighting system, Sanctuary is the perfect backdrop
 for all types of celebrations and meetings.
Fully Customizable Events 
Sanctuary has hosted a variety of events, including concerts, private parties and
 events, weddings, fundraisers, celebrations of life and bar mitzvahs. We enjoy
 working with clients to customize their special events, creating an unforgettable
 experience in a venue that combines historic charm with modern elegance.

On any given night you might see a major celebrity from the film or music industry either having a beer or sitting in on stage jamming with the locals. This award-winning listening room has helped launch the careers of several prominent entertainers. It is also known for booking some of the greatest music legends around, performing in an intimate atmosphere that Kris Kristofferson likened to “playing in his own living room.”
Whether it’s one of the incredible free weekday happy hour shows, a weekend matinee or a late night performance by the next big thing, join us and find out why we are Austin’s choice for live music!
See the award winning documentary “Nothing Stays the Same, The Story of The Saxon Pub” on Amazon Prime.

Son of late songwriting legend, John Prine, Tommy Prine grew up in Nashville surrounded by music, art and writing. As a child, he thought all parents were musicians, as his father “going to work” meant performing shows for adoring fans and writing songs. Tommy learned to play guitar by watching his father play, copying the ways his fingers moved and inadvertently developing his own singular style. Summers in Ireland lent their own inspiration, as did 10 straight years camping at Bonnaroo. Prine’s musical tastes grew to become decidedly eclectic, spanning John Mayer, Outkast, Bon Iver, the Strokes and more.
It wasn’t until Prine reached his mid-twenties, though, that he considered a career of his own in music and began to share with others the songs he wrote in private. His songs were quickly met with excitement and enthusiasm, which sonically brings together a colorful patchwork of musical influences and lyrically explores existential questions and emotional experiences.
Known for his emotionally raw songwriting and spellbinding live performances, Tommy Prine’s music continues to carve out space beyond the shadow of legacy. As PBS remarked after a recent show in Los Angeles, “Listening on this night at Gold Diggers… you might think Tommy Prine was born to the stage.”
Delaney Ramsdell is an artist and songwriter based in Nashville, TN. Originally from Roosevelt, Texas (population 18), her sound, songwriting and storytelling is steeped in the culture of West Texas.
Her newest single “285” tells the story of Texas oilfields and the road that runs through them – 285, the most dangerous highway in America. Listen to “285” here.

Education
The Sound Wall Music Initiative is dedicated to fostering music education and supporting the development of young musicians and songwriters in our community.
Through a variety of enriching programs, we offer students the chance to learn directly from experienced artists and industry professionals. Our master classes provide in-depth, one-on-one mentorship, while our songwriters' workshops offer hands-on opportunities to hone their craft in a collaborative setting.
In addition to these immersive learning experiences, we bring music directly into schools, offering students a unique chance to engage with working musicians through interactive visits. Each year, we also host a Student Showcase at the Opelika Songwriters Festival (OSF), giving young artists a platform to perform and share their talents with a larger audience.
By connecting students with real-world musical experiences, SWMI is helping to cultivate the next generation of creative voices and providing them with the tools they need to succeed.
Student Songwriters Showcase
The Student Songwriter Showcase at the Opelika Songwriters Festival offers emerging talent a unique platform to perform original music in front of a live audience. This showcase provides students with valuable exposure and the opportunity to connect with seasoned artists and industry professionals.
Sound Wall Master Class
The Jazz Masterclass at The Sound Wall offers musicians a chance to learn from renowned jazz professionals in an intimate and interactive setting. Participants will gain insights into jazz techniques, improvisation, and performance skills, enhancing their musical knowledge and artistry.
In-School Visits
SWMI supports music education in our local schools by bringing in professional musicians for live performances, hands-on workshops, and interactive learning experiences. These programs aim to inspire students, enhance their musical understanding, and foster a love for the arts within the school community.

Buy Tickets Online
Tickets are available here on our site. Just click on the TICKETS button next to the show you wish to attend and follow the prompts.
Buy Tickets @tSGHR
Tickets can also be purchased in person in the lounge bar any time we’re open for a show (usually Wed-Sat 5pm-2am). There are NO service fees when paying with cash.
Age Restrictions
Age restrictions vary by show. Some are open to All Ages; others are open to only 18 OR 21 and over. Click on each show to see specific event details. We do not tolerate under age alcohol consumption. State issued ID is required of all guests, regardless of your age.
Seating
All shows are General Admission. Seating availability varies by show (some shows are standing room only, some shows are all seated, most are a mix). Seating is always first come, first serve.
General Policies
No refunds or exchanges on your tickets.
No smoking or vaping in the venue.
No outside drinks permitted.
No weapons permitted.

Welcome to the Spire Center! 
The Greater Plymouth Performing Arts Center, Inc. (GPPAC) is a nonprofit organization founded in 2010 and dedicated to performing arts and educational programs which support the vibrant arts community in the greater South Shore region. The organization founded The Spire, a venue to serve as a mecca for the arts and showcasing music, theatre and dance performances as well as education that supports the performing arts.

 The State Room
You know that The State Room brings great live music... but did you know that it is also one of the most unique and versatile private event spaces in Salt Lake City? The State Room is perfect for any private function—day or night. Our atmosphere is intimate, comfortable and stylish, and our live music capabilities can add a special dimension. Whether you are planning an anniversary party, a surprise birthday party, wedding reception, business meeting, CD release party, film screening or corporate function, The State Room may be the ideal location. We can customize our floor plan to meet your event needs. 
Venue Details 
We are 21 and older venue. 
We have a capacity of up to 299 people. 
Our lobby that can comfortably handle close to 75 people in a standing, cocktail style environment. The lobby is also a fantastic spot for a silent auction or to display art. 
The theatre has fixed seating for 175 and then an open floor that can be used for tables and chairs or used as a dance floor. The State Room can be arranged to fit nearly all types of events. A total of 299 people can fit into the theatre, as well. 
The State Room's first-class sound and lighting systems are included. Tell us what you want and we'll set it up. 
While we can offer your some preferred catering options.. You are welcome to choose a caterer yourself. We have ample space for prep rooms and ease of load in.. 
We have two full service bars, as well as, a portable bar available as needed. 
We can offer group parking at a nominal rate. 
We offer backstage dressing rooms and plenty of space to prepare for performances or catered events. 
We can use our resources as a renowned concert venue to help you find entertainment that meets your needs.

Information
What is The Station Inn? 
The Station Inn is a live music venue in the heart of Nashville, TN, an area referred to as the Gulch. For more than 40 years, The Station Inn has brought great bluegrass, roots and Americana music to millions of fans across the globe. Many of the world’s most successful musicians have played at The Station Inn, including Bill Monroe, JT Gray, Alan Jackson, Dierks Bentley, Vince Gill, Alison Krauss and John Prine. The venue has been around for a long time, and plans to be around for many generations, with a goal to bring great music and great fun to Nashville and the rest of the world.
Where is The Station Inn located? 
The Station Inn is located at 402 12th Avenue South in the heart of the Gulch of Nashville.
See more about location and directions here (stationinn.com/location)
When do the doors open? 
The doors open at 7pm, unless noted on the event page.
When do Station Inn shows begin? 
The Station Inn keeps a calendar online (www.stationinn.com/events) which has the most updated information about all upcoming Station Inn events.
Where do I park for a show? 
There is limited parking available right behind the building that fills up quickly before the show begins. More information about parking in the Gulch can be found here.
Can I bring my children to a Station Inn show? 
Yes, all minors are admitted to The Station Inn with an adult chaperone. The chaperone is responsible for good behavior. But kids will be kids. If your little tyke is making adorable noises that might not fit with the music being performed, we ask that you take them outside so that they can get it all out, then come right back in.
Is The Station Inn handicap accessible?
Yes, the venue is completely handicap accessible. We have a ramp to get in at the front door and the rest of the venue is on a single floor. So come on down! We’d love to have you.

Coworking FAQs
How do I get started? 
Ready to join our creative coworking community? Here’s how the sign-up process works. 
Let us know you’re ready to join through the contact form or phone number. 
We’ll ask you to select a four-digit entry code that will let you access the space. 
Once we activate your code (usually within 24 hours), you’re good to start working! If you need access right away, we can send you a preliminary code to get you started. 
Are there lockers? Can I leave my work things there? 
We don’t have any lockers (Yet!). You’re welcome to leave your things in the space while you go grab coffee or food nearby, but please take your items with you when you leave. Yes, the space is locked and secure, but we don’t want computers and personal items lying around while others use and visit the space. 
What is coworking? 
Coworking spaces are work environments that are shared by creatives and businesspeople from a variety of workplaces. Some of our members use Stockroom East as a second office, others use it as their primary workplace, and others use it as an office for their creative projects! 
The point is – it’s a place where cool people can get to know each other, collaborate, and get work done away from the office and away from home.
What equipment is there? What do I need to bring? 
The space has plenty of desks, chairs, tables, outlets, etc. for your use. Additionally, the podcast studio is equipped with microphones and sound dampening materials to create an optimal sound space for your recording session. Wifi and a printer are also part of your membership. 
There are no public computers — bring your own laptop, charger, and tech equipment. 
Can I bring people who don’t have a membership in for a meeting? 
Yes! Stockroom East is your working space. If you need to meet with someone about your work – bring them on in! The conference room is also available for larger group meetings.

About the Sutter Creek Theatre
The Sutter Creek Theatre Today
Owned by Laura and Byron Damiani Jr., the historic Sutter Creek Theatre is a complete multipurpose entertainment center that hosts events year-round. The theatre features concerts by award-winning artists, films, theater, civic and corporate meetings, and celebrations like reunions and weddings.
A former silent film theatre, the venue comfortably seats 215 people. Because it was designed for viewing film, every seat has a view of the stage. The original art deco ceiling designs are intact and inspired our logo. We have a balcony and the original theatre seats, complete with hat racks underneath. The theatre is renowned for its excellent acoustics and intimate seating.
For traveling artists and theater groups we offer sound engineering and lighting support. We serve beer, wine, other beverages and snacks. The theatre building is also home to Heart & Soul at 42 Main, offering unique home decor, jewelry and more.
 Owners Byron and Laura Damiani
Our Mission
The Sutter Creek Theatre professionally presents contemporary and traditional music, theatrical works, dance, film, and other events that are engaging to the hearts and minds of a diverse regional audience.
To achieve our mission, we strive to:
Present a variety of performance and cinematic experiences for a culturally, generationally and socially diverse audience.
Provide a culturally rich and engaging environment that fosters an appreciation of the arts while providing wholesome and stimulating entertainment.
Educate the community and our young people in an appreciation of the arts.
Serve as a cultural center for the arts in Amador County.
Our values
Commitment to artistic products and programs that enrich people's lives.
Service to the communities in Amador County.
Respect for our audience.
Responsible management of the Theatre as an historic and artistic treasure for the community.

Backline
We negotiate artist fees assuming there is no backline. Please tell us when contracting if you have backline needs, since it affects the fee we can pay. Backline generally has to come from out of the area.
Parking and unloading
Park in front of the theatre on Main Street (old Hwy 49), or unload at the back of the theatre in Boston Alley, which runs parallel behind Main Street. Boston Alley is for unloading only; we'll point you to residential parking a block or so away.
Load-in and sound check
Saturday shows: load in about 3:00, sound check 4:00, dinner 5:00, doors 6:30, show 7:00.
Friday shows: load in 4:00, sound check 5:00, dinner 6:00, doors 7:30, show 8:00.
Sunday shows: usually late afternoon with doors at 3 or 4 pm. Contact us for load-in times.
We build in time for flexibility and can usually move times earlier if needed. The stage is about 15 feet deep by 30 feet wide, with a backstage area about 10 feet deep behind the curtain (home to our Steinway grand piano) and a green room behind that. From Boston Alley, come in through the marked stage door via the green room, or through the fire exit.

Meals: we usually provide the band with a pizza meal.
Guest lists: we're small, so please keep them small and contact us in advance.

Last updated October 27, 2026
By buying tickets for a show at the Sutter Creek Theatre, you agree to these terms. They're short, and we've kept them plain.
Prices and fees
Online prices include a per-ticket online service fee. The price you see is the price you pay.
Advance prices apply until online sales close, usually at midnight before show day. After that, tickets are sold at the door at the door price, while they last.
Some shows sell out online. When they do, a small number of tickets may still be available at the door.
All sales are final
Please review your order carefully. All ticket sales are final, and we can't offer refunds or credit toward other shows if you're unable to attend.
