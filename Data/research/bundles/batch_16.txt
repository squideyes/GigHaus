Welcome to the Room 
 A NEW EXPRESSION WITH OLD BONES 
In a world overrun by copy-paste culture and commercial noise, Tigre Island Room offers something rare: a creative refuge rooted in history, built for the moment, and open to what’s next.
Named after a mysterious island whispered about in pirate lore—caught somewhere between the mainland marsh and Fernandina Beach—Tigre Island Room straddles the mythic and the modern. Our home is a landmark building designed in 1878 by French architect Anton “C.H.” Hout, once a dry goods store, now reborn as a music house with soul.
The bones are old—original yellow pine floors, towering brick arches, and cast iron storefronts still holding court. But the energy is electric. Sleek sound, immersive lighting, lush textures, and lounging nooks create a space where art, music, and conversation flourish. You might come for the show, but you’ll stay for the spell it casts.

About The Tin Pan
Tin Pan Alley was a time and a place in American musical history —literally an alley in Manhattan between the late 1800s into the 20th century, but also a defining moment when popular music original to the United States found its foothold.
We decided to name our restaurant and music venue “The Tin Pan” to celebrate the American music industry as a whole and to highlight the concentration of diverse talent springing up right here at home in Richmond, VA. That’s why our performance stage hosts top-level touring acts alongside crowd favorites from down the street.

 Tickets and Seating
How do I buy tickets to a show?
You can buy tickets online through our website. You may also give us a call at 804-447-8189 to have us process your tickets through our box office, or visit in person Tues-Sat 12PM-5PM. If you leave a voicemail, we will return your call the next time the box office is open. There are lower fees associated with calling us versus buying tickets online.
How do I make a dinner reservation?
You will be asked to choose a dinner reservation time when you purchase your tickets. Tickets and dinner reservations are one and the same; we do not generally offer advance tickets without a dinner reservation, and it is not possible to make a dinner reservation without also purchasing a ticket.
Do I have to make a dinner reservation?
As a general rule, yes. We are a “supper club,” which means that our entrance tickets are sold along with a dinner reservation. A food or beverage minimum of $15 applies to all tickets/reservations. If you choose not to order $15 worth of food and/or beverage, we reserve the right to charge a service fee in the amount of the difference.
Do I have to print out my tickets?
The Tin Pan is a paperless ticketing venue. Bring a valid ID with you and we will be able to check you in promptly.
How does seating work?
When you purchase a ticket online or at our box office, you are guaranteed a seat at one of our tables. When you purchase tickets, you can request a dinner reservation at a certain time with your show.

John Gorka is one of America’s preeminent singer-songwriters. Rolling Stone called him “…the leading singer/songwriter of the New Folk movement” that emerged in the early 1990s. Inspired early on by legendary folk troubadours like Stan Rogers, Eric Andersen, Tom Paxton and Claudia Schmidt, John began performing his own songs, and traveled to New York City, where Jack Hardy’s legendary Fast Folk circle (a breeding ground for many a major singer-songwriter) became a powerful source of education and encouragement.
In addition to his 11 critically acclaimed albums, John released a collector’s edition box featuring a hi-definition DVD and companion CD called “The Gypsy Life.” Windham Hill also released a collection of John’s greatest hits from the label called “Pure John Gorka.” In 2010, he also released an album with his friends and Red House label-mates Lucy Kaplansky and Eliza Gilkyson under the name Red Horse. Getting high praise from critics and fans alike, it landed on the Billboard Folk Charts and was one of the most played albums on folk radio.
Many well known artists have recorded and/or performed John Gorka songs, including Mary Chapin Carpenter, Nanci Griffith, Mary Black and Maura O’Connell. John has graced the stage of Austin City Limits, Mountain Stage, etown and has appeared on CNN.

Housed in the historic former Slovenian Workmen’s Home on Waterloo, The Treelawn sits in the heart of the vibrant Waterloo Arts & Entertainment District in Cleveland.
On the ground floor of the landmark building, you’ll find the Treelawn Social Club – an intimate space with a small stage, ‘40s-era bar with vintage mirrors, and a classic archway that divides the space. The Social Club opened its doors in February 2023 and hosts live music and comedy events 4-5 days a week.
Just a floor above, you will find Treelawn Music Hall – a large classic ballroom, freshly refurbished, that features hardwood floors, a high ceiling with original elegant detail work, the original disco ball from Euclid Beach Park, and original chandeliers. History of The Turning Point
The Turning Point was started back in May of 1976 by John McAvoy and his sister Diane. The name of the club was in dispute between John who wanted to call it “The SoHo” and Diane who wanted “Sojourners Truth” (a real mouthful). Their mother intervened and suggested finding the name of a song or album as the name. Well the name was found on one of John’s favorite performer’s album “The Turning Point ” by John Mayall. The restaurant was created in a small room seating 36 people. After dinner, local musicians would come in to play and be paid by “passing the bottle”.
In 1978 Diane and John with the help of some friends, bought the building that houses the Turning Point today. Finally moving into the new location in September of 1981 “seven trees south of our old location” . Opening on the first floor only at this time, there were always plans to get the Cafe open (in the basement). Diane left the restaurant in 1979 finally settling in Austin, Tx.. In November of 1984 we presented Eric Anderson, the first of many concerts featuring nationally prominent musicians. The next to appear was Rambin’ Jack Elliott. Slowly more and more national acts came to the Turning Point. In August of 1985 we finally got the Cafe open. The opening night performer was a little known blues guitarist from Seattle named Robert Cray. It was a great night. In 2009 we closed the upstairs and rented the space to Stefan, and the staff of Tequila Sol Lemon, It has changed our presentation a bit, but I am happy with the results. More time to spend with the family after 33 years of missed family functions.
In May of 1987 we had an early morning fire in the kitchen. Nobody was hurt but it kept the Cafe closed for four weeks and the upstairs restaurant closed for eight weeks. Twisted Tree Music & Event Hall Indoor Stage & Main Building 
Indoor Concert Venue in Jacksonville Illinois 
The Main Building at Twisted Tree is a 7,000 square foot indoor venue featuring the Twisted Tree Stage, a 384 square foot professional performance space designed for live concerts, corporate events, and private rentals. With full sound and lighting, ample room for seating or standing audiences, and modern amenities, this stage brings the excitement of live music indoors. The layout is versatile enough to host intimate shows as well as large gatherings, making it a cornerstone of entertainment in Jacksonville Illinois. 
Why Go to Events at the Twisted Tree Stage 
The Twisted Tree Stage delivers the energy of a full concert with the comfort of an indoor setting. Guests enjoy professional sound, clear sightlines, and a spacious floor plan that can adapt to seated or standing arrangements. Unlike outdoor shows that depend on weather, the Twisted Tree Stage provides year-round reliability without sacrificing atmosphere.
Artists value the professional production setup, complete with lighting, sound, and backstage space. Guests appreciate the comfort of indoor seating, climate control, and convenient access to amenities. Together, this makes every event feel polished and memorable.
The Main Building can be rented on its own for indoor events or as part of the full Twisted Tree property. This flexibility gives organizers the ability to tailor the venue to the scale and style of their event.

Velvet Elk Lounge 
Live Music • Good Booze • Private Events
Velvet Elk Lounge was born from the desire to create a small, funky, live music venue in downtown Boulder where you can get a proper cocktail and listen to great, local music.
We're also one of Boulder's premiere event spaces for private gatherings of all styles and sizes, complete with the charm and flair that only the Velvet Elk Lounge can provide. 
This space has been home to many Boulder businesses, including a Cadillac dealership, a bowling alley, a long-standing travel agency, and now a palace of music and fun. We pride ourselves on being an artist forward venue. That means we treat our artists with the same hospitality as every guest who walks through our door. 
Our Impact 
Community is at the heart of everything we do, and so is our commitment to the planet. Thanks to your continued support, we’re not only creating a space where music and hospitality thrive, but we're also working together to reduce our environmental footprint and build a more sustainable future. Every step forward is one we take together.

Looking to host something special? The Venice West is your perfect spot right in the heart of Venice Beach. Our space has everything you need - State of the art sound system, full bar, and plenty of room to make it an event to remember. We're available for all your milestone celebrations and special occasions. room information
Venue Room: 270 Capacity Standing. Seated capacity varies per layout. Room equipped with stage, sound and lighting, and projector. 
Bar Room: Total capacity around 130 with 32 seated spaces, with option to add more seating. 
VIP room: Equipped with pool table and sofa furniture, this space is perfect for smaller parties, or for private space for brides or grooms. 
Greenroom: Just built and containing a private full bath with shower, plenty of seating, and fully vanity, this additional space is versatile for your every need.
Greenroom #2: Yep, you read that right, we have two. While it doesn't have a private bath, there is plenty of room and seating, and this room is right off the main venue space. 
Kitchen: Our commercial kitchen is equipment to handle most prep and service needs, and smaller cooking requirements. If you want a full catering experience, our friends at The Melody Ballroom about us are happy to cater. We also allow for food to be brought in for private events.
Bathrooms: We have 7 stalls and 3 urinals in our completely renovated bathrooms.

Historic. Intimate. Vibrant.
Vinegar Hill Music Theatre is a place where people from near and far come together to socialize, celebrate, and connect over a shared love of music and live entertainment. Conveniently located off I-95 in Arundel, ME (close to Biddeford, Kennebunkport, Portland, and Portsmouth), our late-1800s barn, with its exposed wooden structure, exudes rustic New England charm and provides the perfect setting for the one-of-a-kind experiences we aim to foster. (Translation: our barn is really sweet, you should totally come check it out). From intimate acoustic sessions to electrifying performances, our stage is a melting pot of musical talent, nurturing emerging artists and showcasing established musicians alike. 
But we want Vinegar Hill to be more than just a place to listen to music; our goal is to make it an integral part of the local community. When you come to an event at Vinegar Hill, you’re not just attending a show; you’re investing in the power of music and the arts to uplift, inspire, and create a sense of purpose and belonging. Together, we’re building a place where everyone feels welcome, where connections are forged, and where memories are made.
So whether you’re a community member looking for a night out or a curious traveler seeking a taste of local culture, join us at Vinegar Hill—where the music is lively, the vibes are infectious, and everyone is welcome to dance to their own beat!

MEET THE VINEGAR HILL TEAM 
Emily Haranas, Managing Partner 
 emily@vinhillmusic.com 
Emily, who grew up in the high deserts of New Mexico, inherited her love of music from her parents (one of the most enduring memories of her childhood is arriving home to find her dad listening to music so loud it made the windows in their house shake). When her dad died in 2010, Emily also inherited his extensive record collection, which she and her husband Pete add to on a weekly basis. Vinegar Hill is Emily taking a chance on a dream with beloved friends in a place that she knows her dad would have loved. When she’s not working on helping Vinegar Hill grow to meet its potential, Emily spends time with her two daughters, Ella and Nia, teaches barre classes, attends as many live music performances as she can fit into her chaotic schedule, and tries to figure out how to utilize her newly-minted MA in Mindfulness. 
Peter Haranas, Partner 
Pete is Emily’s favorite assistant. When he’s not lending a helping hand, he prefers to spend his time watching the Celtics, going to sparsely attended concerts, and discussing, in great detail, matters of no consequence. 
Craig Cameron, Partner 
From cranking vinyl on a Big Bird record player while wearing footie pajamas, to receiving his first guitar as a gift, to seeing hundreds of live concerts all over the world, to performing music at venues all over New England, Craig’s love affair with music has reverberated throughout his life. He is honored to be working alongside his fiance and partner Michele (who he first met in a music venue!), and his lifelong friends Emily and Peter, as stewards of the experiences created here at Vinegar Hill Music Theatre. About the Venues
Built in 1966 to honor Atlanta’s performing arts community, Center Stage Theater is an iconic live performance venue in Midtown celebrating over five decades hosting music, comedy, wrestling, boxing, eSports, film and television recordings, and private events.
Today, the facility features three diverse venues:
Center Stage Theater, the historic 1050-capacity original theater renowned for its acoustics and intimate live show experience
The Loft, an industrial 650-capacity live music and event space
Vinyl, a 300-capacity staple in Atlanta’s local music scene
Marquee Moments at Center Stage
Originally “Theatre Atlanta”, Center Stage was built by Ms. Frania Tye Lee as a memorial to her daughter, Mrs. Helen Lee Cartledge, after she and 100 other Atlanta arts patrons perished in the Orly plane crash of 1962. The family preserves the Midtown Atlanta property as a home to performing arts in Mrs. Cartledge’s honor.
The Video Music Channel first broadcast from Center Stage in 1982.
World Championship Wrestling (WCW) filmed WCW Saturday Night at Center Stage.
ProgPower USA has taken place at Center Stage for 20+ consecutive years.
Sir Elton John recorded the live-studio album Captain & The Kid on the floor of Center Stage.
Beck surprised Atlanta at The Loft one Halloween night.
Jay-Z launched his seven-city-in-one-day “Hanger Tour” with a 7am show at Center Stage.
Jamie Foxx hosted television comedy special Laffapalooza from Center Stage.
Lady Gaga & Katy Perry each played Center Stage the same week.
Twenty One Pilots, Cold War Kids, Marc Broussard, Citizen Cope, X Ambassadors & countless others were introduced to Atlanta in Vinyl.
Kanye West, Drake, Ellie Goulding, Queens of the Stone Age, Dan & Shay, Khalid, The Weeknd, 21 Savage, The Strokes, Young Thug, J Cole, H.E.R and Shawn Mendez – all launched meteoric careers from Center Stage – The Loft – Vinyl.

Frequently Asked Questions
For the safety of our guests and performers, all ticket holders are subject to magnetic and/or physical search. Any prohibited items brought on to the premises are subject to confiscation.
Firearms and weapons are expressly prohibited on Center Stage property.
Where is Center Stage Atlanta located?
Center Stage, The Loft & Vinyl are located at 
1374 West Peachtree Street, Atlanta GA 30309
Intersection: 17th Street & West Peachtree Street in Midtown
MARTA: Arts Center (walk 0.2 miles north on West Peachtree)
More About Directions and Parking
What is your bag policy?
NO BACKPACKS. You may bring small bags under 12 inches and they will be searched.
How old do I need to be to attend a show?
Unless otherwise stated, all shows welcome all ages to attend.
I am bringing my child to a show. Do we both need tickets? Or is there a place for me to wait?
A ticket is required for each person to enter the building. There are no common/waiting areas within the building. Parents may consider Atlantic Station, the Canopy Hotel or a nearby restaurant to wait for their kids.
I am nervous about leaving my children alone. Is it safe?
Our management, show staff, security and APD officers are here to provide a safe event environment for all attendees. We encourage parents to attend a concert with their children and experience live music together. We expect that you’ll be confident in the experience and gain trust for a solo show in the future.
Where can I purchase tickets?
All tickets can be purchased online at www.centerstage-atlanta.com
Tickets may also be purchased in person at the venue’s box office, Monday – Friday, 10am – 5pm or upon entry to the show if tickets still remain.
We are a CASHLESS venue so our box office ONLY accepts major credit/debit cards. Phone orders are not available from the box office.

About 
The Wake Forest Listening Room was started in 2017 as a series of performances around town featuring local and regional original musicians. The main intent of the WFLR is to give artists a “sacred space” where their music could be focused on with no distractions like sports on TVs, conversations, etc. It also provides the same for audience members.
The series found a permanent home downstairs from Sugar Magnolia Café, where we hosted more than 300 performances before COVID shut us down in late 2021.

In October 2023, we’re reopening in the new space next door to our independent Wake Forest bookstore, Page 158 Books. We’ll have more seating, new sound and light systems, delicious food and drinks, and we’ll keep that same “listening room” vibe that guests and musicians have come to love and appreciate!
All our shows and ticket links can be found on this site or at eTix.com. Join us for a show and enjoy a great evening of live, original music from some of the best local, regional, national and international artists!

It’s what the name implies. It’s a safe space for musicians and audience members, where nobody has to compete with conversations, sports on TVs or loud drunken people (we hope…) It’s a place where you can sit and “be in the music.” (However, we highly encourage clapping, whistling and yelling your support between songs!)
 Where do your listening room shows actually happen?
All Wake Forest Listening Room shows happen in the space next to Page 158 Books in downtown Wake Forest. The physical address is 415-C Brooks Street, Wake Forest, NC 27587.

About The Waldo
In the coastal village of Waldoboro, on Main Street, you’ll find The Waldo Theatre, the heart of arts and entertainment on Maine’s Midcoast. Year-round, we offer live music, theatre, movies, stand-up comedy, and education programs for the whole region. Our stage hosts everything from homegrown productions to nationally and internationally known artists.
​
The theatre itself is something special. Built in 1936 and fully restored, it holds 290 seats in an intimate Art Deco hall known for its acoustics. Performers love this room, and audiences can feel why. With modern lighting, projection, and ventilation, plus the ability to live-stream events, The Waldo connects the Midcoast to the world and brings the world to the Midcoast.
​
None of it happens without our volunteers. They donate their time, talents, and skills to make every show possible, and their passion keeps The Waldo thriving as we celebrate our 90th year.
​
Want to know how this theatre was built, lost, and saved by its neighbors? Read our story here.

FOUNDED IN 1802,
Lawrenceburg has supplied whiskey to the world for over two centuries. For this we are known as “Whiskey City USA”. Our distilleries were founded as early as 1809, and by 1855 Lawrenceburg was home to an abundance of distilleries tapping limestone-filtered water from the aquifer below. Ross & Squibb Distillery continues our legacy of world-class whiskeys from their distillery originally established in 1847 as Rossville Union Distillery.
BUILT IN 1893,
The Liberty Theater was originally a German music hall called Liedertafel meaning “song table”. Converted for movies in 1921 and later named The Liberty during WWII, she has hosted performances and celebrations for over 130 years.
RESTORED IN 2023,
legendary music promoter Dan McCabe has revived the long dormant Liberty Theater as a versatile showcasing venue dedicated to artist development and the celebration of all things “Whiskey City”. Dan has 30+ years in live music industry beginning with his 9-year, 7 nights a week programming of iconic Rock-n-Roll laundromat Sudsy Malones. Dan presented shows at the historic Southgate House, served as Executive Producer of Cincinnati’s MidPoint Music Festival and was a founder and operator of downtown Cincinnati music venues MOTR and the historic Woodward Theater.

What We Do
The Wild Rose Moon Performing Arts Center is a nonprofit listening room, production house, and educational center serving Northern Indiana and lower Michigan. “The Moon” hosts nationally touring musical acts in an intimate environment, and produces The Wild Rose Moon Radio Hour, a monthly program that airs on 88.1 WVPE Public Radio, and WNIT Public Television. Interns and volunteer learn production skills related to event promotion, media production, live performance, and stagecraft, and go on to populate the next generation of skilled producers. The Wild Rose Moon is funded primarily through member contributions, community grants, and ticket sales.

WHAT IS THE WOODSHOP ?
The Woodshop Listening Room is an intimate ticketed concert venue and dinner-and-a-show room in St. Elmo, Chattanooga, operated by Jhett Black and Callie Sioux. With great sound, close-up performances, BBQ, cocktails, and weekly roots programming, The Woodshop is built around a full night out—come for dinner, settle in for the show, and experience live music the way it’s meant to be: up close and personal.
Since opening as a bar in the midst of 2020’s shuttering, we’ve developed into a venue-style neighborhood bar that curates a homelike feeling for anyone who walks inside. Live music is in our DNA, but we’re also known for our cozy seasonal food and craft drinks menu. Between finger-licking BBQ and arguably the best local craft beer and spirits we can find, what we’ve created is a surprisingly wide selection of goods that keeps your heart warm and happy. ZANZABAR LOUISVILLE 
It’s rare to find a bar that your grandpa partied in; rarer still to know that it has only gotten better in the ensuing decades. Such is the case though with Zanzabar, which opened its doors originally in 1938 and has continued to attract and entertain local residents ever since.
Zanzabar has established a local reputation as one of Louisville’s oldest neighborhood gems and newest casual cuisine and live entertainment destinations. Zanzabar offers up Louisville’s only vintage arcade and hosts weekly pinball tournaments. Sunday trivia nights are wildly entertaining, and Zbar also serves as an award-winning, intimate live-music venue for both local and popular national and international touring acts, which grace the stage many nights every single week.
Zanzabar is a perfect destination for a top-notch menu, a large heated patio, and Wi-Fi inside and out! There is a reason that only the great things survive, and Zanzabar is living proof of how it’s done. 
Continue the tradition and make your grandpa proud.

About: Zenbarn
A Stage Artist's Seek Out
Founded in 2016 by Noah, Ari, and Marlena Fishman, Zenbarn was created to bring neighbors together. From Guptil Farm dairy barn, to Golden Horn East, to Tanglewoods Restaurant & Bar, this building has long served the community.
What began as a local experiment has grown into a respected stop on national touring routes
Known for high-quality sound, exceptional hospitality, and a crowd that genuinely listens and appreciates music, Zenbarn offers something increasingly rare: an intimate space where connection matters.
Our stage has welcomed legendary artists across generations and genres — from folk icons Peter Rowan and Ramblin’ Jack Elliott, to members of Wu-Tang Clan, to reggae legends like Black Uhuru.
Members of Phish, Twiddle, and other iconic Vermont bands frequent the venue and sit in regularly, creating spontaneous moments that only happen in rooms built on musical trust and community.
Zenbarn carries that legacy forward — now as one of the most dynamic small live music venues in the country and a premier Vermont barn wedding destination.
The “Zen” in Zenbarn reflects our belief in music, mindfulness, and meaningful connection.
A Family of Vermont Businesses
The Fishmans are community-based entrepreneurs at heart with a group of interrelated Vermont businesses.
In addition to Zenbarn, the family operates:
Vermont Patients Alliance — a vertically integrated cannabis company
Zenbarn Farms — a retail dispensary in Waterbury
Stowe’s Remarkable Things (formerly Stowe Craft Gallery), owned by Susan and Stephen Fishman in the heart of Stowe
Across all ventures, a common thread runs strong:
Quality. Community connection. Sustainability. Vermont pride.
