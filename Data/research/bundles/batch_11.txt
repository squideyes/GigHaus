Welcome Home
Since 1976 the singular objective has been to maintain a welcoming environment. A place where you can walk in, take a deep breath, slow down just a little, relax and let us help you feel at home. Smell the homemade bread, the roast, the brisket on the smoker, the sap boiling, the warmth of the fire. Take in the music, the structure, the gardens, the bird feeders. Founder Don Staber stated it best when he carved into the mantle “A Friendly Place by the Side of the Road”.
That’s it. That’s our mission. 
 Our Barn
Real People
Real Food
Real Good
…we promise, the only bull is on our plates.
We offer inviting food based on classic preparations, featuring locally sourced 
meats ‘n produce from Upstate New York.

Rustic Acoustics
When James Cavert built this barn in 1852, he surely wasn’t thinking about acoustics, but he did a pretty damn good job… and with 15+ years of audio reinforcement from Aaron Kovacik and Dream Lab Productions, we challenge you to find a better sounding space.
Throughout the year, we host a wide variety of world-class musicians, both inside the restaurant and outside on our expansive patio.

Korby Lenker grew up in Idaho on the rim of the Snake River Canyon and has spent two decades as a touring folksinger, routinely logging 150 shows a year. The live set has always been the center of it.
His eighth album, Man in the Maroon, landed on American Songwriter's Best Albums of the Year. He's a first-place winner at the Rocky Mountain Folks Festival, the Chris Austin Songwriting Contest at MerleFest, and the International Songwriting Competition, and has played Telluride Bluegrass Festival, Bumbershoot, and Sasquatch.
"He doesn't so much play the guitar as become part of it." — No Depression
Korby lives in East Nashville with his wife and daughter.

About THE\CUT
Opened in 2024, The Cut has established itself as the North Shore's premier destination for live entertainment and fast casual fare. You can also rent our award winning room for corporate events, weddings and private functions.
The Cut is located in the heart of downtown Gloucester, surrounded by the City’s galleries, museums, shops, restaurants, and hotels. 
Our venue offers state of the art sound and lighting, great sight lines of the stage and two bars overlooking the Gloucester Harbor. Featuring local talent as well as nationally recognized musical acts and comedians.
Our restaurant partner Bravo offers fast casual fare within a fun and lively atmosphere featuring a large center bar, 9 large screen tvs, pool tables and video and bar games. 
Come see for yourself why Yankee Magazine named us 2025 Editors' choice for best live music venue in New England!

 All Things Lounge
 How Does Seating Work?
Seating varies depending on the show. For most events, The Lounge is set up with a mix of tables for 4, 6, and 8 guests, available on a first-come, first-served basis.
For jam bands and other high-energy performances where we expect dancing, seating may be limited to allow for additional standing room.
For listening room shows, folding chairs are arranged in rows directly in front of the stage, with tables positioned along the sides of the room.
 Do You Serve Food?
Doors open at 6PM and we start serving our nighttime menu at that time. The kitchen closes at 8:30PM. Food, drinks, and desserts can be ordered directly at the bar. Please note that we do not offer table service, so our staff will not come to your table to take orders.
 What Drinks Do You Offer?
We offer a great selection of beer, wine, and cocktails.
We're a music venue first and a bar second, so our drink menu is intentionally straightforward. If you're looking for elaborate martinis, Manhattans, or mixologist-level creations, we may not be the spot—but we'll make sure you have something delicious in hand while you enjoy the show.
 What Time Should I Arrive?
Doors open at 6:00 PM, and music typically begins after 7:00 PM.
If you'd like a table or one of the best seats in the house, we recommend arriving right when the doors open. Since all seating is first come, first served, popular and sold-out shows often draw a line before opening. Guests frequently begin lining up around 5:30 PM for sold-out performances.
Please note that doors will not open before 6:00 PM.
 Is The Lounge Accessible?
Yes. We have a ramp leading to our front entrance that can be opened upon request. The entrance typically used for evening shows has three steps, so if you require ramp access, please let a staff member know when you arrive.

 It's More Than Just a Coffee House
Nearly 20 years ago, Kathy had a simple dream: to create a place where community came first. She wanted a place where it didn’t matter if you could tell the difference between a macchiato and a cortado, or if you arrived in a shiny new car or walked in with the wind at your back. Here, everyone’s welcome—we serve the salty natives and washashores alike.
What started as a small dream in 2006 quickly became something far bigger than Kathy and her family could have ever imagined. The Daily Brew has transformed into a cherished local hub, where neighbors become friends and friends become family. It’s a place where we know your name, not just your order. Whether it’s your first visit or your hundredth, you’ll always find the same thing waiting: a seat, a smile, and the sense that you’re exactly where you belong.
The ocean’s just down the road, but here, it’s the smell of fresh bacon and brewing coffee that draws you in. It’s a place where the chatter of laughter and the hum of conversation make every visit feel like a warm embrace. The Daily Brew isn’t just a spot to grab a cup—it’s where you come to find your people.
Looking back on nearly two decades, we’re overwhelmed by the love this community has shown. No matter how long the lines get or how much the world changes around us, the mantra will always remain the same:
Food. Coffee. Community.

The idea for Daryl’s House, the Rock and Roll Hall of Famer’s restaurant and live music club was born in a light-bulb moment when Daryl Hall first conceived his Webby Award-winning Live from Daryl’s House, which bowed on the internet in late 2007 and maintains its status as a groundbreaking series. Since its opening in 2014, Daryl has used the Club to host episodes of the show.
“Why not just play music at my house and let people see what musicians are really like, when they are relaxed and among friends” says Hall about his brainchild. “The restaurant and club is the perfect venue where artists, band and I can hang out, drink some wine, have a good time and make some great music”. And the audience can tune in to Live From Daryl’s House and join us!
Daryl’s hand can be seen everywhere in the Club. “I’ve designed everything, from the tables and chairs to the walls and ceiling. It’s an 18th century barn style…an early American, New England kind of vibe”. The eclectic restaurant menu features a variety of award-winning American cuisine, like steaks, seafood, pasta and sandwiches reflecting his Philly/East Coast roots. Daryl’s House Club hosts both standing and seated shows with free music at weekend brunches and, during the summer, on the back porch.
Boasting a world class sound system and intimate atmosphere Daryl’s House Club builds on the Live From Daryl’s House inspiration and has become a favorite destination for top artists and music fans looking for great food and an up-close-and-personal music experience.
Daryl says, “Welcome to my House!”
Top Notch Entertainment
Our venue shines with top-tier entertainment, with a varied mix of the best local, regional, and nationally touring bands. Our live sound experience is unmatched, drawing performers who love our electric energy and the buzzing excitement that fills the air every night.

The History
We opened our doors in 2016, but Dee’s is a good old fashioned venue that feels like it’s been there for decades. The decor is reminiscent of bars in the ’70s. Just good old fashioned dives where you could hear classic country tunes on the jukebox, see amazing bands, and folks are happy to chat. We serve up classic cocktails, house-made signature cocktails, cold beer, tasty snacks, and live music. You can play a game of darts or pool, sit outside by the fire-pit, hang on the front porch or chill inside while listening to one of the many talented bands.
Our faithful and die-hard regulars make Dee’s the happy place we call our second home, and lifelong friendships are forged here. We fly under-the-radar and cater to “our hood.” It’s that spirit and commitment that people recognize and we were named one of the top 10 music venues in Nashville by NPR. We have been featured in Esquire and Rolling Stone Magazine and are known for an adventurous booking policy that embraces talent from across the globe — as well as local acts from down the block.
We can’t wait for you to drop on by — Amy Dee

Historic Restaurant & Tavern
Come hang out with us.
Ojai’s historic restaurant & tavern.
Over the years, people have been called to Ojai for a variety of reasons. Some came seeking peace or prosperity, soaking in the mineral rich natural hot springs or mining for gold in the hills. Others came to Ojai in search of a mystical utopia, through its association with the Theosophical society, its native Chumash lore or inspired by its depiction as the fabled Shangri-La in the film Lost Horizon.
As the oldest restaurant and tavern in Ojai, the Deer Lodge has long served as a meeting place where weary adventurers from all walks of life come together to fill their bellies and lift their spirits. Today, we are proud to be the newest caretakers of this home away from home for both locals and travelers alike. And even though times have changed, the spirit has stayed the same.
There’s a little somethin’ for everyone here at the Deer Lodge. In keeping with our backwoods heritage, we offer classic Californian cuisine, along with a wide selection of craft beers and spirits from up and down the West Coast. We have vegetarian and vegan friendly options, as well as a large dog-friendly patio and a game room with a pool table and lounge area. With live music and smokey barbecue on the weekends, this is a place where you can bring the whole family during the day, or dance with your friends into the night. This is where memories are made, where the blacktop ends and paradise begins… this is where time stands still.

The History of Duling Hall
Duling Hall, built in 1927, is listed in the National Register of Historic Places. The venue is located within what was the Lorena Duling School (Go Bears!)—an institution that served as a center of the Fondren community for much of the 20th century. The building’s architecture is an intact example of Tudor revival in educational institutions.
Read More About the History of Duling Hall
Duling School
The Lorena Duling School, located at 622 Duling Avenue in Jackson, Mississippi, was designed by Jackson architect Claude H. Lindsley and constructed in 1927. The building was commissioned by the Jackson Public School district as the neighborhood elementary school for the developing community of Fondren.
Located at the geographic center of Fondren, the school was one of the neighborhood’s earliest and most prominent public institutions. Once completed, the school fostered the continued development of the neighborhood. The history of the building from its inception in the 1920s through its expansions in the 1930s and 1940s mirrors the patterns of development of the surrounding community. The building played a prominent role in the community where generations of Fondren’s residents were educated.
Architecture
The school is an important as a representative work of significant local Jackson architect, Claude H. Lindsley, and as an intact example of the application of the Tudor Revival style to a school building. The Tudor Revival style found favorability between 1890 and 1940. Evoking the virtues of learning and permanence, the style was one of the most popular architectural styles for educational buildings built during the 1920s and 1930s.

Where Live Music Matters
Since 1991, Eddie’s Attic has been a popular and intimate live music venue for aspiring and accomplished performing songwriters. Located in Decatur, Georgia, a small artist-friendly town within the metro Atlanta area, the Attic is a true listening room with a superior sound system and acoustics, where customers are asked to silence their phones and actually listen to the songs. Eddie’s Attic draws both fans and artists from all over the world, and continues to be one of the premier music venues in the nation.
And it has been that way from the very beginning.
What started as a bare second-floor space just off Decatur Square became something nobody quite had a name for at the time. A place where the music wasn’t background noise, where the artist on stage deserved your full attention, and where the audience showed up because they genuinely wanted to hear something. Over thirty years and thousands of shows later, that founding idea remains unchanged.
This stage has been an early home to artists who went on to fill arenas, win Grammys, and change the landscape of American music. It has also been the place where countless songwriters you may not have heard of yet played their most honest set to a quiet, full room of people who were really listening. Both things matter equally here.
Our Bi-Annual Open Mic Shootout, now in its 58th edition, has been running for over two decades and remains one of the most beloved traditions in Atlanta’s music community. Each week, new voices earn their shot at the stage. Each Shootout, the room fills with industry professionals, devoted fans, and fellow artists who understand that the next great songwriter could be standing right in front of them.
We do 600 to 700 shows a year. The room holds 177 people. The stage is small, the sound system is exceptional, and the distance between you and the artist performing is measured in feet. That intimacy is not incidental. It is the whole point.

How do I buy tickets for a show?
Advance tickets for all shows can be purchased online at www.eddiesattic.com. When purchasing online, please read the description on your ticket before purchasing.
For day of show tickets, you can come into the venue as early as 5pm to purchase a ticket through our door person. PLEASE NOTE: online prices and tickets purchased in person are exactly the same. THERE IS NO FEE DIFFERENCE.
Is the venue wheelchair accessible?
Because our building is historic, we do not have elevators. The venue is only accessible via the stairs.
I bought tickets, but now I can’t find them.
You won’t need any sort of physical ticket to get into a show – we’ll just look you up by your last name at the door. If you’re concerned about whether you have tickets, you’re welcome to ask us to confirm at info@eddiesattic.com
If I purchased general admission tickets, does that mean there will be seating for me?
General admission tickets DO NOT guarantee seating. It is on a first come first serve basis. If you’ve bought general admission tickets, we recommend getting to the venue as close to 5:00PM (4PM on Sundays) as possible because that is when ticket check-in begins and therefore is your best bet for grabbing seats.
I’m under 21. Can I come to a show?
Yes! Our shows are all ages unless otherwise noted.
If I purchase a table, do I also need to purchase tickets?
No. When you pay to reserve a table, it includes 4 tickets for the 4 seats at the table. If you have more than 4 people in your group, you will need to reserve additional tables or general admission tickets.

Elk Creek Cafe + Aleworks – Since 2007
It all began in 2007 with a vision Tim Bowser had for a small town, community gathering place – a brewpub/ bistro/ music venue that was truly focussed on authentic, local, and flavorful. Since then, Elk Creek Cafe + Aleworks has built a unique identity crafted around honestly community, local food, brilliantly designed beers, an extraordinary lineup of local + touring musicians, and thousands of engaging conversations.

Frequently Asked Questions: 
Getting Here and Parking
How do I get to the Fallser Club? Is parking a nightmare?
Good news! Getting to East Falls is actually pretty easy. The East Falls Train Station is just a short walk from our door, SEPTA bus routes K and 61 drop off closeby, and if you’re driving, there’s plenty of free street parking by the station entrance at 3610 Midvale Ave and in the train station parking lot. Need more options? There’s a paid municipal lot three blocks away at 4100 Ridge Ave. Both Midvale and Ridge Ave are well-lit, so you’re all set even for evening shows!
Note: Parking immediately outside The Fallser Club on Midvale is only 2-hour parking.
Food & Drinks
What’s the food situation?
We’ve got light snacks and refreshments available at the Fallser Club, including free popcorn at most shows (because popcorn makes everything better). But here’s the real deal: we’ve partnered with our favorite neighborhood spot, Taqueria Cresta, which is less than a block away. Show your Fallser Club ticket and score 10% off your meal. Tacos before the show? Yes, please!
Tickets
Should I buy tickets online or can I just show up?
Both work! Online ticket sales close 2 hours before showtime, but we usually have tickets available at the door. That said, buying in advance is a win-win: you’ll save a few bucks with our advance ticket discount, and it helps us and the artists plan ahead for capacity and staffing to make sure the show goes off without a hitch.
Help! I lost my ticket confirmation email. Am I doomed?
Not at all, no need to panic! We keep a guest list of everyone who purchased tickets, so just give us your name when you arrive and we’ll get you checked in. Easy peasy.

FAQ 
 Where do I park?
Limited street parking is available on Summit Ave in front of the venue. After 5pm, guests are welcome to use the museum parking lot directly across the street. For overflow parking, we recommend the parking deck on Davie St across from Center City Park.
 Are you all ages?
We are an all ages club with some exceptions. The best way to find out if a show has age restrictions is on Etix. All guests under 21 will have their hands marked at the door. A $5 SURCHARGE WILL BE ADDED AT THE DOOR FOR ALL GUESTS UNDER 21 (EVEN IF TICKETS ARE PURCHASED IN ADVANCE) Guests 16 and under must always be accompanied by an adult.
 Do you serve food?
We do not serve food, but have delicious options within walking distance.

Music Classes 
Our Mission
The Friendly Music School (501c3) is a musical community that strives to inspire, bring together, and develop musicians of all ages and levels through our unique instructional approach. Our beginner, intermediate and advanced classes are led by professional musicians who guide students at their own pace with attention to individual musical interests. We are a community based organization that is all inclusive, and we strive to make music affordable to all, as well as financially rewarding to our mentors. We provide an environment for all musicians to learn from one another, play together and develop their skills. We encourage our musical family to consider the Friendly Music School a home where everyone is supported and welcome. We especially encourage our youth to choose music and the arts as a recreational outlet.The Friendly Music School charges less for lessons than any Chicagoland independent school while paying our instructors more than any school in the area.

Guitars4Vets is a nonprofit founded in 2007 that provides free guitar lessons and a free guitar to veterans living with PTSD and other service-related trauma. The program started in Milwaukee after a guitar instructor and a Vietnam-era Marine veteran discovered music helped with healing, and it has grown to more than 110 chapters in over 40 states, plus remote options.  Gold Diggers is like if Sound City studios had a pub where you could drink with Stevie Nicks and then wander upstairs to a hotel to hear the band’s new demos and myriad couples’ spats down the hall.
 Today, Gold Diggers’ slogan sums up the synergy the partners hope to cultivate: “Drink. Sleep. Record.” Artists can stay at the hotel for a few weeks, roll out of bed and into the studio and, if the mood takes them, even do a live show at the 150-capacity club.
 It’s an unparalleled opportunity to establish a creative campus where music artists and recording industry professionals can cross paths, share ideas, have a drink, sleep it off, and ultimately bring new music into the world.
 The Gold-Diggers project is an architectural labor of love, preserved with an eye towards its unique history, but also providing the amenities associated with a top-flight studio and luxury hotel.

Serving up state of the art sound design and showcase-quality performances for an intimate audience of 175. An ever-evolving lineup of fresh talent programmed by music heads for music heads. Discover a new artist catching the industry’s attention or be wowed by an old favorite.
Explore our scheduled events so you don’t miss the party you’ve been needing.
