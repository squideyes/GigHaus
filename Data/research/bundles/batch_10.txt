History of Building 
The building at 123 Main Street in Panhandle, Texas was constructed around 1899 by F.H. Hill as a mercantile store. 
Over the years, it has served as a grocery market, hardware store and bus stop. The building sat empty for over 53 years.
Doyle and Brenda Robinson purchased the building in December of 2019. Renovations began in early 2020 and continued through September 2021 when it opened a new chapter of its life as Buffalo Grass Music Hall with its first concert. Since that first concert, we have hosted over 65 concerts as of October, 2024. The building has been used for many events such as birthday parties, retirement parties, business meetings, graduation parties, school banquets and graduation dances, baby showers and reunions.

The Artist's Walkway at Bull Run
The Artist's Walkway at Bull Run is managed by Gallery Sitka.
Gallery Sitka's mission is to support the arts, to be part of the creative economy and provide a space for emerging artists to be seen and to have the experience of showing their work in a gallery-style atmosphere.
A woman-owned and operated New England art gallery, Gallery Sitka® is located in Shirley, MA & also in Newport, RI. The gallery has been doing pop-ups all over the Northeast U.S. since 2014, representing the work of 20+ painters, sculptors, encaustic artists, and a few jewelry designers from New England, New York, Brazil, France & Italy.

About the Bur Oak
The Bur Oak is an independently run event venue in the heart of the Atwood neighborhood. To bring new life to the historic century-old building, the Bur Oak team extensively renovated and restored the space, exposing the barrel roof, brick walls, and hand-laid tile. We used reclaimed wood specific to the era and added a Viennese-style stage to the great hall. The bar top is hand-poured concrete and finished with a hard lacquer to capture an aged look.
The remodel is new, but the venue feels lived in and cozy, with a current capacity of 130 people. We delight in hosting private events, and the addition of the lower level gathering hall and suite makes the Bur Oak an ideal turnkey venue for any wedding and reception.
Whether you're gathering to enjoy entertainment or to celebrate an unforgettable time of your life, the collective dream of this space is to bring all walks of life together for great conversation. We celebrate your freedom to express your true self, to make memories that will last a lifetime, and most importantly, we hope to make you feel “at home.”

Who We Are
Our Mission
Our mission is to create an intimate, high-quality live music experience that brings artists and audiences closer together. We believe great music deserves a great room — one with exceptional sound, thoughtful lighting, warm hospitality, and an audience that truly listens.
Rooted in a community that values creativity and connection, we aim to be a year-round home for artists and music lovers alike. Our size is intentional: it allows for powerful, up-close performances and meaningful moments that larger venues simply can’t offer. What we may lack in scale, we make up for in atmosphere, care, and character.
We’re committed to thoughtful curation — blending emerging voices with established artists, introducing new genres while honoring familiar favorites, and creating a welcoming space for both longtime locals and new audiences. By fostering strong partnerships, supporting artists fairly, and cultivating a vibrant, inclusive vibe, we strive to be more than a venue — we aim to be a place people feel connected to, inspired by, and excited to return to.
ButtonBall Barn is small, loud (in a good way), and full of people who actually came to see music — not just drink near it. The sound is dialed in, the crowd is tuned in, and the vibe is somewhere between “secret show” and “your favorite room you wish more people knew about.”

About Us
The Buttonwood Tree’s mission is to uplift, inspire, educate, and enrich lives through artistic and cultural programming. At the same time, we support the creative endeavors of both emerging and established artists.
Based at 9 Pleasant St, Middletown, CT, The Buttonwood Tree is located in the city’s central business district. As a result, we actively help energize the surrounding community through the arts. TBT offers music, art, literature, poetry, and dance programs. In addition, we present contests, classes, online events, open mic sessions, and many partnership presentations.
Moreover, we curate projects from outside the organization and rent space for rehearsals and events. If you would like to get involved in any way, please get in touch with us. Above all, we strive to remain accessible, inclusive, and welcoming to all.

Anne-Marie Cannata McEwen
Board of Directors
Co-President
Anne-Marie Cannata McEwen moved to Middletown, CT in 1993 with her young family, in roles as soccer mom and dental hygienist. With a deep desire to serve, she developed a connection to the local arts community. Her early involvement in community-based arts shaped her commitment to supporting artists and cultural programming.
She became involved with The Buttonwood Tree in 2007 joining the board and was appointed Executive Director in 2008. In 2023, after a change in Buttonwood leadership, she resigned and focused on her family. During her tenure, she helped guide the organization into a vibrant, inclusive performing arts center. 
Edward Langford
Board of Directors
Edward (Eddie) Langford is thrilled to be a part of The Buttonwood Tree Performing Arts Center family. He is originally from Rhode Island, but has lived in Middletown since 2006.
Eddie is a Licensed Professional Counselor and Behavioral Clinician with an office downtown and has worked in the Mental Health and Human Services fields for more than 35 years, with a strong interest in mindfulness and music. In 2021, He completed the inaugural Mindfulness Facilitator Certification Program at the Copper Beech Institute in West Hartford.

Ownership
​
 The principal owners of Cadenza are Alan & Sue Mooney. Alan & Sue are big fans of live music and theatre. In addition to owning Cadenza, they are strong supporters of Maine State Music Theatre. Sue has served on the Board of Trustees and is part the MSMT Angel volunteers. Sue and Alan often sponsor MSMT events. They also own 5 Depot Street, the building that houses Cadenza.
​
 Steve Poulos is also an owner and supporter of Cadenza. Steve is enthusiastic about live performances and frequently attends Broadway shows and Jazz events in New York. Steve is also a big supporter of Maine State Music Theatre and is currently on the MSMT Board.

About Cafe Eleven
 Saint Augustine Beach
About Us
The very best locally owned and operated St Augustine Beach Restaurant!
Cafe Eleven continues to contribute to St Augustine Beach with the best in restaurant offerings like a vegetarian menu that will satisfy the inner vegan in you, great specialty espresso coffee drinks, FREE wireless internet for patrons, the best craft beer happy hour everyday between 3pm to 7pm and as always, the best all you can eat brunch buffet. Offering an exceptional dinner menu that is light, flavorful & satisfying. In addition to a deliciously diverse dinner menu, that might surprise you, we regularly feature new and exciting dinner specials, so come in often to experience the best of what the beach has to offer!
The Cafe Eleven Story…
The story begins around 2001 in a dirty, broken-down convenience store that is transformed over the course of a year into a full-scale modern decor restaurant and world-class music venue. We specialize in innovative vegetarian meals as well as meaty Southern comfort dishes and have an adventurous selection of craft beers on tap.
On top of that, we are located just across the street from the beach. The combination of locale and cuisine attracts tourists for weekend brunch, hipsters for the music, retirees for the pork barbecue, and surfers for a daily dose of a buffalo chicken panini. Open for breakfast , brunch on Saturday and Sunday, lunch and dinner 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily, later if there is a show.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
 What is Cafe Wha (The Club) Vs Cafe Wha Entertainment?
Cafe Wha? is the iconic club founded in 1959 by Manny Roth began as a Greenwich Village coffee house. It has a rich history of hosting legendary talents like Little Richard, Joan Rivers, Bob Dylan, Jimi Hendrix, and Bruce Springsteen. Today, Cafe Wha? thrives as a landmark in NYC’s music scene, hosting a variety of artists.
Cafe Wha Entertainment is The Club’s sister company offering a diverse range of services, including live bands, DJs, weddings, corporate, and immersive entertainment experiences. Collaborating with clients, Cafe Wha Entertainment strives to customize performances and emerge as a premier choice for events and music.
 What types of events do you typically cater to?
Cafe Wha Entertainment has experience in a wide range of events, including weddings, corporate gatherings, private parties, and more. Our versatile musicians can adapt to the unique atmosphere and requirements of your specific event.
 Do you take musical requests?
Absolutely! At Cafe Wha Entertainment, we tailor each party to your preferences. We’re more than happy to fulfill musical requests in advance, and if there’s a particular song we don’t know, we’ll learn it just for your event!
 What do the musicians wear?
Our male musicians don traditional black tie attire, while female musicians and vocalists grace the stage in elegant long black evening wear. For afternoon affairs, you can even request short cocktail dresses. Alternatively, clients have the option to choose non-traditional attire for the band, allowing you to customize the visual experience.
 Do you provide music for wedding ceremonies or cocktail receptions?
Absolutely, we’ve got you covered! Our talented musicians can play classical music for wedding ceremonies and set the perfect tone with background jazz during cocktail hours. Additionally, we offer an array of classical string and woodwind ensembles, including violins, saxophones, and more, to enhance your special moments.

Hailing from the backroads of central Pennsylvania, Jason McIntyre’s roots run deep, but his music has taken him far and wide. From the mountains of Colorado to his current home in Austin, Texas,

Their music, enriched by contributions from friends across the country, showcases McIntyre’s ability to connect with fellow musicians and craft compelling narratives through song.
Currently based in Austin, McIntyre continues to write and perform alongside his friend and collaborator Michael O’Connor. Our Story
Since 1899 the Church at Waring has been a special gathering place in the Texas Hill Country. Today, it is a one-of-a-kind listening room that welcomes premier songwriters and musicians from all over the world.
Visit us for an intimate experience sharing world-class music, and unforgettable special private and public events.
Music lovers from down the road in Comfort, Fredericksburg, Boerne, and Kerrville to San Antonio, Austin and beyond are discovering this special place we are honored to share with y’all.
Hope to see you at Church real soon.
Jay Boy Adams
A LITTLE HISTORY
According to author Richard Zelade, 
"The Waring Baptist Church was originally a community church where all denominations worshipped on alternating Sundays. It was then abandoned, except for occasional community functions, until about World War II, when the Baptists took it over... The present wooden frame sanctuary is the second church building, built around the turn of the century, no one really knows exactly when."

Lone Star Travel Guide to Texas Hill Country 
Anglo pioneers in a predominantly German-settled area built the west wing of the school building in 1891. Land for this first public school was given by Robert Percival Maxwell Waring, a native of Ireland for whom the town had been named in 1888. Citizens volunteered labor, funds, and materials for the building, and paid their children's tuition of $1 to $1.50 per month. The east wing was added in 1903, and exterior braces were applied in the 1930s. The school closed in 1954.
