 F.A.Q.
TICKETING
 Where can I buy tickets to shows at the Iron Horse?
Tickets can be purchased online, by phone, in person at the Iron Horse box office, or at the door when available.
The box office is located at 18 Center St. in Northampton and is open 2:30–4:00pm on Iron Horse show days. Phone: (413) 200-0425
 Can I buy tickets at the door?
Yes, if the show is not sold out. We recommend buying in advance for popular shows.
 Are tickets digital or physical? 
Tickets are digital. Show them on your phone or print them before you arrive. If you can't access your tickets, bring a valid photo ID and our staff can look up your order by name.
 I did not receive my tickets. What should I do? 
Search your email for the show name, "The Parlor Room," "Iron Horse," or "PatronManager," and check your spam or promotions folder. If you still can't find them, contact us with the name on the order, the show date, and the email address used at purchase.
 I am having trouble logging in to the Membership Portal. What should I do?
Contact memberships@ironhorse.org or call (413) 200-0425.
 Why do ticket fees appear at checkout?
Ticketing fees cover processing and ticketing system costs. We aim to show the full price before you complete your purchase. If something is unclear, please contact us.
REFUNDS, EXCHANGES, AND CHANGES
 What is your refund policy? 
Tickets are generally nonrefundable unless a show is canceled or rescheduled.
If a show is rescheduled, your tickets will be honored for the new date. If you can't attend, you may request a refund within 14 days of the announcement. If a show is canceled, refunds will be processed automatically.
Please note: Supporting acts and openers are always subject to change.
 Can I exchange my tickets for a different show? 
In most cases, no. If you have a specific situation, contact us and we'll let you know what's possible.
 Can I transfer my tickets to someone else?
We don't facilitate ticket transfers, but you can transfer tickets by forwarding the original confirmation email – including the QR code – to the recipient.
We strongly recommend purchasing only through our official website. Tickets from resellers or third-party sources cannot be guaranteed as valid.
 What happens if a show sells out?
Additional tickets may not be available. If more are released, they'll appear on the event page. Purchasing through our official website or box office is the safest way to avoid invalid tickets or scams.
 I am having trouble logging in to the Membership Portal. What should I do?
Contact memberships@ironhorse.org or call (413) 200-0425.
 Why do ticket fees appear at checkout?
Ticketing fees cover processing and ticketing system costs. We aim to show the full price before you complete your purchase. If something looks unclear, please contact us.
DOORS, ARRIVAL, AND ENTRY
 What time do doors open?
For Iron Horse shows, the bar and kitchen generally open two hours before showtime and the main performance room opens one hour before showtime. Matinees may have later door times. For Parlor Room shows, doors generally open 30 minutes before showtime.
Always check the event page for the most accurate information.
 How does Iron Horse entry work?
When the bar opens, guests may enter through 18 Center St. and receive a numbered card indicating their place in the entry sequence. When the performance room is ready, staff call parties in order, usually in groups of 10. A line may form outside if the bar reaches capacity.
 Why might the main room open later than expected?
Live shows depend on artist arrival, soundcheck, and setup. We do our best to open on time, but may need to delay if these are still in progress.
 Do I have to stand in line? 
At some Iron Horse shows, guests may enter the bar area before the main performance room opens. When numbered entry cards are being used, your card determines your entry order into the performance space.
If waiting in line is difficult because of a disability, medical condition, pregnancy, mobility need, etc., please contact us in advance or speak with staff when you arrive. We will do our best to help you avoid unnecessary standing.

PRIVATE EVENTS
THE IRON HORSE
The historic Iron Horse Music Hall is a unique and versatile event space. The Iron Horse is ideal for a variety of private functions, including concerts, parties, corporate events, fundraisers, and more. Capacity: 180-275 guests.
Venue features
Historic Atmosphere: The Iron Horse is the site of thousands of legendary concerts and is well-known for its intimate setting and classic charm. The space features rough-sawn beams, newly-refinished floors, and music memorabilia lining the walls. 
Capacity: The venue comfortably accommodates approximately 180 seated guests or up to 275 standing guests for a flexible experience that can adapt to your needs. 
Stage and sound: Equipped with a professional stage and state-of-the-art sound and lighting, the Iron Horse is perfect for live music performances, presentations, or any event requiring A/V capabilities. Our in-house crew will work with you to manage any technical needs. Additional fees may apply. 
Full Bar: The venue’s newly-built full bar features decorative details created by local artisans. The bar menu can be customized to suit your event needs, whether you’re envisioning an open bar, a cash bar, or a signature cocktail.
Catering Options: Our world-class chef O’Brian Tomalin and our catering team offer a wide range of food options that can often be customized to meet your needs. 
Location: Situated in the heart of downtown Northampton, the Iron Horse is in range of several parking options, and accommodations for out-of-town guests. 
About the space: The Iron Horse Music Hall is approximately 5,000 square feet. This space includes the main seating area, stage, bar, and basement green room space, making it suitable for various event configurations, including seated dinners, dance parties, mixers, or cocktail-style gatherings. 
The hallmark of the Iron Horse experience is one of close conviviality and charm. We’re looking forward to welcoming you and your guests into our historic, lovingly-preserved space. 
Included services:
Event coordination: Assistance with planning and coordinating event details to ensure a smooth day-of run.
Technical support: On-site technicians to manage sound, lighting, A/V and other technical aspects during your event.
Staffing: Professional bar staff, and other event staff as required. Additional fees may apply.
Customization and flexibility:
Flexible layout: The venue allows for a range of seating arrangements and decor setups to tailor the space to your event's theme and needs. 
Customization: Available for daytime, evening, and late-night events, with flexible hours depending on your booking. 
Accessibility: The Iron Horse features accessible restrooms and entries on the same level as one side of the room. The floor in front of the stage is accessed by one step. The stage itself is accessed via three steps with no handrail, and the basement green room is accessed via a flight of steps with a handrail. 
Rental rates and discounts:
Variable rates: Rental fees vary based on the day of the week, time of day, and how far in advance you book. Rates are higher for peak days like Friday and Saturday evenings, and lower for weekdays and daytime events.
Discounts: Special discounts are often available for bookings made on certain holiday weekends and for bookings made less than eight weeks in advance.

Have a question before a show? Start here. If you still need help, contact boxoffice@ironhorse.org or call the box office at (413) 200-0425.
TICKETING
 Where can I buy tickets to Iron Horse Shows?
Tickets can be purchased online, by phone, in person at the Iron Horse box office, or at the door when tickets remain available.
The box office is located at 18 Center St. in Northampton and is open 2:30–4:00pm on Iron Horse show days. Phone: (413) 200-0425
 Can I buy tickets at the door?
Yes, if the show is not sold out. We recommend buying in advance for popular shows.
 Are tickets digital or physical? 
Tickets are digital. Show them on your phone or print them before you arrive. If you can't access your tickets, bring a valid photo ID and our staff can look up your order by name.
 I did not receive my tickets. What should I do? 
Search your email for the show name, "The Parlor Room," "Iron Horse," or "PatronManager," and check your spam or promotions folder. If you still can't find them, contact us with the name on the order, the show date, and the email address used at purchase.
 I am having trouble logging in to the Membership Portal. What should I do?
Contact memberships@ironhorse.org or call (413) 200-0425.
 Why do ticket fees appear at checkout?
Ticketing fees cover processing and ticketing system costs. We aim to show the full price before you complete your purchase. If something looks unclear, please contact us.
REFUNDS, EXCHANGES, AND CHANGES
 What is your refund policy? 
Tickets are generally nonrefundable unless a show is canceled or rescheduled.
If a show is rescheduled, your tickets will be honored for the new date. If you can't attend, you may request a refund within 14 days of the announcement. If a show is canceled, refunds will be processed automatically.
Please note: Supporting acts and openers are always subject to change.
 Can I exchange my tickets for a different show? 
In most cases, no. If you have a specific situation, contact us and we'll let you know what's possible.
 Can I transfer my tickets to someone else?
We don't facilitate ticket transfers, but you can transfer tickets by forwarding the original confirmation email – including the QR code – to the recipient.
We strongly recommend purchasing only through our official website. Tickets from resellers or third-party sources cannot be guaranteed as valid.
 What happens if a show sells out?
Additional tickets may not be available. If more are released, they'll appear on the event page. Purchasing through our official website or box office is the safest way to avoid invalid tickets or scams.
 I am having trouble logging in to the Membership Portal. What should I do?
Contact memberships@ironhorse.org or call (413) 200-0425.
 Why do ticket fees appear at checkout?
Ticketing fees cover processing and ticketing system costs. We aim to show the full price before you complete your purchase. If something looks unclear, please contact us.
DOORS, ARRIVAL, AND ENTRY
 What time do doors open?
For Iron Horse shows, the bar and kitchen generally open two hours before showtime and the main performance room opens one hour before showtime. Matinees may have later door times. For Parlor Room shows, doors generally open 30 minutes before showtime.
Always check the event page for the most accurate information.
 How does Iron Horse entry work?
When the bar opens, guests may enter through 18 Center St. and receive a numbered card indicating their place in the entry sequence. When the performance room is ready, staff call parties in order, usually in groups of 10. A line may form outside if the bar reaches capacity.
