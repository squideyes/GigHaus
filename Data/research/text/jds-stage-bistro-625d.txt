A REFINED HIDEAWAY 
Vinyl Lounge 
The Vinyl Lounge is an intimate, design-forward space that pays tribute to the warmth and ritual of recorded music. Featuring state-of-the-art sound and thoughtfully considered acoustics, it is designed for unfiltered, pure vinyl listening – a testament to a commitment to music in its most authentic form. ~ capacity 20 
A Versatile Gathering Space 
The Newsroom 
The Newsroom is a distinctive private space shaped by storytelling and the exchange of ideas. Set within one of the venue’s unique vaulted rooms, the space is enhanced by chandeliers and adaptive lighting that create a dynamic yet refined atmosphere.
Designed for both professional and social gatherings, it accommodates everything from meetings and presentations to intimate dinners and celebrations. Paired with JDs culinary and service experience, The Newsroom offers a setting that feels both purposeful and memorable. ~ capacity 70 (seated)

Tucked away for a more personal experience, the Wine Nook is a cozy retreat perfect for intimate conversations and small group gatherings. With a curated selection of wines and a quiet, welcoming ambiance, it’s a place to slow down and savor the moment. ~ capacity 9

ABOUT 
FROM PASSION TO THE STAGE 
A Vision 20 Years in 
the Making 
JDs Stage Bistro brings a twenty-year dream to life—an investment in downtown Chelsea shaped by vision, craftsmanship, and community. Co-founded with Jeff Daniels, the concept blends chef-driven, Michigan-forward cuisine and craft cocktails with an intimate, state-of-the-art listening room. What began as an idea behind a Main Street storefront has evolved into a destination where food, music, and atmosphere are thoughtfully intertwined.  
The experience begins at the door: handcrafted cherry wood with stained-glass detail—an olive in a martini glass—hinting at gatherings ahead. An Art Deco-inspired marquee nods to Chelsea’s historic charm, while inside, a 1920s-style chandelier and descending staircase reveal two distinct worlds. The stage room is moody and cinematic, with cabaret seating, a corner stage, and state-of-the-art acoustics designed so every lyric feels personal. In contrast, the bistro is bright and welcoming, centered around a custom wood-fired oven and opening seamlessly to a screened patio for year-round connection.  
Throughout the space, storytelling is intentional. The vaulted Newsroom offers a wine vault, private bar, and adaptable setting for celebrations and corporate events, while the Vinyl Lounge creates a secluded retreat for pure, analog listening. Chelsea history, music memorabilia, and tributes to Jeff Daniels’s career are woven into every corner—honoring the town, its creative spirit, and the community that helped bring this vision to life.

From This Place, To Many. 
From This Place, To Many. 
JDs Stage Bistro is proudly based in Chelsea, with a vision that extends far beyond its walls. Our commitment is to create a world-class venue that strengthens the local economy while welcoming guests from across the region to experience something truly special. 
We celebrate Michigan and regional artists, partner with farms and makers, and create meaningful jobs within the hospitality and arts communities. From curated performances to thoughtfully sourced ingredients, every detail reflects a dedication to quality, creativity, and connection. 
Whether you join us for dinner, a live performance, or a private celebration, JDs is designed as a gathering place—where music, food, and shared experiences bring people together and leave a lasting impression.

JDs Stage Bistro Guest Information 
Welcome 
 We’re delighted to welcome you to JDs Stage Bistro. Whether you’re joining us for an intimate live performance and dinner or dinner only in our stand alone restaurant, we look forward to providing an exceptional experience centered on outstanding hospitality, chef-driven cuisine, handcrafted beverages, and world-class entertainment.
Not joining us for a show? Our separate restaurant is open 6 days a week. 
A REFINED HIDEAWAY 
Dining at JDs 
 You don’t need a concert ticket to experience JDs Stage Bistro.
We welcome guests for dinner and drinks Tuesday through Sunday. The full menu and bar are available, creating an inviting destination for a memorable evening out. 
A Versatile Gathering Space 
Coming to a Show? 
Here is What to Expect 
Before the Show
Doors open 90 minutes before each performance.
Food and beverage service continues throughout the performance.
Guests are encouraged to arrive early to relax, enjoy dinner and drinks, and settle into their seats before the show begins.
We do not have a food and beverage order minimum but encourage an order of both. Most ticket revenue goes directly to the artists and production costs, so food and drink purchases help support the staff, service and unique music venue for shows. 
Tickets & Seating
Please have your tickets ready for scanning upon arrival, either on your mobile device or as a printed copy.
After your tickets have been scanned, our hosts will escort you to your reserved dining seats.
Advance ticket purchases are recommended to ensure availability.
Walk-up ticket purchases are welcome, subject to availability, and may be purchased by credit card only.
For ticket questions, exchanges, or support, please contact Prekindle directly. JDs Stage Bistro is unable to process ticket refunds or transfers. 
After the Performance
The evening doesn’t have to end when the final song is over. JDs Stage Bistro remains open after performances, with our full menu available until 11:00 p.m., inviting guests to linger, enjoy dessert, another cocktail, or continue the conversation. 
WHERE MOMENTS ARE SAVORED 
Our Experience 
JDs Stage Bistro has been thoughtfully designed as a listening room where exceptional food, genuine hospitality, and remarkable live performances come together. We invite guests to help preserve this unique atmosphere by arriving on time, silencing mobile devices during performances, and enjoying the music alongside fellow guests in a setting created for listening, conversation, and connection. 
A Versatile Gathering Space 
Arrival & Accessibility 
Guests may enter through the Main Street entrance or the ADA-accessible entrance located behind the building in the Purple Rose Theatre parking lot.
Complimentary street parking and nearby public parking are available for your convenience.
We look forward to welcoming you soon.
