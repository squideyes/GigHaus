Where can I park?
There is a limited amount of parking available directly behind our building. You can also park on the street or just down the block at the Tire Warehouse (after hours). 
Will there be drinks?
Yes. Beer, wine, liquor, cider, and soda available for purchase. Please bring an ID and cash money. There is an ATM at the venue but our bar is cash only. 
Is there seating?
All of our shows are general admission and seating will be first come first served. We do our best to provide seating for patrons who cannot stand for long periods of time, but for our larger shows seating is limited and views may be obstructed.

JAKE KLAR with BLUE RANGER
Years Go Through Me Now, a new collaborative album by singer-songwriter Jake Klar and folk-rock trio Blue Ranger, is a quiet triumph of weathered hands, the gentle rush of country roads, the laughter of old friends and the silence of rumination — the quilt of dreams and doings we call a life.
GoldFlakePaint describes Klar’s music as “An urgent meeting of folk, roots and good old rock and roll that carries with it an unbreakable spirit.” A fixture in New England’s folk scene, Klar has toured nationally, played festivals including Otis Mountain Get Down, Back Porch Festival and FreshGrass, and supported the likes of Cut Worms, Antwaun Stanley, Joe Pug to name a few.
