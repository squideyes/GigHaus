A BOUTIQUE MUSIC EVENTS VENUE & CRAFT PUB 
The Green Room Social Club welcomes you to join us at the finest music venue in the foothills. Located in Historic downtown Placerville, CA, GRSC offers a full bar with cocktails, craft beer, local wines & paired with live music from local and national touring acts.
The experience continues with our online virtual venue where club members can connect to watch or listen anytime. Members enjoy access to our online library of live performances as well as early-bird access to reservations for all our in-person events.

FAQ'S 
General Info & Ticketing 
 How Do I Buy Tickets?
Purchase Pre-Sale Tickets online at https://tickets.clubgreenroom.com. Day of show tickets may be purchased at the door. 
ALL ACCESS MEMBERS – Show your All Access member card at the door to receive complimentary entry and presale pricing for your guests. (Presale pricing for members available at the door only)
PRIME TIME MEMBERS – Show your Prime Time Member card at the door to receive presale pricing.
 How Do I Reserve A Table?
Tables may be reserved online up to 4pm the day of show at https://tickets.clubgreenroom.com. Unreserved tables are sold first come first served at the door. Reserved tables will be held until 15 minutes after the start of the show unless prior arrangements have been made to hold your table. 
 Where can I watch Livestreams?
Livestreams are broadcasted in full for all members on our website at https://tickets.clubgreenroom.com/music . We also show sneak peeks and occasional shows on our facebook, instagram, and youtube channels. @clubgreenroom
 Where are you located?
251 Main St. in Historic Downtown Placerville.

Membership 
 What is the deal with membership?
While membership is not required, becomiung a GRSC member comes with many added perks including complimentary GA entry to all shows, our mug club, member perks and more. Get the full scoop at https://tickets.clubgreenroom.com/membership
 What's Included in the ALL ACCESS MEMBERSHIP
All Access Pass– General Admission Entry for All the Shows! 
Cred – GRSC All Access Pass card. 
Buddy Pass Unlimited – Pre-Sale ticket pricing for all your guests at the door.
Mug Club! – Upgrade any draft pint to a 20oz member pour!
Swag – Complimentary GRSC Koozie
Birthday Bash – $1Drink of choice on your birthday. One complimentary table reservation during the month of your Birthday with Swag Bag gift!
Heads Up – Advance notice for shows that may sell out! 
Save that $ – Pay annually to receive a $50 discount!
Member Perks – Earn loyalty points with every bar purchase redeemable for a complimentary table reservation with every 250 points. *Must connect your membership in house to the “Club Perks Program” with phone #.
Humbucker Happy Hour – Double Loyalty points during happy hours!
All Access all the time to GRSC Livestream Portal & Archived Performances.
 What's Included in the PRIME TIME MEMBERSHIP
PrimeTime Pricing – Pre-Sale ticket pricing at the Door.
Buddy Pass – Presale Pricing for 1 Guest at the Door
Member Perks – Earn loyalty points with every bar purchase redeemable for a complimentary table reservation with every 250 points. *Must connect your membership in house to the “Club Perks Program” with phone #.
Humbucker Happy Hour – Double Loyalty points during happy hours!
Heads Up – Advance notice for hot shows that may sell out! 
 What's Included in the LIVESTREAM MEMBERSHIP
Livestream Portal – Enjoy all livestreams and membership archives at https://clubgreenroom.com/music
Member Perks – Earn loyalty points with every bar purchase redeemable for a complimentary table reservation with every 250 points. *Must connect your membership in house to the “Club Perks Program” with phone #.
 What’s the deal with Member Perks Points?
Perks points are connected to your member card. You receive ? points for every dollar spent at the club. Points are redeemable for complimentary table reservations.
 How do I get my member card?
Come down to the club and our bar staff can get you hooked up with your card and setup for your membership perks points.

private & curated events 
Rent our space by the hour, day, or more for your private events. Celebrate special events in our comfortable and welcoming venue with catering, bar, and talent booking services offered.
Take advantage of our state-of-the-art equipment to present corporate meetings, events, and instructional classes in person, online, or both. 
 Book Your Event
Group Reservations 
 12-25 Attendees
Group and large party reservations can be made in advance with a minimum per person food and beverage spend. We will arrange seating and provide dedicated staffing for your guests during normal business hours. 
Hourly Rental 
 1-100 Attendees 
Rent the Ballroom or Lounge + Bar by the hour for meetings, private events, receptions, celebrations, or audio/video production. Full use of our professionally designed stage any day of the week 9am-4pm. 
Partial Venue Buyout 
 1-50 Attendees 
Semi private Ballroom, Bar, or Lounge rental including catering. Available for up to 4 hours time 9am-4pm. 
Full Venue Buyout 
 1-100 Attendees 
The Green Room Social Club Full Venue Buyout is available for private experiences and celebrations. Wedding, reception, private party, concert, or any other event where guests will enjoy full use of the entire venue. Private changing room, bar, lounge, and ballroom available any day of the week and any time of day.
GRSC Crew will assist in curating your next celebration or event. We can assist with catering options on request. Our bartenders can create house made wine and beer cocktails based on your personal tastes that will wow you and your guests.
Fully staffed and full use of our space for a day. Note: Subject to availability. Friday and Saturday nights are typically booked up to 3 months in advance.
