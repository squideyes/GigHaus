About Eddie Owen Presents & Red Clay
Duluth, Georgia – Eddie Owen Presents is a songwriter's best friend, offering a music venue, music school, and a welcoming space for performing songwriters and their fans. We are located in Duluth, Georgia, at Red Clay, a 260-seat listening room that showcases some of the most talented musicians on the planet.
Music is a universal language that transcends boundaries. Songwriters and their stories traverse time, with lyrics that resonate with people from all walks of life. Nowadays, many people experience the magic of these artists only through their playlists. However, a renaissance of sorts is taking place in historic Duluth, attracting people from near and far. A significant contributor to this phenomenon is the live music at Red Clay, which has become the songwriter's best friend.
Downtown Duluth Georgia
At this music venue, an eclectic group of patrons regularly gathers to appreciate the displayed talent. Founder Eddie Owen often says, "Life is in the song," and he possesses a unique ability to spot emerging artists. He has played a crucial role in launching the careers of musicians such as John Mayer, the Indigo Girls, The Civil Wars, and Sugarland. His deep connection to the grassroots music scene and unmatched industry credibility set him apart.
Moreover, this music destination plays a vital role in the economic development plan for Duluth, which is the second-largest and fourth-oldest municipality in Gwinnett. Through his humble approach, Owen is reaching demographics that would not typically seek entertainment in Duluth, contributing to the vision of transforming this suburban oasis into a regional music Mecca.
Additionally, the lower level of Red Clay houses the newest location of the Gwinnett School of Music. In addition to offering private music lessons, we provide group and ensemble classroom instruction, as well as workshops.
Eddie Owen Presents: Red Clay, located in Downtown Duluth, hosts live music every weekend, along with music lessons available in our music school downstairs.

FAQ
CAN I RENT EOP/RCMF FOR A PRIVATE EVENT?
Yes, we host everything from showcases for bands to product launches for companies. We’ve even hosted a couple of fundraisers. For rates and more info, please email info@eddieowenpresents.com with proposed dates, times, and info about your event.
HOW THE HECK DO I GET THERE?
Eddie Owen Presents & Red Clay are located at 3116 Main St. in the heart of downtown Duluth, GA. We are located just off Buford Highway, which makes our location easily accessible from all surrounding areas. Our main entrance is located on Main Street. We recommend using Google Maps for directions. Please click here to access exact directions from your location to Eddie Owen Presents & Red Clay.
From Buford (NORTH):
Take Buford Highway (GA-13 S/US-23S) South Turn RIGHT onto W Lawrenceville St then LEFT onto Main St.
From Atlanta (SOUTH):
Take I-85 North to Exit 104/Pleasant Hill Rd. Turn LEFT on Pleasant Hill Rd. Follow Pleasant Hill Rd to Buford Highway (GA-13 N/US-23N) Merge onto Buford Highway North (GA-13N/US-23N) Turn LEFT onto W Lawrenceville St. then LEFT onto Main St.
From Lawrenceville (EAST):
Follow Duluth Highway/GA-120 West towards Duluth This will take you directly to Main St. Turn LEFT on Main St.
From Marietta (WEST):
Take I-75S to I-285N From I-285N take Exit 31B to Peachtree Industrial Blvd (GA-141-N) Continue on Peachtree Industrial Blvd. Turn RIGHT onto Howell Ferry Dr. and continue onto McClure Bridge Rd. At traffic circle, take the 1st exit to W. Lawrenceville St. then RIGHT onto Main St.
From Alpharetta (WEST):
Follow GA-120 E to State Bridge Rd then Pleasant Hill Rd. Turn LEFT onto McClure Bridge Rd. At traffic circle, take the 1st exit to W. Lawrenceville St. then RIGHT onto Main St.
WHAT IS SEATING LIKE FOR YOUR SHOWS?
Most of our shows offer reserved seating, but a small amount are General Admission. Seating formats are noted in the main description of each event. If seating is General Admission, they are first come first serve, so come down early, grab a drink, and lock down a seat! Here's how the Reserved seating works (here's a map): We offer two tiers of reserved seating – Premium Reserved (blue seats on map) & Regular Reserved (red seats on map). Premium offers reserved seating in the Premium Center section, which includes the center first nine rows in front of the stage. Regular Reserved seating is basically everything else. There really isn't a bad seat in the house, but Premium is where it's at! You will be able to choose your own seats (upon availability) during the ticket purchasing process, whether that is online, over-the-phone, or in person. If you have special seating requests, please call us at 678-892-6373.
WHERE CAN I PARK?
All public parking in Downtown Duluth is free! 
ARE YOU WHEELCHAIR ACCESSIBLE?
100%! For wheelchair accessible seating accommodations, please call us at 678-892-6373. For a look at nearby Handicapped Parking spots click here.
I LOST OR DON’T HAVE A DRIVER’S LICENSE, MAY I STILL PURCHASE ALCOHOL?
That depends… Everyone must have a photo ID to pick up tickets and purchase alcohol at our bar. The photo ID could be a US passport (or whatever country you are from). It could be a military ID, motorcycle license or a state issued ID card. We do not accept birth certificates (no picture), student ID, or anything else not issued by the state or federal government or without photo.
LOST SOMETHING, DO YOU HAVE IT, AND CAN I GET IT BACK?
If we find something left at the venue, we hold onto it for one week, and then we dispose of it. If you left or lost something at Red Clay, give us a call and leave a message 678.892.6373. We will see if we can locate it for you.
I LEFT MY TAB OPEN AT THE BAR, WHAT DO I DO?
If you leave your tab, we will close it at the end of the night and add 20% gratuity to it. You are more than welcome to come by anytime we are open after doors to pick it up.
CAN I SMOKE INSIDE RED CLAY?
Smoking is never allowed in Red Clay. This also includes e-cigarettes.
CAN I BRING MY CHILD TO RED CLAY?
Bring the whole fam…we are all-ages all of the time!
ARE THERE SEATS AT RED CLAY?
Yes, we have 260 seats (three of those are for wheelchair access only). 
CAN I GET A REFUND ON MY TICKETS?
No, you own them and they are yours to use as you see fit.
WHAT TYPE OF FOOD IS SERVED AT RCMF?
We offer the finest selection of chips, sweets, granola, Cheez-Its, crackers, nuts, and cookies in the state!
SERIOUSLY, THOUGH. WHERE CAN I GRAB SOME REAL FOOD BEFORE THE CONCERT?
There is a lot of new development going on in downtown Duluth currently! In walking distance from the theatre, your options are Local on North, Main Street Mediterranean Cafe, Maple Street Biscuits (breakfast & lunch), Good Word Brewing, Nacho Daddy, Truck and Tap, Sweet Octopus, Pure Taqueria, Dreamland BBQ, NOONA STEAKHOUSE & OYSTER BAR , Simply Done Donuts and O4W Pizza.
CAN I BRING IN FOOD?
Sure thing!
ARE THERE ANY HOTELS NEAR THE VENUE?
Yes, we have a great number of hotels very close by. The closest is just a few blocks away – the Courtyard Marriott in Downtown Duluth.
I (AND/OR MY BAND) WANTS TO PLAY THERE, WHAT DO I NEED TO DO?
Email a press kit to us at info@eddieowenpresents.com. Please remember that we get tons of submissions every day, but we can’t book everyone. Polite persistence is key.
WHAT KIND OF SHOWS DOES EDDIE OWEN PRESENTS/RED CLAY PUT ON?
Everything from folk to country, bluegrass to funk, jam to jazz, blues to rock and most combinations in between.
ARE YOU HIRING? DO YOU NEED INTERNS?
We just might be…thank you for asking. Please send your resume and some words of what you can do for us, and what sets you apart from the rest. Please send to info@eddieowenpresents.com.
WILL YOU HAVE TICKETS FOR TONIGHT’S SHOW AT THE DOOR?
It depends. We definitely recommend purchasing them in advance… You’ll save some money, and rest easy knowing you’ll be attending a great show!
CAN I CHANGE THE NAME ON MY TICKET IF I CAN’T GO?
You can change the name on your ticket at Will Call. You can give us a call at the Box Office at 404.478-2749 or stop by during business hours (Monday-Friday, 2pm-9pm). Our box office is also open any time we have a show going on. to arrange a name-change. Your friend will need to bring his/her ID to the Box Office the night of the show to pick up the tickets.
HOW CAN I GET FREE TICKETS?
We love giving away some free tickets! The best way to win some is to follow us on Twitter or like us on Facebook.
ARE YOU OK WITH PEOPLE SCALPING TICKETS?
Eddie Owen Presents & Red Clay is not responsible for tickets sold via Craigslist, eBay, StubHub, Facebook and other similar websites. Once a ticket is scanned at the door duplicates are automatically rejected. Be extremely careful when buying a photocopy of a print at home ticket! The best place to purchase a ticket is via our website or our physical box office located at 3116 Main St. in Duluth.
DO YOU ALLOW GUNS?
It is a shame we have to answer this question, but NO, we do not allow any weapons at Red Clay!
