We're so glad you made it 
Flat Iron Crossroads, a 501(c)3 non-profit performing arts center nestled in the peaceful countryside of
Tidewater Virginia, has been bringing together musicians and diverse audiences since 2019.
Come visit our beautiful and acoustically magnificent indoor and outdoor venue in Gloucester,
an hour from Richmond and Norfolk. 
 Mission StatementFlat Iron Crossroads, a 501(c)3 non-profit organization is dedicated to the enlightenment and well-being of its community and visitors through culture, entertainment, and education by providing a diverse selection of performing arts that bring local, national, and international experiences to Eastern Virginia.

Get to know us 
Flat Iron Crossroads is a 501(c)3 non-profit performing art center in Gloucester, VA. The venue is organized by an all-volunteer team who dedicate their time to the enlightenment and well-being of its community and visitors through culture, entertainment, and education by providing a diverse selection of performing arts that bring local, national, and international experiences to Eastern Virginia 
 The venue was revitalized and transformed from a 100-year-old gas station into an intimate, modern performing arts center. Just as gas stations have long prepared travelers for journeys, Flat Iron Crossroads strives to help artists and patrons discover avenues of musical adventure both familiar and untraveled.
Since its inception in 2019, FIX has added an 800-square foot outdoor stage on a sweeping lawn that seats 500 guests. The indoor stage is surrounded by intimate , one-of-a-kind portraits and posters, with a stocked bar, patio access, and seating accommodations with a capacity of 140 guests.
Flat Iron Crossroads exists to celebrate the joy of music and the arts for all ages. We work with the community to put on educational events and provide music-based program sponsorship. In the past, we have hosted performing artists including Daniel Donato’s Cosmic Country, JD Simo, Larry Keel, The Boneshakers, Ron Holloway Band, Anthony Rosano and the Conqueroos, Mighty Joshua, Colby T Helms & the Virginia Creepers, The Nick Moss Band, Jim Quick & Coastline, The Delorans, Isaac Hadden Organ Trio, The Reverend Payton’s Big Damn Band, Clayton Williams and the Garifuna Drummers and many more.
