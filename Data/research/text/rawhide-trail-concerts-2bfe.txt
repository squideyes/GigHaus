About Rawhide Trail Concerts
My husband, Dino Jobe, and I started this series to promote artists we love and to share their talents with you! We loved finding new music and having them play in our home. We also loved the community that House Concerts develop and the friendships that bloom within.
We lost Dino in July of 2025 but I have continued to put on these concerts ​and we are going strong!
If you love meeting new artists and having a chance to actually have a conversation or just the chance to snap a picture with them, this is the place you should be!
Come join us for an evening of pot luck, great people, and amazing music! 
Come casual and ready to be entertained and welcome to our family!
Leslie Cash Jobe

What is a Rawhide Trail House Concert?
What is a Rawhide Trail House Concert?
House concerts are a wonderful alternative to commercial music venues like bars and restaurants. Hosted right in a private home and attended mostly by friends, neighbors, coworkers, and invited guests, our concerts offer an intimate setting ideal for listening, completely free of loud and rowdy crowds. Best of all, 100 percent of your donations go directly to the artist. 
No money goes to the house.
Talent & Invitations
We book incredible new talent as well as well-known touring artists. Because these are hosted in a private home, attendance is strictly by invitation only.
To receive an invitation and hear about upcoming shows, you must join our mailing list through our Contact page.
The Evening Schedule
We typically gather on a Sunday. We encourage you to bring your favorite dish to share for our potluck and bring your preferred drinks, as we are BYOB. We will gladly provide refreshing water and hibiscus iced tea for everyone.
 5:30 PM
Potluck begins, giving everyone time to enjoy the views in our backyard and visit with friends.
 7:00 PM
The musical performance begins.
The Performance Experience
All of our shows are indoors. We have discovered that a living room concert provides unparalleled intimacy and an extraordinary experience for our 50 to 60 guests.
The music ordinarily consists of two 45-minute sets with a 15-minute intermission. During the break and after the show, guests have the wonderful opportunity to buy CDs and merchandise directly from the artist. All merchandise proceeds go straight to them!
Seating & Comfort
We have plenty of chairs available for seating, so there is no need to bring your own furniture.
However, if you would like to bring a cushion, it is great way to save your seat and provide a bit more comfort on the metal chairs. If you ever need any special accommodations, please call and let us know so we can help make you feel welcome.

Reservation Information
Music Among Friends
Bear in mind this is a private party in a private home, limited strictly to friends on our email list. You have to be on our email list to make a reservation—no exceptions!
You can easily get on our email list by visiting the Contact Us page. Once a show is open for reservations (basically, when it is announced), you may secure your spot by prepaying the suggested donation amount.
Securing Your Seat
The suggested donation amount is:
$30 per person for 1 to 3 band members
$35 per person for 4 or more band members
Unless prepayment is waived for a particular show, you can complete this using three ways: Venmo, a check (exchanged for cash the day of the show), or cash at the door.
Paying by Check
If choosing the check option, your check must be dated the day of the show (or within 30 days of the show) and made payable directly to the artist, not to Rawhide Trail Concerts.
Please include a telephone number and the names of all your guests, and mail it to us here:
Rawhide Trail Concerts
 10501 Rawhide Trail
 Austin, Texas 78736
Waiting List & Our Trust Policy
About one week before a show that hasn't sold out, we open unpaid reservations by email request, with donations paid at the door. We operate entirely on trust—artists rely on this income, so if you reserve a seat, please show up or cancel early!
If a show sells out, request to be on our waiting list by emailing us or calling 512-300-6659. Let us know your party size and minimum accepted seats. We make custom name tags in advance, and for Sunday shows, we may call as late as Saturday morning!
