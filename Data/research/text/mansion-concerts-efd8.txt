Greetings
 Welcome to our website. If you are here and reading this, the odds are pretty good that we just invited you to one of our private mansion concerts and music parties in Harrisburg, PA. Since 2015, these special gatherings have combined the world-class music played by our guitar-playing friends and mentors inside the River Room of The Manor On Front. The Manor is a beautiful old home in Harrisburg, PA, converted into a bed & breakfast. Polly and Tim stage these gatherings 6 times a year. All guests are pre-registered, and since seating is limited, walk-ins are not accepted. Only by joining our mailing list, meeting us in person, talking to us on the phone, or emailing us are you going to be added to our growing private invitation list. 
See more on page 28 of the June 2023 issue of TheBurg
 Mansion Concerts Inc. is a 501(c)3 non-profit performing arts organization dedicated to the promotion of instrumental guitar players and singer-songwriters whose main instrument is the guitar. Almost all of our guest performers are teachers in their own right, serving as further inspiration to guitarists and songwriters of all skill levels. 
 All Stars by Polly is now supporting these concerts with the sale of hand-folded origami stars as gifts or decorations. See Polly’s Facebook page here and more information here. And we have also begun our first fundraising campaign to support the series. You can learn more about this here.
 Stay tuned to this website to see who’s next, and please join our mailing list so that you are among the absolute first to know. These concerts are intimate, one-of-a-kind "guitar-centric" performances curated by our hosts. With under 100 seats available for any show, being on our email list is the difference between being invited or missing these shows completely. Join our growing tribe, please.
 These private gatherings are by invitation only, smoke-free, and date-friendly musical experiences staged in a most intimate setting. In this age of compressed, streamed, and diminished recorded music, these private gatherings provide a musical experience unavailable in most large or small venues. After the show, our artists stick around, sign CDs, answer questions, and have the chance to meet and greet the entire audience. Again, this is something you won't experience in a larger venue.
 Please check out our concert series, and if you like what you see, you know what to do. 
Thank you.
Tim and Polly Alexander

MISSION:
Mansion Concerts Inc. is a central-PA-based educational and cultural performing-arts organization featuring some of the finest steel-string acoustic guitar players and acoustic/folk singer-songwriters. The organization has grown into being from a house concert series founded by the organization’s leaders. Each artist is presented in an intimate concert/performance space in the “half-round” where the audience and performer are very close. Performances have a decidedly guitar-centric educational mission, highlighting the unique and prominent contribution of the American acoustic steel string guitar to the development of many genres of instrumental music and song. Currently, Mansion Concert performances are staged only 6 times per year.
 DETAILS:
Almost all featured artists are guitarists whose instrument provides creative inspiration for their instrumental performance and/or songwriting. Mansion Concerts Inc. features many of the nation’s most talented (and sadly unknown) steel string guitarists and singer-songwriters, where the guitar stands central to their craft (See 1 below). Mansion Concerts Inc. includes artists in a wide range of musical genre including (but not limited to): Roots Blues, American Jazz, Folk, Classical, Celtic, Old-Time, Appalachian, Traditional Bluegrass, Funk, Pop/Modern, World Music, Improvisational Solo and Pop/Country. See 2 below for a list of past Mansion Concerts artists.
Each concert experience features an artist making music in a uniquely intimate setting. The physical positioning of the performance space and audience is purposefully designed to enhance the exchange (educational, cultural, and social) between the artist and audience. Almost all performers in this series are educators (and/or instrumental instructors) serving as further inspiration to students attending these special events. Occasionally staged with pre-concert workshops, these evening-long gatherings are musical, social, and always educational. 
 In a time when modern commercial music has grown to be computer-generated, fully electronic, and artificially pitch-adjusted or compressed for data streaming purposes, these small-group live performances are presented as unique learning experiences, bringing students, listeners, and performers as close together as possible in a supportive and interactive environment. Performing artists answer questions from the audience before, during and after their performances. With a limited number of seats for any performance, each attendee is very close to the artist.
Currently, Mansion Concert performances are staged only 6 times per year, and attendance donations from students and listeners (collected before and during the concert) fully support artist fees and expenses. Board members, advisory committee members, and other volunteers regularly provide these traveling artists with overnight accommodations in/near Harrisburg. Often, but not always, the artists join a group of volunteers for a donated evening meal before or after the concert. This all-volunteer crew provides every element of performance staging, lighting, seating, sound system, snacks, and other needs typical of a house concert performance. 
These performances have been staged within two historic mansions in Harrisburg (hence the trade name Mansion Concert). The rental of this space is paid fully by donations made by board members and other volunteers. Tickets are not sold, and seats are not assigned. Instead, listeners register for these events in advance, agreeing to a suggested minimum donation and often making this donation in advance of the performance. Again, this is very much like a traditional house concert with a prominent focus on the role of the American acoustic steel string guitar in the music of the evening. 
 Local guest guitarists and songwriters are recruited by the Board to serve as opening performers for certain concerts. The generosity of these board members, volunteers and audiences allow the organization to attract artists from around the country (and world) who might otherwise seek larger concert venues. Almost all artists who have played in past concerts were playing in Harrisburg for their very first time. And most concert attendees had never seen or heard of these artists before they attended the Mansion Concerts event.

The River Room at The Manor on Front
The Manor on Front - Harrisburg PA's Most Beautiful Riverfront Bed and Breakfast!
A woman-owned & disabled, veteran-owned small business
At The Manor on Front Bed and Breakfast, you can have an unbeatable view of the Susquehanna River from your room. You can relax in any of the many common areas The Manor has to offer, including the library, living room, parlor, and courtyard, or you can explore the many attractions and restaurants in Harrisburg, PA. The Manor on Front has free high-speed WiFi throughout the entire property and free onsite parking. Each room is equipped with a large HD smart TV and includes Netflix for guests. The Manor on Front is also the perfect place to host your next event. You can host conferences, banquets, parties, weddings, fundraisers, reunions, and yes, even concerts in The River Room.
Parking — There is very limited parking at The Manor on Front and these spots are available only for overnight guests. However, there is an abundance of free, well-lit residential, on-street parking all along the northbound and southbound sides of the 30th block of Second Street. Arrive early and be sure to take a short walk along Front Street and/or the Riverfront Park immediately across Front Street from The Manor -- a stunning riverfront view indeed.
