Ownership
​
 The principal owners of Cadenza are Alan & Sue Mooney. Alan & Sue are big fans of live music and theatre. In addition to owning Cadenza, they are strong supporters of Maine State Music Theatre. Sue has served on the Board of Trustees and is part the MSMT Angel volunteers. Sue and Alan often sponsor MSMT events. They also own 5 Depot Street, the building that houses Cadenza.
​
 Steve Poulos is also an owner and supporter of Cadenza. Steve is enthusiastic about live performances and frequently attends Broadway shows and Jazz events in New York. Steve is also a big supporter of Maine State Music Theatre and is currently on the MSMT Board.
​
​
Inspiration
​
 Cadenza grew out of a somewhat serendipitous combination of ideas and energy from several talented musicians, serious live music fans, outstanding sound and lighting technicians, and highly experienced performers and producers.
​
 We are committed to providing a high-quality, comfortable space for both performers and audiences.​

Our Schedule
​
We are normally open for events on Fridays and Saturdays. However, we are flexible and have some performances on Thursdays and Sundays (typically at 4:00 pm). Most performances start at 7:00 PM. Doors typically open 60 minutes before each show. We encourage you to come early and place food orders when you arrive so you can fully enjoy the performance. Please be sure to check our schedule regularly.
​
​
Our shows and our Performers
​
We are offering local, regional, and national talent performing a wide variety of music styles including Jazz, Blues, Tribute shows, Folk, Great American songbook, Broadway, and more! We also often host shows produced by Maine State Music Theatre We are privileged to have many very talented performers who are enthusiastic about performing at Cadenza.
​
We have a well-cared-for Yamaha C-7 piano that has become the centerpiece of a fan club of pianists who love to come to Cadenza to play it!
​
We are “performer ready”, click here to see what we offer each performer.

Our facility
​
Starting in 2018, we created Cadenza by completely renovating a former, abandoned performance venue at 5 Depot Street. Everything is new; lighting, sound system, stage, flooring (including a great dance floor!), furniture, bar, walls, paint; everything!
​
We are a listening room, we encourage respect for our performers and for those who come to relax and listen.
​
We normally seat up to 50 - 60 people comfortably.
We are accessible for the disabled, including grade-level entrance and fully accessible restrooms.
​
Since Cadenza is a performance venue, any age may attend an event. However, most performances are intended for adults unless otherwise advertised. Young folks, under 18, are expected to be accompanied by a parent or responsible adult. Children between 4 and 10 years old are admitted at half price and are expected to be well-behaved. Children under the age of four are not permitted.
​
We offer student prices for most shows.
We offer a smoke-free environment.
​
Animals are not permitted, except service animals.
Deposits from our cans and bottles are donated to Freeport Community Services (FCS). FCS is a vibrant 501(c)(3) non-profit organization that fulfills its mission through a range of programs and emergency and relief services including a food pantry, fuel assistance, summer camp scholarships, summer lunch, holiday meals and holiday helpline.
​
Cancellations and Postponements
In the event of an event postponement or cancellation, we will notify ticket holders by email and post the information on our website and social media.
ABOUT US
Cadenza first opened in the spring of 2019. Then the Pandemic hit and we closed for a few months, between March 2020 and June 2020. We were among the first to re-open, carefully following the CDC rules and developing our
LIVEStreaming capabilities.
Since then, we have fine-tuned our operation and are stronger and better. With a full schedule, a comfortable listening room experience, LIVEStreaming and all complimented by our new Cadenza Community site on Patreon, www.patreon.com/cadenzacommunity. 
We serve wine, beer and non-alcoholic beverages along with some savory snacks at our bar.
Annually, we produce about 90 shows for the enjoyment of more than 3,000 people! It’s exciting! Thank you for your support!!

Performing at Cadenza
Cadenza has developed a great reputation for providing a quality space for performers. We work hard to maintain that reputation.
As a result, we get many requests from performers to book dates at Cadenza. We generally book 6 - 10 shows a month, mostly on Fridays and Saturdays; with some Thursdays and Sundays.
We try to balance booking our regular performers with a selection of performers new to Cadenza. If you are interested in performing, please send us some information about yourself and your music. We are interested in very good musicians who are also experienced performers and who can connect with an audience. We will consider solo performers as well as bands of up to about 8 members. We will consider almost any genre, except hard rock.
Our typical audience demographic is middle-aged and older.
We try to respond to all inquiries but, honestly, we can't get to everyone, there is not enough time. If you don't get a response, please feel to follow up.
We are thrilled that there are so many great musicians who would like to perform at Cadenza. Thank you!
Performer Payment
Effective March 1, 2024, our standard payment is based on a percentage of ticket sales minus a small retainer taken from total ticket sales.
​
While we promote all of our shows, we also encourage our performers to promote their shows enthusiastically. It's a collaborative effort; more tickets sold is better for everyone!
​
For more information about Performer Payment, please contact Alan Mooney at alan@cadenzafreeport.com or 207-831-4311.
Ticket Prices
Effective January 1, 2026, our standard ticket price is $30 when purchased online in advance and $35 at the door. We have a Student Rate of $15. For certain events, we may set higher prices, which will be posted on our website.
​
For more information about Ticket Prices, please contact Alan Mooney at alan@cadenzafreeport.com or 207-831-4311.

About Cadenza's Private Events
We are pleased to offer a variety of options for groups and events including business dinners & presentations, rehearsal dinners, receptions, wine tastings, and everything in between! Our beautiful room accommodates up to 85 people seated at tables of 4. Many different table arrangements are possible including single tables, grouped tables, lecture hall, and boardroom.
​
FACILITIES
Your reservation includes time before your event for setup and after for clean up.
We can provide AV equipment for your event including a projector and screen, wireless hand-held microphone, and connection to the house speaker system.
Our dimmable house lighting can be tailored to the atmosphere of your event.
We can arrange live music in your choice of genre or will provide access to our sound system for your own performers.
A member of Cadenza's staff will be on-site to facilitate any requests or answer questions.
Men's and Women's restrooms are conveniently located just outside of the event space.
​
FOOD & BEVERAGE
​
Food and beverage catering is entirely customizable. You may choose to leave everything up to us. We will arrange food through our partner Brickyard Hollow and provide a bartender for cash bar or drink ticket setup. We can offer a selection of beer, wine, seltzer, soda, water, and coffee.
​
You may also elect to provide all food and beverages for your event yourself or any combination of the two scenarios. i.e. provide your own catering and use our bartender.
​
EVENT PARKING
​
Ample free covered parking is located in the parking garage across the street. Free uncovered parking can be found within a few hundred feet surrounding the venue. There is also limited street parking in front of Cadenza.
​In short, we are prepared to do whatever is needed to make your private event memorable!
For more information and to schedule your event, please contact us at info@cadenzafreeport.com or call 207-560-5300.

Where is Cadenza located?
The venue is located at 5 Depot Street in downtown Freeport, Maine.
​
Is there food available at Cadenza?
Currently offering light pre-packaged snacks and a wide assortment of wine and beer. Patrons may bring in outside food. No outside drinks apart from personal water bottles are permitted.
​
Does Cadenza have reserved seating?
Most shows at Cadenza have open seating - first come, first serve. There isn't a bad seat in the house! For regulars and Patreon/Cadenza Community members call first to reserve a table.
​
​What time do shows start at Cadenza?
Most shows start at 7:00 PM and the doors open at 6:00. However, there are some exceptions, please check our website regularly.
Does Cadenza allow re-entry at ticketed performances?
Yes, re-entry is allowed at most shows. There are exceptions, so please check with the box office on the night of the event.
​
Is there an age-restriction policy at Cadenza?
Cadenza is a performance venue, any age may attend an event. However, most performances are intended for adults unless otherwise advertised. Young folks, under 18, are expected to be accompanied by a parent or responsible adult. Children between 4 and 10 years old are admitted at half price and are expected to be well behaved. Children under the age of four are not permitted.
​
Anyone purchasing or consuming alcohol will be expected to present a legal ID on request
