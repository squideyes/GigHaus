Welcome! 
Welcome to The Listening Room! 
Most music lovers have had the experience of settling in to listen to some great music, only to have a noisy chatterbox sitting next to them. Not so at the Bugle Boy! Our intimate setting offers an 80 seat, cozy venue where everyone is there for one reason only…to listen to and enjoy great music.
For over 20 years, we’ve been bringing an incredible array of talent to our location in La Grange, TX. Folks from all over enjoy The Bugle Boy experience. We’re just a short drive from nearby Round Top, Schulenberg, Fayetteville, Brenham, Columbus and other neighboring towns. But many of our regulars come from Austin, Houston, San Antonio, Dallas/Fort Worth…heck, we have folks from all over the world who make their way to see us in person or through our webcasts for the best music from artists from around the area and around the world.

We Bring the Sound of Music to The Community.
We know that not everyone can get to the Listening Room to hear music. The Bugle Boy Foundation’s musical mission is further extended to our community through our music-based outreach programs that include off-site concerts for nursing home residents and hospice patients, musical workshops and stage performances for and by persons with intellectual disabilities, as well as in-venue recording sessions for veterans.
The success of The Bugle Boy depends on your generosity.
We would love your consideration of support to help us continue all of our programming, whether at the venue or out in the community.

 Warren Hood Trio 
Award-winning country, folk, bluegrass, jazz and blues!Trio performance featuring Warren Hood, Marshall Hood, and Willie Pipkin!
Get Tickets
LIVE WEBCASTS
A highly accomplished multi-instrumentalist and singer-songwriter with a voice that’s been favorably compared to crooner Chet Baker, Warren Hood is a seven-time Austin Music Awards winner who deftly blends country, blues, bluegrass, jazz, and folk into an eclectic style all of his own. The son of renowned musician Champ Hood (of Uncle Walt’s Band fame), Warren started playing classical violin at age 11 in the school orchestra, later studying privately with Bill Dick. He won classical music competitions, including the Pearl Amster Youth Concerto Competition and the Austin Youth Award, which gave him the opportunity to perform as a soloist on “Lalo Symphonie Espagnole” with the Austin Symphony. He later balanced studying at Austin High with touring with Charlie Robison and the South Austin Jug Band, and went on to attend the prestigous Berklee College of Music — where he earned the coveted String Achievement Award. Upon returning to Texas, he continued to turn heads not just as an A-list studio and touring sideman (for such Texas icons as Joe Ely, Alejandro Escovedo, Bob Schneider, Kelly Willis, and most recently, Lyle Lovett), but as a member of the Waybacks and the South Austin Jug Band, and as a critically-acclaimed band leader in his own right. For this performance he’ll be bringing his Warren Hood Trio, featuring the equally formidable songwriters and ace musicians Marshall Hood — Warren’s cousin and longtime collaborator — and Willie Pipkin, a fellow alum of the South Austin Jug Band.

 The Peterson Brothers 
Dynamic and funky soulful Austin blues brothers!"As long as youngsters like the Petersons keep the flame burning, the [blues] genre will never die." — The Austin Chronicle
Get Tickets
The Peterson Brothers have been enchanting audiences with their very own blend of Americana music consisting of classic soul, funk, blues, and jazz for over a decade. The dynamic Bastrop duo consists of Glenn Jr. on vocals and guitar, and his brother Alex on bass, violin, and vocals. Their musical journey began when their mother and grandmother bestowed upon them a treasure trove of assorted albums and a turntable from a garage sale, sparking a lifelong love affair with classic music. Immersing themselves in the sounds of iconic artists such as the Isley Brothers, Earth, Wind & Fire, the Brothers Johnson, and B.B. King, the Peterson Brothers honed their craft by absorbing the nuances of these legends. Drawing inspiration from their musical roots, they crafted a unique stage show that showcases their infectious energy and undeniable musical prowess, radiating sheer joy through constant eye contact, electric smiles, and an infectious ebullience. Onstage, their synchronized movements, from stepping and twisting to swaying in harmony, create a mesmerizing experience that captivates audiences. As torchbearers of classic American music, the Peterson Brothers continue to leave an indelible mark on the music scene, captivating audiences with their infectious spirit and a musical prowess that pays homage to the timeless sounds that inspired their journey.
A big THANK YOU to the Texas Commission on the Arts and National Endowment For The Arts for assisting with funding for this program.

 The Traveling Red River Songwriters: Walt Wilkins, Susan Gibson, Drew Kennedy, Kelley Mickwee, Josh Grider 
Five Acclaimed Texas Singer-Songwriters on One Stage!All-star line-up featuring the founders of the Red River Songwriters Festival!
Get Tickets
LIVE WEBCASTS
Co-founded by Walt Wilkins, Susan Gibson, Drew Kennedy, Kelley Mickwee, Josh Grider, and Brandy Zdan in 2012, the Red River Songwriters’ Festival is held every year in late January/early February in Red River, New Mexico. Billed as three days of love, friendship, food, drink, and the celebration of music and song, it’s a weekend of heaven on earth for the artists and fans lucky enough to attend. It’s so much fun, in fact, that the Wilkins, Gibson, Kennedy, Mickwee, and Grider all get together a couple of months before each year’s festival for a pre-party mini tour, billing themselves as ”The Traveling Red River Songwriters” — and this year will be the third year in a row that they’ve included the Bugle Boy on their route!

 Sue Foley — One Guitar Woman 
Award-winning blues guitaristSpecial Solo Acoustic Show!
Get Tickets
LIVE WEBCASTS
Sue Foley has been playing guitar since she was 13 years old. When Austin nightclub and label owner Clifford Antone saw her at the annual Blues Music Awards in Memphis, he knew he’d met an all-timer. Before long, Foley was living in Austin and, in 1992, recorded her debut album, Young Girl Blues. They say some things are meant to be, and surely it was this connection with Antone and Austin that set the stage for much of the blues woman’s life and award-winning career. You can certainly hear that connection running all through her 2021 album, Pinky’s Blues, named after Foley’s beloved pink paisley Fender Telecaster. “We just wanted to make something representative of the Texas blues that we had been schooled on in Austin,” she says. “So, we picked great songs and I wrote a few of my own to round things out. Everything on it is a labor of love.” So, too, is her latest, 2025’s One Guitar Woman — Foley’s first all acoustic album, recorded as a tribute to the female pioneers of guitar, ranging from Maybelle Carter to Lydia Mendoza to Sister Rosetta Tharpe.
Prepare for a captivating musical journey with ONE GUITAR WOMAN, from Sue Foley’s GRAMMY® nominated solo acoustic tribute to female pioneers of guitar. Foley’s album also received the 2025 Blues Music Award, for Best Acoustic Album in Memphis in May 2025, and the 2026 Canadian Blues Award for Best Acoustic Album.
ONE GUITAR WOMAN is more than a tribute show. Foley doesn’t merely cover these artists’ songs; she absorbs their style and inhabits the soul of the music. Her guitar work is so deep and natural that it seems to rise from the same roots that gave birth to the originals, while her vibrant vocals bring new life to the lyrics and make the songs her own.
Esteemed music writer Hal Horowitz notes in Rock & Blues Muse, “The often spellbinding One Guitar Woman … is an enlightening look into her remarkable talents, filtered through the lens of a more intimate expression of the blues.”
SUE FOLEY is an acclaimed guitarist, singer/songwriter and writer who began her professional career in her teens. She’s released thirteen albums and has garnered several awards, including Traditional Female Artist of the Year at The Blues Music Awards in Memphis five consecutive times. (2020/22/23/24/25).
A big THANK YOU to the Texas Commission on the Arts and National Endowment For The Arts for assisting with funding for this program.

Why Music Matters 
The Bugle Boy Foundation understands the importance of music in our lives and strives to educate, strengthen and heal our community through our music-based outreach programs. We sometimes do this by becoming Ambassadors of other nonprofit programs with music-based missions that have specific expertise and training support for the needed service. As Ambassadors, we work as a local liaison to educate, implement and to raise the necessary funds to support the programs.

Concerts for Seniors 
 lifting hearts and spirits
This free program made possible by your donations allows us to present concerts for residents, staff and family in area nursing homes and assisted living facilities. 
As a lobby full of nursing home residents tap feet, keep time with their hands and sing along, the benefits of a live concert in a group setting cannot be overstated. The music helps dispel anxiety, can alleviate a sense of loneliness and isolation, and encourages participation and connection with other residents and the musician.
“The residents love to hear live music and these concerts provide an opportunity for them to interact with the musicians, request songs and some even sing though they may have trouble with ordinary conversation” – T.P., Schulenburg RegencyConcerts for Seniors are Supported at:
Monument Hill Nursing Home & Rehabilitation Center (La Grange, TX)
Care Inn of La Grange (La Grange, TX)
Jefferson Place Assisted Living (La Grange, TX)
Schulenburg Regency Nursing Center (Schulenburg, TX)
College Place Assisted Living (Schulenburg, TX)
Make a contribution today and help us continue bringing musical joy into area nursing homes and assisted living facilities.

Hospice Concerts 
 Meaningful Moments
This free program made possible by your contributions allows us to provide private concerts for the terminally ill.
As Ambassadors of Austin-based Swan Songs, we fulfill musical wishes at the end of life by organizing free private concerts for individuals with a terminal illness to include residents of Fayette County. A favorite style of music or musician is brought directly into the home, hospital or living facility allowing recipient, family and friends to focus beyond the illness and come together through the music. These concerts are often a family’s final meaningful time together with their loved one.
“These days Mom doesn’t remember when I visit. The morning after the concert she awoke humming and asking for ‘Cowboy George.’ Thank you for stimulating old memories and creating new ones of her favorite music. Thank you for honoring her well.” – Family Member
“The home concert for Mom was a blessing to us all… we are forever grateful for the gift of this experience& the memories we now cherish” – Daughter
“This is such an awesome program, and I truly appreciate the time you give to ensuring – from receipt of the request to completion of the concert- that the listeners are honored in such a way. Thank you again for all you do. You’re a gift!!” – Barb G. – Hospice S. TX Social worker 
Donate today and keep the music in the air for members of our community whom are terminally ill.
