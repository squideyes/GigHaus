Indianapolis, Indiana House Concert Series
The Clear Vibes House Concerts series is a special part of the Indianapolis Music scene.
This series supports live music, poetry, comedy, art, and more!
Clear Vibes House Concerts is a unique, grassroots performance series that creates a welcoming, intimate space for artists to share and experiment with new ideas with an attentive, all-ages audience. These cozy gatherings foster genuine and meaningful connections between performers and listeners, celebrating creativity in a way that traditional venues often can’t.
Clear Vibes House Concerts
An intimate listening space for performing artists and our community
Where creative performers share their art and experiment with new ideas
Where friends and families join together to listen and show support
Where people connect and spread community love
A welcoming space and an extraordinary experience for everyone
Come experience live art up close—where community grows, inspiration flows, and every performance feels personal. Bring your friends and family for an unforgettable evening you won’t find in any club, bar, or theater.

Clear Vibes House Concerts are hosted at a cozy home in a welcoming neighborhood on the Near East Side of Indianapolis.
This is not a venue and it is not a party. 
This monthly house concert series was born for artists to share their creative talents with an actively listening and engaged community of guests. It offers a comfortable and intimate space, both inside and outside, where the magic happens! 
Meet Your Host
Photo by: Gina Shuppert, Bare Unfiltered
Clear Vibes House Concerts are hosted at the home of Kati Taylor, a local artist, small business owner, and independent community arts and music supporter. 
Since 2019, Kati has transformed her living room and backyard into welcoming performance spaces, curating, managing, promoting, and hosting intimate concert experiences that have featured nearly 100 artists. Her dedication to the arts extends well beyond her home — she has partnered with numerous music and arts organizations to produce concert series, festivals, and community events that uplift local talent. 
House concerts thrive because people believe in them. They exist because someone selflessly opens their doors, artists share their gifts, and a community chooses to show up and listen. Kati’s passion for live music and the performing arts is fueled by her desire to nurture a kinder, more connected community. 
Questions? Contact Kati.
“Great vibe, wonderful people. 
Kati is the ultimate host!”
~ Karen Bernat Kovac, guest
The house concert series has blossomed into a premier monthly event with a supportive community of music lovers. 
A friendly, relaxed vibe emerges through the sharing of space, talent, and guest involvement. Each performer brings something unique, making every house concert a rich and memorable experience. Friends and neighbors who attend have the chance to connect closely with the artists, ask questions, and learn more about their creative process. Meaningful moments unfold as artists and audiences truly connect.
“It has this wonderful atmosphere that is intimate and respectful... I just felt so valued performing there. And it's really important for artists to have a safe space where they can go to experiment with new performance ideas.”
~ Eric Salazar, Musician
No tickets are sold, but there is a suggested donation
Clear Vibes House Concerts are free to attend, but guests are kindly encouraged to make a $20 donation in support of the artists. All contributions are appreciated — no amount is too small — and donations are made on the honor system.
If you value intimate shows, authentic artistry, and grassroots music culture, consider supporting Clear Vibes House Concerts as a friend or sponsor. 
Donations directly sustain future performances, uplift musicians, and keep spaces like this alive for everyone who needs them.

A variety of genres and talented performers are featured at house concerts.
Unique talents of all kinds are featured at Clear Vibes House Concerts. Artists from a wide range of genres perform in the series, including singer-songwriter, folk, bluegrass, country, classical, jazz, hip hop, experimental, spoken word, improv, comedy, and more. Sometimes it's a solo artist, other times a full band — and often, the audience gets to participate too!

Jaik Willis
One-man-band Jaik Willis plays an 11 piece drum set with his feet, while playing guitar/bass/harps, and singing while beatboxing simultaneously without any looping or electronics
Join us for an evening of original music.
Saturday, November 28, 2026
Doors at 6 p.m. | Music at 6:30 p.m.
Jaik Willis is a strumming & drumming One Man Band, a wild fire-breathing freakshow; playing an 11 piece drum set with his feet while playing guitars / bass / & harps; while sing and beatboxing simultaneously, live with no looping or electronics. Performing 300 shows a year with appearances at Bonnaroo, Summer Camp Music Fest, Dunegrass, the Chicago Bluegrass & Blues Fests etc. Jaik Willis got a shout out in Rolling Stone magazine for his vocal contribution to the album “FreeStyle” that debuted at #1 on iTunes.
Jaik Willis has shared bills with artists such as Willie Nelson, Snoop Dogg, Wilco, Bela Fleck, MMW, Del McCoury, NIN, Big Brother & the Holding Company, Leftover Salmon, Justin Townes Earle, Digital Underground, Blueground Undergrass, Garaj Mahal, EOTO,The Schwag, Ekoostik Hookah, Tea Leaf Green, Split Lip Rayfield, and performed on stage with acoustic blues dynamo, Corey Harris, and Chicago blues legend Billy Branch as well. He has performed at innumerable festivals and major venues around the US including Bonnaroo in TN, Summer Camp Music Fest in IL, Chicago Bluegrass & Blues Fests, Schwagstock in MO, Dunegrass in MI, Terrapin Hill Music Fest in KY, Walla Walla Guitar Fest in WA, Hyperion in IN, Jammin on the Wolf in WI, Michigan Peace Festival, Shoe Fest, Tall Tree Lake Music Fest, and dozens of others per season.

Songwriter Round
Stuart Poe | Maria Devigili | Christian Taylor | Rebekah Kay
Join us for an evening of original music.
Wednesday, December 9, 2026
Doors at 6 p.m. | Music at 6:30 p.m.
Join us for a special Songwriter Round — an intimate evening featuring four solo singer-songwriters trading songs and stories throughout the night. The artists will take turns performing original tunes, offering a mix of styles, perspectives, and personalities. It’s a relaxed, up-close format that lets the songs — and the stories behind them — shine. Expect variety, connection, and a fun night of great music.
Stuart Poe
Known as the dobro player for the Punkin Holler Boys, Stuart Poe is also a gifted singer-songwriter whose rich, deep voice brings weight to every lyric. His songs range from heartfelt to fun, and sometimes manage to be both at once. Stuart's writing is thoughtful and relatable, reflecting a keen eye for everyday life.
Maria Devigili
Italian singer-songwriter and guitarist Maria Devigili has built an international career with deeply personal songwriting and expressive guitar work. Blending indie rock, folk, and blues influences, her performances pair honest storytelling with a captivating stage presence that has taken her from street performances in Italy to audiences around the world.
Christian Taylor
A familiar face in Indianapolis's Fountain Square music scene, Christian Taylor has spent years performing with projects including America Owns the Moon, Christian Taylor & Homeschool, and Ampersand Blues Band, while collaborating with countless local musicians. His experimental blend of folk, acoustic, and rock is full of quirky charm, thoughtful lyrics, and the playful personality that shines through both his songs and his performances.
Rebekah Kay
Rebekah Kay creates ethereal folk rock infused with existential pop, magical realism, and unwavering optimism. Formerly known as Mother the Band, she's currently releasing a new single every 4–6 weeks leading up to her upcoming album, inviting listeners into a world of poetic storytelling, lush melodies, and cosmic wonder.
Four writers. Four voices.
