Jesse Ruben is a singer-songwriter known for heartfelt lyrics, powerful storytelling, and deeply engaging live performances. WHAT IS A HOUSE CONCERT?
THE ULTIMATE, INTIMATE MUSICAL EXPERIENCE
Private house concerts provide the same level of musical talent as a large commercial venue, but with the ambiance and intimacy of your living room (or in our case, our custom garage clubhouse). Friends and family gather to welcome artists and every show is a potluck. The friendly, welcoming atmosphere provides a unique opportunity for the artist and audience to connect without distractions found in other venues. 
We don’t sell tickets. All donations go directly to the artist. 
All shows have suggested donation of $25 / person.

ABOUT THE CLUBHOUSE
WE LOVE MUSIC. WE LOVE OUR FRIENDS. WE LOVE OUR SPACE.
We host private house concerts so that we can share our love of music with our friends in a way that supports artists along their musical journey. Some of the artists that come across our stage might be on your radio dial, or not. All are gifted songwriters and musicians - and we do host the occasional comedian. We aim to provide an intimate setting, free of distraction, which allows both the audience and the artist to experience an unforgettable evening. Together, we can help artists pursue their dreams, big or small. 100% of your donations goes to the artists.
About the Space
Our Clubhouse is ground level entrance and accessible to all mobility needs. The space is heated, air-conditioned, and seating includes a mix of small sofas and cushioned folding chairs. 
About the Potluck
Every show features a potluck. We invite you to share your family favorites, your go-to recipes, your “whatever I can bring”. The Clubhouse provides coffee and water. There are multiple electrical outlets at the food table and crockpots/roasters are welcome.
About the Beer
The beer on tap is sourced from local microbreweries. Flavors vary every month. We cannot sell it to you. However, if you enjoy it and would like to donate a few greenbacks into the green bucket, it would be much appreciated. Every donation helps us cover costs and ensure that we can continue to supply brew at future shows.
