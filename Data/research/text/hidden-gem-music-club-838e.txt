About
The Hidden Gem Music Club is medium sized music and events venue in the Dayton area.
Most of our events are all ages. Please refer to the event pages for additional info
We're located in the Normandy Square Shopping Center in the back behind The Submariine House.
Service animals are permitted
contact us at hiddengemdayton@gmail.com

Private Events
Event and Wedding Rental 
Maximum occupancy for a show with tables and chairs is 200. For standing room shows the up to 300 guests are permitted. Our standard seating includes chairs for around 140 or supply your own tables and chairs as you like. 
Rates listed include set up and clean up time. Additional add-on hours must be agreed upon at the time of booking. There is a non-refundable* security deposit due to reserve events which is 50% of total rental cost. 
Please note that weekend dates are in high demand due to our music performance schedule and may require booking several months in advance. Weekdays especially earlier in the week are availabe on much shorter notice.
Prices generally reflect evening events. For certain weekend events that conclude by 5pm (including cleanup), the cost may be lower, please contact us for an exact quote. 
Event Rentals (1-5 hours): 
- Weekday: $500
- Friday: $1000
- Saturday: $1000
- Sunday: $750
- Holidays: Typically Fri/Sat rates but can vary 
Weddings and other full day rentals (up to 10 hours): 
- Weekday: $900 
- Friday: $1500
- Saturday: $1500
- Sunday: $1000
- Holidays: Typically Fri/Sat rates but can vary
Add-on hours available
What is included: 
- Event space
- Tables & chairs
- Use of projector and screen 
- Access to our sound system
- Access to meal prep kitchen 
Food 
Utilize our food partner Thai Chili to offer snack based option, or fully catered meals. You can use any catering company for your event or use our parking lot for food trucks. 
Bar 
We have a full service bar with a wide variety of options including bottled and draft beer, wine and cocktails. You can choose between an open bar where you pay the tab at the end, or have it where guests can purchase drinks (also known as a "cash bar") on their own for the event. Bar staff provided. 
Music 
As we are also a music venue we have a professional sound system for your event and a large stage (18' X 20'). We are hapy to make recommendations for bands and DJ's, and can also provide a sound person for your event for additional charge. 
If you have questions or would like to set up a time to tour our space please contact us at hiddengemdayton@gmail.com or text to 937-829-4874
*in the case of event cancellation, security deposit refunds will be considered on a case-by-case basis but is the sole discretion of The Hidden Gem Music Club
