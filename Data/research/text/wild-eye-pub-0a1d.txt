ABOUT US
WILD EYE PUB:
A vibrant, friendly, multi-use venue, anchored by a large dining room with a seasonal, organic and local menu, featuring nightly live music and events, and a full-service bar.
OUR VISION:
Wild Eye Pub gives customers great food, inspired drinks, and attentive service in a comfortable, art-filled space; a perfect place to connect with others, nurture creative ideas, and nourish body and spirit.
WILD EYE PUB'S HIGHLIGHTS:
Food & drink from regional farms, ranchers, brewers and vintners
Local, seasonal, fresh meals: lunch for 1 or banquet for 100 
Full service pub with local brews & wines, craft cocktails, & great, organic coffee 
Shared commercial kitchen: food makers can lease by the hour or month 
Pop-up supper clubs and special, seasonal menus
Lovely stage and listening room: comfy space for music, readings, gatherings, meetings
Welcoming, communal space, reliable Wi-Fi, business-friendly 
Wild Eye Pub is a catalyst for interconnection: connecting community, promoting local business, and supporting a great staff.
