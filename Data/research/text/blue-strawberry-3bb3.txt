FAQ's
 ×
 Do I need to print my ticket confirmation email?
No, you do not need to have a hard copy of your ticket confirmation email in hand. You will give your name at the door.
 What is your refund policy?
Refunds and exchanges: We do not issue refunds on tickets. Tickets are a buy-at-your-own-risk item. Please keep this in mind when making your purchase. If you are not able to attend and wish to give your tickets to someone you know, you can transfer your tickets by calling the Box Office at 314-256-1745 or emailing us at bluestrawberrystl@gmail.com.
 In the event of postponement, cancellation, or rescheduling of the show, we are happy to offer refunds if you are unable to attend the new date. If you choose to continue to hold your tickets when we reschedule the performance, your tickets will automatically be transferred to the new date and you will be notified of the change via email.
Bad weather: We do not issue refunds or exchanges on tickets due to inclement weather.
Changes or cancellations: Performance names, dates and times are subject to change or cancellation. The Box Office will contact you with any changes to the event and review options as necessary.
 Can I sit with my friends or add guests to my table?
Yes, we can accommodate groups of up to 8 people at a table. If you are buying tickets separately, please write the name(s) of the people you would like to sit with in the comments section during the checkout process online. If you need to make changes, please call the Box Office at 314-256-1745 or email us at bluestrawberrystl@gmail.com.
 What is Premium Seating?
Premium Seating guarantees you a table closest to the stage (within the first 5 rows). Please note that Premium Seating is not available for every show.
 Can I request a specific table at your venue?
No. Your table assignment will be given to you by the host/hostess when you arrive. There is often a better opportunity to choose your table location the earlier you arrive. 
 Will other guests that I don’t know be seated at my table?
We do our absolute best to sit you at a table with only guests who are in your party. If a show is sold out, we may seat two parties together.
 Do I have to pay the ticket fees if I purchase my tickets in person?
No. If you come to our venue to purchase tickets, you will not pay the ticket fees. Our box office is open Wednesday through Sunday, 6pm-9pm. Tickets can be purchased at the door the day of the show depending on ticket availability.
 What time do doors open?
Please check your ticket confirmation email to confirm what time the doors open for your show. Most of our shows begin at 7:30pm, with doors opening at 6pm.
 Do I need to make a dinner reservation?
If you have purchased tickets to a show, you may come in anytime after the doors open to have dinner. In the last step of the checkout process online, you may choose a dinner reservation time. This helps our staff to plan accordingly. Dinner and drinks are served before and during the show.
 Do you have a food and drink minimum?
Yes. We have a $15 minimum (per guest) that can be met by purchasing food and/or drinks. Please take a look at our menu.
 Can I see the stage from the bar?
Yes, all of our bar seats have a view of the stage.
 Can I order dinner at the bar?
Yes, you may order food if you are seated at the bar.
 What is parking like?
There is plenty of street parking (with no meters) available near Blue Strawberry. 
 What is your dress code?
We do not enforce a dress code. Business casual attire is typical.
 Where should I stay if I am coming from out of town to see a show?
We have a special rate with Home2 Suites by Hilton which is less than a 5-minute drive from our venue. Get your booking with the special rate.
 Is your venue 21+?
We are an all ages venue. Children are allowed but some performances may not be appropriate for young children due to content concerns. If there is an age restriction for a show, it will be clearly outlined in the show description on our website.
 What if I need disability seating?
Our venue is wheelchair accessible. Please inform us of your specific needs in the comments section of the checkout process online.

 Will other guests that I don’t know be seated at my table?
We do our absolute best to sit you at a table with only guests who are in your party. If a show is sold out, we may seat two parties together.
 Do I have to pay the ticket fees if I purchase my tickets in person?
No. If you come to our venue to purchase tickets, you will not pay the ticket fees. Our box office is open Wednesday through Sunday, 6pm-9pm. Tickets can be purchased at the door the day of the show depending on ticket availability.
 What time do doors open?
Please check your ticket confirmation email to confirm what time the doors open for your show. Most of our shows begin at 7:30pm, with doors opening at 6pm.
 Do I need to make a dinner reservation?
If you have purchased tickets to a show, you may come in anytime after the doors open to have dinner. In the last step of the checkout process online, you may choose a dinner reservation time. This helps our staff to plan accordingly. Dinner and drinks are served before and during the show.
 Do you have a food and drink minimum?
Yes. We have a $15 minimum (per guest) that can be met by purchasing food and/or drinks. Please take a look at our menu.
 Can I see the stage from the bar?
Yes, all of our bar seats have a view of the stage.
 Can I order dinner at the bar?
Yes, you may order food if you are seated at the bar.
 What is parking like?
There is plenty of street parking (with no meters) available near Blue Strawberry. 
 What is your dress code?
We do not enforce a dress code. Business casual attire is typical.
 Where should I stay if I am coming from out of town to see a show?
We have a special rate with Home2 Suites by Hilton which is less than a 5-minute drive from our venue. Get your booking with the special rate.
 Is your venue 21+?
We are an all ages venue. Children are allowed but some performances may not be appropriate for young children due to content concerns. If there is an age restriction for a show, it will be clearly outlined in the show description on our website.
 What if I need disability seating?
Our venue is wheelchair accessible. Please inform us of your specific needs in the comments section of the checkout process online.

Private Events
Blue Strawberry is an intimate, unique and versatile space located in the Central West End of St. Louis. It is the perfect place to host your next birthday bash, company get-together, fundraiser, rehearsal dinner, micro wedding, or a private music performance. Event Inquiry Form
What We Offer 
 A Versatile Space: 
From intimate gatherings of 30 guests to larger parties of up to 100 guests, our venue can be tailored to fit your unique needs. Our venue can be set up in a number of ways, with tables and chairs for all your guests or a more open space with room for people to mingle. We have a small stage equipped with lights and sound where musicians can perform or ceremonies can take place. We also have a full bar area. 
 In-House Catering: 
All of our catering is done in-house by our talented chefs. Choose from a spread of heavy appetizers, a full buffet, or a sit-down dinner. 
 Customizable Beverage Packages: 
Whether you prefer a cash bar, an open bar, or a tailored consumption bar, our skilled bartenders will keep the drinks flowing and the spirits high. 
 Personalized Service: 
From the moment you walk through our doors until the last person leaves, our friendly team is here to ensure your event runs smoothly. We will take care of the details so you can focus on making memories.
