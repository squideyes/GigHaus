Concerts & Events
Our concerts include international, national, and local musicians who perform on the two intimate listening room stages in our venue and at locations throughout the Denver metro area.
Musical genres produced on our stages include Americana and roots, as well as singer-songwriters, world music, rock, bluegrass, country, jazz, blues, and more.
We also host a variety of free community events such as a monthly open mic and a vinyl record listening club.

Go from total beginner to expert with classes for every step of your journey, all taught by actively performing experts who are among the best musicians in the Front Range!
Core Group Classes
These classes teach you the basics of an instrument or singing – whether you’ve never picked one up or used to play a little bit. 
Core classes usually progress through 8 classes from 1-A to 1-B, all the way up to 4-B.

 Kyle Loeffler 
See Kyle’s current classes and private lessons here.
Originally from Northbrook, Illinois, Kyle has been a part of the Denver music scene since 2018. While in school, he studied under local greats like Gonzalo Teppa, Greg Garrison, and Ron Bland. After graduating from MSU Denver with a BA in History, he worked for Imagination Library Colorado, the local affiliate for Dolly Parton’s book gifting program. There, he found a passion for community building and education. 
Now, a full time musician, Kyle joins the Swallow Hill team offering Upright Bass lessons and co-teaching the 70’s Ensemble with his mortal enemy, Caleb Schwing. Outside of work, you can find Kyle reading, painting, or hiking with his dog, Olive.
First concert? U2 at Soldier Field
What’s a song you could listen to all day long? Sailing – Christopher Cross
Favorite Colorado hangout? Wizard’s Chest

 Neva Wilder 
Fiddle/Violin, Voice, Guitar, Songwriting
Neva is from Fairbanks, Alaska and moved around a lot during the first years of her musical journey in classical violin before ending up in Colorado. After discovering alternative styles like Texas fiddle, old-time, Celtic, bluegrass, and jazz, music making and songwriting became a clear passion and direction in her life. This expanded into teaching, where she focuses on self-expression and having fun while building skills in ear training, chord progressions, communication, and arrangements across instruments. 
Outside of music, Neva works in graphic design and illustration, enjoys snowboarding, and loves cooking and trying new foods.
First concert? Weird Al
What’s a song you could listen to all day long? “Day After Day” by Badfinger
Favorite Colorado hangout? Red Rocks Ampitheater
What’s your favorite thing about Swallow Hill? The welcoming community and mission to try new things and connect through music.

 Sonia Dominguez 
Music Therapy
Sonia is originally from Boulder, CO. She attended school at Colorado State university where she attained her Bachelor’s degree in Music Therapy. Music has always played a huge role in Sonia’s life, so fostering a community with other like minded musicians has always been something she has sought out. She is a trauma informed, humanistic, and neurologic informed clinician. She has made great strides to be where she is today using niche evidence-based skills, knowledge, and experiences to support clients to achieve cohesive goals.
She is currently taking over music therapy contracts with the VA and hopes to expand to offer some individual sessions at Swallow Hill Music.
When Sonia is not at work you can find her rock climbing, writing music, or traveling the world. 
First concert? Demi Lovato and David Archuleta
What’s a song you could listen to all day long? Stupid Deep (Acoustic version) by Jon Bellion
Favorite Colorado hangout? Postino Wine bar

 Judy Porter 
Bass, Ukulele, Voice, Piano, Flute
Judy Porter is both a performer and passionate educator with a dynamic career that spans the stage, studio, and classroom. With a degree in Musical Theatre Performance from the College of Santa Fe, Judy launched into professional theatre as a proud member of Actors’ Equity, performing on stages across the country.
Her music career has taken her coast to coast—and beyond. She’s toured the world with The California Girls, lighting up stages on USO tours and even opening for legends like The Beach Boys. She’s also shared bills with rock favorites Hinder and Puddle of Mudd with her band The Hits, and opened for Cindy Wilson of the B-52s with Battle Pussy. Whether she’s rocking out on bass, bringing melodies to life on flute and keys, or delivering powerhouse vocals, Judy brings unstoppable energy to every performance.
Currently, Judy brings her versatile talents to several bands: she sings lead and plays keys and flute with Pretty in Punk, rocks bass, ukulele, and lead vocals with Mermaid and the Magpie, and lays down bass and harmonies with The Battle Pussy Band.
As a teacher, Judy combines her real-world experience with a joyful, encouraging approach that blends music theory, technique, and fun. “What I love most about teaching,” she says, “is sharing the thing that brings me immeasurable joy. Watching my students grow and light up when they learn something new is the absolute best.”
First concert? Brian Setzer

Music School Policies
Photo/Video Release Policy
By registering for a class or lesson, you give permission for any image of the Participant to appear in photographs, electronic images, video, or audio used for promotional or editorial purposes by Swallow Hill Music or our partner organizations, at any time in any place.
If you would like to not be included in promotional materials, please email info@swallowhillmusic.org to arrange this.
Code of Conduct
Please be kind, courteous, patient, and respectful. Swallow Hill Music is committed to providing a professional, friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone (staff, students, volunteers, and patrons) regardless of age, gender, sexual orientation, disability, race, ethnicity, or religion. 
Intimidating, harassing, abusive, discriminatory, derogatory, or demeaning speech or actions will not be tolerated.
Cancellation Policies
Group Classes
If a class is cancelled by Swallow Hill Music, students will receive a full refund.
Students may receive a full refund if the class is dropped before the start date.
Students may drop a 6-8 week class and receive a partial, pro-rated credit or refund if the class is dropped at least 72 hours before the start of the 3rd class. After this deadline, students cannot drop and the credit/refund option is no longer available.
Students may drop a 4-5 week class and receive a partial, pro-rated refund if the class is dropped at least 72 hours before the start of the 2nd class. After this deadline, students cannot drop and will not receive a refund.
Refunds are not available for missed classes within a session (whether or not the teacher and SHM are notified of the absence).
Workshops
If a workshop is cancelled by Swallow Hill Music, students will receive a full refund.
Students may receive a full refund if the workshop is dropped at least 24 hours before the start time. No-shows and cancellations within less than 24 hours will not receive a refund.
A la Carte Private Lessons
If an a la carte private lesson is cancelled by Swallow Hill Music, students will receive a full refund.
Students may cancel a private lesson at least 24 hours before the lesson to reschedule or receive an account credit. No-shows and cancellations within 24 hours will not receive a refund. Students who no-show two times in a row will be removed from future lessons and will need to contact our front desk to get scheduled again.
Recurring Private Lessons
If a recurring private lesson is cancelled by Swallow Hill Music, students will receive a full refund.
Students may cancel a private lesson at least 24 hours before the lesson to reschedule or receive a refund. No-shows and cancellations within less than 24 hours will not receive a refund.
If a student needs to cancel a lesson, that cancellation must be more than 24 hours in advance of the lesson to receive a refund. No-shows and cancellations within 24 hours will not receive a refund. Students who no-show two times in a row will be removed from future lessons and will need to contact our front desk to get scheduled again.
If you need to change your recurring private lesson time or day, give us a call. We’ll do our best to find another time and day that will work for you. This will be at the discretion of the teacher’s schedule and space availability.
To cancel your scheduled private lesson, please call us at 303-777-1003 or email info@swallowhillmusic.org. You are guaranteed a refund if you give us 24 hours notice on your cancellation. If it is the day of your lesson and you are sick or are experiencing a personal emergency, you may still receive a refund if you let us know about these circumstances while canceling your lesson. Refunds for lessons canceled with less than 24 hours notice are given on a case-by-case basis.
If you cancel three recurring lessons in a row, our staff will reach out to discuss your recurring private lesson schedule. We reserve the right to cancel your lessons if you miss three or more consecutive lessons, out of fairness to our students and our instructors. 
If you have extenuating circumstances that will result in an extended absence, please let us know as far in advance. In certain circumstances, we can work something out.
Additionally, students that are going to miss a month’s worth of lessons but don’t want to lose their spot will still keep one or two (depending on lesson cadence) lessons booked in order to retain their recurring lesson slot.
Online Classes and Lessons
All classes and lessons designated as “online” are given on the Zoom platform. Users must download Zoom to be connected with their Instructor at https://zoom.us. If you have any questions, please email info@swallowhillmusic.org.
Online group classes must meet minimum enrollment to run. You will receive a notification the day before if the class is cancelled.
A separate email with the Zoom link for your class will be emailed to you the week of your class. 
Recurring Private Lesson Zoom links are the same for each week. If you need the link to be resent, please email info@swallowhillmusic.org.
Please add info@swallowhillmusic.org and no-reply@configio.com (our registration system) to your accepted sender list to make sure you don’t miss a message from us!
If you don’t receive the Zoom information, please check your Promotions or Spam folder.
Gift Certificates
Gift Certificates are non-refundable.
Gift certificates can be used toward group classes, workshops, and private lessons. Concert tickets are excluded.
Gift certificates are valid for 5 years from date of purchase.
School Support Fee
This fee helps us continue to provide high-quality music classes by covering essential non-class time hours for teachers including planning time and curriculum development, as well as essential admin support and building operations for the school. As a nonprofit, we’ve been absorbing many school-related operating costs and this fee lets us sustain those investments so your class experience remains excellent.
The School Support Fee is $15 for any group class that meets 5 or more times and is included in the itemized breakdown of the class costs when you check out of Configio. 
The School Support Fee is fully refundable up until the class starts. 
The School Support Fee is non-refundable if students drop the class after the class starts.

Music Therapy
Swallow Hill’s Music Therapy programming is facilitated by Board Certified Music Therapists, who use evidence-based music programming and interventions at Swallow Hill and across Denver to support specific physical, emotional, cognitive or social needs.
 sign up for a program
Swallow Hill’s music therapists use music as the tool to help improve people’s lives. We take pride in meeting the needs of a wide variety of individuals who want to learn and experience music including (but not limited to):
Individuals with Sensory Processing Disorders or Autism Spectrum Disorders
Individuals with Developmental Disabilities
Adults living with Dementia and Alzheimer’s disease
Individuals dealing with Trauma or Mental Health issues
What Is Music Therapy?
As defined by the American Music Therapy Association, music therapy is “…an established healthcare profession that uses music to address physical, emotional, cognitive, and social needs of individuals of all ages.” Music therapy is administered by trained professionals who have been educated at an approved music therapy program and nationally certified by the Certification Board for Music Therapy (CBMT). Music therapists possess musical, clinical, therapeutic, and professional skills.
Music Therapy Programs at Swallow Hill
Musical Memories
Join one of our Music Therapists to sing and reminisce together in this free one hour program. In partnership with the Colorado Chapter of Alzheimer’s Association, Musical Memories is for individuals experiencing memory loss and their families and caregivers. We’ll utilize music to increase overall brain functioning and encourage emotional awareness.
Musical Memories takes place the 1st Friday of every month, 10-11 am MT.
 register for free here
Musical Pathways
These free drop-in classes with one of our Music Therapists are designed for adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD). Music can help build new friendships, evoke joy and regulate the nervous system, strengthen neural pathways, as well as support physical movement. 
Musical Pathways takes place the 1st Friday of every month from 11 am – 12 pm MT.
 register for free here
Partner Organizations
In addition to private sessions, Swallows Hill works with many partners to provide onsite programming including Developmental Pathways, Rocky Mountain Region VA Medical Center, the Alzheimer’s Association and various memory care centers.
Please contact us at info@swallowhillmusic.org for more information.
