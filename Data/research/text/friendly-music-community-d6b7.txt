Music Classes 
Our Mission
The Friendly Music School (501c3) is a musical community that strives to inspire, bring together, and develop musicians of all ages and levels through our unique instructional approach. Our beginner, intermediate and advanced classes are led by professional musicians who guide students at their own pace with attention to individual musical interests. We are a community based organization that is all inclusive, and we strive to make music affordable to all, as well as financially rewarding to our mentors. We provide an environment for all musicians to learn from one another, play together and develop their skills. We encourage our musical family to consider the Friendly Music School a home where everyone is supported and welcome. We especially encourage our youth to choose music and the arts as a recreational outlet.The Friendly Music School charges less for lessons than any Chicagoland independent school while paying our instructors more than any school in the area.

The Early Notes: Our Foundation
The seeds of the Friendly Music Community were sown in 2007 with a simple yet powerful vision: to make music accessible and enjoyable for everyone. The Friendly Music Community gradually blossomed into a multifaceted experience destination driven by a deep-seated belief in the transformative power of music, art, mindfulness, and … well … coffee. Even in our early days, the focus is on the joy of creation and the connections forged through shared experiences within the Oak Park and Berwyn communities.
Growing the Ensemble:
Expanding Our Reach
Responding to the diverse needs and interests of our community, we expanded beyond live events and music lessons to encompass a wider spectrum of communal, multimedia exploration. The Friendly Tap began as a vital space for live music and community gathering, becoming a beloved fixture of Berwyn nightlife. The Friendly School Of Folk Music expansion reflected our commitment to being a comprehensive musical resource to the community. The Friendly Community Room adds art openings, children’s workshops, mindfulness and yoga sessions, and intimate live performances. And our Friendly Lodging rentals provide comfortable overnight stays for travelers and locals to enjoy in the Oak Park and Berwyn area.
More Than Just Music:
Embracing Holistic Well-being
Recognizing the profound connection between music, art, and overall well-being, the Friendly Music Community has evolved to embrace a more holistic approach. The recent addition of classes and workshops in yoga, meditation, and mindfulness underscores our commitment to nurturing the mind, body, and spirit alongside artistic talents. We believe that these practices complement and enhance the creative process, fostering a greater sense of presence, focus, and inner harmony for our community members and guests. 
Our Dedicated Instructors:
The Heart of Our Community
At the heart of the Friendly Music Community are our passionate and dedicated instructors. Our team embodies a wealth of musical knowledge and a genuine love for teaching. Many are working musicians with years of experience performing on stages both near and far, bringing real-world insights and inspiration to their lessons. They are more than just teachers; they are mentors who listen to your individual goals, tailor their approach to your unique learning style, and guide you on your personal musical journey within the supportive environment.

The Friendly Tap/Bar: Where Music and Community Meet
The Friendly Tap stands as a testament to our belief in the power of shared experiences. Established in 1968 and renewed in 2007, this intimate Berwyn venue has become a cherished neighborhood hub – a place where the energy of live music mingles with the warmth of conversation and camaraderie. It's a crossroads where local people come together to enjoy a diverse range of musical performances, from jazz and blues to rock and folk (and beyond), all while enjoying expertly crafted cocktails in a welcoming atmosphere.
Friendly Lodging: Your Home Away From Home in Berwyn
Extending our commitment to hospitality and community connection, Friendly Lodging offers a unique way to experience Berwyn, Oak Park, River Forest, Forest Park, and the “Veltway” (our beloved homestead on the Roosevelt Road corridor). Our four eclectic and artfully appointed short-term rental apartments provide a comfortable and inspiring home base for visitors and locals alike. Whether you're in town for a music event, exploring the vibrant Chicagoland area, or simply seeking a unique getaway, Friendly Lodging offers a thoughtfully curated alternative to traditional accommodations. Ask about booking Friendly Lodging for private events, too! By booking directly through our portal, you gain access to the best availability and a truly local experience within the Friendly Music Community.
Book Your Stay 
Friendly Coffee Lounge: Berwyn's Best Cuppa Joe And More
The Friendly Coffee Lounge is lauded for its welcoming atmosphere, often described as a cozy and comfortable "third place" for the community. Reviewers consistently highlight the quality of their coffee offerings, from rich espressos to satisfying lattes, and appreciate the selection of freshly baked goods. Beyond the beverages and bites, the lounge garners praise for its friendly and attentive staff who contribute to a positive experience. Many also appreciate its multi-dimensional nature, transforming into a vibrant bar with live music in the evenings or hosting private events with a giant projector screen for watch parties, fostering a strong sense of local community.
Coffee Lounge Menu 
Friendly Community Room: A Flex Space For Art, Crafts, Mindfulness, And Beyond
There's a newest chord struck in the Friendly Music Community's ever-evolving songbook, and it's called the Friendly Community Room. This is a welcoming, flexible space with welcoming vibes, ready to soak up whatever energy walks through the front door. It’s where art hangs on the walls and openings hum with vivid conversations, where folks stretch out with yoga, where quietude pervades with mindfulness meditation sessions, and where the little ones kick out their jams. You'll enjoy our Craft Fair buzzing with local makers. And sometimes the lights dim, and you find yourself immersed in an intimate musical experience – the kind that seep right into your soul. The Community Room is perfect for private events with a giant projector screen and built-in sound system; from celebrating milestones with family and friends to company offsites and more. 
Commitment to Our Community
The Friendly Music Community is deeply rooted in the Chicagoland Western Suburbs. We are committed to fostering a vibrant and inclusive environment where creativity flourishes, connections are made, and the joy of music and community is shared by all.
Looking to the Future
As we look ahead, our vision remains clear: to continue to grow as a vital resource for music education, performance, well-being, and community building in the Oak Park and Berwyn area. We are excited to embrace new opportunities, expand our programs, and further strengthen the bonds that make our community so special.
Get Involved
Whether you're looking to learn an instrument, catch a live show, find a unique place to stay, or even lend a hand as a volunteer, we invite you to become a part of the Friendly Music Community family. Explore our website, visit us on Roosevelt Road in Berwyn, and discover how you can connect with the coolest place around!

Our Story:
Building Harmony and Community in Oak Park & Berwyn
Setting the Stage
From its humble beginnings to its current vibrant form, the Friendly Music Community has been more than just a place to learn an instrument or catch a live show. Nestled in the heart of the Oak Park and Berwyn area, we've grown into a true neighborhood hub – a crossroads where the universal language of music intertwines with the warmth of human connection. Our journey is one built on a passion for fostering creativity, nurturing artistic growth, and cultivating a strong sense of belonging for individuals of all ages and backgrounds. Step inside and discover the story behind the Friendly Music Community, a place where harmony extends beyond the notes we play.
