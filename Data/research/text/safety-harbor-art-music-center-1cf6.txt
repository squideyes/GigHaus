Located in the Greater Tampa Bay Area of Florida, our mission is to promote knowledge of and education in the fine arts, the visual arts, and the performing arts. We’re powered by volunteers and deeply grateful to everyone who has contributed to SHAMc!
Cultural Equity Statement 
To support a full creative life for all, Safety Harbor Art & Music Center commits to championing policies and practices of cultural equity that empower a just and equitable community.
AI Statement
As an arts organization, we do not support using generative AI to create visual images. We believe that the act of creativity is a sacred process, and we prefer to support human artists in their creative endeavors. Artwork is work!

Who's played the SHAMc stage before?
Billy Strings, Larkin Poe, Fantastic Negrito, Robin Zander, Otis Taylor, The Wood Brothers, Ruthie Foster, Birds of Chicago, Rising Appalachia, Tim Reynolds, Magic Giant, Sister Hazel, Shawn Mullins, Bobby Rush, Delta Rae, Paul Thorn, Blood Brothers, War & Treaty, Steep Canyon Rangers, and so many more!
Mystery Concerts at SHAMc
Love live music and a good surprise? Mystery Concerts are exactly what they sound like: you buy your ticket without knowing who’s performing!
We choose an artist or band that is either well-known or we think is worth discovering, but their identity stays under wraps until the night of the show. You won’t know the genre, the lineup, or what to expect- you just have to trust us.
Mystery Concerts are a chance to take a musical leap, discover something new, and experience a one-of-a-kind night at SHAMc. Come curious. Leave surprised!

A NIGHT OF STORYTELLING
Hosted by Walt Belcher - First Wednesdays
Sign-in 6:30pm/Show Starts at 7pm
Join us on the 1st Wednesday of the month for an evening of storytelling with Tampa Bay's own Walt Belcher. Share a 5 to 7 minute story, or simply enjoy as an audience member. All ages are welcome, and a $2 donation is suggested at the door. Beverages are available for purchase, bring your own food (optional).
Learn More
DRUM & DANCE CIRCLE
Hosted by Jeff K - Second Wednesdays
7pm to 9pm
Bring your instruments and an open heart! Chairs are provided. Beverages are available for purchase, bring your own food (optional). Donations are welcome!
Learn More
All Things
Metaphysical CHAT
Second Saturdays
1-3 pm
Starseeds, join our monthly gathering where like-minded souls explore metaphysical theories and experiences in a non-judgmental environment—bring your own seating and refreshments, with a $5 gratitude donation appreciated.

OPEN MIC NIGHT
Hosted by Ed Derkevics with Ben & Mike
- Third Wednesdays
Sign-in 6:30pm/Show Starts at 7pm
Now in its 10th year, Safety Harbor Writers & Poets Open Mic Night at SHAMc is a lively monthly event where artists of all kinds gather to share their talents. Everyone gets 7 minutes at the mic, and all ages are welcome; a $2 donation is suggested at the door, with beverages available for purchase and optional BYO food.

ARTstart
Led by Sharon Tanner
Ages 4 to 6, First & Third Mondays
4 to 5 pm - $25 for entire month or $10 per session
Introduce children ages 4–7 to creativity, self-awareness, and self-expression through themed weeks in literacy, mindfulness, & music.
Learn More
TWEEN ART SCENE
Led by Echo Sheppard & Siobhan Nehin
Ages 8-12, Second & Fourth Mondays
4 to 5:30pm - $25 for entire month or $15 per session
This program is designed for young artists to deepen students' engagement with the arts while encouraging creativity, exploration, and artistic growth.
Learn More
TEEN ART NIGHTS
EVERY WEDNESDAY
5 to 7pm - Ages 12 to 17 - FREE
Ready to paint your peace, collage your confidence, and doodle out the drama? Join us at SHAMc for an art + wellness adventure made just for YOU. Presented in Partnership with Speak Up: Let’s Talk About Mental Health Non Profit Snacks included. No reservation required. Bring a friend!
Learn More
SENIOR DREAMERS
Hosted by Sharon Tanner
Sept 5, Oct 3, Nov 7
10 am to Noon - Ages 55+ - FREE
​
Our Senior Dreamers Program invites seniors and their families to join us each month for a joyful, hands-on experience in the arts. We'll work together on a community project each session. This is a great way to make new friends, get creative, and do something impactful for Safety Harbor!
Learn More
OPEN STUDIO CERAMICS
with Natty Moss Bond
Some Tuesdays, 1 to 8:30pm
$15 per hour (incl. support and supplies)

*Prerequisite for Open Studio Ceramics: Natty's Ceramics Hand Building Workshop OR previous experience with ceramics. Have access to a workspace, supplies, a kiln, and support from master ceramics artist, Natty Moss Bond, while connecting and creating with others. No RSVP required, just drop in at your leisure!
