About The Tin Pan
Tin Pan Alley was a time and a place in American musical history —literally an alley in Manhattan between the late 1800s into the 20th century, but also a defining moment when popular music original to the United States found its foothold.
We decided to name our restaurant and music venue “The Tin Pan” to celebrate the American music industry as a whole and to highlight the concentration of diverse talent springing up right here at home in Richmond, VA. That’s why our performance stage hosts top-level touring acts alongside crowd favorites from down the street.

 Tickets and Seating
How do I buy tickets to a show?
You can buy tickets online through our website. You may also give us a call at 804-447-8189 to have us process your tickets through our box office, or visit in person Tues-Sat 12PM-5PM. If you leave a voicemail, we will return your call the next time the box office is open. There are lower fees associated with calling us versus buying tickets online.
How do I make a dinner reservation?
You will be asked to choose a dinner reservation time when you purchase your tickets. Tickets and dinner reservations are one and the same; we do not generally offer advance tickets without a dinner reservation, and it is not possible to make a dinner reservation without also purchasing a ticket.
Do I have to make a dinner reservation?
As a general rule, yes. We are a “supper club,” which means that our entrance tickets are sold along with a dinner reservation. A food or beverage minimum of $15 applies to all tickets/reservations. If you choose not to order $15 worth of food and/or beverage, we reserve the right to charge a service fee in the amount of the difference.
Do I have to print out my tickets?
The Tin Pan is a paperless ticketing venue. Bring a valid ID with you and we will be able to check you in promptly.
How does seating work?
When you purchase a ticket online or at our box office, you are guaranteed a seat at one of our tables. When you purchase tickets, you can request a dinner reservation at a certain time with your show.
Priority is given to customers who purchase early and choose to dine with us. Seats are assigned in the order in which you purchase them. The sooner you buy tickets, the closer we seat you to the stage. When a show gets announced, the first person to purchase a ticket will have the best seat. Your reservation time does not effect your seating location. Dinner reservations are subject to a $15 minimum food and beverage purchase. If you choose not to order $15 worth of food and/or beverage, we reserve the right to charge a house charge to in the amount of the difference.
If I show up late, will I not get a seat?
There is never any risk of not having a seat because we never oversell the venue. For sold out shows, we may seat smaller parties together, as most of our tables are designed for a party of four.
How can I sit with my friends?
If you buy more than one ticket in a single transaction, we automatically seat your entire party together. If multiple groups are buying tickets separately and want to be seated together, please first purchase your tickets, then email us at tickets@tinpanrva.com with the order numbers of all parties who wish to sit together.
If I reserve an early seating, will I have to give up my seat later in the evening?
Absolutely not! Once you sit down at your table, that seat is yours for the rest of the evening.
Can I request a specific table?
Since we make our final seating chart the day of a show, we cannot guarantee any specific table. However, you are always welcome to make special requests. We will do the best we can to accommodate your request while maintaining our priority seating protocols. If a seating request is due to a medical condition or disability, please let us know. We are fully ADA compliant.
 About the Venue
How do I get to The Tin Pan?
We’re located at 8982 Quioccasin Road, Henrico VA 23229 — in the West end of Richmond, VA. Look for signs labeled “Quioccasin Station” shopping center.
Do you have parking?
We have our own parking lot with ample parking. There’s also additional parking on the side of the building and around back. Seriously, parking shouldn’t be an issue.
Do you have an age restriction?
We are a family-friendly, smoke-free restaurant and music venue. All ages are welcome. With that in mind, please understand that we might ask to see your ID—take it as a compliment!
Can I bring the kids/a nursing infant?
We are 100% family-friendly, but we do ask that all our guests respect both the performers and their fellow guests. If you have to quiet a child or take an emergency phone call, we have a private room that you can duck into.
Do you have a dress code?
We’re casual, so you should feel free to dress casual too 🙂
Do you offer gift cards?
Yes, we offer gift cards valid toward food, drink, or ticket purchases. Read more about gift cards.
Are you accessible?
Yes, we are ADA-compliant and can accommodate wheelchairs and service animals. Accessible restrooms are available. Please let us know in advance if you require special considerations, seating, etc. via tickets@tinpanrva.com
I’m coming from out of town. What hotels do you recommend?
The Tin Pan does not have any hotels within walking distance. We are located in the West End near Regency Square Mall and in the neighborhood of the University of Richmond, so finding the most convenient place to stay is best suggested by the direction you are coming from.
Read more about hotels we work with.
What kind of music do you feature?
There are so many different types of great music out there, we try to highlight them all. We feature “any music you’d feel comfortable having dinner with.” That might mean folk, bluegrass, jazz, or even a string quartet. We like to bring in a mix of up-and-coming singer-songwriters alongside touring regional and national stars. see our full calendar
Is there a ‘no-talking’ policy?
We’re not that stuffy! However, we definitely want to respect our musicians. The level of conversation really depends on the act and the evening. Some acts go better with a side of conversation, others shine alone. Be sure to take a look at our calendar to get a feel for what each evening will feature.
Do you allow dancing?
Generally speaking, we are not a dancing venue; there are no “standing room” tickets allowed at The Tin Pan and no space for dancing on busy nights. From time to time, we do make a small dance floor to accommodate specific bands, and dancing is allowed for private events. Please contact us to inquire whether dancing will be permitted during a specific event.
Can you seat large groups/parties?
Absolutely. We can easily move tables around to seat up to 8 people at a table. Larger parties can be seated at multiple tables in the same area. Just to be safe, though, please give us a call at 804-447-8189 ahead of time.
Do you do private events?
Yes, we do! Our performance stage and our professional audio/visual system is great for corporate presentations and special events. Read more about private events.
Are you hiring?
We’re always excited to work with enthusiastic staff. Please call us or drop by to inquire about currently open positions.
 About Food and Drink
Do you offer a full menu?
Yes! We offer a seasonal menu and fabulous rotating specials.
Can I bring my own bottle of wine?
Yes, The Tin Pan reserves the right to charge a $20 corkage fee for serving bottles of wine purchased from off premises.
Why is a 20% gratuity automatically included with all food and beverage purchases?
A fair percentage of our patrons have been leaving the standard 20% gratuity on their check. However, this has not been consistent.
Since we believe that we have talented and dedicated service staff, we felt the need to institute a minimum service charge to ensure they always earn a consistent living wage.
Since we are music venue and do not operate like a standard restaurant, our staff already has to accept inconsistent shifts and one seating per night. Therefore, we are doing our best to help stabilize their pay on a nightly basis.
Does food service continue during the show?
Yes! We continue to serve food and beverages throughout a ticketed performance. However, we deeply respect our musicians and do our best not to disrupt their performances. The full menu is available until one hour after the performance begins. The bar remains open and we will still be serving deserts after that time.

Pricing
In order to book an event with The Tin Pan, you simply need to meet a “food and beverage minimum.” That is, if you or your guests spend a minimum amount on food or at the bar, there is not additional venue or rental fee.
Our general minimums are as follows:
Daytime (any day ending before 4PM): minimum spend of $1,500
Evenings (Sun-Thu): minimum spend of $3,500
Evenings (Fri/Sat): minimum spend of $5,500.
All prices are +10% tax and +20% Gratuity
This price is inclusive of the venue, staffing, setup, cleanup, tables, chairs, flatware, and glassware.
Production management for live music or any use of our in-house PA system requires an additional fee. We do not book bands directly for private events, but we are happy to work with any band you’ve booked for your event.

Private Event Policies
The Tin Pan requires a 33% deposit of an event estimate in order to hold a date.
Events cancelled or moved less than 30 days prior to the event date will forfeit the full deposit.
Events cancelled or moved less than 7 days prior to the event date will be charged 50% of the event estimate.
Events cancelled or moved on the event date will be charged the full event estimate.
A final guest count is requested no later than 1 week before the event.
Significant increases in guest count within 72 hours may result in additional charges per guest.
A decrease in guest count within 72 hours of the event will still be subject to the final guest count.
State tax of 6%, Henrico meals tax of 4.3% and a 20% service charge will be applied to all private events.
Service charge includes event planning and coordination, hourly staff compensation and other costs associated with providing space for your event.
Frequently Asked Questions
The Tin Pan is a modern music and event venue located in the West End of Richmond, VA, near Regency Square Mall. The venue features live music — both national touring and local acts — and is also available for private and corporate events. We can accommodate groups of 40 to 150 for lunch or dinner, seven days a week.
Why choose The Tin Pan for your event?
There’s an abundance of event venues in Richmond, including hotels, restaurants, bars, theaters and museums. Most have a few great features, but it’s rare to find a venue that consistently hits every mark: stellar service, top-quality food, handcrafted cocktails and flawless event execution. The Tin Pan does it all.
Owners Patrick Zampetti and Kevin Liu personally oversee all events on-site to make sure everything goes smoothly. Because the Tin Pan is a live-music venue, we have a spacious stage and state-of-the-art sound, lighting and projection systems for use during your presentation, meeting or any special event. Our warm and modern décor perfectly complements any casual event, whether a big birthday party, holiday party, networking event or happy hour.
Looking for affordable party venues in Richmond, VA? The Tin Pan staff works hard to plan parties around your budget, We offer abundant menu options for your event, including light lunches, boxed lunches, hors d’oeuvres, plated dinners and buffets. For beverages, choose a cash bar, hosted bar or open bar with varying levels of service.
Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What kind of environment and service can I expect at The Tin Pan?
A: The Tin Pan is a casual, intimate restaurant. Staff are attentive and well educated in the food and beverages at The Tin Pan. Private events lean toward fine dining service in a more casual environment. Guests will feel comfortable in jeans and suits alike.
Q: Can I bring in an outside caterer?
A: Yes, though we do require all alcohol purchases to be made through our bar. Food & beverage minimums can be adjusted if an outside caterer is used.
Q: How do I reserve the space?
A: You can confirm the availability of a date by emailing Kevin at kevin@tinpanrva.com, or by using our booking form. If the date is available, we place a hold on the date upon receipt of a signed contract. The date is considered confirmed upon receipt of a 33% deposit.
Q: Can I reserve the entire restaurant for my event?
A: Yes, you can reserve the entire restaurant. The prices in this guide reflect a full venue rental. For partial rentals and semi-private events, please contact us directly.
Q: Can I host a networking social or client dinner at The Tin Pan?
A: Absolutely! Depending on the date in question, we’ve found that treating clients to dinner and a show make for a lasting favorable impression. Please contact us to schedule a time.
Q: What sorts of decorating options do I have?
A: We work with preferred vendors and are happy to provide tablecloths, centerpieces, custom lighting, etc.
Q: Is The Tin Pan accessible?
A: Yes. We are fully ADA-compliant.
Q: Will I be working with a dedicated event manager?
A: Yes. We are a small business, so you will be working with one of the owners directly and they will be on-site throughout your event.
