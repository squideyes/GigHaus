about us
In 2011, Laura & Lisa started a house concert series showcasing incredibly talented independent singer-songwriters; creating shows that are as much fun for the performers as they are for the audience.
The artists perform outside, perched above Bend, Oregon. These shows are by invitation only and space is extremely limited. Be sure to get on our Invitation List so that you receive the invite and can reserve your seats early by making your ticket donation. And yes, feel free to spread the word! We love seeing new faces at the concerts!
Questions? Check out our FAQs & Details section.

FAQs & Details
What are house concerts?
House concerts are private events in the homes (or in this case on the decks) of music fans. Friends, neighbors, and acquaintances are invited to attend a two-set performance. The performer is paid via your donation. It’s a fun, intimate way to enjoy music.
How do I get an invitation?
Since these house concerts are private events, you must join our invitation list in order to receive an invitation to the shows. Note, space is super limited and the shows will fill up fast, so when you receive your invitation, be sure to reserve your seat right away. 
How long are the concerts?
Usually, the artist performs two 45-minute sets and we have a 20-30 minute intermission.
Doors open one hour prior to showtime for you to enjoy food & beverages and socialize with your fellow music lovers.
Shows begin promptly at the starting time, so please do not arrive late.
How much is the ticket donation?
It can vary, but generally, the ticket donation is around $40 per person, made in advance to reserve your seat. 3% per ticket is added to cover the credit card fee. If you would rather pay via cash or check, contact us. If tickets are still available, we will hold them for 5 days so you can get us your donation in advance. 
Note: You may also want to bring cash to purchase artist merchandise.
Why is the ticket donation non-refundable?
For us to book nationally touring singer-songwriters, we have to provide a substantial guarantee. We base that on our capacity and cannot afford cancellations as that guarantee then comes out of our own pockets.
Where are these concerts? 
Our home is on Awbrey Butte, overlooking the city of Bend. Once you reserve your seat with your donation, you’ll receive the address.
Will there be food and drinks?
Yes! We’ll have snacks (an assortment of cheese, crackers, & various nibbles) and beverages out for you to enjoy. Doors open one hour prior to showtime for you to enjoy food & beverages and socialize with your fellow music lovers.
Shows begin promptly at the starting time, so please do not arrive late.
Can I bring children?
We love kids, however, in trying to keep this a “listening space” that is as fun for the artist as it is for the audience, only kids 15 years or older may attend.
Since it's outside, can I bring my dog?
No. Sorry, no pets.
Is there a dress code?
Casual! Remember, evenings in Bend get cool and the concerts are outside. Bring a jacket and warm hat … whatever you need to be comfortable if the temperature drops.
Are there opening acts?
No. There are no opening acts.
Can I perform at Juniper View House Concerts?
We host two shows per year, June through September. You are welcome to submit your music to concerts@juniperview.com for consideration. We are looking for exceptional singer-songwriters. It is not necessary that you have a following in Oregon as the shows generally fill regardless.
