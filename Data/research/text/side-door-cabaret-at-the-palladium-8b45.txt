 Music Center at St. Petersburg College Gibbs Campus 
The 2026-27 Palladium Chamber Series continues at the Music Center on SPC’s Gibbs Campus while the Palladium at SPC undergoes its historic renovation. This welcoming 300-seat recital hall offers wonderful acoustics, two Steinway pianos, an intimate audience experience perfect for chamber music, and ample free parking.
Location & Address
Music Center on SPC’s Gibbs Campus
6605 Fifth Avenue North
St. Petersburg, FL 33710
Music Center Seating Chart
View the seating layout for Palladium Chamber Series performances at the Music Center. Seating is reserved, and availability will vary by performance and subscription package. 
On a mobile device? Click or tap image below to view the full-size seating chart.
Planning Your Visit
Arrival and Doors
For all Palladium Chamber Series performances, the Music Center lobby, remote box office, and doors to the concert hall will open at 6 p.m. Performances begin at 7 p.m.
Remote Box Office and Will Call
A Palladium remote Box Office will be set up in the northwest corner of the Music Center lobby. Tickets held at Will Call will be available for pickup there.
Tickets may be purchased in advance online or by calling our box office at 727.822.3590. Tickets may also be purchased at our remote box office on the evening of the performance, subject to availability.
Parking
Free parking can be found in the SPC campus lots surrounding the Music Center. Accessible parking spaces are also available. Please see the campus map below for parking locations and additional details. 
Music Center detail map
Concessions
Complimenary water and coffee will be available pre-show at Palladium Chamber Series concerts. Outside food and beverages are not permitted inside the building.
About the Palladium Chamber Series
The Palladium Chamber Series brings internationally acclaimed soloists and chamber musicians to St. Petersburg for vibrant performances of the great classical repertoire.
For concert dates, artists, subscriptions and ticket information, visit the Palladium Chamber Series page.

WHY YOUR GIFT MATTERS
Your gift helps sustain the performances, artists, and programs that bring our community together. 
For more than 25 years, the Palladium at St. Petersburg College has been a home for live music, dance, and cultural experiences in the heart of St. Petersburg. 
Each year, the Palladium:
Presents more than 250 performances and events
Welcomes over 67,000 patrons
Supports more than 2,000 artists
Contributes an estimated $14 million to the local economy

About the Palladium at St. Petersburg College
The Palladium at St. Petersburg College is a historic performing arts venue in downtown St. Petersburg, Florida. Since opening as the Palladium Theater in 1998, it has brought artists and audiences together through music, dance, theatre, opera, and community programming. 
The Palladium at SPC is currently undergoing a major renovation and anticipates reopening in fall 2027. During this transformation, Palladium programs and its commitment to Tampa Bay artists and audiences continue beyond the walls of the building. 
Follow the Palladium’s Transformation
Contact Us
Explore the Palladium Chamber Series 
Located just blocks from St. Petersburg’s waterfront, the Palladium is part of downtown’s vibrant arts and cultural district. 
Video of the beautiful Palladium Theater in St. Petersburg, FL
The Palladium includes two entertainment spaces; Hough Hall and Side Door Cabaret.
Hough Hall, the Palladium’s mainstage concert hall, has welcomed classical music, jazz, blues, opera, theatre, dance, and community performances for more than 25 years. As part of the Palladium’s current renovation, Hough Hall is receiving new seating, improved sightlines, enhanced acoustics, updated production technology, and accessibility improvements – all while preserving the historic character of the space. 
The Palladium’s Side Door Cabaret is Tampa Bay’s critically acclaimed listening room, known for bringing audiences close to established and emerging artists in jazz, blues, comedy, acoustic music and more. Its small tables and nightclub-style setting creat a distinctive performance experience centered on the connection between artist and audience. 
The Palladium is a department of St. Petersburg College. We are committed to equal access/equal opportunity in our programs, activities, and employment. For additional information visit www.spcollege.edu/eaeo.
Mission
The Palladium fosters artistically and culturally diverse performing arts, educational and community programs that bring artists and audiences together in a first-class professional, affordable setting.
The Palladium reaches audiences throughout Tampa Bay and beyond by…
Presenting and producing a schedule of innovative arts performances and events,
Providing a full-service, affordable rental facility for artists, producers and community organizations,
Nurturing relationships through partnerships with established and emerging local artists and cultural organizations that use the Palladium as a venue for artistic growth,
Showcasing selected programs of St. Petersburg College and affording students myriad opportunities to work intimately with renowned touring professionals as well as with local artists.
History
The Palladium’s story begins in 1925, when the building was constructed as the First Church of Christ, Scientist. Designed by architect Howard Lovewell Cheney, the building includes significant Arts and Crafts details, including tilework by Ernest A. Batchelder. 
In the late 1990s, a group of arts philanthropists recognized the need for a professional, affordable venue that could serve local artists and community organizations. They transformed the church into a performing arts center, preserving much of its historic character, including the original seating. The Palladium Theater officially opened in 1998 with a mission to welcome audiences across Tampa Bay and serve the entire community. 
In 2007, the Palladium became part of St. Petersburg College. In accepting the property, the College recommitted to the Palladium’s community-focused mission while creating new opportunities to connect SPC students and programs with professional artists and live performance. As a department of St. Petersburg College, the Palladium has continued to grow as a vital part of Tampa Bay’s cultural life. 
Now, as the Palladium undergoes a major historic renovation, the building is being prepared for the next century. The transformation will preserve its distinctive architectural character and historical designation while creating more a comfortable, accessible, and acoustically enhanced experience for future generations of artists and audiences.

 Artists In Residence 
ARTISTS IN RESIDENCE
Inherent in the Palladium’s mission is its commitment to artists that promises to help them develop, flourish and grow. The Palladium has delivered on that promise to many, but especially to three programs for which the Palladium has been their artistic home from each Artists in Residence’s respective beginnings.
Palladium Chamber Players
This dynamic group of internationally renowned soloists and chamber musicians bring vibrant interpretations of the great classic classical repertoire. This core group of musicians, often joined by world class guest artists, is led by The Florida Orchestra Concertmaster Jeffrey Multer, and includes violist Danielle Farina, cellist Edward Arron, pianist Jeewon Park, cellist Julian Schwartz, and pianist Marika Bournaki.
BEACON Contemporary Dance
BEACON, the critically acclaimed platform for contemporary dance and multidisciplinary collaborations among artists working locally and nationally, makes its home here at the Palladium. BEACON Co-Producers Helen Hansen French and Lauren Slone have seen the growing circle of artists and supporters who work to create these unforgettable evenings of dance – an artistic form that prioritizes the live experience of physical connection.
