Welcome to The Livery in Benton Harbor
Step into The Livery, a Benton Harbor restaurant rooted in history and community. Located in a restored 1800s building, crafting our beer flights for the last 21 years. We serve hand-tossed pizzas, shareable plates, and fresh, made-to-order dishes in a warm, laid-back space. Enjoy a pet-friendly outdoor patio, live music, and a welcoming downtown atmosphere perfect for gathering with friends and family.

Frequently Asked Questions
What dishes are you best known for?
We are best known for our Livery Chili Bowl, Cheese Bread, Chicken Wings, Gluten Free Chicken Wings, The Main Stage Pizza, The Open Sesame Pizza, Tout That Sprout Pizza, Thai BBQ Chicken Pizza, The French Dip Sandwich, Turkey Bacon Avocado Sandwich, The Yardbird, Bob Cobb Salad, Build Your Own Mac And Cheese, Vainilla Ice Cream Sandwich, and more.
What type of cuisine do you serve?
We serve Big Game Specials, Snacks, Wings, Shareables, Pizzas, Sandwiches, Salads, Pastas, Craft Beer, Sodas, Juices, Ice Cream Sandwiches, and Mini Ice Cream Cups.
What services do you offer?
We are a Brewpub and offer Lunch, Dinner, Catering, Dessert, Bar Games, Fast Service, Great Beer Selection, Karaoke, Live Music, Live Performances, and Quiz Night for Outdoor Seating, Kerbside Pickup, Delivery, Pickup, Takeaway, Dine In, and Solo Dining.
What areas do you serve?
We service Downtown Benton Harbor, Benton Harbor Arts District, Millburg, Benton Heights, Fairplain, Shoreham, Sodus, Stevensville, St Joseph, Lake Michigan Shoreline, Silver Beach County Park, Pipestone Road, Main Street Benton Harbor, Berrien County, and surrounding areas.
