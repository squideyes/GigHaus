Welcome to Serenity Ledge...
Where Art Meets Nature
Serenity Ledge House Concerts is an exclusive performance series not advertised to the general public. Guests get to meet and sit several feet from incredible musicians in our living room situated on a picturesque, ten-acre ridge in Winter Harbor, Maine, above Frenchman Bay and Acadia National Park.
Our concerts are the perfect way to spend an evening and a GREAT GIFT IDEA!. Where else can you go in Downeast Maine, in the shoulder seasons of spring and autumn, to see stellar, up-close and personal performances with delicious foods to eat?
Check back often to see what's happening on the Ledge. We fill a niche by offering high calibre concerts during the shoulder seasons and look forward to having you as our guest at Serenity Ledge, where art meets nature.
Your hosts,
Gordon and Veronica Ward
Things to Know for 2026...
1. The concert address in Winter Harbor will be provided with your ticket confirmation.
2. All shows (except Sunday, March 29) are on Saturdays. Doors open at 5:00 p.m. Music begins at 6:00 p.m.
3. Bring a heavy hors d'oeuvre, enough to share with 6 or 7 people. Dessert, tea, and coffee are provided. Other beverages are BYOB.
4. There's time between and after the sets to chat with the performer, purchase merch, and make some new friends.

Music: Albums & Singles
“Your music touches my heart.” ~ Anne Chmielewski
"It comes as no surprise to me at all to discover that Gordon Thomas Ward is referred to as 'the total package' by his fans because he truly is that good. Gordon Thomas Ward is the quintessential voice for today as he carries on the legacy and embraces the torch when it comes to masterful singer/songwriter material." ~ Skope Magazine
My new, long-awaited record Walking on the Wire was released on June 14, 2024.
Believe me when I say it was worth the wait!
WALKING ON THE WIRE
ALBUM released June 14, 2024
See STORE for contents and purchase. 
Walking on the Wire includes musicians Noel Paul Stookey (from Peter, Paul, and Mary), Kevin Barry (James Taylor’s and Jackson Browne’s bands), and Eric Troyer (James Taylor, Billy Joel, Aerosmith, and ELO II). In addition, you'll hear Pat Wictor, Emily Mure, Ronnie Bouffard, Jud Caswell, Robby Coffin, Scott Elliott, William J. Hall, Andy Happel, Ben Hunsberger, Marilyn Krentzman, and April Reed-Cox.
“Much like [Gordon] Lightfoot in his heyday, Ward’s lyrics are so vivid that listeners will want to hang on to every turn of phrase in anticipation of the next. Walking on the Wire is an instant indie folk/AAA classic!”
~ Jonathan Widran, The JW Vibe: Music that Sticks to My Soul
Whispers from the Woods
ALBUM released September 1, 2022 
See STORE for contents and purchase. 
Balloted for a GRAMMY nomination! Whispers from the Woods charted #4 nationally, #3 in Maine, and was nominated by jurors worldwide for the 6th annual Blues & Roots Worldwide Album of the Year Award! "This is the fourth album for Gordon Thomas Ward, and I feel this is some of Gordon’s most inspiring and insightful work to date. The material on Whispers From The Woods is beyond well-written and simply showcases Gordon Thomas Ward as a top-tier singer/songwriter in this music industry. With elements of modern folk, rock, americana, country, and adult alternative, Gordon is able to capture your imagination and attention one note and lyric at a time. It’s also worth mentioning that Gordon was accompanied by many talented musicians during the making of Whispers From The Woods. The word is out, and the verdict is that Whispers From The Woods is a modern day masterpiece that comes straight from the heart - showing the music world what pure quality and human impact sounds like. ~ Skope
Providence
ALBUM released 2018
GRAMMY®-balloted
See STORE for contents and purchase.
Grammy-balloted Providence provides listeners with a comprehensive peek into what will likely end 2018 as one of the year’s finest singer/songwriter efforts… [Gordon's] musical DNA seamlessly incorporates modern folk rock, Americana, country, and adult alternative with acoustic and electric instrumentation weaving together for a spectacular effect… Ward shows he’s one of the best singer/songwriters working in the Americana/Indie scene today.” ~ Mindy McCall, No Depression: The Journal of Roots Music ~
“Providence promises more thrills, chills and introspective moments than any record I’ve heard or reviewed this year.” ~ Indie Band Guru
Eiderdown 
ALBUM released May 1, 2020
See STORE for contents and purchase.
Balloted for a GRAMMY nomination! "Eiderdown is comprised of sixteen, standout songs on a double album full of heart, truth and inspiration. With heavy elements of folk and contemporary folk rock, Gordon delivers an unforgettable performance. This multi-dimensional artist takes listeners on a lyrical journey on his new 2-Volume set called Eiderdown. Gordon brings descriptive stories to life on the new record and handles many roles on the new album including: lead vocals, harmony vocals, guitars, rhythm and a slew of other instruments and sound effects. There are also a number of talented musicians backing Gordon Thomas Ward on the sixteen tracks... I feel pure greatness was created here in the form of music. Eiderdown is a pure, musical work of art that supplies carefully constructed compositions that speak to different parts of our psyche. Gordon Thomas Ward is the quintessential voice for today as he carries on the legacy and embraces the torch when it comes to masterful singer/songwriter material." ~ Jimmy Rae, Skope

living room Concerts
Gordon's Living Room Concerts
Fully Acoustic Concerts Your Guests Will Love!
Do you enjoy live music and prefer a relaxed setting rather than a crowded venue full of strangers?
Do you have a space for guests to gather who are looking for something special, memorable, and unique?
I can perform a concert in your living room! It's a trend that's growing quickly, and for good reason! They're an EASY, fantastic, and personal way to experience a live and fully acoustic, musical performance with your friends and neighbors, often with very little, if any, preparation and cost to the host. There's really nothing quite like it!
Living Room Concerts can be held any day of the week and usually involve 2 sets of music and something to eat. A potluck or a couple of pizzas and your guests are all you need. A suggested attendance of about 20 people is suggested, with the upper limit being whatever your space can comfortably hold. The timing equates to a 2-3 hour event. I may require a meal, travel expenses, and overnight accommodations, depending on your location. To learn more about living room concerts in general, watch these short videos that explain house concerts for hosts and guests.

Children and Families
Mr. Harenten and the Serenity Ledge Picnic Presentation
Imagery, Puppets, Storytelling, and Live Music…50 Minutes
“Superb and engaging! Our sold-out, children's audience was transfixed!” ~ The Grand Theatre
“Absolutely wonderful and adorable! Perfect for families!” ~ Kathy P.
 Let your imagination soar with this delightful, multimedia presentation of the beloved children’s literature story Mr. Harenten and the Serenity Ledge Picnic. Author and award-winning songwriter Gordon Thomas Ward will transport you to a charmed woodland realm along the coast of Maine that is home to Mr. Harenten, a thoughtful and timid Snowshoe Hare, and an assortment of his animal friends.
 Mr. Harenten dreams up the idea of having a grand picnic with his friends. When he realizes he’s created a predicament, his solution relies on devising a plan to overcome his fear of gathering clover behind the dark and imposing Ledge House. Mr. Ward guides the audience through the story with the help of projected images, puppets, storytelling, and the performance of several original songs.
Ideal for grades K-5 children, family audiences, theatres, libraries, and school groups!
Music and History
An Evening with
Gordon Thomas Ward
inspiring adventures, engaging music, and spine-tingling tales
 Audiences have loved Gordon’s varied and engaging presentations, songs, and stories for years. This new program provides audiences with a cross section of some of the best of everything this Renaissance man has to offer from his story-songs, books, and other presentations. This entertaining, multimedia show is filled with live music, photos, stories, adventure, history, and ghostly tales presented by this accomplished singer-songwriter, author, podcast host, historian, and researcher. Plus, if you’re new to Gordon’s work, this is the best way to get a wonderful taste of what he has to offer. Something in this show is always new and is perfect for just about any venue. Its diverse content is sure to keep people entertained and leave them wanting more!
 “Gordon’s enthusiasm and engagement with the audience gave the evening the feel of a ‘private tour’ with Gordon’s many talents on display. His musical performance was particularly spot-on, providing both the narrative and evocative resonance of the past which only music, well performed, can draw forth from our collective heart.”
 ~ Dr. Jude Pfister, Local historian, author, and Chief of Cultural Resources at Morristown National Historical Park ~
Troubadour Tales
live music and stories
 Everyone loves good stories, and when they’re paired with music and song, they get even better! That's why audiences adore this captivating, live performance of stirring tales, told through music and spoken word that capture their attention and imagination. Entertaining anecdotes complement his acoustic, contemporary folk songs, which include haunting melodies, multi-instrumental accompaniment, evocative vocals, and poetic lyrics. Similar to a HOUSE CONCERT, listeners embark upon a captivating journey that may find them in the company of Revolutionary War soldiers from Maine and NJ, Robert Frost, the ghosts of a Headless Hessian or a woman on Schoodic Point, or transported to the historic Trail of Tears, the Civil War, the trail of Lewis and Clark, and other times and places! This presentation has been described by adults as "spellbinding," and, as a high school student remarked, "The people and events seem real, so I can relate to them." Audiences will find this program to be a real treat! Troubadour Tales is absolutely perfect for libraries, historical and community groups, or for an extraordinary house concert with a group of your friends!
 "Gordon Thomas Ward was amazing! Bravo! He has one of the best voices I've heard in a long time and well-tuned instruments to go with it. The way he weaves stories and historical events in his acoustic songs is truly an art form - it is very engaging. His ability as a raconteur and minstrel were both provocative and soothing at the same time. He totally amazed me!" 
 ~ Tom Martin, Director of Communications at Rutherfurd Hall ~
Retracing the
Lewis & Clark Trail
live music and adventure stories 
 This engaging program draws from the author’s book Life on the Shoulder and details his entire 1,800 mile journey by canoe and bicycle along the Missouri, Clearwater, Snake, and Columbia Rivers and by foot over the Bitterroot Mountains. Described as “like hitting the trail with Lewis and Clark” by the Mystic Seaport Museum, this presentation includes stunning, projected photographs and Gordon Ward’s personal anecdotes, which allow attendees to travel back through time, revisiting the places explored by Meriwether Lewis and William Clark and recorded in their journals and the pages of history as they searched for the Northwest Passage. One also embarks on a journey through the diverse environments of the current American West. Aside from the historical aspects of the topic, the presentation compares and contrasts the differences between a modern retracing and the original expedition two hundred years ago. In addition, audience members will also hear how the original journey was also very much a story about the strength and guidance of women and investigate the two journeys from psychological and philosophical perspectives.
 "You were witty, knowledgeable, energetic and engaging. You brought in the largest crowd that we have ever had, and you held them to the end, which is a tribute to the dynamics of your presentation.”
 ~ Karin Soderberg, Membership Program Manager, Mystic Seaport, The Museum of America and the Sea ~
