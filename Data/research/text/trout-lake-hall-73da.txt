Trout Lake Hall is a historic 120 year old building nestled in a quaint town at the foot of Mt. Adams. Set against a backdrop of green hills and golden fields, and framed by the majestic silhouette of Mt. Adams, our venue offers a serene and intimate setting for couples looking for a little country charm to celebrate their special day.
Our special place can be yours too…
 let's make it happen!
Good Times are what we do…
get hitched with mt. adams in view, cozy up with your guests around plates of home smoked bbq for dinner, and dance your boots off in the historic hall for a wedding that will not soon be forgotten! 
Our team of experienced event producers are excited to help you have a memorable time. 
see what we have to offer
 Vibes are right
We offer multiple entertaining areas and customizable set-ups to fit the vibe and size of your event. Feel like having a live band? Our stage, sound system, and lighting for our live shows is perfectly adaptable to bands, or an epic dj dance party. 
 Food you actually want to eat
Our buffet style menu of home smoked BBQ and delicious sides featuring local ingredients won't leave any guests hungry
 Flexible beverage options
Licensed bartenders on staff. Flexible beverage options for any budget. Full bar available: Local beer and wine, cocktails (ask about custom batched cocktails!), and all of your non-alcoholic classics
 In-house services
Day of Venue coordination is included in all bookings. Cross decor and flowers off of your to-do list by booking our in-house designer and talented florist who provides stunning arrangements featuring home grown and locally foraged florals that feel at home in our beautiful, naturalistic setting.

 About
Established on july 4th 1904, the building has been known by many names; thode’s hall, trout lake tourist club, doodle’s place, trout lake tavern, trout lake country inn . . . 
and now, Trout lake Hall.
This beloved 120 year old building has brought joy to the community of Trout Lake in the form of a silent movie theater, hand-set bowling alley, dance hall, airplane hangar, dinner theater, inn, and more. 
Trout Lake Hall today is a bar and restaurant, live music venue, community space, and wedding venue, serving good food, good vibes, and good times.
we hope to see you soon!
