 Host Your Event at eTown Hall
 Housed inside a historic, solar-powered facility in downtown Boulder, eTown Hall offers three versatile rental spaces backed by world-class, professional production tech. From full-scale concerts and private celebrations in our 220-capacity main hall to intimate gatherings in the Café & Bar, our dedicated production staff handles every detail to make your event extraordinary.
 eTown Hall
 Concerts, corporate keynotes, galas, and film screenings 
 Cafe and Bar
 Cocktail receptions, networking mixers, acoustic sets, and private parties 
 Studio
 VIP listening parties, intimate acoustic showcases, and exclusive private receptions 
 Learn More 
 State-of-the-Art Recording Studio
 Designed by renowned acoustician Sam Berkow (SIA Acoustics), The Studio at eTown Hall is a premier audio production facility engineered for pristine sound. Featuring an Avid D-Command console, Pro Tools HD Native setup, premium preamps (API, Neve, Grace Design), and an exceptional microphone locker—plus access to our Kawai baby grand piano and live hall split—it’s an inspiring environment for record production, live tracking, post-production, and broadcast mixing.
 Record at Our Studio 
 Our Mission
 To educate, entertain and inspire a diverse audience through music and conversation in order to create a socially responsible and environmentally sustainable world.

 What is an eTown Episode?
 Every episode of eTown brings together live music, compelling conversations, and social impact in a dynamic, one-of-a-kind broadcast format:
 Two Featured Musical Artists
 Live performances and in-depth interviews with two world-class musicians.
 Interview Guest
 Conversations with authors, scientists, activists, and changemakers working to make a difference.
 Group Finale Performance
 A unique, collaborative musical finale featuring all the show’s guest artists performing together on stage.

 eTown Hall
 More Than Just a Music Venue
 eTown Hall is a home for the values and ideas that have helped make eTown thrive since 1991. It’s a place for music, certainly, but also so much more. It is our solar-powered clubhouse for music, ideas, and community. It's also a 220-seat venue, recording studio, and community space.
While it serves as the proud home base for eTown’s daily offices, it truly comes alive as a world-class live music venue, a state-of-the-art recording studio, and a welcoming community center where local and global voices converge.
 Sustainability in Action
Since 1991, eTown has been committed to environmental education and “doing the right thing.” Converted from an old church facility, this building is quite possibly the greenest music and media center in the United States!
It’s recycled from a former church and rebuilt through a total renovation from the ground up. It’s renovated with state-of-the-art sound systems and a full recording studio. It’s repurposed as a green building that makes its own solar power, uses extremely efficient heating and cooling, and features recycled materials throughout.

 History of eTown Hall
1918 The congregation of Boulder Church of the Nazarene purchases the lot at 16th and Spruce.
1922 The congregation hand excavates the basement, puts a roof over it, and begins to hold church services.
1925 The second floor of the rustic stone church building is completed and the Nazarenes begin holding services in the sanctuary.
1943 The congregation continues to renovate the church themselves during their personal free time. The renovations include the expansion of the façade, removal of the bell tower, completely covering the stone exterior with stucco, adding a steel truss roof and a loft for the choir.
1944 The renovations are completed, and the congregation resumes holding services in the newly expanded space.
1959 A gymnasium is added to the north of the church built of cement block.
1960
Volunteers build additional classrooms with masonry blocks, adding 6,550 sq. ft. of floor space to the existing structure.
1980 The building is sold to Unity Church.
1991 The building is sold to The Salvation Army.
1996 Boulder developer Stephen Tebo purchases the building.
2008 eTown purchases the building from Stephen Tebo and begins planning to renovate and transform the structure into eTown Hall.
2009 eTown embarks on a capital campaign to raise funds for the extensive renovation and restoration of the historic building.
2012 eTown Hall opens its doors to the Boulder community.
