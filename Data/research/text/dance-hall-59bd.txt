The Marc Chillemi Quintet brings the sound and spirit of 1960s jazz to the stage with a tribute to the legendary Blue Note Records. Led by trumpeter Marc Chillemi (Primo Cubano/Raging Brass), the group celebrates the soulful sound, hard-swinging grooves, and adventurous improvisation that defined an era of modern jazz.
Inspired by the music of iconic Blue Note artists and bandleaders like Horace Silver, Blue Mitchell, Lee Morgan and Art Blakey, the quintet blends tight arrangements with energetic interplay and spontaneous expression. Joining Marc are Jesse Beckett- Herbert (Future Mailmen) on tenor saxophone, Clifford Cameron (Rot on the Vine/Vinyl Quartet) on piano, Duane Edwards (Primo Cubano/Bess Jacques and the Strays) on bass, and veteran drummer Jan van Voorst van Beest (The Fiasco Brothers/Bess Jacques and the Strays).
Following a year-long residency at The Henry in Portland’s Old Port, the band is excited to make its debut at The Dance Hall. Whether you’re a lifelong jazz listener or simply curious to experience this timeless music live, expect an evening of rhythm, soul, and the unmistakable Blue Note sound—alive, expressive, and deeply human.

About the Hall
OUR MISSION
The Dance Hall’s mission is to build community by promoting, advancing, and sponsoring artistic endeavors, creative work, and education in music, dance, film, and the performing arts. We are dedicated to providing you with affordable, accessible, participatory, and intergenerational performing arts opportunities.
ABOUT THE DANCE HALL
The Dance Hall (TDH) is the Seacoast's premier venue for intimate, diverse music, dance, and performing arts experiences. Founded in 2010 by dancer and producer Drika Overton, TDH was created to inspire and engage people of all ages to experience the joy of music, movement, and dance. Weekends are booked with live performances and guest are frequently encouraged to hit the maplewood dance floor. A variety of dance classes for all levels are available midweek. The Dance Hall is located in the heart of Kittery Foreside in an historic 1928 Grange Hall, creating an authentic, vintage vibe with wonderful acoustics. Affordable ticket prices, a curated lineup of eclectic performances, and welcoming community make The Dance Hall, Kittery one of the most charming experiences north of Boston. 
“A gem. Open airy, cozy, charming. Dress it up or dress it down, so lucky to have this place in Downtown Kittery.” - Lynn Beck-Sullivan
STATEMENT OF BELONGING
We are passionate about providing access to the arts for all, and for the most part TDH events are family-friendly. When age restrictions and/or parental advisories are required, we will note this in the event description both on our website and on our ticketing platform. The Dance Hall’s Board and Staff are committed to building a culture of belonging that reflects all people–where folks of every age, race, national origin, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, ability, and religion feel welcome, seen, heard, and supported. We also respectfully acknowledge that The Dance Hall is located on N’dakinna, which is the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory and homeland of the Abenaki, Pennacook, and Wabanaki peoples past and present. We honor the enduring relationship between Indigenous Peoples and this land, and commit to meaningful reconciliation.
The Dance Hall is a 501(c)3 tax exempt, fiscally responsible not-for-profit organization, managed by a volunteer Board of Directors and a professional staff. All contributions are tax deductible to the full extent allowable by law. 
Our tax ID is 45-0964224.

FAQ’s
 Ticketing Fees
The fees applied to tickets at TDH are a little different from surcharges at other venues, so here is some helpful information about our fees:
TDH is committed to removing the financial barrier to experiencing art by holding affordability and accessibility as organizational tenets. We consistently keep our ticket prices low, ranging from $12 - $40, with ~60% of that revenue going directly to support the artists you see on stage. 
Our Facilities Fee of $2 per ticket directly supports the operations of our small non-profit organization, and the upkeep of our intimate, 200-seat theater. 
When you purchase tickets online, know you are supporting not only TDH, but also a local, Portsmouth-based ticketing company (PortsmouthNHtickets.com) that is dedicated to providing an easy and personal ticketing experience. 
When you purchase a ticket with a credit card, there is a small processing fee charged by the payment processor for the credit card company. This covers the cost of business for offering the ability to pay by credit card. 
We appreciate your support of TDH, and thank you for helping to keep this vibrant cultural hub thriving, bringing people together for a moment of breath, connection, and joy through the arts.
*By purchasing tickets, you agree to be added to The Dance Hall’s email list to receive updates on upcoming events, promotions, and important announcements. You can unsubscribe at any time. 
 Box Office
TDH does not have a walk-up box office open outside of show hours, so if you need to purchase day-of tickets for an event, it is best to do so either online or by arriving at TDH around the time doors open. 
 Parking
We recommend you plan ahead to secure the most convenient parking for you. Parking on public roads in Kittery is free with a time limit posted on signage. There is also parking available in the Municipal Lot on the corner of Walker Street and Government Street, and at the adjacent Kittery Public Library (after hours only!)
 Refunds
We are unable to issue any ticket refunds except for the following two circumstances:
Show cancellation: if a show is cancelled and a reschedule date is not provided, TDH will automatically refund all tickets AND credit card and ticketing fees for the cancelled show.
Rescheduled show: if an event is moved to a new date due to inclement weather, artist illness, or any other reason, refunds will be made available to all ticket buyers for a period of seven days (7 days) from the announcement of rescheduling. After the 7-day window has closed, refunds will no longer be available and all remaining tickets will be automatically valid for the newly rescheduled date.
 Seating
Seating is by general admission and seats are available first-come, first-served. For select shows where a “Dance Party” is noted, seating will be very limited. We will note this in the event description on our website and on our ticketing platform. When attending a “Dance Party” show, please keep the steps of the risers clear by not sitting in the empty spaces in order to provide safe access up and down. A general rule for “Dance Party” shows, if seats are key, please come on the early side.
 Doors
In general, “doors” open one hour before show time. Please do not arrive before door time– we have lots to do to prepare and get ready, and no early-entry is allowed.
 Beverages
We offer a variety of beverages to quench your thirst for purchase at our lobby bar. All drinks are allowed into the theater– we simply ask that you be mindful of your cup, can, or bottle to avoid any spilling on our dance floor. We serve: three, rotating draught beer options; red and white wine; prosecco; Freedom’s Edge alcoholic cider; seltzer water; Spindrift; bottled water; and Woodland Farms Non-Alcoholic canned beer.

Resident Artist Program
There is no doubt we are living in a difficult time– a time of reduction, uncertainty, tension, and polarization. Arts funding– both directly to artists and via organizations that support them– is being reduced or eliminated on the federal, state, and local level. Artists have no artistic home due to the lack of physical space for creation. And opportunities for growth and mentorship through organizational administrative support is being deprioritized.
If artists are to continue to be the catalysts for hope we need, we must step up and in to offer support to our storytellers in all disciplines.
This is why in 2025 The Dance Hall launched our first-ever Resident Artist (RA) program.
RAs receive a range of resources customized to their needs, such as: development, rehearsal, and performance space; production support; and opportunities for group and individualized mentorship and growth.
Meet the Resident Artists
How does it work?
Each artist will meet with TDH Staff to determine when and how the space can be made available to them to work on their craft. The usage of space could include classes, workshops, rehearsals, performances– how each artist uses the space and time is completely up to them, on each day. Some may want to pull back the proverbial curtain on their craft and invite the community in to experience what making looks like for them. TDH staff will be on-site to support whatever is needed, meeting each artist where they are in the moment
How will it benefit the community, the artists, and TDH?
As an arts organization, it is critically important that we show up for our artists at this moment. TDH is doubling down on our commitment to not only showcasing art, but investing in our local creative talent.
The community will have opportunities to experience the process of creation from sitting in on an evening of development or rehearsal to then witnessing a public performance as part of TDH’s ticketed programming. 
Resident Artists will build connections with each other, sharing experiences, ideas, and challenges, and will have the opportunity to collaborate and create with each other, possibly even teaming up and sharing their residency time to co-create.
RAs will also act as champions for TDH in the community, and this first cohort will help guide the program overall by sharing radically candid feedback about what is working well and where they need more support. In some ways, this will be a bit like building the plane as we’re flying it, but now is the time for action, and with patience and grace our first cohort will share lessons that guide the program’s future years
How can I become a Resident Artist in future years? 2026/27 APPLICATION PERIOD IS NOW CLOSED
This program is on an annual cycle. The next application period extends from May 1st - May 31st 2027. A small committee will review applications and choose the next cohort of RAs for The Dance Hall for 2027, beginning in July. 
TDH will take a holistic approach to the selection process and prioritize artists who are:
based in Southern Maine or the Greater Seacoast area of New Hampshire
at a transformational point in their career
aligned with TDH's values of accessibility, collaboration, respect, and curiosity.
If you have specific questions about the Resident Artist program, please reach out to Artistic Director, Eric Klaxton : eric@thedancehallkittery.org
