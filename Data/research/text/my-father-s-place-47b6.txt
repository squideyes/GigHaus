Eppy
My name is Michael “Eppy” Epstein
In 1971, I opened My Father’s Place at 19 Bryant Avenue in Roslyn with Richie Havens as our first performer. I operated at that location until 1987. It was fun and dangerous. Read the book! Since then I have operated My Father’s Place in various locations in Roslyn, Queens, Westbury and recently in Glen Cove. Now, we are back in Roslyn and back in business as a Supper Club inside The Roslyn H0tel.

The Fine Print
Welcome to My Father’s Place at The Roslyn Hotel. We are here to provide a friendly, musical and gastronomically pleasing environment for your edification. However, there are a few things to keep in mind:​​
​
The venue is a Supper Club.
All shows, dates, times and venue locations are subject to change without notice.
All ticket holders will be seated on a first come first serve basis. 
Tickets must be purchased prior to seating - no reservations.
Doors open at 6 pm for dinner and drinks
Shows start at 8 pm unless otherwise stated
We are here to have fun - but not at the cost of our fellow attendees. 
Drink responsibly.
Remember - smoking and vaping anywhere in the hotel is STRICTLY prohibited. A designated smoking area is available outside.
Refund Policy - If an event is cancelled, no action is required. We will refund the ticket price to the original method of payment used at the time of purchase within 30 days. Service fees are not refundable.
If an event is postponed and rescheduled, the original ticket will be honored on the new date.
Events may be cancelled or postponed by venue, promoter, or performer for a variety of reasons.
MFP will use reasonable endeavors to notify the ticket holders and general public of the event’s status. However, we cannot guarantee that ticket holders will be informed of status changes before or on the original date of the event. 
It is advised that ticket holders ascertain the status of the event by visiting the website, social media pages or by calling (516) 580-0887.
As a general rule, all ticket sales are final and non-refundable.

Frequently Asked Questions
HOW DO I GET TO MY FATHER’S PLACE?
First, DO NOT follow GOOGLE. We are located in the Roslyn Hilton Tapestry Hotel at 1221 Old Northern Blvd. in Roslyn, NY. 
WHAT ABOUT PARKING?
We have a 3 level parking garage in the rear of the building. When you pull in the top level, you will find a ramp leading down in the far left corner. The club is located on the second level of the garage. PARKING IS FREE.
WHAT TIME DO THE DOORS OPEN FOR A SHOW?
Typically the doors will open TWO HOURS prior to show time.
HOW LONG DOES THE SHOW LAST?
That is totally up to the artists. Typically the shows last 1 ½ - 2 hours after the artist takes the stage, but that is at the artists discretion.
​
CAN I RESERVE A TABLE?
Sorry, we DO NOT take reservations. Seating is on a FIRST COME, FIRST SEATED basis. The earlier you arrive, the better seats you will have. 
WHAT AM I ENTITLED TO FOR MY TICKET PURCHASE?
You purchase allows you entry to the venue to see the performance. As long as you arrival is timely (NOT late), it also entitles you to a seat.
CAN I GET FOOD AND A DRINK?
YES! We are a Supper Club. At each table you will find a menu. We have full wait staff service. A sample menu can be found at the bottom of our HOME page on this Web site.
You will enjoy the show from the table you are seated at – no need to move!
​
IS THERE A MINIMUM/COVER CHARGE?
We currently do NOT have a minimum or cover charge. That being said, we DO expect patrons to dine. We couldn’t survive has a music venue without folks enjoying the offerings.
HOW MANY CAN FIT AT A TABLE?
Most of our tables are set up for 2 and 4 people. Please note that tables can NOT be pushed together. (A few reasons for this. It causes an undue burden on the wait staff, it causes problems with the Fire Marshall. As well, many of the professionals who play the club comment on the amount of conversation coming from larger groups that makes it difficult for them and other patrons to enjoy the show.)
​
ARE TICKETS REFUNDABLE? CAN I USE MY TICKETS FOR A DIFFERENT SHOW?
Sorry, no. Ticket money goes to pay our artists. Once you purchase your tickets, we are committed to making sure the artists for that evening are paid from your purchase. If you can’t make a show you purchased tickets for, check with friends and family to see who might be able to use them.
WILL “EPPY” BE THERE?
YES! The legend who started the club in 1971 at the age of 22 will indeed be at the front door when you arrive!

Our Musical Journey
Established in 1971, My Father's Place was founded on the principle of presenting original music by original artists to a discerning audience of Long Island music fans. The live music scene developed and evolved over the years to include live concerts broadcast on 92.7 WLIR FM in association with record companies, agents and artist management. The musical journey of Eppy and My Father's Place was memorialized in the book, FUN+DANGEROUS by photographer Steve Rosnefield and Michael "Eppy" Epstein. More adventures were covered in the documentary film Dare To Be Different by film maker ...And coming soon, an independent documentary film about My Father's Place and Eppy will be released sometime in the near future.
The film by Meshakai ... has a working title - Names Don't Mean Shit.
