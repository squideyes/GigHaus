Private Events & Parties
The most boisterous and energetic venue on a popular stretch of nightlife destinations in Wayne, 118 North is dedicated to live music, upscale riffs on classic pub fare and a seriously enviable bar menu. 118 North is the perfect place for your next party.
Wedding After Parties
Open late with a lively atmosphere and plenty of late night snack packages to choose from – 118 North is a naturally perfect place to continue celebrating your big day well after your venue closes.
Group Dinners
Looking to book a group table and enjoy dinner + show? Inquire with us about our table packages – perfect for groups of 10 or more. Get 10% off your food & beverage experience and two complimentary tickets to a future concert of your choice.
118 North Capacity
Perfect for after parties or happy hour gatherings with competitive pricing for exclusive rentals! 118 North can accommodate up to 50 guests with a semi-private reservation or up to 150 guests for a full buyout.
The venue comes complete with a full production suite – lighting and sound engineers, flat screen televisions great for slideshows, professional stage. We have in-house DJ packages available and our talent booking department can happily help you to arrange live music!
118 North Parties include:
• A personal liaison to help plan all aspects of your event
• WiFi Access and use of our Flatscreen Tvs
• Live Sound & Lighting Technician
• Bar Staff & Servers
• Hosting & Box Office Staff
Additional 118 North Amenities
• Full Bar including 12 draughts
• A 16’ X 12’ stage complete with state of the art sound and lighting systems
• Various catering options from a menu designed by Chef Biff Gottehrer from Ripplewood Whiskey & Craft in Ardmore.
• Access to talent (bands, DJ’s, event hosts, etc)
• Printing capabilities (posters, fliers, tickets, etc)
