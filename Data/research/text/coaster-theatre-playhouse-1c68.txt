History
Nestled in the beautiful coastal community of Cannon Beach, Oregon, Coaster Theatre Playhouse has brought the world to locals and visitors alike through the magic of live theatre. From 19th century Siam in The King and I to a townhouse on Beekman Place in Auntie Mame, from a little Russian village in Fiddler on the Roof to a 1930s hotel suite in Lend Me a Tenor, Coaster Theatre has presented scores of plays and musicals performed by hundreds of actors for thousands of appreciative patrons.
From its early beginnings as the Coaster Roller Rink in the 1920s, this historic site operated as a skating rink through the 1950s and was used to show silent movies to the locals once each week. In 1972, the building was purchased by Maurie Clark, a Portland patron of the arts. Under his direction, a major remodel was undertaken, resulting in the building theatre patrons see today. Portland State University Summer Stock Company became regular performers each year and other groups regularly presented concerts and performances throughout the season. Upon Maurie’s death in 2001, Coaster Theatre Playhouse became a non-profit organization.

WHAT’S THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A SEASON SUBSCRIPTION AND A SEASON PASS?
Save up to 20% when you purchase a six or three show pass.
Passes give you more flexibility than a traditional subscription.
At this time season passes must be purchased by calling the box office at 203-436-1242 or visting the box office
Box office hours: Fridays & Saturdays from 1:30-5:30 pm
Season passes are purchased at a flat rate depending on the number of shows. A pass entitles you to your selected amount of tickets to be assigned to your shows now or later.
Passes can only be purchased by calling or visiting the box office. Choose your seats and shows now to secure your seats or wait until closer to the show dates before you commit.
Passes are available in 3 and 6 show groups to use in any combination for any shows you choose.
NOTE: We have been notified that third party ticket sites are “selling” tickets for our shows and events for amounts far above our listed price. To protect our patrons, we recommend that you purchase tickets directly from the theatre. The best way to get your tickets for any show is directly from our website, by calling 503-436-1242 or in person at the box office.
We cannot guarantee tickets purchased from a third party and entry may be denied.
