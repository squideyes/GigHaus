The Delphi Opera House enriches the cultural life of the Central Indiana region by providing a wide range of arts and entertainment experiences in a historic venue. 
Mission:
The mission of the Historic Delphi Opera House along with the Delphi Preservation Society, is to continue a tradition of providing opportunities for artistic production, creative exploration, arts education, the enjoyment of arts and entertainment while promoting tourism, preservation and community pride.
Vision:
Our vision is that art and preservation will enhance people’s lives and foster pride in our community.
The Historic Delphi Opera House along with our partner, The Delphi Preservation Society will: 
Inspire and create a community where artistic excellence happens 
Offer access to creative expression 
Foster programs for youth to experience art through participation 
Generate a positive impact on the culture and economy of the region 
Create communities around preservation of historic structures

 Directions & Parking 
The Delphi Opera House is located at 109 S. Washington Street, on the east side of the historic courthouse square in Delphi, IN. Delphi has three exits off the Hoosier Heartland Highway.
1. Old State Rd. 25: The Old State Rd 25 exit takes you to Main Street from the west which intersects with Washington Street at the stoplight. Turn left and the opera house will be on your right in the middle of the block. 
2. SR 421 Exit: The SR 421 exit brings you into town on Washington Street. Proceed to the courthouse square and the opera house is on the right. 
3. E. Main Street Exit: The Main Street exit brings you into town on Main Street from the east, turn right at the light onto Washington Street at the Courthouse Square, Opera House is on the right.
 Parking
All parking downtown is free. Parking around the square is limited to 2 hours during weekdays. 
A parking lot is located on the corner of Washington and Franklin streets just north of the square. 
Side street parking on Franklin and Monroe Streets within walking distance of the Opera House. 
 Plan Your Trip to Delphi 
Historical Downtown
The Delphi Courthouse Square Historic District is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Pick up a brochure inside the Opera House describing the architecture of many buildings constructed from the 1850s. 
Art Galleries
Downtown Delphi is home to two art galleries: 
Opera House Gallery of Contemporary Art is right next door to the Opera House lobby 
The Chapel Gallery 321 West Main Street 
Wabash and Erie Canal Park and Interpretive Center/Historical Museum
In addition to the historic Delphi Opera House, plan to visit the Wabash and Erie Canal Park and Interpretive Center. The Park and Center is ten blocks north of the square on Washington Street. Nearby is the Carroll County Historical Museum. 
Trails and Park System
Delphi has miles of walking trails and several parks within the city in addition to the Canal Park. The Deer Creek runs through town and can be enjoyed from Riley Park, which is south on Washington Street from the square.

 Accessibility 
Wheelchair access to the elevator is at the back entrance off the Franklin Street alleyway. (Turn right in the middle of the block on Franklin Street between Washington and Union Streets.)
