Mountain Top Music Center
At the heart of the Mount Washington Valley’s cultural landscape is Mountain Top Music Center, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to enriching the community through exceptional music education and live performance.
Our History & Growth
Founded with a deep commitment to music education in the region, Mountain Top reached a historic milestone when it acquired the fire-damaged Bolduc Block in downtown Conway. Through an ambitious, multi-year fundraising and restoration effort, the organization rescued the landmark from its post-fire ruin and brought the historic building back to life. Today, that permanent home at 36 Main Street serves as a thriving cultural anchor for the community.
Our Family of Programs
Mountain Top proudly operates several key pillars under one roof:
The Majestic Theatre: Our historic, state-of-the-art 250-seat performing arts center, hosting major musical acts, touring artists, theatrical productions, and community events.
The Majestic Café: An intimate, vibrant street-level listening room and piano bar that hosts a popular weekly Friday night jazz series and serves as a lively gathering space during mainstage events.
Mountain Top Music School: Located on the second floor of our vibrant campus, our community music school offers comprehensive instruction, lessons, and ensemble programs for students of all ages and skill levels.
By uniting education, performance, and community hospitality under one roof, Mountain Top Music Center is committed to keeping the arts accessible, vibrant, and thriving right here in the White Mountains.
Visit the Mountain Top Music School website
EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY : EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR
Core Responsibilities 
Strategic Leadership: Translate the organization’s mission into actionable operational goals, long-term plans, and measurable community outcomes.
Fundraising & Revenue Generation: Drive donor cultivation, major gifts, grants, corporate sponsorships, and special campaigns to ensure financial health.
Financial Management: Oversee annual budgets, monitor cash flow, review financial statements, and ensure compliance with nonprofit tax laws (such as Form 990) and audits.
Board Relations: Partner closely with the Board of Trustees, providing timely data, strategic recommendations, and transparent updates.
Staff & Culture Leadership: Hire, coach, and retain high-performing staff while fostering an inclusive, mission-driven workplace culture.
Public Relations & Advocacy: Serve as the chief spokesperson to build community partnerships, media visibility, and stakeholder trust.
Ideal Qualifications & Background 
Experience: 5 to 10+ years of progressive senior leadership or executive experience in an arts-related nonprofit, public, or mission-driven private sector. A music background is preferred.
Education: Bachelor’s degree in Arts Management or related field required; Master’s degree (such as an MBA) preferred.
Track Record: Demonstrated success in business development, meeting fundraising targets, and leading budget oversight.
Core Competencies: Strong emotional intelligence, transparent communication, ethical integrity, entrepreneurial problem-solving, and governance fluency.

Majestic Café
UPCOMING SHOWS
The Majestic Café is an intimate 40-seat live music venue in the storefront beside the Majestic Theatre. The Café takes inspiration from 20th-century jazz clubs and hosts several live performances in a variety of styles every week. We serve an impressive selection of craft cocktails & mocktails, beer & wine, and dinner options including charcuterie & panini.
Reservations are recommended to guarantee your seat and to indicate a seating choice. Walk-ins are welcome, space allowing.
The Majestic Café prides itself as a dedicated listening room. We will never expect complete silence from our patrons for the duration of a performance, but we ask that you be considerate of the musicians and your fellow patrons – please keep the focus on the music!
Doors open at 6pm; Music runs from 7pm – 9pm, unless otherwise indicated.
Parking is available to the rear of the building (the rear door, to the right, will be unlocked), across the road behind the town hall (after 5pm), or next door at The Station Bistro (after 2pm).

Broken Arrow: The Music of Neil Young

Broken Arrow delivers a definitive, high-energy tribute to the legendary career of Neil Young, masterfully navigating the full breadth of his iconic catalog. The band captures every nuance of the classic sound, shifting effortlessly from the raw, garage-rock intensity of the Crazy Horse era to the poignant, pedal steel-driven textures of Young’s celebrated country-rock repertoire.
Led by front man Joe Mass, whose vocal performance and harmonies are so authentic they must be heard to be believed, Broken Arrow honors the spirit of the original recordings while bringing a fresh, live energy to the stage. By blending faithful renditions of timeless hits with tasteful, expansive improvisational jams, the band ensures that no two performances are exactly alike. Whether you are a lifelong devotee looking for the deep cuts or a casual fan eager to hear the classics, Broken Arrow delivers an electrifying experience that keeps audiences on their feet and calling for more until the final note.
