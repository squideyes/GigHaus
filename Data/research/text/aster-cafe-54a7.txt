Reservations
The Aster Cafe accepts reservations for indoor dining and live music only.
Patio seating is walk-in only.
For questions or parties larger than 8 people, email info@astercafe.com and we will accommodate you.
For groups larger than 30, please make a private event request.
Multiple small-party reservations: While we welcome everyone, we cannot guarantee adjacent seating for separate reservations made for the same time.
Dog Policy: Well behaved dogs are allowed outside on the patio only at our discretion, not inside.
 Indoor Dining Reservations
Live Music
To secure your seat at the show, please make a reservation. Your reservation will include a table for the duration of the show and you are free to order food and drinks.
Please note the cover charge will be added to your tab.
If you would like to show up early to enjoy dinner and drinks before the music starts, please make your live music reservation before the noted show time, up to 1 hour before.
