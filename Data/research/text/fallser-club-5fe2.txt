Once upon a time we were a movie theatre... 
We kept a few pieces of our history alive. We still have the catwalk leading up to the old projection booth that memorializes our past as an iconic East Falls entertainment venue. Now, we proudly carry on the entertainment tradition in the form of a community music and event space. 
We are a sustainable non-profit event space and music venue located in the heart of East Falls, Philadelphia, right off Kelly Drive. 
The Fallser Club’s mission is to create community and enable connection by expanding access to music, arts, and inclusive gathering spaces. Through partnerships, sustainability practices, and an activist spirit at our core, we work to build equity, connection, and vibrancy. We believe music heals, and that creative community spaces are essential to a more just, resilient, and thriving community.
 Music is our passion and our state-of-the-art sound system will impress any music aficionado. The Club epitomizes a warm, industrial atmosphere- two things you may not think go together until you see our renovated, modern loft design. Our 5000 square-foot space boasts unique decor with intimate cocktail tables, flexible seating upstairs and down, a den lounge with cozy coaches, an open bar area, and a second floor that allows you to have a bird’s-eye view of the stage and open Round. Our space can easily be transformed into a dreamy wedding location, a theatre stage, a premiere movie screening, a techno dance club, an intimate jazz experience, or a community meeting space. Activism is built into our business model and we are dedicated to advancing equity and inclusion and creating a more sustainable community for all. We partner with other nonprofits, screen innovative documentary films, hold lecture series and educational and art events. We have it all. And you belong here. 
From Hard Times to the Best of Times 
The historic curved building at 3721 Midvale Ave operated as a silent film theatre called The Midvale from 1914 to 1929 during its silent film era. The Midvale Theatre was opened in November 1914, seating nearly 500 people as a silent film venue. It was designed by architect Lawrence Visscher Boyd who resided in East Falls on West Penn St and designed many houses including his own in the Queen Lane Manor area between Henry and Wissahickon Avenues, Coulter Street and West Queen Lane. Built as a movie theater in the Arts and Crafts style, the building retains many of its original architectural features despite its adaptive reuse. Helen Collier with a local address at the time of construction was the owner/developer. She was a film actress working in NYC.
The Midvale Theatre was renamed The Falls Theatre as a sound venue when it became a “Talkie,” from 1929 to 1939. The theatre hit some hard times and on August 16, 1939, it was auctioned off to the highest bidder who unfortunately turned it into a grocery store. After a long run as the local grocer, it became a space for several social service providers and was eventually the home of two tech startups, Bulogics and STRATIS. When the businesses were sold, the tech entrepreneur duo, Felicite Moorman and Ryan Buchert chose to keep the building, saving it from becoming apartments. They opened The Fallser Club in 2022 as a nonprofit venue to offer community, activism and music to the greater Philadelphia area. 
Our Programming Philosophy 
The Fallser Club presents programming that spans multiple genres and cultural traditions, reflecting the rich creative fabric of Philadelphia.
We believe music is a living archive: shaped by migration, neighborhood stories, ancestral rhythms, and contemporary voices. Our stage honors those lineages by creating space for artists rooted in different traditions while encouraging collaboration and shared experiences.
From jazz, folk, hip-hop, experimental, global, and genre-defying work, our programming celebrates the diverse cultural roots of Philadelphia and the ways they continue to evolve. We intentionally curate shows that welcome audiences from every path and perspective, fostering connection across generations.
At The Fallser Club, diversity is the foundation of the work we do. Each performance is an opportunity to listen more deeply, learn from one another, and experience the city through sound.

Parking 
Parking in East Falls is no problem! There is ample free on street parking near the East Falls train station driveway at 3610 Midvale Ave and a paid municipal lot three blocks away at 4100 Ridge Ave. Midvale Ave and Ridge Ave are both well lit!
We are wheelchair accessible, and our entrance is at street level.

Frequently Asked Questions: 
Getting Here and Parking
How do I get to the Fallser Club? Is parking a nightmare?
Good news! Getting to East Falls is actually pretty easy. The East Falls Train Station is just a short walk from our door, SEPTA bus routes K and 61 drop off closeby, and if you’re driving, there’s plenty of free street parking by the station entrance at 3610 Midvale Ave and in the train station parking lot. Need more options? There’s a paid municipal lot three blocks away at 4100 Ridge Ave. Both Midvale and Ridge Ave are well-lit, so you’re all set even for evening shows!
Note: Parking immediately outside The Fallser Club on Midvale is only 2-hour parking.
Food & Drinks
What’s the food situation?
We’ve got light snacks and refreshments available at the Fallser Club, including free popcorn at most shows (because popcorn makes everything better). But here’s the real deal: we’ve partnered with our favorite neighborhood spot, Taqueria Cresta, which is less than a block away. Show your Fallser Club ticket and score 10% off your meal. Tacos before the show? Yes, please!
Tickets
Should I buy tickets online or can I just show up?
Both work! Online ticket sales close 2 hours before showtime, but we usually have tickets available at the door. That said, buying in advance is a win-win: you’ll save a few bucks with our advance ticket discount, and it helps us and the artists plan ahead for capacity and staffing to make sure the show goes off without a hitch.
Help! I lost my ticket confirmation email. Am I doomed?
Not at all, no need to panic! We keep a guest list of everyone who purchased tickets, so just give us your name when you arrive and we’ll get you checked in. Easy peasy.
Accessibility
Is the Fallser Club accessible for people with disabilities?
Yes! We do our best to accommodate everyone at our venue. Learn more at our dedicated accessibility page here: https://thefallserclub.org/accessibility/.

Celebrate your way! 
People who choose to have their events with us are more than clients. They become our friends and are part of the Fallser Community. They come back for our music events, invite friends, and plan additional celebrations. Revenue from private-event rentals helps support The Fallser Club’s nonprofit music, arts and community programming. 
customize every aspect 
We are your perfect a la carte venue for any event. This will allow you to save money while enjoying complete control over every aspect of your wonderful event. 
We are a party Palace 
We have everything you need to make your party a unique event your guests will remember forever. The Fallser Club is the perfect location for a family party, hosting a movie screening, dancing the night away, and so much more. 
Music at its Finest 
Musicians love us because we make them sound so good! Our professional sound engineers will fine-tune your surround music and our two large screens can project the band so your guests don’t miss a beat while refreshing their drink or wandering around to chat with friends. We can also project a slide show of your choice. 
What is a party without a photobooth and Karaoke? 
Our photobooth is easy to use, self-serve, and comes with an array of fun props to choose from. The images can be emailed to each person and the host can get a copy of all of them after the event for their keepsake album. 
Love karaoke? We’ve got that too on our big screen. Fun for all the guests! Oh dear, is Uncle Steve singing “Come Fly With Me” again?? 
Unique Amenities 
Have a performer dazzle your guests on our aerial circus silk. 
Add our haze bubbles, mirror ball, and colored rotating lights that move with the music and you will elevate any event you can imagine.

Talent Corner 
Getting Booked at The Fallser Club:
Calling All Musicians, Comics, Talented Performers!
Are you interested in performing at The Fallser Club? We’d love to hear from you!
To get started, please send your Electronic Press Kit (EPK) to director@thefallserclub.com. Be sure to include:
A brief introduction about yourself or your band (e.g., your genre, where you’ve performed, your influences).
Links to your social media or music platforms so we can check out your work.
Please keep in mind that we are a 225 capacity venue and we need to aim for a minimum of 100 tickets for each show. Before you reach out: can you draw 100 paid tickets or more? Can you commit to a four week marketing plan to drive ticket sales? Both are important to consider. Musicians need to be dedicated to marketing their own shows. (Yes we market too!)
A great way to connect with us is by attending our Open Mic Night, held on the last Sunday of every month. It’s a fun, informal way to showcase your talent and meet our community. (Check our events calendar for details!)
If you don’t hear back right away, feel free to follow up with a polite reminder. We receive many inquiries each week, and occasionally, messages can slip through the cracks.
Lastly, if we can’t book you at the moment, please don’t be discouraged. Our goal is to create a diverse lineup, and sometimes we have to make tough choices based on scheduling and variety. Keep making your amazing music, and don’t hesitate to reach out again in the future!
We can’t wait to hear from you!
