City Winery St. Louis is a premier live music venue offering an intimate setting for unforgettable performances. Enjoy live music nearly every night, from rock, R&B, and jazz concerts to stand-up comedy shows and live podcast recordings. Over the years, the venue has welcomed legendary artists including Prince, Robert Glasper, Taj Mahal, Kindred The Family Soul, Lyfe Jennings, 10000 Maniacs, and many more to its stage. As a leading St. Louis concert venue, we pair world-class live entertainment with dinner service and award-winning wines for a true. dinner and show experience.
Browse our St. Louis concert calendar to discover upcoming concerts, comedy events, and special performances throughout the month. Whether planning a date night or searching for live music or concerts in St. Louis, City Winery delivers exceptional performances in a seated venue.
 Plan Your Visit
Directions, Parking & Dining at City Winery St. Louis
City Winery St. Louis is located at 3730 Foundry Way in the City Foundry in the heart of Midtown, just blocks from The Grove and the Grand Center Arts District. Whether you're coming for live music, a comedy show, or dinner and a show, getting here is easy.
Parking: There are multiple free parking lots in the City Foundry. Covered parking is available in the the multi-level garage on the North side of the property off Forest Park Parkway. The lot on the West side near the Vandeventer Avenue entrance, across from IKEA, is the best option for ADA parking. There is also available street parking along Forest Park Avenue outside of the garage.
Public Transit: City Winery St. Louis is accessible via public transportation through Metro Transit. MetroLink light rail and MetroBus routes serve the Midtown area, with nearby stops within walking distance from the venue.
Rideshare: Uber, Lyft, and other rideshare services frequently drop off and pick up guests directly in front of the venue, making it convenient to arrive or leave after a show.
 FAQ
 Where is City Winery St. Louis located?
City Winery St. Louis is located at 3730 Foundry Way, Suite 158, in Midtown St. Louis at City Foundry STL, a vibrant dining, shopping, and entertainment destination. The venue sits in the heart of Midtown, a growing cultural hub known for its arts scene, nearby universities, and central location just minutes from downtown. Many guests visit City Winery for a full evening experience, combining dinner, wine, and live music in one location.
 What types of concerts and shows happen at City Winery St. Louis?
City Winery St. Louis hosts a diverse lineup of live music, comedy shows, podcast recordings, brunch performances, and special events throughout the year. Our calendar regularly features national touring artists, singer-songwriters, jazz musicians, tribute concerts, and curated local performances. Because of the venue’s intimate concert setting, audiences are able to experience performances up close in a seated environment.
 Can I order food and wine during the show?
Yes. City Winery St. Louis is known for our dinner and a show experience, allowing guests to enjoy full dinner service and award-winning wines during most performances. Guests can order food and drinks directly from their table while watching the show, or arrive early to enjoy the restaurant or wine bar before the performance begins.
 Is seating reserved at City Winery concerts?
Most shows feature reserved seating so guests can relax and enjoy the performance with food and wine service throughout the evening.
 What time do doors open for shows?
Door times can vary depending on the event, but guests are typically able to enter the venue 60 to 90 minutes before showtime. Arriving early is recommended if you plan to order dinner or drinks before the performance begins.
 What makes City Winery different from other St. Louis concert venues?
City Winery St. Louis offers a unique concert and dining experience that combines live music, award-winning wines, and chef-driven cuisine under one roof. Unlike many traditional music venues, guests can enjoy a full meal and wine service while watching the performance. The venue’s intimate concert hall also allows audiences to experience artists in a closer, more personal setting than many larger St. Louis venues.
 What is the concert experience like at City Winery St. Louis?
City Winery St. Louis offers an intimate concert experience where guests can enjoy live performances in a seated venue with table service. Unlike larger concert halls, the space is designed to bring audiences closer to the music, allowing guests to enjoy dinner, wine, and cocktails while watching the show. The venue’s acoustics and smaller capacity make it a favorite for fans looking to experience artists in a more personal setting.
 Do shows frequently sell out?
Because City Winery St. Louis is an intimate venue with limited seating, many concerts and comedy shows sell out in advance. Popular touring artists, tribute performances, and special events often fill up quickly. Guests interested in attending a show are encouraged to check the event calendar regularly and purchase tickets early to secure their preferred seating.
