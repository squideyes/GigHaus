About Us
Main Street Crossing = Up-Close Concert Venue + Sunday Home for Multiple Small
Churches + Non Profit Organization + Funding Resource for Charities +
Davis Family Operated
MINISTRY AND CHARITY
Funded, built and founded in 2004 by the Davis Family of Tomball, the original idea was as follows: To co-locate and share this 150 seat live-music-concert venue with small church communities who may not be able to afford such a beautiful and functional venue for their Sunday church services. We have had 4 different churches gather at MSC through the years: Journey, Sondays, Well Spring, and Freedom House. We currently host Sondays Community Church on Sunday mornings at 10:30 A.M. For more information about this church please visit Tomball Renewal Center. 
In recent years, Main Street Crossing has also begun offering the use of this beautiful venue, with its stage, it’s sound system and its kitchen to other charities and ministries, not directly affiliated with Main Street Crossing for fund raising events to fulfill their ministry and charitable purposes.
UP-CLOSE CONCERTS (ALL SEATS WITHIN 45 FEET OF THE STAGE OR CLOSER)
We present Country, TexasCountry, Americana, Folk, Blues, Oldies 60s/70s, Tribute Bands, and more in an “Up Close” setting. For most concerts, there is an opportunity to meet the artist after the show, get a photo together, etc. We also provide food and beverage table service before and during the concert.

Sandi Patty: The Enduring “Voice” of Christian Music
Key Takeaways
Overnight fame: Patty’s 1986 “Star-Spangled Banner” at the Statue of Liberty rededication launched her into the national spotlight.
Record-setting success: 40 Dove Awards (including 11 consecutive Female Vocalist of the Year wins), five Grammys, and more than 11 million albums sold.
Resilience: In the 1990s, she reinvented herself through symphony pops concerts.
Still going strong: At 70, she’s releasing a reimagined “Love in Any Language” on Oct. 23, with the album Back to Love due in February 2027.
Rising Star
Born July 12, 1956, in Oklahoma City, Sandi Patty first drew attention singing backup for Bill Gaither and the Bill Gaither Trio. Her first two Dove Awards came in 1982, and she headlined her first national tour in 1984. For about 15 years, she even performed as “Sandi Patti” because a printer misspelled her name.
The Breakthrough
On July 4, 1986, her “Star-Spangled Banner” aired during the Statue of Liberty rededication, and she became a national favorite almost overnight. Johnny Carson invited her to The Tonight Show, where she sang “Love in Any Language.” Christmas in Washington and Disney’s Fourth of July special followed.
Peak Years
Patty averaged over 200 concerts a year in the late ’80s and early ’90s.
She sold out arenas and had a staff of more than 30.
She reached No. 1 on Billboard’s Christian albums chart with Sandi Patty and the Friendship Company, which won a 1990 Dove Award.
Branching Out
In the 1990s, she began performing pops concerts with symphonies, including the Atlanta Symphony and Cincinnati Pops, and hosting the Indianapolis Symphony’s Yuletide Celebration.
By the Numbers
Dove Awards: 40, including a record 11 consecutive Female Vocalist of the Year wins
Grammys: five
Billboard Music Awards: four
Albums: 24, selling more than 11 million units (one source says 12 million), with three platinum and five gold records
Honors: Gospel Music Hall of Fame (2004) and Oklahoma Hall of Fame
Book: 2005 autobiography Broken on the Back Row
Still Singing
Now 70, Patty has recently returned to the Grand Ole Opry and shared the stage with Kristin Chenoweth. Her single “The Voice I Have Now,” her first studio recording in almost a decade, is being promoted for Grammy consideration. A reimagined “Love in Any Language,” marking the song’s 40th anniversary, arrived Oct. 23, followed by the album Back to Love in February 2027. 
Offstage, she says faith, family, and friends matter most. She and her husband, Don Peslis, have eight children and 11 grandchildren.
See Sandi Patty Christmas at the Centrum
See the Sandi Patty Christmas show at the Centrum (Cypress Creek Community Center) in Spring, Texas, on Dec. 11, 2026, at 8 p.m. The event is hosted by Main Street Crossing (MSC). Get tickets today!
MSC in Tomball, Texas, is one of the best small music venues in the Houston metro area. This unique nonprofit in a historic venue hosts country, Americana, folk, blues, and other artists in an intimate setting: The farthest seat is only 45 feet from the stage. MSC also offers food, beverages, table service, and community seating, making it easier to interact with fellow concertgoers and even performing artists. Parking is convenient and stress-free.
MSC also functions as a multiuse venue for church services, concerts, and more in Tomball. Check out our event calendar and enjoy entertainment that supports good causes.

Get in on a Merry Christmas Season at Main Street Crossing
Key Takeaways
Five Christmas shows are coming to Main Street Crossing in Tomball, Texas, this December.
Dec. 8: Jared Freiburg & the Vagabonds bring a rockin’ piano-driven holiday revue.
Dec. 14: Ian Moore’s Acoustic Family Christmas features Bukka Allen and Warren Hood.
Dec. 19: The Texas Tenors perform matinee and evening shows.
Dec. 20-22: Elvis tribute artist Vince King performs three shows.
Dec. 23: Tianna Hall & The Houston Jazz band’s holiday show
Tickets are available on the Main Street Crossing website.
Greater Houston, your holiday soundtrack is waiting just up the road in Tomball. Main Street Crossing (MSC) puts you close to the stage for four Christmas concerts this December, whether you’re coming from Cypress, The Woodlands, Katy, or downtown. The four don’t-miss Christmas shows take place in an up-close setting where the music feels personal.

“A Rockin’ Holiday Revue“

High-energy rock and roll echoing Ray Charles, Frank Sinatra, Elvis Presley, Jerry Lee Lewis, and Little Richard

Dec. 14: Ian Moore’s Acoustic Family Christmas
With Bukka Allen & Warren Hood
A singer, guitarist, and songwriter who has played stadiums with the Stones and arenas with Dylan and ZZ Top

Get in the Holiday Spirit With Christmas Shows at Main Street Crossing
You can see live Christmas music performances at Main Street Crossing throughout the month of December. Click here to purchase tickets. 
MSC in Tomball, Texas, is one of the best small music venues in the Houston metro area. This unique nonprofit in a historic venue hosts country, Americana, folk, blues, and other types of music artists in an intimate setting — the farthest seat is only 45 feet from the stage. MSC also offers food, beverages, table service, and community seating, making it easier to interact with fellow concertgoers and even performing artists. Parking is convenient and stress-free. 
MSC additionally functions as a multiuse venue for church services, concerts, and more in Tomball. Check out our event calendar, and enjoy entertainment in support of good causes!
