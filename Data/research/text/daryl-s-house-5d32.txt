About Daryl’s House
The idea for Daryl’s House, the Rock and Roll Hall of Famer’s restaurant and live music club was born in a light-bulb moment when Daryl Hall first conceived his Webby Award-winning Live from Daryl’s House, which bowed on the internet in late 2007 and maintains its status as a groundbreaking series. Since its opening in 2014, Daryl has used the Club to host episodes of the show.
“Why not just play music at my house and let people see what musicians are really like, when they are relaxed and among friends” says Hall about his brainchild. “The restaurant and club is the perfect venue where artists, band and I can hang out, drink some wine, have a good time and make some great music”. And the audience can tune in to Live From Daryl’s House and join us!
Daryl’s hand can be seen everywhere in the Club. “I’ve designed everything, from the tables and chairs to the walls and ceiling. It’s an 18th century barn style…an early American, New England kind of vibe”. The eclectic restaurant menu features a variety of award-winning American cuisine, like steaks, seafood, pasta and sandwiches reflecting his Philly/East Coast roots. Daryl’s House Club hosts both standing and seated shows with free music at weekend brunches and, during the summer, on the back porch.
Boasting a world class sound system and intimate atmosphere Daryl’s House Club builds on the Live From Daryl’s House inspiration and has become a favorite destination for top artists and music fans looking for great food and an up-close-and-personal music experience.
Daryl says, “Welcome to my House!”
Top Notch Entertainment
Our venue shines with top-tier entertainment, with a varied mix of the best local, regional, and nationally touring bands. Our live sound experience is unmatched, drawing performers who love our electric energy and the buzzing excitement that fills the air every night.
FAQs
 What are the Daryl’s House Club Hours?
Weds – Fri: 3:30pm-Close
 Sat – Sun: 11am-Close
 We close for some holidays and occasionally for weather or show cancellations. We also open sometimes on Monday’s and Tuesday’s for shows. Check out our SHOWS page for a most accurate listing of shows coming to the club. Showtime?
Check each individual show listing for specific times, although doors typically open at 5:00pm. All shows are ALL AGES unless otherwise noted. How do I check for ticket availability or make a reservation at the club?
You can easily check ticket availability and make reservations for our club’s shows through our website. However, on rare occasions, due to unforeseen circumstances (i.e., dangerous weather, artist illness, etc.), shows may be canceled, rescheduled, or postponed. If you are a ticket holder, you will be notified and offered options. Daryl’s House Club is available for private parties. How do I purchase tickets?
Tickets are only available online through www.darylshouseclub.com via TicketWeb / over the phone by calling 1-866-468-3399 / or at the door. This all depends on availability! ONLY VALID TICKETWEB TICKET ORDERS ACCEPTED. DO NOT PURCHASE TICKETS FROM ANY SECONDARY MARKET, SCALPERS, OR OTHER POTENTIAL SCAMS… YOU WILL BE DENIED ENTRY. Seating?
Most shows have a ticket type available called “Reserved Table Seating” but they tend to sell out quickly. For “General Admission/No Guaranteed Seating” ticket holders there is a limited amount seating along the perimeter of the club that is first-come, first-serve. This seating is not meant to watch the show from but as temporary respite for those who want to get off their feet for a minute. Please be considerate to offer the seating to those in need.  Daryl’s House does do some fully Standing Room Only shows – these situations will never have Reserved Table Seating Tickets available for purchase. Please do not move the tables and chairs. Their location often corresponds to egress and traffic flow so there’s a rhyme to our reason. I purchased “Reserved Table Seating 6:30 PM” tickets. Can I order dinner from my table… and can I arrive early?
Yes, we’ll provide full service to your table. Also, we recommend you plan on arriving 15 minutes early to your reservation time. Does Daryl’s House Club serve alcohol?
We have a full-service bar serving a full array of libations, a large wine list, draft beer, a large liquor selection, and specialty cocktails! Can I get a refund on my ticket purchase?
All sales are final. No refunds or exchanges. For more information on your tickets you can write to info@ticketweb.com Are there hotels nearby? Any special rates?
There are a handful of hotels nearby! Tell them you’re visiting Daryl’s House and ask for the House discount… if it’s available they’ll try to hook you up!

No professional cameras, video, or audio recording allowed. We have a strict no-flash policy on photos. Absolutely no video is permitted, ever. In addition to these rules if venue management, security, or a band’s representative asks you to stop taking photos… Abide! Will you release any tickets to a sold out show?
Sold out means SOLD OUT. We encourage you to buy tickets as far in advance as possible to secure your entry. We will not honor tickets purchased through 3rd Party Vendors. Where can I park?
You can park right in front of the club. Once our main lot fills up, we’ll direct cars to our off site parking and we’ll transport you via Shuttle. Will Daryl Hall be at the club tonight?
If it’s a Daryl Hall show, you can count on it. On occasion Daryl does come to his own club. So you’ll have to be in attendance to find out! If I reserve tickets over the phone do the additonal service fees still apply?
Fees are applicable to all ticket purchases. Does Daryl’s House have a Happy Hour? And if so, what time does that start?
Daryl’s Hour is from 3:30pm – 6pm Does Daryl’s House Club offer takeout?
Yes, we do takeout. What forms of payment are accepted inside of the Club?
Cash or Cards. Is the venue wheelchair accesible?
Yes, Daryl’s House is wheelchair accesible. What is the cancellation policy for shows?
No refunds, no exchanges. If an artist postpones or cancels we’ll open up a refund window, and email all ticket holders with instructions. What are the noise levels like during a typical concert?
It’s live music, it can be loud. if you’re concerned, please bring your own ear protection.
