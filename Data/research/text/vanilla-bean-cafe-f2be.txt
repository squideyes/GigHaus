Entertainment Info 
Please Note: All shows are General Admission unless otherwise noted in the Calendar. 
Admission is Cash Only.
We produce shows from October to April each year. We book mainly well-known performers and groups, reoccurring shows, and benefit concerts. As always, check out our online calendar or newsletter for more info.
The Bean has been the forum for many types of entertainment. It started at our first “Java Jive”, or Open Mic, in 1990. Over the years, we have hosted many benefits and provided space for many different genres of artistic presentation. It seemed that this type of entertainment, which is all based in the Folk Tradition, just wanted to be here.
The entertainment has ranged from Celtic Folk to Cajun Zydeco, from storytelling to poetry reading to even an Off Broadway Play. Over the years we have booked many talented people to help create awareness for this type of entertainment, and continue to do so, including here on the internet.

What We’re About 
What We’re About
The Vanilla Bean Café opened in 1989 with a seating capacity of 16, today we seat 90 inside and 60 outside! Located in a restored, early 19th century barn in scenic Pomfret, Connecticut, The Vanilla Bean Café is owned and operated by the Jessurun Family: Barry and Brian, with occasional appearances by and help from the rest of the family.
Our Philosophy was simple: “Create a place where we would feel comfortable and serve food that we would want to eat. If we wouldn’t eat it, we certainly wouldn’t serve it.”
House-made soups, sandwiches, and light dinners comprise our basic menu, but daily specials made with creativity, imagination, and seasonal ingredients, spice up the menu. Baked goods, such as muffins, bagels, croissants, and cookies, are available every morning, not to mention our decadent desserts.
Try a specialty drink from our Barista bar, featuring Grand Italia Coffee, Asian Matcha and David Rio Chai Tea. We also feature Harney Teas and Numi Teas.
Relax with a glass of wine, enjoy the sunshine, and dine al fresco on the patio. Our menu takes on all new dimensions during the growing season. Fresh fish and other seasonally inspired grill delights appear in addition to our regular kitchen menu. 
Be sure to check out the blackboard breakfast, lunch or dinner specials. Also available on this website!
Our goal is to serve you good homemade food in a comfortable, casual atmosphere. As Eileen told a customer, “We provide comfort foods in a comfortable place.”
After decades in business, The Vanilla Bean Café remains guided by the same simple idea that inspired its opening: good food belongs in a comfortable place. Its historic surroundings, family-run spirit, house-made favorites, local art, and live entertainment combine to create an experience that feels both distinctive and familiar. Whether stopping in for a quick bite, lingering over coffee, enjoying a seasonal meal on the patio, or spending an evening listening to music, guests find a restaurant that values both the food on the table and the people gathered around it.
In a world where dining can sometimes feel hurried and impersonal, The Vanilla Bean Café offers something refreshingly timeless: a place to eat well, connect with others, and enjoy the charm of Connecticut’s Quiet Corner.
All of these years would not be possible without our valued guests and friends who come through our door on a regular basis, ur longevity is also a testament to the hard work and dedication of our employees.
We hope that you enjoy our establishment and will come back and see us again soon!
The Jessurun Family
Our Pricing
Please note that our prices INCLUDE 7.635% CT Sales tax. (6.35% CT Sales Tax and 1% Prepared Meals Tax).
What does that mean?
At The Bean, a $18.00 menu item is really $16.76 + $1.24 sales tax. We don’t use pennies, dimes, or nickels. We don’t like to count them, and our bet is you don’t like them either.
Why?
Well, it’s faster and easier, and yet the outcome is the same–we just don’t have to count small change.
We like to keep things simple–and whole!
Art Gallery
The Café displays artwork by local artists year-round. The shows are one or two months in duration. Some have openings and some do not. Most of the artwork is for sale and often prices are listed with the piece or on a list located in the room. One hundred percent of the sale price goes to the artist!
For more information, contact Sothea Semmelrock.
