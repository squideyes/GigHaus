We are excited to show off our newest space, Show Bar. Its first floor location offers a stunning full bar, 3 large projection screens, a stage, state of the art sound system, plus a private outdoor patio. This space has classic charm and is very versatile for parties of 20 – 275+. Click on the Show Bar rental link below for more information.
Revolution Hall offers clients and their guests world-class amenities, state-of-the-art production, and the friendliest food & beverage staff in town. The spacious auditorium + expansive hallways, show bar, the stunning 2700 sq. ft. roof top deck (**limited evening availability), the auxiliary rooms (which include Martha’s, The Assembly Lounge, our Greenroom, the Sunset Room, 2 auxiliary conference rooms) can accommodate private events ranging from corporate luncheons to multi-faceted experiences including speaking engagements, award shows, and more. We accommodate events of all kinds for 20 to 1200 guests. Our seasoned event staff can help make your dreams work.

About Rock 'n Roll High School 
Revolution Hall is a state-of-the-art concert venue which sits at the heart of Washington High School. Founded in 1906 as Eastside High, the school was renamed in 1909 and moved to the iconic brick landmark – where it stands today. The existing building was rebuilt in 1924 after a fire destroyed the majority of the original building. The school boasts many notable alumni such as food-enthusiast / chef / author James Beard and the twice-decorated Nobel Laureate, Linus Pauling.
The school was closed in 1981 due to low enrollment. The facility was occasionally used for various purposes but remained predominantly vacant over the years. Since its purchase in 2013, the 113,000+ square foot building has been completely renovated and restored to accommodate offices, The Assembly Lounge and Marthas – our pre and post show bar and cafe, a smaller venue – Show Bar (which serves our sister venue’s famous Bar Bar Burger’) + the historic auditorium. Topping off Revolution Hall (literally) is our Roof Deck Bar, open weather permitting April thru October, offers the best 360 degree views of Portland bar-none for show goers to enjoy before, during, or after a show.

From the Roof Deck to Show Bar to the Assembly Lounge to our expansive Parkside Patio, a great drink, slice of pizza, or burger is never far away. The Roof Deck is open April thru October. Minors OK with parent or guardian until 6pm w/ exception to all-ages events night-of.

Show Bar burgers are yummy. Grab a classic with cheese, a vegan treat, and don’t forget the fries. No ticket or reservation required! Just grab an open table on the Parkside Patio or come in and see us at the bar!
Or catch a Blazer game, Timbers match, and maybe even some tennis on our big screens.
