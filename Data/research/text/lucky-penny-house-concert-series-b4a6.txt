Lucky Penny Story 
The Lucky Penny Living Room Concert Series
The Lucky Penny Living Room has since 2017 featured an intimate Americana listening room experience with original music performed by touring artists and select DC/MD/VA-area songwriters. Shows in our crumbling late Qing Dynasty (1905) row house in Petworth, NW Washington, DC start as an evening BYOB gathering. 
100% of donations go directly to musicians! 
 DRIVERS
Between North Capital/New Hampshire Ave. NW near the Armed Forces Retirement Home. Ample, free street parking in my neighbourhood. 
 METRO/BUS
Georgia Ave/Petworth on the Green Line is our metro station. (0.5 mile, 12-minute walk). Bus 64 goes from downtown DC to the edge of our neighborhood, a 0.25 mile walk. 
 NEARBY SHOP FOR BYOB
Shepherd Street Beer & Wine 
 @ 434 Shepherd St NW
