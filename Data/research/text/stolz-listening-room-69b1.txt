SARA B. RYAN ENDOWMENT FUND
Sara Ballantine Ryan lived most of her life in Talbot County, Maryland with her husband, Roderick (Rod) Newbold Ryan. An accomplished goldsmith and jewelry designer, Sara loved and appreciated the arts and relished every opportunity to visit art galleries and museums, and to experience live music and theatre. She encouraged a love of the arts in others, especially her own children, Christopher and Jonathan, and she generously supported programs that did so for others.
After Sara’s passing in January of 2022, her family collaborated with the Avalon Foundation to create the Sara B. Ryan Endowment Fund to honor her legacy and to make certain her good works and passion for arts education are remembered long into the future. The seed grant of $500,000 given by Sara’s family will be used to expand the much-needed Avalon Foundation arts programming for the children of Talbot County.

MORTGAGE LIFTER
On Sept 16, 2022 the Avalon Foundation purchased the final part of the Theatre Building, the restaurant formerly known as Bannings Tavern. Reaching total site control of the Avalon Theatre building is an especially poignant milestone as the Theatre celebrates its 100th anniversary. Because community-building is the cornerstone of the Avalon Foundation’s mission, community input will be solicited prior to finalizing plans for the usage of this expanded space.

“She sells her narrative lyricism with the vocal resonance of a rock chameleon reminiscent of Linda Ronstadt.” - For Folk’s Sake
Tickets: $25 (fees not included)
Chloe Kimes, an Americana singer/songwriter based in Nashville, is a name you ought to know. 
Consistently producing difficult and honest storytelling, incomparable vocal delivery, and an undeniable sound, she needs not much explanation but simply your attendance at her show to see and know the real Kimes. 
Her forthcoming album Daredevil, due out this fall, presents a collection of songs that exude the same authenticity. With her bare-all approach, Kimes has built a reputation as one of the most compelling emerging voices in the Americana scene. Her songs carry grit, warmth, and the hushed tone of a well-kept secret. 
Vivid lyrical imagery paired with a spirit that is deeply relatable and intimately her own is only bolstered by a rafter-reaching vocal talent.
