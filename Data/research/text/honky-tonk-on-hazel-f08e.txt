Gabriel is at home anywhere acoustic.
From Bach suites to bar stages, Gabriel has carved out a niche for himself in Philadelphia’s acoustic music wold. While most active and visible as a sideman, backing up his favorite local bands, Gabriel’s scope ranges from contemporary classical to old-time and bluegrass music.
