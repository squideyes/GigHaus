​The Emperor's New Clothes 
Nov 6 - 22 2026
Adapted from the Hans Christian Anderson tale 
by Laura Connors Hull and Lenny Mendez
Music by Matt Conner and Stephen Gregory Smith 
​
​A vain Emperor almost bankrupts his kingdom by feeding his obsession for lavish clothes. When some rogues disguised as weavers offer to supply the king with a magnificent wardrobe the King is intrigued. Their beautiful colors and elaborate patterns, they state, are invisible only to those who are either incompetent or stupid. The King and his entire court pretend to see the non-existent clothes to avoid looking foolish until an innocent child steps up to proclaim the real truth. This humor-filled, Spanish infused Learning Theater production, reminds us that one should never pretend to believe something just to follow the crowd and… it takes courage to speak the truth.
A Learning Theater Production - Perfect for the whole family! Recommended ages 4 to 104!!
Estimated Run Time: 1 Hour

Madeline's Christmas
December 4 - 20, 2026
​Based on the book by Ludwig Bemelmans
Book & Lyrics by Jennifer Kirkeby
Music by Shirley Mier
Directed by Matt Conner
Music Direction by Bharati Soman
A Holiday tradition at Creative Cauldron! “In an old house in Paris, covered in vines, lived 12 little girls in two straight lines.” And with that familiar phrase, author and illustrator Ludwig Bemelmans sets in motion the adventures of the brave and resourceful Madeline! In this musical adaptation, Madeline’s schoolmates and tutor are all sick in bed on Christmas Eve, unable to go home for Christmas to be with their families. So it’s Madeline to the rescue! And with the help of a magical rug merchant she takes her friends on a Christmas journey they will never forget. 
Recommended for Ages 4 to 104!!​

Creative Cauldron was founded by our Producing Director, Laura Connors Hull, in February 2002. Since its incorporation Creative Cauldron has grown tremendously and has called the Little City of Falls Church home since taking up residence in ArtSpace Falls Church in 2009.
OUR MISSION
Guided by the belief that creativity is a fundamental need of the human family, Creative Cauldron is dedicated to providing affordable, enriching, and diverse experiences in the performing and visual arts to Northern Virginia and the greater Washington, DC community.
Creative Cauldron gives people of all ages, incomes and backgrounds opportunities to connect and grow through the arts. We create original productions, present a broad array of art and artists, and offer transformative educational programs in an intimate and collaborative atmosphere.
​Our programs and performances currently serve approximately 12,000 patrons annually from the City of Falls Church and surrounding counties as well as from Maryland, the District and visitors to the DC metro area. Many of our productions are completely original works conceived of by our Founder and Producing Director, Laura Connors Hull, alongside our amazing circle of teaching artists, including acclaimed composer Matt Conner and scenic designer Margie Jervis. Thirteen of Creative Cauldron's productions have been Helen Hayes Awards © Recommended - Nevermore, Cole Porter: You're the Top!, Thunder Knocking on the Door, Marry Me a Little, Jacques Brel is Alive and Well and Living in Paris, The Turn of the Screw, Once on This Island, The World Goes 'Round, Monsters of the Villa Diodati, Caroline, or Change, Ruthless! The Musical, Blues in the Night, Kaleidoscope, Lady Day at Emerson's Bar and Grill and the 2025 revival of The Turn of The Screw. Jacques Brel actor Alan Naylor won the 2015 Helen Hayes Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Musical (Helen Production) and both Iyona Blake and Tiara Whaley brought home the 2017 Helen Hayes Award for Outstanding Lead Actress and Supporting Actress in a Musical (Helen Production). Monsters of the Villa Diodati actor Sam Ludwig was nominated in 2017 for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Musical and Creative Cauldron also received five Helen Hayes Award nominations in 2016 for Once on This Island (Outstanding Musical, Outstanding Supporting Actress - Jade Jones) and The Turn of the Screw (Outstanding Ensemble, Outstanding Supporting Actor - Ethan Miller, Outstanding Director - Matt Conner & Stephen Gregory Smith).
Our Programs
Live Theater
Professional, affordable theater in an intimate black-box theater
Learning Theater
A unique and inclusive youth drama program for youth 
Bold New Works
Creative Cauldron's program for creative, unique original musicals
Classes and Camps
Educational programs for youth ages 4 and up in both drama and visual arts
Creative Cauldron strives to make all of its programs affordable and accessible to the community it serves with reasonable ticket prices and enrollment fees as well as providing scholarships for those families in need to ensure that those with a passion for the arts are not denied the opportunity to express themselves!
​
We believe that the community is a family and we look forward to having you be a part of ours here at Creative Cauldron!

Creative Cauldron was founded by our Producing Director, Laura Connors Hull, in February 2002. Since its incorporation Creative Cauldron has grown tremendously and has called the Little City of Falls Church home since taking up residence in ArtSpace Falls Church in 2009. In 2025, Creative Cauldron moved into a new state of the art theatre space in the mixed use development at Broad and Washington in Falls Church. 
OUR MISSION
Guided by the belief that creativity is a fundamental need of the human family, Creative Cauldron is dedicated to providing affordable, enriching, and diverse experiences in the performing and visual arts to Northern Virginia and the greater Washington, DC community.
Creative Cauldron gives people of all ages, incomes and backgrounds opportunities to connect and grow through these experiences. We produce innovative works of theater, including original premieres, present a broad array of musical artists, and offer transformative educational programs in an intimate and collaborative atmosphere.
​
​
OUR VALUES
We provide a home for creativity in all of our programs - a nurturing environment in which artists and students feel they have the freedom to explore their ideas, and can achieve their highest artistic goals.
We recognize that we live and work in a community with broad diversity (ethnic, racial, cultural, age, gender, sexual orientation, disability) and appreciate and value the richness that this brings to our lives. We embrace and encourage a diversity of voices, stories, and art forms, and reflect that in our programming.
We foster an environment for intergenerational engagement and support the human family in all of its complexity through our educational programs, our theatrical productions, and our outreach in the community.
We make our programs accessible and affordable, removing barriers that keep patrons unable to participate and enjoy the arts.
We believe in equitable pay for artists and staff and that people should have an anti-oppressive, anti-racist environment to do their work and create artistry.
We are a listening and learning organization that stays open to feedback from artists and audiences, with special attention to Black, Indigenous, People of Color(The Global Majority), LGBTQ+, persons with disabilities, and those who are underrepresented in our industry.

Live Theater

Learning Theater

Creative Cauldron strives to make all of its programs affordable and accessible to the community it serves with reasonable ticket prices and enrollment fees as well as providing scholarships for those families in need to ensure that those with a passion for the arts are not denied the opportunity to express themselves!
​
We believe that the community is a family and we look forward to having you be a part of ours here at Creative Cauldron!

Ticket Policies
We have been so excited to see our audiences grow with each production. Unfortunately, our space has not grown with our audiences and with more sold out performances we must set strict ticket policies that you need to be aware of.
We strongly encourage you to purchase your tickets online at least 72 hours in advance of the performance you wish to see
Carefully double check the date you have selected when purchasing online to avoid showing up for the wrong performance
All performances, including those deemed "Family Friendly" require a minimum age of 4 years old as most performances last over an hour with no intermission. 
You should check-in at the Box Office no later than fifteen (15) minutes prior to the start of the performance. Late arrivals are seated at the sole discretion of the House Management. Additionally, exchanges into other performances as available are also subject to the sole discretion of House Management. Per ticketing policies, late arrival tickets are not refundable nor eligible to be applied as future credit. Patrons causing disruption within the lobby or the theater will be directed to leave the premises. 
We retain the right to re-sell or donate late arrival and unused tickets (seats) without notice, refund or future credit. 
We do NOT refund any pre-purchased tickets but when possible, are happy to exchange your tickets for another performance if we are notified 24 hours in advance of an issue preventing your attendance. We do not refund processing and other service fees.
Large group tickets may be reserved in advance by coordinating via the box office directly. 
When available, Senior ticket pricing is reserved for patrons age 62 or better. Proof of age may be required. 
As a part of the NoVA community, Creative Cauldron welcomes the chance to contribute to local auctions, raffles, fundraisers, and other events by offering ticket vouchers for the ongoing or upcoming seasons. Due to the volume of requests, we generally only accept voucher requests from registered community nonprofits. All other vouchers are approved on a case-by-case basis by Cauldron leadership.Questions??
