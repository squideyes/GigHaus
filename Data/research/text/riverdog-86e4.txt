​Riverdog is a 20 acre private estate in Henrietta Township about 5 miles west of Oberlin, Ohio.
The owners periodically host house concerts in an historic barn by invitation.
All donations collected are passed on to the performers.

Concerts are held in our historic barn with two attached tents and open air seating if desired. Tables and chairs are provided.
If the show is not sold out, walk-ups will be welcome.
Guests are invited to bring whatever they wish to eat or drink to our shows, including alcoholic beverages. Coolers are welcome.
No food or beverages are sold at Riverdog. 
​All funds are given to the artists.
​
There is no smoking inside the venue.
​​To reserve seats: CLICK HERE
Be sure to tell us which show (s), how many seats. We take reservations in order and try to honor seating requests but we cannot guarantee a specific location.
All reservations include a table/chairs. Parking is free.
Personal event chairs may be used in the open area only.
We can usually cluster large groups.
We will send a secure link to pay your donation.
You may also pay by check and if there is enough time to mail it, we can tell you how to do that.
We also accept contributions via Venmo payments at: @terry-speer-6 (2189, if needed).
You may also pay using Zelle: [email protected]

Children at Riverdog
Although we prefer to not exclude anyone from our shows, we have found that many children become bored with music at a "listening" venue and the distractions can make it less enjoyable for other audience members. We kindly request that rambunctious youngsters stay at home. That said, we are thrilled to have young people who are really excited to experience the music.
Visiting dogs at Riverdog
Unless your dogs are friends with our dogs, we ask that you leave them home...we will all be happier.

We are part of the Folk Alliance International approved house concert venues. As members, we are exempt from paying royalties to BMI and ASCAP. We ask that artists who perform here should avoid reporting their playlists if they belong to these organizations.
We accept independent artists as well as artists affiliated with BMI and ASCAP.
We are unable to accommodate artists affiliated with SESAC, nor can music licensed by SESAC or BMI be performed.
Musicians who wish to be considered for a performance at Riverdog should first consider the type of roots-Americana music that we focus on before contacting us. 
(progressive bluegrass, folk/Celtic, old-time, roots-rock/blues/jazz, songwriter)
We are also focused mostly upon artists who create music, rather than simply perform it.
Although creative interpretations of the music of others is welcome as a part of any show, we generally do not consider "cover" bands or "tribute" bands. We have nothing against them, but it is not our thing.
We also mostly have artists who have a "focus" rather than willing to play any genre we wish. If your regular gig is a winery or wedding, it will probably not work here.
Please correspond by email: [email protected]
Please do not call with booking questions!
"The best answer after 'yes' is 'no'." Ray Bonneville
Unless you are locally known, it is unlikely that you will obtain a booking here unless you have the following:
​
1. Website
2. High quality photos
3. High quality videos.
4. Social media links.
5. A clear definition of what you are as an artist.
​
​Although slick music videos may help us promote a show, for booking purposes, we prefer a video taken at a live performance. We highly recommend hiring a professional videographer who can record directly off the soundboard to minimize venue noise. We strongly recommend shooting the performance from multiple angles and have it professionally edited. Although this can cost some money, we believe it will pay off in the end with higher quality bookings.
If you are a touring band, Riverdog Is located about an hour from Cleveland or Akron. We are about 2 hours from Columbus and about 2 1/2 hours from Ann Arbor.
The bulk of our enquiries are from solo singer/songwriters. As much as we admire many of them, they are the toughest category to build an audience for so we can only book a very limited number of solo shows.

A not-for-profit grassroots venue for the enjoyment of Americana music by artists who create it or interpret it in unique ways.
​Riverdog is located on a private 20 acre property five miles west of Oberlin, Ohio, at the edge of the beautiful Vermilion River Valley. Concerts, which began in 2013, are informal, intimate and comfortable for guests. No one has to squeeze into an auditorium seat, no one has to stand and no one has to spend a fortune for a ticket or a beverage! Riverdog is not a business...we sell no food or beverages and guests may bring in whatever they like to eat or drink. Like a "house concert", only in a barn...donations collected are used to support the artists who perform.
​​
Amy LaVere
