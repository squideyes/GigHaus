FAQs 
 Are you dog friendly?
Yes, we love dogs. We do require any dogs that are visiting to be leashed and controlled by their owners at all times. Please not we have a house dog that wanders about but minds her business
 Are you 21+?
Most shows are 21+ unless you are accompanied by a parent. Please see individual show descriptions for specifics. During the day we are 100% family friendly but require children to be watched at all times. 
 Are kids free? 
Kids 12 and under do not need a ticket for music. 
 Do you offer to-go beer?
We will happily pour you a growler but we do not package at this time (nor do we plan to). 
 Where else can I find your beer?
We distribute locally. You can find our beer at Morning Social, The Hub, Cedar Mountain Canteen, Cedar Mountain Outpost, Headwater’s Outfitters
 Where can we park?
We unfortunately do not have a private parking lot for our business. There is street parking available and the city-owned lot at the Railroad Depot just a short walk down the road at: 390 Railroad Ave, Brevard, NC 28712
 When are doors/when does ticketing begin
On the day of a show we are generally open before the show starts. If you are coming to hang out but do not plan to stay for the show we do last call about a ½ hour before the show starts. We check for hand stamps at the beginning of the show and ask that you decide if you are going to stay or not at that point in time. We do have a small seating area in the front of our business where you can grab a beer and hang out if you are not wanting to pay for live music.
 How do I reserve a table?
First, check if the show is general admission or seated. If the show is general admission we do not take table reservations. If the show is listed as seated and there are only general admission tickets left that means that the tables are sold out. We do have plenty of general admission space for shows both indoors and out in our backyard
 Is your venue indoor or outdoor?
Both! The stage is at the back of our venue and there is a garage door right behind the stage that we keep open in good weather. This allows guests to enjoy music from inside or outside in Brevard’s Backyard.
 Are you a restaurant? 
We have a menu that can be found here. We operate café style so you will order at the front of the venue/bar and then your food is brought out to you.

185 King Street 
185 King Street opened their doors in 2011 with the mission of providing a space to support local musicians. At the time all the surrounding commercial real estate was empty, a skeleton left from the closure of Brevard Lumber in 2008. The concept of opening a music venue on King Street was met with a decent amount of skepticism and opposition, but founder Jackson Wine and supporters from the community worked tirelessly to figure out a way to make it work. Over a decade later, 185 is thriving and serves as a pillar in the regional music scene.
Current owners, Madeline Magin and Cody Noble took the helm in November 2019 with the vision of adding a brewery to the space. The future was very uncertain when the Covid-19 pandemic broke out mere months later. Fortunately for them, the number of supporters in the community had grown over the years and when it was time to re-open they were ready to ensure 185 would survive. The brewery was able to open by the fall of 2020, the kitchen was built out, and the backyard was transformed into a biergarten. Come join us for music, food and brews in Brevard’s backyard.

 Blue Ridge Betty 
 Buy Tickets 
 Saturday, November 14
 Doors: 12 pm // Show: 8 pm 
Blue Ridge Betty is a 6-piece Americana Groove band who burst onto the live music scene of Western NC in the Fall of 2024. In less than 2 years, they’ve released two albums of original music, independently booked slots at beloved regional festivals Toe Down (June 2026) and Albino Skunk Fest (October 2025), and traveled from the High Country to the Upstate spreading their passion for live performance. Blue Ridge Betty has something to offer every music lover with their genre-crossing repertoire. Sets often include classic rock covers from bands like Pink Floyd and Derek and the Dominos, to clever mashups like their Talking Heads/David Bowie/Grateful Dead trifecta, to traditional bluegrass and contemporary folk, alongside a growing collection of original music that reflects their many influences. Though the setlists are always changing, Blue Ridge Betty remains grounded in their joy and gratitude for each opportunity to share their music with their audience and each other. Come see for yourself and let them know what genre they should add to the mix next!
Venue and ticketing information:
We are family friendly, but shows are 21+ unless accompanied by a parent.
This is a general admission show. There are tables within the venue that are available on a first come, first serve basis. 
There is a garage door behind the stage that remains open in good weather so that you can enjoy the show from inside or outside.
For more information visit our FAQ page or reach out to us directly.
