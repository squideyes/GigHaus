ABOUT US
Since 1999, we have been hosting local and national musicians here in the comfort of our home and our backyard in Billerica, MA.
100% of all money collected goes to the performers (and their managers, booking agents etc). We do it for the love of the music and to share music we love with others in the community. Come join us to see what it's all about and be sure to signup on the email list to find out who will be here and when!
