 Wine & Song - Gráinne Hunt with Jules Stewart
 Wednesday, November 25 @ 7:00PM — 9:30PMWed, Nov 25 @ 7:00PM — 9:30PM
 Square Deal courtyard, 1108 Mission Street, South Pasadena, CA
In the courtyard behind Square Deal barbershop. 1108 Mission Street, South Pasadena. Beverages and light snacks available, or bring your own. Doors 6:00, music 7:00. After ticket purchase, your name will be on a list at the door, so no need to bring a ticket.
Irish songstress Gráinne Hunt enchants audiences with her heart-melting vocals and emotional songs. Glen Hansard called her “The hardest working woman in Irish music.” Her two original records and new live EP draw from deeply personal experiences, weaving stories of pain, longing and joy, earning her comparisons to Tracy Chapman and Natalie Merchant. Beyond her solo work, Gráinne thrives in collaboration, working with award-winning guitarist Brendan Walsh on Songs from Ireland, and Hibsen, a literary folk project based on James Joyce’s “Dubliners”. She’ll be joined by percussionist and backing vocalist Jules Stewart.
Double Knot is an Americana and bluegrass sibling duo consisting of Anna Lee Fleming and Wili Fleming. The two are stalwarts in the San Diego Americana scene and the Steamboat Strings camp. They are cultural composers leading yearly orchestrations that include celebrations of music such as the 2026 "Oh Brother Where Art Thou” orchestrated performance. They actively perform in San Diego County and at festivals on the West Coast.

About
When LA singer-songwriter, Brad Colerick, signed on to host eight Wednesday night events in 2009, he had no idea what was in store. Word spread quickly about the great songs and stories being shared, and the impromptu performances from surprise guests each week. It quickly “bubbled into an inviting community hub for musicians, songwriters, wine-geeks and [music fans.…]” wrote Bliss Bowen of the Pasadena Weekly. Fifteen years, multiple venues, and a pandemic later, it's still going strong.
