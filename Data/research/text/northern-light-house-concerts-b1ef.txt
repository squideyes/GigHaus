Robinson Treacher
It’s been several years since Robinson has played Northern Light, but he’s one of our favorites.
At the crossroads of Blues, Folk, Americana, and Country, you’ll find the music of Robinson Treacher. He has shared the stage with notable artists from Vince Gill, Arlo Guthrie, and Tedeschi Trucks Band to Los Lobos, Iris Dement, G.E. Smith, Jorma Kaukonen, and Willie Nile. Robinson has been featured on Sirius/XM’s series, “The Loft: Live from The Living Room,” and has been a frequent guest of legendary DJ John Platt on New York’s famed WFUV Radio. Hall of Fame songwriter Don Schlitz has even touted Robinson as being, “ … impossible to follow in concert.”
He’s always got something new. Can’t wait to see this guy again……

Recent Events
Becky Shaw with Dawn Shaw Patrick
June 29, 2025
What fun, to have these two sisters sharing the stage at Northern Light on a warm Sunday afternoon.
Becky Shaw is a talented singer/songwriter and”multi-instrumentalist” from Decatur Georgia. She has been performing across the United States as a solo and also with Just Roxie, a popular Atlanta band. She has appeared on many stages including the Bluebird Cafe (Nashville) and The Pier (Seattle.) Having just returned from a tour in Colorado, we were fortunate to be able to host her at Northern Light.
When we booked her for our concert, she told us that we would bring her sister, Dawn Shaw Patrick, as a special guest because she lives in Mendham NJ.
We were very happy she did! These two sisters brought a synergy to the stage that wowed the audience. Dawn’s violin playing fit right in with Becky’s original music, and these sisters completely entranced 45 or so lucky people who were in the audience.
Dawn Shaw Patrick is a talented violin player from Mendham NJ. She and Becky were raised together in a musical family in the Seattle WA area. Over time, each went their separate ways but both stayed in music. Dawn plays with Wilma the band, a rocking female trio who play in our area. Also books solo gigs for events at Melodic Meanderings.
She accompanied her sister beautifully, both with her violin and with her harmonies. We were extremely pleased that she had many family members (her children) in attendance with friends from the area. They were able to see these two talented sisters play live music together. What a treat!
Kray Van Kirk 
June 1, 2025
Kray opened our 2025 season with an outstanding concert.
This was our first time seeing Kray in a live performance and it certainly was worth the wait. He wove his magic throughout the afternoon playing many of our favorite songs including My Lover was a Sailor, O my Fathers, The Library Song, The Midnight Commander, and Lost Lovers Waltz. His Toll Booth Ladies was especially appreciated by the mostly New Jersey audience. Of course he played Days of Mercy and two of our personal favorites, The House about Kirk Michael, and Shiloh. He closed with a haunting version of one we had never heard, The Fisherman and the Selkie. 
If you haven’t seen or heard Kray Van Kirk we suggest you become familiar with his music. We’ve become big fans!
Rupert Wates
October 13, 2024
For our final event of 2024, Rupert Wates performed on a brilliant fall day in October.
Rupert Wates can best be described as a troubadour. His travels bring him to all 50 states, Canada, and Europe singing original songs and ballads in the best tradition of troubadours. He draws his inspirations for his songs from true stories and from classical stories of days of olde.
True to his way, he did two sets of original music, delighting the audience of about 25 with humor, stories and song. Songs included If I ran to you, Guinevere, A florin to the beam, and the incomparable Days of Mercy. The beautiful music and stories matched the flawless day.
The Kennedys
September 22, 2024
“And we find at the end of a perfect day, the soul of a friend we’ve made.” Carrie Jacobs-Bond
The Kennedys were the first performers at Northern Light when we opened back in 2019. They have been back several times since because we, and all our friends who attend Northern Light concerts, love them.
All the stars and planets aligned for this one. The weather was grand, the food was wonderful, and the music was spectacular.
Courtesy Brian McCluskey
The Kennedys opened with Life is Large and continued with several songs from their latest album, Headwinds, including the title track. They closed their first set with River of Fallen Stars, which is one of my personal favorites. The second set featured a variety of songs from their large repertoire of music. They closed with three covers of Beatles songs, which brought a number of people from the audience up to dance. Everyone was smiling as they closed out the day.
So, “at the end of a perfect day,” we found ourselves with many renewed friendships as well as many new ones. We’ll be seeing Pete and Maura again next September. Stay tuned! 
Jesse Terry
June 23,2024
Jesse Terry performed at Northern Light for a second time in June 2024. He is a consummate performer, and he lived up to expectations again. We had to bring the concert indoors because of rain, but the more intimate setting worked well for this show.
Jesse did a mixture of songs from some of his published albums including Strangers in our Town, Hanging the Stars, and Star Gazer. He also gave us a preview of songs from his new (yet unpublished) album, Arcadia.
Jesse is a crowd favorite and it was demonstrated by the standing ovation he received at the end of the show.
Robinson Treacher
August 27, 2023
Robinson was back at Northern Light by popular demand two years after his 2021 concert. He always exceeds our expectations. He mixes his original music with stories and commentary which keeps the audience very entertained. He played some of his familiar favorites, such as Born and Woman, as well as a variety of songs he has written in the two years since we last had him at Northern Light. The audience ate it up. One person, who has been at many Northern Light shows, remarked that he felt it was our best show yet!

Kevin Higgins & Barbara Maltese 
May 21, 2023
Northern Light hosted this talented couple for their first engagement in our venue. Kevin and Barbara are well known in this part of the country, having toured here for over 15 years. The audience of new and long time fans was treated to another one of their wonderful performances.
Their two sets included such favorites as The Levee Boys, Curtains, and Infinity, and a cover of John Prine’s Clay Pigeons as well as a segment featuring Barbara’s music. The second set also moved into some of the new music that Kevin has been writing since they moved to New York State. Their performance of Sundowners (Bag of Mixed Blessings) brought a hush to audience.
The beautiful weather for an outdoor concert, and the appreciative audience made for a memorable afternoon in May. They will be invited back again.
Craig Bickhardt
September 25, 2022
We had to bring our late September concert indoors (for the first time) because of weather. We were hoping the indoor setting would work well for comfortable seating and acoustics.
Craig excelled in this more intimate setting. The audience of about 40 people was comfortably seated when Craig started with Prayers for You. He played two 45 minutes sets which included some new as-yet unpublished songs along with some old favorites including You are what love means to me, Even a cowboy can cry, This old house, If he came back again, and In between dances,
People who know him knew what to expect, but many people who are new to his music were wowed by this show. He delighted the audience with lead in stories before most of his songs. He finished to a standing ovation.
This was a very successful first indoor show at Northern Light and a great way to end the 2022 season.

She performed songs from her new album Last Days of Summer along with many favorites from previous albums.

About House Concerts
Craig Bickhardt telling one of his stories
Could anything be better than an afternoon listening to world class music in an intimate setting with a small group of friends? Well that’s what a house concert is. Check out this CNN article about the house concert scene.
The host invites musicians to do a show at their home. They gather a group of friends and fans for the concert. Usually there is a pot-luck meal before and after the show. People bring a covered dish and something to drink.
There is a suggested donation which goes entirely to the musicians. Ours is usually $25 each. ALL the money that is collected goes to the musicians. If the musicians are touring they are often invited to stay at the host’s home overnight to help with travelling expenses.
All donations go directly to the performers.

About Us
Why Northern Light?
When we purchased our property in 2010 we were not familiar with house concerts. By the time we started designing our home we had been to many house concerts at Foul Rift, Silo Series, and Hobbit Hill.
We wanted to add to this wonderful community of music lovers and to be able to also promote local artists in a gallery in which they could show off their art.
So we designed our house with these two ideas in mind. In 2017 we completed construction and moved in. It took another two years to have our first concert with The Kennedys and also our first gallery showing with Brian McCluskey’s beautiful photographs of musicians he has seen over the years.
We named our venue Northern Light because our house is situated to take advantage of the light from the north and the beautiful view of the sky we get from our decks and patio. We hope you notice the gorgeous view and 360 degree view of the sky when you visit.
Barbara and Kevin are both New Jersey born and bred with a little bit of Texas thrown in. We have 3 children (well- actually adults now), 8 grandchildren, and 2 great grands. We moved to Austin in the 1980’s for Kevin to attend The University of Texas where he was graduated with a degree in engineering. We lived in Texas for five years before returning to our roots in New Jersey.
We share a passion for horses. We have owned and boarded horses at Northern Light for years. Sadly, we lost our last horse, Altair, in the winter of 2024 so we no longer have these beautiful animals in our barn. But we will always have a special place in our heart for them.
Barbara is a former electrologist who owned Bel Esprit Skin Care Center, an adoptee advocate who started a progressive adoption group in Austin, graduated Warren County Community college with highest honors at the age of 67, and is now a working potter.
We’re both passionate about live music. Kevin loves riding and working with horses as well as riding his bicycle on the beautiful roads of Warren and Hunterdon counties.
Barbara with Orion, a very sweet Morgan X Percheron gelding.
Some of our friends have asked us why we do the house concerts because they involve so much work and (often) stress. The answer is simple– we are doing our best to support the arts, to support and encourage these talented musicians who so graciously share their craft, and to bring these wonderful experiences to our friends and family. Music is important; it is the universal language. It connects us humans with each other in the most fundamental way. In our world, which is so fraught with division and anxiety, it is a bridge to each other. We are trying to make our little corner of the world a better place.
Kevin with Altair

Artist Showplace
Photographer Brian McCloskey with his show of photographs of musicians from concerts he has attended.
There are many talented painters, sculptors, and photographers who need a place to display their work. We’ve provided and inside art gallery so that local artists can display their work for concert guests.
Occasionally we’ll invite various artists to do their own show. They are able to arrange a private showing for their own guests– and of course the show will be open to our house concert guests.
The art may or may not be for sale– the idea is for the artist to gain exposure and for all of us to enjoy their creations.
