Tupelo’s home stage for original music since 2011. Songwriters, jam bands, country, blues, and whatever else sounds good loud. Gary Clark Jr., Alabama Shakes, Sturgill Simpson, Tyler Childers, Leon Bridges, Red Clay Strays​​​​​, as well as and many, many more have played this room before they were household names. National acts route through Tupelo because they know what they’re getting.
Forty-six rotating taps​​​​​​​ anchor one of the deepest beer walls in north Mississippi. The cheeseburger landed in Mississippi Magazine’s top ten​​​​, and our Connie’s Blueberry Doughnut Bread Pudding has hauled home five Best Dessert titles​​​ from the same.

Otis Walker Band 
November 14 @ 8:30 pm 
Free show!! • Genre: They describe their sound as “Musical Gumbo”—a blend of Delta blues, New Orleans funk, Southern soul, roots rock, and Americana. The band is fronted by Otis Walker, who plays piano, Wurlitzer electric piano, Hammond B3 organ, and harmonica while handling lead vocals. Their swampy, soulful vibe draws inspiration from legends like Dr. John, Otis Redding, and The Allman Brothers.
