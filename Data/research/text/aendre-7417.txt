ændre
i)verb, danish:
to shift, to alter
ii) an arts collaborative and destination in the Driftless Region 
our mission
ændre is dedicated to uniting creative talents in visual, performance, and culinary art to celebrate and enrich the culture and community of the Driftless Region

the heartbeat of our nonprofit is a growing community of artists and art lovers celebrating the power of dynamic visual, auditory, and taste experiences to explore place and bring communities together. 
ændre directors Sarah Farrell and James Mackessy bring together a diverse range of experience in the service industry, performance art, design-build, installation art, gallery and event curation. ændre’s board is dedicated to supporting the arts through collaboration, production, and promotion.
join us at our next art happening and participate as we develop and curate an artistic gathering space set on an old 16 acre farm site in the natural splendor of the Driftless region.
ændre arthouse is a non-profit organization 501(c)3. As such ændre arthouse does not discriminate based on gender, age, or race. We are an equal-opportunity organization that seeks to serve the community through enriching arts, music, and cultural events.
