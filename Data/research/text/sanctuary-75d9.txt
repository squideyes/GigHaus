Why Sanctuary? 
More Than a Music Venue 
Sanctuary is a nonprofit cultural arts center dedicated to bringing people together
 through music, arts, education, and community engagement.
Every ticket purchased, membership purchased, volunteer hour donated, and event
 attended helps support a thriving cultural community in downtown Maynard.

Welcome to Sanctuary 
Sanctuary is a 5,000 square foot concert and event space. With high ceilings,
 historic non-denominational stained-glass windows, grand chandeliers and a
 state-of-the-art sound and lighting system, Sanctuary is the perfect backdrop
 for all types of celebrations and meetings.
Fully Customizable Events 
Sanctuary has hosted a variety of events, including concerts, private parties and
 events, weddings, fundraisers, celebrations of life and bar mitzvahs. We enjoy
 working with clients to customize their special events, creating an unforgettable
 experience in a venue that combines historic charm with modern elegance.
Our Services 
Sanctuary's event team aims to assist throughout your party planning journey,
 ensuring a streamlined experience from start to finish.
Our basic rental package gives you access to the main event hall and stage, the bar
 and lounge area, as well as a private suite where you can catch your breath during
 a day filled with excitement and celebration.
Why Choose Sanctuary? 
 Historic Charm - A beautifully restored church with over 150 years
 of history with its grand ceilings, stained-glass windows, and elegant chandeliers
 Customizable Space - Tailor the lighting and décor to match your
 unique vision and style. A variety of seating and dance floor layouts are available
 to suit you and your guests needs.
 Modern Amenities - Enjoy a stylish lounge area, private bridal
 suite and a full bar to keep the festivities flowing all night long.

About 
Sanctuary Cultural Arts Center was launched in 2019 with a mission to support
 the community and the arts, to host great live entertainment, and to preserve
 82 Main Street and its legacy.
We are committed to bringing outstanding live entertainment to downtown
 Maynard and showing love and support to this incredible community.
Our Vision 
To create a vibrant gathering place where people of all ages and backgrounds
 connect through music, arts, education, and shared experiences.

FAQ 
Location & Parking 
 Where is Sanctuary Cultural Arts Center located? 
Sanctuary Cultural Arts Center is located at 82 Main Street in Maynard, MA. We are 40 minutes west of Boston, 30 minutes northeast of Worcester and 25 minutes south of Lowell. 
 Where can I park? 
Because we are located on Main Street, there is ample public parking available. Visit townofmaynard-ma.gov for more information. Please note from December 1 - March 15 there is no parking on the street overnight from 2am to 6am. 
 Can I go to the bar if I’m not attending the concert or event in the main hall? 
Yes, check Bar & Lounge for hours of operation. The bar is accessible through the River Street entrance on the left side of the building. This door will be locked when doors open for our main hall shows. On nights that we have a sold out show in the main hall, the bar will be closed to non-ticket holders beginning at “Doors” for the concert. At this time, non-ticket holders will be asked to settle up and exit the building. On nights that we have a private event, the bar will be closed to the general public. Please check our social media for updates. 
General Questions 
 What is your capacity? 
Sanctuary is a 5,000 square foot multi-purpose facility with a general admission capacity of 367 and a seated capacity of 269. 
 Is Sanctuary Cultural Arts Center wheelchair accessible? 
Yes. Please use the ramp to the right of the front doors to enter the building. ADA requests should be directed to [email protected]. 
 Is Sanctuary Cultural Arts Center hearing accessible? 
Yes. To access our WaveCAST WiFi system, please download the app onto your phone in advance of the show you are attending here: https://williamsav.com/product/wavecast-app/. Upon arrival, please ask to speak with a sound engineer who will give you a password to access the router. During the show, audio is streamed to an app on your personal device, which can then connect via Bluetooth to an assistive listening device. 
 Where can I get tickets? 
Grab tickets to our shows by clicking on “Concerts and Events” and selecting “Buy Tickets” on the event of your choice. We can only guarantee the validity of tickets purchased through our website. Under no circumstance do we advise purchasing a ticket through a third party website or through Facebook or other social media platforms. Please be aware that it is a common scam to “resell” tickets this way. 
 Can I bring a bag with me? 
Small bags and purses are allowed. No oversized bags, purses, duffle bags or backpacks are allowed. All bags may be subjected to search. 
 Am I allowed to bring drinks or food with me? 
No outside beverages are allowed inside Sanctuary when the bar is open. This includes personal water bottles. At this time, you are allowed to bring food into the venue. Maynard Pizza Bar will deliver right to your table - scan the QR code on your table to order and receive a 10% discount. (Please check MPB’s website for hours of operation before ordering.) El Hupil also serves tacos every Tuesday in the main hall. 
 Do you have a coat check? 
There is an unmonitored coat rack by the door that you are welcome to use. We are not responsible for lost or stolen items. 
 Does my young child need a ticket to attend an event with me? 
Everyone who attends an event at Sanctuary needs a ticket. Unless specifically noted, shows at Sanctuary are “All Ages.” 
 Am I allowed to bring a camera? 
Phone camera use is permitted but flash photography is not allowed. 
 Am I allowed to reenter the venue if I leave during a show? 
Yes, all attendees will receive a wristband upon admission that will give you access back into the venue. Reentry is only permitted through the front doors by the ticketing counter. 
Attending A Show 
 What is the refund policy? 
All sales are final. No refunds will be issued unless an event is cancelled. 
 If a show is cancelled, how do I get a refund? 
If you purchased a ticket online through our website, you will be refunded automatically. All other refunds can be processed at the point of purchase. 
 I cannot attend. How do I resell my ticket? 
You can transfer tickets to a friend or family member using the instructions found here: https://help.opendate.io/transfer. 
 I purchased a ticket through a third party. How do I know it’s real? 
We can only guarantee the validity of tickets purchased through sanctuarymaynard.com. Under no circumstance do we advise purchasing a ticket through a third party website or through Facebook or other social media platforms. Please be aware that it is a common scam to “resell” tickets this way.
