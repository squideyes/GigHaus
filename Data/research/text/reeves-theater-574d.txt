Recurring Shows & Events:
The Reeves House Band
Join Bandleader Tommy Jackson as the Reeves House Band takes on a new exciting show every month. Past show themes include The Beatles, Led Zeppelin, The Grand Ole Opry, The Band’s “The Last Waltz,” and more!

The Martha Bassett Show
The Reeves is proud to partner with The Martha Bassett Show to bring you good music for good people! Tickets available now for this season’s shows, every 1st and 3rd Thursday of the month. Catch the show on 88.5 WFDD every Saturday at 7 PM, and online via their livestream performances. 
 Learn More
Free Music Wednesdays: Open Mic, Karaoke, and Songwriter Night!
Join us every Wednesday (except major holidays) for a free music. On the first (and occasional fourth) Wednesday, our regular open mic has sign-up at 6:30, show at 7. Covers and originals are welcome. The second Wednesday of the month is Band Night, where we provide the back line and bands get a chance to perform a few songs on our stage. We try to keep these shows PG-13, so please feel free to ask the host at sign-up if you have any questions about your material. We also have Karaoke on the third Wednesdays, also at 7.
One Wednesday per month is now the Reeves Open Mic Songwriter Night, and twice a year the winners of each songwriter night compete in the Songwriter Showcase for a chance to win a spot on The Martha Bassett Show! You can sign up for each month’s songwriter night until one week before the show, and twelve people will be randomly selected from those who sign up. Original songs only.

Joe Thrift & Friends Old-Time Jam
Join us in the lobby every fourth Thursday of the month (except major holidays) for an Old-Time Jam. Primarily focused on Surry County and other NC Old-Time traditional music, this jam is all-ages and free to watch or play. Jam starts around 5:30 PM, and is hosted by Joe Thrift and his colleagues and friends.
This event is generously sponsored by Explore Elkin.

Watch our documentary!
Want to learn more about how the Reeves was renovated and reopened? Enjoy the documentary Reeves: A Home for Music by filmmaker Michael Lippert about the renovation and reopening process.
​Most people who grew up near Elkin, NC have fond memories of the Reeves. The beautiful Art -Deco movie house was originally opened in 1941, showing films and news reels of the day. Later, it was converted into the Elk Twin, also a movie theater, before eventually falling into disrepair. The people of Elkin knew this place was too special to lose, but times were tough and funds were low. Years passed and the Reeves sat empty, slowly being improved and preserved by dedicated citizens.
Finally, in 2013, the Reeves was up for sale, and three long-time Elkin residents decided to buy it and turn it into a live music venue. Employing and partnering with as many local craftspeople and manufacturers as possible, they fully restored the original building facade, and rebuilt the sign and marquee to the specifications of the original architectural plans. The inside was converted into a modern industrial music hall, with great acoustics and superior sound quality as the primary goal. 
In 2020, like so many small businesses, the Reeves was forced to close due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Reeves patrons rallied behind the venue and its staff to raise money and keep up an interest until a safe reopening was possible in the summer of 2021. 
At the end of 2024, after much careful consideration, the Reeves decided to close the café portion of the business and focus on live music, drinks, and events.
The Reeves Theater owners and staff hope the space will serve as a home for music, showcasing not only the rich local musical traditions of the Yadkin Valley and Foothills, but also the talents of artists from around the country and the world. The Reeves is a symbol of the strength and diversity of this beautiful region, the richness of tradition, and the dedication of the local community to art and music.

Quality music instruction for all ages featuring group classes, individual lessons, workshops, & more!
Our Mission at RDSM:
To provide exceptional year-round musical education and performance opportunities to students from our community and beyond.
To promote and encourage musicians of all genres through performances, workshops, and master classes; and to expose students and audiences to a diverse world of musical expression.
To enhance the artistic imprint of the Yadkin Valley region, and to encourage arts tourism.
To inspire future generations of musicians and music enthusiasts to keep musical traditions alive and to explore new forms of self-expression through instrumentation, sound, and rhythm.
