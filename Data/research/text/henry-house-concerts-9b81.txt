Henry House Concerts is an invitation-only private venue just south of Pacific, Missouri for emerging and established musicians. Musicians receive 100% of profits from all shows.
We don’t generally invite people we don’t know, but you can contact us and see. And, if you’re a musician, or know a musician, or are related to somebody that knows a musician who might want to play, contact us.
The artists we host generally run in the folk, singer-songwriter, and blues genres, but we’re pretty open minded.
