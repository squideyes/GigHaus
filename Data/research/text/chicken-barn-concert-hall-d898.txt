Chicken Barn Concert Hall 
A fabulous concert venue on Whidbey Island, WA featuring a wide array of talented musicians including local, national and international artists. 
Carol Flax, co-owner 
Ed Fickbohm, co-owner
Eric Jones, audio technician
chickenbarnconcerthall@gmail.com 
Fall/Winter seasons generally run from November through April and take place in our cozy indoor space with amazing acoustics. The space holds approximately 60 people and most concerts sell out quickly
Summer concerts are held outdoors and have had up to 250 people in attendance. They're sort of like a mini music festival with pot-luck dinner, lots of socializing and great music 
We ask for a $30 donation
For reservations and information chickenbarnconcerthall@gmail.com
We send out directions and additional info including summer start times a few days before each show.
find us on facebook
