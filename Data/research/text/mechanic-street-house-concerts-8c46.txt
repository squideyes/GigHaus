Teiku
Friday June 26, 2026
Doors open 7:30pm, music starts at 8:00pm
 "The interplay between instruments is masterful … Teiku is a testament to the power of music to transcend time and space, offering listeners a journey of introspection and spiritual connection." - Downbeat Magazine
"This music is so beautiful--I've already cried 3 times!" - Audience member, 15 minutes into Teiku's 2019 Mechanic Street House Concert
We hosted Teiku in 2019, and it is Joel's favorite of all our concerts. We are so excited to have them back! 
Chicago pianist Josh Harlow and Detroit percussionist Jonathan Barahal founded Teiku around the concept of using their respective families’ unique Passover melodies as conduits for new forms of spontaneous musical expression. They are joined by two stellar musicians, Jaribu Shahid (upright bass) and Jason Stein (bass clarinet). 
The result is powerful--think John Coltrane's "A Love Supreme" blended with Jewish music. They have been deepening and developing their music and their 2nd album, Klang, is coming out in June. 
Suggested donation is $25 per person, which all goes to the musicians.
We hope you can join us for this amazing concert! Please email us at team@mechanicstreetmusic.com to reserve your spots or for more info.

About Us
We love great live music. We don’t love going to see shows at venues with bad sound, high volume, and noisy, rude audiences. Our house concerts have the opposite: great sound, reasonable volume, and fun, attentive audiences.
We have about 6 house concerts per year. Most of the artists who have performed fall somewhere in the Americana spectrum. Our tastes are more diverse and we are looking to host a wider variety of artists. We often like hard to describe music that bridges multiple genres. Favorites include Buddy Miller, Kevin Welch, Brigitte DeMeyer, John Hiatt, brown bird, Etta James, the David Wax Museum, Gillian Welch, Willie Mason, Mulatu Astatke, gypsy jazz, and the Art Ensemble of Chicago. We like lively music with a bounce to it, not gentle, nod inducing singer-songwriters.
The life of the touring musician is tough, especially when they go to cities where they haven’t established a fan base. That is why we prefer to host shows for musicians from outside of Northeast Ohio. This also lets us introduce Cleveland audiences to great musicians they may not have heard before.
As you may have guessed, the shows are at our home, which is a large 100-year-old diamond-in-the-rough. It has lovely woodwork, cool leaded glass, an old mural, and – most importantly – great acoustics. It also houses 3 dogs and a cat. It’s in Cleveland’s Ohio City neighborhood, close to I-90 and many great restaurants.
Mechanic Street House Concerts isn't a business. We don't make any money from these private, invitation only events. A donation is requested, 100% of which goes to the artists who have spent a lifetime of hard work honing their skills to provide the best possible entertainment for their audience.
Its called Mechanic Street House Concerts because the street we live on used to be called Mechanic Street and we thought it had a nice ring to it.
Please sign up for our mailing list above on the right and we’ll keep you posted on upcoming house concerts. For anything else, please email us at mechanicstreetmusic@yahoo.com.
We hope you can join us for a show!
Lynn & Joel

House Concert 101
So just what is a House Concert?
A House Concert is simply a live musical performance hosted in someone’s home. It is the perfect way to experience amazingly talented independent artists in an up-close-and-personal setting. Here's an NPR story about house concerts and a Fresh Water Cleveland story about Mechanic Street House Concerts.
Mechanic Street House Concerts isn’t a business, and we don’t make any money from the performance. They are private, invitation only events. A donation is requested, 100% of which goes to the artists who have spent a lifetime of hard work honing their skills to provide the best possible entertainment for their audience.
 What to Expect 
Our concerts are non-smoking. There will be finger food. Feel free to bring your favorite beverage to share. There are usually two 40 minute sets, with chatting and chewing in between. Just come and plan on having a good time listening to great music, getting to know wonderful artists, and enjoying the company of new and old friends. Typically doors open at 7:30pm and the music starts at 8:00pm.
How to reserve a spot
RSVP by sending an email to mechanicstreetmusic@yahoo.com with your name and the names of up to three people who will be coming with you. If your group is larger than that, each person or couple should make their own reservation. Within 24 hours, we'll send you an email to confirm your seats. Then a day or two before the house concert, we'll send you an email with our address and other details.
What if I RSVP and then can't make it?
You'll never, ever get invited again! Kidding! Really, just kidding!
But please, consider your RSVP a firm commitment. Make sure everyone has it in their calendar, line up a babysitter, check out the artist's website, whatever else you do when planning a great evening out.
We know sometimes things happen to cause your plans to change. If so, please let us know as soon as possible that you won't be able to make it. It is increasingly common for us to reach capacity a few days beforehand and have a waiting list of those who would like to come. We don't want to turn anyone away unnecessarily, so please let us know -- even at the last moment. Especially since all of the money goes directly to the musicians, we don't want to have even one empty seat!
Why do I need to RSVP?
These are private events we are holding in our home. We need to know who and how many people are coming. This helps us determine how much food to prepare, how many seats we need to set up and the best arrangement of the space. It also is extremely helpful for the musicians to have a sense of the audience as they plan their performance.
Think you might like to come sometime?
If this sounds like fun, please sign up for our mailing list above on the right and we’ll keep you posted.

Duo Balalaika
Sunday January 18, 2026
"With music of élan, finesse and passion, so virtuosically rendered, this duo’s performances are irresistible."
- Robert Spano, Music Director, Atlanta Symphony Orchestra
Named for a Russian folk instrument, Northeast Ohio's Duo Balalaika
 is phenomenal! Siberian balalaika virtuoso Oleg Kruglyakov and American
 concert pianist Terry Boyarsky perform with the precision of classical 
musicians and the joy and energy of folk musicians. Their music draws on
 everything from Siberian folk music to classical music to tangos.
The duo is as entertaining as they are excellent--Oleg cracks us up every time.

Sara Jean Kelley
Thursday June 26, 2025
Doors open 6:30pm, music starts at 7:00pm 
"Kelley can hang with the Nashville legends whose path she follows, in her own unique style." - Shawn Donahue, Glide Magazine
"Ms.
 Kelley has a flirtatious vocal that has precision & grace. Songs 
are carefully captured, well-crafted & superb." - John Apice, Americana Highways 
If you love Brandi Carlisle, Patti Griffin, or Lucinda Williams, this house concert will be special.
Nashville native Sara Jean Kelley
 is a lover of wild things— plants, animals, people, and music. She 
crafts songs that combine upbeat, pop-inspired country hooks with 
thoughtful, often philosophical lyrical themes that explore our human 
connection to the natural world.
Due to her dark sensibility and 
slightly melancholic sense of humor, Kelley’s particular brand of 
Americana is multidimensional, with one foot firmly rooted in her 
Nashville Country/Americana background, another foot exploring the 
realms of alternative rock, rock and roll, and pop. Her voice is at 
times husky, dark and sultry, at times floating light-as-a-feather.

Cruz Contreras
Sunday March 8, 2020
Doors open 6:30pm, music starts at 7:00pm
Multi-instrumentalist, vocalist, songwriter, and producer Cruz Contreras has roots in Tennessee and Michigan, but has impacted Americana music worldwide.
The frontman, founder, and driving force behind The Black Lillies’ raw and rootsy sound and has collaborated with musicians ranging from John Oates and Jim Lauderdale to Langhorne Slim and The Turnpike Troubadours. Cruz’s projects have topped both the Billboard and Americana radio charts, won Independent Music Awards, and been nominated for the Americana Music Association’s Emerging Artist of the Year award.
We've been fans of Cruz's music since he put the "CC" in Robinella and the CCstringband and The Black Lillies are one of our favorite bands. Cruz is a great performer and an incredible songwriter, covering amazing range and depth. His debut solo album is coming out this spring - come get a sneak peek at this concert!
