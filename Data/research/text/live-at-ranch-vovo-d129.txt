Ranch Vovo is
 films, music, a podcast, and a small room for live shows.
about us
Ranch Vovo started in 2022 as a production company built for exactly one project: Morse Code, a scripted TV pilot and the first thing Randa Newman and Korby Lenker made together. The pilot picked up multiple awards and more than a dozen festival screenings, and the company grew like kudzu. Today it’s a working studio in East Nashville for music, film, and podcasting. As of Fall 2026, it’s also a salon: a handful of seats, a short list of artists we love, no ticket fees. Lera Lynn played the first one.
We make the kind of things we’d want to stumble onto.
