Grand Annex Music Hall Box Office & Ticketing
Location: 434 W. 6th Street, San Pedro, CA 90731
Box Office Hours: Monday-Friday, 9:30 AM-6 PM, except holidays
Box Office Phone: (310) 833-4813
Ticketing Policy:
Grand vision uses variable pricing. All prices are subject to change without notice.
Tickets are required for all attendees, of all ages.
Tables are sold as a package of four seats. There are no discounts or refunds for parties of fewer than four people.
Have your tickets ready to scan at the door at the event. All advance ticket sales are issued by email. Your digital receipt will be emailed to you with your tickets attached.
Fees: All tickets are subject to processing and credit card fees. We aim to keep ticket fees as low as possible.
Refund Policy: All Grand Annex ticket sales are final and non-refundable unless the date of the performance changes or is canceled. While we encourage guests to dance on the sides of the stage, away from other guests, there is no refund if a guest dances in front of you.
Exchange Policy:
You may exchange your tickets from one show to another show in the same season, pending availability. The exchange fee is $10.
If you make an exchange for tickets that are valued higher than the original purchase, you are required to pay the difference in addition to the exchange fee.
Exchanges must be made at least 48 hours in advance.
Reselling Policy:
Please be aware that tickets sold by resellers may cost more and may not be authentic. Grand Vision can not honor duplicate, counterfeit, or altered tickets.
If you give your tickets to another party, you must notify the box office at least one day in advance of the show so the name on the ticket order can be updated.
Pre-sale: Allows members of Grand Vision Foundation early access to ticket sales for select concerts. Members can access pre-sale tickets by logging in to the Member Portal, or by calling the Box Office at 310-833-4813.
Advanced Sales: Tickets can be purchased online, by phone or in person. Ticket prices may change due to demand as the event date approaches. Advanced sales close five hours before the event.
Door Sales: Limited tickets are available for purchase at the door unless the event is sold out. Doors open one hour before Annex events unless otherwise noted. Tickets sold at the door will be more expensive than tickets bought during advanced sales.
Waitlist: When an event is sold out, there will be a waitlist. Call (310) 833-4813 to join the waitlist. If a ticket becomes available, you will be called. Placement on the waitlist does not guarantee a ticket.
Independent Organizers: While most events are produced by Grand Vision, some events are produced by independent organizations. Grand Vision Foundation does not sell tickets for events produced by independent organizers. You can find the ticket link, as well as the name and contact info for the event organizer on the event page.
Grand Annex Music Hall Seating & Accessibility
Grand Vision Foundation does not discriminate against any person on the basis of race, color, national origin, disability, age or other characteristic in employment, or participation in its programs, services and activities.
The Grand Annex is committed to making its programs accessible to all people. If you or a member of your party need ADA accommodations, please leave a note in the “comment” section at check out when purchasing tickets or call (310) 833-4813, M-F, 10AM-6PM with your request at least 1 week in advance of the event you wish to attend.
Venue: The Grand Annex entrance is located on street level with a wide front door. All reserved and general admission seating sections are ADA accessible except the VIP Deck area, which requires the use of steps to access. The restrooms are also accessible.
Reserving General Admission ADA Seating: General admission seating will only be reserved for those who need ADA accommodations. To reserve an aisle seat, wheelchair space or companion seat: leave a note in the “comment” section at check out with your request when purchasing tickets and/or call Grand Vision at least one week in advance of the event you will be attending.
ASL Interpretation: For those who need an ASL interpreter, we ask that you please notify us at least two full weeks in advance of the event you will be attending, so we can schedule an interpreter for your event.
Seating Notes: General admission may be seated or standing. Check the seating chart of the show you wish to see or call our box office before purchasing. Front row cabaret and cabaret tables are standard folding-chair height. VIP Deck and Side Cabaret Tables are high tops with barstools.

Love the Lobby Restoration Campaign
PLEASE NOTE: The Warner Grand Theatre is currently closed for renovation. We will not be able to place plaques on any seats at the theatre until renovations are complete. We appreciate any donations toward this campaign in the meantime. Please reach us at (310) 833-4813 with any questions.
Help Grand Vision restore the Art Deco ceiling and wall paintings in the main lobby of the Warner Grand Theatre and throughout the theater. When you adopt a seat, you’ll receive a brass plaque inscribed with the name of your choice. It’s a great way to be a part of the Theatre or remember a loved one.

Adopt a Seat, Restore the Lobby!
WGT-prof-SPF-2021-lobbyceilingrendering
Restored Lobby (SPF:a rendering)
The Warner Grand Theatre is an Art Deco masterpiece. The main auditorium and mezzanine lobby endure as stunning examples of early 20th Century movie palace design. The main lobby, on the other hand, needs love.
In the 1970s, the main lobby’s painted ceiling and wall designs were covered in off-white paint. Grand Vision hired LeGrande Studios, a paint restoration company, to advise on the project, and in 2019, LeGrand revealed several sections of original paint on the lobby’s ceiling.
We examined the ceiling’s condition and sought bids for its restoration, estimated at $350,000. With the help of our community, we are halfway to this goal. You can join the Love the Lobby campaign by adopting a seat in the Theatre!
The goal is to restore the Warner Grand Theatre’s main lobby and recapture its original grandeur. Visitors will once again experience the theater’s magic the minute they walk in the front doors!

Friends of the Warner Grand
Friends of the Warner Grand
Grand Vision Foundation was formed by a group of concerned citizens in the mid-nineties to save and reactivate the Warner Grand Theatre, an art deco movie palace in downtown San Pedro. In 1996, Grand Vision successfully advocated for the City of Los Angeles to purchase the Theatre. The City’s Department of Cultural Affairs operates the Theatre, and Grand Vision has a Memorandum of Understanding to act as its official Friends Group. As the Friends, we assist the theater with advocacy, a corps of trained volunteers, restoration projects and the marketing of its events.
Adopt a Seat!
Love the Lobby Restoration Campaign
Grand Vision launched a campaign to restore the original painted art deco designs on the main lobby ceiling and make improvements to the upper, main, and lower lobbies, including restoring historic light fixtures.
You can support by adopting a seat in the Theatre – which will be dedicated with a commemorative arm rest plaque.
 ADOPT A SEAT
Friends Group Activities
Promotion
The Warner Grand Theatre is run by the City of Los Angeles Department of Cultural Affairs. Events are produced by individual renters. As the Friends Group, Grand Vision supports renters by marketing all public events put on in the theatre.
 EVENTS AT THE THEATRE
Restoration
Grand Vision’s restoration work preserves the theatre while outfitting it for 21st Century productions. From restoring frescos to upgrading lighting and projection, Grand Vision is ensuring the theatre can be enjoyed by generations to come.
 RECENT PROJECTS
Advocacy
We love sharing the Warner Grand and its history through our docent program, our historical restoration blog, and advocating for the theatre in our local community and beyond. Volunteer and get to know other community members that care.
