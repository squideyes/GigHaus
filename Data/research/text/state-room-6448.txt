 The State Room
You know that The State Room brings great live music... but did you know that it is also one of the most unique and versatile private event spaces in Salt Lake City? The State Room is perfect for any private function—day or night. Our atmosphere is intimate, comfortable and stylish, and our live music capabilities can add a special dimension. Whether you are planning an anniversary party, a surprise birthday party, wedding reception, business meeting, CD release party, film screening or corporate function, The State Room may be the ideal location. We can customize our floor plan to meet your event needs. 
Venue Details 
We are 21 and older venue. 
We have a capacity of up to 299 people. 
Our lobby that can comfortably handle close to 75 people in a standing, cocktail style environment. The lobby is also a fantastic spot for a silent auction or to display art. 
The theatre has fixed seating for 175 and then an open floor that can be used for tables and chairs or used as a dance floor. The State Room can be arranged to fit nearly all types of events. A total of 299 people can fit into the theatre, as well. 
The State Room's first-class sound and lighting systems are included. Tell us what you want and we'll set it up. 
While we can offer your some preferred catering options.. You are welcome to choose a caterer yourself. We have ample space for prep rooms and ease of load in.. 
We have two full service bars, as well as, a portable bar available as needed. 
We can offer group parking at a nominal rate. 
We offer backstage dressing rooms and plenty of space to prepare for performances or catered events. 
We can use our resources as a renowned concert venue to help you find entertainment that meets your needs. 
Pricing? 
We like to be flexible since every event is different. 
Contact us via this form and we will get right back to you to set up a tour of The State Room. 
 The Commonwealth Room
Our sister venue The Commonwealth Room opened May 5, 2018. Its capacity of 600 makes it incredibly versatile. The lobby area is absolutely huge and comfortable holds 200 plus 21 and over patron. A box office/coat check, large bar, and bathrooms are all conveniently located in the lobby. There is an overhead door opening to a patio area in the parking lot that can be designed to your imagination! Step through the two doors of the lobby into the theater area which has a raised platform that holds up to 100 people either fully seated or high boy tables. The dance floor comfortable holds 400 revelers to mingle, dance or network. 
Whether you are planning an anniversary party, a surprise birthday party, wedding reception, business meeting, CD release party, film screening or corporate function, The Commonwealth Room may be the ideal location. We can customize our floor plan to meet your event needs.

 Tickets and FAQs 
Ticket Information 
BUY TICKETS ONLINE 
AXS.com is the only official ticket source for The State Room. We are unable to offer support for orders placed with third-party outlets. All tickets purchased online will be accessed by downloading the AXS App. 
BUY TICKETS IN PERSON 
The State Room Box Office is open on show days at the posted door time. Please note that Day-of-Show ticket prices are typically more than advance purchase prices. 
Tickets are also available in person at Graywhale Entertainment. Contact your local store for ticket availability. 
We are a 21 and over venue with full bar. No Refunds/Exchanges. 
Accessibility 
Accessible seating tickets are available for all shows at The State Room. Please select these tickets if you require these accommodations and they will be set up by the time you arrive at the venue the night of the show you are attending. 
Ticketing App 
AXS Mobile ID Tickets are digital and used with the AXS app via mobile device – no paper tickets required – and they make getting in easier than ever. 
Follow these simple steps to use them: 
Get the AXS app if you haven’t already (iOS or Android). 
Open the app and sign into your accounts to access tickets. 
Show the tickets in-app at the gate to scan for entry. 
If you’re going with a group, make sure everyone enters together, as only ONE barcode appears. This barcode will be valid for ALL seats. 
Or transfer tickets to friends before the event: view this video for an overview of how to transfer tickets. 
For any additional assistance with your digital tickets, please visit the box office upon arrival.

Frequently Asked Questions 
 Where is The State Room?
Location 
The State Room is conveniently located at 638 South State Street in downtown Salt Lake City, Utah, right across the street from The Bayou. From 1-15 South, take the 600 South Exit and make a right on State Street and we are about mid-block between 600 South and 700 South. From I-15 North take the 400 South Exit and take a right on State Street. The TRAX station at the Courthouse is also within walking distance. 
 The State Room & Ride Share
Ride Share 
We're teaming up with Uber so you can drink responsibly, and in ride in style, to your next show at The Commonwealth Room! To get $20 off your first Uber ride, sign up at uber.com/GO/RIDETSR, or download the app, and enter the promo code RideTSR. Uber is an app that allows you to request a ride using your smartphone. Not sure how to Uber? It's easy! Find out more here. Questions? Contact support@uber.com. 
 What seating is available at the venue?
Seating 
The State Room features a mix of fixed seats, tables and chairs, and open floor space. For some shows, floor seating or cabaret seating is available on a first-come first-serve basis. All tickets are general admission. 
The State Room is handicap accessible. 
 Is there a coat check?
Yes! Our coat check can be found at the box office. It's $2 an item (priced per item, not per hanger). Both cash and card are accepted. 
 What is the bag policy?
Bags are allowed! We are not nearly as harsh with our bag policies as *ahem* some other venues... 
Backpacks and large bags must be checked at the box office. All bags are subject to search. 
 Where do I park?
Parking 
Diamond Parking controls the lot immediately south and west of our building, their pricing is posted. There is street parking available, but please observe all parking regulations and meters. We always recommend ride share. 
 Is there food or beverage permitted at The State Room?
Food & Beverage 
No outside food or drink is permitted inside The State Room. We have a full bar and some snacks. 
 Is The State Room an all-ages venue?
Over 21 Venue 
No. The State Room is a 21 and older venue. Proper photo identification will be required for entry. 
 What is the photo policy?
Photo Policy 
The State Room has a no-flash photography and video recording policy. In our venue it is very distracting to the performance. We also do not allow cameras with a removable lens unless there is prior approval. It is nearly impossible to set an exact policy relating to photos since there are occasions where bands encourage it. Please check with the Box Office.

 Daniel Donato's Cosmic Country Night 1
There are a lot of musical influences and sources that Daniel Donato has drawn on during his career and that inform his latest album, Horizon (Retrace Music), his second all-original album. But within those, Donato has carved out a unique and individualized spot for himself, one that speaks to the deep American music heritage that inspires him — and that he’s pushing towards the future with inspired, intentional vigor. 
He calls it Cosmic Country, a moniker that’s both self-descriptive and a statement of purpose. It’s an organic rock band aesthetic with plenty of roadhouse twang; a showcase for Donato’s instrumental virtuosity and facility for melodically infectious songcraft. Bridging Nashville and the Great West, Kentucky and mid-60s northern California, tie-dye and plaid, it’s a world of his own, and a wide world of musical adventure at that. 
While growing up in Nashville his his father, who turned his young son on to music of all genres, suggested the fledgling and industriously minded (even at just 14) artist start busking in Nashville’s lower Broadway area and outside concerts, for eight hours at a time on the weekends. One night, the two happened by Robert’s Western World where legendary honky-tonk local mainstay the Don Kelly Band were performing. Donato eventually became a member of the band, playing four hours a night at Robert’s (464 shows in total). 
Horizons is the follow up to 2023’s Reflector, which received critical acclaim from a wide range of press, including No Depression, Glide Magazine, Magnet Magazine, and more. Donato the subject of features in Rolling Stone, UPROXX, Garden & Gun and Relix. Daniel Donato’s Cosmic Country made their network television debut on CBS Saturday Morning and appeared on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert’s #LateShowMeMusic online series. 
The whole package of player, singer, writer and band leader was in place. Three albums in, Daniel Donato’s Cosmic Country, featuring Nathan “Sugarlegg” Aronowitz (keyboards/guitars/vocals), Will “Mustang” McGee (bass/vocals) and Will “Bronco” Clark (drums, percussion/vocals) are selling out venues throughout the country and bringing their brand of Cosmic Country to an ever growing, passionate fanbase
