5 Reasons to Host Your Event at Bright Box
1. Great Value
When you meet your reasonable food & beverage minimum, your space at Bright Box is free. On a budget? As the host, you can assume all costs or share the costs with guests by having a cash bar.
2. Entertainment
Built with great sound, great staging, and great lighting, Bright Box is the perfect place for entertainment — from music and comedy to a movie or professional speaker. Bright Box can even book entertainment on your behalf to make your planning easier.
3. Creative, Flexible Space
We are in a renovated downtown Winchester historic building, blending the best of the old with creative colors, stylish lighting, and retro touches. You can rent the entire three-story facility or just one of our rooms. We regularly accommodate parties between 30 – 450 guests!
4. Attentive Staff
Our wonderful staff is competent and eager to make your special day memorable and fun! With friendly and knowledgeable bartenders, servers, and event staff, we’ll ensure your special day goes flawlessly.
5. Delicious Food
Resident Chef Patrick (of Dine One-One) offers delicious menus for all taste buds and price ranges. We also serve excellent local craft beers and liquors.
WEDDINGS
If you’re looking for a nearby “destination wedding” consider the town of Winchester. With it’s vibrant downtown historic district, fine dining options and hotels, Winchester offers a wonderful backdrop for your wedding weekend. Located on the beautiful pedestrian mall, Bright Box, known for our edgy music and comedy shows, is perfect for a dynamic wedding reception, funky rehearsal dinner, or imaginative after party. We believe that love is love and we want to make your wedding day one that you’ll never forget.
Some of the our best features include:
A gorgeous, flexible black box theater with state-of-the-art sound and lighting, perfect for dancing, dinner, live music, or ceremonies
Sophisticated bar area where we will treat your guests to craft liquors, champagne, local wines, and premium beverages
Lower level dressing rooms and private bathrooms for your bridal party or performers
Out of the Box Suite on the upper level with a magnificent view of the Walking Mall, Old Courthouse, original tin ceilings now a vibrant gold, a dance floor, and copper-topped bar
A grand staircase and a retro escalator, both perfect for photographs
CORPORATE EVENTS
Company Parties * Conferences * Meet & Greets * Holiday Parties * VIP Receptions * Strategy Sessions * Team Building Activities * Retreats * Seminars * Employee Appreciation (tickets to shows)
Instead of holding a meeting or party in a traditional (boring) meeting space, you can invigorate your employees and clients at Bright Box. Get those creative juices flowing!
With our state-of-the-art sound, lighting, & projection you can stream or project multimedia or video. Need access to WIFI? We have you covered. And we can make your events dramatic and professional with our high-end mics, lights, and HD projection.

SPECIAL EVENT FAQs
What measures are being taken at Bright Box due to Covid-19?
We are taking this situation very seriously. The safety and comfort of our guests, performers, and staff are of utmost importance. Please visit our Covid-19 Updates page to learn of the precautions we’re taking to host Covid-responsible performances and private events.
What is the maximum occupancy?
Bright Box can accommodate up to 450 guests. And we have several rooms that are perfect for smaller parties!
Theater: Our state-of-the-art performance room and bar can accommodate 50-270 guests, depending on the desired setup of the room.
Out of the Box Room: Overlooking the Down Town Winchester Walking Mall with its own full service bar, the Out of the Box Room can accommodate 30-140 guests.
Bistro Room: Perfect for small birthday parties and cocktail hours, the Bistro Room can accommodate 10-50 guests.
Is there a curfew for the facility?
Many of our events go into the early hours of the morning.
Are we allowed to decorate Bright Box?
Certainly, although many guests find the facility decorated beautifully already. You are more than welcome to set up props and use non-permanent, non-wall damaging materials. Our lighting system in the theater can provide the full color spectrum and can be tailored to your color scheme.
What does it cost to rent a room at Bright Box?
Rental fees are free as long as you meet a food/beverage minimum, which ranges from $350 – $3,000 depending on the room, date, and time. Dark days (days where we do not already have pre-scheduled shows) are the most cost effective. A full venue buy-out, which many of our clients enjoy, begins at $2,700 for 8-hours.
How do I reserve a date for my event?
To block off a date and time for your event, we require a signed contract along with a 50% deposit of your total contractual amount. This allows us to hold your event date/time and order materials in preparation for your event. The remaining balance will be due prior to your event.
Am I allowed to bring in my own food and beverage?
You are welcome to bring in your own celebratory cake if desired, but all other food/beverage must be provided by Bright Box.
Do you have a food and beverage minimum?
Bright Box offers numerous food and beverage options which are based on the date, time, and room chosen for your event. Food and beverage minimum range from $350 – 3000. If your minimum is reached, your room rental is free.
Can we have a cash bar for your event?
Yes. We are happy to provide your guests beverages at our standard venue price throughout your event as long as you meet a minimum in food sales.
Can I pre-pay for beverages or set it as an open bar?
Absolutely. Bright Box offers numerous open-bar or pre-paid options for 1-3 hours. Additional hours can be added as well.
Can we hold a food and beverage tasting?
We are happy to host a food and beverage tasting for you and four guests to help you decide menu items. Tastings last one hour and are $150 – $250.
Does Bright Box provide entertainment?
Our state-of-the-art theater has premiere lighting, sound, and projection equipment, which is perfect for all types of live entertainment. Bright Box is happy to book bands, comedians, DJ’s or other exciting acts for your special event. Clients are also able to book their own performances and coordinate with Bright Box staff.
Does Bright Box have a House DJ?
Bright Box offers House DJ services at the rate of $950 for 4-hours. Our DJ’s will work with you in the planning process and put together the perfect soundtrack for your event. Or if your budget is tight, you can enjoy a mix of your choice on Spotify for free.
How is the staffing fee determined?
We take 20% of your food and beverage totals to determine how much staff is needed for your event. If you are interested in a seated meal or passed options, we can bring in additional staff at a customized rate.
Can Bright Box provide tables, linens, dinnerware for my event?
Bright Box can provide select options for tables, chairs and linens for your event. We can also order any event materials such as larger tables, colored linens, dinnerware, etc. that we do not have in the venue for an additional charge.

Bright Box is the ideal venue for Music
Our Story
Since opening in May 2013, Bright Box has hosted more than 2,000 shows of all kinds — bluegrass, jam, punk, reggae, jazz, rock, Conservatory performances, comedy, drag shows, burlesque performances, plus elaborately themed private parties and fundraisers.
We have brought in tens of thousands of locals and visitors and served as the central beating heart of downtown Winchester, Virginia. In 2019 alone we hosted more than 220 events, and over the years, have featured regionally and nationally famous musical acts and comedians such as Keller Williams, Nappy Roots, Tim Reynolds, Moon Hooch, Gallagher, New Riders of the Purple Sage, Dustin Diamond, Seldom Scene, Carl Palmer, Hackensaw Boys, Leyla McCalla, Gaelic Storm, Street Corner Symphony, Jimmie “JJ” Walker, Amish Outlaws, Bubba Sparxxx, Abby The Spoon Lady, and Reckless Kelly, to name just a few.
In addition to Grammy-winning artists and comedians that have been seen on Comedy Central and The Tonight Show, we’ve also been a platform for local talent to develop a homegrown fanbase, with several of these acts going on to become nationally touring performers. A partnership with Shenandoah University has allowed us to present performances featuring students, faculty, and world-renowned composers and musicians. We’ve also collaborated with countless members of the community to host benefit events, fundraisers, film screenings, and city-organized celebrations.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Bright Box?
Bright Box is a dynamic performance space located at the very heart of Old Town Winchester on the Pedestrian Mall. Our beautiful, flexible space and superb acoustics and lighting makes us suitable for live music, comedy, receptions/parties, and many other kinds of creative events. Please enter from the Pedestrian Mall, at 15 N. Loudoun Street, right across from the Old Town Courthouse. Note that we are general admission, first come first served. For most performances, doors open an hour before the show.
What measures are being taken at Bright Box due to Covid?
We are taking this situation very seriously. The safety and comfort of our guests, performers, and staff are of utmost importance. Please visit our Covid-19 Updates page to learn of the precautions we’re taking to host Covid-responsible performances and private events.
How do I get there?
Bright Box is located at 15 N. Loudoun Street in Old Town Winchester directly across from the old courthouse on the gorgeous pedestrian-only Walking Mall. Click this link for a map and directions.
What are your hours of operation?
The Box Office is open one hour before doors open on nights that we have a show. For a tour of the venue please schedule an appointment with Matt Martz at matt@brightboxtheater.com or take a virtual tour HERE.
Where Do I Buy Tickets?
This website lists all upcoming events and provides links to purchase advance tickets on line. Take a look at what we have coming up here. Typically online sales end two hours before the event begins. Additionally, you can purchase tickets – by credit card or cash – at the door one hour before show time (provided the show is not yet sold out). For most shows tickets are cheaper when you purchase in advance. Please note that our ticket office remains open throughout most shows and you can purchase tickets for any upcoming show at advance ticket prices during that time. We accept cash and most credit cards.
We offer 10% ticket discounts for groups of 10+ and 15% ticket discounts for groups of 20+. Please contact nick@brightboxtheater.com to reserve your group tickets and pay via credit card. This offer is only available in advance, not on the day of show. Group tickets must be purchased together, under one credit card, and are non-refundable. We do not offer military or senior citizen discounts.
How Old Do You Have To Be To Attend?
Unless otherwise listed on the event page on our website, Bright Box welcomes all ages as long as they are well behaved. Discounts are not available. We encourage that an adult accompany children under the age of eighteen. We STRONGLY suggest children under 5 to wear ear protection.
Can I Reserve a Table?
Due to the intimate size of the venue we DO NOT take table reservations. Bright Box is all general admission. Seating is on a first come, first served basis. We encourage you to arrive early to choose your favorite seats. We have a full bistro menu, so join us for dinner and drinks before the show!
On select shows we offer VIP seating that allows you admittance half an hour earlier than the general public. This will be noted on the event page.
What if I Have Tickets But Can Not Attend?
All sales are final; we do not give refunds or exchanges. Refunds are ONLY available for cancelled or rescheduled shows.
Does Bright Box Offer Discount Tickets?
For most shows tickets are cheaper when you purchase in advance. We offer 10% ticket discounts for groups of 10+ and 15% ticket discounts for groups of 20+. Please contact nick@brightboxtheater.com to reserve your group tickets and pay via credit card. This offer is only available in advance, not on the day of show. We do not offer military or senior citizen discounts.
Is Food and Alcohol Available?
We offer a delicious array of snacks and desserts. We also offer over 20 craft and domestic beers on tap and in bottles, a selection of fine wines, and local craft spirits. Arrive one hour before show time to grab a bite to eat and snag a good seat!
Where Do I Park?
There’s a big parking garage directly behind Bright Box on Braddock Street (Braddock Auto Park). There’s another on Cameron Street (Court Street Auto Park) — both are within one block from Bright Box. You can often park on the streets as well. See also Directions & Parking.
Is Bright Box Accessible?
Absolutely. The infrastructure in Old Town Winchester is friendly toward those with disabilities. Park in one of the close-by parking garages and come to the main door at 15 N. Loudoun. We will be happy to escort you to a place best suited to your needs in the theater or to take you to the elevator to access the upstairs Out of the Box room.
Is Bright Box Smoke Free?
Absolutely. This includes illegal substances and electronic cigarettes.
Do You Allow Photography?
Photography is allowed (share it with us on our Facebook page or Instagram account!) unless the artist specifically requests otherwise, but please be mindful of the artists and fellow fans around you. Flash photography is NOT permitted.
What Happens if There is Inclement Weather?
If the entertainment is able to arrive at Bright Box the show will go on. If the show is rescheduled, all tickets purchased will be honored on the rescheduled date.
Is There Lodging Nearby?
There are lots of great and affordable lodging options — from hotels and motels to camp grounds and bed and breakfast spots — just minutes away from Bright Box. Right around the block is the elegant George Washington Hotel, and Courtyard Marriott is quick drive and within a stones throw to the beautiful (and recommended) Shenandoah Valley Museum. Check out these websites to explore your options and discover all the wonderful opportunities in Winchester:
http://www.visitwinchesterva.com/lodging/hotels-and-motels
http://oldtownwinchesterva.com
How Do I Keep Up With Upcoming Events?
You can sign up for our email updates on the homepage of this website or follow us on Facebook. And you can always come to this website (www.brightboxwinchester.com) to get the latest show information.
How Do I Book a Performance?
We are always on the look out for quality music that works well in our intimate, upscale performance space. Keep in mind that Bright Box is an entertainment venue, NOT a bar. You must already have a fanbase in the area that is willing to pay a ticket price to see you perform. Please send your information to Nick Helmut at nick@brightboxtheater.com. Include any recent Winchester-area market history, who you might pair well with, links, etc. Please be aware that we receive tons of inquiries. If we feel you will work well at Bright Box we will get back to you.
Can I Book a Private Party or Reception at Bright Box?
YES! We can help you plan the perfect event – from a wedding reception to a business meeting, and everything in between. Check out all that we have to offer here.
What is the Capacity of the Venue?
The main theater can hold up to 120 guest for all-seated events or 280 guests for all-standing events. The upstairs, Out of the Box room can seat 90, or fit approximately 150 with a mix of seating & standing. The entire venue can accommodate over 400 people at once.
What Genres of Entertainment Do You Host?
While we are open to hosting nearly any genre as long as there’s a fanbase/demand, we really shine with the following styles of music: bluegrass, rock, blues, jam, and folk. We also feature drag performances, murder mysteries, and comedy shows.
