Our Story 
Winnsboro Center for the Arts is a vibrant cultural center which anchors the Cultural Arts District and hosts a variety of activities from concerts, theater productions, and art exhibits to classes, workshops, and festivals. Activities geared especially for children include music, art lessons, and summer camps.
We invite you to view our video, Winnsboro Center for the Arts – Twenty Years in the Making, to gain a better understanding of our history and our vision for the future.

Local World Class Performances
Right Here in Winnsboro, Texas 
The Bowery Stage is WCA’s unique theater and concert venue. It supports an intimate 88 seat listening room with bistro tables that can expand to a larger 120 seat theater performance hall. Musicians and actors tell us that the acoustics provide some of the best sounds they’ve experienced. 
Concerts usually have two 45 minute sets with an intermission which provides an opportunity to meet the performers. 
Theatre performances have an intermission about half-way through and a performer reception line after the performance.
For all performances, wine is available for a small donation and concession refreshments are available for purchase. Also, for our concert performances, you are welcome to bring a bottle of wine. We’ll supply the glasses and uncorking for a small donation. 
Join us for any of our performances and discover why both performers and guests love the Bowery Stage!

Eve’s Road blends alt-rock, shoegaze & groovy beats with Charla Murray and Jason Solley, Nov. 21 at 7:30 PM on The Bowery Stage
Eve’s Road is an evocative blend of alternative rock, shoegaze effects and groovy beats hailing from the Tyler, Texas area. Eve’s Road consists of harpist/vocalist Charla Murray and drummer Jason Solley.
Charla and Jason began making music together in the late 1990s. After a couple of decades outside of Texas, Charla moved back “home” to Texas, and the duo transitioned their online music work to live performances and have been in regular rotation all around East Texas and the Dallas/Fort Worth area ever since. Utilizing looper pedals and incorporating bass and keys alongside the harp, the band has rapidly established a reputation for their rich soundscapes and electrifying live performances, whether that be their original music or their carefully selected and curated cover setlist spanning the 1960s to current.
Eve’s Road is definitely poised to make a lasting impact on the music scene.
