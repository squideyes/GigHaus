Tulsa native John Calvin Abney returns to the city that helped shape his musical roots for an intimate evening at Tulsa Time House Concerts. A gifted multi-instrumentalist, songwriter and producer, Abney blends thoughtful storytelling, alternative folk and melodic Americana into songs that are both deeply personal and quietly captivating. Join us for a special night with an artist whose music deserves to be heard up close, where every song feels like a story shared among friends.

We are so excited about Ian's annual acoustic tour stop at Tulsa Time House Concerts! Tulsa is one of Ian's favorite cities because of our strong music community and the feeling is mutual.
As always, this is a BYOB event with potluck.
Ian Moore, the Austin-based blues-rock guitarist and songwriter, draws inspiration from a diverse range of artists, including The Beatles, Curtis Mayfield, Stevie Wonder, and Sly & the Family Stone. He honed his skills on the road as a guitarist with Joe Ely, an experience that eventually led to solo tours opening for the Rolling Stones and ZZ Top. Moore released his debut album in 1993, followed by his final Capricorn release, Modernday Folklore, in 1995. The album marked a creative shift, expanding beyond the blues-rock traditions of his Texas roots and incorporating elements of Southern soul, gospel, and Beatles-influenced pop alongside the powerful blues riffs that had defined his early sound.
While Moore continues to explore the blues in his recordings, it's his live performances that have earned him a devoted following. From blistering electric blues to deeply personal songs that showcase his more soulful side, Moore brings an unmistakable energy and emotion to the stage. Still touring extensively, he continues to captivate audiences—and his loyal fans eagerly anticipate every performance.

Tulsa Time House Concerts 
My house concerts started about 7 years ago with Dustin Pittsley and Jesse Aycock in my backyard. Then Don White packed the house 2 nights in a row! Hmm, we might have something here...I had managed the Dustin Pittsley Band for several years prior and it seemed like a natural progression.
Each show has a suggested minimum donation per person which I request be paid in advance online with a credit card or with cash/check. All proceeds (minus 10% for expenses) goes to the musicians. Your seat is not reserved prior to receipt of payment. This is to ensure we have enough people to make it worthwhile for the artists and for me to know how to set up the house depending on the number of guests. THE PRICE GOES UP THE DAY OF THE SHOW.
The shows are always BYOB with a voluntary potluck. I encourage you to come early to mingle, grab some food and select your seats.
The musicians present outstanding shows and deserve our respect so I ask guests to remain seated while a song is being played. Also kindly keep voices low during the performance so everyone who has traveled and paid to enjoy them may do so.
If weather permits, please bring a lawn chair for outdoors. I will notify you if it is inside or out before the day of the show.
We have plenty of street parking.

FAQ's
WHAT IS A HOUSE CONCERT
As the name implies, it is a concert.... in a house as opposed to a commercial venue where the audience and musicians are almost certainly more separated by distance and formality. Guests are there for their favorite artists among other like minded music lovers in a cozy setting!
REGISTRATION
For information/registration, please resister with a link on the event above. Once registered, you will be sent a confirmation email with the location – so yes, I will need your email address. You must prepay to guarantee admittance.
COST
The price of admittance goes directly to the musicians minus 10% to the host to help cover costs. Tips are always welcomed.
WHERE IS THE VENUE/HOUSE?
Due to its being in a private residence, you will receive the address upon ticket purchase. It is important for your host to know the number of people attending prior to the show so preparations can be made and seating is appropriate. You really don’t want someone sitting in your lap, do you?
TIME
Generally, *unless specifically noted*, the optional potluck social hour begins 30 minutes before show time. There is an intermission where you may purchase the artists’ CDs, grab more food/drink, and mingle.
OPTIONAL POTLUCK
You may attend the concert without coming to the potluck social beforehand. Eating, drinking and mingling with cool people is optional but recommended. Potluck social hour begins about 60 minutes before the show starts. There is usually an intermission where you may purchase the artists’ CDs, posters, grab more food/drink, and mingle.
I’m coming from out of town.
I don’t cook. What to do about the potluck?
No worries. The main point is to be here. Snack items and items purchased from the deli are totally fine. We also have some excellent cooks who bring amazing dishes. This is not a cooking contest.
WHAT DO I BRING FOR THE POTLUCK?
We are definitely putting the “luck” in potluck and not assigning specific dishes. Bring a DRINK and a DISH to share. Some food/drink, and all plates, utensils etc will be provided.
CHANGE IN REGISTRATION
You are under no obligation to attend and sometimes things come up in life. Please contact us ASAP so we can free up your seat(s) for other attendees.
LAST MINUTE REGISTRATION
Go for it. If we have the space, meaning tickets are still available, we’d love to have you here.
HOW MANY PEOPLE DOES THE VENUE SEAT/IS THERE PARKING?
Indoors – Up to 50. Outside, 50 to ??. We have some outdoor seating but suggest you bring your own lawn chairs or blankets. Plenty of neighborhood parking but please be mindful of neighbors’ driveways etc.
