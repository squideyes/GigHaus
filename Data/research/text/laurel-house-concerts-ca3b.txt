Welcome to Laurel House Concerts, Oceanside CA.
Supporting Local Music
 Hello! Joann and Brian Bourguignon here and we are part of a growing group of music lovers who open their homes to local and national artists. We do this because it’s a wonderful intimate atmosphere for us the audience, and for the artists. We look forward to seeing you at our next show. This is truly a community event- we enjoy bringing our neighbors together and share an evening of music, food, and good company. Tell your friends and neighbors to stop by our website and check us out. We always welcome new friends and new artists. (if you know someone who is talented pass our info along too).
Mission Statement:
“Laurel House Concerts is a nonprofit dedicated to enriching our community by providing an intimate, welcoming venue for musicians to share their art. We bring live music into the heart of the neighborhood, fostering connection, creativity, and cultural vibrancy for all.”
We are located in Oceanside CA. You will receive a ticket confirmation with the address after tickets are purchased.
To keep in the know, join our mailing list, follow us on Facebook and Instagram (laurel house concert series).

Happy Baby Band
(Steph Johnson and friends)
August 29th, 2026 @7:00pm
Steph Johnson is an award-winning recording artist, activist, and the co-founder and CEO of Voices of Our City Choir, an internationally recognized nonprofit that amplifies the voices of individuals experiencing homelessness through music. With a career spanning over two decades and five studio albums, Steph has been honored as a San Diego Music Award winner, a California Arts Champion, and a 2020 Woman of the Year. Her work has been featured on PBS, NPR, and America’s Got Talent, where the choir earned the Golden Buzzer and advanced to the semifinals. Through her leadership, Voices of Our City Choir continues to transform lives, using music as a powerful tool for connection, dignity, and change.
Steph’s new project, Happy Baby, is an electronic funk soul dream with key people from her musical journey that have inspired her evolutionary sound. What started as a 5 song demo, cowritten with Grammy Winning pianist and composer, Rachel Eckroth (St Vincent, Rufus Wainwright, Chris Botti) has expanded into a 12 song debut album from the group titled Cartwheel Galaxy, inspired from the actual Cartwheel Galaxy in our solar system. Steph hopes to travel together with you on August 29th for the debut full band concert.

If this is your first show with us here are a few FAQs
Frequently Asked Questions
Where are you located?
We are in Oceanside CA. We will provide address when tickets are paid for.
What Is a House Concert?
It’s simply a concert at someone’s house, with the emphasis on “concert”. (Not to be confused with a “house party”.) At its best, a house concert provides music lovers an intimate concert experience, and gives performers an attentive and appreciative audience. In San Diego County, we’re lucky to have quite a few ongoing house concert series.
How do I make a reservation?
We accept donations (for the artists) to make reservations and reserve seats:
Use PayPal to donate now by credit card or PayPal account: or You can pay by cash or check please contact us via email. www.laurelhouseconcerts.com.
No tickets will be printed or sent to you. When we receive notice of the PayPal payment, or when the check is received and cleared, we will send you a confirmation email and add you to the guest list for the evening. Requests for reservations without payment will be honored on a best-effort basis. Please make these requests by email, either through our website. www.laurelhouseconcerts.com
Are reservations necessary?
Yes
Is there an age limit?
There is no age requirement for our house concerts—everyone is welcome! Our concerts are family-friendly, so feel free to bring the whole crew and enjoy an evening of live music together.
What if I need to cancel?
If you need to cancel, please let us know 48 hours ahead of time. If we know you are not coming, we can still try to fill those seats. “No shows” lose money for the performers, because those seats stay empty.
I made a reservation. Will I receive paper tickets?
No, we’ll add your name to our reservations list, and check you in at the door.
Where does the money go?
All proceeds go to the artists.
Do I need to bring chairs?
No we provide seating (no need to bring your own chairs)
Will refreshments be available?
We will provide a main dish. You may bring other food or beverages of your choice, and we encourage you to bring finger foods to share (more fun for everyone!).
Guests are also welcome to bring their own adult beverages to enjoy during the concert. Please drink responsibly and help us keep the atmosphere welcoming for everyone.
Is it a potluck?
Every event at Laurel House is a potluck. But it’s an unorganized potluck; just bring something of your choice to share. (It always works out!) But don’t stress: No one’s going to ask you at the door (or anywhere else) if you’ve brought anything.
Is the concert indoors or out?
The concert may be held outdoors or indoors. (You’ll want to check the weather forecast and dress accordingly.) We provide seating (no need to bring your own chair).
 See you soon! Joann and Brian
