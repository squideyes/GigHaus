WHAT IS THE REFINERY?
The Refinery is a mixed use development located in the "Neck" region of the Charleston Peninsula. It features over 40,000sf of office space, a 2,000 person capacity outdoor amphitheater, various private event spaces available for rent, and two independent food and beverage establishments: Cleats and The Whale. 
Built for a work and play lifestyle, The Refinery is where office hours turn into happy hours!
CONTACT INFORMATION
The Refinery is located at 1640 Meeting Street Rd. Charleston, SC 29405
If you need to contact The Refinery please do so via the email below and we'll be in touch as soon as possible: 
info@therefinerychs.com 
WHERE ARE THE CONCERTS HELD?
Concerts at The Refinery are held in the outdoor amphitheater on the east side of The Refinery building. This area is partially paved and there is also a lawn area to spread out on.
The entry and exit gate is located at the southeastern end of the property. Check out our venue map for more detail!
There are no dedicated seats in either General Admission or VIP. All seating options are available on a first-come, first-served basis. 
REFUNDS, CANCELLATIONS, + INCLEMENT WEATHER
All ticket sales are final and non-refundable.
The Refinery is an outdoor venue and shows will be held in all elements of Charleston weather. All events are rain or shine. 
Please be prepared for a wide range of weather conditions. This may include full exposure to the sun, heat, and rain. We recommend that patrons bring rain gear (no umbrellas), sunscreen, and bug spray. Event staff will monitor weather conditions and prioritize guest and staff safety. 
The Refinery reserves the right to cancel any event at any time for any reason. 
AMPHITHEATER HOURS
On event days, The Refinery amphitheater will be closed to the public for sound checks and show operations. 
Occasionally, the amphitheater may be closed on a non-concert day for a private event being held on the campus.
Gates generally open one hour before the start of a concert/scheduled event. Please check your ticket or the event details on our calendar to confirm the gate times for the event you plan to attend. 
HEALTH + SAFETY
Patron, artist, and staff safety are of the utmost importance at The Refinery. We do everything we can to make events held here a safe experience for everyone involved. 
​
If medical attention is needed during an event at The Refinery, please contact uniformed event staff. We always have police and EMS on-site during concerts. 
LOST & FOUND 
Lost and found items can be retrieved at the event entrance during event hours. After event hours, please email info@therefinerychs.com.

Plan Your Next Charleston, SC Event at The Refinery
What is The Refinery?
Originally the site of Standard Oil’s southeastern headquarters, The Refinery is now Charleston’s most unique concert and event venue, containing a mix of indoor and outdoor spaces that can accommodate nearly any event you have in mind. From intimate cocktail parties to 2000 person corporate events, we have what you need. 
Where is The Refinery Located?
1640 Meeting Street Rd. Charleston, SC 29405.
Do you have parking?
We have roughly 175 off-street parking spaces in addition to the plentiful, free street parking on Meeting Street Rd. Not bad for our location in the “Neck” of the peninsula!
Can you cater the event?
While we do not provide catering services in-house, we do have preferred vendors that are incredible to work with. At the same time, we don’t limit our clients, so if you have a caterer you love, we are open to hearing all about them to see if we can work together. 
What about alcohol and bartenders?
We have a fully licensed beverage program and bartending staff that can handle anything from a cold beer to a customized cocktail. Outside bar services/BYOB are not permitted. 
Do you have a bar minimum?
Nope. We don’t think it's fair to try to fit every event into the same box. Always happy to work with you and your budget to create a plan that makes sense. Our event manager is here to guide you. 
Can we tour the property?
We absolutely encourage, and almost insist, that you do. We try to provide as much detail as we can online, but pictures just don’t do it justice. You’re also welcome to swing by and walk the outdoor spaces on your own at any time. 
Can we bring decorations, balloons, florals, etc.?
We love when you can take any of our available spaces and make it your own. The only thing we ask is that you don’t stake anything into the grounds or utilize any decorations that could damage the premises or be difficult to clean up (i.e. confetti/glitter).
Do you provide furniture or linens?
We offer our spaces in as-is condition. Any furniture that is currently in any of our spaces can be utilized for your event at no additional charge. Labor fees will apply to remove any furniture and if you’d like anything additional we can recommend preferred local rental companies. 
Can we bring entertainment?
Yes! Entertainment is always encouraged. Ask about our equipment rentals if needed. We can also provide recommended vendors for lighting, sound, production management, etc.
Do we need to make a deposit to secure our date?
Yes, a 50% deposit is required along with a $500 refundable security deposit to hold your date. The remaining balance is due no later than 30 days prior to the event date.

built on a century of industry
In 1926, The Standard Oil Company established its headquarters on this property — making Charleston the oil capital of the carolinas. The Charleston Refinery supplied all of the gasoline being sold by The Standard Oil Company in the region, and its Asphalt was used to pave the streets of downtown Charleston.
This refinery played an extensive role in both the social and economic development of Charleston. By increasing the ship movements and tonnage flowing through the port, the refinery not only created jobs, it built a community. 
the next chapter
that original headquarters building still stands today, reimagined as creative office space for innovators and makers of modern charleston. in 2020 we completed the refinery as you know it today: a mixed-use complex and amphitheater designed to carry this site's legacy forward. Since 2023, over 150,000 people have gathered here for music, community, and something you can't quite put into words. And there's no end in sight!
