From dimly-lit, solo acoustic songbird confessions to rambunctious & rollicking full-band jubilees, award-winning songwriter Jessica Malone takes listeners on a musical journey with the destination landing somewhere between self discovery and reverie.

Embracing her Northern California roots, Malone blends soulful lyrics with an earnest sound, bringing to life the landscapes of her home. Her oft-mellifluous, occasionally gritty, and always captivating vocals—accompanied by rich upright bass, heavenly harmonies, and soaring violin—will guide you across a musical terrain of retro soul, indie folk, and Americana vibrations.

Watch Misner & Smith
Northern California-based unclassifiable duo Misner & Smith have been sitting with iterations of their new album for six years. With All is Song they turn in their finest work to date, an album of inspired declarations to the power of music, song, and collaboration. Technically precise songwriting mirrored with an improvisatory spirit and soaring harmonies have made the band—consisting of Sam Misner and Megan Smith—one of the most acclaimed acts in the Americana world. Blending elements of that genre with rock and roll, traditional folk ballads, and more pop-leaning ideas, Misner & Smith makes music gloriously nonconforming, and they treasure the unpredictability of their band. Teaming up with the duo on both the new album and for this special show will be two of the musicians whose contributions were foundational to the new record. About the Event
Jim Bruno is a Northern California based published songwriter. Songs he’s written or co-written have been recorded and released by Shawn Colvin, Chuck Prophet, Thad Cockrell, Mary Lou Lord, Cliff Eberhardt, Maurice Tani and Laurie MacAlister. 
As a performing songwriter he’s performed at the Greenwich Village Folk Festival, the San Francisco Folk Festival, the Udine Folk Festival in Udine, Italy and the Madame Guitar Festival in Tricesimo, Italy and has toured many times in Europe. He also played bass, guitar and sang in the Shawn Colvin Band and often performed with Shawn as a duet. 
He has signed numerous song publishing agreements. Among others these publishers include Bug Music and the Welk Music Group in Los Angeles, and Largo Music in New York.
