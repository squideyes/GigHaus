About
Humphreys Backstage LIVE is an intimate music venue and lounge located on San Diego’s Shelter Island. Over the years, Backstage LIVE has provided fans an incredible range of entertainment from nationally renowned artists to rising local talents. Our extensive selection of SoCAL microbrews and craft cocktails, handpicked by our acclaimed in-house mixologist, adds to the longstanding allure of the venue.
Amid world-class waterfront views, Backstage LIVE shares the spotlight with Humphreys Concerts by the Bay, Humphreys Restaurant and Humphreys Half Moon Inn. Together, they offer a truly fantastic SoCAL experience on the edge of the Pacific Ocean.
Check out our calendar for dates and details on upcoming acts.
Humphreys SoCAL Dining and Music & Backstage LIVE are available for exclusive and unique events. Call or email us for details.
For adults 21+
PLEASE NOTE – For your convenience and safety, we are a cashless establishment.
We accept all major credit & debit cards.

 Johnny Britt – “You Are Beautiful” Tour 
 6:00 pm Jazz/R&B/Soul
 Johnny Britt – “You Are Beautiful” Tour
 November 1 @ 6:00 pm - 9:30 pm 
Johnny Britt is an American singer, trumpeter, songwriter, arranger and producer and author in the entertainment industry. Britt received his break into the industry when he was hired as the youngest ever musical director by the legendary Otis Williams founder of “The Temptations” … Williams quotes Johnny Britt will forever be a part of our Motown Family. Britt toured the world and performed on countless TV shows with the group. This led to Britt being hired as the co music producer for the Emmy Award Winning TV Miniseries “The Temptations”. Britt was then hired by director Robert Townsend to be the co music producer of the critically acclaimed “Little Richard Story”.
Britt was signed to Motown/ MoJazz Records in 1995 with the group he founded the forward thinking hip hop/ jazz group Impromp2. Impromp2 would become one of the first groups that would combine hip hop and jazz on the west coast. Impromp2 would go on to record 4 albums along with appearances at The U.S. Open, Soul Train, The Olympics, The White House, The Lady of Soul Awards and more. Britt has written 3 number 1 Billboard hits for Boney James and has written for many other artists.
As a soloist in 2024 Britt received the Best Group or Duo of the Year Award for his duet with Will Downing on his hit “Butterflies” from Soul Café Radio Indie Soul Awards. In 2023 Britt would go on to receive his first number 1 Billboard hit single “After We Play” featuring Peter White from the same album.
SiriusXM Watercolors Radio’s rotation of the Johnny Britt song catalog include “Summer Nights”, “Hot Fun In The Summer” feat. Najee, MoJazzin feat. Norman Brown, The Way I Feel feat. Marion Meadows, After We Play feat. Peter White, Ocean Waves feat. Blair Bryant and Walk On By.
The 2025 release of “Midnight In Paris” marks Britt seventh studio album which he wrote, produced and performed.
Britt’s life show is mesmerizing and a MUST SEE!!!

 Will Donato 
 6:00 pm Smooth Jazz
 Will Donato
 November 8 @ 6:00 pm - 9:00 pm 
https://www.willdonato.com/
One of the premier saxophone players in the smooth jazz world, Will Donato puts on a show that will keep you on your feet the whole time!
Will Donato is one of music’s most dynamic performers, gifted with the ability to bond and connect with his audiences. His latest radio single “GOOD ON YOU” was a Billboard #1 hit in Winter 2022.
A previous single release “INFINITE SOUL” (January 2019) has over 3.5 MILLION streams to date on Spotify alone and reached the coveted #1 spot on the Billboard smooth jazz airplay chart in 2019.
A cancer survivor who truly celebrates each day and each person he meets, Will has headlined or shared the stage as a special guest at the Blue Note in New York City, The Seabreeze Jazz Festival, The Algarve Groove International Jazz Festival in Portugal, The Rehoboth Beach Jazz Festival, Humphrey’s By The Bay, The Capital Jazz Cruise, and Rick Braun’s New Year’s Eve.
Will has performed with some of the best known musicians of the groove / smooth jazz genre, namely Al McKay of Earth Wind and Fire, Richard Elliot, Bruce Conte of Tower of Power, Steve Reid of The Rippingtons, Brian Simpson, Steve Oliver, Gregg Karukas and Nick Colionne. He has worked in the pop music genre as well – most notably with Gerald Levert, Wynonna Judd, Toni Childs, and superstar Richard Marx. His music can be heard on the number one contemporary jazz legacy hit from Steve Oliver, “High Noon” as well as on the soundtracks from several movie scores including Thelma and Louise, The Wedding Planner, Under the Boardwalk, and No Man’s Land.

 Chris James & Patrick Rynn
 November 27 @ 5:00 pm - 7:00 pm

Chris James and Patrick Rynn are at the forefront of today’s traditional Blues movement.
 They’ve been playing together since 1990, proudly dedicating their musical collaboration
 to the high-energy sound of real, unadulterated Chicago Blues. They lead their own widely
 acclaimed band, and have toured the world playing behind some of the greatest Blues legends in the field.
36 years of playing Blues as an indivisible duo has made guitarist Chris James and bassist Patrick Rynn one of the tightest,
 most satisfying units working in the genre today. Chris and Patrick are the real deal, whether delving bone-deep into slashing
 electric Chicago blues classics, cooking up their own meaty originals, or making the occasional foray into acoustic material.
James’ vocals ring with ferocity, his fretwork never less than immaculate. Nominated for no less than 13 BMAs as bassist of the year,
 Patrick lays down grooves as deep as they are subtle; he’s the definition of rock-solid.
Chris and Patrick have honed their deeply resonant traditional sound to the proverbial razor’s edge.
 Their telepathic interplay always in evidence whether they’re onstage or in the studio.
