ATTENDING A SHOW: FAQ | Live at Andre's
top of page
LIVE AT ANDRE'S
HOUSE CONCERT VENUE
​
​
​
How do I get tickets for a show?
Simply go to the TICKETS page.
​
How much are tickets?
Ticket prices vary by show and are posted on the TICKETS page.
​
How will I receive my tickets?
There are no physical tickets! Instead, your name will be at the door after your purchase.
​
How do I transfer my tickets if I can't make the show?
To transfer your tickets, contact info@liveatandres.com with your request.
​
I have tickets but can't use them. Can I get a refund?
We do not issue refunds; however, your ticket can be used for a different or later show.​
​
​
​
​​
Where is the venue? Nine Mile Falls, just north of Spokane. See the CONTACT/DIRECTIONS page for address, map, and parking instructions.
​
When should I arrive? Doors open 2 hours before the concert begins. Show times are listed on the TICKETS page.
​
What is the venue like? Our shows are held in a hillside amphitheater with an expansive view of the Spokane River.
​
Where do I park? There's plenty of parking on site.
​
What's the venue's capacity? Around 150 people.
​
How long do the shows last? Generally, about 2 hours.
​
Do your shows sell out? Yes, so plan in advance. Don’t wait 'til the last minute!
​
How do I get on your mailing list? Email info@liveatandres.com and request to be added to our mailing list. 
​
Are children allowed? Music-loving children are welcome. Kids ages 14 and under get in free.
​
​
​
​
​How about dogs? Please keep your canine friends at home. If you have a service animal, please contact info@liveatandres.com to make arrangements.
​
Is it wheelchair accessible? Yes.
​​
Will there be food available? Yes, there will be food available for purchase. Check the Live at Andre's show emails for details.
​
Will there be alcohol? Alcohol is permitted. Our policy is BYOB.
​
Should I bring my own chair? Yes, camping/lawn chairs or blankets are suggested, as you'll be sitting on grass.
​
What if it rains? Shows will go on regardless of weather, so dress appropriately. However, in case of thunderstorms or other severe weather, we'll move the show inside.
​
Is photography allowed? How about video?
Cell phone photography and video are allowed, but please be courteous and don't block your fellow attendees' views. No flash!
​
​
