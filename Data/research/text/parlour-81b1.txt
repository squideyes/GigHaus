THE PARLOUR JAZZ SERIES PRESENTS: THE TOM WHITE TRIO CD RELEASE SHOW ALL-AGES
 Saturday Nov. 14, 2026 @ 5PM 
 $10.00
Four years after our first recording, the Trio will be releasing our second album, “How To Live In Strange Times”, on Saturday November 14th. We will be celebrating this release on the same day with a performance at The Parlour Providence from 5-7pm!
The album will feature fourteen tunes (!!) composed by Tom White and Tom Casale, brilliantly captured by master recording engineer Steve Rizzo at Stable Sound Studio. The graphic design by Amy Archambault features the visual art of my grandfather, the late Alvan White. The music depicts and documents events from our own lives in the past four years as well as the unprecedented recent happenings in the wider world.
We are elated to have the opportunity to share this album with you. Mark your calendars for 11/14, and come join us at The Parlour to celebrate!
ALL-AGES $10.00

 Thursday Nov. 19, 2026 @ 7PM

As Dead Gowns, Maine songwriter Geneviève Beaudoin navigates between folk-rock, alt-country, and indie with a certain, weather-worn intimacy. On her second album, Vegas, she turns bright lights and dark waters into an exploration of quiet ruptures, blurred memories, and grief, guided by a voice that anchors even as it unfurls.
