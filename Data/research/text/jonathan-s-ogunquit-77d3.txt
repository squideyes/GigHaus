About Us 
A cornerstone of Ogunquit’s culinary scene since 1976, Jonathan’s has been serving exceptional farm to table, Maine seafood and classic cuisine to the local and traveling public for decades. Our landmark building, once Jonathan’s family home, has been thoughtfully preserved while being transformed into an elegant dining destination with several dining room, lounge and upstairs listening room and banquet space.
From intimate dinners to memorable celebrations, we’ve maintained our commitment to culinary excellence while honoring Maine’s rich traditions. Our dining rooms blend historic charm with contemporary comfort, creating the perfect setting for both our acclaimed dining experience and world-class entertainment.

Frequently Asked Questions 
About Jonathan’s Ogunquit 
 What is Jonathan’s Ogunquit? 
Jonathan’s Ogunquit is a locally owned restaurant and intimate live music venue in Ogunquit, Maine, known for elevated dining and nationally recognized musical performances in a listening-room setting. 
 Where is Jonathan’s Ogunquit located? 
Jonathan’s Ogunquit is located in Ogunquit, Maine, just minutes from Ogunquit Beach and the village center. Jonathan’s Ogunquit is also the closest restaurant to the Ogunquit Playhouse. 
 How long has Jonathan’s Ogunquit been in business? 
Jonathan’s Ogunquit has been a part of the Ogunquit community for nearly 50 years, welcoming locals and visitors alike for memorable food and live music experiences. 
 Is Jonathan’s Ogunquit locally owned? 
Yes. Jonathan’s Ogunquit is proudly locally owned and operated, with deep roots in Ogunquit and southern Maine. 
 What makes Jonathan’s Ogunquit unique? 
Jonathan’s Ogunquit offers a rare combination of a chef-driven restaurant and an intimate live music venue, where guests can enjoy dinner and experience performances by acclaimed artists in a relaxed, listening-focused environment. We are the epitome of the perfect “dinner & a show” experience. 
Reservations, Tickets & Dining 
 Do I need a reservation to dine at Jonathan’s Ogunquit? 
Reservations are strongly recommended, especially on performance nights and during peak season. 
 Are tickets required for live music performances? 
Yes. Most live music events at Jonathan’s Ogunquit require a ticket, which is separate from dinner unless otherwise noted. 
 Is dinner included with a show ticket? 
Our premier ticket includes a dinner credit that can be redeemed in our downstairs dining rooms before a show . Guests are encouraged to arrive early and enjoy dinner before the performance. 
 What time should I arrive for a performance? 
We recommend arriving at least 90–120 minutes before showtime if you plan to dine, allowing ample time to enjoy your meal before the performance begins. 
Live Music Experience
 What type of live music does Jonathan’s Ogunquit feature? 
Jonathan’s Ogunquit features a wide range of live music, including folk, singer-songwriter, Americana, jazz, and acoustic performances by nationally touring and well-known artists. 
 Is Jonathan’s Ogunquit a listening room? 
Yes. Jonathan’s Ogunquit is designed as a listening room, meaning performances are intended to be enjoyed attentively in a respectful, intimate setting. Our listening room is located on the second floor, separate from the downstairs dining rooms. 
 How often do you host live music? 
Live music is scheduled regularly throughout the year, with an especially robust lineup during peak seasons. 
 Can I eat during the performance? 
Dinner service is served downstairs. The only food served upstairs for a show is typically a dessert. 
Food & Drink 
 What type of food does Jonathan’s Ogunquit serve? 
The menu features thoughtfully prepared, approachable cuisine made with high-quality ingredients, designed to complement a relaxed yet refined dining experience. We focus on local Maine and New England seafood and farm to table offerings. 
 Do you offer vegetarian or gluten-free options? 
Yes. Jonathan’s Ogunquit offers vegetarian and gluten-free options, and we do our best to accommodate dietary needs when notified in advance. 
 Do you serve cocktails, wine, and non-alcoholic beverages? 
Yes. The bar features craft cocktails, curated wines, and non-alcoholic options. 
Accessibility, Parking & Practical Details
 Is Jonathan’s Ogunquit wheelchair accessible? 
Yes. The main dining and performance areas are wheelchair accessible. We have an elevator to go upstairs to the listening room as well. 
 Is parking available? 
Yes, Jonathan’s Ogunquit offers parking for our guests at the restaurant and we have an auxiliary lot across the street on Route 1. 
 Are you open year-round? 
Jonathan’s Ogunquit operates year round, with dates and hours varying throughout the year. Current hours are always listed on the website. 
Events, Policies & Special Occasions
 Do you host private events or buyouts? 
Yes. Jonathan’s Ogunquit is available for private events, including celebrations, weddings, corporate gatherings, and special occasions. 
 Is Jonathan’s Ogunquit appropriate for special occasions or date nights? 
Absolutely. Jonathan’s Ogunquit is a popular choice for anniversaries, birthdays, and memorable evenings out in Ogunquit. 
 Is there a dress code? 
There is no formal dress code. Guests typically dress smart-casual, especially for evening performances. 
Artists & Reputation
 Why do artists choose to perform at Jonathan’s Ogunquit? 
Artists value Jonathan’s Ogunquit for its attentive audiences, exceptional acoustics, and intimate setting that allows for meaningful connections with guests.

Live Music, Comedy & Fall Events in Ogunquit: October & November 2026
By 
 / 
Summer may get most of the attention in Ogunquit, but anyone who knows us knows that some of the best nights at Jonathan’s happen after the beach chairs are packed away.
The pace around town slows down a little, the evenings get cooler, and upstairs at Jonathan’s, the stage stays very busy.
This October and November, our Upstairs Listening Room at Jonathan’s Ogunquit goes from blues piano and legendary folk singers to Bob Marley comedy, Fleetwood Mac, U2, the Eagles, Tom Petty, American Idol favorite Crystal Bowersox and plenty in between.
And because this is Jonathan’s, you can have dinner downstairs, walk upstairs and settle in for a show, all without moving your car.
Here’s what we have planned for fall 2026.
Jonathan’s Ogunquit’s fall 2026 entertainment schedule runs from October 2 through November 28, with live music, comedy, tribute shows and special events in the Upstairs Listening Room. Highlights include Bob Marley, Crystal Bowersox and Paula Poundstone, along with tributes to the Eagles, Fleetwood Mac, U2, Tom Petty, The Rolling Stones, Steve Miller and Van Morrison. 
Live Music in Ogunquit Kicks Off October 
We open October with two very different nights featuring musicians with some impressive résumés.
On Friday, October 2 at 8:00 PM, Aztec Two-Step 2.0 returns to Jonathan’s. Aztec Two-Step has been part of the American folk-rock landscape for more than five decades, beginning when Rex Fowler and Neal Shulman met at a Boston open mic in 1971. The current lineup, led by Fowler and Dodie Pettit, carries that musical legacy forward with Aztec Two-Step favorites and music from their acclaimed Simon and Garfunkel repertoire.
The following night, Saturday, October 3 at 7:30 PM, pianist Anthony Geraci brings a Night of Jazz and Blues to Jonathan’s.
Geraci is a two-time Blues Music Award winner of the Pinetop Perkins Piano Player Award and has earned 19 Blues Music Award nominations. Over more than four decades, he has performed and recorded with artists including Ronnie Earl, Sugar Ray and the Bluetones, Charlie Musselwhite and Duke Robillard.
His Jonathan’s show also comes just after the September release of his newest album, Where Were You, on legendary blues label Delmark Records.
Not a bad way to start October. 
’80s and ’90s Dance Music and an Intimate Night with Suede 
There are listening-room nights, and then there are nights when sitting still is probably not going to happen.
On Friday, October 9 at 8:30 PM, Carbonated is back for an ’80s and ’90s dance party.
Expect the music of Journey, Rick Springfield, Loverboy, Duran Duran, The Cars, Goo Goo Dolls, Sublime, Simple Minds, The Outfield and Poison, with the occasional Led Zeppelin or Rush song making its way into the mix.
The next evening brings a complete change of pace.
On Saturday, October 10 at 7:00 PM, Suede, accompanied by Fred Boyle on piano, returns for an intimate evening upstairs. Suede’s mix of jazz, blues and popular song, combined with her unmistakable voice and wonderful relationship with an audience, is exactly the kind of performance our small listening room was made for. 
Folk, a Steve Miller Tribute and Comedy in One Ogunquit Weekend 
This three-night stretch from October 16 through 18 might sum up Jonathan’s better than anything we could write about ourselves.
Where else can you spend Friday with an influential singer-songwriter, Saturday singing along to Steve Miller and Sunday laughing with one of Maine’s most famous comedians?
It begins Friday, October 16 at 7:00 PM, when Cris Williamson comes to Ogunquit for a special Breathing Room album release celebration.
Williamson has been an influential voice in independent music for decades. Her landmark 1975 album The Changer and the Changed became one of the defining recordings of the women’s music movement and helped build an independent musical community outside the traditional record industry.
Then we’re turning up the volume.
The Space Cowboys take the stage Saturday, October 17 at 8:30 PM with their Steve Miller tribute and the music behind The Joker, Fly Like an Eagle, Rock’n Me, Jet Airliner, Take the Money and Run and more.
And on Sunday, October 18 at 7:30 PM, it’s Bob Marley.
No, not that Bob Marley.
Maine’s Bob Marley has been making New England audiences laugh for decades with his rapid-fire observations about family, everyday life and, naturally, Maine. He’s appeared on Comedy Central and on late-night television with David Letterman, Jay Leno and Conan O’Brien.
He’s also a longtime Jonathan’s favorite, and if you’ve seen Bob here before, you already know why we keep bringing him back. 
The Rolling Stones, Richard Shindell and a Psychic Medium 
October keeps going.
19th Nervous Breakdown returns to Jonathan’s on Saturday, October 24 at 8:30 PM, after a blast of a show with us this past spring. They’ll bring the riffs, swagger and enormous songbook of The Rolling Stones back upstairs for another night.
Then, on Sunday, October 25 at 7:00 PM, we switch gears again with acclaimed singer-songwriter Richard Shindell.
Shindell is one of contemporary folk music’s most respected storytellers, known for richly drawn characters and songs that reward actually listening to the words. He’s also known for Cry Cry Cry, his folk collaboration with Dar Williams and Lucy Kaplansky.
And because Halloween is approaching, things get a little more mysterious.
Psychic medium and spirit messenger Vicki Monroe joins us Thursday, October 29 at 7:00 PM for an evening unlike anything else on our fall calendar. 
Halloween Weekend in Ogunquit: U2 and Creature Features 
Halloween weekend starts with some very big songs.
On Friday, October 30 at 8:00 PM, Joshua Tree brings its U2 tribute to Ogunquit.
Think Sunday Bloody Sunday, Pride In the Name of Love, With or Without You, Where the Streets Have No Name, One and decades of U2 favorites.
Then, on Halloween itself, Saturday, October 31 at 7:00 PM, we’re doing something completely different with Creature Features, a Wild and Scenic Film Festival.
The special Halloween-night program celebrates wildlife, nature and the environment through film. We couldn’t resist putting something a little wild on the calendar for October 31. 
November Live Music and Comedy at Jonathan’s Ogunquit
If October jumps all over the musical map, November might have you digging through your old record collection.
We have the Eagles. Van Morrison. Tom Petty. Fleetwood Mac. Plus Crystal Bowersox and some very funny people.
First Stop Hotel California
Another Tequila Sunrise returns for two nights, Friday, November 6 and Saturday, November 7 at 7:45 PM.
From Take It Easy and Peaceful Easy Feeling to Desperado, Life in the Fast Lane and Hotel California, the Eagles created one of those catalogs where you don’t realize quite how many songs you know until a band starts playing them.
There is a reason Another Tequila Sunrise keeps coming back to Jonathan’s, and a reason we gave them two nights this fall.
From Van Morrison to a Night of Comedy
On Friday, November 13 at 8:00 PM, Moondance takes over the stage with the music of Van Morrison.
Brown Eyed Girl. Into the Mystic. Domino. Tupelo Honey. Moondance.
Van Morrison has spent more than six decades moving between rock, folk, blues, soul and jazz, and Moondance brings that unmistakable catalog to life upstairs at Jonathan’s.
The following night, Saturday, November 14 at 7:30 PM, we’re swapping music for stand-up with Jimmy Cash Comedy.
A New England comedian with a working-class perspective and an easygoing storytelling style, Cash finds plenty of material in family, work and everyday life.
Tom Petty Followed by Crystal Bowersox
The weekend before Thanksgiving is another great example of just how varied our little stage can be.
On Friday, November 20 at 7:30 PM, Runnin’ Down a Dream celebrates one of the great American rock songbooks with a Tom Petty tribute.
American Girl. Refugee. Free Fallin’. Learning to Fly. Mary Jane’s Last Dance. We could keep going.
Then on Saturday, November 21 at 7:30 PM, Crystal Bowersox returns to Jonathan’s.
Millions of people first discovered Bowersox when she finished as runner-up on Season 9 of American Idol, but that’s only part of her story. She’s developed a career as a singer-songwriter rooted in rock, blues, country and Americana, with a powerful voice that’s particularly impressive when you’re hearing it from just a few feet away.
And that’s one of the things we love about this room. Artists you’ve watched on national television or seen on much larger stages suddenly feel very close.
