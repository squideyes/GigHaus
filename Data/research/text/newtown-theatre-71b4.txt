Live Music and Tribute Bands in Bucks County
The Newtown Theatre hosts live concerts and nationally touring tribute bands every week in Bucks County, PA. Located just 20 minutes from Doylestown and 40 minutes from Philadelphia, our 260-seat historic venue features classic rock, pop, folk, and country tribute acts—perfect for fans who love legendary music in an intimate setting.

Private Parties and Family Events
An Event You’ll Always Remember
People rent the historic Newtown Theatre for all kinds of fun and creative events: anniversary parties, grown-up birthday parties, bachelor/bachelorette parties, showers, weddings, bar and bat mitzvahs, family reunions. If you can plan it, we can host it! 
The Newtown Theatre can accommodate 2 to 260 people. During your rental, you’ll get the entire facility to yourself. You can bring in your own food and drink (limitations may apply), play a movie, or bring your own entertainment (limitations may apply). From start to finish, your event will be guided by our friendly and hospitable staff. 
Note that we no longer offer childrens’ birthday parties. 
What We Offer
The Newtown Theatre provides:
Access to the concession stand throughout the party if you or your guests wish to purchase anything
Tables and chairs for up to 30 guests
Staff assistance throughout the party
A microphone and sound equipment if needed (additional charge applies)
Ability to project onto the big screen (additional charge applies)
You may provide:
Food, cake, or beverages (alcoholic or non-alcoholic…restrictions may apply)
Tablecloths, utensils, paper products
A movie on DVD or BluRay or other entertainment
Decorations (glitter, confetti, helium balloons, or piñatas are NOT allowed)
Book Your PRIVATE EVENT
The quickest way to reach us about a private family event is by contacting us. We'll get back to you as soon as we can, usually within 48 hours.

 Corporate Meetings and Seminars
Your Best Foot Forward
Wow your employees or clients with a meeting or seminar at the historic Newtown Theatre. 
Dating back to the 1800s, the theatre is America’s oldest continuously operating movie theatre and hosted anti-slavery meetings, including sermons by Lucretia Mott and Frederick Douglass.
The theatre, which can accommodate up to 260 people, has undergone nearly $2 million in renovations in recent years. This has brought the theatre up to today’s standards of comfort, convenience, and accessibility.
When you rent the theatre, our entire facility is yours. You can bring in your own food and drink, project slides or video onto our big screen, and use our stage and lobby to present talks or provide supplemental materials. 
From start to finish, your event will be guided by our friendly and hospitable staff. 
What We Offer
The Newtown Theatre can provide:
Access to the concession stand throughout the event if you or your guests wish to purchase anything
A microphone if needed (additional charge applies)
Projection capability if needed (additional charge applies)
A small lectern
Folding tables
Staff assistance throughout your event
You may provide:
Food and beverages (alcoholic or non-alcoholic) - restrictions may apply
Audio/visual presentations
Reports, posters, workbooks, or other materials guests can pick up in the lobby
Book Your Corporate Event
The quickest way to book your corporate event is through our website. We'll get back to you as soon as we can, usually within 48 hours.
