Frequently Asked Questions 
Do you serve food? 
We serve beer and wine, soft drinks, water, light snacks such as pretzels and chips, and complimentary popcorn. 
Can I bring in my own food and beverages? 
No. 
What is the attire? 
Casual attire. 
Where do I park? 
We have FREE parking on site, street parking and there are parking lots nearby. 
How long are the shows? 
Most shows are about 2 hours total. 2 sets, with a 15 min intermission. 
What is the capacity? 
We can seat up to 182 guests. 
Is there VIP seating? 
There are cafe tables near the stage and high-top tables toward the rear of the room. The rest of the room is arranged with chairs in rows. There is a premium price charged for table seats. 
How can I book my band? 
Use this booking request form. 
Can I rent Blue Bamboo for a private event? 
Blue Bamboo is not set up for weddings, birthday or anniversary parties. For private corporate events, please email assist_team@bluebambooartcenter.com for information.

About Us
The show does go on. 
The Show Does Go On
Blue Bamboo continues to bring live performances and a welcoming arts community to Winter Park. Our board, artists, staff, volunteers, and supporters are carrying the music forward.
We are continuing to book performances, welcome new audiences, and keep the Thursday Night Hang free and accessible to the community.
Remembering Chris Cortez
Our founder, Chris Cortez, brought Blue Bamboo to life through his love of music and his belief in bringing people together. After his passing in December 2025, the community he built has kept the stage alive.
Join us for a show, bring a friend, or support the music. Every visit helps this nonprofit arts center continue its work.

The show delivers an enchanting live experience covering everything from the iconic rhythms of Woodstock to the smash hits of Supernatural and beyond! ​Expect elite musicianship, authentic arrangements, and that unmistakable Latin rock groove. ​Blue Bamboo is an intimate room and tickets fly fast.
