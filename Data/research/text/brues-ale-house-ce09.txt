About
Who We Are...
Driven By Our Love of 
Craft Beer, Culinary Arts, &
Community Growth...
 Brues Alehouse Brewing Company was founded in 2015, with a powerful common goal: Create a brewpub unlike any other. Located on the Historic Arkansas Riverwalk in Pueblo, Colorado, Brues brings forth fresh craft beer, from-scratch food, and live music from around the world.
Our company is comprised of creative and driven people who continually work to foster an exceptional experience. From classic European beer styles, to locally sourced dishes, to delicate desserts and espresso, to packed-house concerts, we have you covered for an amazing experience. See you here. 
News
The Brewery
Our Love of Beer
Our brewing philosophy is decidedly German. We obsess over process details with a commitment to both science and art. We strive to brew elegant European and Continental Lagers, fresh IPAs, and complex Belgian Ales. Our ingredients are always top quality: Malts from Bamberg, Germany. Hops from across the globe. Reverse-Osmosis Water. Yeast sourced from world-class breweries. Fresh fruits and whole spices.

The Bar
A lot of brewpubs are just that. Not us. Our knowledgeable bar staff works together to come up with an array of cocktails, blending old world classics with new-age twists. If you're not a beer drinker, we still have a whole bar devoted to serving you.

The Kitchen
Culinary Experts Working as one...
Pueblo, Colorado is known for its unique cultural cuisine: a blend Mexican, Italian, German, Native American, and American styles. Here at Brues Alehouse, not only do we understand the local palate for rich, flavorful dishes, but we also have a fierce creative drive to push the boundaries with modern recipes and culinary techniques. The result? A menu beaming with possibilities. Want one of the best burgers you've ever had? We've got you. You're Vegan or Gluten-Free? We have multiple Vegan and Gluten-Free options. Brunch? Every Sunday from 10 to 2. Fresh vegetable dishes? We source our produce from the source (Musso Farms) when in season. Check out the menu and see what gets you excited!
The Bakery
Excitingly Fresh...
Our bakery is both elegance and technique. From traditional Macarons to delicate tarts, our bakery evolves with the seasons to provide our guests with some of the finest specialty desserts in southern Colorado. With a constant rotation of baked goods, you should plan to arrive early in the day before the case sells out. We can also do custom orders for cakes and specialty items for any event or gathering you may have!
WHILE YOU'RE HERE...
Just east of us on the Riverwalk is our fantastic partner, Leonardo da Vinci Museum of North America. This incredible museum brings the genius, creativity, and curiosity of Leonardo da Vinci to life through hands-on exhibits, art, science, and innovation. Whether you're visiting for the first time or bringing the family back for another adventure, it's a great way to spend some time exploring Pueblo's Riverwalk. Make a day of it—stop by the museum, take a stroll along the Riverwalk, and then come see us at Brues Alehouse for a cold beer, great food, and a relaxing end to the day!
