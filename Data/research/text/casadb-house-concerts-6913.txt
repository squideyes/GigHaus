 An Evening with Jess Klein and friends
 Sunday, March 2, 2025 @ 5:00PM — 8:00PM (CST, UTC-06)Sun, Mar 2, 2025 @ 5:00PM — 8:00PM (CST, UTC-06)
 Casadb House Concerts, 7704 El Dorado Drive, Austin
Jess Klein came to this world to pull firebrand power and childlike joy from our darkest shadows. Her acoustic-driven, hook-laden story songs revel in the polychrome of human experience - joy, rage, sensuality and resilience. The New York Times calls her “A songwriter with a voice of unblinking tenacity.”
Jess has performed across the globe in pin-drop listening rooms, cozy theaters and at raucous festivals. She has toured nationally and internationally supporting Arlo Guthrie, Josh Ritter, Damien Dempsey, John Fullbright and Jonathan Byrd and has opened for such legends as Ani DiFranco, Steve Earle and Alejandro Escovedo. Jess has appeared on Good Morning America and NPR’s All Things Considered and has performed at the Newport, Winnipeg, Falcon Ridge and Philadelphia Folk Festivals as well as Fuji Rock Festival in Japan.
Jess is currently based in Hillsborough, NC, but will be in Austin the weekend of the show to start work on her newest album.
Jess’s 2023 single, “Annie’s Place”, is an ode to the sweetly dysfunctional neighborhood of musicians Jess lived with and loved when she was based in Austin from 2008-2016.
She can also be heard hosting the podcast “Jess Klein’s Big Table: Conversations with Artists and Activists” on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube.
At the March 2nd show Jess will be joined by songwriter Jenny Reynolds, producer, multi-instrumentalist and songwriter Mark Addison, and perhaps another renegade or two.
What: Jess Klein and friends
When: Sunday Mar 2, 2025 - Doors: 5 PM - Music: 6 PM
Where: CasaDBs 7704 El Dorado Drive, Austin, Texas 78737
Bring: Your fav drinks, food and snacks.
Cost: $25 suggested donation accepted at the door.
Venue: We'll be in the CasaDB backyard performance space. Open concert seating in a covered space off the backyard.
 Suggested Donation of $25 to the artist
