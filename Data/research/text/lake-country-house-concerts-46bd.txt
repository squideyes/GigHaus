About Lake Country House Concerts 
Rich Hancock has been hosting live music at his home since 2016. This is not a business. It’s a place where friends and guests can gather to support and enjoy live music. 
Each show has a suggested donation to attend, and 100% of all contributions go directly to the performers. We enjoy bringing in both regional and touring artists to our indoor and outdoor stage, and occasionally also partner with local friends to host shows in their barn venues. 
All shows are general admission and all ages. Indoor shows and barn shows have capacity limitations, so an RSVP is required in order to attend. RSVPs are encouraged for outdoor shows but are not required.
We encourage guests to BYOB. We do not provide or sell beverages.
New shows are announced on our website, on our Facebook page, and via email to our subscribers.

About Valley Fox 
Valley Fox is a Milwaukee-based folk power trio whose music lives somewhere between folk, bluegrass, country, and Americana. Featuring Laura Bomber on guitar, Joe Wais on mandolin, and Tori Yocum on upright bass, the group pairs gritty acoustic instrumentation with rich, haunting three-part harmonies.
There’s an intimacy to Valley Fox’s music that feels especially at home in a house concert setting. Their sound moves from delicate and reflective to driving and energetic, with thoughtfully crafted songs and vocal harmonies at the heart of it all.
In 2025, Valley Fox released their debut full-length album, Bear Dance, an eight-song collection showcasing the sound the trio has built together. Their music feels rooted in traditional acoustic music while remaining distinctly their own, sometimes tender, sometimes foot-stomping, and always centered around the chemistry of three musicians playing and singing together.
Writer Jeff Stehr calls Valley Fox “champions of acoustic charm, haunting harmonies and intimate, well-crafted songs” whose music carries a magic that “flicker[s] like fireflies” around those fortunate enough to hear it.
We’re excited to welcome Valley Fox for an intimate evening of live music.
