Lucy Clearwater & Lennon VanderDoes
Lucy Clearwater is a gifted Los Angeles–based folk/Americana singer-songwriter who writes songs the way some people keep journals. Honestly, carefully, and with a deep respect for feeling. With a background in classical violin and a sound rooted in acoustic instruments and human-scale storytelling, her music carries emotional clarity, wit, and the warmth of California folk traditions. A 2024 Kerrville Folk Festival New Folk Competition winner, Clearwater has spent the last several years on the road, touring across the U.S., Canada, the U.K., and Europe. 
Her debut album, "People ≠ Possessions" was produced by Grammy-winner Tyler Chester and recorded mostly live to tape at Bell Choir Studios in Los Angeles. Featuring contributions from her vibrant creative community including Madison Cunningham, Kenneth Pattengale (The Milk Carton Kids), Sean Watkins (Nickel Creek) and many more, the record reflects Clearwater’s belief that music is something handmade, shared, and meant to be lived with.. We are so excited for her to play at Lonely Roads - her voice is pure butter...
Lennon VanderDoes is an indie singer-songwriter from Arizona whose evocative music draws inspiration from artists like "Lord Huron," "Sufjan Stevens" and "City and Colour." His unique sound captures the essence of the indie-folk scene, mixed in with elements of Americana, indie rock and alt country.
lennon gained widespread recognition as a standout contestant on NBC's The Voice, where one of his emotional performances moved John Legend to tears.
lennon's music is deeply inspired by his life on the road, reflecting the stories and experiences he gathers while touring. Based in Phoenix, he continues to evolve as a songwriter, crafting songs that resonate with audiences through their authenticity and emotional depth... Lennon performed at our first Lonely Roads indoor concert a few years ago - we are thrilled he is returning!

Lucia Flores-Wiseman
Lucia Flores-Wiseman is a Mexican-American singer-songwriter whose music glows with emotional depth and quiet power. Since 2018, she’s captivated audiences with a sound that blends Indie, Folk, Soul and Jazz influences -offering poetic storytelling, and a rare ability to make every note feel like a conversation with the heart. Lucia finds inspiration fromBrandi Carlile, Amy Winehouse, and Norah Jones - Lucia crafts songs that are rich, intimate and emotionally raw—inviting listeners into a space of reflection, healing, and connection. 
Lucia gained national attention as the 4th place finalist on Season 27 of NBC’s The Voice, where she earned a coveted four-chair turn in her blind audition and joined Team Adam Levine. Throughout the season, she impressed with her versatility, soulful interpretations, and original artistry—often transforming classic songs into emotionally resonant experiences. Her collaboration with Adam Levine in the finale showcased the unique connection she builds with both songs and audiences. 
Lucia has shared the stage with Brandi Carlile and performed at numerous festivals and venues across the country. Lucia is an accomplished songwriter and has released several singles that reflect her introspective and emotionally rich style. She is currently wrapping up her debut EP, a project that promises to further reveal her voice, vision, and vulnerability as an artist. 
We recently heard Lucia perform live and were immediately transported by the depth, richness and tone of her voice - she is a unique talent and we are so fortunate to have Lucia share an evening with us here on the Kep Peninsula...

Megg Farrell - "sweet megg"
Megg Farrell aka "Sweet Megg" is a dynamic vocalist and songwriter originally from New York City, now based in Nashville. Her music blends jazz, country, and blues, drawing influence from legends like Billie Holiday, Patsy Cline, and Edith Piaf. 
Megg’s musical journey includes studying jazz in Paris and extensive international touring, including collaborations with Postmodern Jukebox and Cirque du Soleil. Her work captures the nostalgia of classic styles and a modern creative edge, making her performances engaging and timeless.
Her story starts in the East Village and Brooklyn - in cramped bars, coffee shops, bistros and DIY lofts. At 22 she moved to Paris to study jazz vocals and played gigs around the City of Light. Back in New York, she spent ten years as a full-time jazz singer, steeped in early jazz and soulful blues. 
Her studio album, Never Been Home, climbed to #1 on the Alt-Country Radio chart and #23 on the Americana chart and showcases for her strong songwriting talents. Her recent jazz album recorded at Old Soul Studios daringly moves from Tom Waits to Sugar Pie DeSanto to Duke Ellington. She calls it Americana jazz, and it puts each musician’s personality up front. The result is playful, curious, and deeply personal.
We’re thrilled to welcome Megg to Lonely Roads Music - expect great songs, a great band, and a night that’s certain to be memorable...

why do we host house concerts?
We believe in supporting live music and sharing good food. We believe that live music has the transcendent ability to bring people together in a common bond. We want musicians to be able to share their gifts with our community and be able to make a living. Let's support live music on the Key Peninsula - it's all about the music and the love...
who are we?
Richard and Cheryl are two people who simply love live music and the magic that happens when it is performed by dedicated and talented independent artists in an intimate setting... 
where do find the artists?
When we hear outstanding musicans who are passionate about creating original work we simply invite them to play for Lonely Roads Music. We have very high standards and try to bring in a wide variety of artists with a focus on rising stars who are just starting to tour nationaly / internationaly. 
how do the artists get paid?
We simply ask those who attend to make donations - 100% of all money collected goes directly to the artists - the suggested donation is $25 and up per person...
what should I bring?
For our outdoor summer concerts bring lawn chairs or blankets - we provide seating for the indoor concerts. We provide homemade street tacos and bottled water but you are also welcome to bring a beverage of your choice or a dessert to share...
where are the concerts located?
Our home is in Lakebay, Washington, on the beautiful Key Peninsula. The address will be sent to you in an email once you RSVP for an event...
do I need to make a reservation in order to attend?
YES, please. Attendance is by invitation only. We would like to know who's coming and how many to expect. Our indoor listening room capacity is limited to about 70 people. Outdoors, we can accommodate larger groups...
where do we park?
Like our name suggests, we are on a lonely road... the last house on a dead-end road... Simply find a spot to park along the dirt road and take the short walk in. Let us know if you have anyone with special needs...
