Velvet Elk Lounge 
Live Music • Good Booze • Private Events
Velvet Elk Lounge was born from the desire to create a small, funky, live music venue in downtown Boulder where you can get a proper cocktail and listen to great, local music.
We're also one of Boulder's premiere event spaces for private gatherings of all styles and sizes, complete with the charm and flair that only the Velvet Elk Lounge can provide. 
This space has been home to many Boulder businesses, including a Cadillac dealership, a bowling alley, a long-standing travel agency, and now a palace of music and fun. We pride ourselves on being an artist forward venue. That means we treat our artists with the same hospitality as every guest who walks through our door. 
Our Impact 
Community is at the heart of everything we do, and so is our commitment to the planet. Thanks to your continued support, we’re not only creating a space where music and hospitality thrive, but we're also working together to reduce our environmental footprint and build a more sustainable future. Every step forward is one we take together.

We Love a Good Party!
Whether you're celebrating a birthday, hosting wedding receptions, holiday parties, welcome cocktails, rehearsal dinners, corporate lunches, panels, or simply need a place for a group lunch or brunch, our private event room or a buyout of the full Velvet Elk Lounge has everything you need.
We can accommodate 105 guests seated or up to 170 guests standing with a full buyout of the Events & Entertainment room and Velvet Elk Lounge bar, and 75 guests in just the Velvet Elk Lounge bar. The Events & Entertainment Room leads into our Velvet Elk Lounge bar area, creating a shotgun-style space together. 
We serve a delicious menu by our sister concept next door, The Post, and feature an innovative beverage menu with seasonal cocktails and local craft beer and wine. 
For all inquiries, please fill out the form below and we’ll be in touch soon.
Check out Menu Inquire Now
Events & Entertainment Room 
Part music hall part event space, our fully private Event & Entertainment Room is made for unforgettable moments. 
This space accommodates up to 65 guests and features a top-notch sound system and stage. Whether you're planning a birthday, team party, comedy night, panel, or intimate performance, this space is all yours to make magic happen, with cocktail-style beverage service, passed appetizers, or an elevated buffet service .
Velvet Elk Lounge Bar
Whether you're hosting a cocktail party, birthday bash, or an exclusive after-hours gathering, the Velvet Elk Bar sets the scene. 
With its deep green booths, full-length bar, and dim, golden-hour lighting, this space is equal parts stylish and intimate. Expect great music, expertly crafted cocktails, and a vibe that’s effortlessly cool from the first drink to the last dance.
