Private Events
Welcome Reception | Rehearsal Dinner | After-Party | Corporate Event | Private Party
Tucked into the heart of Buena Vista, The Lariat is where Colorado’s mountain-town charm meets a stage built for unforgettable nights. Known for incredible live shows, our 3,000 sq. ft. restaurant + music venue transforms seamlessly into a private event space that feels alive with energy and framed by panoramic views of the Collegiate Peaks. Whether you’re planning a wedding welcome reception, rehearsal dinner, after-party, or corporate gathering, this is where food, drinks, and music come together and where your guests won’t stop talking about the night long after last call. For couples, companies, and communities who love a great celebration, The Lariat offers a space that feels both authentic and unforgettable.
Weddings
Your wedding weekend is more than the ceremony, it’s about the celebrations before and after. At The Lariat, we help you set the tone and keep the party going:
Welcome Receptions: Gather your guests with craft cocktails, scratch-made food, and the warm, connected atmosphere of a true music venue.
Rehearsal Dinners: Pair a custom menu with a stage-ready space designed for togetherness.
After-Parties: When the wedding dance floor closes, ours is just getting started. With late-night bites and room for live music or DJs, this is where your people cut loose.
Gourmet Catering: featuring a diverse, internationally and locally inspired cuisine and artisanal desserts
Signature Cocktails: craft beers, wine, and customizable specialty drinks
Unparalleled Music & Entertainment Options: live bands, DJs, personal playlists
If you and your guests love music, this is where your wedding weekend will feel complete.
Corporate Events
Bring a fresh energy to business and social gatherings. Unlike a ballroom, our stage, lighting, and sound create an atmosphere that’s engaging from the first toast to the last song.
Corporate Retreats & Offsites: A mountain-town backdrop that sparks creativity.
Holiday Parties: Food, drinks, and a lively vibe that gets everyone out of “conference mode.”
Networking Events & Fundraisers: Use the stage for speakers, auctions, or entertainment.
Team Celebrations: Mark milestones with great food, cocktails, and a night everyone remembers.
Customized Menus: multi-course family-style dinners, buffets, and passed hors d'oeuvres
Advanced A/V Technology: 6 large format screens, projector
Team-Building Activities: guided hikes, rafting, and more
Food & Beverage
Everything is prepared in-house with menus tailored to your event.
Hand-crafted small plates and passed apps for mingling
Family-style or plated dinners with Colorado flair
Late-night snacks to fuel the dance floor
Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options available
Pair it all with our full-service bar, signature cocktails, Colorado craft beers, and a curated wine list.
Why The Lariat?
Authentic Colorado atmosphere in downtown Buena Vista
Historic event space with 2 outdoor patio spaces
On-site catering and event coordination
Stage, pro sound, and show-ready lighting (live bands, DJs, or playlists)
Flexible layouts for 25–200+ guests with two outdoor patios
In-house food & beverage and event coordination
A/V: state-of-the-art system, 6 large-format screens + projector
20' historic bar and a cozy, intimate room that feels like a great show night
Complimentary, abundant parking + steps from boutique hotels and adventures on the Arkansas River

FAQ
 Are all of your shows +21? 
Most (but not all) performances are 21+. We can make exceptions based on the circumstances. It’s best to call during our open hours to discuss your situation or email info@thelariatbv.com.
 Is there seating for shows? 
Yes! We do have seating available for most shows. There are also stools lining either side of the dance floor and various tables throughout the space.
 How can I purchase tickets? 
We have three options for ticket purchases:
ONLINE: Hop over to the Live Music page on our website, open the event of your interest, and click the Buy Tickets button.
BOX OFFICE: Visit us during our open hours and purchase a real hard copy ticket from your bartender or server for your keepsake box!
DOOR: Although waiting until the last minute can be risky, we will have tickets for shows at the front door if it’s not sold out. However, you are encouraged to purchase tickets ahead of time to guarantee entrance.
 Can I transfer my ticket? 
Yes! Simply forward the electronic ticket or meet in person to hand over the physical ticket to the new recipient.
 Do you have a lost and found?
Credit cards can be retrieved with a valid photo ID. For all other lost and found items please call 719-966-5065 or show up.
 Do you have an ATM? 
Nope. But there is one conveniently located across the street at Collegiate Peaks Bank.
 Can I purchase band merch? 
When the band has merchandise available, it will be for sale. Each performance is different.
 Does The Lariat sell merchandise? 
We sure do! See the current offerings here. Purchases can be made on-site. Sorry, no online sales at this time.
 Can I watch the game when a concert is happening? 
We love sports too, but all TVs will be off when we have a show. Sorry for the inconvenience.
