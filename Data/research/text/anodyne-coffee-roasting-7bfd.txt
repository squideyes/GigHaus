Born in 1999 
Anodyne Coffee Roasting Company is a small-batch roaster proudly working in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. We aim to provide an exceptional cup of coffee using just enough technology coupled with the experience of our crew to ensure satisfaction with every roast, every time. 
Coffee is what we do. It is what we know and are dedicated to perfecting.

 Donations
Interested in Anodyne donating to your cause? Please send an email to donations@anodynecoffee.com with your name, organization, and desired donation. Please provide at least six weeks notice of the request.
Due to the high volume of donation requests we receive, we are unable to reply to all requests. If the request has been approved, we will reply a few weeks prior to the event.

 About
Founded in Milwaukee in 1999, Anodyne Coffee Roasting Company is a small-batch roaster dedicated to crafting exceptional coffee with care and consistency. We use just enough technology, paired with the experience of our team, to ensure every roast meets the same high standard, every time.
Milwaukee is our home, and community has always shaped who we are. Our cafés and roastery are places where coffee brings people together. Whether that’s over a daily ritual, a conversation with a barista, or time spent gathering with friends. We’re proud to be part of the neighborhoods we serve.
