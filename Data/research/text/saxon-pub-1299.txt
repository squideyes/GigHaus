Opened in 1990, the Saxon has hosted well over 30,000 musical performances. It has been the location for films, videos and countless live recordings.
On any given night you might see a major celebrity from the film or music industry either having a beer or sitting in on stage jamming with the locals. This award-winning listening room has helped launch the careers of several prominent entertainers. It is also known for booking some of the greatest music legends around, performing in an intimate atmosphere that Kris Kristofferson likened to “playing in his own living room.”
Whether it’s one of the incredible free weekday happy hour shows, a weekend matinee or a late night performance by the next big thing, join us and find out why we are Austin’s choice for live music!
See the award winning documentary “Nothing Stays the Same, The Story of The Saxon Pub” on Amazon Prime.

FAQ's
AGE REQUIREMENT?
Our Venue is 21+
ACCESSIBLE SEATING
We have limited availability for wheelchairs and disability seating. We can accommodate up to two seats per request, but we require a minimum of 48 hours’ notice. Please call Saxon Pub at (512) 448-2552 during business hours to submit your request. 
DO YOU OFFER REFUNDS FOR SHOWS? 
All sales are final; no refunds! 
DO YOU SERVE FOOD?
We have partnered with Wrigleyville South Dogs & Beef, just outside our front door.
IS THERE A COVER CHARGE EVERY NIGHT?
Yes, however, we do have free happy hour shows. Please check our social media.
WHAT ARE YOUR HOURS
Our Summer hours will be Sunday-Saturday 4pm-12am. Fall hours will change for game days!
CAN YOU SMOKE AT THE SAXON
No. Austin now has a public smoking ban that went into effect September 1st, 2005. For smokers, we now have a beautiful patio outside.
DO YOU HAVE A HAPPY HOUR?
Every day from open-6pm. 
DO YOU RESERVE TABLES?
Not as a rule, but it depends on the show. Often, artists require us to reserve tables for their use.
IS THE SAXON AVAILABLE FOR PRIVATE PARTIES?
Yes, please fill out the form under the Private Events page.
DO YOU SHOW SPORTING EVENTS?
Yes, we will be showing sporting events on the weekends, both indoors during the day and on the patio. At night, however, the music has priority. The Super Bowl party is the only exception.
HOW CAN I BOOK MY BAND AT THE SAXON?
Email us at saxonbooking@gmail.com or call us at (512) 448-3663. For a faster response, please email us with your booking requests, questions, or promotional and press packets.
DO YOU HAVE HOUSE PA AND LIGHTS?
Yes, and we have received numerous favorable reviews for our sound. 
 Interested in an internship in sound engineering or Talent Buying?!? Email us at saxonbooking@yahoo.com
