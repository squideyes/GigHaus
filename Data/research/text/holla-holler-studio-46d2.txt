What is
Holla Holler?
Our mission is simple: to bring artists and audiences closer together in a space built for deep listening and real connection. Limited to 40 VIP guests, our intimate live and streaming venue offers pristine sound, thoughtful curation, and performances that feel personal, powerful, and rare. This is live music the way it’s meant to be experienced.​

Built for the artist
At Holla Holler, everything we do is built for the artist and in service of the music. We take pride in creating a live experience that honors both the performer and the listener—where artists are supported, heard, and given the space to do their best work.
Qualifying touring artists can ask about our Stay & Play Songwriter Lodging, which includes:
Deeply discounted studio and practice space
A private Songwriter Suite
Lodging for the full band (up to 5 people)

Frequently Asked Questions
01
What is a listening room?
A listening room is all about the music. The small, intimate setting allows you to really connect with the artist. With perfect acoustics, the environment of a listening room allows you to experience live music in a way you never have before. Listening rooms are not bars, but an immersive way to connect with music. All shows at Holla Holler are live streamed, recorded, broadcast international, so others can get a taste of what we do!
02
Do you serve food and drink?
We do not serve food and drink.
03
When do shows start?
Shows start promptly on the hour, typically at 7:00pm. Please arrive 15 minutes before show time to grab your seat. 
We ask that you not talk during the shows out of respect for the artist and other listeners, but cheering, clapping and feedback and fun is HIGHLY encouraged. The shows are recorded and live streamed internationally. 
04
How long do shows last?
Our shows last 90 minutes. Depending on the band, there may or may not be a 15-minute intermission. Each artist runs their own show, and format varies. Sometimes we have an opening act, sometimes there are numerous encores - based on audience encouragement. Cheer your favorites on!
