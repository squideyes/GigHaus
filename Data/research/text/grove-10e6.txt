FAQ
Questions? We’ve got you.
Everything worth knowing before a night in the trees. Still stuck? Reach out — we’re friendly.
Before You Come
How do I get tickets?
Head to our Shows page, pick your night, and hit Get Tickets — you’ll check out through Curtain Call, our ticketing partner. Buy ahead when you can; the good ones move.
Do my kids need a ticket?
Nope. Kids 12 and under always get in free — every show, every time.
Is it really all ages?
It is. Bring the whole crew. The only age line is the bar — that’s 21+ with a valid ID.
What does it cost to park?
Nothing. Parking is free and on-site.
Can I bring my own snacks?
Yes — pack a snack and bring it on in. There’s also a food truck (usually more than one) at every show, so come hungry either way.
Can I leave and come back?
Yep — re-entry is welcome. Your wristband’s good for the night.
What should I wear?
You’re in the woods after dark, so: comfortable shoes, a layer for when it cools off, and maybe a little bug spray. Dress for a campfire, not a red carpet.
Is The Grove accessible?
Yes — we’ve got accessible parking and pathways, and we want everyone to enjoy a night in the trees. Give us a call ahead of time and we’ll make sure you’re set.
The Drinkery
Is there a bar?
A full one — including the Treehouse Bar, pouring from up in the trees. Cold beer, cocktails, and a seat by the fire.
Wait, $4 beers?
For real. No stadium prices out here — honest pours, every show.
Weddings & Events
Can I get married at The Grove?
You can — and people do. String lights, a stage, fire pits, and a treehouse make for the kind of woodland wedding folks remember. Start with our wedding inquiry and we’ll take it from there.
What does a wedding cost?
Venue pricing starts at $2,000 Sunday–Thursday and $2,500 Friday/Saturday, with simple add-ons like a bartender or sound engineer. The numbers are right up front — no request-a-quote runaround.
Can I rent The Grove for a private event?
Reunions, parties, retreats, get-togethers — yes. Tell us what you’re dreaming up on the Contact page and we’ll see if the woods are free.
The App & Staying in the Loop
Is there an app?
There is — download it for iPhone. It’s the easiest way to keep shows in your pocket.
How do I hear about new shows first?
Join the list at the bottom of any page and follow us on Instagram and Facebook — that’s where ‘just announced’ goes up first.
Finding Us
Where is The Grove?
702 Happy Valley Road, Glasgow, Kentucky — tucked in the woods behind Ralphie’s Fun Center. Once you turn in, just follow the string lights.
