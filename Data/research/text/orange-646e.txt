Announcing Orange! A Coffee, Art, and Music experience! Tucked into the restored historic landmark Green Lantern Building in a bucolic village on the banks of the mighty Delaware River, on the Appalachian Trail, a new and fresh arts venue is born. Orange is the first e-conscious, ethical, and carbon neutral arts adventure in the region. Coffee is slow at Orange. Conversations are gentle; friendships are warm.
The Coffee: We proudly serve ethically-sourced, small farm / big impact roasts of Passenger Coffee. At Orange, every organic espresso is measured carefully, our specialty coffees are dripped one cup at a time, our signature sweets and savories are baked daily in small batches.
The Art: With fast paced art openings and rotating exhibits of New York, Philly and local artists, Orange is a gallery that promises engagement, enlightenment, and a celebration of diversity and inclusion, proud to shine a light in a gloomy world. 
The Music: Professional touring folk acts will take the stage at Orange: an intimate space with original tin ceiling, maple floors, clear sight-lines, pro sound and lighting. Following the traditions of Godfrey Daniels, Club Passim, Caffe Lena, and New York City’s famed Gerdes, Orange offers a first class listening and gathering room energy.
