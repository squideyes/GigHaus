Located in the historic Whiteaker neighborhood, The Hybrid shines as a home for creativity and community. Founded by artists and musicians with a focus on uplifting local art in all of its expressions, it provides a space where people can come together to share their talents and experience local culture. Boasting a gathering hall, stage, art gallery, Equiano Coffee Roaster, Brown Hornet Records, and Anna Vintage & Art Shop, The Hybrid has become a beating heart of creativity in Eugene.

A singer-songwriter known for his lyrical acumen and plaintive harmonica style, Joe Pug dropped out of college and moved to Chicago where he worked as a carpenter before breaking into the city’s music scene. Since 2008 he has released a string of critically-acclaimed albums and toured heavily in the U.S. and abroad. Paste Magazine wrote of his music: “Unless your surname is Dylan, Waits, Ritter or Prine, you could face-palm yourself to death trying to pen songs half as inspired."
He has toured with Steve Earle, Levon Helm, The Killers, Justin Townes Earle, Sturgill Simpson, and many others. He has appeared at Lollapalooza, Bonnaroo, and The Newport Folk Festival. His music has appeared on NPR’s “Prairie Home Companion” and “Mountain Stage”. His music has been released by Lightning Rod Records, which features an alumni roster of Jason Isbell, Billy Joe Shaver, and James McMurtry.
Additionally, he is the creator and host of the popular podcast The Working Songwriter.

About
The Hybrid Gallery is a third space. 941 W. 3rd Avenue boasts a music venue, art gallery, and a space for creative expression in all its forms. It is a space for creatives by creatives, hosting workshops, local makers’ markets, performances, community focused open mics, and countless other opportunities to gather, foster creativity, and build and strengthen relationships. 
The Hybrid Gallery offers a queer and neurodivergent friendly container that is low barrier, inclusive, and accessible and fosters creative culture in Eugene.
To enter the Hybrid Gallery is to enter a place of possibility. Located in the historic Whiteaker district of Eugene, the Hybrid has existed for the last decade, serving as a venue, a gallery, events space, and a community hub. In the last couple years, the focus on the arts has been revitalized with new passion and vision. The arts team has invested in creating a safe space for queer and neurodivergent community, and the visual and performing arts. Curating art shows and open calls to artists of all experience levels. Supporting individual artists with guidance and coaching in sharing their work with the public. Providing networking opportunities between experienced professional artists and new to the scene creatives. Offering sliding scale programming, including inclusive figure drawing, artist peer to peer support groups, poetry and writing circles. open mic, art and crafting workshops, artists and maker pop up markets, gallery receptions, and opportunities for artists to show, talk about, and sell their work.
We can’t wait to meet you!

Programming Support
There are many programming support funding opportunities available. 
These are avenues to support specific things you like to do or experience at the Hybrid such as:
Arts + Creativity (Arts Workshops, Art Shows, Opening Receptions, Artist Talks)
Performing Arts (Dance, Theater, Comedy)
Storytelling, Books + Writing (Eugene Poetry Club and Rainbow Writers’ Guild)
Music (Musician Meetups, Live Music, Open Mics, Jams)
Community Focused Gatherings (such as Book Clubs, Peer Support Groups, Dances, Trivia)
Mind + Body + Spirit Offerings (such as Liminal Space, Yoga, Meditation, Herbalism, Sound Baths)
Local Artisan in our Retail Space (sell your goods and help pay for staffing)
There are two other tiers currently available:
Free Popcorn Tier $10 per month, self explanatory
Open Mic Regular $25 per month - your contribution to help us put on our Community Open Mic
Scholarship Fund
We are planning to launch a non-profit arm of the Hybrid in early 2027 that will help fund scholarships. This will enable us to provide low barrier access as well as pay instructors and facilitators fairly.
Most of our offerings are low cost, accessible, and/or sliding scale. What is sliding scale? Sliding scale is a tool used to offer access to goods and services at multiple price points so that financial resourcing is not the deciding factor for access. (If you'd like to learn more about how that works, here is a great resource: https://www.wortsandcunning.com/blog/sliding-scale)
Thank you for being here. The Hybrid quite literally wouldn't exist without you.
