“When the house is rockin’, don’t bother knockin’, just come on in”
Hamilton Park House Concerts offers a unique opportunity to see singer/songwriters, bands, & events performed in the intimate setting of an 1864 Second Empire Style home on Staten Island, NY. 
 View Past Shows
Upcoming Events
“Between our dreams and actions, lies this world” 
Bruce Springsteen
From the sole perspective of the concert series, if we can take 1 positive from the impact of the onset of the pandemic it is that it opened up to us a new space for our shows. Prior to Covid we never seriously entertained doing shows outdoors. Where better to save our sanity and safely host shows. So outside it was. Our yard has served us well these past 2 years. Blessed with ample space for both the audience and band alike to safely gather. We want to thank the musicians and our audience, whether they numbered 45 or 125, for their support! In addition, we offer tremendous gratitude to our neighbors, without whom we could never succeed and continue!
Thank you, as always, for your continued support!

Just one man with his guitar, voice and the poetic capacity to spin a tale to music. We are lucky to have him. Won’t you please join us. Tickets are available at $25. “A unique opportunity to see singer/songwriters, bands, & events performed in the intimate setting of an 1864 home on the North Shore of Staten Island, NY”
Founded by Maureen Campbell and Ray Heffernan in 2006, Hamilton Park House Concerts is an intimate setting to see top quality live music. 
The Civil War era home, circa 1864 happens to lend itself to wonderful acoustics. 
Ray has always been a huge music fan and when the opportunity presented itself to host Francis Dunnery in the house in 2006, he jumped in with enthusiasm. 
In the ensuing years, the shows have grown and Ray has established not only a true venue that artists love to play, but also a loyal audience who look forward to the winter/ spring series as well as the autumn series.
