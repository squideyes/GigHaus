Weddings & Events
Weddings 
The White Horse Black Mountain offers a truly memorable and unique setting for your wedding or reception, blending artistic charm with mountain views.
Link to Gallery
Gatherings
Our venue offers a warm, artistic space perfect for reunions, birthdays, community events, and special celebrations. Our flexible venue creates an inviting atmosphere that brings people together in a truly memorable setting.
Business Events
Host your next meeting, retreat, seminar, or team-building event in a space that inspires creativity and connection. With a one-of-a-kind setting, White Horse Black Mountain is ideal for businesses seeking something beyond the usual boardroom.
Music / Sound / Audio-Visual: 
White Horse Black Mountain offers a state-of-the-art sound system plus excellent lighting options for your event. We offer a high-definition projection system with a large 14×17-foot screen along the back of the stage.
more
Food and Catering:
We work with the best caterers in the area, we are happy to connect you or you may bring in your own food. There is no cooking allowed inside the premises.
Decorations:
White Horse is happy to work with you on decorations, including flowers, tablecloths, etc. You may elect to provide your decorations as well. We do have restrictions on hanging anything on our walls /ceilings without prior approval.
Set Up/Clean Up Costs:
White Horse staff will assist with arranging WH tables and chairs as needed. White Horse will provide trash cans and trash bags. You are responsible for removing all decorations, flowers, trash, etc., and placing them in the garbage outside unless you have contracted us for cleaning services.
Deposits:
The renter will be asked to sign a rental agreement which will spell out all the fees and options chosen. Upon complete execution of the agreement, a 50% deposit will be due. The remaining 50% will be paid at least 90 days before the rental date.
Preferred Vendors
To help make your event seamless and stress-free, Black Mountain Weddings has curated a list of trusted local vendors who are experienced, reliable, and ready to bring your vision to life.
As a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, your generous donation to White Horse comes with exclusive donor pricing. Feel free to inquire about the specifics. There’s no better feeling than transforming your special day into a timeless gift for future generations.

ABOUT WHITE HORSE
White Horse is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to charitable, educational, and cultural enrichment. We exist to support and elevate organizations and initiatives that also qualify as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or any successor provision. Candid Charity Check
Our Purpose
We create high-quality, art-centered experiences that celebrate artists, audiences, and community connection.
At the heart of White Horse is a true listening room—an intentional space where music is honored, distractions fall away, and the relationship between artist and audience becomes the focus of the experience.
Our Mission
To provide an empowering community space for a wide variety of music and art as we engage music education via exposure to a wide variety of genres, as well as music education workshops. To provide a gathering space for groups who seek to bring the community together. To offer direct financial aid to support and empower other area nonprofits that nourish the Swannanoa Valley.
What We Believe
We believe communities need a place to intentionally connect with music and art. We believe that these moments of connection can repair some of the tears in the fabric of our society and that art can have a powerful ripple effect throughout the region and world. We believe that art lightens our load, gives solace to the weary, can help heal the wounded, and honors the part of our lived experience that we can’t find adequate words to express.
Our Facility
White Horse is housed in the historic McMurray Building, originally built in the 1940s as an automotive dealership.
Our 3,000-square-foot listening room seats 180 guests, with additional bar seating and standing room for up to 50 more. The curved wooden ceilings and intimate design create exceptional natural acoustics, making every performance feel close, present, and immersive.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I purchase tickets?
Online is the quickest and easiest way to buy tickets. At the Box Office you can purchase tickets in the lobby before a show. But shows can sell out!
Can I rent the White Horse for a private event?
We are available for rental for a variety of events. The space is used for weddings, rehearsals, birthday parties, reunions, seminars, workshops, corporate events, non-profit events, films, fairs, festivals and more. We offer reasonable rates and top notch service and attention to helping you make your event a rousing success.
How do I book a show at your venue?
For more information about booking shows at White Horse please click here
Do you serve food?
White Horse serves a variety of snacks including crackers, chips, and other items. There are a number of great restaurants within easy walking distance and encourage folks to carry out and bring it to the White Horse.
 Do you accept credit cards?
Yes! We accept major credit cards both through this web site and at the door, and credit cards are also accepted at the bar.
Can I reserve a specific seat?
All of our shows our general admission with no assigned seating. We offer cabaret-style table seating for most shows.
Does White Horse host benefit events?
White Horse Black Mountain prides itself on helping to raise money and awareness for worthy causes, be they extremely local or worldwide. We’ve hosted over 80 benefit events throughout our history. To Inquire About
Producing a BENEFIT EVENT at White Horse CLICK HERE
Do you serve liquor?
We serve a variety local and regionally brewed draft beers. Our Wine List includes a selection of both red and white wine from around the country and around the world.
We also have serve a variety of soft drinks, juices, sparkling water, and several specialty beverages.
How do I find out about upcoming concerts?
Sign up for our NEWSLETTER and check out our Events Page
Do you allow smoking?
Smoking and vaping are only permitted outside the venue at least 20 feet away from any entrance, following any additional local ordinances that may be in place. 
Can I leave and come back during the show?
Yes, you must show your ticket and ID to match name on the ticket upon re-entry
ID Requirements 
To purchase alcohol you must 21 and have a driver’s license, North Carolina special identification card, passport and/ or military identification card. 
The law specifically notes an NC special ID card – but no other state’s ID cards are acceptable as proof of age. Any state’s drivers licenses may be accepted, but not ID cards from out-of-state.
For more information, visit the NC Alcohol Beverage Controls’ website: http://www.ncabc.com/education/permittee/faq.aspx
Can you accommodate Wheel Chairs / special needs?
We always do everything we can to make sure that every guest we have is comfortable and gets to enjoy a great night of live music! Our building is ADA friendly. Feel free to email us if we can assist you in any way.
Can I take pictures?
Our house policy is that you are allowed to use cell phone cameras with NO FLASH for personal use . We do not allow independent cameras, or cameras with zoom or detachable lenses. No professional grade photography without prior written approval. Some bands may have additional restrictions.
