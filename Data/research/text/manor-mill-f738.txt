Bri Bagwell
The Loft
Texas Female Artist of the Decade, Bri Bagwell, is a force to be reckoned with from her rousingly fun live performances to trailblazing recordings garnering her twelve #1 singles on Texas Country Radio and counting. With multiple Female Vocalist of the Year awards, Bri is making waves with her music drawing national attention. People Magazine raves, "Bagwell increasingly finds her name being mentioned amongst country music truth-tellers such as Ashley McBryde and Morgan Wade." Her latest album, Corazón y Cabeza (Heart and Head), has already spawned three #1 singles, "Trenches," "Free Man," and "Hello Highway," the latter inspiring her 2023 summer tour across 10 states and Mexico. Performing over 120 shows a year, Bri has shared the stage with a long list of esteemed artists, including Willie Nelson, Miranda Lambert, Robert Earl Keen, Kacey Musgraves, and Dwight Yoakam. Catch Bri Bagwell live on tour or listen to her latest podcast, ONLY VANS, at www.bribagwell.com.

Thursday, November 19 · 7:00 PM
Slap Dragon
The Loft
Slap Dragon was born in 2023 when bassist Scott Mulvahill and vocalist Laura Berens began writing together and quickly stumbled into a sound that felt both fresh and familiar. Backed by some of Nashville’s finest session musicians, the band fuses soulful vocals, acoustic textures, and groove-heavy arrangements—a sound that blends bluegrass, disco, funk, and R&B into something unmistakably their own. Their music is crafted with intention, yet their live performances always leave room for improvisation, surprise, and play.
The band made its live debut on June 1, 2024 in Nashville, followed shortly by a performance at Bonnaroo, where their infectious energy and musicianship helped them stand out. Their first singles, “Girl Dinner” and “Already Won,” introduced listeners to their signature mix of tight arrangements and joyful spontaneity. On social media, Slap Dragon has begun building notable momentum, with steady growth on TikTok and Instagram—including an Instagram video of “Already Won” surpassing 260,000 views.
Community is at the core of Slap Dragon’s identity. Scott and Laura aim to create spaces where audiences feel free to move, laugh, and connect— shows that feel more like shared celebrations than performances. Their blend of groove, humor, and musical depth attracts listeners across genres and generations.
In October 2025, Slap Dragon surpassed their Kickstarter fundraising goals, paving the way for their debut full-length album, set for release in 2026. With growing online traction, an engaged fanbase, and a dynamic live show, Slap Dragon continues to expand their presence onstage and online—offering a musical experience rooted in community, musicianship, and pure fun.Get Tickets

A Celebration of John Prine with Peaceful Waters
Saturday, November 21 · 7:00 PM · The Loft
With more than 80 years of combined experience in the acoustic music scene around Baltimore, Mark Henkelman, his brother Paul, and their longtime friend Jeff Christopher have spent a lifetime trading songs, stages, and stories. After years of playing everything from barroom favorites to deep‑cut folk tunes, the trio made a heartfelt decision: to focus their talents on sharing the timeless music of John Prine.
Performing under the name Peaceful Waters, the band brings Prine’s catalog to life with the kind of authenticity that only comes from decades of playing together. From the aching beauty of Angel From Montgomery to the grit and truth of Sam Stone, from the rolling nostalgia of Paradise to the wry humor tucked into so many of Prine’s gems, Peaceful Waters delivers each song with love, warmth, and the quiet devotion these stories deserve.
For newcomers, it’s a chance to hear Prine’s songs the way they were meant to be played—up close, honest, and full of heart. For longtime fans, it’s a reminder of why these songs stay with us. And for Mark, Paul, and Jeff, it’s simply the greatest joy they know: honoring a songwriter who shaped their lives, one tune at a time.View moreGet TicketsTickets
Dan + Claudia Zanes The 3rd Annual Turkey Friday Concert with Tyree Austin
Friday, November 27 · 11:00 AM · The Loft
Grammy Award winning rocker turned all-ages entertainer Dan Zanes and Haitian-American jazz vocalist / music therapist Claudia Zanes have been singing in harmony since the day they met in 2016. They perform their soulful style of folk music extensively throughout the United States (and recently returned from a three week tour of Finland and Estonia). Dan + Claudia have released two records on the legendary Smithsonian Folkways label. Their most recent, Pieces of Home expands their vision of joyful intergenerational porch party music. 
Tyree Austin from Gary, Indiana is a recent graduate of Howard University’s jazz vocal program. He brings the uplifting spirit of his hometown’s heroes, The Staple Singers, into every song that he sings. He officially joined Dan + Claudia in 2024. 
This new trio is heavy on vocal harmonies, 12 string guitar, and a social spirit that invites people of all ages to sing along and dance from the very start.

Come visit Manor Mill and see our creative spaces in person. Walk through The Loft, explore the gallery, and discover what makes this historic building so special.
Take a virtual tour
Manor Mill is a restored pre-revolutionary grist mill that houses a fine arts gallery, classes, a live music venue, ceramics studio and workshops, just over the hill from the Monkton Hotel in Baltimore County, MD.
Voted Best of Baltimore 2024 for "Cornucopia of Classes," Manor Mill is a community built upon creativity and learning, rooted in history and situated in the most beautiful area of rural Baltimore County in Monkton, Maryland.
