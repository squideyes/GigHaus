Support OLS By Sharing The Music 
Help One Longfellow Square expand access to live music across our community.
For years, One Longfellow Square has worked to make live music more accessible both inside our venue and out in the community. Through free concerts, open mics, library passes, livestreams, and artist development programs, we’re creating more ways for people to connect through music regardless of age, income, or circumstance.
Thanks to our community of live music supporters, we raised over $30,000 to help fund the programs that allow us to keep sharing the music far beyond our walls.
Your gift supports:
Why This Campaign Matters:
Summer is one of our busiest seasons for free programming and one of the slowest seasons inside the venue. While audiences gather outdoors across Maine, your support helps us continue presenting free community programming while sustaining the listening room that makes it all possible year-round.
Tickets support shows. Membership supports your experience at OLS.
A gift to our Share The Music Fund helps us serve the broader community.

Longfellow Emerging Artist Fellowship (LEAF) 
About LEAF 🌱
The Longfellow Emerging Artist Fellowship (LEAF) is a yearlong program from One Longfellow Square created to support Maine-based musicians as they grow their careers as working artists. Through financial support, mentorship, professional development, performance opportunities, and connection to Maine’s music community, LEAF gives Fellows the time, resources, and relationships to explore what comes next in their work.
Now in its inaugural year, LEAF is already bringing artists together with musicians, venues, industry professionals, and community partners across Maine while creating opportunities to learn, perform, experiment, and build lasting connections. As our first five Fellows continue their year together, we’re excited to begin looking ahead to the 2027 LEAF cohort, with applications open September 15 through October 9.
Learn More
What you’ll receive as a fellow:
$3,000 artist stipend to support your work throughout the fellowship
Monthly workshops, panels + conversations with musicians and music industry professionals
Paid performance opportunities at OLS and with partner organizations throughout Maine
A headlining show at One Longfellow Square
Professional artist assets + promotional support, including high-quality photo and video
Dedicated rehearsal space at OLS
A weekend artist retreat focused on connection, creativity, and professional growth
Networking + mentorship with artists, venues, presenters, community organizations, and industry professionals
Hands-on experience hosting OLS Open Mics and connecting with Maine’s music community
Community engagement opportunities throughout the fellowship year
Applications for LEAF 2027 Are Open!
We’re looking for five Maine-based musicians to join the next year of LEAF. If you identify as an emerging artist and are ready to invest time in your music, build new skills and connections, and become part of a supportive statewide community of artists and industry professionals, we want to hear from you. Applications are open September 15 through October 9.
Apply Today!

More About LEAF 🌿 
Empowering early-career Maine musicians through a year-long journey of growth, mentorship, and performance.
What is LEAF?
The Longfellow Emerging Artist Fellowship (LEAF) is a yearlong program from One Longfellow Square designed to support the artistic and professional growth of Maine-based musicians who identify as emerging artists.
LEAF launched in 2026 with an inaugural cohort of five Maine artists, bringing together financial support, professional development, mentorship, performance opportunities, practical resources, and connection to Maine’s music community. Through monthly sessions, hands-on experience, a weekend retreat, and opportunities to perform and engage with audiences throughout the year, Fellows are given space to invest in their work while building skills and relationships that can support what comes next.
LEAF recognizes that there is no single path to a career in music, and that “emerging” can mean something different for every artist. The program is designed to meet Fellows where they are while helping each artist identify their own goals and the tools, knowledge, and connections that can help them move forward.
Our Mission
LEAF was created to address some of the very real challenges musicians face in building sustainable creative careers. Artists need opportunities to perform, but they also need time, financial resources, professional guidance, strong networks, practical knowledge, and room to experiment and grow.
By bringing those pieces together over the course of a full year, LEAF aims to help Fellows better understand the many sides of a career in music, strengthen how they present and advocate for their work, and build relationships that extend beyond the fellowship itself.
At the same time, LEAF is an investment in Maine’s larger music community. By connecting artists with one another and with venues, presenters, mentors, industry professionals, community organizations, and audiences, we hope to contribute to a stronger and more sustainable music ecosystem across the state.
What Fellows Receive
Over the course of the fellowship year, each LEAF Fellow receives:
$3,000 artist stipend to support their work throughout the fellowship 
Monthly workshops, panels, and conversations with working musicians and music industry professionals, including (but not limited to):
Best booking practices
Marketing your show
Branding
Pitching yourself to industry professionals
Album distribution
Artist taxes, grants, licensing
Songwriting
Paid performance opportunities at OLS and with community partners, including opening slots, outdoor concerts, festivals, showcases, and other events 
A culminating headlining show at One Longfellow Square
Professional artist assets and promotional support, including high-quality photo and video 
Dedicated rehearsal space and access to OLS resources 
A weekend artist retreat focused on creativity, collaboration, and connection 
Networking and mentorship with artists, venues, presenters, community organizations, and industry professionals
Hands-on experience hosting OLS Open Mics and connecting with Maine’s music community 
Individual artist features on 98.9 WCLZ, including interviews, airplay, and promotional support for Fellows’ shows and events throughout the year
Additional community engagement and professional opportunities that arise throughout the fellowship year
No two fellowship years, or Fellows, will look exactly alike. As new partnerships and opportunities arise, we work to connect Fellows with experiences that complement their individual goals and help them put what they’re learning into practice.
Designed with Intention
LEAF was built thoughtfully and deliberately, shaped by years of conversations with musicians, venue operators, presenters, industry professionals, and others across Maine’s music community. We listened closely to the challenges artists face when building a career in music, where support was falling short, and what resources and opportunities could make a meaningful difference.
Those conversations became the foundation for LEAF.
Our inaugural cohort launched in 2026, but that process of listening continues. As LEAF grows, we’ll continue learning from our Fellows and community, refining the program with the same care and responsiveness that went into creating it.
Timeline
Applications Open: September 15, 2026
Application Deadline: October 9, 2026
Notification of Artist Selection: Early January, 2027
Fellowship Begins: March 2027
Fellowship Ends: February 2028
Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for the Longfellow Emerging Artist Fellowship, applicants must:
Be a current Maine resident at the time of application and throughout the fellowship period.
Be 18 years of age or older at the time of application.
Identify as an “emerging” artist. While we understand this term can be subjective, we are looking for artists who are early in their professional music career, are developing their unique voice, and demonstrate significant potential for growth and impact. This typically means you are not yet widely recognized nationally or internationally, have not regularly performed in rooms/venues exceeding a capacity of 50-100, and are actively seeking opportunities for professional development and exposure.
Be available on the second Monday of each month from 6pm-8pm throughout the duration of the LEAF year (March 2027-February 2028)
Demonstrate a commitment to their musical practice and a clear vision for how the fellowship will support their artistic development.
Be available to participate in fellowship activities within the Greater Portland area and across the state of Maine, which may include mentorship sessions, workshops, an artist retreat, and public performances/presentations throughout the fellowship period.
Application Review Process
Submitted LEAF applications will undergo a thorough review, beginning with staff verification for completeness and adherence to the above eligibility criteria. Following this initial stage, each application will be reviewed and scored by at least two members of a diverse, volunteer panel of arts and culture professionals and OLS community members.
