​Our Story...
Robbie and Christie love going to music festivals. After seeing Corinne West at the Live Oak Music festival we became fans. We found Corinne at a house concert in Atascadero and traveled north to see her. Nothing beats sitting in someone's living room, ten feet away from an artist as they sing directly to you. After the show we asked her if she would consider doing a house concert in the Big Bear Valley and she said YES. At the time we lived in a 875 square foot house, so of course we had to build a bigger house in order to have our treasured house concerts.
This started our adventure of designing and building a new home in Baldwin Lake,
California, which we have named Piñon Hill because our hilltop home is surrounded by Piñon (pinyon) trees. Our new home is powered by 20 solar panels, which means "green" music. Our concerts are now outdoors in our forest on our terraced stage.
Concert Details
Our events are ZERO WASTE. No trash - pack it in, pack it out, everything recycled or composted. Guests are asked to bring their own chair, picnic, and drinks.
Our house concert donation is $20 per person. All donations go directly to the artist(s). We are NOT a public venue and
attendance is by invitation only. We have a mailing list of friends, family, and local music lovers.

Ryanhood - September 2025
International Acoustic Music Awards winners for ‘Best Group/Duo’, Ryanhood’s tight vocal harmonies and big acoustic riffs have made them favorites on the touring circuit for over 20 years. A merging of virtuosic musicianship with rich and transformative storytelling, their shows are driven by strong acoustic guitar interplay and turn-on-a-dime harmonies forged in a decades-long friendship. At turns energetic, hopeful, and quietly moving, Ryanhood invites audiences to reflect, laugh, sing, and to stand up and cheer.
​https://www.ryanhood.com
Skelpin - August 2025
Skelpin is an Irish folk music group based in San Diego, California. They perform a fusion of Irish Music, and Spanish Flamenco, with Middle-Eastern percussion and have toured with this Irish Fusion globally. Skelpin is associated with the "Greening of the Gaslamp", a movement of increased Irish culture in the San Diego area. Skelpin also performs each year at the World Music Festival on Catalina Island, held near the end of each October.
​https://www.instagram.com/skelpin/
Finnegan Blue - July 2025
Finnegan Blue is a folk band that blends together Celtic, Bluegrass, and New Orleans Funk with a Punk Rock edge. Spearheaded by Anna Lee Fleming, Finnegan Blue is a group of San Diego musicians specifically rooted in North and South Park. They perform high energy original music written by sibling duo Anna Lee and Willie Fleming with songs such as, "Rye Whiskey," "Come Follow Me," and "San Francisco," to name a few. 
Finnegan Blue has an eclectic instrumentation, allowing them to cover tunes spanning between the Grateful Dead, The Pogues, and Flogging Molly, to Stevie Wonder, Amy Winehouse, and New Orleans Second line tunes. They have performed all over San Diego including the North Park Festival of the Arts, Adams Avenue Unplugged Music Festival, and the Adams Avenue Artwalk. With residencies in the Gaslamp, North Park, and Ocean Beach, you're bound to run into this band if you're a local San Diegan. So check out Finnegan Blue at a show, or feel free to contact them to take your event to the next level!
​https://www.facebook.com/Finneganblue/

Freebo and Alice Howe - June 2023
Freebo is a genuine folk, rock and blues icon who, after over 40 years of recording and touring with many of the great artists of our time (Bonnie Raitt 10 years, CSN, Maria Muldaur, John Mayall, Ringo Starr, Dr. John, Neil Young, & many more) is regarded as one of the most gifted singer-songwriters of today.
Alice Howe knew. There she was, recording at the legendary Fame Recording Studios in Muscle Shoals, Alabama, standing on ground soaked in soul, and she knew the truth. She had to dig deeper than ever before. Her voice had carried her this far in folk circles, but now she needed to tap into the spirits of the musical titans pictured in photos on the walls. They called to her, demanding nothing less.
Freebo https://freebomusic.com
Alice Howe https://www.alicehowe.com
