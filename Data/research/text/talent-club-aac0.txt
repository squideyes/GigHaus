About the Talent Club 
The Talent Club originally opened in 1905. It has been a landmark and favored watering hole in the town of Talent, Oregon for over 100 years.

Live Music & Entertainment 
Bringing the freshest live music and entertainment to Southern Oregon, “Talent Club Live” is known for hosting some of the greatest traveling bands such as Object Heavy, Mike Dillon, Ghost Note, Tony Furtado, Sam Chase and more! 
Regional favorites such as Scott Pemberton, Smiley T and the Tall Trees, Yak Attack, Sweet N Juicy, OWR, and stand-up comedy hosted by Black Market Comedy are always a hit with fans! Click below to see who’s coming to the stage next.
