About Us 
The 443 Social Club & Lounge (formerly The Listening Room at 443) is an eclectic live music venue located in the historic Hawley Green neighborhood.
We have created an intimate home for live, original music boasting a top-notch sound system and a comfortable, unique, and funky vibe.
We host regional and national singer-songwriters in our cozy room without all the usual distractions. Our guests enjoy music the way it’s meant to be experienced, learn the stories behind the songs, and get up close and personal with our performers.
We are passionate about live music, our community, and creating a venue where all are welcome.
We fill our calendar with talented touring musicians from around the country – many who have never visited Syracuse before – and the very best regional performers. The connection between artists and storytellers with an appreciative, attentive audience creates a unique and powerful magic.
The flipside of our “up close and personal” vibe is the financial constraint of our limited square footage – our seated capacity is only 75 people. We depend on the support of our community to survive.
If you’d like to help us keep original music coming to Syracuse and become part of our passionate community of live music lovers, consider becoming a member – you can find details, price levels and all the groovy perks on our website HERE.
We would be honored if you would join us.

 What should I expect from a visit to The 443? 
The 443 is an eclectic, intimate, live music venue with a full bar and a carefully curated selection of wine, craft beer, and specialty cocktails. We are only open for scheduled live music performances and other events.
Our room is a mix of regular height and bar tables set up cabaret-style, and the sightlines are excellent. Our friendly staff will visit your table to take your order, and we have a $15 per person minimum food/beverage purchase requirement. You are expected to limit conversation during the performance and focus on the artist gracing our stage.
 Back to Index 
 How can I get the best seats for a show? 
We are a tiny room, with just 75 seats, so there is not a bad seat in the house.
TICKET INFO:
The 443 is an intimate listening room with table service.
The green squares/circles on the floor plan are bar-height tables, and the orange squares are regular-height tables.
Most of our seats are sold as “Tables for Two” – meaning the ticket admits TWO people.
We have a limited number of single seats available, and single attendees may be sharing a table with other single attendees.
Multiple tickets purchased under the same name will automatically be seated together (up to 6 people).
Requesting a specific table is a perk reserved for our VIP Patron members. If you’re interested in supporting our mission, you can find membership information HERE.
If anyone in your party needs special seating arrangements – walker, cane, service animal, wheelchair, low table requirement, or you want to be seated with another party – please email us after you place your order, and we’ll make it happen.
All tickets are “WILL CALL” – no need to print anything out, just give your name at the door.
 Back to Index 
 I noticed your shows are “listening room style”. What does that mean? Can’t I talk to my friends during the show? 
At the 443 our focus is squarely on the music.
That means attendees should limit conversation and give their attention to the performer on stage. If you are chatting and it becomes disruptive to the performer and other guests, you will be asked to keep your voice down.
Yes, we will SHUSH you.
We understand you’re excited to see your friends, but conversing loudly during the show is disrespectful to the performer and the people around you who paid to see and hear it. Remember – nobody bought a ticket to listen to your chatter.
If you continue to disrupt the performance and/or become abusive to the staff member who politely asked you to lower your voice, you will be escorted out, and your ticket will not be refunded.
 Back to Index 
 My favorite band is coming to the 443, I’m so excited! Can I buy a big block of seats for all my friends so we can all sit together? 
Mmmm… this is a sticky one for us.
We spend a lot of time and energy working to sell tickets because if we can’t put butts in the seats, nothing else we do matters. It’s tempting to encourage people to buy as many tickets as possible, but we’ve learned the hard way that it causes a new set of problems.
Our shows are “listening room style”, which means you’re not allowed to talk during the performance. We know it’s tough not to talk when you’re with a big group of friends, so we’re just not the venue for that kind of gathering.
We also have limitations on how many food orders we can pump out at the same time in our tiny food prep space behind the bar. We simply are not able to deliver more than 6 orders to the same table at the same time while everything is still hot.
We have a cap on the number of tickets you can purchase on EventBrite. You can buy up to 3 tables of two for our tiered ticket shows or 6 GA tickets for local bands. You can work around it by buying them in multiple transactions, but we will not seat more than 6 people at one table.
We understand that you might be disappointed that your group of 8 or 10 cannot sit at the same table. We strive to say “YES” as often as possible, but this is one of those situations where we have to consider what’s best for the entire room. It doesn’t make sense to make the other tables in the room have their 443 experience disrupted by a large, chatty table, and it’s not fair for them to wait longer for their food because we’re trying to accommodate a larger group.
We appreciate your understanding.
 Back to Index 
 What is your capacity? 
Our official capacity is 88 indoors but we can only seat up to 75 people.
 Back to Index 
 I see you have a $15 required minimum purchase for your shows. What if I don’t want to order anything? 
We appreciate the enthusiastic presence of everyone who buys a ticket or pays a cover charge to attend our shows. But we keep very little (if any) of that money – the majority goes to the artist gracing our stage. We keep ourselves in business by selling food and drink.
We do not allow guests to bring in food or drink of any kind. 
If you’re not a drinker, we have a mocktail menu and 5 kinds of N/A beer. If you truly don’t want anything at all, we will present you with a bill for $15. 
 Back to Index 
 I’m taking break from alcohol right now – do you have any NA options? 
We sure do!
We carry a variety of NA beers plus a full mocktail menu, coffee, tea, and assorted bottled options. You can find the full menu HERE.
PLEASE NOTE: 
Some of our mocktails contain flavored bitters containing trace amounts of alcohol. 
If you prefer a 100% alcohol-free beverage, avoid those drinks or ask your server to leave out the bitters. 
 Back to Index 
 Do you offer table service? 
We do! Our servers will visit your table to take your order so you can relax and enjoy the show.
 Back to Index 
 We’re coming in with a group of 6 for a show. You can give us separate customized checks, right? 
We strive to say “YES” to our guests as often as possible, but we are just not able to manage convoluted split-check arrangements for larger parties without negatively impacting everyone else in the room.
If you have 5 people or more in your group, please have a plan when you arrive, let your server know BEFORE you order if you want anything other than a single check, and learn how to use Venmo, PayPal, or CashApp.
 Back to Index 
 What time are your shows? 
Our doors open at 5:30pm and showtime is 7pm for Monday – Saturday performances. Sunday shows vary, and sometimes we have afternoon matinees on the weekend.
Our musical contracts require one 90-minute set plus a possible encore or two 45-50 minute sets with a break. That said, it’s ultimately up to the artist how long they will play for.
Please make every effort to arrive well before the band begins. It’s distracting to other guests and difficult for our staff to take your order once the music starts.

443 Music Collective 
MISSION:
The 443 Music Collective is a 501c3 non-profit organization producing extraordinary music events, offering education opportunities, and creating powerful up-close and personal live experiences.
We are a registered public charity on the New York Public Charities Registry. 
TAX ID #: 39-2805107
We are dedicated to curating unique musical experiences, offering workshops from world-class touring musicians, and bringing our community together in a creative and supportive environment.
The Collective operates in conjunction with the 443 Social Club, a 75-seat listening room-style venue in Syracuse, NY. Our focus at the club is on original, touring artists. We provide space for the powerful magic that happens when talented artists and storytellers meet an attentive and enthusiastic audience.
We don’t ask our artists to compete with TVs, Quick Draw, or pool tables, and we encourage our guests to put down their phones, refrain from talking, and be present in the moment. In an increasingly “connected” online world, we are more DIS-connected than ever from our fellow humans. At the 443, we invite our guests to connect on a deeper level with the artist on stage and the other attendees sharing the experience.
Here’s the catch –
The traditional business model of music venues is almost 100% dependent on alcohol sales – and that model is collapsing as we speak.
The culture around alcohol consumption has changed drastically over the last few years… younger people do not drink the way older generations did, and older generations are more aware of the mental and physical health risks associated with alcohol use. They have reduced their consumption drastically or quit imbibing altogether. Weed is legal and perceived as a healthier buzz. Alcohol sales are cratering at venues all over the country, and venue owners are sounding the alarm.
Unless we want to see independent music venues disappear completely, we need to create a new business model. 
THIS IS NOT A DRILL. 
For some reason – probably because of their close association with alcohol – music venues don’t seem to be in the same category as public libraries, museums, theaters, sports stadiums, and parks. All of these places are publicly funded, but in general, music venues are not.
Why?
Independent music venues contribute to the local economy, YES. Even on the micro level of our small venue, on any given night, we see guests from other counties in NY, other states, and Canada. These people stay in hotels, eat at local restaurants, and spend money locally. Music tourism is a real thing. We also support a dozen other local businesses.
But it’s more than that.
We are not just a tourism driver. We are a community gathering space, a place to connect with other music fans, make new friends, and meet new partners.
We are the places where stories happen, the ones you tell for years –
“You should have been there!!” 
We give our communities a place to experience the profound and powerful connection of music and storytelling.
We are the destinations that help give a city or neighborhood its cool factor, that make it a place where talented and engaged people want to live and work. There is no rich arts and entertainment culture without a vibrant music scene.
And – maybe most importantly – music helps to heals our souls when things get heavy, and gives us an escape from the ugly stuff happening outside our doors every single day.
Isn’t that worth supporting?
The 443 is following in the footsteps of other great listening rooms in the Northeast, like Caffe Lena, Club Passim, and The Listening Booth, and turning to a not-for-profit model to keep the music alive.
DONATE
Here at the 443 Music Collective, we are dedicated to curating unique musical experiences, offering workshops from world-class touring musicians, and bringing our community together in a supportive and creative environment.
How you can help power our mission:
Donate: Every dollar helps.
Share: Share our campaign with your friends, family, and on social media.
Volunteer: Your time and skills can make an impact, too.
We appreciate your support, it means the world to us!
