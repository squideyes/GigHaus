The Grass Roots Cultural and Performing Arts Center
The GRCPAC is a registered Massachusetts 501(c)(3) Non-Profit organization on a mission is to preserve and promote live music by operating an inclusive performing arts center that offers diverse musical experiences to both local and online communities.
Our Mission
Our mission is to preserve and promote live music by operating an inclusive performing arts center that offers diverse musical experiences to both local and online communities.

Community

The Fallout Shelter
