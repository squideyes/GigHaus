 Onstage at the ZACC
The Show Room at the ZACC is a performance venue that hosts live music, comedy, theatre, film screenings, dance performances, community gatherings and more. Throughout the year, we welcome local and regional artists alongside nationally touring acts, offering an ever-changing lineup of performances and experiences.

 INDIGENIZING THE ZACC 2026 - FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 6
Explore artwork by Indigenous artists, shop from Indigenous makers, enjoy live music and food, and come together in recognition of the strength, resilience, and continued presence of Indigenous communities. The evening begins with an MMIR Awareness Walk and smudging at 4:15 PM, starting at the library, followed by festivities at the ZACC from 5–9 PM.   Featured Artwork: Rebirth from the Flames by Lauryn Gordon (Blackfeet) This year's featured poster artwork, Rebirth from the Flames, by Lauryn Gordon, reflects the themes of resistance, resilience, and renewal through the imagery of a butterfly dancer rising like a phoenix. Inspired by the phoenix's symbolism of rebirth and transformation, Gordon's work depicts a dancer whose shawl becomes wings of flame, representing the strength to rise, overcome restrictions, and emerge renewed.

About Us
The Zootown Arts Community Center is Missoula’s nonprofit arts center, dedicated to supporting local and regional visual artists, performers, musicians, educators, and creative individuals of all backgrounds. 
We strive to foster an open, transparent, and welcoming environment where creativity can flourish. Through accessible classes, exhibitions, performances, studio spaces, and community programming, we provide opportunities for people to create, collaborate, learn, and share their work. Above all, we are committed to being a safe, inclusive, and inspiring space where community members and visitors alike feel empowered to connect, express themselves, and thrive through the arts.

We provide the same protections for these electronic communications that we employ in the maintenance of information received by mail and telephone.

Similar to other websites, our site may utilize a standard technology called "cookies" (see explanation below, "What Are Cookies?") and web server logs to collect information about how our website is used. Information gathered through cookies and server logs may include the date and time of visits, the pages viewed, time spent at our website, and the sites visited just before and just after ours. This information is collected on an aggregate basis. None of this information is associated with you as an individual.
How Do We Use the Information That You Provide to Us?
Broadly speaking, we use personal information for purposes of administering our business activities, providing service and support and making available other products and services to our customers and prospective customers. You may at any time to notify us of your desire not to receive these offers.
What Are Cookies?
Cookies are a feature of web browser software that allows web servers to recognize the computer used to access a website. Cookies are small pieces of data that are stored by a user's web browser on the user's hard drive. Cookies can remember what information a user accesses on one web page to simplify subsequent interactions with that website by the same user or to use the information to streamline the user's transactions on related web pages. This makes it easier for a user to move from web page to web page and to complete commercial transactions over the Internet. Cookies should make your online experience easier and more personalized.
How Do We Use Information Collected From Cookies?
We use website browser software tools such as cookies and web server logs to gather information about our website users' browsing activities, in order to constantly improve our website and better serve our users. This information assists us to design and arrange our web pages in the most user-friendly manner and to continually improve our website to better meet the needs of our users and prospective users.
Cookies help us collect important business and technical statistics. The information in the cookies lets us trace the paths followed by users to our website as they move from one page to another. Web server logs allow us to count how many people visit our website and evaluate our website's visitor capacity. We do not use these technologies to capture your individual email address or any personally identifying information about you.

AN ARTS CENTER FOR EVERYONE
Inclusivity and accessibility are paramount to the ZACC's mission: we are continually working to become an arts center that is truly within reach for all. 
ZACC spaces are wheelchair-friendly:
An elevator provides access to classrooms, galleries, and studios.
A ramp provides access for performers on the ZACC stage.
Wheelchair-accessible bathrooms are located on every floor and include:
A gender-neutral individual bathroom
Two Family-friendly restrooms with baby-changing tables
Service animals are always welcome in the ZACC!
On-street accessible parking is available in front of the ZACC, including one designated accessible spot with loading room on the passenger side. 
We are working to improve:
The ZACC newsletter and website: Please keep an eye out for improved features in the near future.
The Show Room and Galleries: The ZACC is working to create a truly inclusive place for those who may experience difficulty seeing or participating in ZACC shows, and we hope to provide complimentary auditory experiences in the future. The ZACC is actively evaluating best practices for future events.
We would love to learn more about making ZACC more accessible to you. If you would like additional assistance or information, please reach out via email or call (406) 549-7555.

About Us
The Zootown Arts Community Center is Missoula’s nonprofit arts center, dedicated to supporting local and regional visual artists, performers, musicians, educators, and creative individuals of all backgrounds. 
We strive to foster an open, transparent, and welcoming environment where creativity can flourish. Through accessible classes, exhibitions, performances, studio spaces, and community programming, we provide opportunities for people to create, collaborate, learn, and share their work. Above all, we are committed to being a safe, inclusive, and inspiring space where community members and visitors alike feel empowered to connect, express themselves, and thrive through the arts.

ZESTMarch 27, 2026 2027 Date TBA
ZEST is the Zootown Arts Community Center's signature annual fundraiser, a one-of-a-kind celebration of art, music, performance, and community. Each spring, the ZACC transforms into an immersive festival filled with live music, comedy, interactive art experiences, installations, local food and drinks, and the beloved Mini Show Art Auction, featuring hundreds of original works by regional artists. More than a fundraising event, ZEST is a celebration of Missoula's creative community, bringing together artists, supporters, and local businesses to champion accessible arts programming. Proceeds directly support the ZACC's galleries, Show Room, education programs, opportunities for emerging artists, and initiatives that expand access to the arts throughout Missoula and beyond. 
Last Best Print FestJuly 2026 
Last Best Print Fest is the Zootown Arts Community Center's annual celebration of printmaking, bringing together artists, students, and community members to explore the many possibilities of the printed image. Centered around the ZACC's Laura Grace Barrett Print Shop, the month-long festival features a curated exhibition, portfolio exchange, print auction, hands-on workshops, live demonstrations, artist talks, and community art projects. Each year, a featured artist and unique theme inspire new work from regional and national printmakers, showcasing a wide range of printmaking techniques and creative approaches. Since its founding in 2014, Last Best Print Fest has become a cornerstone of Montana's printmaking community, fostering collaboration, education, and creative exchange while supporting the continued accessibility of the ZACC Print Shop. 
Missoula Monster ProjectOctober 2026
The Missoula Monster Project is one of the Zootown Arts Community Center's most beloved annual community art events, created in partnership with the Missoula County Public Schools Fine Arts Department. Each year, local kindergarten students dream up and draw imaginative monsters, which are then paired with adult artists who bring those creations to life in their own unique artistic styles and mediums. The original drawings and finished artworks are displayed side by side in a month-long exhibition at the ZACC, celebrating creativity, collaboration, and the power of imagination across generations. The project culminates in a family-friendly opening celebration and serves as a fundraiser supporting arts education and providing much-needed art supplies for both MCPS and ZACC classrooms. 
Indigenizing the ZACCNovember 2026
Indigenizing the ZACC is an annual celebration of Indigenous art, culture, and community at the Zootown Arts Community Center. Through exhibitions, artist talks, workshops, and community gatherings, this event highlights the creativity, knowledge, and voices of Indigenous artists while creating opportunities for learning, connection, and cultural exchange. Each year, the program brings together artists community members to honor the rich traditions and expressions of Indigenous creativity, reinforcing the ZACC's ongoing commitment to fostering an inclusive and welcoming arts community.
