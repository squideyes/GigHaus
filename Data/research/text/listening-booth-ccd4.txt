We’re Listening isn’t just a 501(c)(3) organization; it’s a cultural experiment aimed at addressing one of the most insidious problems of our time: the loneliness epidemic. In an era where everyone is supposedly “connected,” more people than ever feel like they’re floating through life without an anchor. Enter We’re Listening, a nonprofit dedicated to combating this emotional isolation through the arts—specifically, original music, songwriting, and storytelling.
Operating under the name The Listening Booth, this isn’t just another venue at the Delaware beaches. It’s a place where melodies become a shared experience, where lyrics act as a salve for collective solitude, and where people aren’t just entertained—they’re seen. We’re Listening doesn’t simply aim to entertain; it seeks to remind us all that we’re part of something bigger, using the universal language of music to cut through the noise of modern disconnection.
“I think people who truly can live a life in music are telling the world, ‘You can have my love, you can have my smiles. Forget the bad parts, you don’t need them. Just take the music, the goodness, because it’s the very best, and it’s the part I give most willingly”
― George Harrison

MEET ME AT THE BOOTH
The Listening Booth isn't just a venue; it's a sanctuary for those who believe music is more than background noise. Nestled in Lewes, Delaware, this space is where melodies meet meaning, and every note tells a story.
​
Founded by Marissa Levy—a singer-songwriter who traded Brooklyn's bustle for Lewes' laid-back charm—the Booth emerged from a desire to fill the void in the local original music scene. Marissa's journey from sharing stages with Matt Nathanson and Lisa Loeb to creating a haven for artists and audiences alike is a testament to her commitment to authentic musical expression.
​
At The Listening Booth, the focus is on original music that resonates. It's a place where artists can share their narratives, and listeners can find a piece of themselves in the songs. With a capacity of about 100 seats, the venue offers an intimate setting that fosters genuine connections between performers and the audience.
​
The Booth's calendar is a curated mix of acoustic open mic nights, songwriting workshops, and performances by both emerging talents and seasoned musicians. It's a space where the community comes together to celebrate the art of storytelling through music.
​
For those looking to be part of this musical journey, The Listening Booth offers opportunities to support its mission. Whether through sponsorships, donations, or simply attending a show, every contribution helps keep the music playing and the community thriving.
​
In a world where music often feels commodified, The Listening Booth stands as a reminder of its true essence—a shared experience that connects us all.
THE BAND
We are a team of badass women (and Nate) looking to bring a world of awesome music to Southern Delaware
Marissa Levy
Owner & Chief Roadie
Marissa Levy is the kind of singer-songwriter who bridges the gap between introspection and outright relatability—a musical alchemist turning her own experiences into songs that feel like they’ve always belonged to you. Hailing from Lewes, Delaware (by way of the concrete jungle of NYU), her career has unfolded alongside artists like Matt Nathanson, Gavin DeGraw, Lisa Loeb, and Ron Pope—basically, the folks you turn to when you need to feel all your feelings.
​
With four albums to her name, Marissa’s catalog is equal parts sharp wit and tender reflection. But she’s not just about her own story; she’s about all the stories. Her passion for original music extends beyond her guitar strings. That’s why she’s embarking on her boldest venture yet: The Listening Booth, a music venue she’s opening in early 2023 in her hometown of Lewes. It’s less about her and more about creating a space where songs—real, raw, unfiltered—can take on a life of their own.
Victoria Rioux
Head of Hustle
Victoria is our Head of Hustle, currently living in Milton Delaware, hailing from Upstate New York originally. She has over 25 years of experience within the food industry and customer service. With a keen ear and earnest heart for singer songwriter artists and the like, her joining The Listening Booth seemed nothing short of serendipitous. 
After meeting our Chief Roadie Marissa, at the infamous open mic hosted by Tyler Greene, it was clear the path of friendship ahead included business endeavors with the heart and soul for original music. Thus began her journey with us here at TLB.
Nate McCormick
Director of Noise
Nate McCormick is a performing guitarist, with almost 2 decades of experience on stage and playing - either solo, or in various bands and groups. With years of one-on-one and classroom teaching experience, Nate is able to cover a wide variety of guitar subjects suitable for players of most styles and proficiency levels. His performance experience will provide you with all the tools you need to get out and get playing! Now available for guitar and bass instruction.
​
Nate wants his students enthusiastic and curious - music is a language! The best way to learn is to immerse yourself in habits and a lifestyle that surrounds making great music, and learning every day. He wants you to be the best player you want to be.
Chana Fitzkee
Head of Harmony
Chana Fitzkee is all about great music and awesome humans! Known as "Team Fun" when on tour, Chana brings the energy and positive vibes wherever she goes. With a proven track record as a talent buyer, she's a pro at discovering talent and building brands. Since 2018, Chana has brought numerous notable acts to the state of Delaware, including Duane Betts, Daniel Donato, Bruce Katz, Crack The Sky & John Ford Coley, . As a booking agent, she matches artists with venues, using her unique perspective to understand both sides of the stage, some of her clients include Melody Trucks, Bobby Lee Rodgers, Jackson Stokes, The Fitzkee Brothers, Gordon Sterling & The People, Jonathan Sloan & Tony Tyler to name a few.
She frequently tours and books for nationally touring musicians, and her passion lies in creating connections between great musicians and great venues. The proud mother of "The Fitzkee Brothers," Chana has raised two successful touring musicians, booked countless acts, and even partnered in a venue. She’s deeply immersed in the world of live music, always on the lookout for the next exciting collaboration.
Good vibes. Great people. Unforgettable music. That's Chana!

Making the world less lonely through music, community and storytelling
WE RE LISTENING, INC IS A 501(C)3 PUBLIC CHARITY. TAX ID: 92-3245964
We're Listening, A 501(c)(3) nonprofit public charity located in the beautiful Delaware beaches is committed to building up a musical community full of talented musicians and enthusiastic audiences and cultivating a once thriving original music scene in Southern Delaware by providing unique events, classes and workshops for musicians of all abilities and listeners of all genres. We believe in combatting the national loneliness epidemic through bringing people together in community to make music and lasting friendships. Offering opportunities for musicians and songwriters under our venue name, The Listening Booth, We’re Listening is bringing a whole new experience to the Delaware beaches.​​
We are committed to:
ORIGINAL MUSIC. We want to hear your story. We believe that telling your story through music is one of the greatest forms of self expression. We believe there is no better way to express your feelings and make connections with audiences than singing your own words and playing your own music.
SHARED AND TRANSFORMATIVE EXPERIENCES. Developing and promoting unique experiences that help people of all ages discover the arts, tap into their creative potential, and share their passions and talents with new audiences. We combat the loneliness epidemic plaguing our country by gathering in community to sing, play and share our songs to heal and move forward.
EDUCATION. Everyone can learn to play, sing, write and it is never too late to do so. Through our private lessons, group lesson, adult rock band camp, sing-a-longs, open mic nights, song circles, jam sessions and myriad other educational events we promote musical education in a fun and digestible manner. We believe it is never too late to start and never too late to start over. We believe in the healing power of music for what it does to the brain and the body.

Meet Our Advisory Board
Meet the dedicated members of our advisory board, who are instrumental in raising funds to ensure We're Listening continues to thrive. Their expertise and commitment play a vital role in supporting our mission, and we are grateful for their invaluable contributions. Together, we are making a difference and fostering a community where every voice is heard.​
Neil Ditchek
Neil Ditchek brings a wealth of experience in leadership and advocacy to The Listening Booth. Before joining, he retired as Assistant and Special Counsel to the General President of the Teamsters Union. With a deep commitment to community and the arts, Neil has served on the boards of numerous non-profit organizations. He currently resides part-time in Millsboro, Delaware, and is passionate about supporting live music and fostering a vibrant creative space.
Mike Miller
Mike Miller is a singer-songwriter from central Delaware and leader of the band, “Mother’s Highway Junction.” Professionally, he is a retired mental health therapist, a field he entered after working as a cultural historian in South Dakota, Washington, D.C., and Delaware. His volunteer work has included serving on the boards of the Mid-Atlantic Folklife Association, the Delaware Humanities Council, and the Delaware Friends of Folk.
Rosalyn Matz
Rosalyn (Roz) Matz “walks the walk, talks the talk, and sings the songs” of living “The Booth Life,” engaging in a variety of musical and community activities at The Listening Booth, including Open Mic Night, Sunday Social Open Jam Night, guitar lessons, the inaugural “Rock Camp for Real — Adult Band Camp,” and attending ticketed concerts. A lifelong musician with over 50 years of experience in singing, songwriting, guitar playing, and song-leading, Roz has also pursued a wide range of professional paths, most recently as a researcher, analyst, and journalist covering high-growth, cutting-edge technology companies for a DIY investor newsletter. Now “tickled pink” (or “tickled orange”) to serve on the “We’re Listening” Advisory Board, she is dedicated to contributing to the success and sustainability of this vibrant musical community.
Bob Piemonte
Bob Piemonte grew up in the Chicago suburbs with a deep love for music that fueled his passion as a lifelong concertgoer and collector of vinyl, 8-tracks, cassettes, and CDs. Professionally, he works for a company specializing in mitigating RF and acoustic energy, giving him an insider’s view of the science behind modern communication and sound. Outside of work, Bob has explored RC car racing and, more recently, developed a passion for building and setting up guitars, combining his love of music with craftsmanship. His life reflects a continuous pursuit of creativity, learning, and the beauty of well-crafted instruments.

Corporate Partnerships
Corporate partnerships offer a strategic opportunity to make a meaningful impact in the community while expanding your brand’s visibility. At The Listening Booth/We're Listening, we believe in the power of collaboration to forge connections and amplify voices. Partnering with us allows you to contribute to a mission rooted in inclusivity and creativity through the art of listening, all while broadening your reach.
We're Listening offers customizable corporate sponsorship packages to suit your company’s marketing, client engagement, and hospitality goals. Our approach is both creative and collaborative, ensuring our partnerships deliver impactful results.
Whether you're interested in promoting your product through onsite activation and sampling, hosting clients or prospects at a pavilion concert, rewarding employees with show tickets, or gaining exposure to our 350,000+ annual visitors, our packages are designed to meet your unique objectives. We'll work closely with you to create an alignment that enhances your brand and builds deeper customer connections.
To explore partnership opportunities, please contact Marissa Lerer for more information.

About The Event
Grammy nominated singer-songwriter Chris Trapper has traveled the world over, performing to a dedicated and ever growing fan base with nothing but his guitar and his songs. Raised on Prine and Kristofferson, Trapper's first foray in the music industry was as frontman of the critically acclaimed alt-rock band The Push Stars (Capitol Records). Over the past decade, Chris has become a modern day acoustic troubadour, performing over 150 dates a year as a headliner and sharing the stage with the likes of Rob Thomas, Pat Benatar and even John Prine himself.
Chris’s newest record “Watching Sparks Fly By” is the 12th full length solo album by Grammy nominated singer-songwriter Chris Trapper. The Boston based troubadour employed multiple producers to bring 13 brand new tracks to life (plus 3 bonus tracks including a studio version of the fan favorite “Keg On My Coffin”). About The Event
Founded in Washington DC in 2008 by singer/songwriter Justin Trawick, “The 9 Songwriter Series” is a touring collective and live show featuring nine acts performing together for one evening of songs and collaboration. In an industry where artists are often working independently, “The 9” brings performers together, often whom have never met, to combine their talents and perform to large crowds around the country. Each installment features an ensemble of nine acts performing in a unique and captivating format, telling stories, and sitting in on each other’s songs with little to no rehearsal for a community experience that can’t be duplicated or emulated. To date, over 500 different artists have participated in “The 9” at shows in DC, Baltimore, Annapolis, Philadelphia, New York, Fairfield, Boston, Richmond, Charlottesville, Charlotte, New Orleans, Atlanta, Los Angeles and San Francisco.
