Open Mic 
Welcome to Passim’s open mic series! We’re hosting an open mic, and you’re invited. Each show will feature 2 hours of performers, from 7pm-9pm Eastern. While we’re predominately a music open mic, we also welcome poetry, storytelling, and comedy. All ages welcome!
Upcoming open mics:
Tuesday, October 27th, 7pm – sign up in advance here or sign up starting at 6pm at the door
Monday, November 23rd, 7pm – sign up in advance here or sign up starting at 6pm at the door
No further open mics are currently scheduled. Stay tuned to this page for updates.
How it works:
Each performer gets 2 songs or 10 minutes, whichever comes first. Covers are allowed, but originals are encouraged. Vocal mics, instrument mics, cables, DIs, and a Korg keyboard will be available for your use on stage. You will have a short line check with the sound person immediately before your set. This is an in-person performance at Club Passim in Cambridge, MA. Show starts at 7pm ET. All in-person attendees (both performers & audience) will need to have a ticket. Tickets are $10 for the public or free for Passim members. You can purchase tickets at passim.org/live or at the box office the night of the show.
Performers are only allowed to perform once per month to allow for more participants. No-shows will not be allowed to sign up for future open mics. If you have extenuating circumstances or need to change your performance time, please email us ahead of time so we can plan.
Any questions? Email Abby at abby@passim.org.

Festivals & Summer Series 
Club Passim has a strong presence in the Cambridge community throughout the year, often collaborating with the Harvard Square Business Association, Cambridge Arts Council, and other local businesses. In January, Club Passim hosts its annual BCMFest, which celebrates local Celtic music artists.
