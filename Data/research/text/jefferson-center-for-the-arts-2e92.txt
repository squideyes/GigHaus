Jefferson Center for the Arts (JCA)Mount Shasta's Premier Nonprofit Educational Center for Music and the Performing Arts!
 JCA is Mount Shasta's premier nonprofit arts education venue incorporating the natural beauty of the area for the purpose of improving social, economic and cultural assets.
 The JCA is a regional hub for Live Music, Theatre and Visual Arts for local residents and tourists alike.
 Jefferson Center for the Arts is Mount Shasta's own community arts and culture center.

Music at JCA
 Jefferson Center for the Arts hosts an eclectic mix of musical genres including…
 Classical, Jazz, Americana, Blues, Folk and other acoustic musical styles.
 With only 60 seats, the JCA brings you up close and personal to the music.
 The community will often be able to meet the artists in a workshop setting or Q&A before/after each show.
 Our programming caters to you… the community. 
 So, please drop us a line with your suggestions!
