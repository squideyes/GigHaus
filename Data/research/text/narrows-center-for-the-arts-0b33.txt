ABOUT US
The Narrows Center for the Arts was founded in 1995 for the promotion and enjoyment of the visual and performing arts. The Narrows, a fully accessible venue, is comprised of two visual art galleries, a performance theater, visual artist studios, and rental space for events, classes, and meetings.
FROM HUMBLE BEGINNINGS
The Narrows Center for the Arts started as a small gallery exhibition space devoted to local artists and craftsmen with an open mic for local musicians. The initial location, where the North and South Watuppa Ponds come together, was near a waterway the locals refer to as the Narrows.
When a development project took over the initial space, the property owner offered rental space on the third floor of the former American Printing Company (APC) mill building on Anawan Street on the historic Fall River Massachusetts waterfront, our current home. In the early 20th century, APC was one of the largest textile operations in the world. 
Thanks to generous community support and strong stewardship, the Narrows Center grew from 1,000 to 15,000 square feet and with the help of dedicated volunteers the 3rd floor space was redesigned. It took almost four years to complete the transformation as we embraced the elegant architectural design, high ceilings and spectacular views of Mount Hope Bay and Battleship Cove. The 20-foot ceilings provide the ideal acoustic and visual backdrop for what has come to be known as a regional destination for the visual and performing arts. In 2017, the Narrows purchased our gorgeous mill building and tripled the area in which we present live music, visual art, artists’ studios, classroom instruction, and arts-related businesses. 
The Narrows is a multi-disciplinary arts center that houses a 430-seat performance area, two art galleries, 11 artist studios, 2 multi-function spaces and a craft brewery. For the last 23 years, the Narrows Center has put Fall River on the map as a cultural destination. We have hosted over 2,300 concerts and over 250 art shows. We are an integral part of the Fall River community. A Boston Globe article had this to say about us: “The Narrows Center for the Arts is one of the best repurposing’s of one of the sprawling mills that for decades made Fall River hum. With high ceilings, towering windows that offer spectacular views of Battleship Cove and glorious sunsets, and great acoustics, The Narrows since 2001 has been a premiere destination for live music in the region.”
The Narrows is an economic engine and a tourist destination for the City of Fall River. In 2023, 35,000 people visited the Narrows Center – 80% of those visitors came from outside a 20-mile radius of the city. Many of these visitors frequented restaurants, hotels and other service-related industries.
The Narrows Center embraces every opportunity for arts education and has risen to the challenge of providing a cultural education option for our community.
Since 2021, the Narrows has presented “Summer Evenings in the Park” an eight-week program that goes into local neighborhood parks for a free family-centric event. These events include live music, multiple arts and crafts stations, free hot dogs, hamburgers and ice cream as well as a bag of groceries for all families. The Narrows partnered with the City of Fall River and other local non-profits to provide a high-quality evening for some of the most economically challenged in our community.
In 2023, the Narrows in partnership with the City of Fall River and the Fall River Arts and Cultural Coalition formed South Coast Arts Lab, an after-school arts and music program for 7th and 8th graders. This program is now recognized as a regional model for after-school programming.
We also partner with VetTix, a non-profit that provides free concert tickets to veterans. In 2023 over 500 active military and retired veterans attended a Narrows’ concert for Free.
The Narrows Center is an organization that the local people can be proud of as we continue to bring positive attention to this city. We are Music that Matters, Art that Inspires and a steadfast Commitment to Community.
The Narrows Center is a 501(c)(3) non-profit charitable organization continuing to present exciting ways to engage the public, host one-of-a-kind musical guests and showcase unique works of art. Offering educational arts programming, local school tours, free gallery shows and free Spindle City Festivals, the Narrows proudly presents an ever-growing access and fellowship in the arts for both local guests and those travelling to visit us.
MISSION
Our mission at the Narrows Center for the Arts is to present music that matters, art that inspires, and commitment to community, to engage and educate people of all ages in Fall River and the southern New England area, and to partner with other like-minded local and regional organizations as a hub for culture and the arts.
FOOD & DRINK
Attendees are welcome to bring their own food, beverages, and alcohol to all events in bags and small coolers that will fit under your chair. We sell fresh brewed Coastal Roasters coffee at our performances, along with teas, soda, bottled water, and pre-packaged snacks. On many nights we also serve freshly baked desserts.
BEHAVIOR
The Narrows provides a fun, relaxed and welcoming environment for performer and audience alike where musicians can connect to those who appreciate their work. Rowdies or those who detract from the ability of those around them to enjoy the show will be asked to leave.
PARKING
There is on-street parking available on Anawan Street (our side) and a fenced-in parking lot behind our building. You can also park in the back of our building alongside the parking lot fencing. There is some parking on Water Street which is not metered, but please take notice to posted parking restrictions.

The founding mission of the Narrows Center for the Arts was to promote and develop an appreciation of the visual and the performing arts. Artist studios are one component of our ever-growing and successful cultural arts center.
Over the years several artists have used studio workspace at the Narrows to create their artwork in the company of like-minded artists. An open studio door is a welcome invitation to all the arts enthusiasts who attend live performances and/or visit our galleries to view exhibitions.
Current Studio Artists:
Abigail Parsons
Abigail Parsons is a multidisciplinary artist living in Providence, RI. Primarily working in painting and drawing, her work explores humor, absurdity, and moments of failure. Abigail received her MFA from the Rhode Island School of Design and her BA from the College of the Holy Cross. She teaches at the Rhode Island School of Design in the Continuing Education program.
Ashley Pelletier
Ashley Pelletier is a painter living and working in the Boston and Providence areas. Her work is about play, humor, and the formal issues specific to oil painting. Ashley received her MFA from the Massachusetts College of Art and Design and her BFA from Rhode Island College.
Her work has been exhibited at the Bristol Art Museum (Bristol, RI), Abigail Ogilvy Gallery (Boston, MA), Rhode Island College (Providence, RI), and other galleries throughout the Northeast. She is a 2024 recipient of a ‘Make Art Grant’ from the Rhode Island State Council on the Arts. Ashley currently teaches at the Massachusetts College of Art and Design and the Community College of Rhode Island.
Gracen Viveiros
Gracen Viveiros is a Fall River artist who uses portraits as a pathway to spiritual healing and growth. Through sharing their work with the world, Gracen hopes to encourage others to recognize the beauty and possibility within themselves. Each painting is created as a source of inspiration, reminding viewers that they have the power to create, to dream, and to live the life they truly desire and confidently. In a world that often teaches people to doubt their potential, Gracen’s art serves as a gentle reminder that everyone is worthy of their dreams and capable of achieving them.
Contact: PortraitArtbygv@gmail.com
Jules - Heron Pond Studio
Jules at Heron Pond Studio has been a Printmaker for most of their years and has added Intuitive Painting to their repertoire in the past six years.
“I love listening to the story as it develops when I am drawing an idea, making material choices, and feeling the connection between the lines and the motion as an image is carved out of wood or linoleum. The final gesture of the ink transferring from the printing plate to paper brings me the joy and revelation that keep me working in the creative arts. The surprises discovered through works in Intuitive Painting has added to my creative joy, and in 2026 the project will be to combine the printing and painting together and watch the images come together with greater life and depth of story.” 
Contact: juleshps@icloud.com
Kris Samuelson - Heron Pond Studio
Kris Samuelson at Heron Pond Studio is a Photographer, Woodworker, Carpenter and Glass Artist. Now exploring the world of Mosaics.

Kayla Mateus
Kayla Mateus is a Fall River native and multidisciplinary artist whose work is deeply rooted in nature, motherhood, and faith. Largely self-taught, Kayla also studied fine art at Bristol Community College, where she further developed her artistic foundation and love for creative exploration.
Working across a range of mediums, Kayla’s art is known for its organic textures, thoughtful symbolism, and heartfelt storytelling. Her creative practice spans commissioned fine art, large-scale murals, and most recently, custom breastmilk jewelry—intimate keepsakes that honor motherhood and life’s most meaningful moments.
Kayla’s work has been featured in GreenPrints magazine and can be found in both public and private spaces throughout the South Coast. Through her studio practice, she aims to create art that is not only visually engaging, but emotionally resonant—pieces that invite connection, reflection, and a sense of grounding.
Kevin Lamoureux
Kevin”Lamb” Lamoureux is an artist based out of Providence, RI. Using a primary medium of spray paint, Kevin started painting as a release from the work that he was bringing home with him as a Social Worker with incarcerated youth. Developing his own style and showing his art at a number of different venues over the last few years, he has also started to offer spray paint classes to youth from a number of different organizations and locations (high schools, elementary schools, girl scout troops, group homes, and residential treatment facilities among others).
He has continued his education as well, earning a masters of social work and a school administration certification. He serves as an Assistant Principal at a local Middle School by day, and continues to pursue his passion for art at night.
Watch Kevin’s spotlight video here!
Instagram: @baaaalamb
Marquelynn Raposa
Encaustic Artist
Creation begins with a memory, an impactful moment, or a found object that tells a story. The layers of wax are applied, altered, and built so that the piece takes on its’ own reason for being.
When I begin a piece I may start with a found object I want to build a story around, or a memory of a time or place I wish to preserve by melding images together. I progress at the beginning by building my surfaces with a plan in mind but through the process I allow surprises to create new directions and in the end the art work tells its’ own story.

Beautiful Space for Any Event
We invite you to consider the Narrows Center as the ideal venue for your next special occasion! 
Our gorgeous mill building, with its elegant architectural design and views of Mount Hope Bay; can offer a distinctive atmosphere for corporate events, private parties, and fundraisers of any kind. With two beautifully restored levels of space to choose from, you will find flexibility to satisfy all of your event’s needs. 
The Narrows Center can accommodate up to 430 participants in multiple spaces, including our performance space, galleries, or the entire facility. Our staff will work to find the right space for your needs, be it a one-time event or an ongoing program.
There is nothing typical about an event at the Narrows!
Flexible spaces – rent a room, a floor, or the entire facility. We have multiple spaces that can accomodate 80, 300, and 430 people 
Unique Space – High ceilings, large windows, exposed brick walls, and polished floors create an artsy vibe, ensuring your event will stand out
Professional-grade sound system and lighting in performance space, while the second-floor space also features a high-end sound system
Easy highway access from Massachusetts and Rhode Island
Free Parking
Accessible Facility – Elevator available for all guests. A freight elevator is available for set-up and removal
Air Conditioned/Heated
Art Galleries with year-round exhibits
Email events@narrowscenter.org or call 508-324-1926 for additional information, a facility tour and pricing.
