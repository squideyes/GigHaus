private events
Looking to host something special? The Venice West is your perfect spot right in the heart of Venice Beach. Our space has everything you need - State of the art sound system, full bar, and plenty of room to make it an event to remember. We're available for all your milestone celebrations and special occasions. Whether you're throwing a birthday bash, an intimate wedding, a launch party, or just getting friends together for a private show, we've got you covered. 
For more information, scroll down and fill out our form - we'll help you figure out the details and make it happen.
room information
Venue Room: 270 Capacity Standing. Seated capacity varies per layout. Room equipped with stage, sound and lighting, and projector. 
Bar Room: Total capacity around 130 with 32 seated spaces, with option to add more seating. 
VIP room: Equipped with pool table and sofa furniture, this space is perfect for smaller parties, or for private space for brides or grooms. 
Greenroom: Just built and containing a private full bath with shower, plenty of seating, and fully vanity, this additional space is versatile for your every need.
Greenroom #2: Yep, you read that right, we have two. While it doesn't have a private bath, there is plenty of room and seating, and this room is right off the main venue space. 
Kitchen: Our commercial kitchen is equipment to handle most prep and service needs, and smaller cooking requirements. If you want a full catering experience, our friends at The Melody Ballroom about us are happy to cater. We also allow for food to be brought in for private events.
Bathrooms: We have 7 stalls and 3 urinals in our completely renovated bathrooms.

Welcome to The Venice West! 
Our venue is the culmination of a few longtime local friends who love live music and grew frustrated with having very few places on the west side that could deliver on a consistent basis. After many late nights and missed shows, we decided to stop complaining and do something about it. Five years (and a pandemic) later — after a long and twisty road — the project finally came to fruition and TVW was born. 
‍
“Venice West” was coined to describe the lesser known “Beat” scene in Venice and surrounding areas during the late 1950s and early ’60s. This era helped define Venice as a global brand known for its cultural diversity, eccentricity, and expression through music and the arts, where places like “The Gas House” and “Venice West Cafe” created a haven for poets, musicians, and eccentric locals to share their words, sounds, and creativity.
‍
There are a number of moments in time that contribute to Venice’s storied history, but the “Beat Generation” certainly played a big part. We chose our name as a nod to this defining time and place in our town’s unique past and to help keep some of the qualities that Venice is celebrated for alive and flourishing for years to come.
‍
The Venice West is about community. Our goal is simple: create a welcome place for music fans and artists alike to enjoy consistently great food, music, and good times. We are humble in our approach and appreciative of your feedback so please don’t be shy and let us know what you think. Thank you and we hope you enjoy the show!
‍
-Rob, Allen and The Venice West Team

How can I get tickets to a show at The Venice West?
You can purchase tickets online by clicking the "Tickets" icon on any show page on our site.
How old do I have to be to get into the club?
All shows at The Venice West are ages 21+ unless otherwise stated.
Where do I park?
We highly encourage you to walk, ride, Lyft/Uber, or carpool. Valet service will be provided on weekends for a fee.
What are your hours?
Doors and dinner service typically begin at 6 PM. Showtimes may vary. We will be open until 12 AM most weekdays and 2 AM most weekends.
What are the set times?
Set times are subject to last-minute changes. Please note that one or two support acts typically precede the headliner.
If a show is canceled, where do I get a refund?
If a show is canceled, refunds are available at the point of purchase. You will be refunded automatically if you purchased a ticket through Ticketweb. Openers are subject to change.
What are my payment options in the venue?
We accept cash, American Express, Mastercard, and Visa. If tickets do not sell out in advance, they will be sold at the door on the night of the show.
My friend and I just started a band. How can I get us a show booked at The Venice West?
Please email talent@venicemusicgroup.com. Booking requests are not accepted via phone or at the club itself.
Include the following in your email:
Band name
Links to music (SoundCloud, Bandcamp, etc.)
Links to social media (Facebook, Twitter)
Local show history (when, where, ticket price, and your individual draw)
Due to high volume, we may not be able to respond to all booking requests.
Does your venue have seats?
Seating is available for dinner service and some performances. Tables and chairs will be removed before showtime for all full-capacity events-This means standing room only. To guarantee a seat for the entirety of an event, you may book an “Experience” on OpenTable.
Do I need to bring ID?
Yes, a valid, government-issued photo ID is required for entry to all shows. For all-ages events, those under 16 or without ID must be accompanied by an adult.
Can you scan my tickets off my phone?
Yes! Please have the brightness on your phone turned up to make scanning easier.
What kind of drinks do you have?
We have a full bar stocked with a variety of water, soft drinks, beers (on tap and bottled), wine, and spirits.
I have a blog. Do I count as press?
The Venice West does not issue press or photo passes. All press and photo inquiries should be directed to the band’s publicist, label, or management.
Can I bring my camera?
Photo policy varies for artists and is usually not determined until the day of the show. You can bring a non-professional camera to most shows. Please inquire about the photo policy at the door. Any camera with a detachable lens will be considered professional and require clearance by the artist in advance.
Do you rent your rooms for private events?
Of course! Please email events@venicemusicgroup.com for more information.
Other The Venice West rules and FAQs
No flyers, stickers, or handbills are allowed in the club.
You will be ejected for throwing anything.
No outside food, beverages, markers, spray paint, or weapons (including pocket knives).
ADA seating is available. Please arrive 15 minutes before doors open to make accommodations.
You must show proof of age with a government-issued photo ID upon entering the venue. A stamp or wristband is required to be served at the bar.
