What's on at the
Arcata Playhouse
Arcata Playhouse is a welcoming performance space where audiences can experience theater, music, movement, storytelling, and a wide range of artistic expression.
As part of Playhouse Arts, the venue brings artists and audiences together, supports creative collaboration, and offers a place for the community to gather around live performance.
Give the gift of experience with an Arcata Playhouse gift card.

Arcata's Local Arts Agency
The purpose of Playhouse Arts is to build community through the arts, working to create a society that is inspired and empowered to work together for the common good in meaningful and effective ways. 
Playhouse Arts is Arcata’s Local Arts Agency, a community arts center where anyone can see and take part in amazing art, music, and self-expression. Art is for everyone; this is why Playhouse Arts is committed to supporting free public events and festivals in the great outdoors within and throughout Humboldt County. Playhouse Arts firmly believes that the arts are an economic stronghold and that the contributions of artists to our community are irreplaceable.
To support this vision of a society that uplifts artists, Playhouse Arts is home to a number of programs that provide opportunities for artists to make impactful work, including Our Space Arts, Humboldt Hot Air, and Artists in Schools. Playhouse Arts is a hub of art, creation, and collaboration – be it a night of music, a mask making workshop, movement classes, or a meeting of the minds, Playhouse Arts is here to help. 
 Learn more about the City of Arcata's Stragetic Arts Plan on their website.

Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion Statement
Land Acknowledgement
Playhouse Arts is committed to the principles of racial equity and justice in our inner structure and policies, in the way we create opportunities for diverse artists, and in the programs, projects and events we organize and/or host, facilitating and advocating for visibility and normalization of different cultures, languages and ways of knowing and being.
We believe in the democratization of art for artists and audiences by sharing resources, networks, venues, as well as creating opportunities for free public art and low cost private art: art that has the potential to benefit all people and our planet.
We believe in creating and nourishing an environment in which artists and audiences of any background, age, color, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, or sexual orientation feel welcomed, respected, supported, empowered and heard.
We are committed to continued learning, to adapting to what we learn, and to creating brave spaces that allow meaningful dialogue, action, change and progress.
Ancestral Land of the Wiyot People
Playhouse Arts acknowledges the First Peoples of America, their Elders past, present, and emerging.
We pay our respects to the traditional storytellers, artists, and owners of the land on which we live.

Artists in Schools
The Artists in Schools program brings working artists to schools to teach & inspire students of all ages throughout Humboldt County.
For over a decade, Playhouse Arts has brought high quality teaching artists and local schools together in our Arts Education program. Our program provides experienced teaching artists, assistant artists, and all the materials needed to complete the project. Schedule art classes for a specific grade level or your entire school community. We take great pride in bringing the joy of kinesthetic learning to your campus. Contact us for pricing details based on mileage.
Residencies : Choose one or more options to develop a sequential art curriculum that spans from Fall to Spring. Please note that a minimum of two one-hour classes is required for booking. Our workshops typically offer one hour long class per week for several weeks in a row.
Mask Making
A one hour class where students learn mask history and etiquette, while designing, sculpting, painting, and developing characters. 
Mask Performance
A one hour class each week where students get to move, develop character, write stories, learn stage direction, create their own stories, and host a performance of their work based on the masks that were created in the previous residency.
Shadow Puppetry
A one hour class each week where students learn about the art of collaboration, speaking, listening, plot development, writing, storytelling, and character development through building their own puppets and presenting a performance of their work.
Creative Drama & Dance
A one hour class each week where the teachers use creative play, movement, storytelling, and music to create a performance adapted from a familiar story.
Devised Theater
A one hour class each week where students collaborate in creating an original play or series of skits. Through improvisation, exploration and experimentation, students will learn the art of collaboration to generate their own material developing their own characters, plot and script. As an ensemble they continue to rehearse and refine the collective work until they have an original and innovative piece, culminating in presenting a performance.
