Concert Ticket Information:
When you select BUY TICKETS from our website, the eTix cost will display all-in pricing. The total price listed will include the following fees: 3% for credit card processing, a 23% convenience fee, and a $3 facility fee. 
Purchasing tickets in-person at our box office eliminates all facility and convenience fees. And don’t forget, Music Box VIP Members do not pay convenience or facility fees when buying online!

Table Reservations 
If you have already purchased tickets to a show in our first floor supper club or to a general admission show in the concert hall, please use the Toast button below to make one reservation for your group, up to 8 guests per table. If you have a group of 9 or more, please call 216-242-1250, extension #1, to make your dining arrangements.
Any questions? Call 216-242-1250
If you need to cancel your reservation, please email musicboxcle@gmail.com

Spaces
supper club
If your event plan includes live music, space for business exhibitor tables, or a must-have area for large training sessions, the Supper Club is an ideal option. This space is also a popular choice for weddings.
private dining room
Our private dining room provides the perfect atmosphere for your small-scale event. Access to a large media screen and separate A/V make this a great space for conducting business presentations.
private room + mezzanine
This unique space can be configured for a reception and dining event. Guests have a great view of the stage and separate loge access.
concert hall
Our Concert Hall is a premier venue for weddings, formal business meetings, private parties and large group dinners. This spaces also boasts direct access to the riverfront rooftop deck.
riverview
Located within the Concert Hall, Riverview provides an outstanding backdrop for your next cocktail reception. Views of downtown paired with a high-end sound system and deck access make this space a popular choice.
rooftop deck
If outdoor fun is more your speed, we offer amazing river and lake views for your guests. And, if the weather doesn’t cooperate, the deck has the capability to be fully tented.
