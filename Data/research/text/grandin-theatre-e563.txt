The Judy Chops formed in 2008 in the Shenandoah Valley, and has become a staple of the Virginia music scene. What began as a four piece acoustic act, has evolved into a dynamic musical collective that has been winning over audiences of all shapes and sizes due to the group’s high energy live shows, and deft musicianship. The Judy Chops have been a perennial favorite at Red Wing Roots Music Festival after being named 'Best Local Roots Band' at the inaugural Red Wing back in 2013. The group would go on to win Rockn' to LOCKN' in 2018, culminating in a huge set at LOCKN' Music Festival following Keller Williams' Grateful Gospel. 
The Judy Chops have been honing their craft on other festival stages including FloydFest, Rooster Walk, Bristol Rhythm & Roots, Front Porch Fest, and select venues including support slots with Lake Street Dive, Donna The Buffalo, Squirrel Nut Zippers, and Southern Culture on the Skids, California Honeydrops, and more. Their latest album The Good Days was released in 2024. The Judy Chops are currently working on new music, and gearing up for a big year! This band shows no signs of slowing down, because #chopsdontstop!

Carbon Leaf is a Richmond, VA-based indie rock band known for their brand of “ether-electrified porch music” that bridges roots, Americana, and pop with folk-driven songwriting and an indie-rock backbone.
Now in their 33rd year as an independent act with 21 releases and over 3,500 live shows nationwide, they deliver a high-energy, multi-instrumental set (acoustic/electric guitars, fiddle, banjo, mandolin, cello, penny whistle, rich harmonies) and have built a loyal audience through consistent U.S. touring.
The band broke nationally as an independent act with “The Boxer,” winner of the 2002 American Music Awards for New Music and became the first unsigned band to perform on the AMAs.
Their 2004 follow up single “Life Less Ordinary” became their biggest radio hit, peaking at #5 on Billboard’s Adult Alternative (AAA) chart and #28 on Hot AC, expanding their touring base nationwide.
After three charting albums on Vanguard Records and multiple songwriting awards, Carbon Leaf returned to full independence on their own Constant Ivy Music imprint and continues to tour heavily in support of their latest album, Time is the Playground. Their sound reaches a broad, cross-generational AAA/adult-alternative audience.
