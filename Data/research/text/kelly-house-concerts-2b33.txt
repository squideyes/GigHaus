About Us
From the Ranch House to the Kelly House: Angela has always loved live music, especially in her own home! Her previous series, Ranch House Concerts, hosted its first concert in 2007 and hosted twenty-six shows from 2007-2011. The last show in the Ranch House series was Butch Hancock on September 9, 2011. Ranch House also hosted artists including Carrie Rodriguez, Justin Townes Earle, Will Kimbrough, Tommy Womack, and Kevin Gordon, to name a few. Following a move to a new home, Angela decided to begin a new series, Kelly House Concerts. She and her partner, Richard, enjoy supporting touring musicians in this way. Their home is located in St Louis County, in Maryland Heights, MO.

FAQ
What are the "ticket" prices? 
Kelly House Concerts doesn't sell actual tickets. Our “ticket” price is a suggested minimum amount to contribute to the artist for their performance. All that we collect goes directly to the performers. We have had people contribute more than the minimum in the past, and all of the extra money goes directly to the performers as well. Once you RSVP by email, we encourage you to pay for your “ticket” in advance. This gives us a more accurate count of how many are coming, and we don’t have to spend as much time worrying about the money on the night of the show. The email invitations include instructions on how to pay either by check or paypal. We keep a list of people who have RSVPed/paid at the door and will check you off as you arrive.
How is food and drink handled? 
All food and drink is bring-your-own, pot-luck style. We have plenty of coolers and ice available. Keep in mind that we feed the performers as well, so a few heartier items are always appreciated!
Where are the concerts located? 
Kelly House Concerts is located in central St. Louis County in Creve Coeur. We will send you detailed directions and instructions on where to park when we’ve received confirmation that you are coming.
What happens if I RSVP and am unable to come?
In order to to be able to ensure the best possible audience for each show, it is very important that you let us know if you will not be able to come, even if it is on the day of the show. Often, we have a "waiting list" for our shows with eager fans who may be "waiting" by the phone or their computer even at the last minute in the hopes that there will be room for them to attend. If you have given the suggested donation for a show that you are not able to attend, there are no refunds. If we are able to find someone to "take your spot," we can usually apply what you paid to a future show. 
OK, this sounds great! I want to come to your next show! How do I get on your email list?
To receive invitations, send an email to Angela at kellyhouseconcert@gmail.com, and we will add you to our email list. It is helpful if you list particular artists you would like to see, since invitations are not always sent to everyone on the email list.

Tickets/Invitations
Kelly House Concerts are invitation-only events. Angela and Richard hope to provide a place where avid music fans can hear live music in a comfortable setting. We support touring musicians by providing them with a venue where people will really listen to their music, giving them high-quality exposure to sincere music fans. 
To receive invitations, send an email to Angela at kellyhouseconcert@gmail.com, and we will add you to our email list. It is helpful if you list particular artists you would like to see, since invitations are not always sent to everyone on the email list. 
Kelly House Concerts generally sends out an email notification of a new concert a few weeks in advance. If you are interested in a particular event, we will be happy to send you an invitation if there is more space available. We request that everyone who plans on attending RSVP soon after receiving the invitation so we have an accurate sense of how many are coming and can offer the show to more folks if we are not at capacity.
