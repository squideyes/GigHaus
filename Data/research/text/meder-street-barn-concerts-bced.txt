Monthly Concerts at the Meder Street Barn
We host barn concerts in Santa Cruz, Calif. about once a month from the Spring through the Fall.We welcome anyone who loves singer-songwriter music! You just need to request an invitation, and be willing to pitch in a donation to the artist of the day.We collect payment in the form of donations at the door with a suggested donation of $25 per person.
 All donations go directly to the artistJoin us for a potluck and meet & greet before the show, or just come for the music
 Potlucks begin at 2:30, with the show starting at 4PMShows will be open for invitation requests around one month prior to the show date
 Specifics will be emailed to you upon acceptance of your request.To be notified of future concerts and of when invitation requests will be accepted, Sign up on our Mailing List.
