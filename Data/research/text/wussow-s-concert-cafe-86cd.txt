Wussow’s Concert Cafe is a locally owned coffeehouse featuring live music, a full espresso bar, homemade fare, and a fun variety of beer and wine. Since 1999 Wussow’s has strived to be an inclusive and supportive home for local artists, musicians, writers, and everyone in between. We are excited to offer food, coffee, tea, and beer that comes directly from our local community of friends and dreamers. Our stage continues to be the stomping ground for many local musicians and a favorite stop for regional and national touring acts. We love good music, real food, delicious coffee, and great friends! The next time you’re in the neighborhood, stop in and check out what’s cooking in our kitchen and on our stage.

Open Mic
Open Mic happens every Wednesday at Wussow’s Concert Cafe. If you have a favorite local musician, chances are they have some fond memories of getting up on our stage. Wussow’s has been voted Best Open Mic by The Reader Weekly and it’s the place to be if you want to check out up-and-comers. We provide the stage and the sound, and this amazing community provides the talent.
Wednesdays from 6-9 PM. The sign up list goes out 5 minutes before the start of the event and every performer gets 15 minutes on stage.
Free Thursday’s
Every Thursday we have an event that is aimed at bringing our local community of artists, writers, and thinkers together. Thursdays start at 6pm and they are free and all ages.
1st Thursday – Art Opening
Every month we showcase the work of a new local artist on our walls and the 1st Thursday is the opening reception. Grab a bite, meet the artist, and enjoy live music while you mingle.
2nd Thursday – Jazz with Billy Barnard & Veikko Lepisto
Legendy jazz guitarist Billy Barnard joins forces with stand up bassist Veikko Lepisto to bring the perfect sound to you’re thursday night.
3rd Thursday – Spoken Word Open Mic
Are you a writer? Do you know a writer? Do you love words? Well, Spoken Word Open Mic is the place to be every 3rd Thursday. Show up to sign up for 10 minutes on stage to share your work or just sit back and enjoy. This open mic is open to all genres of the written word AND there’s trivia!
4th Thursday – Russ Sackett’s Guitar Bar
Think piano bar with a guitar! Throw out a song to Russ and see if you can pull it off.
