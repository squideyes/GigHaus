ROOTED IN PORTLAND, OREGON — since 1973
Artichoke Community Music is a nonprofit venue, music school, and instrument store in the folk tradition. We are driven by the belief that music builds bridges between people, generations, and cultures.
MISSION
Building community through music.
Leah Mócsy - Executive Director/Booker
Eddie Michael - Store and Venue Coordinator
Kyle Keilman - Store and Venue Support Superhero!

Make music easy to learn, share and appreciate.
Easy to join!
Keep events low-cost or free
Support local musicians and emerging voices
Expand programs for kids, seniors, and underserved communities
Help music traditions thrive for future generations
Folk music is more than a genre, it's a shared language of stories, traditions, and values.‍ ‍
At Artichoke we honor that spirit through not just music, but poetry, dance, and storytelling. Whether you're picking up an instrument for the first time or taking the stage, we want this to be a a place to belong.
VOLUNTEERS RUN ALL OF OUR EVENTS!
Come join our dedicated and fun team of volunteers who bring all of our events to life!
 JOIN US
Are you interested in booking yourself or your band at The Artichoke? We would love to hear from you!
Just send a booking inquiry to booking@artichokemusic.org. 
Please include the following:
- Your name and/or the name of your act
- A link to a video of a live performance
- A link to your recorded music
- Website, Facebook, and/or Instagram links
 BOOK THE VENUE
Artichoke Music Venue is a vibrant space in Portland dedicated to bringing the community together through live music. Since 1973, it has been a hub for diverse performances, fostering a welcoming environment where musicians and audiences connect and celebrate the joy of music.
Location
2007 SE Powell Blvd, Portland, OR 97202
Southeast Portland
Parking: Limited onsite, street parking available
Venue Specs
Venue: 50 upgraded upholstered chairs plus additional bar stools and bistro tables. Standing configurations available by request.
Stage: 14’ x 9’ hardwood stage
12’ x 8’ hardwood stage
Both suitable for dance performances. Large stage carpet is normally used for comfort and sound control.
PA: Full system including: QSC 12.2 main speakers; QSC 10.2 stage monitors; PreSonus Series III – 16 channel digital mixer; 16 by 8 integrated digital stage box; additional 16 channel analog snake; generous selection of mics, DI’s, stands, cables, etc.
Lighting: Full DMX controlled LED lighting system.
Video: Fixed video streaming and recording system featuring: 3 stationary and 2 operable cameras; Blackmagic Designs 8 input switcher with recording capability; dedicated Apple streaming computer; high speed internet connectivity.
Instruments: Yamaha digital piano; Pearl house drum kit; numerous guitars, mandolins, banjos, and other string instruments available if requested.

Volunteer With us
Are you interested in serving the local music community? We have lots of opportunities for you to put your special traits, skills, and talents to good use at Artichoke Music! 
Just read the position descriptions below. If something sparks your interest please download our Volunteer Application and send it to Volunteer@artichokemusic.org or drop it off in person at the store during regular business hours (12pm-5pm, Thursday - Sunday). Please note: we process volunteer applications once a week on Wednesdays. 
Volunteer Positions at Artichoke
Sound Tech - assist with running audio for live events in the venue (approx. 4 hours/shift)
Video Tech - assist with video broadcasting of live events in the venue (approx. 4 hours/shift)
Door Host - assist with door, welcome guests, and process cash admissions (approx. 2 - 3 hours/shift)
Bartender - Bar service during events - MUST have or agree to get OLCC servers license (approx. 3 - 4 hours/shift)
Venue Emcee - Introduce the band and make announcements at the beginning of the show & at intermission (from the stage), assist with light clean up at the end of the show (approx. 2 - 3 hours/shift)
Custodian - help with maintenance, clean-up, janitorial and other housekeeping duties, light general construction services - both requiring or not requiring a contractors license (1 hour minimum/shift)
Store Helper - (knowledge about stringed instruments preferred) assist with greeting and helping customers, may need to help with housekeeping duties - sweeping, vacuuming, cleaning bathrooms, watering plants, etc...(2.5 hours minimum/shift)
Thanks to our community supporters!
Special Thanks to the Oregon Cultural Trust. 
For 25 years, the Oregon Cultural Trust has played a vital role in our state's cultural vibrancy, supporting nearly 1,700 nonprofits across Oregon. How? It's simple: The Cultural Trust empowers Oregonians to leverage their support for arts, heritage, and humanities nonprofits with the Oregon Cultural Tax Credit.
First, donate to one or more of the nearly 1,700 qualified cultural nonprofits. Then, make a gift to the Cultural Trust. When you file your taxes, you claim the 100% credit and deduct from your bill — up to $500 filing individually, $1,000 filing jointly and $2,500 for C- and S-class corporations. By using the Cultural Tax Credit, Oregonians and the Cultural Trust have invested $50 million in every corner of our state. Give to the Cultural Trust by December 31 to get your 2026 tax credit!
Learn more: https://www.culturaltrust.org/donors/ - Learn more via their video here.
