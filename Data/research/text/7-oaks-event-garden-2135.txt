Bringing The Art of Music back to SETX 
If you’re looking for a unique concert experience, look no further than 7 Oaks Event Garden & Listening Room in Southeast Texas. This hidden gem offers intimate dinner shows for 100 listeners on a beautifully curated 90-year old homestead in rural LaBelle, Tx., with the added bonus of being able to bring your own food on their Sunday and Tuesday shows. Whether you’re a music lover or just looking for an escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life, 7 Oaks is the perfect venue for you. Come enjoy amazing live music in a truly one-of-a-kind setting!

 About 7 Oaks
 How do I buy tickets?
Click this link to the event you want to attend and check out online. You can also call me if you are having any issues with online checkout!
 Do I need to print tickets to get in? 
No! We have you on our list and the link via your phone will suffice when needed(larger events).
 Do y’all have assigned seats?
Yes! Early ticket purchases are placed closer to stage and so forth! There isn’t a bad seat in the house . Email me for seating notes or accommodations.
 Do y’all have ADA access?
Yes we do! 
Wave your blue pass to the parking attendant and he will park you upfront! Working on a poured slabbed sidewalk from parking to venue this winter! 
Bathrooms are ADA accessible. 
 Can I pay at the door?
Most events do not allow this! As we need to prepare the room for our guest. Call or text me to see if a pay at the door option is possible for the event in reference.

Booking Inquiry 
7 Oaks Event Garden & Listening Room is the perfect place to host your special event! Our beautiful outdoor garden, as well as our indoor listening room, can accommodate a variety of events. From weddings and family reunions to corporate meetings and private concerts, we have all the right elements to make your event a success.
At 7 Oaks Event Garden & Listening Room, we offer a unique and special opportunity for musicians to record their live performance in an intimate and professional setting. Our state-of-the-art sound system creates a perfect environment for artists of all genres to capture their music in the best quality possible. We also provide pre-production services and support, so you can focus on making great music and ensuring a seamless recording process. If you’re looking to book a live recording at 7 Oaks Event Garden & Listening Room, simply get in touch with us today and we’ll help you get started. We look forward to working with you!
