Rental Space
Over 4,500 square feet of space. Rich interior colors, soft lighting, 15′ ceilings and beautiful hardwood floors define the spacious concert hall to create a warm and charming environment. The entire space is equipped with central heating and air conditioning and is ADA accessible.
Your rental of Polaris Hall is for the full building and includes:
The Concert Hall, which includes the stage, full PA, screen and projector and theatrical lighting
The Main Hall Bar on the second floor
The Parlor Bar on the first floor
The Parlor lounge which includes a pool table, shuffleboard and cozy seating areas
A patio with 2 fire pits
Two dressing room areas
Access to in-house tables and chairs
A fully staffed Rock 'n Roll events team who will be on hand during your event.
Click the "Book An Event" link below and we'll send you more information on pricing and options!

History
This charming building, 'Star Ballroom,' was originally built in 1930 by a culturally diverse, working class fraternal organization in Portland. Men from “every rank and station” did work in the name of “friendship, love and truth” here, as did the great women who subsequently bought the building and operated it as the North Star Ballroom for several decades.
