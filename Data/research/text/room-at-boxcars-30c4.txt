THE ROOM -
60-Seat Indoor Concert Venue
The Room is our 60-seat indoor venue where we feature singer/songwriters from all over the United States and UK. It’s a place where musicians and music lovers come together to share in the stories behind the songs the way they were written and meant to be performed. 
This intimate venue is set up as a listening room where you can actually hear what the artists have to say. We have been told it’s a very special place for a performer to connect with their audience. Even comparing it to the iconic Bluebird in Nashville. Our artists can’t wait to come back and perform on our stage again and again.

About
Full Bar • Full Menu
BOXCARS is a fun friendly place to meet and hang out. We are family owned and community minded. We’ve been in business since 1999 serving daily specials, homemade soups, cold drinks and so much more.
Join us for our Friday Fish Fry, Packer Games or one of our live music events. Our comedy series runs from November to April with comedians from all over the United States. Come in, relax, and enjoy your time in a fun, friendly atmosphere.
