Miller's - A Gathering Place
Located in the heart of historic downtown Carnation, Miller’s serves as a gathering place in the beautiful Snoqualmie Valley. Miller’s hosts a wide variety of “in-house” events, including live music, community dinners, and art & educational classes. The space is also available as a rental for private events, complete with full catering options. Please join our mailing list for announcements on upcoming happenings, or contact us to book your own event.
Voted 2024 "Best Rural Small Business" in King County
Voted 2025 "Best Meeting & Event Space" by 425 Business 
Voted 2025 "Best Live Music / Entertainment" by Snoqualmie Valley Record

About Us
We LOVE bringing people together! It’s what we do. Creating the magical blend of food, music, and laughter. All of the touchstones that bring life to a community. 
We understand the power of the live event and the healing energy of simply gathering. 
If you have been to Miller’s, you know what a gorgeous venue it is, warm and inviting to all who enter. Together, we always dreamed of having a venue on the main street of our sweet little town of Carnation, where we have lived for 20 years. Our deepest gratitude to beautiful soul’s, Jules Hughes and Lee Grumman, along with the support of our friends and neighbors who have made this dream possible. Here we go! 
Nancy Colton:
Nancy spent years as an accomplished, award-winning singer-songwriter. She has performed locally and nationally in venues including The Gorge, Tractor Tavern, 5th Avenue Theatre, Harvard Square and Bumbershoot. Nancy is also a passionate baker, curator of flavors, and alchemist of apothecary magic! Her zest for life and adventure shines brightly in her love of family, nature, hiking, skiing and paragliding.
Bill Pritchard:
Bill’s resume is rich in music and food as well, with a long career as a chef and live music producer. As owner of a premier Seattle music venue (Madison’s Café & Music House) along with stints with Bumbershoot and the Backstage, Bill has produced well over 1,000 events. Fostering his passion for food and live events, Bill started Mosaic Catering in 2003. Time with family, playing guitar, and paragliding are among Bill’s true joys.
Cheers!
Nancy & Bill

Music Booking Guidelines
Miller’s is an intimate concert venue, hosting a wide variety of local and touring original artists. We are currently booking music on weekends only, making an occasional exception for touring artists. We have a ticket capacity of 90, and book our shows in the hope of filling the room.
The best way to reach out to us is by email, events@millerscarnation.com. Please include the following information:
Any links to your music (your website, Spotify, YouTube, Soundcloud, Bandcamp).
The dates you are available to play.
What you think your draw might be in the Snoqualmie Valley and greater Seattle.
If you have anyone else that you will be sharing the evening with.
Anything special about your show (record release, benefit, anything that might help bring folks in.
We get a high volume of inquiries and may not reply to everyone. You will definitely hear from us in a timely manner if we feel we have a spot for you. Miller’s is a relatively new venue, just in our second year. We are building a small, but wonderful following from our “regulars”, but definitely depend on the artist for their draw. We promote all of our shows and appreciate artists that do the same!
Feel free to reach out to us. We would love to hear from you!
Nancy and Bill

Zach Shaw, singer/songwriter and former fixture of the Seattle music scene (Lanford Black, Barefoot Contingent) has joined with his wife Kate Shaw (vocals), John VanDuinen (Drums), and Brett Nakashima (Bass) to form Zach and the Kickbacks. The band is propelled by Shaw’s rugged vocals, elevated by Kate’s effusive harmonies, and steered by VanDuinen and Nakashima’s pocketed grooves in a mix of rock, soul, and folk that will keep your toe tapping.
*Tickets are non-refundable, unless the show is cancelled or rescheduled. If you cannot attend a show, tickets can be transferred from the purchaser to another individual, or individuals. Tickets purchased for a specific event cannot be transferred to a different event.
*Doors open one hour before scheduled show time
*Seating is on a first come basis and sold-out shows are partially seated. Please arrive early for the best seating options. Reservations not available.
*Food and beverages. Beer, wine, and non-alcoholic beverages are available for purchase. We are not currently serving food, but food can be brought in from home, or neighborhood restaurants. No outside drinks please.
Shows are all ages.
