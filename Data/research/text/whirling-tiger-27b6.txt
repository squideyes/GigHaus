Private Events 
We’re more than a music venue! Not only does Whirling Tiger host hundreds of local, regional & nationally touring artists each year but we’re also a very versatile event space that you can rent for a holiday party wedding, corporate event & more! 
Located in the quickly expanding Butchertown neighborhood – we are just down the road from the popular NULU district & minutes from downtown. With the building’s original structure built in the late 1800’s, it retains the stunning architecture of its time & will make for a truly unique experience. The front lounge of Whirling Tiger is split into 4 different areas on the first floor & a loft area on the 2nd floor. With the exposed brick & archways built in 18th century, our space is unlike any other. Additionally, Whirling TIger offers full production, bar & security staff for any event’s needs. 
Contact us for availability & rental rates
