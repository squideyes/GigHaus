About Folk Fest
The Club
With live music over 300 nights each year The Ark is considered one of the top music clubs in the world and one of the few nonprofit music venues of its kind left in the country.
About
Folk, Roots, and More
The Ark is renowned for the quality and breadth of its programming and is committed to presenting performers throughout the entire arc of their career.
Explore Genres
Welcome!
We provide a safe and welcoming atmosphere for ALL people to listen to, learn about, perform, and share music. The Ark depends on members, donors, and volunteers to stay afloat. Get involved.
Support The Ark
“Folk music is not an acoustic guitar – that’s not where the heart of it is. I use the word ‘folk’ in reference to punk music and rap music. It’s an attitude, it’s an awareness of one’s heritage, and it’s a community. It’s subcorporate music that gives voice to different communities and their struggle against authority.” – Ani DiFranco
“It’s a folk singer’s job to comfort disturbed people and to disturb comfortable people.” – Woody Guthrie
“We never walk into any other place and feel so much at home as we do at The Ark.” – Amanda Sudano-Ramirez, Johnny Swim

 Joshua Radin 
Special Guest: Raye Zaragoza
 The Ark 
 316 S. Main, Ann Arbor, MI, United States 
Joshua Radin emerged in 2004 as an empathetic, engaging, and often enthralling troubadour. Over the years, he’s tallied north of 1 billion streams, moved 1 million-plus albums worldwide, and scored a gold certification for the single “I’d Rather Be With You.” His music has appeared in over 200 films, television series, and commercials. During 2025, he turned the page on the next chapter of his career and life with his tenth full-length LP and first independent album since 2017, One Day Home. Musically, he taps into two sides of his sonic palette, leaning into soul and singer-songwriter stylings while tracing the journey of a romantic relationship.

 Ticket Information 
Tickets to performances typically range in price from $20-$50. The Ark also offers free shows as well, so nothing will stop you from experiencing great music.
JUMP TO: ONLINE | BY PHONE | IN PERSON | FREE SHOWS | FAQ
Online
Tickets for most Ark events are available for purchase online through the University of Michigan’s Michigan Union Ticket Office (MUTO).
Click on any ticket link in our calendar or the BUY TICKETS ONLINE button found on each event page.
As of 5/12/25 ticket prices on our website reflect all-in pricing for purchases made online or by phone. This means the price you see includes the ticket price plus MUTO’s ticket processing fees.
By phone
To order tickets by phone, call The Michigan Union Ticket Office at (734) 763-8587.
Hours: Monday–Friday, 11 am–4 pm (11 am–5 pm during the U-M school year)
The fee structure is the same for online and phone orders.
In person
At The Ark Box Office
The Ark’s box office is open on evenings when there is a show. The box office is open from 30 minutes before showtime until 60 minutes after showtime.
There is no service charge for tickets purchased in person at The Ark. 
Buy tickets at the door means no advance tickets are available for that event and must be purchased in person at The Ark box office before showtime.
We kindly ask that you wait to purchase tickets to future shows until after we have admitted the line for that night’s show. 
There are some exceptions to box office hours. Feel free to call to make sure the box office is open before you visit.
At the Michigan Union Ticket Office
The Michigan Union Ticket Office is open Monday through Friday 11 am–4 pm (11 am–5 pm during the U-M school year).
The MUTO ticket office is located on the ground level of the Michigan Union, 530 South State Street, on the University of Michigan’s central campus.
There is a $1.00 per ticket service fee for tickets purchased in person at MUTO’s ticket counter.
Please visit the MUTO website for latest updates and hours.
Free shows
Artist Spotlight Series
The Artist Spotlight Series is a free monthly concert series. Programming is made possible with support from Ford Philanthropy.
No tickets are required for entry. 
Doors open 30 minutes before showtime. 
The Ark accepts donations for Food Gatherers at these shows, but attendees are not required to make a donation to attend the concert.
Become an Ark Member!
Memberships at the $50 level and higher receive free entry to a designated Ark performance on a quarterly basis. Get membership info.
Students
Attend Open Stage (The Ark’s Open Mic night) and Artist Spotlight Series for free.
Ticketing for other venues
Tickets for most Ark produced shows at other venues (e.g. Michigan Theater, Royal Oak Music Theatre) are not available through MUTO or at The Ark box office. Please check our website event listings or venue websites for direct ticket links and information about these events at other venues.

 About 
Considered one of the top music clubs in the world, The Ark is Michigan’s nonprofit home for folk and roots music. With live music over 300 nights each year in one of the best listening rooms anywhere.
Know Before You Go
The Club
At The Ark, we take seriously our role as part of a larger musical community. We nurture rising artists, we listen to the wisdom (and the humor) of those who’ve been around for awhile, and, frankly, we’re not afraid to present music that you just won’t hear anywhere else.
