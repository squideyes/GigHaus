November 18, 19 & 20 at 7:30pm

Plucked
Friday, November 20 Cash Bar 6:30 to 7:30pm followed by screening at 7:30pm
Set in Gilded Age New York, Plucked follows Katherine Greene, a woman determined to divorce her emotionally unstable husband. There’s just one complication: an important dinner party meant to keep up appearances. As secrets emerge and tempers flare, the elegant evening unravels into jealousy, revenge, and outrageous, increasingly dangerous chaos.

Warren Miller: Days Off
Sunday, November 22 at 5pm
Drop in on Warren Miller’s 77th film, Days Off. Join host Katie Burrell as she pulls back the curtain on what the pros do on their days off. Go skijoring in Banff, join Craig McMorris and friends in Whistler, and explore Alaska’s spines with Sam Kuch and Craig Murray.  Tickets
Zach Nugent’s Dead Set
Saturday, November 28 at 5:30pm

Expressive solos, rich musical interplay, and wide-ranging improvisation invite you to rediscover familiar favorites and follow the band somewhere unexpected. Every performance is its own journey.

LEARN AT THT
The Seligmann Center for Learning & Engagement (CLE) is a hub for creative programming and life-long learning. We provide workshops, classes, camps and events that offer opportunities in the performing arts and beyond. Our offerings cultivate belonging through confident expression using the arts. Year round classes let families craft together, kids perform on stage, and adults make puppets – Story, ritual, and celebration! Scroll further to see the full offerings from the CLE and sign up for a class today! 
Our “semesters” are broken up into 9-week programs, 4 week programs and special one-off events. Continue scrolling to select courses for kids or adults! Want to sign up for all of them, but unsure of how to pay for all the things you now want to do? The CLE offers scholarships to people of all ages thanks to generous donors and our community partners. Don’t want to take a class just yet, but want to pay it forward so someone else can enjoy the current offerings? Contact the Business Office to make a donation specifically for scholarships by calling 802-388-1436, or email erin@townhalltheater.org today!
Questions?
Please contact the Interim Director of The Center For Learning & Engagement Keziah Wilde at keziah@townhalltheater.org

DONATE
The arts are vital to our quality of life and and we need your support to continue the work we do. We are here to help people find themselves, to help people make connections, and to provide windows to new worlds and ideas. We couldn’t do it without contributions from the community. We’re happy to accept donations of any amount, but we should note that gifts of 50 dollars or more come with membership benefits.

WHO WE ARE
Town Hall Theater is much more than just our historic building, lovingly restored in 2008. THT is a cultural anchor for Addison County and beyond; a shared resource for artists, non-profits (including over a dozen resident companies), businesses, and educators; and a destination that unites a diverse cross-section of our community around unique, shared experiences. THT is an inclusive, inviting space for everyone! Your support through ticket purchases, donations, and volunteering helps us continue to thrive with 165 events per year.

Our Resident Companies
Watch the Documentary
In the summer of 2023, as Town Hall Theater stood on the cusp of a major expansion project, Middlebury College interns Naomi Clark and Peyton Mader embarked on a journey to document the theater's past, present, and future. Together, they delved into boxes of archival material and conducted hours of interviews to create this mini-documentary, which was completed in the spring of 2024. To read a written version of the theater’s history, click here. 
Our Historic Bell
This 10-minute documentary by THT Alum Sophie Pope McCright tells the story of our 1887 Meneely bell, which once served as Middlebury's fire alarm and town curfew bell before being saved from scrap in 1961 by the community's "A Buck for the Bell" campaign and, finally, returned home to THT in 2005.
Now a film major at the University of Chicago who will be continuing her work in the field when she graduates this June, Sophie's THT journey began at age 11 playing Macbeth in Shakespeare It's Elementary, continuing through numerous Young Company productions including Annie and The Glass Menagerie. She also acted in several MCP shows including The Nutcracker and The Crucible before graduating from MUHS in 2021.
