Old Town Center for the Arts Launches Its 19th Season in Old Town Cottonwood 
The heart of the Verde Valley's cultural landscape is beating stronger than ever as the Old Town Center for the Arts (OTCA) proudly announces the rollout of its 19th Season in historic Old Town Cottonwood. Over nearly two decades, OTCA has earned a stellar reputation as one of northern Arizona's premier performance spaces, known for its intimate acoustics, exquisite lighting design, vibrant community spirit, and a consistently eclectic curation of world-class talent. Housed in a beautifully renovated historic building that brings a memorable warmth and rustic elegance to every event, this beloved venue serves as the perfect canvas for unforgettable artistic expressions. From legendary acoustic storytellers to gravity-defying aerial movement arts, the upcoming OTCA fall lineup promises to be a dazzling and diverse display of ‘must see’ performance art and entertainment. 
Tickets for all 19th Season performances are on sale now. Audiences are encouraged to purchase their tickets early online via Cur8.com. 
For added convenience, local physical ticket outlets include Mount Hope Foods in Cottonwood and the Mary D. Fisher Box Office in Sedona.

Eddie Cochran: Don’t Forget Me 
Monday, November 16, 7:00 PM 
Eddie Cochran died aged just 21, yet his influence can still be heard in generations of artists from The Beatles and The Rolling Stones to today’s contemporary artists. 
From BAFTA and Academy Award-winning producers and filmmakers Goldfinch comes the first official biography on rock & roll icon Eddie Cochran. Eddie Cochran: Don’t Forget Me is made in partnership with the Cochran Family Estate, chronicling the extraordinary but tragically short life of the rock & roll pioneer, with dramatic recreations featuring Jack Harris as Eddie Cochran and Frances Barber as his mother, Alice Cochran.

Donate! to the Old Town Center for the Arts
Friends of Old Town Center for the Arts is a nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization established to enhance the quality of life in Cottonwood and the greater Verde Valley area by helping to support the Old Town Center for the Arts theater and Studio B, in its mission to provide arts and entertainment events, cultural activities, live theater, art & movement classes, and arts education. 
Please consider making a tax-deductible donation and become a Friend of Old Town Center for the Arts (FOTCA). Your $25, $50, $100, or whatever amount you choose, will go directly towards concert performance events, community outreach, operations, and facilities & equipment improvements. 
Or make a check payable to FOTCA (Friends of Old Town Center for the Arts) and mail to: 
Friends of Old Town Center for the Arts

Acappella Sedona & Regional Stars Unite for a Spectacular Night of Harmonies 
 Acappella Sedona 
The premier vocal powerhouse Acappella Sedona will take the stage at OTCA and will be joined by an elite lineup of regional stars for a rare, star-studded celebration of local performance art. 
Celebrated throughout the Sedona and Verde Valley area for their intricate, rich harmonies and vibrant community spirit, Acappella Sedona delivers a performance like no other. This highly anticipated fall production showcases the magic and charm of human voices blending together in soaring arrangements. This unique collaboration promises a brilliantly dynamic, feel-good event designed to have the entire audience smiling, tapping their feet, and singing right along. 
To make the evening even more enjoyable, Acappella Sedona will be shared by a magnificent roster of special guest performers, each bringing a distinct flavor to the stage: 
Shondra Jepperson: Known affectionately as the "Star of Sedona," this celebrated local performer brings her immense stage presence, dynamic vocal prowess, and captivating theatrical flair. 
Patrick Ki: An extraordinarily talented guitarist and ukulele master, Ki infuses the evening with his signature smooth, melodic strings and soulful instrumental textures. 
Red Rockappella Quartet: This fantastic ensemble delivers classic barbershop-style charm, elevating the night with tight, precision harmonies and delightful vocal synchronization. 
Tommy “Rocks” Anderson: A multi-talented instrumentalist and music educator, Anderson breaks the rules with his whimsical, energetic string style alongside his accompanying orchestra. 
Village Ukulele People: Bringing joy wherever they strum, this delightfully energetic group adds an infectious, uplifting rhythm that perfectly complements the community spirit of the event. The Village Ukulele People have been strumming and singing together for 10 years, meeting weekly for strumming practice and performing benefit concerts for the community. 
There is nothing quite like the goosebumps sparked by a room filled entirely with pure, passionate performance. This one-of-a-kind concert brings together some of the finest musical performers in the region, bridging the gap between soaring vocal works and the innovative stylings of some truly talented instrumentalists. 
Don't miss this spectacular, star-studded gathering of local talent in the heart of Old Town Cottonwood at Old Town Center for the Arts. It is recommended that you get your tickets early for this premier fall engagement.

National Touring Artist
 Nathan McEuen - Time To Go Home Album Release Tour 
 This concert is sponsored by Best Western Cottonwood Inn. 
Raised in the heart of the American root’s music tradition, Nathan McEuen carries an incredible musical lineage as the son of John McEuen, the legendary founder of the Nitty Gritty Dirt Band. However, Nathan is an exceptional talent in his own right. Over a career built with dedication and a lifelong passion for live performance, he has produced nine independent albums and garnered a loyal following across the country. His formidable reputation has allowed him to share the stage with an astonishing roster of legendary musical icons, including Willie Nelson, Bon Jovi, Steve Martin, and Kris Kristofferson. 
Audiences attending the Cottonwood performance will experience a seasoned artist at the height of his powers celebrating his latest material. McEuen’s live performances blend top-tier storytelling with excellent acoustic guitar accompaniment, offering an intimate yet dynamic concert experience. His sets weave together folk, rock, and bluegrass traditions, filled with humor, experienced wisdom, and newly minted classics from his latest acclaimed albums. It is a rare opportunity to witness a legacy continue through a performer who embodies the finest elements of American acoustic music. 
“We are incredibly proud to bring an artist of Nathan’s caliber to the Old Town Center for the Arts,” remarked Co-Director William Eaton. “He has played at OTCA in the past with his legendary father John McEuen and as a soloist. His profound connection to roots music history combined with his unique modern voice offers our community an exceptionally rich evening of entertainment.” 
This album release tour has received great reviews, and is a must-see event for fans of acoustic music, folk, and bluegrass. The Old Town Center for the Arts, located in the heart of Cottonwood, provides an intimate acoustic setting perfectly suited to McEuen's rich vocals and intricate guitar work. Concertgoers are encouraged to get your tickets early for an experience with one of today's most captivating Americana storytellers.
