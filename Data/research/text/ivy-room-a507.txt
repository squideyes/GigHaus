About
Ivy Room is a women-owned, all-inclusive venue with an audience spanning different age groups and ethnicities, and a special home for the LGBTQ+ community. Patrons come from the Bay Area and beyond to see artists from all genres of music—rock, punk, indie, jazz, folk, Americana, funk, metal, psych rock & more! — including local acts and touring artists.
Our success is founded on a deeply held mission of creating a safe, friendly, and diverse environment for our community to express their art and enjoy a show. 
Ivy Room is a historic neighborhood bar in Albany, California that was founded in the early 1940’s. Its beloved past owner, Dottie MacBeath, managed the Ivy in the 1990’s. Her son, Bill MacBeath, booked blues, rockabilly, country, rock, and swing acts like Big Sandy & His Fly-Rite Boys, Jim Campilongo, the Loved Ones, Deke Dickerson, and Chuck Prophet. A recurring sentiment that the music community kept telling us when we took over was how we “brought the Ivy Room back to what it was and more”. 
LANI TORRES
Owner 
Lani got her start in the bar business at The Blue Lagoon in Santa Cruz where her love for live music inspired a move to New York to experience what the world had to offer. In 2001, she joined the team at CBGB’s and The Bowery Ballroom. With the East Village quickly becoming her stomping ground, Lani’s work at these two legendary venues built her foundation of how live music shows should run night after night. 
 In 2004, Lani moved to San Francisco and began working for Another Planet Entertainment at The Independent. As Bar Manager since 2008, she credits the venue with teaching her attention to detail and the ins and outs of running a successful music venue. 
Today, Lani's deep understanding of live music venues shapes the vibrant programming at the Ivy Room, where she serves as the booker for both national and local talent.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
GENERAL QUESTIONS
WHEN DO YOU OPEN? / WHAT ARE YOUR BOX OFFICE HOURS?​
We usually open an hour before our shows start. Check our schedule for specific times.
Most of our shows end by Midnight midweek and before 1am on Weekends.
You can purchase advance tickets online via our online box office or at the door any night we have a show and open. Cash only for door sales. 
​HOW ABOUT PARKING?
Free parking is available in front of the Ivy and around our neighborhood after 6pm M-F;
Free parking on Saturday and Sunday. 
​PATRON LOST AND FOUND?
You can come check if your lost item is here anytime we are open. You can also email us at info@ivyroom.com for lost items. We keep lost items for 2 weeks and then donate to Goodwill. 
WHAT ABOUT MY CREDIT CARD?​
You can pick up your card anytime we are open for shows. Please bring your Photo ID so we know its you…
Email us at info@ivyroom.com. 
BAND LOST AND FOUND?
We have a separate area for band lost and found. We will not be able to keep your gear after 2 weeks. Please come by and see if it’s here right away. No, we cannot stash your gear for your next show that you might have in 3 months. Email info@ivyroom.com for lost items. 
DO YOU SERVE FOOD? 
We have some bar snacks for sale, however you can bring your own food if you’d like. We suggest not bringing food into sold out shows since we clear the tables. 
ARE YOU ADA COMPLIANT?
Yes, our entrance, bathroom plus music and bar area are ADA compliant. Please email in advance if you would like us to save you a seat or area to see a show. info@ivyroom.com
ARE YOU AN ALL AGES VENUE?
Ivy Room is a 21+ venue. Valid Government Issued Photo ID is required to enter.
CAN I SMOKE?
Ivy Room is close to the bay and sea creatures don’t want to eat your butts. Please use our big, yellow and hard to miss receptacle. It lives 30 ft to the right of our entrance. Please respect our neighbors and do not smoke in front of open businesses, especially the restaurant next door.​
AND BRING MY DOG?
We are dog friendly for friendly dogs! But please do not bring them to packed shows. ​
DO YOU HAVE SEATING?
Ivy Room is a standing room club for shows with very limited first come first serve seating. We have a few booths and bar stools around the club. If we advertise a show as a partial seated show then we will have a few rows of seating for you to grab if you come early enough to get one. If you need special seating we can provide one for you. We will have a few special seating options available for each show. Email us in advance if you need special accommodations.
DO YOU TAKE CREDIT CARDS?
We take credit cards for bar, snack and merch purchases. We do not take credit cards for show ticket purchases. If you’d like to purchase a ticket with a credit card you can buy tickets for all our shows on our website. Please bring cash for tickets purchased at the door the night of the show. 
DO YOU HAVE AN ATM?
Yes, our ATM is located to the left of the front door and right across from the bar. 
HOW DO I AVOID ONLINE TICKET FEES?
You can purchase FEE FREE tickets at the bar at Doors same day. You can also now purchase advance tickets, cash only, at the door for future shows.
IS THE SHOW TONIGHT REALLY SOLD OUT?
We might be able to release more tickets before the headliner goes on, but we’ll have to see if there are any no-shows with our pre-purchased ticket list before we can release. Feel free to email us at info@ivyroom.com. 
WHEN DO THE BANDS GO ON?
We typically have bands start 45 minutes after doors are listed. Show time listed on show listing is when the first band starts. 
I'M NOT 21 YET, CAN I STILL COME?
Sorry, we're 21+ only, with no exceptions.
CAN I GET A REFUND FOR TICKETS? 
All sales are final and non-refundable except in the event of a show being cancelled. All questions and concerns for tickets purchased online can be emailed to info@ivyroom.com. If you would like to transfer tickets for any reason, please contact info@ivyroom.com. If you purchased tickets at the Ivy feel free to email us at info@ivyroom.com. We will gladly transfer tickets for most shows. If we have WILLIE NELSON or JAWBREAKER performing here we will have the show listed as non transferable so scalpers can’t buy up all the tickets. You will see that info listed when purchasing. 
IS THERE A DRESS CODE?
No, just wear shoes. because, eww gross.
CAN I TAKE PHOTOS AND VIDEOS?
You can! No press pass needed. We reserve the right to use them as well. 
ARE YOU HIRING?
Feel free to reach out at info@ivyroom.com. Send your resume or just write us something cool about yourself. 
DO YOU HAVE A MAILING LIST?
Yes, feel free to sign up on the bottom of our page for our weekly lowdown on upcoming shows and special last minute surprise shows. 
CAN I RENT IVY ROOM FOR A PRIVATE EVENT?
Yes, please head over to the Private Events tab and tell us what you're looking for.
I HAVE MORE QUESTIONS, WHO CAN I ASK?
Okay, sure... send us a line...
BANDS
WANT TO PLAY IVY ROOM?
We want you to, but first make sure you've hit all our requirements. Here's what we look for...
A healthy draw. We look for artists who can regularly bring people to watch them perform in the 100-200 range. How were your last 5 shows? Don't forget to be honest. If you have a bill you want to pitch us that can bring out 100-200 collectively, that would count. We tend to host local bands we haven’t worked with (yet) on weekdays and then move them into supporting slots on national act bills, if you have a draw. 
Weekday gig: We're looking for 3-4 bands, with each band bringing in 15ish people. Figure a person or two, or three for each member, is pretty reasonable. Have you played with any bands that would be a good fit to have join you? Bring us a full bill and we’ll find you an open date.
Excellent social media activity. Are you always busy posting photos and talking up your shows? We don't think 'likes' are the end-all, be-all, but it's great to know you and your bandmates promote as much as we do. 
You are excited to play our room. Ivy Room is a must-play in the East Bay, and we are working hard to band together and support the local music community. We like working with folks who feel the same way.
You are down to preserve your draw. Meaning, you don't have shows booked in the East Bay or San Francisco 2 weeks before or after any requested date at Ivy Room. 
CAN WE PROJECT OUR VISUALS?
​Yes, but you would need to bring your own projector. We can hang our sheet/screen for you. 
DO YOU HAVE A HOUSE BACKLINE?
We have gear available for a small fee. Feel free to ask for info from booking team.
HOW DO I BOOK A SHOW?
Visit our BAND BOOKING page for more info and/or send an email to lani@ivyroom.com or booking@ivyroom.com
