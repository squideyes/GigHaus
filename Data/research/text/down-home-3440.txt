“Entertainment ranges from regional acts to nationally known stars.” — New York Times
Come listen and enjoy the music played in one of the South’s premiere venues!
Music is a common ground for all kinds of folks and that’s why The Down Home’s “come as you are” atmosphere has truly been a treasure to so many people. Established in 1976 to provide a listening, intimate setting for a wide range of artists, from Jerry Douglas, Old Crow Medicine Show, Larry Sparks to Billy Strings. We've had theater, political rallies, writer’s series, poetry slams, all night parties. Come as soon as the doors open and enjoy the ambience of a room surrounded in oak wood. We got a menu of some delicious items so you won’t be hungry while you’re enjoying the great music.

 Ticket Information
Advance Tickets
Purchase tickets here on the website by clicking on the show of your interest. Follow the prompt to purchasing tickets
By Mail
Send a check for the ticket show you’d like to come see. Please be advised if the show gets sold out before we receive your mail we can not process your check. Tickets purchased by mail will be processed with your name and placed on our paid list at the door.
The Down Home
Alternatively, you can drop an envelope with a check in the mailbox at the Down Home at 300 West Main Street Johnson City, TN 37604 (located to the right of our glass doors). 
Receiving Any Confirmation
Please ensure that your mailed check will be received a good ten days before the show, allowing for processing and availability.
You can reach us at 423-929-9822 and confirm we received your payment and still had tickets available when your check was received.

Authenticity + Character… we are 100% about bringing good music to good folks.
Still going strong, The Down Home is one of the country’s longest running music venues.
Founded in 1976, The Down Home has become a mecca for music lovers in Upper East Tennessee and Southwest Virginia. The primary emphasis is on quality music, and the performance atmosphere promotes listening rather than socializing. There is plenty of time for friendly conversation before the show and between sets. With an excellent sound system hung from the ceiling, the club is a favorite of performers as well as listeners.
