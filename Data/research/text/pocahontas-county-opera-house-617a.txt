Join us at the Pocahontas County Opera House
We Are the Cultural Heart of the Community
The Pocahontas County Opera House is a 250 seat historic theatre located in Marlinton, West Virginia. The Pocahontas County Opera House Foundation puts on an annual performance series to bring in both local and international musicians, theater groups, and other performers to Pocahontas County, West Virginia.
THE JUDY CHOPS
SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 21 AT 7PM
Virginia’s The Judy Chops mix swing, blues, soul, rock and Americana with strong vocals and a lively horn section. The Shenandoah Valley band brings polished musicianship and a loose, celebratory energy to the stage.

BLACK MOUNTAIN BLUEGRASS BOYS
SATURDAY, DECEMBER 12 AT 7PM
A beloved Pocahontas County tradition returns with West Virginia’s longest-running bluegrass band, led by Richard Hefner. Hard-driving banjo, fiddle and mandolin, traditional harmonies and familiar faces make this December concert feel like a homecoming.

OUROS WITH DANNY KNICELY
SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 7 AT 7PM
Ouros and Virginia multi-instrumentalist Danny Knicely bring Appalachian and Latin American traditions together through acoustic playing, rich vocal harmonies and improvisation. Songs in Spanish and English connect flamenco, bluegrass, jazz and global roots.

PRESSING STRINGS
SATURDAY, OCTOBER 24 AT 7PM
Annapolis trio Pressing Strings blends roots rock, blues, folk and soul with expressive vocals and three-part harmonies. Guitarist Jordan Sokel, bassist Nick Welker and drummer Justin Kruger bring a warm, groove-filled sound to the Opera House.

West Virginia pianist Kelton Boblits brings ragtime, blues, boogie-woogie and early jazz to the Opera House. Expect rolling rhythms and lively old-time piano from this two-time junior world champion.

Pocahontas County Opera House season tickets
ON SALE NOW
Buy an Early Bird Season Ticket before September and save over 20% on the door price for all Opera House events this year. Starting in September, get a season ticket for $125 that admits you to every performance in the season’s series.
Friends pass
Back by popular demand, the Pocahontas County Opera House is again offering a Friends Pass that, for $50, gives six admissions for the price of five. This punch-card pass can be used to bring six friends to one show, three friends to two shows, two friends to three shows, or… you get the idea.
Ordering a Season Ticket or Friends Pass for yourself or as a gift for a friend or family member is a wonderful way to show your support for keeping the performing arts alive and well in Marlinton and Pocahontas County.
By purchasing a Season Ticket or Friends Pass, you acknowledge and agree to the terms and conditions outlined below.
Purchase by phone:
You can also call us to purchase your season tickets or friends passes. You can reach us at 304-799-6645. Please feel free to leave a message and we will get back to you.
purchase by mail:
Please send a check made payable to the Pocahontas County Opera House Foundation to:
Pocahontas County Opera House Foundation
Season Tickets
P.O. Box 282
Marlinton, WV 24954
Please make sure to include your address and phone number.
Purchase On-line:
Terms and Conditions
By purchasing and using your ticket or pass at the Pocahontas County Opera House, you agree to the following terms and conditions:
Season Pass:
Entitles the pass holder to one complimentary admission to each event in the Pocahontas County Opera House Performance Series from July 2024 to June 2025.
This pass is non-transferable and can only be used by one person.
Half Season Pass:
Entitles the holder to one complimentary admission to 7 selected shows from February to May 2025.
This pass is non-transferable and can only be used by one person.
Friends Pass:
Allows the holder any combination of up to six admissions to one or more Opera House events.
The pass must be presented for punching at the ticket desk.
General Conditions for All Tickets and Passes:
Your Season Pass, Half Season Pass, or Friends Pass must be presented at the door on the night of the scheduled event to receive admission.
Tickets and passes are valid only for events in the Pocahontas County Opera House Performance Series.
Season Passes and Half Season Passes are non-transferable and can only be used by one person.
Tickets and passes should be treated like cash; replacements will not be issued. If you lose your ticket or pass, you may purchase a new one at a pro-rated value.
The Opera House will not issue refunds for shows canceled due to weather or by the performer. However, your ticket will entitle you to admission to any performance rescheduled or substituted by the Opera House under such circumstances.
Seating is on a first-come, first-served basis.
Note that private groups and individuals often rent the Opera House and may produce public or semi-public events with their own admission charges. Your Season Pass, Half Season Pass, or Friends Pass does not grant free admission to these events.
Thank you for understanding and for your support of our community's vibrant arts scene.
Feel free to let me know if any other adjustments are needed.

Support the Pocahontas County Opera House
Over twenty years ago, the Opera House was just a dusty old building and the dream of a handful of determined people. The vision and enthusiasm of those people quickly spread, and with the help of the local community, the Opera House has since become a thriving center for live music, theatre and dance, as well as an affordable community gathering place for special events. 
We want you to be a part of the great things happening here at the Opera House. Ticket sales cover only a fraction of our annual operating expenses. Your support will help the Opera House to continue bringing great music and performances to Marlinton and Pocahontas County. The Pocahontas County Opera House Foundation is a non-profit 501c3 organization. 
How Your Contribution Helps
Your donations play a vital role in sustaining the Opera House and enabling us to:
Host diverse performances.
Support local and visiting artists.
Maintain and upgrade our historic venue.
Offer educational and outreach programs.

Tax Credits:
We also have an added incentive to offer our supporters. Individuals and businesses who donate $500 or more may be eligible for West Virginia state tax credits through the Neighborhood Investment Program (NIP). Fifty percent of your donation may be taken as an actual credit on your West Virginia state taxes. This is in addition to the federal and state deductions allowed for charitable contributions. We have a limited number of tax credits, which will be awarded on a first come, first served basis.
 Pocahontas County Opera House Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Your contribution is tax-deductible to the extent allowed by the law.
