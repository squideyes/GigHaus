Our mission...
To provide a home for musicians from near and far, help cultivate the RVA music scene and provide a safe place for guests, musicians and employees to have beer, play a song or earn a paycheck- every night of the week.
Booking
If you are a band interested in playing The Camel, please contact booking@thecamel.org.
Box Office
Avoid the fees! Physical tickets are now available at The Camel during normal hours of operation. Ask for more information at the bar. Remote box office locations coming soon.
Advancing Your Show
If you need to advance an upcoming show, contact booking@thecamel.org. Shows are to be advanced no less than 48 hours prior to show night.

Online
Tickets are available here on our site. Just click on the TICKETS button next to the show you wish to attend and follow the prompts. We accept Visa, Mastercard, AmEx, and Discover for online ticket sales.
At The Door
If tickets are not sold out, they will be available for purchase at door time for each show. Cash or card accepted.
Box Office
Tickets are available for purchase during hours of operation
Age Policy
Shows are all ages unless otherwise noted. *Concert goers under the age of 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian*
Sold Out Shows
To ensure that you are guaranteed entry to the show, we recommend that you purchase your tickets in advance. Once a show is sold out, we can’t do anything to help you, other than allow access to one individual each time one leaves. This scenario is avoidable by buying in advance.
Seating
All shows are General Admission. There is some seating on the event side available on a first come first serve basis.

Private Events & Venue Rentals (Spellslingers Speakeasy™)
The Camel is ready to host YOUR next private event! We can offer a full service package to include the room rental, catering, drinks (alcohol and non-alcoholic), as well as full stage production (even securing a band!). 
Pricing
Please contact us for additional info and availability. We will do everything we can to accommodate you and your needs. catering@thecamel.org
Production
Included with your rental is access to the in house production. The Camel has a raised stage with a professional sound and lighting system. There is also an HD projector and projection screen on the stage. The screen can easily raise and lower at anytime during your event. 
Bar Options
The Camel offers a number of options for Drink packages during your event.
Open Bar – Your guests are welcome to all the beer, liquor, wine, and soda options The Camel has to offer and the price is by consumption. We will work with you to get a solid price estimate going into the event and can even set a limit for when to cut the open bar off (switching immediately to cash bar so the party never stops!).
Limited Open Bar – We will work with you to pair down the options to whatever makes the most sense for you and your guests. That may be 2 draft beers, 2 wines, house liquor, or a few bottle beer selections. The options are endless! Similar to the full open bar, we will get you a price estimate going into the event and can set a limit for when to cut the limited open bar off (also switching to cash bar then).
Cash Bar – Your guests are welcome any of the beer, liquor, wine, and soda options The Camel has to offer. They will be responsible for their own tabs.

WNRN Presents: Jesse Roper
 w/ Big Tobacco, Tyler Meacham (solo)
 Friday, November 13
 at The Camel
 Doors 9pm, Show 9:30pm 
Jesse Roper is a modern-day troubadour; a Canadian artist defined by his authenticity and adventurous spirit; guitar in hand and a grin on his face. 
Jesse, along with long-time bandmates the Pretty Good Band, has spent much of the last few years travelling the highways, byways and backroads of North America, bringing his own brand of modern, indie-infused Americana blues to fervent audiences across the continent. Known for his powerhouse live performances, Jesse is truly electrifying on stage - his soulful vocals expanding across silky guitars and hard-hitting grooves. You’d never guess that this soldier of the six string - all mischief, mayhem and virtuoso guitar playing - experienced crippling stage-fright long into his 20s. Today, the stage is his home and he’s inviting you in, cold one in hand, for one helluva good time. 
Of course, he’s more than a magnetising stage presence and great hair - Jesse has the goods to back this up. A master at connecting his personal experiences to his songwriting, he crafts wildly entertaining narratives of the challenges and eccentricities of life, pulling inspiration from all that surrounds him. His musings on love (both won and lost), the call of the road and the promise of what's round the corner draw similarities to JJ Cale, Leon Bridges, and Alabama Shakes. His innate musical talent and flexibility of style have seen him play with soul icon Booker T. Jones, rock veteran Colin James, blues belter Beth Hart, and Canadian legend Burton Cummings. Across streaming platforms, he’s racked up over 500,000 monthly listeners and millions of streams. 
While based in Victoria BC, Jesse is back on the road with the Pretty Good Band throughout 2026. All seasoned musicians themselves, the band is Matt Reid (bass), Chris Van Sickle (keys), East Coast Pete (guitar) and Brent Norman (drums). “Each one of these guys on their own is funnier and smarter than I am, but somehow I’m the band leader,” Jesse laughs. “I’m a lucky lead guy to have these cats in my band. They’re Pretty Good.”
