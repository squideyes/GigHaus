City Winery Boston is a premier live music venue offering an intimate setting for unforgettable performances. Enjoy live music nearly every night, from rock, R&B, and jazz concerts to stand-up comedy shows and live podcast recordings. Over the years, our venue has welcomed legendary artists including Melissa Etheridge, Chrisette Michele, Lyle Lovett, Eric Benet, Musiq Soulchild, Vanessa Carlton, Robert Glasper, and many more to our stage. As a leading Boston concert venue, we pair world-class live entertainment with dinner service and award-winning wines for a true dinner and show experience.
Browse our Boston concert calendar to discover upcoming concerts, stand-up comedy shows, and special performances throughout the month. Whether planning a date night or searching for live music or concerts in Boston, City Winery delivers exceptional performances in a seated venue in Downtown Boston near the North End.
 Plan Your Visit
Directions, Parking & Dining at City Winery Boston
City Winery Boston is located at 80 Beverly Street in Boston’s historic North End neighborhood, steps away from Faneuil Hall Marketplace and the TD Garden Arena and entertainment complex. Whether you're coming for live music, a comedy show, or dinner and a show, getting here is easy.
Parking: Discounted parking is available through the Haymarket Garage for $3 up to three hours. Please present the ticket that you receive at the garage to our front desk team to validate. Additional paid lots are available throughout the North End and Downtown Boston area.
Public Transit: City Winery Boston is easily accessible via public transportation. For MBTA riders, we are located within walking distance to the Haymarket Station and North Station MBTA subway stops. There are also several MBTA bus routes with stops in the area.
Rideshare: Uber, Lyft, and other rideshare services frequently drop off and pick up guests directly in front of the venue, making it convenient to arrive or leave after a show. Rideshare services are widely available throughout Downtown Boston and the North End.
 FAQ
 Where is City Winery Boston located?
City Winery Boston is located at 80 Beverly Street in Downtown Boston, one of the city’s most popular dining and entertainment areas. The venue sits in the heart of downtown, surrounded by restaurants, bars, and nightlife. Many guests visit City Winery for a full evening experience, combining dinner, wine, and live music in one location.
 What types of concerts and shows happen at City Winery Boston?
City Winery Boston hosts a diverse lineup of live music, comedy shows, podcast recordings, brunch performances, and special events throughout the year. Our calendar regularly features national touring artists, singer-songwriters, jazz musicians, tribute concerts, and curated local performances. Because of the venue’s intimate concert setting, audiences are able to experience performances up close in a seated environment.
 Can I order food and wine during the show?
Yes. City Winery Boston is known for our dinner and a show experience, allowing guests to enjoy full dinner service and award-winning wines during most performances. Guests can order food and drinks directly from their table while watching the show, or arrive early to enjoy the restaurant or wine bar before the performance begins.
 Is seating reserved at City Winery concerts?
Most shows feature reserved seating so guests can relax and enjoy the performance with food and wine service throughout the evening.
 What time do doors open for shows?
Door times can vary depending on the event, but guests are typically able to enter the venue 60 to 90 minutes before showtime. Arriving early is recommended if you plan to order dinner or drinks before the performance begins.
 What makes City Winery different from other Boston concert venues?
City Winery Boston offers a unique concert and dining experience that combines live music, award-winning wines, and chef-driven cuisine under one roof. Unlike many traditional music venues, guests can enjoy a full meal and wine service while watching the performance. The venue’s intimate concert hall also allows audiences to experience artists in a closer, more personal setting than many larger Boston venues.
 What is the concert experience like at City Winery Boston?
City Winery Boston offers an intimate concert experience where guests can enjoy live performances in a seated venue with table service. Unlike larger concert halls, the space is designed to bring audiences closer to the music, allowing guests to enjoy dinner, wine, and cocktails while watching the show. The venue’s acoustics and smaller capacity make it a favorite for fans looking to experience artists in a more personal setting.
 Do shows frequently sell out?
Because City Winery Boston is an intimate venue with limited seating, many concerts and comedy shows sell out in advance. Popular touring artists, tribute performances, and special events often fill up quickly. Guests interested in attending a show are encouraged to check the event calendar regularly and purchase tickets early to secure their preferred seating.
