 About The Hook & Ladder 
The Hook & Ladder 
A fiercely independent venue where artists, audiences, and community come together. 
Our Story

Located in the historic Firehouse #21 in South Minneapolis' Longfellow neighborhood, The Hook & Ladder Event Center is a community-rooted, artist-driven venue built around three unique stages and a simple belief: live music brings people together. 
Home to The Theater, The Zen Arcade Stage & Lounge, and Under The Canopy, The Hook offers a diverse range of experiences, from intimate listening-room performances and canna-forward lounge events to full-production concerts and outdoor summer festivals. Whether you're discovering a new favorite artist, celebrating a milestone, attending a fundraiser, or dancing under the stars, The Hook is a place where connection happens. 
Operated by Nobool Presents, The Hook has been serving Minnesota's creative community since 2016 by providing performance opportunities, event space, and a platform for artists, organizers, nonprofits, and community leaders. As proud members of both NIVA (National Independent Venue Association) and MNIVA (Minnesota Independent Venue Association), we remain committed to supporting independent artists and preserving vibrant local culture. 
The Hook presents a wide variety of programming, including live music, comedy, film, dance, visual arts, literary events, culinary experiences, educational workshops, community gatherings, and multidisciplinary performances. We are dedicated to maintaining an inclusive, welcoming, and accessible environment for artists and audiences of all backgrounds, identities, ages, and abilities. 
Beyond concerts, The Hook hosts nonprofit fundraisers, community celebrations, outdoor festivals, album and book releases, private parties, weddings, memorials, corporate events, and everything in between. Our flexible spaces, professional production capabilities, and experienced staff allow us to create memorable experiences for groups of all sizes. 
As a venue rooted in community values, we also strive to reduce our environmental impact through solar power, sustainability initiatives, and low-waste event practices whenever possible. 
More than a venue, The Hook & Ladder is a gathering place for music lovers, artists, neighbors, and changemakers. A place where creativity thrives, community grows, and live experiences still matter. Independent. Inclusive. Community-rooted. 
Our Community Partners 
Building a stronger community through music, art, and collective action. 
The Hook & Ladder Event Center believes that live music and the arts can be powerful tools for connection, healing, and positive change. That's why we're proud to partner with local nonprofits and community organizations working to make arts access, mental health resources, cultural programming, and community support available to everyone. 
Throughout the year, we collaborate with organizations that share our values of creativity, inclusion, education, wellness, and social impact. Together, we're helping strengthen the communities we serve while creating opportunities for people to connect through meaningful experiences. 
When purchasing tickets, guests have the option to make a donation at checkout to support our nonprofit partners and community initiatives. Every contribution helps expand access to the arts, support vital community programs, and ensure that creative spaces remain accessible for future generations. 
Because a thriving music scene starts with a thriving community. A stronger arts community creates a stronger Minneapolis. Meet Our Partners

Venue Policies 
Age Policy 
Most shows are all-ages unless noted otherwise on the event page. Patrons under 18 must be accompanied by a guardian after 9pm. 
ID 
A valid photo ID is required for alcohol service. We accept government-issued IDs only. 
Re-Entry 
Re-entry is permitted with a wristband for most events. Re-entry may be restricted for sold-out shows — check with the box office. 
Bags & Prohibited Items 
Small bags are welcome. No outside food, drink, professional cameras, or weapons. All patrons are subject to a bag check at entry. 
Refunds & Exchanges 
All ticket sales are final. In the event of a cancellation, refunds are issued to the original method of payment. 
Photography 
Personal photography is welcome. Professional photographers (detachable lenses, flash, video) must be approved in advance. 
Code of Conduct 
Everyone deserves to feel safe, welcome, and respected at The Hook. 
The Hook & Ladder Event Center is committed to fostering an environment where all people are treated with dignity and respect, free from discrimination, harassment, intimidation, or harm. Whether you're an artist, audience member, staff member, volunteer, vendor, or guest, we expect everyone to contribute to a culture of inclusion, kindness, and accountability. 
We proudly partner with Squeerity to help support a safer and more welcoming environment. The Hook & Ladder reserves the right to refuse entry, remove individuals from the venue, or deny future attendance and performance opportunities to anyone who violates this Code of Conduct. 
By purchasing a ticket and/or entering The Hook & Ladder Event Center, you agree to abide by these guidelines. Violations may result in immediate removal from the venue without refund. 
The Following Behavior Is Not Welcome at The Hook 
Sexual harassment, misconduct, or unwanted sexual attention of any kind, including verbal, non-verbal, or physical conduct. 
Unwanted physical contact, intimidation, or invasion of another person's personal boundaries. 
Racist, sexist, ableist, transphobic, homophobic, xenophobic, or otherwise discriminatory language, actions, or behavior. 
Aggressive, threatening, abusive, stalking, or harassing behavior toward any guest, artist, staff member, or volunteer. 
Abuse of power, including behavior that exploits or intimidates others based on status, wealth, race, gender, sexuality, position, or influence. 
Violence, threats of violence, or conduct that creates an unsafe environment for others. 
While at The Hook, We Ask That You 
Help create a community-oriented atmosphere where the safety and well-being of others is valued. 
Respect the physical, emotional, and personal boundaries of fellow guests, artists, and staff. 
Celebrate diversity and contribute to an inclusive environment for everyone. 
Reject discriminatory, harassing, or violent behavior. 
Take responsibility for your actions and understand their impact on others. 
Look out for one another and notify venue staff if you witness behavior that makes someone feel unsafe. 
If You Need Assistance 
If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please notify a staff member, manager, security team member, or Squeerity representative immediately. We take all reports seriously and will respond appropriately. Together, we can create a space where music, creativity, community, and respect thrive. 
Accessibility & Safe Spaces 
Everyone is welcome. Everyone deserves access. 
The Hook & Ladder Event Center is committed to creating a welcoming, inclusive, and accessible experience for all guests, artists, staff, and community members. 
All venue spaces, including The Theater, The Zen Arcade, and Under The Canopy, are accessible via ramps and level-access entrances. Accessible restrooms are available on the main floor, and designated accessible viewing areas are available during performances. 
We offer a variety of seating and standing options throughout the venue. If you need assistance upon arrival, please connect with a member of our staff and we'll be happy to help. 
Accessibility Services 
To request accommodations such as: 
American Sign Language (ASL) Interpretation 
Voice Captioning Services 
Hearing Assistance Devices 
Accessible Seating Arrangements 
Other Accessibility Accommodations 
Please contact us as early as possible. We request a minimum of one week's notice for ASL interpretation and at least 72 hours' notice for most other accommodation requests. 
At this time, we are unable to provide programs in Braille. 
Service animals are always welcome. Emotional support animals that are not trained service animals are not permitted.

Clean Plate Club with Becca Murray & The Wildflowers and Auronova 
 Date Friday, November 20, 2026 
 Doors 6:00 PM 
 Show 7:00 PM 
 Room Theater 
 Ages 18+ 
 Get Tickets All Events 
Hailing from Minneapolis, Minnesota, Clean Plate Club is a pop/soul and jazz-funk band bringing infectious grooves, rich harmonies, and high-energy performances to the Twin Cities music scene. Drawing inspiration from 70s legends like Earth, Wind & Fire, Michael Jackson, and Tower of Power, as well as modern funk revivalists Cory Wong, Couch, and Lawrence, Clean Plate Club delivers a sound that’s both nostalgic and fresh. 
Formed by University of Minnesota Jazz I students, the group’s chemistry and musicianship quickly set them apart. In early 2025, Clean Plate Club made waves at the U of M Battle of the Bands, winning their first round and earning a spot at Spring Jam 2025. 
That same year, they released their debut single “Tourist Trap” — a bright, groove-heavy track that captures their signature blend of tight rhythm sections and soulful hooks. The band has since brought their dynamic live show to Minneapolis favorites including White Squirrel, Day Block, and Amsterdam Bar & Hall. 
Beyond the stage, Clean Plate Club connects with fans through their playful short-form content on Instagram and TikTok, often filmed while literally sitting on the floor — a fitting reflection of their down-to-earth vibe and creative spirit. 
Becca Murray & the Wildflowers 
Formed in Fall of 2025, Becca Murray and the Wildflowers are a five-piece indie pop band crafting soulful songs about connection, growth, and staying true to oneself. Led by Becca Murray’s heartfelt vocals and reflective songwriting, the band’s rich grooves are driven by warm sax, keys, and guitar melodies to create music that feels both nostalgic and deeply personal. 
AuroNova is an igh-Energy, 8-Piece Funk Band only playing the greatest hits from the 60's to the 2020's! Led by singer Naomi Tschernach, this local crew kicks these classics into the next gear with fresh, never-before-heard arrangements of the tunes you know and love.

GETDOWN & GIVE - The Brothers Allmanac • Shoeless Revolution • Shane Cox & the Funky Spuds 
 Date Saturday, November 28, 2026 
 Doors 7:00 PM 
 Show 7:30 PM 
 Room Theater 
 Ages 21+ 
 Get Tickets All Events 
Community Donation Drive 
Get down for a great cause! We're teaming up with Community Kindness Project, a local mutual aid group supporting our unhoused neighbors, to collect essential supplies for those in need. Bring new or gently used coats, hats, gloves, shelf-stable food, and other winter essentials to the show. Every donation helps spread a little warmth, kindness, and community love! 
Dance. Donate. Make a Difference. 
The Brothers Allmanac is a top-tier Allman Brothers tribute band, known for their authentic renditions of Southern rock classics. With standout performances at iconic venues, they capture the essence of The Allman Brothers' signature sound—complete with soulful guitar harmonies, powerful vocals, and extended jams. Faithfully recreating the hits and also digging into lesser known deep cuts, The Brothers Allmanac delivers a high-energy experience that pays homage to one of rock & roll's greatest bands. 
Shoeless Revolution is a Twin Cities band built for the dance floor and the stage. Reed Grimm, Jack Kolb-Williams, Ben Peterson, and guitarist David Feily combine rock grit, jam-heavy grooves, and R&B soul with a level of musicianship that turns every show into an intricate, alive performance. Soaring guitar solos, lyrical sax lines, and rich vocal harmonies weave seamlessly through tight rhythms and extended jams, keeping the energy high and the crowd moving. Rooted in the Twin Cities music scene, Shoeless Revolution delivers nights full of melody, movement, and musical chemistry that sticks long after the last note. 
Shane Cox & the Funky Spuds bring a fresh groove to the Twin Cities scene in their mission to elevate the sousaphone and brass-driven music. With a sound that fuses jazz, funk, and a little New Orleans swagger, the band unites some of Minneapolis’ top jazz and funk talent: David Feily (BZ3 Organ Trio, Rhythm Street Movement) on guitar, Pete Suttman (Dr. Mambo’s Combo) on drums, Frank Merchlewitz (Your Smith) on keys, Andrew Gillespie on percussion, and Ryan Freitason saxophone. Frontman Shane Cox anchors the group with his dynamic work on trumpet, trombone, and sousaphone. Drawing inspiration from a diverse array of genres with bands like T-Square, John Scofield, and Trombone Shorty, The Funky Spuds blend tight grooves, bold brass, and infectious energy.

Follow the Firefly Album Release w/ Guest Matt Wilson 
 Date Saturday, November 21, 2026 
 Doors 7:00 PM 
 Show 7:30 PM 
 Room Zen Arcade 
 Ages 21+ 
 Get Tickets All Events 
Matt Wilson starts the night off with a solo set and then Follow the Firefly takes the stage to celebrate the release of their debut album on Pravda Records. 
Follow the Firefly is an imaginative trio comprised of guitar, bass, harp and voices - chamber pop at its finest. The trio twists and turns through female empowered singer-songwriter originals, musical haikus, traditional music from Eastern Europe and the occasional cover song. Follow the Firefly is: Phala Tracy, Natalie Nowytski and Scott Keever. 
Matt Wilson 
There are a few Matt Wilsons out there making music. I'm the one from Minneapolis. Some people know me from the bands Trip Shakespeare or the Twilight Hours. I've been writing music non-stop since I was a boy. Nowadays I record with a harp, banjo and electric bass combo that I have come to call an orchestra. And on rare occasions I play alone.
