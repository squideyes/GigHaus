Lakeview Houseconcerts in Sharon, MA Presents: 
Heather Pierson Trio
 6 PM Sunday Nov 15th 2026 Potluck lunch at 5 PM
RSVP to info at Lakeviewhouseconcerts.org to reserve a seat. You must get an invitation email from me to attend.
For more info see heatherpierson.com
Call 508 528 0026 for questions, please leave a message.
$25 suggested donation in the tip jar but pay what you are able. 100% of the donations go directly to the artists. There are no tickets, but please RSVP so I know how many will be here. Join us at 5PM here for a potluck lunch. Bring a dish, we supply drinks and ice cream at intermission.
"If you're one of those people who likes any style done well-- old-school jazz, folk, pop, classical--you'll love this prodigiously talented threesome. Keyboards, brass, all sorts of strings, and beautiful singing make for a dynamic show packed with energy and surprises." 
Sarah Craig | Caffe Lena | Saratoga Springs, NY

Mark Piepkorn | Stage 33 Live | Bellows Falls, VT "The audience at Music on Centre in Boston was dazzled by their introduction to the versatility and vitality of the Heather Pierson Trio.
