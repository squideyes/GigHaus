 Can I invest?
We are so grateful for the outpouring of love and support after we recently announced our plan to close our shop in Pasadena later this fall. We love what we do, and believe a space like ours should exist. After hearing from so many of you, we feel you believe that too. We are here to ask for your help to keep the story going into the next (and more sustainable) chapter of HFOTU.
What began as a record store became one of our cities’ most intimate and beloved homes for live music, learning, and community. Now we have an opportunity to give that community a permanent home. Here’s the plan:
Healing Force of the Universe moves its sanctuary for music from Pasadena to the historic G-Son Studios in the vibrant heart of Atwater Village, and the world rejoices.
Along with the move comes Healing Force’s warm hospitality, excellent sound, and deep attention to the feeling of a room to create meaningful nights that feel human, immersive, and resonant.
G-Son - home to the Beastie Boys’ legendary creative headquarters in the 1990s - once again becomes a clubhouse to listen, discover, learn, collect, dance, and feel human again. 
And together we build a cultural institution for Los Angeles and the world. 
Our Pasadena home gave us an incredible beginning, but we've reached its limits. The room is too small for many of the artists we'd like to present, the business model isn't sustainable long-term, and we've simply outgrown the space.
G-Son gives us room to grow: a licensed bar that supports the business, nearly double the concert capacity while remaining intimate, a larger classroom, a proper green room for artists, a recording studio, more rental opportunities, and a walkable neighborhood where the record store can become part of everyday life.
What is G-Son Studios: G-Son Studios is a historic creative compound upstairs on Glendale Boulevard in Atwater Village best known as the Beastie Boys’ recording studio, rehearsal space, clubhouse, and headquarters during the 1990s. Originally, the building was the Atwater Community Center - a ballroom and gathering place for the neighborhood in the early 1900s. In 1991, the Beastie Boys transformed it into G-Son Studios after the high cost of making Paul’s Boutique convinced them they needed a permanent creative home. They converted the ballroom into a space where they could rehearse, record, experiment, skateboard, play basketball, and collaborate without the pressure of commercial studio time. 
Today, we have the opportunity to continue that legacy by transforming G-Son into a new home for independent music and culture.
With your help, we'd like to build a music clubhouse at G-Son Studios: a record store that transforms throughout the day. The space will welcome morning wellness classes, daytime record shopping, after-school programs and happy hour, evening concerts, and late-night dance parties. 
In this partnership, HFOTU will bring its operational ability to host and curate great artists in front of new and familiar audiences in a beautiful, welcoming environment to celebrate Music’s present. G-Son will provide an incredible facility with a licensed bar in a prime retail and nightlife neighborhood and the legacy of its iconic Music history. And together, we will discover Music’s future.
We do need to raise some money! We will be launching a crowdfunding campaign this week; stay tuned - AND we are looking for mission-aligned investors to launch this endeavor. Please click a button above and reach out if someone - or anything - comes to mind.
If it doesn’t come through, we are extremely proud and grateful for all of the experiences we’ve shared, and look forward to our last season with you here in Pasadena. It’s gonna be a greatest hits for the next few months.
Whether you invest, volunteer, introduce us to someone, come to shows, manifest, work magic, pray, or simply tell a friend, every act of support helps Music work. Thanks for giving us the courage to dream.
WE LOVE YOU.
Peace and love,
Austin & Healing Force of the Universe team

Music Improv Workshop
with Seth Ford-Young
Music Improv Workshop
With Seth Ford-Young (Phi-Psonics, Tom Waits, Rocco DeLuca & more) Tuesdays · September 8, 15, 22, 29 · 6:30–8:00 PM Healing Force of the Universe, Pasadena
Unlock your creativity. Step into the circle. Find your voice.
Whether you’re picking up your instrument for the first time or have been performing for decades, improvisation opens new doors. In this 4-week workshop, you’ll explore rhythm, melody, and texture through hands-on group exercises and creative prompts - designed to inspire fresh sounds, deeper listening, and real-time collaboration. All instruments and all levels welcome.
Los Angeles has one of the richest improvisational music scenes in the world - full of talented players creating in the moment. If you’ve ever thought, “I wish I could jump in… but I’m not ready yet” - this workshop is for you. Over four weeks, you’ll gain the skills, ears, and confidence to not only join the conversation but shape it.
From Seth: “This is for composers, singer-songwriters, and instrumentalists who want to open up their ears to new sounds and find ideas, or arrange their pieces. Even in written-out music, there’s room for interpretation - micro-improvs that bring the music to life in a new way.”
Who This Is For
Composers & songwriters seeking new ideas
Performers looking to add nuance and spontaneity
Beginners curious about making music
Experienced players wanting fresh approaches
What You’ll Do
Play with new people and discover new musical expressions
Follow group prompts designed to inspire new ways to approach your instrument
Learn to listen deeply and know when not to play
From Seth: “Students will explore new aspects of their voice and instruments as musicians and the different sounds and approaches one can get out of them. If you’re writing your own music, I hope this will open up different colors and approaches to writing.”
The Format
Small group — 10 students max
4 Mondays: September 8, 15, 22, 29
6:30–8:00 PM (90 min each session)
$140 for all 4 classes (6 hours total)
Each session:
Q&A and quick setup
Verbal intro to the week’s idea
Group exercises built from custom prompts
Play, explore, and discuss
About Your Teacher – Seth Ford-Young
From his early days as a punk vocalist in DC to performing and recording with Tom Waits, Edward Sharpe & the Magnetic Zeros, Dave Harrington, Rocco DeLuca, and The Hot Club of San Francisco, Seth has built a career spanning genres and continents. He currently leads Phi-Psonics (Gondwana Records), crafting music that balances structure and freedom—perfect for guiding musicians into the art of improvisation.
Limited spots available – only 10 participants!

We are so grateful for the outpouring of love and support after we recently announced our plan to close our shop in Pasadena later this fall. We love what we do, and believe a space like ours should exist. After hearing from so many of you, we feel you believe that too. We are here to ask for your help to keep the story going into the next (and more sustainable) chapter of HFOTU.
What began as a record store became one of our cities’ most intimate and beloved homes for live music, learning, and community. Now we have an opportunity to give that community a permanent home. Here’s the plan:
Healing Force of the Universe moves its sanctuary for music from Pasadena to the historic G-Son Studios in the vibrant heart of Atwater Village, and the world rejoices.
Along with the move comes Healing Force’s warm hospitality, excellent sound, and deep attention to the feeling of a room to create meaningful nights that feel human, immersive, and resonant.
G-Son - home to the Beastie Boys’ legendary creative headquarters in the 1990s - once again becomes a clubhouse to listen, discover, learn, collect, dance, and feel human again. 
And together we build a cultural institution for Los Angeles and the world. 
Our Pasadena home gave us an incredible beginning, but we've reached its limits. The room is too small for many of the artists we'd like to present, the business model isn't sustainable long-term, and we've simply outgrown the space.
G-Son gives us room to grow: a licensed bar that supports the business, nearly double the concert capacity while remaining intimate, a larger classroom, a proper green room for artists, a recording studio, more rental opportunities, and a walkable neighborhood where the record store can become part of everyday life.
What is G-Son Studios: G-Son Studios is a historic creative compound upstairs on Glendale Boulevard in Atwater Village best known as the Beastie Boys’ recording studio, rehearsal space, clubhouse, and headquarters during the 1990s. Originally, the building was the Atwater Community Center - a ballroom and gathering place for the neighborhood in the early 1900s. In 1991, the Beastie Boys transformed it into G-Son Studios after the high cost of making Paul’s Boutique convinced them they needed a permanent creative home. They converted the ballroom into a space where they could rehearse, record, experiment, skateboard, play basketball, and collaborate without the pressure of commercial studio time. 
Today, we have the opportunity to continue that legacy by transforming G-Son into a new home for independent music and culture.
With your help, we'd like to build a music clubhouse at G-Son Studios: a record store that transforms throughout the day. The space will welcome morning wellness classes, daytime record shopping, after-school programs and happy hour, evening concerts, and late-night dance parties. 
In this partnership, HFOTU will bring its operational ability to host and curate great artists in front of new and familiar audiences in a beautiful, welcoming environment to celebrate Music’s present. G-Son will provide an incredible facility with a licensed bar in a prime retail and nightlife neighborhood and the legacy of its iconic Music history. And together, we will discover Music’s future.
We do need to raise some money! We will be launching a crowdfunding campaign this week; stay tuned - AND we are looking for mission-aligned investors to launch this endeavor. Please click a button above and reach out if someone - or anything - comes to mind.
If it doesn’t come through, we are extremely proud and grateful for all of the experiences we’ve shared, and look forward to our last season with you here in Pasadena. It’s gonna be a greatest hits for the next few months.
Whether you invest, volunteer, introduce us to someone, come to shows, manifest, work magic, pray, or simply tell a friend, every act of support helps Music work. Thanks for giving us the courage to dream.
WE LOVE YOU.
Peace and love,
Austin & Healing Force of the Universe team

Night 3 of the Minaret Winter Jazz Festival takes place at Healing Force’s new, expanded home at G-Son Studios in Atwater Village. The evening offers a special sneak preview of the venue, which is currently under construction ahead of its anticipated official launch in 2027. A rebuild within the historic G-Son Studios (once home to The Beastie Boys), the space marks an exciting new chapter for Healing Force and its ongoing commitment to Los Angeles independent music.
A Sunday double bill! The evening opens with a quartet led by stalwart bassist and composer Billy Mohler, featuring guitarist Jeff Parker, alto saxophonist Devin Daniels, and drummer Justin Brown. The second set brings drummer Jonathan Pinson’s Boom Clap to the stage. Led by Pinson, the collaborative ensemble features David Otis, Andrew Renfroe, Neon Phoenix, Brandon Cordoba, and Melo Gía.
Minaret is an independent jazz label and event series based in Los Angeles, founded by Egyptian-American artist Yousef Hilmy in late 2019. Symbolized from the Arabic منارة (lighthouse, source of light), we use an open source approach to publish creative music, produce concerts in LA + beyond, foster community/collaboration, and connect progressive art practices to the public.

The Wildwoods continue to bring sonic beauty and simplicity to today’s increasingly complicated and cluttered musical landscape. With Dear Meadowlark, the band’s fourth full-length album released on April 11, 2025, The Wildwoods have once again taken the hallmarks of the folk-Americana sound and dressed it in indie sensibilities a la heroes Nickel Creek and Gillian Welch as well as genre mainstays Bon Iver and Mumford & Sons.
The Wildwoods are Noah Gose on vocals, acoustic and electric guitar; his wife, Chloe Gose on vocals and violin; and longtime friend Andrew Vaggalis on vocals and upright bass. Their delicate embrace of the past, present, and future has transformed The Wildwoods into a potent entity. Recently, The Wildwoods were finalists in the 2024 Rocky Mountain Songwriter Contest in Red Lodge, Montana. In 2023, the trio enjoyed being finalists in two high-profile band competitions – FreshGrass Music Festival and VHS “Gems in the Rough.” Also, their songwriting strengths have been recognized by semi-final placements in the International Songwriting competition with tracks such as “Untitled” and “Way of Train.”
