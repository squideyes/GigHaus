Our ticket policy · Every show, no exceptions
accessforall
We ask folks to pay full price if you are able to, but every ticketed night at Nova also has a name-your-price option, aka Access for All, supported by Savings Bank of Walpole.
We want to break down the economic barriers around music, art, and culture. Your support helps us do that.
Choose Access For All on the show page and put in what you can pay, then buy for everyone you are bringing in one go.

What is Nova
A roomfrom here
Nova is a nonprofit arts space at 48 Emerald Street in downtown Keene. We book touring and local artists on the same bill, we host residencies that make art with the community instead of for it, and we keep the door open to people who can’t pay full price.
We’re interested in the combinations nobody else would try, which is how the Sun Ra Arkestra ended up on a bill with an experimental cellist, and how Yo La Tengo ended up here on a weeknight in September. Bands from away, in a room from here.

What is Nova
About Nova
Nova is a nonprofit arts space at 48 Emerald Street in downtown Keene. We book touring and local artists on the same bill, we host residencies that make art with the community instead of for it, and we keep the door open to people who can’t pay full price.
See what’s onSupport Nova
Mission
Nova Arts is a nonprofit, collaborative arts initiative focused on emerging artists, underrepresented voices, and unexpected juxtapositions. We present and partner with creative individuals, organizations, and ideas.
Nova presents, incubates, and promotes arts and culture. We make room for performances, arts education, and community collaborations that have a social impact, enhance culture, and encourage creative inquiry.
We’re interested in the combinations nobody else would try, which is how the Sun Ra Arkestra ended up on a bill with an experimental cellist, and how Yo La Tengo ended up here on a weeknight in September.
The room
Capacity. The room feels great with 20 people seated or 200 people standing.. Seating is limited and first come, first served.
Ages. Every show is all ages.
Tickets. Usually $20, general admission, with a name-your-price option on every night.
Artists. Paid fairly, and told what they’re getting before they load in.
Also here. Coffee, beer, Brewbakers, records, flowers, and more. Make a day of it.
What we run
Shows. Touring and local artists, most weeks of the year. See the calendar.
Community Collaborations. We partner with creative organizations and individuals to make an impact in our community.
The Thing in the Spring. The festival founded in Peterborough in 2008, which now calls Nova home.
SPAR(K). Our annual social practice artist residency, bringing socially engaged artists to Keene to make work with the community.
House rules
A safe space. Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, xenophobia, or ableism will not be tolerated.
All ages. Please read show descriptions to decide whether an event is right for your party. If you’re over 21 and want to be served alcohol, bring your ID.
Tickets are non-refundable. Thank you for understanding.
Who we are
Nova Arts is run by a board of directors and is fiscally sponsored by Arts Alive, which is how a donation to Nova becomes tax deductible.
Get in touch at eric.gagne@novaarts.org.

A serious night of bluegrass that doesn't take itself too seriously!PreviewMr. Bill - Blue Grass Night at Nova Arts/Brewbakers
Mr. Bill is a local bluegrass band based out of Keene, New Hampshire. They play a mix of traditional and contemporary bluegrass music along with original music and grassification of some familiar favorites from the 60s and 70s. The band consists of Steve “Zuber” Armstrong on bass, Matt Atkinson on guitar and vocals, John Meehan on mandolin and vocals, and Rich Sullivan on five string banjo.
Keene resident Josiah Sterling brings his style of melodic clawhammer banjo to play traditional tunes from the old time songbook.
With influences from Europe and Africa, old time music is a uniquely American genre whose DNA can be seen in bluegrass, country music and blues. Josiah plays a wide assortment of melodies from lively fiddle tunes to groovy backwoods rhythms.
