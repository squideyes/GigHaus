November 29, 2026 (3:00 PM) - Amy LaVere featuring Will Sexton
We were introduced to Amy LaVere through an appearance of her husband, Will Sexton at the Monday Night Supper Club. 
Amy and Will have been music collaborators and touring companions for 11 years. LaVere’s songwriting is pointed and clever with lyrics that are hard to shake. Once you hear her captivating voice, it will be forever instantly recognizable. 
Amy has been recording albums since 2005 with This World is Not My Home, Anchors & Anvils, Stranger Me, Runaway’s Diary, Hallelujah I’m A Dreamer, and her latest Painting Blue. She recorded Go On Now, You Can’t Stay Here (The Wandering), Sisters Of The Strawberry Moon, and as part of the band Motel Mirrors, Motel Mirrors, In The Meantime, Gotta Lotta Rhythm. There has also bene other collaborations on a number of additional albums.
Will has a long history with music, from his early days with Will & the Kill through his collaboration with his brother in the Charlie Sexton Sextet (Under A Wishing Tree) to his solo albums, Don’t Walk the Darkness, Hallelujah, I'm a Dreamer, Move the Balance, Scenes From Nowhere, and Bus Stop Gossip, Other Lies, & Respected Crimes. 
Take a listen to some of their music.
The requested minimum donation is $30 per person at the door. All donations go directly to the artists.
Reservations are open.

MJ and I have seen both Jenny Reynolds and Emily Shirley many times. We will a special December show with these two songwriters in a song swap. Katie Lessley will be joining them with harmony vocals. While this technically is not a Christmas show, a few Christmas songs with be performed.

 Emily Shirley is an accomplished songwriter, releasing 3 EP and many, many singles. She performs solo, with her own band and as part of Sister Golden Hair the band. Katie Lessley performs frequently with The Emily Shirley Band.
I'll add more information shortly.

We first saw Tony Kamel when he was part of Wood & Wire at Fischer Haus Concerts. Since Wood & Wire broke up, we have seen the Grammy nominated Tony (and sometimes with Kym Warner) perform many times. Tony and Kym opened for Robert Earl Keen and we have seen him have a few festivals (Donkey Grass). Tony plays guitar, banjo and even fretless banjo! His shows are always entertaining.

February 7, 2027 (3:00 PM) - Michael O'Connor and Gabriel Rhodes
Neither Michael O'Connor nor Gabriel Rhodes are strangers to the Cantina Sound House Concerts audience. Both have previously performed here with other people. Now Michael and Gabriel are releasing a duo album (Dance While The Whole World Burns) 0in the Fall of 2026, so it's time to have them here together.
Michael O'Connor is a guitarist, singer and songwriter currently living in Washington State, but still spends a lot of time in Texas. For decades he has performed and recorded with musicians including Ray Wylie Hubbard, Slaid Cleaves, Adam Carroll, Susan Gibson, Terri Hendrix, and Jeff Plankenhorn.
We first saw Michael playing with Slaid Cleaves at the Sycamore Creek House Concerts, long before I got involved with Sycamore Creek. Since then we have seen him many times at the Dripping Springs Songwriters Festival, Gruene Hall, Fischer Haus, Sam's Burger Joint, SlimFest in Terlingua, and probably many other places.
"Michael O’Connor is well-acknowledged as a great guitarist, but you should listen to his songs as well. He writes beautifully, authentically and comes by that gritty gravel road of a voice honestly. You’ll not find a forced moment in his work.”
- Rod Picott

Gabe is well known as a producer (including producing Barbara Nesbitt's Lucky Street and his mother's [Kimmie Rhodes] release, Hypnotized, featuring the sone No Tom Petty).
Gabe released a solo album last year, Selling Tumbleweeds.

House Concerts
Cantina Sound House Concerts hosts both local and touring artists, primarily Americana style. In all cases, we have previously seen everyone that plays at Cantina Sound House Concerts. In most cases, we have seen them perform multiple times.
We have now hosted 49 shows in our workshop.
● Located halfway between Dripping Springs and Johnson City (several miles east of Pedernales Falls State Park)
● Indoors and air-conditioned
● Seating for 38 people
● Full sound (QSC mains and monitors/Midas mixer) and lighting
● 16' x 10' stage, 18" high
● Six camera video set up in fixed locations

Barbara Nesbitt CD #6 "Lucky Street" Project available and streaming
Barbara Nesbitt has performed at Cantina Sound House Concerts three times. At the first 2 shows, she performed new unrecorded songs. Her show in 2024 included mostly new songs that have not been recorded. She launched a Kickstarter campaign for her next recording project. There were so many great unrecorded songs since her last CD that MJ and I stepped up to help support this effort to get them recorded. Recording has completed and files have been sent for mastering. 11 tracks were recorded with a stellar band that included Gabriel Rhodes, Scrappy Judd Newcomb, Matt Slagle, and Rick Richards. Three more songs were recorded without a full band (one of which will not be included on the album and will be released separately). Stephano Intelisano, BettySoo, Wendy Colanna, Nevada Newman, Kevin Flatt, Marty Muse, John Mills, and Brian Standifer all added special touches to some of the songs. I took some behind the scenes photos and videos. A behind the scenes photos video with the song "Lucky Street" is on Barbara's website and is also on our video page. More information is on our Barbara Nesbitt Lucky Street page.

 House concert information
What are house concerts?
A house concert is a private event and requires an invitation. Subscribe to our mailing list or if you make reservations for a show, you will be automatically added to the invitation list. 
Where is the Cantina Sound Workshop?
Cantina Sound Workshop is located at our house that is a few miles east of Pedernales Falls State Park, off of Fitzhugh Road, between Dripping Springs and Johnson City. Complete directions will be sent with your final confirmation.
The workshop is air-conditioned with comfortable seating.
No need to bring your own chairs. Let us know if the workshop is too cold or too hot. We can adjust the temperature and there is an overhead fan.
Are advanced reservations required?
Yes, shows are invitation only and reservations are required. The room has a limited capacity and reservations are required on a first come, first served basis. Reservation requests are handled on the Cantina Sound website.
We enable reservations for a show approximately 1 month prior to the show.
If you need to cancel, please contact us as soon as possible, however failing to cancel prior to 3 days before the show, requires sending a donation to the artist. Failing to donate will put you on the naughty list preventing attendance to future shows.
How much of the door donation (suggested donation published) goes to the artists?
All of the donations at the door go directly to the performer(s). Cantina Sound House Concerts does not receive any compensation for these shows.

October 25, 2026 (3:00 PM) - Austin Lounge Lizards
Way back in May 1994, I went to an early evening Austin Lounge Lizards show at Antone's, the old location on the drag. There was a beautiful woman sitting at the next table. At the break Tom Pittman (then a member of the Lounge Lizards) stoped and talked to this woman and then stopped at my table to talk to me before he went back on stage. I then talked to her until the end of the break. During the second set, her date finally joined her. I called Tom the next day and asked if he knew how to get hold of Mary Jo. He said he didn't but about 6 weeks later, he had her meet him at the Threadgill's Wednesday night Sittin' and Suppin', knowing I would be there. After dinner, I asked her to go over to The Cactus Cafe to hear some other musicians I knew. We met for lunch the next day and 5 months later we were married, 31 yers ago.
The Austin Lounge Lizards were founded in 1980 with Conrad Deisler, Hank Card and Tom Pittman. That is a long time for a band to be around. Yes, there has been a somewhat rotating list of musicians in the band over all of those years, with Tom retiring from the band in 2011. The current line up consists of Conrad, Hank, and joining them in 2016, former members, Kirk Williams and Tim Wilson. 
If you are not already familiar with the Austin Lounge Lizards, their songs spoof subjects such as politics, religion, romance, and themselves. The self-proclaimed "Most Laughable Band in Show Business" features pointed lyrics, precise harmonies and instrumental expertise. From "Creatures from the Black Saloon" to "Home & Deranged" they currently have 11 CD's and 2 DVD's available on their website. 
The requested minimum donation is $30 per person at the door. All donations go directly to the artists.

September 20, 2026 (3:00 PM) - Susan Gibson
It will have been too long since Susan Gibson played at Cantina Sound House Concerts (1.5 years), but this time we are featuring just Susan.
For the few who are not familiar with Susan Gibson, she came to prominence when The Chicks recorded her song "Wide Open Spaces". She got her musical career started after college as a member of the band The Groobees. Susan's latest CD is "The Hard Stuff", but she has released six other CD's of music, including a live album. Susan regularly hosts and participates in songwriter workshops.
"The Hard Stuff is what happens when a brilliant intellect conjures heart and life experience into song, conveyed in a voice you want to crawl inside forever, and produced to perfection without any hint of compromise." - Eliza Gilkyson
"When I listen to a Susan Gibson song, I know she is sharing a piece if her heart and soul with me. Susan writes about true stories in her life. She writes with courage puts forth her message with powerful and heartfelt guitar and vocals. It only takes a few lines of her recorded songs for me to recognize that "Susan Sound." Her new record has that for sure. Enjoy....." - Lloyd Maines - Music producer and musician.

August 16, 2026 (3:00 PM) - BettySoo *** Updated Date ***
BettySoo is a Texas born and raised singer-songwriter, currently based in Austin. She played here at Cantina Sound House Concerts on August 20,2023, so she will be appearing again, 3 years later.
Betty has currently been touring the US with James McMurtry and Chris Smither. Previously, She toured with Alejandro Escovedo. She toured the US and Europe as part of the Americana/Pop supergroup trio, Nobody's Girl.
She is no stranger to house concerts, she even has a page dedicated to describing house concerts on her website.
BettySoo's latest CD is If You Never Go Away. Her first solo CD release was 2007's Little Tiny Secrets, followed by Heat Sin Water Skin, When We're Gone, and a demos disc, Insomnia Waking Dream: Insomnia Waking Dream. She has also recorded 2 CD's with Doug Cox, Lie To Me and More Lies. Nobody's Girl released an EP, Waterline, and a full length CD Nobody's Girl.
