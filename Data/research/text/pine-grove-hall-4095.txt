RESERVATIONS 
Book your Pine Grove Hall reservation on Resy
DINNER ONLY
Click Resy “Book Now" -
Select the number of guests and date. Select a time using the blue “Dinner-Only” button.
For parties over 6, please call or email us directly
TICKETED EVENTS (Dinner + Show)
 Go to our Events Calendar, find your event, scroll to the end of the description, and click Get Tickets.
Your ticket includes entrance to the show and reserved seating.
If you are using the Resy App, make sure you click the Event Reservation - not the Dinner-Only button. You don’t need to make a separate dinner reservation if you’re attending an event!
Event Timing
Seating for events begins 1 hour before showtime so we can serve food and drinks at a comfortable pace. You may also order food and drinks during the show.
Where 
Events take place upstairs and downstairs — check the event details. Please note that we do not have handicapped access for upstairs. 
We (almost) always offer dining in the opposite room from the music, so feel free to book “Dinner Only” even on music nights.
📞 Call or 📧 email us anytime if you have questions. 
HOURS
Wednesday through Saturday, 5 - 9pm
Sunday, 4 - 8pm. 
Last seating is 15 minutes before closing. 
Reservations are encouraged. Walk-ins welcome. For parties over 6, please call us at 814-954-5419.
Free parking is available in our lot and on the street. Please do not park at the 5 spots in front of the post office. These spots are for our neighbors who do not get mail delivered to their homes. You may be towed!

 2026 Touring Artists 
Each year, Pine Grove Hall welcomes a small number of touring artists seeking intimate rooms and attentive audiences. As an independent venue, we treasure hosting independent musicians from across the country whose work spans folk, Americana, blues, jazz, and indie—artists we believe in and love sharing with this community. 
When you show up, artists notice. Agents notice. And more touring musicians come to Happy Valley.
Feel free to comment with the bands you would like to see. As more shows are confirmed, we’ll continue to add them here, as well as on our regular Shows & Events page.

Private Events 
Pine Grove Hall is an ideal space for your private or semi-private event. Whether it be a rehearsal dinner, a corporate dinner or a holiday party, we strive to provide a unique experience with some of the best food and cocktails in central Pennsylvania. 
We also welcome conversations regarding full or partial buyouts of our restaurant for corporate events, film recording or music and video recordings.
Please fill out the form below and someone from our team will contact you. If you are unable to access the form, send us an email.
