RAVEN ROCKS HOUSE CONCERTS
Raven Rocks 
House Concerts
Enjoy a quality listening experience in the beautiful 
outdoor stage amphitheater, OR in our indoor theatre.
We are committed to providing a sublime listening experience
Our series is registered through the Folk Alliance House Concerts as a member of Folk Alliance. 
We hold concerts primarily in Summer averaging one concert a month. 
All concerts are by suggested donation and all proceeds go to the artists. 
2026 SCHEDULE BELOW!!
To Reserve your seat or for more information contact us 
via email lisa@ravenrockshouseconcerts.com
Raven Rocks Concert Season Opens in June through September 2026. 
FINAL CONCERT OF THE SEASON
September 26th 
Maia Sharp
Season Closer Maia Sharp • Sept 26th Showcasing her new award winning album Tomboy! Check Maia out here: https://www.maiasharp.com/
Most concerts are held in our outdoor venue. 
Usually folks bring a dish to share before the concert and a beverage to share. The host does provide some beverages.

The Black Feathers
All the way from England. 
They stunned the crowds at Kate Wolf Festival 2022 The ability to write songs that are both modern and ancient is a rare thing, and they have it. The product of an arcane art of weaving in traditional influences so thoroughly that they become the warp and weft of fresh creations.
