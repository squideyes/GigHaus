About 
The Kennett Flash is a nonprofit performing arts organization which operates a 100-seat, all-ages, stage-centered, BYOB, café-style listening room. The venue showcases professional national, regional, and local talent for diverse events in music, comedy, children’s programs, and more.
Mission
The Mission of the Kennett Flash is to be a leader in southern Chester County in providing diverse, quality performing arts programming and education that is accessible for all people.
Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion 
The Kennett Flash is committed to creating a warm, welcoming environment that celebrates the diversity of its staff, its audience, and its artists. We are committed to a healthy environment of mutual respect and appreciation for all humans regardless of age, race, sexuality, or gender identity, with the goal of creating a more inclusive space that represents all the members of our wonderful community.
Ticket Sales
The Kennett Flash sells tickets exclusively through Etix.com or at the door. We do NOT use any third party sites. These are scams and scalpers.

Membership FAQs 
 Why is the Kennett Flash doing this now?
The Kennett Flash, like many other non-profit organizations, needs to diversify its revenue stream beyond ticket sales and donations. Your contribution provides the Flash with additional financial stability, allowing us to focus our attention on growing our audience, booking a wider variety of performances, and improving our outreach to the underserved populations in the Kennett Square community.
As a member of The Kennett Flash, you will join a community of music lovers and arts supporters, enjoy benefits at each show you attend, and serve as an ambassador of local music and the arts.
 How does a membership help with the financial stability of the Kennett Flash?
Membership is a source of revenue to the Flash, in addition to ticket sales and corking fees. Members of the Flash will create a great network — helping to grow our audience.
 What is the time period for Memberships?
The time period is one year from activation. You can sign up anytime during the year, and you can renew after 12 months. You can also choose to upgrade your membership during renewal time. 
 Can I change (upgrade or downgrade) my membership during my membership year?
You can upgrade at any time, but you’ll have to wait for the membership renewal if you want to downgrade.
 Can I buy a membership as a gift?
Of course! When purchasing a membership, gift options and information are provided on the website. Feel free to call us if you need any help!
 Is a membership refundable or pro-rated if I move?
Memberships are not refundable and can not be pro-rated.
 Can I share a Membership with my spouse or partner?
Yes, Opener (Level 2) and Headliner (Level 3) members can authorize a secondary card holder for the membership. If you would like to add a secondary card holder to your Level 2 or Level 3 membership, please contact Michael Hahn at [email protected].
 Can I let a friend use my card?
Cards are not transferable and photo IDs may be checked when you show your membership card.
 Can my friends use the Flash Pass line with me?
Members who have pre-purchased tickets for their guests may let their guests join them in the Flash Pass line. If the guests of the member need to purchase their tickets at the door, they will need to stand in the General Admission line. Members are welcome to waive corking fees for their guests (if additional waived corking fees are included in their membership). Our volunteers will hold those guests’ names at the podium until they come through the General Admission line.
 What is a Member Spotlight Show?
A Member Spotlight Show is a performance that we believe you will really enjoy, regardless of whether or not you’ve heard of the performer. We understand that price is oftentimes a barrier to discovering new music, so we are happy to offer a discount to our members so that they can continue to expand their musical horizons. 
Member Spotlight Shows occur once a month, and members are able to receive $5 off up to 4 advance tickets (per member). Limited member tickets will be made available at each performance, so watch for the monthly member email to make sure you’re the first! 
Member Spotlight Shows are separate from other deals and discounts The Flash may offer members throughout the year.
 What is the Members Annual Party?
The Members Annual Party is a celebration of the Flash community! The party will include food from local vendors, drinks, and music!

Seating & Table Reservations 
SEATING: All seating at The Flash is general admission, first come, first served. To ensure your party has seats together, please arrive early. 90% of our seating is table seating with some couch and stool seating in the “balcony” and some standing room for shows at capacity.
RESERVATIONS: We are happy to reserve tables for MEMBERS ONLY with parties of 4 or more (or parties of 2 or more if you are a Level 3: Headliner Member). Please fill out this Google Form with the name the reservation should be under, your contact information, as well as the name and date of the show. Your reservation will be confirmed by email.
Your reservation is not confirmed unless you receive an email response.
Tickets must be purchased through The Kennett Flash website (our ticketing provider, Etix) or directly at The Kennett Flash Box Office to qualify for table reservations. Tickets purchased on secondary websites are not applicable for table reservations. 
YOU MUST HAVE AN ACTIVE MEMBERSHIP WITH THE KENNETT FLASH.
Reservation requests will be accepted up until 24 hours before the show.
Note: Reservations are seated in the best available seats in the order in which the requests were received. Specific seating requests are not guaranteed. Larger groups will be split across multiple tables.
Table reservations are forfeited 15 minutes after the performance starts (1 hour and 15 minutes after doors open).
BALCONY RESERVATION: The balcony can be reserved for parties of 15 or more. Tickets MUST be purchased together. Other parties will be offered to join you in the balcony should we reach capacity.

Rentals & Private Events 
Make you next event memorable at the Kennett Flash!
The Kennett Flash venue is a great choice for small gatherings of up to 100 guests. We’ve hosted birthday and anniversary parties, business meetings, album release parties and a variety of other celebrations. Our staff will work with you to ensure that you have everything to make your event a success. We can also help you find caterers and entertainment for your event.
Rentals may not conflict with scheduled events. Only technicians hired by The Kennett Flash may operate lighting and sound equipment. A rental agreement must be signed and proof of liability insurance relating to renter’s use of the facility may be required. 
We have three options for renting the Kennett Flash:
EVENT TIER: Lighting and Sound Required
$200 / hour for a minimum of 4 hours
up to 100 guests
Kennett Flash technician included
access to stage lighting and venue sound
access to stage, green room, tables, chairs, table linens
PARTY TIER: No Stage Lighting or Professional Sound Necessary
$150 / hour for a minimum of 2 hours
up to 100 guests
access to stage, green room, tables, chairs
NON-PROFIT TIER:
The Kennett Flash is first and foremost a community collaborator. If you’re a local non-profit looking to host an event, please contact Michael at [email protected] to discuss your needs and we’ll find a rental agreement that works for you!
