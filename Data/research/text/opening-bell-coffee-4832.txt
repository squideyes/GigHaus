DALLAS' BEST ACOUSTIC LIVE MUSIC VENUE & COFFEE BAR
Live music most nights. We work with the greatest local vendors! 
Come to Opening Bell Coffee, slip into one of our comfortable chairs, sip on an excellent espresso or one of our specialty coffee blends, Enjoy a wonderful selection of Harney & Sons silk tea or our Cultured Cup Loose Leaf Teas or treat yourself to one of our popular David Rio CHAIS... Pressed for time? Grab a cup of our House Blend and a kolache on your way out! We'd love to have you visit us later for lunch or after work for some amazing live music from some of the most sought out musicians from all over the globe! Check out our beer and wine selections on our menu tab, along with our full menu served all hours!
You'll love the comfort we offer and the synergy of networking. Take advantage of our Free WI-FI to study, work, or just catch up!
We love meeting new people and would really like to make your day better!
News
SOUTHSIDE ON LAMAR
THE CEDARS DISTRICT
We are located in the basement of the South Side on Lamar Lofts (the Historic Sears Roebuck Building) just south of I-30 between the Dallas Convention Center and the Jack Evans Police Headquarters. Parking is FREE in the black gated Retail Parking Lot (which recently moved to a new lot on Austin Street.

Book a Private Event
Your people. Your playlist. Your night.
Looking for a space that feels a little more memorable than the typical event venue?
Opening Bell Coffee is a neighborhood coffee shop, bar, and live music venue in the heart of Dallas — and we’d love to host your next gathering. From intimate celebrations and birthday parties to corporate events, happy hours, listening parties, private concerts, and everything in between, our space gives you the flexibility to create an event that feels like you.
MAKE IT YOURS
Whether you're planning a casual get-together or a full-blown celebration, we'll work with you to create the right experience.
​
Depending on your event, we can offer:
Live Music & Entertainment - Bring in a musician, band, DJ, or let us help you find the right sound for your night.
Coffee, Cocktails & More - Keep it casual with coffee and cold brew, or add beer, wine, and cocktails for an evening event.
Food & Catering - From light bites to a catered spread, we'll help you build a food experience that fits your event and your budget.
A Space Built for Gatherings - With a stage, sound system, seating, bar service, and a relaxed atmosphere, Opening Bell is made for bringing people together.
PERFECT FOR
Corporate Events • Team Gatherings • Birthday Parties • Rehearsal Dinners • Wedding Events • Happy Hours • Private Concerts • Listening Parties • Holiday Parties • Fundraisers • Networking Events • Celebrations
LET'S PLAN SOMETHING GREAT
Tell us a little about your event — the date, number of guests, type of gathering, and what you have in mind — and we'll get back to you with availability and options.
No two events have to look the same. That's the fun part.

Frequently asked questions
What drinks do you offer, other than coffee?"
We offer a wide variety of drinks other than espresso. We also serve an excellent selection of domestics, imports and fine wines. 
Do you deliver to the South Side Lofts?
Yes! Please call 945-994-4618 for all call in orders and current delivery schedule.
If I have suggestions for your shop or theme would you mind if I contacted you by email?
We'd love to hear from you! 
Do you sell breakfast or lunch items or just drinks?
Yes! Check out our MENU link or stop by for lunch breakfast or dinner! We claim to have the BEST CHICKEN SALAD IN DALLAS and folks drive from Frisco for our soups!
Are you doing Speed Dating at Opening Bell Coffee?
Not currently although we have hosted several with match.com in the past. Several have met special others while enjoying themselves at Opening Bell so you never know who you might meet! 
Do you charge for your Wireless access?
No! Our Wireless is FREE to our valued customers. Just walk in with your laptop or smartphone 
I'm a musician, who do I contact about playing there?"
Please complete the form on our BOOKING link and you'll receive instructions. PLEASE DO NOT DROP OFF PRESS KITS! Please note that our focus is ORIGINAL music! 
I want to rent your place who do I contact?
How fun! Shoot an email to hello@openingbellcoffee.com for more info!

About the event
Longest Running OPEN MIC in DFW - over 20 years!! Musicians play in rotation based on sign up times. Sets are generally 2 songs, or 10 minutes depending on sign ups. Mainly Acoustic & Keyboards (please have your instruments tuned prior to your set) anything more requires approval prior to sign up. Original material is always preferred, but some covers allowed. It's always a great time loaded with some incredible talent!
THIS IS AN ALL AGES SHOW. NO EXPLICIT LANGUAGE OR DISCRIMINATORY CONTENT IS ALLOWED.
