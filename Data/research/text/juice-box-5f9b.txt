About The Juice Box Public House
It’s not just food. It’s a work of art.
At the Juice Box Centralia, you can dive into a creative and eclectic menu that beautifully blends comfort food with global flair. From loaded fries, poke bowls, gourmet burgers, tacos, and scratch-made soups, every dish is crafted with bold flavors and a playful twist. With plenty of vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options, there’s something for everyone at the Juicebox Centralia. Whether you’re here for a quick bite, a full meal, or just dessert. We feature over 30 taps of beer from around the PNW. We also offer a fun variety of non-alcoholic beverages such as kombucha, nitro coffee, fountain and CBD sodas.

About Us
Owners Levi and Sarah Althauser with their four children.
Long time locals, who met at Centralia High School. They live downtown and always wanted a place for music, events and community. Sarah is a former city counselor and director of the Boys & Girls club of Lewis County. Levi Also works as a real estate agent when not found at The Juice Box Public House.
Live Music and Entertainment
At the Juice Box you can enjoy a variety of entertainment options, including live bands, comedians, DJs, and open-mic nights. Don't forget to check out our events calendar to see what's coming up at the Juicebox Centralia!
Games Galore
At the Juice Box you can discover a wide variety of board games perfect for a family outing, a fun date night, or simply a way to relax after a long day.
