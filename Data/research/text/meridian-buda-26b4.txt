How It Works at Meridian
How shows work here: showtimes, sound and load-in. We'll go over terms when we reach out.
Capacity · inside / outside100 / 174
ShowtimesThursdays, Fri & Sat nights, happy hour
Thursdays Two sets in the 7–10 PM window, 1–3 bands. Usually two sets, 7:30–9:30.
Fri & Sat Two to four sets in the 7 PM–midnight window. Usually two sets, 8:30–11ish.
Happy hour Monday–Saturday, 4–6 PM. One or two sets.Sound & BacklinePro rig, in-house engineer, drums + bass amp
Pro sound and light rig with an in-house engineer. Bringing your own FOH? Let us know.
Board Behringer X32. Multitrack out available.
Drums Yamaha Manu Katche shells and hardware. Bring breakables, snare, throne and any preferred hardware.
Bass Ampeg 150 Max bass combo amp.Load-in & HospitalityMain St. front door, 1 hour before, 2 drinks each
Parking Close to the front door on Main St., or in the back lot.
Load in Through the front Main St. door. Parked in back? Bring gear through the alley between Meridian and Cigar Vault.
Load-in / check 1 hour before start time, unless otherwise noted.
Hospitality 2 drinks per player.

Nik Parr & The Selfless Lovers
8:00 PM CT$28.16Get Tickets
Saturday, November 14, 2026Showtime 8:00 PM CTEnds 10:30 PM CT
Nik Parr & The Selfless Lovers are a piano-driven roots rock n' roll act from Austin, Texas. Led by pianist, saxophonist, singer Nik Parr, the show is like a crazy fever dream of Jerry Lee Lewis piano, Bruce Springsteen blue collar rock n' roll and James brown frenetic funk party. Nik even plays saxophone and piano at the same time on stage. With a well earned reputation as one of the most exciting acts in roots music, Nik and The Selfless Lovers are a whirlwind of energy. Channeling blues, soul, funk, rock n' roll into something new and exuberant, this band is the reason you go EXPERIENCE live music. Hailing from Austin, Texas, Nik and his band have broken out into the national scene through a blistering tour schedule of over 150 live shows per year since 2018. This is a bonafide, REAL rock n' roll band.
