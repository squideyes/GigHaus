FAQ & Contact
 FAQ & Contacteperrier2026-09-03T09:00:53-04:00 
Need To KNow
CONTACT US 
Email us info@therockwell.org for general questions and inquiries. Please expect 48 hours for a response.
INSTAGRAM
Follow us here for updates on the latest shows: Instagram
PHONE NUMBER
Our phone is very old. Any urgent questions, please email info@therockwell.org with “URGENT” in the subject line.
AGE LIMITS
Fridays and Saturdays = 21+ unless specifically noted.
Matinees, and Sunday – Thursdays = All Ages, 16+ or 18+
Babes in arms are always free. 
Kids under 10 may attend most shows at the discretion of their parents.
ADA ACCESSIBILITY
ELEVATOR: 
1. Enter the street entrance for The Rockwell / Saloon
2. Before going down the stairs, there is a DOOR ON THE RIGHT that says “ADA ACCESSIBLE ELEVATOR” in white letters. 
3. Enter into the mailroom and there is the elevator. 
4. Take the elevator to “B” for BASEMENT. 
5. Proceed out of the bright wood foyer past to the HOST STAND 
6. Proceed RIGHT for The Rockwell (or left for Saloon)
RESTROOMS: 
There is access to a full size restroom in Saloon from The Rockwell. Proceed to the Rockwell Ticket Booth and a Rockwell Employee will assist. When you leave The Rockwell, five feet past the Ticket Booth, is a door that says “EMPLOYEES ONLY.” Proceed through and there are larger restrooms to the right.
SEATING: 
The seating is raked, as in, there are three rows of seats with steps between each one. On the floor of The Rockwell, there is plenty of space for wheelchairs and a Rockwell employee can provide a seat for an accompanying attendee. For seated performances, viewing is unobstructed. Unfortunately, for standing music performances, viewing is obstructed. 
BE AWARE OF SCAMS
We do not accept third-party tickets.
We use OpenDate, SeeTickets, and occasionally Eventbrite. We will not ask you to confirm information, unless from a @rockwell.org account. 
BOX OFFICE
We do not have a box office to buy tickets in advance…yet! But rest assured that the online fees go to paying graphic designers, buying ink, paper, printing posters, and more! Thank you for your understanding and support.
BACKPACKS
Backpacks are not allowed. We do not have a coat check. Please leave backpacks at home.
Coat Check
Unfortunately, we do not have a coat check or bag check. There is space for your bag/coat by your seat. Please note: Any bags may be inspected upon entry to ensure safety.
EMAIL LIST
Join our email list! Sign up here: 
https://mailchi.mp/therockwell.org/sign-up-for-the-rockwell-newsletter
HOTELS NEARBY 
Davis Square Inn (Clarendon or Walnut Hill Room)
Morrison House (Morrison Room or Winter Hill Room)
Harvard Square Inn
SEATED or STANDING?
There are 145 fixed seats in the space at all times. Depending on the show the center is kept open for standing shows (most concerts) or seats are added into the center for a more intimate experience (most comedy shows). At any concert, you will be able to find a seat.
PARKING
Parking is tough! Meters are 2-hours and free after 8:00 PM and completely free on Sundays. For 3-hour parking, try Grove Street Lots, Herbert Street Lot, and Rite Aid Lot which can be found on Google Maps. 
PUBLIC TRANSIT
The closest train station is “Davis Square” off of the Red Line and is a 3-5 min walk from our door. There are also many MBTA buses that stop nearby.
WIFI & CELL SERVICE
There is limited cell service, but yes, of course there is wifi! Please take this into consideration for calls and coordinating with others. We do however offer free wifi so you can locate your tickets or message others when inside.

Celebrate Your Ridiculousness! Led by Shannan Calcutt
It’s time to risk being seen in all your glory! Discover the freedom and joy that comes when you embrace your clown and celebrate your ridiculousness.
In this two-day workshop, you will:
Take big risks
Flaunt your flaws
Follow your impulses
Laugh in failure’s face
Connect with your audience
Experience the power of the ensemble
And much more!
This is a rare chance to train in Somerville with Shannan Calcutt, a world-renowned artist and teacher. Her workshops have inspired artists from all walks of life to live in the moment, play from the heart, risk being vulnerable, overcome obstacles, and be the kind of collaborator that lifts up everyone in the room. Over the past six years, she has led a number of workshops at Celebration Barn, including Celebrate Your Ridiculousness and Make a Show with Shannan. The latter has helped over two dozen artists create new shows that have toured throughout North America. Learn more about Calcutt and her remarkable work below.
“Shannan made everyone feel as though they could do anything they wanted and always come out ahead by creating an amazingly supportive attitude where we all had each other’s back and the egos were in check. Failure became success, we laughed a lot, and everyone was able to bring their best work to class.”
– Hilary Chaplain, performer and teacher
Enrollment is capped at 16 participants, so register today to secure your spot!
SOCIAL MEDIA: @shannancalcuttofficial
About Shannan Calcutt
Declared “a national treasure” by the CBC, Shannan Calcutt is a performer, writer, educator, and director.
She has played everywhere from Saskatoon’s Wash ’n Slosh Pub to the Sydney Opera House, and performed for millions on the Vegas Strip with Cirque du Soleil and Spiegelworld. Her new show, Things I Shouldn’t Tell You, was hailed “a masterclass in one-person shows” by Eat More Art Vegas.
She’s a leading educator in clown and play, and is featured in Clowns: In Conversation by Ezra LeBank and David Bridal. Shannan’s superpower is creating courageous playgrounds, where artists can play big, risk gloriously, and fail better.
She has devised and directed comedy for Cirque du Soleil, Spiegelworld, and Berlin’s Friedrichstadt-Palast, and is currently the director of The Wizard of Oz Experience at Sphere.
She is fluent in English, sarcasm, and sophisticated silliness.
More about Shannan at shannancalcutt.com.
