 Live Music Comedy Theater
Sellersville Theater is an intimate live music and comedy venue with 325 seats featuring national and international performers. Enjoy dinner next door before the show and stay overnight at the hotel after an entertaining evening of music or laughter. It’s the perfect date night or night out with friends. The venue is available to rent for weddings, presentations, meetings and special events and boasts an impressive live show schedule of five or more shows per week.

Sellersville Theater 1894 Privacy Policy Statement
The Washington House Hotel and Restaurant and Sellersville Theater’s Privacy Statement - 9/14/18: This privacy statement describes our current privacy practices, as of the date above, with regard to the information we collect when you or your computer interact with us, our website, and our social media sites. By using our website, you acknowledge and understand our privacy statement and consent to the information gathering and its use as described here. Information we collect: 1) We collect your name, address, phone number, email address; 2) Credit card and billing information if you purchase something from us, either by phone or through our 3rd party booking engines: 3) Information you post on our social media sites: 4) Information about events you like, products you buy, etc.; 5) Information about the browser you are using to access our site, which site you came from and location information. How we collect your information: 1) Directly from you when you call us, post on our site, purchase tickets or make reservations for the restaurant or hotel; 2) By using cookies, web beacons and other browser tracking tools, including 3rd party analytics services like Google Analytics. What we do with your information: 1) We respond to your questions and suggestions, 2) We improve our products or service, including faster check-ins, reservations, ticket purchasing, etc,; 3) We look at site trends; 4) We offer you other products or services we think you may like. Do we share your information? The Washington House Hotel uses InnRoad to facilitate online booking of hotel rooms. See their privacy statement here. The Washington House Restaurant uses OpenTable to make online restaurant reservations possible. See their privacy statement here. Sellersville Theater uses Etix to make online ticket purchasing possible. See their privacy statement here. When purchasing our services online, you are agreeing to their privacy practices as well. We do not share your information with any other 3rd parties . We understand the importance of online security and are constantly evaluating our practices and those of our online purchase partners. We are PCI compliant and have regular external vulnerability scans. Our servers are protected with a multilayered firewall based security system. What you can do regarding your information? 1) You can get information regarding your stored data by calling us at 215-257-3122 ext 224 or emailing at [email protected]. You have the right to request that it be deleted or corrected; 2) You can unsubscribe from our emails by calling 215-257-3122 ext 223 or by following the instructions on our email;. 3) You can decline cookies in you web browser settings and delete cookies that have already been placed on your computer or other devices; 4) You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking. Google has developed the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on; if you want to opt out of Google Analytics, you can download and install the add-on for your web browser here.
What are the Box Office Hours?
Monday – Saturday 10 AM – 5 PM, Sunday 12 PM – 4 PM. On days when there is a show our hours are extended and we answer phones until 7 PM and take walk up sales until 9 PM.
Do you have a will call option?
We encourage you to select a contactless, paperless ticket option: Print At Home or Mobile. They are greener options, and are one less point of contact during your ticket sale and on show day. If you prefer a printed ticket, there is a $1.50 charge per order.
In an effort to continue to help defend our fans and patrons from predatory third-party ticket sellers, patrons outside of a 750 mile radius of Sellersville Theater will need to select the Will Call ticket delivery option. A valid photo ID is required to pick up Will Call tickets.
How do I buy tickets?
There are three ways to purchase tickets: securely online, by phone at 215-257-5808 and in person at the theater Box Office, located at 24 W Temple Ave in Sellersville, PA.
In an effort to continue to help defend our fans and patrons from predatory third-party ticket sellers, patrons outside of a 750 mile radius of Sellersville Theater will need to select the Will Call ticket delivery option. A valid photo ID is required to pick up Will Call tickets.
How do I use a gift certificate or gift card?
There are three types of gift certificates: plastic gift cards, e-gift cards, and paper gift certificates. Plastic gift cards act like a debit card and can be swiped in person or used by phone by giving the 12 digit number. E-gift cards act like a debit card and can be used in person or by phone or by giving the 12 digit number. Paper gift certificates are most easily used in person and the value must be used all at once. 
How do I get the best seats?
All seats are good seats here! We hold the Cabaret seats and first four rows for Sellersville Theater Members until one month prior to the show. To purchase the Member Section before the one month mark, add a Membership to your order! If the Member Section is still available within one month of the show, non members may purchase or upgrade. There is a $7.00 per ticket fee to upgrade. If you are upgrading to the Cabaret where the ticket price is higher, a balance will also be owed.
What is Cabaret?
The stage side area of our theater is a flat, flexible use space that can be cleared for dancing or can accommodate two rows of cocktail tables and chairs. A premium price usually applies for seating in this “Cabaret” area. Theater Members can purchase these seats before the general public.
What if I want to stand and dance at a seated show with a full cabaret section?
For certain concerts, audience members may prefer to stand or dance, especially when encouraged by the artist to do so. Guest expectations differ, so we simply ask all attendees to extend mutual respect toward one another and maintain awareness of those around them. 
Why is there a processing fee on each ticket?
The $7.00 per ticket processing fee helps defray the costs of staffing our box office with a friendly, efficient team, processing sales and paying for the software that brings you real time ticketing. It is applied when you purchase tickets online or over the phone. If you place your order over the phone, but pick your tickets up in person, the fee will be applied. The fee is waived for in person sales at our Box Office. Our fee is one of the lowest of comparable theaters in our region. All tickets can be emailed to you for free, or held at Will Call for $1.50 per order fee.
Do you sell less expensive tickets at the door or partway into a show?
No, tickets are always the publicly listed price.
Do you reprint tickets?
We can resend your tickets to your email or mobile phone at no charge. If you request a physical, paper ticket there is a $1.50 fee per ticket order.
Can I return tickets?
All ticket sales are final.
What about parking?
Most parking is free in Sellersville! There is a small lot between The Washington House and Sellersville Theater. There is also a municipal lot directly across the street from Sellersville Theater. However, the lot adjoining the municipal lot is private and the owners charge a parking fee.
There is a new municipal gravel lot at the bottom of Lawn Avenue, which is two blocks west of the theater. Enter that lot from the road marked PWWAR.
Street parking in the surrounding neighborhood is free and there are numerous small public lots within two blocks of us. If you are interested in free parking, just give yourself a few extra minutes to find something close by.
When does the theater open for a show?
On show nights, the bar opens about 45 minutes before the show. The auditorium doors open about 30 minutes before the show. Please note that these times are estimates and schedule delays sometimes occur. Feel free to speak to a House Manager.
Do you sell food or beverages?
We have a bar located in our lobby that sells water, soda, coffee, tea, draft and bottled beer, wine by the glass and snacks. You may bring any lobby purchased items into the auditorium, but outside food or drink is not permitted. 
What staff is present during a show?
There are one to two House Managers that circulate throughout the venue. Please do not hesitate to ask for a House Manager to address any concerns you may have. There are one to two sound and light engineers running the show on stage. There is a Box Office

Comedian Bob Marley (5 PM)
Saturday, November 21, 2026
Show | 5:00PM // Doors | 4:30PM 
$39.5 to $66.5
Type: Concerts
Originally from Maine, Bob Marley began performing stand-up comedy in his hometown of Portland. He moved to Boston early in his career, gaining experience in a city known for spawning comedy all-stars. He now has a weekly radio call in segment with several radio stations around the country called “The World According to Bob.” Winner of the 2014 XM/Sirius ‘Super Bowl of Comedy’, he continually makes new fans around the country.
Making his first television appearance on Comedy Central, he is now one of the few comics to do the complete late night circuit including The Late Show with David Letterman, The Tonight Show with Jay Leno, Late Night with Conan O’Brien, and The Late Late Show with Craig Ferguson and Craig Kilborn. He has also appeared on 100 Television shows including VH 1’s “Super Secret Movie Rules”, Comedy Central’s “Premium Blend,” “USO Comedy Tour" and most recently "Comedy Central Presents Bob Marley," Bob’s half-hour special.
Many movie fans will recognize him from his appearance in the cult favorite movie, Boondock Saints, and the highly anticipated sequel released in fall of 2009, All Saints Day. Bob co-stars with Willem Dafoe, Sean Patrick Flanery and Norman Reedus (as the McManus brothers), Billy Connolly ( ll Duce), new female lead Julie Benz (Rita on Showtime’s ‘Dexter’) , brat packer Judd Nelson and film legend Peter Fonda. His comedic take on “Detective Greenly” inspires fans to constantly approach him and quote lines from the film. Bob Marley brought back the always funny and incredibly wrong Detective Greenly, in a much larger role in the second film.
Audiences are in for a treat whether they catch this Mainer’s act on stage or on screen.
