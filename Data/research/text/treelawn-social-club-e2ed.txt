Housed in the historic former Slovenian Workmen’s Home on Waterloo, The Treelawn sits in the heart of the vibrant Waterloo Arts & Entertainment District in Cleveland.
On the ground floor of the landmark building, you’ll find the Treelawn Social Club – an intimate space with a small stage, ‘40s-era bar with vintage mirrors, and a classic archway that divides the space. The Social Club opened its doors in February 2023 and hosts live music and comedy events 4-5 days a week.
Just a floor above, you will find Treelawn Music Hall – a large classic ballroom, freshly refurbished, that features hardwood floors, a high ceiling with original elegant detail work, the original disco ball from Euclid Beach Park, and original chandeliers. The Music Hall is a great space for weddings, class reunions, and larger concerts.
This 100+ year old building has undergone extensive restoration to bring a century of charm and character to any event.
