Quack’s Bakery Presents A South Side Listening Room
✶ est. 2020 ✶
Captain Quack’s Soundspace, located at 5326 Menchaca Rd. north of Stassney Ln., is a stellar hybrid; nestled inside a coffeeshop/bakery you get the signature bakes and out-of-this-world caffeinated options Quack’s fans know and love and the magic of a music venue and bar, hosting local acts and offering a selection of craft cocktails, local beer, and a well-curated wine list.
