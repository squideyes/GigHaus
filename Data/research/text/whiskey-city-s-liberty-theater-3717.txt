FOUNDED IN 1802,
Lawrenceburg has supplied whiskey to the world for over two centuries. For this we are known as “Whiskey City USA”. Our distilleries were founded as early as 1809, and by 1855 Lawrenceburg was home to an abundance of distilleries tapping limestone-filtered water from the aquifer below. Ross & Squibb Distillery continues our legacy of world-class whiskeys from their distillery originally established in 1847 as Rossville Union Distillery.
BUILT IN 1893,
The Liberty Theater was originally a German music hall called Liedertafel meaning “song table”. Converted for movies in 1921 and later named The Liberty during WWII, she has hosted performances and celebrations for over 130 years.
RESTORED IN 2023,
legendary music promoter Dan McCabe has revived the long dormant Liberty Theater as a versatile showcasing venue dedicated to artist development and the celebration of all things “Whiskey City”. Dan has 30+ years in live music industry beginning with his 9-year, 7 nights a week programming of iconic Rock-n-Roll laundromat Sudsy Malones. Dan presented shows at the historic Southgate House, served as Executive Producer of Cincinnati’s MidPoint Music Festival and was a founder and operator of downtown Cincinnati music venues MOTR and the historic Woodward Theater.

Celebrate your milestones in our 
lovingly restored 132-year-old theater
Weddings (shower, ceremony, reception… or all 3!), Graduation, Birthday Celebrations, Holiday & Corporate Events.
Historic downtown Lawrenceburg is the perfect setting to gather with colleagues, friends and family.
The Liberty Theater offers a full-service event production facility, informed and managed by a lifetime of event production experience.
The Liberty Theater features a bar with 24 draft beer taps, concert quality sound system and stage, a variety of furnishings and set-up choices, in-house catering options (outside caterers welcomed), ample Free Parking, an historic arched vestibule entrance and a private bridal suite.
Let’s hear your ideas and how our 30+ years of professional event production experience can help make it happen!
