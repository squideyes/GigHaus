About Us
"The Smartest Rendezvous in the Catskills."
The Colony Hotel opened in 1929. It was the tallest building in town, and served as a stopping point for visitors who were on their way up to the Overlook Mountain House. While they stayed, they’d dine and listen to big bands in Colony’s great room.

Official Ticketing Policy – Colony Woodstock
Eventbrite is the only official ticketing platform for Colony Woodstock.
We strongly recommend purchasing tickets directly through our official Eventbrite page. Tickets bought through third-party resale sites (StubHub, SeatGeek, Facebook Marketplace, etc.) are often listed at inflated prices and may cause issues at entry.
While we will make reasonable efforts to honor third-party tickets, we cannot guarantee pricing accuracy, transfer validity, or resolve disputes for tickets purchased outside our official platform.
To avoid overpaying or complications, please purchase directly through Eventbrite.

PRIVATE EVENTS
Host Your Event at Colony Woodstock!
Colony Woodstock hosts special events throughout the year. From unforgettable weddings to milestone anniversaries and birthdays, to corporate outings and on-premise shoots for film, TV and photography.
The moment you step inside our historic venue, you’ll instantly feel the welcoming vibe and spirit of a true Catskills artists’ haven. Although our building is nearly a century old, our amenities include modern state-of-the-art sound and lighting systems. 
Located in world-famous Woodstock, our venue is situated in the heart of the happening village, within walking distance to an array of charming boutiques, cozy cafés, and stellar eateries, yet, set back just enough to provide your event with ample privacy.
