 Our Winemakers
From the vineyards of France and Australia to the tasting rooms of California, our winemakers bring decades of hands-on craft and a shared belief that great wine starts with great grapes. Meet the team behind every bottle poured across City Winery's locations.
David LeComte
Chief Winemaker
Born in Tournon in the Northern Rhône, David got his start in the vines at 16, harvesting for the Delas & Fils estate. After a biology degree in Lyon and formal training in viticulture, he worked vintages in the Rhône, then spent two years managing vineyards and harvests in China. He later earned a winemaking master's in Montpellier and worked in Virginia, Long Island, and Southern California before joining Michael Dorf in 2008 to help launch City Winery from the ground up.
Travis Van Caster
Winemaker, Hudson Valley
Travis got into wine through a Craigslist ad in Oregon's Willamette Valley in 2007 and was hooked after his first harvest. He studied at five schools across Oregon, France, and New York, graduating from Cornell with a degree in Enology and Viticulture. He then made wine in California for E&J Gallo and Miller Family Wine Company before joining City Winery in 2020.
Caroline Schrader
Winemaker, Chicago
An award-winning Illinois winemaker known for hands-on, craft-focused production, Caroline oversees both the West Loop and Chicago Riverwalk locations. Before wine, she was an architect with a master's from the Illinois Institute of Technology. She later studied enology and viticulture at UC Davis and joined City Winery Chicago in 2014. Favorite variety: Pinot Noir.
Aurélien Gousse
Assistant Winemaker, New York City
Raised in a small medieval town in the Loire Valley, Aurélien grew up around his parents' friends' vineyards, where harvest meant celebration as much as work. His curiosity eventually took him to McLaren Vale in South Australia for further wine education. He joined City Winery as a winemaker in 2015, bringing a deep sense of terroir and a playful, creative personality that comes through in his winemaking.
Michelle Foletta Bell
Winemaker, Nashville
A California native, Michelle started as a professional actor, including national tours and a run at the Grand Ole Opry, before wine became her driving passion. She studied with the Guild of Sommeliers and worked tasting rooms across California wine country before moving to Nashville in 2017 and joining City Winery in 2018. She's known for her whiskey barrel-aged wines.
Tristan Bohling
Winemaker, Atlanta
Raised around the Atlanta food scene thanks to his chef father, Tristan began his wine journey in 2015 and worked harvests at Amici Cellars in Napa and R. Stuart and Co. in the Willamette Valley. In 2019 he joined City Winery Atlanta, working his way up from Tasting Room Associate to Wine Technician to Winemaker.
Rachel Navarro
Assistant Winemaker, Chicago
Rachel brings a strong science background in enology and viticulture from California State University, Fresno, with experience in chemistry, lab work, and plant pathology. She's worked with the USDA and at premium estates including Ponte and Bottaia Winery in California before joining City Winery Chicago in 2022. Favorite varieties: Riesling and Italian varietals.
 Our Vineyards
Built over years of collaboration, City Winery has developed strong relationships with the owners and vineyard managers of these exceptional properties. Our winemaking team works closely with each vineyard to source the highest-quality fruit, vintage after vintage, allowing us to craft distinctive wines that showcase the unique character and expression of these important vineyards.
Bettinelli Vineyards
Napa Valley, California
Bettinelli Vineyards started in 1990. Based in the Oak Knoll area between Napa and Yountville, the company farms hundreds of acres. Owned by fifth generation Napa Valley farms, Bettinelli has been honored by the Napa Valley Grapegrowers organization for their contributions to local wine business and community.
Scopus Vineyards
Sonoma County, California
Farmed by one of California’s most respected vineyard management teams, the Scopus vineyard is 15 acres, all Chardonnay, sitting at roughly 700 feet above sea level, sloping eastward above the town of Glen Ellen in Sonoma County.
Bacigalupi Vineyard
Russian River Valley, California
Bacigalupi Vineyards have been owned and grown by the Bacigalupi family since 1956. The family gained recognition as growers during the 1976 Paris tasting when the 1973 Napa Valley Chardonnay from Château Montelena with Bacigalupi fruit triumphed over many acclaimed French wines to be declared the undisputed winner.
Durant Vineyards
Willamette Valley, Oregon
The family that operates the Durant Vineyards have been farming in the Willamette Valley for over 5 generations. In 1973, they were among the first in Oregon to attempt growing wine grapes in the Dundee Hills, and the area has since grown into one of the most popular terroirs for Pinot Noir in the world.
Uva Blanca Vineyard
Finger Lakes, New York
The Uva Blanca vineyard is nestled at the base of Lake Seneca, in Hector, NY. Known for its signature view, overlooking Seneca Lake, the Uva Blanca vineyard is one of the preeminent growers in the Finger Lakes.
Obsidian Ridge Vineyard
Napa Valley, California
In 1973, Michael Terrien, Peter Molnar, and Arpad Molnar planted a vineyard on an old abandoned walnut orchard high in the Napa Valley. They soon discovered that they were farming on a volcano, on a ridge of solid black Obsidian glass. Today, the extreme climate, altitude, and volcanics result in a cabernet sauvignon like no other.
Windrem Vineyard
Lake County, California
The Windrem Vineyard is located along Kelsey Creek in the Big Valley area of Lake County. The soils are alluvial, well drained and limiting with small rock cobbles from the former creek bed.
