City Winery New York City is a premier live music venue offering an intimate setting for unforgettable performances. Enjoy live music nearly every night, from rock, R&B, and jazz concerts to stand-up comedy shows and live podcast recordings. Over the years, the venue has welcomed legendary artists & entertainers including Lyle Lovett, Kevin Hart, Jackson Browne, Melissa Etheridge, Macy Gray, Gregg Allman, Taj Mahal, Lalah Hathaway, and many more to its stage. As a leading New York City concert venue, we pair world-class live entertainment with dinner service and award-winning wines for a true dinner and show experience.
Browse our New York City concert calendar to discover upcoming concerts, comedy shows, brunch events, and special performances throughout the month. Whether planning a date night, searching for live music & concerts in New York City, or looking for things to do in NYC, City Winery delivers exceptional performances in a seated venue at Pier 57 in Chelsea on Manhattan’s west side.
 Plan Your Visit
Directions, Parking & Dining at City Winery New York City
City Winery New York City is located at 25 11th Avenue at Pier 57 in Chelsea, just steps from the Meatpacking District on Manhattan’s west side. Whether you're coming for live music, a comedy show, or dinner and a show, getting here is easy.
Parking: Paid parking is available at Pier 57, including an on-site parking garage for convenient access to City Winery New York City. Additional parking garages and lots are located throughout Chelsea and the surrounding neighborhood. Street parking may be available nearby, though availability can vary depending on the time of day and traffic.
Public Transit: City Winery New York City is easily accessible via public transportation. Nearby subway lines include the A, C, and E trains at 14th Street, as well as the L train at 8th Avenue, all within a short walk of the venue. The 1, 2, and 3 trains at 14th Street–7th Avenue are within walking distance. Multiple MTA bus routes serve the Chelsea area, making it easy to reach Pier 57 from across Manhattan and the greater New York City area.
Rideshare: Uber, Lyft, and other rideshare services frequently drop off and pick up guests directly at Pier 57, making it convenient to arrive or leave after a show.
 FAQ
 Where is City Winery New York City located?
City Winery New York City is located at 25 11th Avenue at Pier 57 in Chelsea, just steps from the Meatpacking District, one of the city’s most popular dining and entertainment areas. The venue sits along the Hudson River on Manhattan’s west side, surrounded by restaurants, bars, and nightlife. Many guests visit City Winery for a full evening experience, combining dinner, wine, and live music in one location.
 What types of concerts and shows happen at City Winery New York City?
City Winery New York City hosts a diverse lineup of live music, comedy shows, podcast recordings, brunch performances, and special events throughout the year. Our calendar regularly features national touring artists, singer-songwriters, jazz musicians, tribute concerts, and curated local performances. Because of the venue’s intimate concert setting, audiences are able to experience performances up close in a seated environment.
 Can I order food and wine during the show?
Yes. City Winery New York City is known for our dinner and a show experience, allowing guests to enjoy full dinner service and award-winning wines during most performances. Guests can order food and drinks directly from their table while watching the show, or arrive early to enjoy the restaurant or wine bar before the performance begins.
 Is seating reserved at City Winery concerts?
Most shows feature reserved seating so guests can relax and enjoy the performance with food and wine service throughout the evening.
 What time do doors open for shows?
Door times can vary depending on the event, but guests are typically able to enter the venue 60 to 90 minutes before showtime. Arriving early is recommended if you plan to order dinner or drinks before the performance begins.
 What makes City Winery different from other New York City concert venues?
City Winery New York City offers a concert and dining experience that brings together live music, award-winning wines, and chef-driven cuisine under one roof. Unlike many traditional music venues, guests can enjoy a full meal and wine service while watching the performance. Our intimate, seated concert hall allows audiences to experience artists up close in a more personal setting than many larger New York City venues.
 What is the concert experience like at City Winery New York City?
City Winery New York City offers an intimate concert experience where guests enjoy live performances in a seated venue with table service. Unlike larger concert halls, the space brings audiences closer to the music, allowing guests to enjoy dinner, wine, and cocktails throughout the show. The venue’s acoustics and smaller capacity make it a favorite for fans looking to experience artists in a more personal setting.
 Do shows frequently sell out?
Because City Winery New York City is an intimate venue with limited seating, many concerts and comedy shows sell out in advance. Popular touring artists, tribute performances, and special events often sell out quickly. Guests interested in attending a show are encouraged to check the New York City event calendar regularly and purchase tickets early to secure their preferred seating.

Add river views and warm hospitality, and you’ve found NYC’s most inviting brunch scene.
