World Music Series
Upcoming Performances 
experience the richness and diversity of global cultures through live performance
Featuring artists from around the world and across traditions, the World Music Series at CSPS celebrates music, movement, language, and storytelling that reflect the deep cultural roots and contemporary expressions of communities near and far.
Each performance offers an opportunity to connect—across borders, histories, and artistic traditions. From intimate solo performances to powerful ensemble works, the World Music Series showcases artists who blend heritage and innovation, offering audiences an immersive cultural experience that goes beyond sound alone.
Guided by the World Music Series Committee, the series is thoughtfully curated to reflect artistic excellence, cultural significance, and community impact. The committee plays a vital role in identifying artists, shaping the series, and expanding access—particularly by engaging underserved communities and creating meaningful entry points for new audiences.
Together, these performances form a vibrant, ongoing celebration of global creativity—one that positions CSPS as a gathering place for cultural exchange, discovery, and shared experience.

Emerging ARtist Series 
Cultivating the Next Generation of Artists
The Emerging Artist Series is a performance focused mentorship program that pairs nationally touring artists with local and regional emerging musicians. Designed to support early-career artists, each event in this series provides emerging artists with a paid performance opportunity alongside an established touring artist, while connecting them with new audiences and professional networks.
In addition to performing, participating artists have the opportunity for informal mentorship with both the touring artist they've been paired with and CSPS staff. Conversations within these mentorship gatherings focus on practical aspects of building a sustainable music career, including touring, the business side of being a professional musician, career development, and audience engagement.
The Emerging Artist Series intentionally supports artists from a range of backgrounds, including solo performers and bands. By combining fair compensation, live performance experience, professional mentorship, and industry insight, this series provides meaningful support and pathways for emerging artists to grow their careers.
