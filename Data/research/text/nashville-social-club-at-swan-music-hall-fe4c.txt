Our Place or Yours!
 Catering & Banquet Space in Carson City
Nashville Social, Carson City, offers complete banquet and catering services. Our private dining spaces accommodate 20-200 for seated or buffet style events.
Hosting an office or at home gathering?
 Leave the planning and execution to us!
Our experienced staff will be delighted to serve your guests allowing you the time to enjoy your special event. Event planning, menu selection, entertainment, all available from our experienced leadership team.
We offer many options though welcome your requests and are happy to accommodate you with flexible dining formats.
Plated Service
Thoughtfully curated courses beautifully presented directly to your guests. 
Buffet Service
Offers a relaxed, inviting dining experience with a spread of chef-crafted favorites your guests can enjoy at their own pace.
Appetizer Reception
Appetizer Reception- Also known as a “Grazing Table” Perfect for mingling; passed or stationed hors d’oeuvres.
We require a minimum of 25 people for each themed buffet.
We do offer plated options. If you do desire to customize a plated dining option please feel free to reach out.

Southern Charm, Dining for 20-45 guests, Private or Semi-Private Gatherings
