EVENTS
Pine Creek Lodge is a unique live music venue featuring shows under the stars and among the pines. We strive to book some of the best Bluegrass, Rock, Jam, Country and everything in between. We sell domestic and local beer and wines. 
There is free parking on site for every show.
Arrange A Concert Shuttle
Purchase Overnight Car Camping
Sign Up For The Green Crew
 “- Tickets are not transferable or refundable - all purchases are final
- All tickets are Will Call. We do not print or mail any tickets.
- All tickets are general admission, no assigned seats
- Concerts go on rain or shine
- No re-entry & no outside food or drink
- No chairs within 25’ of the stage
- Dogs must be leashed
- Ages 12 and under are free - no ticket required (13+ need a ticket)
- Respect our neighbors as you enter and leave the venue - don’t litter & pipe down!”
 — The Fun Police

FAQS
We are currently CLOSED until January
If you don't see the answer you're looking for - email us!
LIVE MUSIC
Shows are all ages. Kids 12 and under are free (no ticket required), 13 and older require a full price ticket. Kids may attend all shows as long as they are accompanied by a parent.
There are no ticket transfers.
All purchases are final.
All tickets are Will Call only. We do not print or mail any tickets. 
Arrange A Shuttle. Contact Party Bus Rentals directly - (406) 220-3072
Park in our FREE parking lot. The lot opens at noon the day of the show.
Gates open at 6p for all ticketed shows. There is No Re-Entry. 
Beer, wine and wide variety of food items are available on site. No outside food or drink. 
A sample concert menu is available here. Offerings vary by the night.
No hard alcohol is allowed anywhere on the property.
We have water refill stations.
Concerts go on rain or shine. This is Montana!
No firearms are allowed inside the venue.
What to bring: No coolers. Feel free to bring umbrellas in case of rain. Tents or canopies can only be used in the Tent Spots. Backpacks are fine but will be searched.
There's space for chairs and blankets, but we ask you not to set these up within 20 feet of the stage to leave room for dancing. 
Pets are welcome at all concerts but must be kept on a leash.
Sign up here to receive email updates about upcoming events.
Sign up for the Green Crew.
RESTAURANT
Typical January-April Hours: Wednesday Trivia - 6p-9p || Concert Nights 6p-10p. Check our Events calendar before you come!
From January-April, we are CLOSED Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday.
The Main Lodge Restaurant is always CLOSED from May-December.
We no longer serve Brunch on the weekends. 
On Main Stage concert nights, we serve a variety of food inside the venue after the gates open at 6p.
On Beer Garden side stage concert nights, we serve food and drinks “takeout window style” from 5p-8p ish.
A sample concert menu is available here. Offerings vary by the night.
Park in our FREE parking lot or in the parking area adjacent to the main Lodge. Do NOT park along East River Road.
We do not host weddings, outside events or parties.
CAR CAMPING
Stay overnight in our grass parking lot.
This is CAR camping - this means camping in your CAR. Not a tent or a yurt or a hastily assembled log cabin.
If your vehicle is more than 20 feet long or you have any type of trailer - you must buy TWO passes.
You will NOT have access to the bath house.
You will have access to port-a-potties and running water.
This does NOT include tickets to the show.
Keep your email receipt handy for security staff.
CRASH PAD CABINS & Canvas TENTS
Check in time is 4pm, check out time is 11am.
Our Crash Pads are gathered around our music venue. We have concerts most nights outside during the summer - most shows begin at 7pm and run until 11pm.
Our Canvas Tents are just a few feet outside the music venue along the fence line.
Park in our FREE parking lot.
There is No Re-Entry to the venue after 6p on concert nights. Bring anything you may want from your vehicle. Canvas Tent’s may not return to their tent during the concert.
If there is a concert, Crash Pad and Canvas Tent guests receive 2 free tickets to any/every concert that occurs during your stay. Seriously, we’re not lying. There is no need to double check. See the lineup.
Outside food and drink is limited to overnight Crash Pad & Tent Spot guests only. Canvas Tent guests may not bring any food or alcohol into the venue. 
No hard alcohol is allowed on the property.
Our unique accommodations offer a lot of amenities, however they do not have plumbing. The bathhouse is 20 to 50 feet from your door, depending on where you are staying - the bath house contains four private suites, each containing a commode, sink and shower. 
Enjoy the absolutely free fresh air while you’re here.
We provide linens, towels, soap and shampoo. The Crash Pads and Canvas Tents also have in-room coffee.
Pets are welcome for a $25 cleaning fee per accommodation (not per night). If you fail to register your pet at check in, you will be charged $100. We do ask that you generally keep dogs leashed, especially during shows, for their own safety. Please don’t leave behind any landmines.
TENT Spots
Check in time for Tent Spots is 4p.
Tent Spots are gathered around our on-site music venue and are designed to be concert tent camping. We have concerts most nights outside during the summer - most shows begin at 7pm and run until 11pm. If you are looking for private camping, this is not the place.
The Inside Venue Tent Spots, are located within the venue fence.
Park in our FREE parking lot. 
There is No Re-Entry to the venue. Bring anything you may need into the concert. 
If there is a concert, Tent Spot guests receive 2 free tickets to any/every concert that occurs during your stay. Seriously, we’re not lying. There is no need to double check. See the lineup.
Outside food and drink is limited to overnight Crash Pad & Tent Spot guests only.
No hard alcohol is allowed on the property.
One tent per Spot, with a maximum of 2 adults. 
There’s no way to get your car close to the Tent Spot.
Access to four bathroom suites, each containing a commode, sink and shower.
There's a fresh water pump next to the bathhouse.
Our Tent Spots do not have electricity but they do have access to free Wi-Fi.
Pets are welcome. We do ask that you generally keep dogs leashed, especially during shows, for their own safety. Please don’t leave behind any landmines.
