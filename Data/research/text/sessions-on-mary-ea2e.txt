About Sessions
Sessions @ Mary Street
Sessions On Mary is the living room at 604 W Mary. All shows are BYOB. For most shows some mixers, cups, ice, lemons/limes, are provided; along with some booze. South Austin friendly, inside and out. Light snacks are usually provided. Feel free to bring some, but definitely not expected.
Special thanks to Tito's Handmade Vodka
Sessions @ the Farm
The Farm is a hidden little meadow at 2202 Riverside Farms Road. Shows there are also BYOB. Bring a chair and chill out. Clean port-o-pot provided. Definitely okay to bring in food, drink, or whatever gets you going.
Support Austin Music
We're proud to be a part of Support Austin Music TV, a music video program on Austin public access Channel 16.
