Commemorating 10 Seasons of Shows on October 4, 2025
We launched Five Points Live! in May of 2016, and on October 4, 2025, we presented a celebration of our 10th season of shows with a special concert honoring the musical history of the neighborhood, and supported by a generous grant from Denver Arts & Venues, and with support from Gift of Jazz. The sold out show at Savoy Denver featured Nani Vazana singing Nina Simone and a hint of JS Bach, plus Ley Line harmonizing on world folk, blues, and funk, and Chantil & The Dukes of Art, a trio led by jazz pianist Chantil Dukart. In addition to these mainstage artists, local fave Laurie Dameron rocked the jazz and blues guitar​ on the mezzanine. ​Tickets were just $10 (for 10 years!) and the awesome space at our neighborhood treasure was also activated with the Maiz food truck, craft cocktails and Jakrabbits zero proof beverages, and Thick's local gelato, to help us celebrate a decade of Five Points Live!​

REMEMBERING JILL SOBULE, 1959-2025
Jill Sobule played the first season of our living room series in 2016, and again the next year, and she was the artist whom we presented the most during the first decade of Five Points Live! Jill was an award-winning singer-songwriter, a human rights activist and queer icon, and she was our friend. Jill Sobule died in a tragic house fire in the Twin Cities area on May 1, the day she was to fly to Denver, her hometown. Beyond our living room, we worked to present Jill in the home where she grew up in Denver’s Hilltop neighborhood, as well as at the home of her late mother’s best friend. We arranged for Jill to be a surprise guest at Jews Do Jews in 2023, and our final show with Jill was at the Savoy Denver for 100 people in December of 2024. We were to serve as her Tour Manager in May of 2025, and had planned to announce that Jill’s autobiographical musical, F*CK 7th GRADE, was booked for a run in Denver in September of that year. We miss Jill so much - her always original music, the way she made friends with everyone, her spontaneity, and those texts we'd send during Nuggets and Broncos games. Stay tuned for some opportunities to celebrate Jill's life in the coming months, and join us in spinning Jill Sobule tunes any time you need a lift, a laugh, a good cry, or some inspiration. Maggie & Doug's Suggestions for Grabbing a Bite to Eat and a Drink Before a Show 
Our house concert venue is located near many great places to eat and drink, and we're often asked to offer recommendations for where folks can grab a bite before a show. These are some of our favorites that are closest to home: (Click on the name of each establishment for more info:)
Famous Original J's Pizza & 715 Club - walk-up window for slices, beverages next-door, outdoor seating, at the 5 Points. 
Taco Uprising - new favorite on Welton Street, the birria taco is fabulous and great cocteles complete a meal.
​Work & Class - Lively, yummy.
Cart - Driver - next to W&C but not affiliated; a quirky favorite, we enjoy the oysters, pizzas, and negronis.
Xiquita - highly anticipated new restaurant bringing the vibrant flavors and culture of Mexico City to the Mile High City.
Las Delicias + La Pasadita Inn - a couple of nearby, traditional Mexican restaurants.
Walters 303 - our go-to pizza, plus wings, salads, calzones, cocktails and robust beer list in a sit-down Uptown location.
​Campfire - focus on Colorado-sourced ingredients, beers, and spirits make this a good pre-concert option.
Coperta - Southern Italian fare from an accomplished chef in a warm setting with a full bar.
Yazoo BBQ - slow cooked Memphis Style BBQ and a rooftop bar.
Uchi - this Austin, TX, export is the closest sushi to our place. It's a unique approach to the cuisine, and rather high end.
Ratio Beerworks Taproom - our liquid sponsor has the best beer garden and brew choices plus plenty to eat nearby.
The Block Distilling Co. - our occasional liquid sponsor offers handcrafted spirits, awesome cocktails and plenty to eat nearby.

"Music is our home base, and having concerts here makes our house a home."
Who We Are (music lovers)
Greetings Fellow Music Fans! We are Doug Gertner and Maggie Miller, pictured here at a music festival in Barcelona, Spain. Our lives in Denver and beyond always circle back to live music, so when we moved into a suitable space - a modern townhouse in Denver's Historic and Cultural Five Points neighborhood - it was only fitting to open our home to live music. We are not professional promoters; in fact, all proceeds from our house concerts go directly to the musicians. For us, the joy of hearing and sharing music is what it's all about. Please join us.
Our Home (the venue)
Here's the truth: We've grown tired of large venues and stadium concerts, and clubs with no place to sit. We love a good live show, and the more intimate the better. Our house concerts - held in the living room of our modern urban townhome - are limited to 40 people, and everyone gets a seat. If weather permits, please take in the sweeping city views from our rooftop deck. Please note: the venue may not be well suited to young children; also, our home and concert area requires stairs to enter, and the rooftop deck is up four more flights of stairs.

Simple Steps to Reserve Your Seats
FIVE POINTS LIVE! is all about putting you in a great seat for a wonderful house concert, and all about putting ​all proceeds directly into the hands of the performers. That's why we offer a limited number of reservations for every show, and collect donations in advance. Just use the buttons below to reserve your seats for our upcoming concerts. 
ALL SALES ARE FINAL. We pass along all donations directly to the artists and thus cannot provide refunds. Reservations may be transferred to others. WHAT HAPPENS NEXT? Everyone who reserves a seat gets a CONFIRMATION EMAIL at the address that was used to make their reservations; on the day before the concert, another EMAIL is sent with​ CONCERT LOCATION ADDRESS, PARKING, and ADDITIONAL INFO.
