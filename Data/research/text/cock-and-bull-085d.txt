Welcome Home
Since 1976 the singular objective has been to maintain a welcoming environment. A place where you can walk in, take a deep breath, slow down just a little, relax and let us help you feel at home. Smell the homemade bread, the roast, the brisket on the smoker, the sap boiling, the warmth of the fire. Take in the music, the structure, the gardens, the bird feeders. Founder Don Staber stated it best when he carved into the mantle “A Friendly Place by the Side of the Road”.
That’s it. That’s our mission.

Rustic Acoustics
When James Cavert built this barn in 1852, he surely wasn’t thinking about acoustics, but he did a pretty damn good job… and with 15+ years of audio reinforcement from Aaron Kovacik and Dream Lab Productions, we challenge you to find a better sounding space.
Throughout the year, we host a wide variety of world-class musicians, both inside the restaurant and outside on our expansive patio.

Korby Lenker

 6:00 PM
 7:00 PM
 Google Calendar

Korby Lenker grew up in Idaho on the rim of the Snake River Canyon and has spent two decades as a touring folksinger, routinely logging 150 shows a year. The live set has always been the center of it.
His eighth album, Man in the Maroon, landed on American Songwriter's Best Albums of the Year. He's a first-place winner at the Rocky Mountain Folks Festival, the Chris Austin Songwriting Contest at MerleFest, and the International Songwriting Competition, and has played Telluride Bluegrass Festival, Bumbershoot, and Sasquatch.
"He doesn't so much play the guitar as become part of it." — No Depression
Korby lives in East Nashville with his wife and daughter.
That’s the official bio Korby provided. I can’t help but elaborate a bit. Korby is a tremendous guitarist and he’s a beautiful singer indeed. He’s also a terrific storyteller, an incredibly kind and thoughtful person, a great writer/author and he hosts a podcast, Morse Code, where he goes beyond interviewing folks in the music industry. He asks thought provoking questions and gives the guests room and time to think and answer appropriately. Korby is a good human. He’s been away too long and I’m particularly happy he’s returning. Come meet him and find out for yourself…
Seats are $15

Zan & The Winter Folk is an Americana band from Troy, NY. Led by Zan Strumfeld, The Winter Folk create anthems for the revivalists, lullabies for the lonely, and ballads for the hopeful. Vacillating between an acoustic quartet — two guitars, banjo and upright bass — and a rocking quintet with drums, The Winter Folk blend an upbeat, rootsy spirit with a melodic longing and vulnerability in their storytelling. With a captivating sound and comedic banter, The Winter Folk reveal a unique and intimate performance wherever they play. The band has two EPs, “How To Be Alone” (2019) and "Your Girl No More" (2018). Their full-length album, “New Morse Code,” released in June 2024, won Album of the Year at the 2025 Eddies Music Awards, as well as Americana Band of the Year. They are currently doing the finishing touches on their second album.
Seats are $15

Bluegrass endures because of bands like midnight choir that see limitless possibilities in the
blending of mandolin, guitar, bass and voices. The twang of the southern mountains whispers
through the melodies of original songs by upstate New York's midnight choir. With melodic and
playing techniques drawn from jazz, classical and global traditions, this is brilliantly arranged
stringband music that stands out like a cool island in the sea of bluegrass. Inspired by various
bluegrass, jazz, classical, and world influences, this three-man ensemble is an exciting addition
to the Northeast roots music scene.
Jake Ali | Mandolin – A double major in performance and composition at the Crane School,
Jake is trained and proficient on several stringed and wind instruments. Jake is also an active
member of Maggie’s Clan, playing fiddle for the Irish rock band.
Jon Doll | Bass – Founding member of Binghamton, NY’s Driftwood, Jon has been making
waves in the Cincinnati, OH music scene for the past few years, earning a Best Of from
CityBeat magazine, and providing music for Jerry Springer’s podcast.
Jeff Wasbes | Guitar – Although he found bluegrass later in life, Jeff went on to help found Blind
Crow, a progressive bluegrass quartet.
No cover!

We generally don’t host concerts in December, other than Christmas shows. I make an exception when Jeffrey Martin is on tour. Among my favorite songwriters on the planet, with his thunderous baritone vocals and articulate acoustic playing, he can play here whenever he wants!
Jeffrey Martin has been reaching for quiet and stories since he was a kid hiding under the
covers with a Discman. He wrote his way through a writing degree, a teaching degree, and
years of high school English classes filled with unruly brilliance, all while making records and
playing shows whenever he could. Eventually, he left teaching to follow the same dream he
encouraged his students to chase. After releasing Thank God We Left the Garden, he recorded
Alive July 25, 2025, a no-edits live album captured in Portland with Sam Weber. Jeffrey tours
full time now, still missing teaching like an old friend he’ll meet again.
Corey Parsons opens.
