FAQ
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - Most of the information you need for the shows or open mics can be found here.
 1. HOW DO I BOOK A SHOW AT THE MUSE?
If you are sending a booking request (original music) please email info@eveningmuse.com with the following information:
Dates & Times requested
Solo or Full Band
Recent hard-ticketed headline shows (& number of tickets sold)
Show history in the Charlotte area
Approx. attendance anticipated for a live show at the Muse
Website/socials and live and recorded video and music links.
While we review every request, if you are an up-and-coming act, we strongly encourage you to network with established acts to schedule a show opening for them or let us know if you see a compatible act that might be interested in your support. Keep in mind that headliners are usually looking for someone with a history in the area, and the ability to bring in 15-20. 
We typically look for headliners that can draw 60+ on a weeknight, or 80+, bar heavy bands, on Fri. & Sat. night. Sundays & Tuesdays we're usually closed unless we're certain to fill the room. Mondays are our weekly open mics. 
Due to the high volume of requests, we might not get to all the EPKs submitted, so setting up a show with an established act is the best way to get your first show here, or to schedule a return date more quickly. You should also come to our weekly Monday night Open Mics to get seen and heard by a large audience, as well as our staff members. 
We book about 3 months in advance. 
 2. IS THIS AN ALL AGES VENUE?
A Valid State Issued ID is required of all guests to enter the venue, regardless of your age (unless you are a minor with a parent). Anyone under the age of 18 requires accompanying parent or adult guardian. There is a $5 surcharge for anyone under the age of 21. Most of our events are ALL AGES, but still under 18 must have a parent or adult guardian.
On occasion, we might have a performance that requires 18+ due to the adult nature of the content. Most of the comedy shows fall under this category. We will post on our website if the show is 18+, or adult supervision suggested. 
 3. IS THERE SEATING?
Some of our shows will have the chairs down, occasionally with reserved seating, (which will be noted on the website link for that show). Some of the shows are standing only, and some have a combination. This is determined by the performers prior to the show, sometimes not until their arrival. Some artists prefer the quiet intimacy of a seated audience, and some want the audience up close, participating, and occasionally dancing. All scenarios can be extremely enjoyable and make for an entertaining, emotional, and sometimes magical, experience. We encourage you to take advantage of all show types.
GENERAL ADMISSION SEATING IS ON A FIRST COME-FIRST SERVED BASIS. There are times when the room is very full, and unfortunately we cannot guarantee a seat per ticket purchase. The exception is when a show has reserved seats sold in advance. These limited number of seats usually sell out quickly, so be sure to follow and check our website, and follow our socials for low ticket alerts.
RESERVED SEATING
No Guaranteed Seating unless you’ve purchased a Reserved Seating ticket option (not offered on all shows). 
 4. HOW DO I GET TO THE EVENING MUSE?
Directions: The Evening Muse is located just two miles north of Uptown Charlotte.
From Uptown/Center City: Travel north on North Tryon Street approximately 2 miles; turn right on 36th Street; travel 1/2 mile to North Davidson Street.
From the University area: Take Highway 29/Tryon Street south (towards uptown); turn left on 36th Street; travel 1/2 mile to North Davidson Street.
From I-77: Take the I-77 exit to 277/Brookshire Freeway East (exit 11 East/Downtown). You will also be fine if you take I-277/John Belk Freeway, since I-277 loops into I-77. Take the Davidson Street exit and turn onto Davidson Street going north, away from downtown. Travel north on N. Davidson Street approximately 2 miles; the Muse is at the intersection of N. Davidson & 36th Street.
From I-85 North: Take the Sugar Creek exit and take a right onto Sugar Creek Road. Continue about a mile to N. Tryon Street. Take a right onto N. Tryon Street south (towards uptown); proceed about .5 miles on N. Tryon and turn left on 36th Street; travel 1/2 mile to North Davidson Street.
From I-85 South: Take the Sugar Creek exit and take a left onto Sugar Creek Road. Continue about a mile to N. Tryon Street. Take a right onto N. Tryon Street south (towards uptown); proceed about .5 miles on N. Tryon and turn left on 36th Street; travel 1/2 mile to North Davidson Street. 
 5. WHERE CAN I PARK?
Parking
Here is the most recent information we have on parking in NoDa. We cannot guarantee there won’t be any changes, so follow all signs and parking laws in the neighborhood.
While The Evening Muse does not have its own parking lot, the NoDa area has many available parking lots. Please keep in mind that each of these parking facilities charges a fee. It’s important to note that The Evening Muse does not own or operate any of these parking facilities.
The Mercury Parking Garage
The Mercury parking garage behind Neighborhood Theatre has some public parking available. You must pay to park in this parking garage. The parking garage entrance is at 3310 N. Davidson Street, near the 36th Street intersection.
Parking rates (subject to change): 3 hours- $12.00 / 5 hours- $16.00 / 10 hours- $25.00
Yadkin Ave/E 35th St Parking Lot – 3217 Yadkin Avenue/513 E. 35th St
Yadkin Ave Parking Lot are located across from the venue between 36th Street and 35th Street on Yadkin Ave. From the venue, turn on Yadkin Ave and go past the Smelly Cat Coffeehouse parking lot. There is a gravel parking lot on your right.
Parking rates (subject to change): Mon-Wed: $11.00 / Thu-Sun: $15.00
3320 N Davidson St Parking Lot
From the venue, cross over N. Davidson Street, and the parking lot is located on the left after the first set of railroad tracks.
Parking rates (subject to change): Mon-Wed: $10.00 / Thu-Sun: $14.00
36th Street Parking Lot – 436 E 36th Street
35th Street Parking Lot – 410 E 35th Street
Parking Lot behind The Protagonist building.
YMCA Parking Lot – 3025 N Davidson Street
*This lot is only open to the public after YMCA business hours.
Light rail and bus services also provide easy transit options into and out of the neighborhood, with the light rail stop at 36th Street and bus lines 3 and 23 making several stops in the neighborhood. The 36th Street stop is less than one block behind The Evening Muse. 
 6. WHAT TIME IS THE SHOW?
Our hours vary depending upon the night and the show. Please check our website and each show detail for show times.
**Please note, on most Friday and Saturday nights we try and book 2 shows. The first 7:30pm-9:30pm and the 2nd at 10:30pm-12:30am (sometimes later). Each of these shows require a separate ticket or tickets will have to be purchased for each show. We empty the room at 9:30pm to open the doors at 10pm for the late show. 
 7. DO YOU SELL ALCOHOL?
Yes. We provide a wide variety of locally brewed beers, soft drinks and a full line of liquors mixed by our friendly bartenders. 
 8. CAN I MEET THE BAND?
Most all the artists welcome the opportunity for a meet and greet with their fans following their performance. This is a great way to say hello, possibly get a photograph and of course most importantly, to buy their merchandise. We want them to be able to keep touring and return to the Muse!

About us and reviews

The Evening Muse opened in April of 2001 and thanks to the music-lovin’ citizens of Charlotte and venturous visitors to our fair city, the Muse has had the opportunity to present literally thousands of original independent music acts and performing artists since opening. From our own Carolinas’ music scene to performers from all corners of the globe, an amazing array of artists has graced our stage: from acoustic music to full electric bands; alternative, Americana, blues, country, folk, hip-hop, jazz, pop, reggae, rock, R&B – and where it all begins, with the songwriter.
The Muse brings acoustic music and much more to Charlotte in a great-sounding room. There is excellent original artwork on the walls, a great selection of beer, wine and spirits, and music from some of the finest local, regional and national talent. The Muse is located in the NoDa Arts District in Historic North Charlotte, a great neighborhood with a diverse mix of galleries, shops, restaurants and homes. 
Things you should know before attending a show at The Evening Muse:
We strive to provide a wonderful experience for all attendees and performers. Please keep conversations during performances to a minimum, so all can experience the artists in a listening room environment with an excellent sound system and engineer.
All concerts and events are open to all ages unless otherwise specified. Under 18 requires accompanying parent or adult guardian. (Comedy shows are usually 18+ but check the website first.) There is a $5 surcharge at the door if you are under 21.
We are only open during show nights, so check our website schedule. Typically, early shows doors are at 7pm, showtime 7:30pm; Late Fri/Sat shows doors 10pm; showtime 10:30pm. We close shortly after showtime and merch sales/meet and greets are complete.
If you do not have a legal ID, even if you are over 21, we will not be able to serve you alcohol - NO EXCEPTIONS. 
Some of our shows will have the chairs down, and some of the shows are standing only, and some have a combination. This is determined by the performers prior to the show, sometimes not until their arrival. Some prefer the quiet intimacy of a seated audience, and some want the audience up close, participating, and occasionally dancing. All scenarios can be extremely enjoyable and make for an entertaining, emotional, and sometimes magical, experience. We encourage you to take advantage of all show types.
GENERAL ADMISSION SEATING IS ON A FIRST COME-FIRST SERVED BASIS. There are times when the room is very full, and unfortunately we cannot guarantee a seat per ticket purchase.
Most shows start promptly at the advertised time. If there is an opener, it will be noted on the website and Facebook Event page. Occasionally there will be co-bills, with 2 bands sharing the entire length of the show. It this case, it is usually decided at sound check which band will perform first.
Club capacity: 120. Full PA and sound engineer provided. We book ORIGINAL bands, not cover bands. Please do not have any other shows booked in the area for 30 days before or after a Muse date.
