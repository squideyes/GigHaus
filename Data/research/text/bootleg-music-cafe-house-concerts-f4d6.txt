Pat Byrne
Spectacular Singer-Songwriter who easily fills a stage as a Solo Artist
Hi Guys…Please…Do your ears and heart a big favor; Go to your favorite music platform and put Pat’s latest self-released album, Into the Light, on repeat. I swear you will be an immediate fan. He is a musician just how I adore them: Fabulous Lyricist with a voice equal parts grit and luscious, big hearted with a witty-witty mind. (And a just the right amount of “dark roast” for my taste buds.)
I am SO EXCITED for Pat to return to the Bootleg. He played here just about 2 years ago, bringing us to tears & laughter, and wandering, “How have we not heard of this guy before?!”. I’ve been working with his Agent in the immediate-after, to bring him back. He is a Music Dream, do not miss your chance to see/hear him live.
An Irish singer-songwriter, Pat Byrne has come a long way since his first deal with Universal. In late 2017, Byrne began his migration to Austin, Texas. In 2019, Pat took the US by storm, with breakout performances at the 30A Festival, SXSW, Kerrville Folk Festival and the Americana Festival. Being immersed in the Texas music scene, Pat's sophomore US release, Into the Light, has an edgier feel underscoring both emotional depth and greater confidence, while blending new influences with his rich Irish heritage.
What people who have an ear for these things are signing to the rafters:
“The overall sound draws easy comparisons to modern gritty Americana artists like David Ramirez, Ryan Adams, and Jason Isbell. Byrne deserves to be in that company with his gritty vocal and poignant lyrical style.” ~ Ear to the Ground Music
“When you combine the transcendent poetry of Bob Dylan with the gritty, real-world rock-n-roll storytelling that Bruce Springsteen made so famous, and then add a dash of the political activism embodied by Woody Guthrie and Pete Seeger, you’ll have Irish singer-songwriter Pat Byrne” ~Folk Alley at the 30a Songwriters Fesival
“The raspy melodic soul of Byrne’s voice recalls the emotional spells the late Austin troubadour Jimmy LaFave used to cast, though Byrne’s songwriting bears a more distinctive Irish stamp. He’s more contemporary than strict traditionalists, putting him more in the league of Glen Hansard or Luka Bloom, perhaps; yet at times he conjures a deeply old-school feel — especially on the spectacular “Hills of Killedmond,” ~ Peter Blackstock - Austin American Statesman
“You won’t find this on his website, but it’s right there in Wikipedia: Pat was the first winner of “The Voice of Ireland.” Maybe he thinks it’s corney and Un-Americana to tout this, but I think it signals what a talent he is.” ~ Cassie Baugher - The Bootleg Tiki Lounge & Music Cafe’ (hahaha)

The Bootleg is in Sacramento’s Hollywood Park Neighborhood
I am beyond grateful to the professional musicians who have trusted me to perform at the Bootleg. Oh, what you have gifted Bootleggers; Your glorious music & lyrics, your hearts & souls, stories & wit. Many of you had no idea what you were walking into, and yet you came, and transported us to the Bootleg Musical Love Bubble. My life has been forever transformed by your beautiful, generous spirits. Thank you for knowing in your bones that the World needs Music.
