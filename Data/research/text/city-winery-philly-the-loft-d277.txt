 Plan Your Visit
Directions, Parking & Dining at City Winery Philadelphia
City Winery Philadelphia is located at 990 Filbert Street in the Fashion District, just steps from Center City and near Rittenhouse Square, one of the city’s most vibrant dining and entertainment areas. Whether you're coming for live music, a comedy show, or dinner and a show, getting here is easy.
Parking: City Winery Philadelphia is conveniently located near exits for I-95 and I-676 for those who are driving to the venue. There are a number of paid lots and street parking in the area, including The Autopark at the Fashion Districted directly across from the venue.
Public Transit: We are easily accessible via public transportation, located just one block away from SEPTA and NJ Transit bus routes at 10th & Market Street, one block from the SEPTA Regional Rail Jefferson Station, two blocks from the New Jersey Transit 8th Street Station, and a few blocks from the SEPTA Market-Frankford Line and Broad Street Line subway stops. These transit options make City Winery Philadelphia easily reachable from across Center City and the greater Philadelphia area.
Rideshare: Uber, Lyft, and other rideshare services frequently drop off and pick up guests directly in front of the venue, making it convenient to arrive or leave after a show.
 FAQ
 Where is City Winery Philadelphia located?
City Winery Philadelphia is located at 990 Filbert Street in the Fashion District, just steps from Center City and near Rittenhouse Square, one of the city’s most vibrant dining and entertainment areas. The venue sits in the heart of downtown Philadelphia, surrounded by restaurants, shopping, and nightlife. Many guests visit City Winery for a full evening experience, combining dinner, wine, and live music in one location.
 What types of concerts and shows happen at City Winery Philadelphia?
City Winery Philadelphia hosts a diverse lineup of live music, comedy shows, podcast recordings, brunch performances, and special events throughout the year. Our calendar regularly features national touring artists, singer-songwriters, jazz musicians, tribute concerts, and curated local performances. Because of the venue’s intimate concert setting, audiences are able to experience performances up close in a seated environment.
 Can I order food and wine during the show?
Yes. City Winery Philadelphia is known for our dinner and a show experience, allowing guests to enjoy full dinner service and award-winning wines during most performances. Guests can order food and drinks directly from their table while watching the show, or arrive early to enjoy the restaurant or wine bar before the performance begins.
 Is seating reserved at City Winery concerts?
Most shows feature reserved seating so guests can relax and enjoy the performance with food and wine service throughout the evening.
 What time do doors open for shows?
Door times can vary depending on the event, but guests are typically able to enter the venue 60 to 90 minutes before showtime. Arriving early is recommended if you plan to order dinner or drinks before the performance begins.
 What makes City Winery different from other Philadelphia concert venues?
City Winery Philadelphia offers a unique concert and dining experience that combines live music, award-winning wines, and chef-driven cuisine under one roof. Unlike many traditional music venues, guests can enjoy a full meal and wine service while watching the performance. The venue’s intimate concert hall also allows audiences to experience artists in a closer, more personal setting than many larger Philadelphia venues.
 What is the concert experience like at City Winery Philadelphia?
City Winery Philadelphia offers an intimate concert experience where guests can enjoy live performances in a seated venue with table service. Unlike larger concert halls, the space is designed to bring audiences closer to the music, allowing guests to enjoy dinner, wine, and cocktails while watching the show. The venue’s acoustics and smaller capacity make it a favorite for fans looking to experience artists in a more personal setting.
 Do shows frequently sell out?
Because City Winery Philadelphia is an intimate venue with limited seating, many concerts and comedy shows sell out in advance. Popular touring artists, tribute performances, and special events often fill up quickly. Guests interested in attending a show are encouraged to check the event calendar regularly and purchase tickets early to secure their preferred seating.
