Welcome
Welcome to the historical Blue Door — The Best Listening Room in Oklahoma. 
Housed in a space steeped in local music history, The Blue Door has built its reputation on one simple idea: give great songwriters a room where the audience actually listens. Every week, we bring you live performances from the best local, regional, and national singer-songwriters and musicians, in a setting built for the music, not distractions.
​
Come pull up a chair and settle in for a night that feels different from your typical concert venue — intimate, unplugged in spirit, and centered entirely on the songs. Whether you're a longtime regular or walking through our doors for the first time, you'll find a listening room experience that's become a beloved part of Oklahoma's music scene.

Good to Know
A few essentials before you head to the show.
FOOD & DRINK: The Blue Door does not serve food or drinks. We are a BYOB establishment — wine, beer, and non-alcoholic drinks only. Please, no mixed drinks or large coolers.​
TICKETS: TicketStorm is the only authorized ticket seller for The Blue Door — please avoid third-party sites or resellers. 
AT THE DOOR: Tickets are available at the door — CASH ONLY. We do not accept credit or debit cards for door sales, so please come prepared with cash.
DOOR TIME: The doors open 30 minutes before showtime. Most shows start at 8:00 pm, so doors generally open at 7:30 pm.
AGE LIMIT: There is no age limit to attend Blue Door shows. Minors must be accompanied by an adult.
PARKING: Free parking is available in the lot South of The Blue Door. Street parking is also available around the venue, but please be respectful of our neighbors when choosing your spot.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
All
Where can I purchase tickets?
Tickets can be purchased through TicketStorm prior to the show. This is the only authorized ticket seller for The Blue Door — please avoid third-party sites or resellers, as there are many ticketing scams and we are unable to honor tickets purchased elsewhere.
​
Tickets are handled electronically; you will not receive a paper ticket. Instead, your name will be added to our Will Call list at the door.
​
After purchasing: Check your email (including spam/junk folders) for a confirmation of your order. If you don't receive a confirmation email, check your bank or card statement to verify the purchase went through — if it did, your name will still be on the list at the door.
Can I buy tickets at the door?
Yes, tickets are available at the door — CASH ONLY. We do not accept credit or debit cards for door sales, so please come prepared with cash.
What time do the doors open?
The doors open 30 minutes before showtime. Most shows start at 8:00 pm, so doors generally open at 7:30 pm.
Is there a student discount?
In an effort to encourage young musicians and songwriters to visit The Blue Door and learn from master players and writers, we offer a discount for high school students with a valid student ID, for tickets purchased at the door.
Is there an age limit to attend The Blue Door?
There is no age limit to attend Blue Door shows, and we occasionally host shows specifically for children. Minors must be accompanied by an adult.
Is seating reserved or general admission?
Seating is general admission and available on a first-come, first-served basis. Doors open 30 minutes before showtime, so arriving early is recommended for the best seats.
Where can I park?
Free parking is available in the lot South of The Blue Door. Street parking is also available around the venue, but please be respectful of our neighbors when choosing your spot.
Does The Blue Door serve food?
The Blue Door does not serve food. Patrons may bring snacks to enjoy during the show, provided it does not negatively impact the show.
Does The Blue Door serve beverages?
No. The Blue Door is a BYOB establishment — wine, beer, and non-alcoholic drinks only. Please, no mixed drinks or large coolers.
Is The Blue Door handicap accessible?
Yes.
What is your refund/exchange policy?
All ticket sales are final. We are unable to offer refunds or exchanges, except in the case of a cancelled show.
What happens if a show is cancelled or rescheduled?
If a show is cancelled or rescheduled, ticket holders will be notified via email and through our website and social media. Tickets for cancelled shows will be refunded automatically; tickets for rescheduled shows will remain valid for the new date unless you request a refund.
