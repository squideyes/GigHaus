The Artist's Walkway at Bull Run
The Artist's Walkway at Bull Run is managed by Gallery Sitka.
Gallery Sitka's mission is to support the arts, to be part of the creative economy and provide a space for emerging artists to be seen and to have the experience of showing their work in a gallery-style atmosphere.
A woman-owned and operated New England art gallery, Gallery Sitka® is located in Shirley, MA & also in Newport, RI. The gallery has been doing pop-ups all over the Northeast U.S. since 2014, representing the work of 20+ painters, sculptors, encaustic artists, and a few jewelry designers from New England, New York, Brazil, France & Italy.
To purchase art seen on our Artist's Walkway, contact Gallery Sitka owner
Tamar Russell Brown HERE.

BY PURCHASING TICKETS FROM OUR WEBSITE, YOU AGREE TO OUR TERMS OF SERVICE. 
Answers to the most common questions about attending a concert can be found here. 
​
There is no physical ticket. Your email receipt when you complete a purchase is your ticket and your dinner reservation. Please bring it with you printed out or have it in your email on your phone to check in.
No Flash Photography.
No standing during shows
​
Refund & Change Policy
Tickets are non-refundable for date changes or rescheduled shows.
If a show is cancelled, refunds will be issued for the ticket price only.
Ticketing and processing fees are non-refundable.
A $5 per-icket fee applies to seat changes requested after a purchase has been completed.
​
There is a $20. food/drink minimum per person for all shows. Your patronage while here ensures that we will be able to continue to present quality entertainment in our intimate, friendly & locally owned business. Thank you for supporting the independent venues that support local farms & live music.​
For all performances, you will have full table service for food & beverages to dine right in the same room where you see your show. 
No outside food or beverages may be brought in to the Bull Run. We reserve the right to remove anyone who does so. 
 Food, Beverage & Dessert service is not included in your ticket price. 
You do not need to make a separate dinner reservation to dine in the same room with your concert.
 ​
Our 3rd floor Ballroom shows seat 65 people with assigned seating. There are no obstructed views; no table is more than 25 feet from stage.
Our Sawtelle Room seats 300 people and has assigned tables. There are no obstructed views; no table is more than 40 feet from stage.
​
Your ticket purchase guarantees you a seat at a specific table, not a specific seat. All tables seat 8. Other patrons will be seated with you.
"Saving" seats is not allowed. You must be present in the room to choose the specific seat at your designated table. 
​
We cannot add another person to a table's seating capacity. 
Some shows include a dance floor which means dancing may occur near your table. Please consult the room diagram for your show to see whether there is a dance floor. If you do not want to be sitting next to a dance area, purchase your seat location accordingly.
Whether or not there is a dance floor is always at the performer's discretion.
​
We provide complimentary coat check on site. In order to allow easy access between aisles for staff, we do not allow coats to be hung over the back of your chair. You can wear it, or keep it on your lap, or check it at no charge. 
​
There is absolutely no vaping or smoking inside the building or anywhere immediately outside the building. 
Please do not smoke on or near our Covered Bridge or near the protected brook outside.
​
This is a listening venue, not a bar band situation. Patrons who disrupt the show or disrespect performers by talking loudly during a set will be removed.​​
​
If a show is postponed to another date, your ticket will automatically transfer to the rescheduled date at the same table. 
If for any reason you cannot make a rescheduled date, please contact our Front Desk @ 978-425-4311.
Please be aware that we are closed on Mondays and Tuesdays. Send us an email at info@bullrunrestaurant.com.
​
​
Our Bull Run Code of Conduct & BE NICE Rule
The Bull Run has been in our family for 80 years. Over eight decades, we have established a strict BE NICE rule. This means we encourage you to bring any concerns to the attention of ownership or management during your visit - not after the fact - and we will go out of our way to ensure your comfort and satisfaction. Our expectation is that you will respect our staff, the performers & other patrons.
By entering this property, you agree to comply with the following policies:
​
 We do not tolerate hate speech, whether verbal or written on articles of clothing.
Firearms and weapons of any kind are prohibited anywhere on this property, including all indoor and outdoor areas, event spaces, patios, parking areas, and vehicles used for business operations. This policy applies to all guests, visitors, vendors, and contractors, including individuals licensed to carry firearms. Law enforcement personnel acting in an official capacity are exempt.
We do not tolerate loud talking or other noise during performances. We will ask you to leave if your behavior is disruptive to performers, staff or other patrons. 
We do not allow you to block the view of patrons near or behind you by standing up, or filming/photographing shows or using flash.
ALL of our wait staff, bar staff & managers in every room are T.I.P.S. Certified (Training & Intervention Procedures for Servers). We back them up 100% if they feel any patron should not be served anywhere on site. The Bull Run reserves the right to refuse service in response to disrespectful behavior of any kind, particularly when it comes to our employees or performers. 
The Bull Run is not responsible for the personal opinions expressed from the stage by performers. 
More than 95% of our shows are appropriate for well-behaved children of all ages. If you have questions about a specific performance, we provide a write up on each performer on our ticket page. Management will be happy to answer any questions.
