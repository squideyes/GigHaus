FAQ 
 Where do I park?
Limited street parking is available on Summit Ave in front of the venue. After 5pm, guests are welcome to use the museum parking lot directly across the street. For overflow parking, we recommend the parking deck on Davie St across from Center City Park.
 Are you all ages?
We are an all ages club with some exceptions. The best way to find out if a show has age restrictions is on Etix. All guests under 21 will have their hands marked at the door. A $5 SURCHARGE WILL BE ADDED AT THE DOOR FOR ALL GUESTS UNDER 21 (EVEN IF TICKETS ARE PURCHASED IN ADVANCE) Guests 16 and under must always be accompanied by an adult.
 Do you serve food?
We do not serve food, but have delicious options within walking distance. 
Staff recommendations:
Cafe Europa- European cuisine–mussels, salads, sandwiches, steaks (table service)
Parkside Pull Up- burgers, hotdogs, milkshakes (counter service)
Lawn Service- coffee, beer, wine, treats

 Flat Iron
Bluegrass visionaries Hank Smith (banjo) and Pattie Hopkins Kinlaw’s (fiddle) innovative twist on traditional bluegrass music is a soulful brew flavored with classical, Motown, jazz, and pop Influences. Hard-hitting bluegrass pickers who moonlight as symphonic classical musicians, Hank & Pattie have forged their unique sound by refreshingly embracing their myriad of musical influences to create a unique sound, true to themselves. Their overall musicianship, vocal harmonies, impassioned delivery and innovative arrangements captivate all generations of music lovers.
-Rachel Baiman
