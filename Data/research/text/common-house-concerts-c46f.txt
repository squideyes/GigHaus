Common House Concerts
Common House Concerts features an eclectic mix of touring artists and local talent. Please join us for some memorable evenings of great music and homestyle merrymaking!
There are no advance ticket sales—admission is by donation at the door.
Generally, it's OK to just show up, but because our space is limited and we like to know how many guests to prepare for, we ask that you add your name to the guest list, please. Each artist's page contains a link to the guest list form for that show.
For shows that are expected to fill the room, reservations may be required. 
Questions? Email commonhouseconcerts@gmail.com
