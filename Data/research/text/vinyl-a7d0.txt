About the Venues
Built in 1966 to honor Atlanta’s performing arts community, Center Stage Theater is an iconic live performance venue in Midtown celebrating over five decades hosting music, comedy, wrestling, boxing, eSports, film and television recordings, and private events.
Today, the facility features three diverse venues:
Center Stage Theater, the historic 1050-capacity original theater renowned for its acoustics and intimate live show experience
The Loft, an industrial 650-capacity live music and event space
Vinyl, a 300-capacity staple in Atlanta’s local music scene
Marquee Moments at Center Stage
Originally “Theatre Atlanta”, Center Stage was built by Ms. Frania Tye Lee as a memorial to her daughter, Mrs. Helen Lee Cartledge, after she and 100 other Atlanta arts patrons perished in the Orly plane crash of 1962. The family preserves the Midtown Atlanta property as a home to performing arts in Mrs. Cartledge’s honor.
The Video Music Channel first broadcast from Center Stage in 1982.
World Championship Wrestling (WCW) filmed WCW Saturday Night at Center Stage.
ProgPower USA has taken place at Center Stage for 20+ consecutive years.
Sir Elton John recorded the live-studio album Captain & The Kid on the floor of Center Stage.
Beck surprised Atlanta at The Loft one Halloween night.
Jay-Z launched his seven-city-in-one-day “Hanger Tour” with a 7am show at Center Stage.
Jamie Foxx hosted television comedy special Laffapalooza from Center Stage.
Lady Gaga & Katy Perry each played Center Stage the same week.
Twenty One Pilots, Cold War Kids, Marc Broussard, Citizen Cope, X Ambassadors & countless others were introduced to Atlanta in Vinyl.
Kanye West, Drake, Ellie Goulding, Queens of the Stone Age, Dan & Shay, Khalid, The Weeknd, 21 Savage, The Strokes, Young Thug, J Cole, H.E.R and Shawn Mendez – all launched meteoric careers from Center Stage – The Loft – Vinyl.
MTV Unplugged and Rockstar Energy Drink’s concert experience highlighting Atlanta-born artist J.I.D at The Loft in 2022.
Three seasons of the series “Wild N’ Out” were shot at Center Stage Theater from 2018 through 2020.
Comedian Marlon Wayans: God Loves Me on HBO Max filmed at Center Stage Theater 
The “Plastic Cup Boyz” Netflix special feature
World Wrestling Entertainment NXT LIVE! hosted at Center Stage
Rival Entertainment
Center Stage is operated by independent Atlanta promoter Rival Entertainment.
Since 2004, Rival has produced concerts and festivals, presenting many of Atlanta’s most acclaimed live events, including the Candler Park Music Festival, Georgia State Stadium’s inaugural concert featuring Foo Fighters, ONE Musicfest, the infamous three-day Outkast ATLast reunion and Prince’s last live concert performance.

Frequently Asked Questions
For the safety of our guests and performers, all ticket holders are subject to magnetic and/or physical search. Any prohibited items brought on to the premises are subject to confiscation.
Firearms and weapons are expressly prohibited on Center Stage property.
Where is Center Stage Atlanta located?
Center Stage, The Loft & Vinyl are located at 
1374 West Peachtree Street, Atlanta GA 30309
Intersection: 17th Street & West Peachtree Street in Midtown
MARTA: Arts Center (walk 0.2 miles north on West Peachtree)
More About Directions and Parking
What is your bag policy?
NO BACKPACKS. You may bring small bags under 12 inches and they will be searched.
How old do I need to be to attend a show?
Unless otherwise stated, all shows welcome all ages to attend.
I am bringing my child to a show. Do we both need tickets? Or is there a place for me to wait?
A ticket is required for each person to enter the building. There are no common/waiting areas within the building. Parents may consider Atlantic Station, the Canopy Hotel or a nearby restaurant to wait for their kids.
I am nervous about leaving my children alone. Is it safe?
Our management, show staff, security and APD officers are here to provide a safe event environment for all attendees. We encourage parents to attend a concert with their children and experience live music together. We expect that you’ll be confident in the experience and gain trust for a solo show in the future.
Where can I purchase tickets?
All tickets can be purchased online at www.centerstage-atlanta.com
Tickets may also be purchased in person at the venue’s box office, Monday – Friday, 10am – 5pm or upon entry to the show if tickets still remain.
We are a CASHLESS venue so our box office ONLY accepts major credit/debit cards. Phone orders are not available from the box office.
Complimentary parking is provided for box office customers during daytime box office hours in the garage beneath Center Stage. Park only in spaces marked “Box Office Parking”. A 15 minute parking limit is strictly enforced.
What is ‘General Admission’?
Unless a show is specifically advertised as “Reserved Seating”, it is General Admission (or ‘GA’), which means that there is not assigned seating for your ticket and is likely Standing Room Only. If you have any questions, feel free to call our box office at (404)885-1365.
How do I purchase ADA Accessible Seats?
Please call (404)885-1365 or email info@rivalentertainment.com for accessible seating.
My ticket says General Admission, but has a row / seat number…
Row and seat numbers on General Admission tickets are included for internal inventory purpose. If the ticket says General Admission, GenAdm or GA, no reserved seating exists.
It says a show is sold out. Is it really?
YES – It is sold out and there are currently no tickets available for the event. This means tickets will not be available day of show at the box office, however you can subscribe on the Ticketmaster listing to have an alert sent to you if more tickets become available.
Where is Will Call?
Will Call is located at the entrance to each room. You must provide a valid photo ID.
Someone said they put me on the guest list; can you check?
You will need to contact the person who arranged this for you. Sometimes guest lists won’t be received until the very last moment.
I lost my tickets. What do I do?
We’ll do our best to help you out. Bring your photo ID and credit card used for purchase to the Box Office. If you have a confirmation email, bring that along too.
What is the refund / return policy?
All sales are final. No refunds or exchanges, unless a performance is cancelled.
What’s a third party site?
They’re typically resale sites. Examples are: StubHub, Vivid Seats, and any other site that is not Ticketmaster. We are not responsible for the validity of any tickets purchased via third party vendors.
Where should I park?
*Center Stage does not operate any parking lots, not even the one beneath our venues. This information is intended to be helpful but we cannot guarantee rates or availability of lots.
Parking in the lot beneath our venues ranges from $8 – $20.
Other public lots are located throughout Midtown.
See Available Parking Lots
Are cameras or video recording allowed?
Generally, phone cameras and small point-and-shoot cameras are permitted, unless an artist prohibits photos altogether. Video recording, Ipads, tablets, GoPros and audio recording devices are prohibited. Show policies, if known in advance, will be posted on the event page on CenterStage-Atlanta.com
How do I get a photo pass?
Photo passes must be requested for approval through the Artist and their management. You may reach out via our venue’s Instagram DM’s to find the correct email contact.
What is the smoking policy?
Smoking, electronic cigarettes and vaping is prohibited. Violators are subject to ejection without refund. Please be respectful of your fellow concert goers and exit the building to get your fix.
Do you accept credit cards or have an ATM?
All major credit cards are accepted at the bars and box office for most shows.
Artist merchandise and the occasional private event may accept cash only. 
ATMs are located in each venue for your convenience.
What should I wear?
As long as you are fully clothed and comfortable, you’re good to go.
What time should I arrive?
Doors are scheduled to open 1 hour prior to the time on your ticket. Lines often form on the corner of 17th Street and West Peachtree Street early.
Do you offer a coat check?
No, we do not offer a coat check. You must be responsible for your own items as staff will not watch your items for you.
Do you serve food?
Certain shows offer pizza and we have snacks available at the bar.
I lost something, how do I get it back?
Please email info@rivalentertainment.com with a description of the item as well as which show you were attending when it was lost. If anything has been turned in, you will be contacted with details on how to get it back.
Does each venue have a bar?
Yes – Center Stage, The Loft, and Vinyl all have bars available for guests. While we are a cashless venue, cash tips are appreciated for bartenders. 
Can I camp out overnight or throughout the day to get a good spot in line?
We know you’re super excited for the show (we are too!), but we highly discourage camping out overnight, as we are not responsible for any issues that may occur when camping.
We encourage lineups to begin no earlier than 10:00 AM, also keeping in mind that standing outside all day may impact your ability to safely attend the show. While we understand the excitement and wanting to get the best place possible in the crowd, we often see that being in line too long may translate into health issues that prevent patrons from being able to stay for the duration of the show and enjoy it, so we ask that people be mindful to eat, hydrate, and rest during the day.
