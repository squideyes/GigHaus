Delicious Bites & Unforgettable Nights Since 2005!
Martin's Downtown, a locally owned, independent and favorite destination for Roanokers since 2005, is known for exceptional American fare, live music and "Can't Miss" daily specials. Martin's has been recognized in area publications and won many awards including: Best Burger, Best Wings, Best Chili, Best Happy Hour, Best Daily Specials, Best Live Entertainment and more!
