Welcome to Folk ‘N Great Music 
Welcome to Folk ‘N Great Music house concerts in Rockville, Maryland, where you can enjoy talented singer/songwriters in an intimate, un-amplified, convenient, smoke-free listening room.
Since 2003, Folk ‘N Great Music has been welcoming neighbors and colleagues to enjoy national touring singer/songwriters. 100% of the suggested per person minimum donation goes directly to the performers. In addition, at many shows, we collect items like canned food, cleaning products, school supplies, or winter coats to support various local nonprofits.
Folk ‘N Great Music is proud to have been the very first unionized house concert series in the country with a Collective Bargaining Agreement with Local 1000’s American Federation of Musicians. You can learn more about it here.
