Frequently asked Concert questions
please email venue@avondalebrewing.com with any questions
CHECK THE CALENDAR FOR MORE DETAILS ON YOUR EVENT!
we are 100% CASHLESS
TAPROOM HOURS: 
tue & WED: 12-10pm | Thurs: 12-11PM | FRI & SAT: 12pm-12am | SUNDAY: 12-8pm HOURS MIGHT BE AFFECTED ON BIG CONCERT DAYS (ALWAYS CHECK OUR SOCIAL MEDIA FOR MORE INFO) 
rain or shine
Avondale Brewing Co. is an outdoor venue. All Avondale Brewing Co. concerts are rain or shine. We do not allow umbrellas.
SEVERE WEATHER POLICY
In the event of inclement weather and/or lightning within an 8-mile radius, we will evacuate the venue. Security will guide guests out of the venue to find shelter. Please seek shelter in one of the neighboring businesses and stay tuned to social media for updates every 30 minutes. We will be in constant contact with the National Weather Service. When we get the all-clear, we will announce this on Avondale Brewing Co.'s social media. @AVONDALEBREWING on FB & IG 
Age restrictions
ABCo. is all ages unless otherwise specified. If an event has an age restriction, it will be listed on Ticketmaster. Any person over the age of 2 requires a ticket to enter. Anyone over 18 must present a valid ID. 
Clear Bag Policy
To reduce staff contact with guest belongings, we have implemented the following bag policy:
We will allow clear plastic, vinyl or PVC tote bags no larger than 12” x 6” x 12” and/or small clutch bags (4.5”x 6.5”). Small clutch bags do NOT have to be clear.
Diaper bags and larger bags that are needed for medical reasons are allowed but will be subject to search upon arrival. If you require additional accommodations, please email venue@avondalebrewing.com within 48 hours of the show you’re attending.
Concert attendees are encouraged to avoid bringing any bag, if feasible. Bags that are not in compliance will not be stored and must be returned to the patron’s vehicle.
Can i bring a chair?
Typically ABCo. is a standing, general admission venue. We do not allow chairs or blankets at most shows. Please check the specific show on our calendar via the Concerts & Events tab for details.
ADA seating request
You must buy your ticket first.
For ADA accommodations, please email venue@avondalebrewing.com at least 48 hours prior to the door time. The door time is listed on the calendar via the Concerts & Events tab. The ADA section has a limited number of seats. Accommodations depend on availability. 
The Upstairs at Avondale venue is accessible by stairs & elevator 
ID REQUIREMENTS
Per Alabama law, we can only accept the following as identification for consuming alcoholic beverages: 
Any Valid State Driver’s License - printed, and non-expired -- virtual copies or representations are NOT acceptable. If you have a temporary license, you MUST have the expired license along with it.
Military ID
Valid Passport
Valid State ID card with photo and birth date
DO YOU HAVE FOOD?
Yes! We have food vendors on-site for most shows — check out the day-of show post to see the food lineup. Outside food is also always welcome!
Do you serve more than beer?
We do. We are a full bar offering beer (craft always + domestics during concerts), spirits, wine, seltzers, and more. Our NA options vary but Coke products and water are always available. 
Where do i park?
We recommend using Uber or Lyft. If you would like to bring your car, we have two nearby paid lots and street parking. There is a paid lot behind Avondale Common House and the Avondale Village Lot on the corner of 41st St S and 1st Ave S.
Who do I contact for a media pass?
Media passes are given through the artist and/or promoter. Do not request a media pass through Avondale Brewing Co.
I See tickets for your shows on Stubhub + Vividseats — should i buy These?
NO! The ticketing for Avondale Brewing Company is operated exclusively through Ticketmaster. We are unable to verify the authenticity of tickets bought from any other website. If a show is sold out, Ticketmaster offers resale tickets that we can verify as authentic tickets.
AVOID THIRD-PARTY TICKET SITES AND PEOPLE YOU DO NOT KNOW.
We want to see you at the show!
You can bring in empty water bottles
Yes! We have two water refill stations in the backyard. Guests are welcome to bring one empty plastic bottle to refill — bottles made of stainless steel or glass are NOT ALLOWED.
BAND LINEUP
Check Ticketmaster or our website for the most up-to-date band lineup. Lineups are subject to change without notice.
VAPING + SMOKING
As we are an outdoor venue, smoking is allowed -- however, we request that patrons vape and smoke away from crowds near the entrances to our venue.
AVOIDING THE TRAIN
DON'T TAKE 1st AVE N TO GET TO THE VENUE! Stay on the south side -- if you're coming from downtown or from the East, take 4th Ave S directly to the venue.
Help! I can’t find my a-lIst Or ELEVATE Passes!
Don’t worry, we’re here to help! On the day of our show, our Box Office staff at the A-List entrance or Will Call tent will have a list of names of A-List purchasers. We’ll make sure you get where you need to be!
Are A-List OR ELEVATE passes available for sale on the day of the show?
A-List & Elevate passes are subject to availability - if not sold out, we will sell them at our Box Office when doors open for the show. Check out our day-of-show posts on social media to learn if we have A-List passes available!
Prohibited items
Absolutely NO firearms (nothing concealed is allowed on the property) , pepper spray, knives, pocket knives, mace or weapons of any kind. 
Cameras or video cameras with removable lenses and flashes. No GoPros or Drones
Outside beverages or alcohol, backpacks, strollers, umbrellas, coolers, chains, or studded clothing.
Laser pointer or any type of light capable of distracting the performance. iPads or any large tablet device. 
Professional cameras without a photo pass issued via the Artist, Promoter or Venue. Any audio or video recording device unless prior approval was issued via the Artist, Promoter or Venue. 
Lighting worn on the body and NOT attached to their physical clothing; Christmas lights, string lights, pins, etc. glow sticks. 
Items that may be swung or invade personal space of others; ie hula-hoops, swinging balls, umbrellas, jump ropes, etc.
