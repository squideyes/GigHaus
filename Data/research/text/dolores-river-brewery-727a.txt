Frequently asked questions
General
Live Music
Where are you located?
We’re located at 100 S 4th St. , Dolores, Co. Just search our name on Google Maps for easy directions.
What are your hours?
We’re open 6 days a week for craft beer, food, and occasional live music:
Monday–Thursday: 4pm–9pm
Friday: 4pm–10pm
Saturday: 4pm–10pm
Sunday: 4 pm–9pm
Hours may vary for live music and special events. Check our Events page or social media for the most up-to-date info
Do you have outdoor seating?
Absolutely. Our outdoor patio and beer garden are a big part of what makes us a favorite outdoor dining spot. It’s the perfect place to relax with a locally brewed beer and enjoy live music.
Voted Cortez/Montezuma County's best outdoor dining
What kind of beer do you serve?
We brew a rotating selection of house-made craft beer, including year-round favorites, like our Snaggletooth Pale ale, seasonal releases like our famous Black Goat Munich Lager, and limited-batch beers like our yearly Krampus. We also offer wine, cider, and non-alcoholic options.
Are you dog friendly?
Yes! We’re a dog-friendly brewpub. Well-behaved dogs are welcome in our outdoor beer garden, as long as they’re on a leash and comfortable around people, music, and other pups.
Do you have other drink options?
Yes indeed! We feature a rotating wine list featuring 3 reds, 3 whites and a featured varietal. We also offer an elderberry cider from local producers Fenceline. 
Our non alcoholic offerings include our very own NIX hop water, locally produced sodas from Zuberfizz, Perrier sparkling water, and a variety of NA beers from Athletic.
In addition, we offer H20, and lemonade. 
Do you serve food?
Yes! We offer a food menu designed to pair with our craft beer and available for both indoor and outdoor dining. Our menu's vary during the week so it's best to check the website if there are specific menus you are excited about.
On Tuesdays we feature Kelly's soup and sandwich menu - 
On Wednesdays we feature Kelly's Katsu chicken menu
On Thursdays we feature Kelly's Taco menu
and on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday we feature DRB's own famous Pizza menu.
Is this a good stop for tourists?
Definitely. We’re a popular stop for travelers looking for local craft beer, outdoor dining, and live music in a relaxed, welcoming setting. Whether you’re passing through or staying awhile, you’ll feel right at home.
Do you have live music?
We do! We regularly host live music on our beer garden stage, or in the event of potential weather, in our indoor/outdoor lab stage, featuring local favorites and touring artists. Check our Events page or social media to see who’s playing and when.
Is the beer garden family-friendly?
During the day and early evening, yes! Later in the evening—especially during live music—the vibe becomes more adult-focused.
Can we bring a large group?
Large groups are welcome! Seating is first-come, first-served. For private events, parties, or group reservations, please contact us directly.
How can I contact you?
email is your best bet - drbrewery@mac.com 
or peruse our website: www.doloresriverbrewery.com 
For immediate concerns call 970-882-4677

About the event
Hailing from Cork in the south of Ireland, award-winning songwriter and much sought-after guitarist Patsy O’Brien is renowned for his ability to meld songwriting and guitar styles seamlessly. It’s a trait that caught the attention of NPR who featured his arrangement of The Star Of The County Down (see below) on their All Songs Considered program.

Patsy has traversed the globe, guitar in hand, accompanying, creating and recording with some of the giants of Irish music. About the event
Eric Johanson has built his career on the idea that roots are a foundation, not a boundary. The New Orleans guitarist, singer and songwriter draws from blues, rock, psychedelia and the musical language of his Louisiana home, turning those influences into songs that are heavy, melodic, raw and unmistakably his own. His guitar may be the first thing that grabs attention, but it has always served a larger picture: songwriting, voice, groove and the electricity of a band playing in the moment.
Johanson’s 2023 album, The Deep and the Dirty, debuted at #1 on the Billboard Blues Albums chart, expanding his audience with a harder-edged sound that paired thick guitar textures and swampy rhythms with increasingly focused songwriting. He followed it with 2025’s Live In Mississippi, another Billboard Top 10 release that captured the intensity and unpredictability of his power-trio stage show.
