About Elk Creek
History + mission + who we are.
Elk Creek Cafe + Aleworks – Since 2007
It all began in 2007 with a vision Tim Bowser had for a small town, community gathering place – a brewpub/ bistro/ music venue that was truly focussed on authentic, local, and flavorful. A place rooted in Millheim, where friends, neighbors, farmers, musicians and artists could come together around good conversations supported by great food, great ale, and original music.
Since then, Elk Creek Cafe + Aleworks has built a unique identity crafted around honestly community, local food, brilliantly designed beers, an extraordinary lineup of local + touring musicians, and thousands of engaging conversations.
One of the first craft breweries in Central PA, Elk Creek has introduced the community to the tastebud popping flavors of over 50 unique clean and balanced ales and lagers, from crisp, traditional pilsners, to a wide variety of crushable ales and IPAs, and very approachable malt-forward dark beers.
Tim’s background in sustainable agriculture placed a premium on the importance of supporting local farms, and created the farm-to-table concept in Penns Valley, long before it was trendy. From the gitgo, Elk Creek’s food ingredients are, to the highest degree possible, sourced from local grass-based meat and dairy producers, as well as organic vegetable farmers and a variety of gourmet artisans. With talented chefs at the helm of the kitchen, Elk Creek continues to serve diverse, flavorful and unique dishes with a local flair.
The focus on original music has always been an integral part of the Elk Creek ethos, and wide-ranging acts from across the nation and the world have grace the stage in Millheim. Audiences get to experience roots, blues, world, and jazz over dinner and brews. Local musicians play the informal Pub Hang on Thursday nights.
Tim Bowser, Proprietor + Tim Yarrington, Head of Brewing Operations
“It’s about community. Our team at Elk Creek. The people that come to Elk Creek from all over. The farmers we work with. The musicians that come through. It’s really a cool, convivial scene that meshes on a lot of fronts. Truly the Elk Creek Community.”
 Tim Bowser
Proprietor, Elk Creek Cafe + Aleworks
Tim Yarrington
HEAD OF BREWING OPERATIONS
Tim has been with Elk Creek Cafe + Aleworks since day one serving as founding brewer, head of brewing operations and brewmaster. Yarrington has crafted over 50 unique beers at Elk Creek since its start in 2007.
Previously, Yarrington worked in Manhattan as a brewmaster at Times Square Brewery Restaurant II. He began his brewing career at The Long Valley Pub & Brewery in Long Valley, New Jersey, capturing one gold and two bronze medals at the renowned Great American Beer Festival in Denver, Colorado.
Tim is now teaching Brewing & Fermentation Science at Pennsylvania College of Technology. Find out more about him and his philosophy on brewing here: https://www.pct.edu/makers/tim-yarrington

Signature + Seasonal/Rotating Brews
Elk Creek Craft Beer
We invite you to visit our cafe and taste the difference in our craft beer. In addition to our five standard beers, we craft a variety of seasonal favorites guaranteed to satisfy. Whether you like light and crisp, bright and hoppy, or dark and toasty, you’ll find what your palate is craving at Elk Creek Cafe + Aleworks.
Seasonal/Rotating Beers on Tap
Little Village MFA
What’s an MFA?? 
Millheim’s Finest Ale, Multifaceted Ale…. 
Whatever it is, this is a hoppy beer but only to the extent that malt allows with apparent malt notes of toffee and biscuit or even chocolate. There is a slight sweetness up front with a cleansing bitterness that leaves the palate ready for another taste. The malt and hop flavors remain entwined throughout. 
6.2% ABV
Valley Vista Cream Ale
This beer offers a light malt sweetness with subtle corn flavor & low bitterness. Easy drinking kind of beer that pairs well with yard work. Also, it’s a cold fermented ale made with 80/20 barley/corn grain base. 
5.1% ABV
Tail Dragger Imperial Stout
 In the style of an Imperial Stout. This beer is traditionally made by British breweries to serve the Russian market. It contains notes of strong and assertive black ale. It’s also rich and malty, hinting of chocolate cake.
 7.5% ABV
Hand-Pull Beer Engine
Cask conditioned beer is drawn to the glass via the beer engine. Cask beer hand-drawn from a beer engine pre-dates but shares much in common with modern Nitrogen-dispensed beers.
Cask beers are traditionally served with lower carbonation levels yet, when delivered to the glass, they present a creamy foam and smooth texture due beer engine design and hand-drawn effect.
Typically, hand-drawn beer elevates malt-associated attributes and softens the texture of beer and smooths the edges of hop-derived bitterness, flavors and aromas.

Great Blue Heron Pale Ale
Much like the local icon it is named for, our version of American Pale Ale has a bold character. We add a healthy dose of hops in both the kettle and the finishing tank.
5.6% ABV
Double Rainbow IPA
Named for the spectacular and complete double rainbow that appeared in the northwest sky just outside the brewhouse during the inaugural mash in. With a foot in both the new and old worlds, this IPA is generously dry-hopped with English Fuggles, resulting in a strong, deep golden, very fresh, and fruity English flavor and aroma.

Elk Creek Copper Ale
This beautifully colored ale strikes an agreeable balance between malt and hops. Caramel + toffee notes from the use of more highly kilned malts step up to complement this beers assertive hop profile.

Brookie Brown Ale
Proof that all dark beers, like trout, are not created equal. Chocolate and caramel malt flavors are at the heart of this very accessible and drinkable brown ale.
5.2% ABV?
Poe Paddy Porter
Named for our beloved State Park this Robust Porter draws its uniquely enticing roasted character from a thoughtful combination of malts. Generous hop additions ensure a satisfying beer drinking experience.
