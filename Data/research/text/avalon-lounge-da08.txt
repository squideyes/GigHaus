Hours:

The Avalon Lounge is a live music venue and dance club with a Korean kitchen and Soju/Sake/Beer/Wine/Cider bar. We have three interconnected spaces - Our bar room, an upstairs lounge with additional seating and pool table, and a performance room/dance floor. There is no dining space in the performance room.
We are not a full-service restaurant, but rather offer bar service - place your order with our bartender, and food will be brought to your table. 
Cover charges for shows are usually collected at the door to the performance space, with continued free entry to the bar and lounge. Occasionally, we may have parties on the weekends where cover is instead collected at the main entrance after 9 or 10PM. If this is a concern, please call ahead to see what’s happening.
Address:
29 Church St. Catskill, NY 12414
