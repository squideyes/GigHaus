HOW IT WORKS 
Hey Nonny is transforming to be a two-stage music venue with great "NonnyQ" barbeque food available in both rooms. For ticketed shows, just buy a ticket-no dining reservation is necessary for you to eat at your show seat. Reserving a seat for Lounge shows is encouraged. 
Hey Nonny is cashless and only cards are accepted in both rooms.

Getting Here: Metra, Parking & Transit
Address: Hey Nonny is located at 10 South Vail Avenue in downtown Arlington Heights across from Harmony Park.
‍Transit: Hey Nonny is two blocks from the Arlington Heights Metra station (Union Pacific Northwest Line), making it one of the most accessible live music venues from downtown Chicago. No car required.
‍Parking: The street in front of Hey Nonny is closed May - September for Arlington Al fresco. During the rest of the year, street parking is available and free. The pro move is to park in the parking garage immediately south of Hey Nonny. The garage offers free evening parking on the 1st, 4th, and 5th levels. Please read the signs closely!
Tickets
Purchasing: Tickets are available at HeyNonny.com or in-person at the host stand. The usual ticket service fee applies either way, so for your convenience, buying online in advance is the best move. We admit patrons to shows from our Will Call list. You just give the ticket buyer’s name; you don’t need a physical ticket for admission.
‍Seating Categories: Most shows on the Venue Stage have three ticket categories: Premier, Cabaret and GA Standing. Most tables have 4 seats, and if you buy less than 4, chances are you'll have some other nice people sitting with you. 
 Premier Seat: are at high-top tables along the window with great stage views. They provide more room behind the blue ropes.
 Cabaret Seat: are at smaller tables, closer to the stage.
 General Admission-Standing: The GA standing area is along the Venue bar.
Lounge Shows: Shows on the Lounge stage have two ticket categories: Lounge Bar and Lounge Tables. All have a great view of the Lounge stage. If you buy fewer than all of the seats at a tables, you'll probably have some other nice people sitting with you.
No Wall Shows: Sometimes, we do "no wall" shows that combine our Venue and Lounge rooms. For these shows, there are four ticket categories: Premier, Cabaret, Lounge Bar, and Lounge Tables. All as described above. 
‍‍‍
‍Ticket Confirmation: You’ll receive an email confirming your ticket purchase for concert at Hey Nonny. Please add “Hello@HeyNonny.com” to your address book to ensure you’ll receive your confirmation email. Hey Nonny does not print or mail tickets.
‍No Refunds: All ticket sales are FINAL AND NON-REFUNDABLE unless Hey Nonny cancels a show. In that case, your refund will automatically be applied to the credit card you used for purchase. If you can’t attend a show, you can sell or give away your tickets. Just have the recipient give the purchaser’s name at the door.
In the Venue
Admission: Admission is according to the name of the ticket purchaser. If you’re buying tickets for someone else, or having others join you, have them give the purchaser’s name at the door. If you’re giving your ticket to someone else at the last minute, just forward your confirmation email to them.
Doors: Doors to the venue open one hour prior to show time unless otherwise noted.
All Ages: Our shows are all ages unless otherwise indicated. Get the kids in to hear good music!
Accessibility: Hey Nonny is accessible to people of all abilities. If you need accessibility accommodations, please call us at 224-202-0750 or email us at Contact@HeyNonny.com at least 24-hours in advance so that we can help.
Bag Policy: All bags large or small are subject to visual inspection upon entry to Hey Nonny.
Photography and Recording: Non-flash photography is permitted during performances. Don’t forget to share and tag @HeyNonnyAH! Live streaming of any show is prohibited. Professional photography/video/audio recording equipment is only allowed into the venue with the prior consent of the artist and venue.
Frequently Asked Questions
What kind of live music?
At Hey Nonny, we showcase a wide range of live music - from rock, pop, reggae, jazz, and blues, to folk, bluegrass, country, classical, and beyond. Our focus isn’t on specific genres, but on intimate listening experiences and the unparalleled creativity of our performers. You won’t find many cover or tribute bands here; instead, Hey Nonny is a space for original, inspired music that connects deeply with our audience. 
What's the deal with music at Hey Nonny?
Hey Nonny has nightly ticketed shows in its Venue Room Tuesday through Saturday at 7:30pm(usually). By October 1, 2026 or so, Hey Nonny will also have low-cost nightly ticketed shows in the Hey Nonny Lounge-at 6pm on weeknights and 7pm on weekends. There are also HeyDay Happy Hours with live music every Friday and Saturday at 4pm. Hey Nonny will also be adding Saturday and Sunday matinee shows ar good opportunities arise. 
What's the Food Story?
Hey Nonny's classic NonnyQ barbeque menu is available in both the Venue and the Lounge. You can order with and order taker when you check in or with your phone using the QR code at your table. We'll bring your food and drinks to your table. Hey Nonny has gone cashless-card only.
Is the music quiet enough so I can talk to my friends?
Yes and no. The Venue is a "listening room," we ask that you keep conversation to a minimum so that everyone can enjoy the music. The Lounge is a vibrant social room, where the music and the audience are interactive. Conversation is part of the action. 
How long are the shows?
Most shows last about two hours, with a short break between sets.
Can I or my cousin Stevie play there?
Hey Nonny features the best Chicago-based musicians as well as top touring artists. If Stevie falls into one of these categories, she’s in!
If you know a talented professional musician or band, please email information to Katie@HeyNonny.com.
Please do not call, mail, or drop off materials at the venue - submissions sent in any other format will not be accepted.
What are the rules on tickets?
All ticket sales at Hey Nonny are final. If you can’t attend a show, you’re welcome to sell or give your tickets to someone else - just make sure the recipient provides the original ticket buyer’s name at the door, as we admit from our Will Call list.
Like most venues, ticket fees apply, but unlike some, these fees are the same whether you purchase tickets online or in person.
What does “Hey Nonny” mean?
“Hey Nonny Nonny” comes from Old English songs and verse, where it expresses a sense of cheerful bliss and carefree joy. In Shakespeare’s Much Ado About Nothing, the song “Sigh No More” advises women that “men were deceivers ever,” and suggests turning all your troubles into “Hey Nonny Nonny” - a joyful, whimsical refrain.
We also love the Violent Femmes’ 1991 song “Hey Nonny Nonny”, in which the singer transforms the tension of suppressed desire into a playful “Hey Nonny Nonny”. At Hey Nonny, the name captures our spirit: fun, music, and letting go of your cares.
Do I need a reservation?
Nope, just buy a show ticket and you'll be able to order whatever food you'd like. Before October 1, 2026, you can make a reservation for dining in the Lounge. After that, your ticket gets you a seat at a table.
Can I request a specific table?
Yes! When making a reservation, you can let us know your seating preference. While we’ll do our best to accommodate requests, we cannot guarantee specific tables during busy times, especially during the Sounds of Summer patio series.
Do you accommodate large groups or private parties?
Absolutely! We love hosting showers, birthday parties, corporate events, weddings, and end-of-probation parties. We can accomodate up to 225 people for a party. For inquiries, contact us at events@heynonny.com.
‍
Do you have vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free options?
Yes! Our classic NonnyQ menu is limited but has something for (almost) everyone.

Hey Nonny offers unforgettable private event experiences in the heart of downtown Arlington Heights. Whether you’re planning an intimate wedding, shower, luncheon, meeting, rehearsal dinner, milestone celebration, or bar/bat mitzvah, our team is here to make it exceptional. 
‍
Events can be hosted in the Lounge, the Venue, or across both rooms combined, with our signature retractable wall creating one flowing space with customizable menus featuring elevated seasonal comfort cuisine, and our bar team can craft drink packages tailored to any occasion. The Venue offers a truly unique backdrop: a functioning, intimate music hall that has welcomed world-class performers from across the country. It’s an environment your guests won’t soon forget.
