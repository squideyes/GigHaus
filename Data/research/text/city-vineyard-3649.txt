 Waterfront Dining

 FAQs
 Where is City Vineyard located?
City Vineyard is located at Pier 26 in Tribeca, right on the Hudson River waterfront.
 Are City Vineyard Sessions only about music?
Not at all! While City Vineyard Sessions features intimate, stripped-down concerts, the lineup goes well beyond music. You can also expect stand-up comedy, interactive experiences like bingo and trivia, and unique cultural and social events such as Lectures on Tap.
 Can you dine during the show?
Absolutely - in fact, dining is part of what makes the experience so special. Guests can enjoy our full food menu along with wines made by City Winery, including selections on tap, all while taking in the show.
 What makes City Vineyard Sessions so intimate?
It starts with the room. With a capacity of just 100 guests, you’re incredibly close to the artist, performer, or experience. Musical performances are also presented in a more stripped-down format, creating a personal, one-of-a-kind show you won’t get in a traditional venue.
 What is the relationship between City Vineyard Sessions and City Winery NYC?
City Vineyard Sessions is brought to you by the same team behind the programming at City Winery NYC. City Vineyard is City Winery’s unique waterfront sister property, offering the same passion for great entertainment, food, and wine in a more intimate Tribeca setting.

Our Story
City Winery was founded in New York City in 2008 by Michael Dorf to deliver a unique combined culinary and cultural experience to urban wine enthusiasts.
 Company Timeline
 Our Founder
City Winery is the brainchild of Michael Dorf, founder of the iconic Knitting Factory, one of New York’s longest running music venues, who wanted to create a space in Manhattan where he could combine his shared passions for wine and music. After making his first barrel of wine in California with his brother Josh and winemaker David Tate, formerly of Ridge Winery and now at Barnett Vineyards, Michael caught the bug: "For a long time I'd been enjoying wine as a fan - drinking it, taking classes, attending wine dinners - but it was not until I actually started participating in the process of working with the grape, turning it into wine, putting it into a bottle, and watching it develop over time, that I really started to understand wine. That same hands-on approach still shapes every wine we make today. And when I would give my friends a bottle of wine with my name on it, most would respond by saying: 'I wanna do that too!' " It was this experience that led Michael to conceptualize and start working on the City Winery business plan in 2006.
 Learn More
 Our Team
We’ve built a team of people passionate about music, wine, food, and the love of life. Several of our managers began their journeys in entry-level positions or internships and have since evolved within the company. We love what we do and are grateful for the opportunity to share it with you.
 View Team
 Core Values
 Conscious Company
From donating our spaces to giving away wine, the Company partners with many non-profit organizations to do our part. Our fully functioning wineries integrate an innovative tap model, reduce sulfites and preservatives, and have worked in various ways to consciously reduce our carbon footprint and bottle waste.
 Infinite Potential
Starting out in entry level positions and managers, through executive level roles, many of our team members have successfully advanced their careers internally. City Winery-ians are the experts in what we do and we love to see people grow along with their vision for the company.
 Diversity & Inclusion
An equal opportunity employer. Diversity, inclusion, and a sense of belonging are fundamental to the City Winery experience. We are dedicated to embracing diversity and strive to create conditions that provide everyone with an equal opportunity to thrive. Bringing your whole and genuine self to work is the version of you we love to see.
 Philanthropy
We believe strongly that what goes around, comes around and that we have a role in caring for, nourishing, and helping repair the world. We feel fortunate to be in a position to leverage our resources including our space, wine, human capital, and energy for philanthropy.
Reflective of our ethos to be a conscious business, philanthropy holds a special place in our hearts. From disaster recovery efforts such as post-hurricane clean up in Puerto Rico to cleaning up schools in New Orleans to better support the educational system, philanthropy is ingrained in our DNA.
