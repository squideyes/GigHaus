About the Show
Robin Tricker and Boose Rutledge are two well-known artists who have joined forces on a shared musical journey, blending their unique styles into a dynamic, feel-good sound.
Robin Tricker is a passionate songwriter, ukulele lover, and true musical chameleon. Known for crafting multi-layered songs with unforgettable hooks, she has built a strong presence in the trop-rock scene. Her original tracks, including “Salt Water Happy” and “Need More Bob,” both reached #1 on Florida’s Radio A1A Trop 40, earning her recognition for her infectious, beach-inspired sound.
Boose Rutledge brings powerful guitar work, rhythmic precision, and an unmistakably authentic voice. A fresh and rising presence in the original music scene, Boose blends classic beach influences with a country-western edge. His passion for music is evident in every performance, connecting with audiences wherever he plays. His song “Just One More Beer” is quickly gaining traction, with crowds instantly singing along.
Together, Robin and Boose create a vibrant, high-energy experience rooted in strong songwriting, rich harmonies, and a shared love for music that brings people together.
You can find their newest single “Just Smile” on all platforms! They also have a remake of the beloved classic “(Sittin’ On) The Dock of the Bay” They are thrilled that both have reached the Trop Rock charts! They have a brand new single out on the Trop Rock Stations/chart and on Nov 13th at The Habitat we will be featuring a fellow Jersey shore musician Todd Raupp on bass.
This will be her Robin’s time coming to The Habitat, and she’ll have the band! We are looking forward to this show – note that this is a RARE FRIDAY show to help her with her scheduling to make the tour work!

The Habitat is the private home of Ken and Heather Settlemyer who are passionate about the importance of nurturing both local plant and animal wildlife and independent musicians.
Live Music & A Wildlife Sanctuary… The concept of the House Concert is to provide a venue where musicians can come to be heard by those whose primary intention is to listen rather than to have music in the background.
We host musicians to perform at The Habitat approximately one to two times per month, either on our outdoor stage, or on the indoor stage in the event of inclement weather or upon musician request.
We are also proud to maintain over 1/4 acre of wilderness within an urban environment, providing much needed habitat for a variety of native species. We plant only native species and manage or eliminate the non native species in that area. We are delighted to play host, not just to excellent musicians, but to a plethora of beautiful butterflies.
All Habitat concerts are private events. Contact us for an invite or follow our Facebook Page.
We are a not-for-profit venue. All donations go entirely to the artists, some of whom will have traveled from out of state! 
BYOB and food to share is always welcomed, but not required.
We respect our neighbors and all noise ordinances and we time the concerts in consideration of both. 
You are welcome to schedule a tour! We will be proud to show you our work in progress as we make a miniature wildlife preserve in the middle of Bradenton, a haven for bees, birds, bats, butterflies, and music lovers!
We sincerely hope you join us when you are able!
The Habitat has chosen Singing For Change as our official Charity! We will now make available “The Habitat” branded items including the Singing For Change Logo with 100% of the proceeds benefiting the charity.
The Habitat is proud to sponsor the Trop Rock Music Association. If you are a Trop Rock Music Supporter, Please consider Joining and supporting the Academy!
The Habitat is proud to sponsor Radio Trop Rock, the mobile Trop Rock station, with “EB” and “GB” Babin broadcasting straight from their RV!
The Habitat is proud to have sponsored the Trop Rockin’ Magazine’s Peoples Choice Award (Horizon Award). Congratulations winners Cory Young in 2018 and Danny Rosado in 2019!

The Habitat is the private home of Ken and Heather Settlemyer who are passionate about the importance of nurturing both local plant and animal wildlife and independent musicians.
The concept of the House Concert is to provide a venue where musicians can come to be heard by those whose primary intention is to listen rather than to have music in the background. We will be inviting musicians to perform at The Habitat approximately once per month.
We are proud to maintain over 1/4 acre of wilderness within an urban environment, providing much needed habitat for a variety of native species. We plant only native species and manage or eliminate the non native species in that area. We are delighted to play host, not just to excellent musicians, but to a plethora of beautiful butterflies.
The Habitat is BYOE and a donation for the musicians. This is completely not for profit, all donations go in entirety to the artists, some of whom will have traveled from out of state! Food to share is always welcomed, but not required.
We respect our neighbors and all noise ordinances and time the concerts in consideration of both. We sincerely hope you join us when you are able.
You are also welcome to stop by and ask Ken for a tour, he will be proud to show you our work in progress as we make a miniature wildlife preserve in the middle of Bradenton, a haven for bees, birds, bats and butterflies!

Carrie Welling, Brent Burns, and Mick Fury – a Queen between two Jokers! 
About The Artists:
Check out Carrie’s website – Carrie Welling Music
Check out Brent’s website – Brent Burns Music
Check out Carrie’s website – Mick Fury Music
Fans have loved Carrie Welling’s music from the inception and it’s clear to see why. Her bold, sultry voice, reminiscent of Sheryl Crow’s grit, Tom Petty’s pen, and Stevie Nicks’ heart, is upstaged only by the genuine love and connection this natural performer has with her fans both on and off stage. Melodies rich in Americana pop blend seamlessly with her singer-songwriter roots and empowered, honest lyrics. But while this new collection of songs while she maintains her signature raw vulnerability and authenticity – qualities that are essential to her creative process – fans will certainly take note of a more mature and evolved sound.
Brent Burns was born in Oklahoma, raised in Arizona and arrived in Gulf Shores, Alabama in 1972. This son of the desert has become the poet laureate of the beach. In 2006, Burns was named “Official Music Ambassador of the Alabama Gulf Coast Convention and Visitors Bureau.” In 2008, Burns won three awards in the first ever Trop Rock Music Awards. Burns won “Entertainer of the Year,” “CD of the Year (for “Ragtops and Flip Flops”)” and a special WAVE award for his charitable work. 2010 brought more Trop Rock Music awards, CD and Song of the Year for “I’ve Got A Beach in My Backyard” and Songwriter of the Year. In 2011 he added two more awards – Songwriter of the Year and Entertainer of the Year! 2012 brought awards for CD and Song of the Year for “Don’t Come Knockin’ If The Tiki Hut’s Rockin’.”
Mick Fury is an award winning singer/songwriter and is acclaimed for doing the 90 minute score and songwriting for Mel Gibson and Cole Hauser (Rip of Yellowstone) $10 million dollar theatrical film PANAMA. He’s shared the stage with everyone from the Stone Temple Pilots to Vertical Horizon to the Brothers Osborne, and is known for his intimate storytelling and big personality. Mick started his musical journey playing in his old man’s Southern Rock band in Syracuse, NY as a drummer. When the keyboardist left the band, Mick slid in as his replacement. When the band needed a back up guitar player and harmony singer, he happily jumped into that slot. After a few years of late night gigs and learning the ropes of being a dive bar musician, Mick packed up his stuff and headed out to LA to be a rockstar on the Sunset Strip. After an incredible year of performing at the Colorado Parrothead Festival, Meeting of the Minds, and Trop Rock n’ Duval, Mick began releasing a fun set of drinking songs from his upcoming album “Not Not Drinkin’”. The first single is the incredibly catchy and heart wrenching love song “Good Afternoon Tequila”. He’s currently touring festivals and house concerts across America teaching folks the benefits of “Not Not Drinkin’”.Doors open at 5:30, Show at 6:30. Park on our side of the street only, use our driveway and the one at the house closer to Manatee Ave (511 49th), park in the grass if available (avoid the water meters marked by orange cones please!)

About the Show…
Christie took First Place at the International Fingerstyle Guitar Championship in 2017, was voted Acoustic Guitarist of the Year by England’s Music Radar in 2019, and was named one of the “Best Acoustic Guitarists in the World Right Now” by Guitar World Magazine in 2020.
And she is coming back to The Habitat
Christie Lenée is a captivating performer with a devoted following among instrumental, folk-pop and rock band audiences. Combining guitar mastery, contemporary singer/songwriter and a passion for storytelling, she thrives onbringing people together through the language of music.
Christie’s unique guitar work and profound instrumental compositions are well described as ‘Michael Hedges meets Joni Mitchell and Dave Matthews.’ Among the vast community of fingerstyle and tapping guitarists, her niche is the integration of melodic pop lyricism with catchy hooks that excite both modern commercial listeners and classic purists. With a menu of percussive and sonic effects, and larger than life sounding instruments, her solo shows embody layers of orchestral elements with a wide array of dynamics and harmonic textures.
We also encourage you to check out her web site: Christie Lenée Music

About The Show and Charlie…
When singer-songwriter Charlie Imes penned the rhyme “I’m leaving behind a trail of sand, bringing my beach all across this land,” he summed up his approach to life. Imes comes by his coastal vibe honestly. He honed his craft on the beaches of Southern California from an early age, entertaining friends around the fire pit with his guitar, songs and stories. His performance style has stayed true to those early times. Whether imparting his laid back, jazzy folk rock sound or directly addressing his audience, Imes is a above all storyteller. The songs he chooses, whether his own or borrowed from other musicians, speak to his life experiences, nuggets of which he shares with his audience.
In 2008, Imes recorded his first CD, “On an Island,” which was nominated for a 2009 San Diego Music Award in the category of Best Americana or Country Album. He followed that with three subsequent albums, “Summer’s Here” (2014), “Gypsy Soul” (2017) and “Sittin’ Here” (2024), squeezing in the release of his single “Sailing To Nowhere” in 2023. He’s spent many summers touring the continent playing house concerts, festivals and commercial venues in Mexico, Canada and across the U.S., covering the 4 corners (San Diego to Seattle to New England to Key West) and much of the country in between. In 2016 he relocated from San Diego to Tampa, FL, lured by the beauty, weather and professional opportunities. Charlie has established some roots and loves it in Tampa, but he will always consider himself a San Diegan in his soul.
