OUR MISSION

ABOUT US
The creation of The Royal – Glenside’s newest 100-person capacity live music venue – was driven by life experience, daily struggles, and a desire to be inclusive. 
The COVID-19 global pandemic changed our world forever. Over the past few years, many cherished Glenside businesses, including The Blue Comet, The Bike Works, and others were forced to close their doors. The music community was especially hard-hit by the loss of music venues, not just in Glenside but throughout the Philadelphia area.
Our founders – Bridgett Gordon and Jerry Clarke – believe that Glenside and its neighboring communities needed a destination, a home for music, as we inched our way out of the pandemic.
Our vision is to provide a creative home where people of all backgrounds, music of all genres, and musicians of all experience levels feel welcome. We invite members of the community to come together and celebrate the local music and comedy scene, local artists, and local businesses. We want the musicians and comedians who play in our venue to feel they are participating in a “red carpet” event, whether they are novices just starting out or professionals who have toured for years. We want to treat musicians – and their listeners – like the royalty they are.
We chose the name “Royal” as our way of expressing the high status we believe artists deserve in our society. But by no means does the name suggest we expect lofty perfection. Quite the opposite. There is no expectation for being flawless. Some of the best music has come, and will always come, from an artist making a “mistake,” one that leads to a new and surprising path. We embrace that. Make mistakes. Make tons of mistakes - BUT learn from them.
As Miles Davis said, “Do not fear mistakes – there are none.”
In 2022, after celebrating our first anniversary, we transitioned to a 501(c)3 nonprofit organization and expanded our mission. While music is our core, we knew this community needed more - comedy shows, karaoke, musical bingo, private parties, vendor fairs. The Royal is no longer just a listening room, it is a community resource, a gathering place, and a venue for artists to thrive. 
Royal Staff
Meet The Royal Crew
Bridgett Gordon
Bridgett is a passionate leader, musician, and community advocate. With a Bachelor's Degree in Music from Temple University, she specialized in classical percussion and discovered her deep love for performing. These artistic roots have shaped her mission to help others uncover their true potential and explore their talents.
​
As the Executive Director and Co-Founder, Bridgett thrives on serving her community, whether through performance, running sound at Open Mic and weekend shows, or fostering creative spaces. Her love for music extends to playing drums and singing, where she continues to express her artistry.
​
Outside of her professional endeavors, Bridgett cherishes quality time with her family and her loyal companion, Fozzie, the Royal mascot. When she's not working at her office as a paralegal, or planning a Royal event, Bridgett is probably listening to true crime podcast or making pasta fagioli.
Joe Petta
Joe is our General Manager who has been a part of The Royal family since it's inception. You can often find him greeting guests at the door and fixing problems when they occur!
Joe is a passionate music enthusiast. His love for classic rock, punk, and metal is deeply rooted, with Pink Floyd holding a special place in his heart as his favorite band. Whether it's attending live concerts, discovering new artists, or revisiting iconic albums, music is an essential part of his life. 
A self-proclaimed tech nerd, Joe has a keen interest in exploring the ever-evolving world of technology. From staying updated on the latest gadgets to diving into software innovations, he enjoys immersing himself in the tech sphere. Joe is responsible for the set-up and maintenance of our live stream and our in-house IT person.
​
Joe would like to thank ChatGPT for this bio.
Steve Delaney
Steve Delaney is a veteran drummer, bassist and guitarist who has worked in live audio production since the 1990s with Goo Goo Dolls, Blues Traveler, and countless local performers at a host of venues including the Navy Marine Corps Memorial Stadium in Annapolis, and Veterans Stadium in Philadelphia. He brings his keen ear for live sound and an artistic approach to supporting a great live performance with the perfect balance of clarity and enhancement, informed by decades of live and studio engineering.
​
Steve can also be found performing on the Royal Stage, or with South Jersey's premiere grunge tribute band Sanctuary. 
When he's not working in music and production Steve collects pens, which he one day intends to melt down and recast as a giant sword.

Where are you located?
We are located at 1 South Easton Road in Glenside, PA. We are on the corner of Mt. Carmel Avenue and Easton Road. Entrance to the music venue is on Easton Road. Entrance to the event hall is 2278 Mt. Carmel Avenue. 
Where can I park?
Guests of The Royal are permitted to park in the parking lot of St. Paul's Lutheran Church. Enter the lot on Mt. Carmel Avenue, on the other side of Easton Road. Parking is available at the Glenside Train Station, but at your own risk as we are not associated or contracted with SEPTA. Street parking is also available.

ABOUT THE PROJECT
Artist Development Workshops
Educational workshops and resources to help artists build promotional skills, content and improved performance quality.
The Royal Live Sessions
A 10-Concert Series with professional audio and video recordings to showcase artists and help grow their audience. Artists will receive a guarantee and also full access to all recordings.
Production & Equipment Upgrade
Upgraded technology to deliver high-quality recordings, live broadcasts and production support.
Accessibility Initiatives
Improving access through inclusive programming, select concerts with ASL interpreters and additional enhancements.
FOR ARTISTS
Reliable Compensation through artist guarantees
Professional Audio & Video content to help promote their work
Workshops focused on career growth and sustainability
​
FOR AUDIENCES
Suggested donation admission instead of traditional ticket fees
Improved live stream experiences
Accessible programming for deaf and hard of hearing individuals
Diverse programming to reach a wide audience

Additional paid staff and production support
