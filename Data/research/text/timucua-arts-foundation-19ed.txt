Authentic Selves Poetry Open Mic
Artists interested in performing, fill out this application.
What is Authentic Selves?
Authentic Selves is a poetry open mic show. Its unofficial pilot was held February 2022. It was officially launched in March 2022. The event is hosted by Lauren White. Authentic Selves aims to be a community safe space where participants can feel comfortable performing – whether they be seasoned artists or are performing for the first time!
When is Authentic Selves held?
Authentic Selves is the third Sunday of every month from 7:30-9:00pm. (With the exception of July, when Timucua is closed.) Doors open at 7:00pm.
Where can I find event information?
Tickets are pay-what-you-like donations. Please “purchase” tickets in advance or donate at the door.
How much do tickets cost?
Tickets are pay-what-you-like donations. Please “purchase” tickets in advance or donate at the door.
Where do I get tickets?
Tickets are available through Timucua’s Eventbrite page here.
I cannot attend in person; is there a livestream?
Yes, the show is livestreamed on Timucua’s YouTube channel. The stream will start approximately 15 minutes before the show and be synced with advertisements showing in the venue.
What is the format of the show?
The format is generally a 15-20 minute set by a featured artist followed by the open mic portion of the show.
How do I sign up to be the featured artist?
Please contact Lauren White at [email protected]. We will require your name, alias (if applicable), a short biography, a short description of what you will be performing, and a high-resolution photo for marketing purposes. (If you have any suggestions for a feature performer, please let us know!)
How long is the featured artist set?
The featured artist has a 15-20 minute set at the beginning of the show.
How do I sign up to participate in the open mic portion of the show?
Fill out the application here. Please include your first and last name so that we may credit you in the video recording. If you wish, an alias can be provided, too. The form will also ask for a contact email, a short description of what you will be performing, and your consent to be recorded. If you do not sign up in advance, you will be added to the end of the list and may be able to perform if time allows.
How long is an open mic spot?
Five minutes. Note: We will not be timing you; this is on the honor system. We simply ask that you prepare in advance and be courteous. We want as many performers as possible!
Is there a deadline for signing up for the open mic?
Yes, the form must be completed by midnight the Saturday before the show.
Is signing up a guarantee that I will perform?
Due to limited time, there is no guarantee that you will be able to perform, but you can still attend!
I signed up for the open mic, but can no longer attend. What should I do?
Notify our host Lauren White at [email protected]
What format can I perform in?
Authentic Selves is focused on the literary arts – one can do poetry, spoken word, stories, songs, or any other content that is spoken and/or lyrical. This does not include simply playing music. Instruments that accompany the lyrical content are allowed.
What content can be performed?
The goal is sharing our authentic selves; all topics are welcome! Note that this may include mature content, profanity, or wording that may not be suitable for children. Some topics may also be triggering for audience members. If you become uncomfortable, please reach out to one of the staff or volunteers so that we can assist you. (Note: While all content is welcome, we prefer not to have overly pornographic content.)
Do I have to perform original content?
Original content is strongly encouraged. If you are doing a reading or a cover, please cite the original content.
Does the performance have to be in English?
No! You may perform in any language desired. Translations are encouraged but not required.
Can I include musical tracks in my performance?
The featured artists may include musical tracks. Please let us know in advance to facilitate testing and sound checks.
What is the proper audience etiquette and participation?
We want the audience to be involved. It adds to the energy of the event! You may snap, applaud, etc., in a courteous manner. Please do not be so loud as to drown out the performance or distract from the experience of other guests. Anyone exhibiting discourteous behavior will be asked to leave.
We also ask that you silence your cell phones and turn off Bluetooth devices to prevent audio interference. Pictures and video can be taken; please turn off flash and be mindful you are not obstructing the view of others around you.
What equipment is provided?
If needed, there is a music stand and stool.
There is a microphone on the stage that is not to be touched. The acoustic qualities of the venue will capture the sound when you are near the mic. Anyone who grabs or damages the microphone or other equipment will be asked to leave.
Can I bring my own equipment?
The venue does not support outside equipment. Due to the acoustic qualities of the venue, amplifiers or plug-ins are not needed.
I’m part of a group that wants to collaborate with the show – how do I do that?
Contact Lauren White at [email protected] to schedule an initial discussion. We will require a short description of your group and a copy of your logo (if applicable) for marketing purposes.

ABOUT US
 Our mission is to inspire with transformative, world-class performances, education, and artist support in a warm and inviting atmosphere.
Timucua Arts Foundation is a multi-faceted arts and education institution, presenting concerts and festivals and delivering education and wellness programming to people of all ages.
Timucua Arts Foundation presents distinctive integration of the performing and visual arts consistently and affordably, most often presented in the unique white house venue. 
Our values are rooted in community service, inclusivity, education, excellence, and sustainability.
Our core belief – The Arts Belong to Everyone – is embodied in an expansive performance schedule unique in Central Florida, encompassing internationally-acclaimed jazz artists, indigenous and folkloric music and dance, contemporary classical music, avant-garde improvisation, film, site-specific theater, and more, all while visual artists work live on stage.

FAQs
How early should I get to the show?
Doors open 30 minutes before showtime and seating is general admission. It is advised to arrive as close to doors opening as possible to select your seats.
Do you serve food? Is there a bar?
No, we do not serve food. We have a bar with complimentary wine available from donations. It’s a Timucua tradition to bring a bottle of wine to share. The bar closes at showtime out of respect to our performers.
Do I need to bring my own wine glasses?
No, we have metal tumblers on site and pour for you. The bar is open until the show starts. Drinks are not available after the show starts. Glass is not allowed in the performance space.
Is there anything else to drink besides wine?
Yes, we have water on site, plus other donated non-alcoholic beverages.
What is the white house and how is it different from Timucua Arts Foundation?
the white house is the physical location where most of Timucua Arts programming occurs, located at 2000 S Summerlin Avenue. Timucua Arts Foundation is the non-profit entity that produces events at the white house.
May I take pictures and videos during shows?
You may, but flash photography is prohibited. Please be mindful of other guests while trying to capture the moment. Performance trailers are available on YouTube.
What is your refund policy?
Timucua will issue a refund for in-person tickets if the request is made by email to [email protected] prior to when we open our doors, 24 hours before the event starts. Timucua will issue a refund for livestream tickets if we were unable to stream the event live. Request your refund by email to [email protected]. Where can I park?
Free parking is available on Summerlin Avenue and at Wadeview Park.
Can I rent the white house for a private event?
Yes, please email [email protected] for more information. Rates range from $120–$160 per hour, based on your needs. Recordings are included.
How can I perform at a Timucua show?
You can submit an application on our For Artists page to perform, be our visual artist of the month, and/or sign up for our open mic poetry series, Authentic Selves. Timucua’s artistic director will contact you if he would like to schedule you to perform or be our visual artist of the month. If selected to perform at Authentic Selves, our host will contact you.
Do you need volunteers?
Yes! Volunteers are valuable to us! Please visit our Volunteer page to see what events we need volunteers for. If you volunteer, you get to see the show for free!
Are you associated with the Timucua Native American tribe? Timucua Arts Foundation is not associated with the Timucua Native American tribe. The name comes from the development that co-founders Benoit and Élaine lived in when they began hosting concerts in their home in 2000.
