Bringing Nationally Recognized Comedy and Events to Mahomet.
Visit our beautiful event space in Mahomet's Patton District. Our events include live shows, such as Stand Up Comedy, Musical Acts, Trivia Nights, special shopping events and more! Our venue has bar with alcoholic and non alcoholic beverages. 
We bring high quality, nationally recognized talent to our venue,
with acts featured on late night TV, American Idol, The Voice and more. Weather permitting, we have a large patio area opened up. Right next door is our retail location: Yellow & Co. Boutique. "Please exit through the gift shop!"
Touring Comedians
Catch a comedy show in our artistic event space from one of our talented touring comedians you may have seen on HBO, Showtime, The Tonight Show, David Letterman, Dry Bar Comedy, Comedy Central, Last Comic Standing, America's Got Talent and The Bob and Tom Show! (to mention a few!)
We offer bar service during our events to those 21 and up. Soft drinks also available. 
Listening Room Music Shows
 Experience an intimate listening room–style show featuring top Nashville songwriters and standout artists from The Voice, American Idol, and America’s Got Talent. Each performance blends storytelling and song, giving you an up-close, unforgettable night with some of the most talented voices in the industry.

About Us
Our story began with a shared passion for creativity, community, and a little bit of serendipity.
Elizabeth, who grew up in Missouri and has journeyed through vibrant cities like Atlanta,
Wichita, San Francisco, Ecuador, and Bogotá, studied design in Atlanta and forged her unique
path as a faux painter. Her natural talent soon evolved into teaching and a popular sip-and-paint
business that became a beloved local activity.
Mike, a lifelong Illinois resident and Illinois State University graduate, brought his expertise in
Information Technology management to the partnership. Their paths converged online while
Elizabeth was in Decatur and Mike in Mahomet. Beyond their professional pursuits, their shared
sense of humor—and one unforgettable Bob Zany comedy show—sparked a connection that led
to marriage and the merging of two families.
In 2014, Elizabeth established her studio in Mahomet, and a year later, the couple tied the knot.
Initially a space dedicated solely to sip-and-paint sessions, the studio quickly became a
community hub when visitors mistook it for a boutique. In 2017, inspired by the bright yellow
door of her studio, Elizabeth launched Yellow & Co., offering carefully curated merchandise that
resonated with locals and visitors alike. Within six months, the boutique's growing popularity
necessitated a move to a larger space, which soon evolved into a dual-purpose venue featuring
both a boutique and a creative classroom.
By 2019, the success of Yellow & Co. led to the expansion into a neighboring storefront,
dedicating the original location entirely to the boutique. As trends shifted post-pandemic, the
demand for sip-and-paint sessions declined, prompting Mike and Elizabeth to reimagine their
space. They transformed it into a dynamic venue hosting comedy shows, live music, trivia
nights, and other events that brought nationally recognized talent to Mahomet.
Their journey is one of adaptability and community spirit. With a brand-new, custom-designed
building boasting enhanced features like additional restrooms, a dedicated green room, expanded
storage, and a welcoming patio, Yellow & Co. continues to flourish. Today, the boutique and
entertainment venue stand as a testament to creativity, resilience, and a heartfelt commitment to
bringing people together.
Elizabeth and Mike McDermith, Owners of Yellow & Co.!
