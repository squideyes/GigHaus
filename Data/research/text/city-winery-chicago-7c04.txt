City Winery Chicago is a premier live music venue offering an intimate setting for unforgettable performances. Enjoy live music nearly every night, from rock, R&B, and jazz concerts to stand-up comedy shows and live podcast recordings. Over the years, the venue has welcomed legendary artists including Prince, Robert Glasper, Taj Mahal, Kindred The Family Soul, Lyfe Jennings, 10000 Maniacs, and many more to its stage. As a leading Chicago concert venue, we pair world-class live entertainment with dinner service and award-winning wines for a true dinner and show experience.
Browse our Chicago concert calendar to discover upcoming concerts, comedy events, and special performances throughout the month. Whether planning a date night or searching for live music or concerts in Chicago, City Winery Chicago delivers exceptional performances in a seated venue in the West Loop near Fulton Market.
 Plan Your Visit
Directions, Parking & Dining at City Winery Chicago
City Winery Chicago is located at 1200 W Randolph Street in Chicago’s Fulton Market District, one of the city’s most vibrant dining and entertainment neighborhoods. Whether you're coming for live music, a comedy show, or dinner and a show, getting here is easy.
Parking: We offer valet parking daily beginning at 4pm through West Loop Valet for $20 per car. This valet parking is complimentary for our Vinofile members and can be validated at the concierge desk inside the venue. There are paid lots nearby, including a ParkChicago lot located nearby at 1389 W Randolph St.
Public Transit: City Winery Chicago is easily accessible via public transportation. Take the Pink or Green Line train to the Morgan stop, which is just a short walk to & from the venue.
Rideshare: Uber, Lyft, and other rideshare services frequently drop off and pick up guests directly in front of the venue, making it convenient to arrive or leave after a show.
 FAQ
 Where is City Winery Chicago located?
City Winery Chicago is located at 1200 W Randolph Street in Chicago’s Fulton Market District, one of the city’s most popular dining and entertainment neighborhoods. The venue sits in the heart of the West Loop, surrounded by restaurants, bars, and nightlife. Many guests visit City Winery for a full evening experience, combining dinner, wine, and live music in one location.
 What types of concerts and shows happen at City Winery Chicago?
City Winery Chicago hosts a diverse lineup of live music, comedy shows, podcast recordings, brunch performances, and special events throughout the year. Our calendar regularly features national touring artists, singer-songwriters, jazz musicians, tribute concerts, and curated local performances. Because of the venue’s intimate concert setting, audiences are able to experience performances up close in a seated environment.
 Can I order food and wine during the show?
Yes. City Winery Chicago is known for our dinner and a show experience, allowing guests to enjoy full dinner service and award-winning wines during most performances. Guests can order food and drinks directly from their table while watching the show, or arrive early to enjoy the restaurant or wine bar before the performance begins.
 Is seating reserved at City Winery concerts?
Most shows feature reserved seating so guests can relax and enjoy the performance with food and wine service throughout the evening.
 What time do doors open for shows?
Door times can vary depending on the event, but guests are typically able to enter the venue 60 to 90 minutes before showtime. Arriving early is recommended if you plan to order dinner or drinks before the performance begins.
 What makes City Winery different from other Chicago concert venues?
City Winery Chicago offers a unique concert and dining experience that combines live music, award-winning wines, and chef-driven cuisine under one roof. Unlike many traditional music venues, guests can enjoy a full meal and wine service while watching the performance. The venue’s intimate concert hall also allows audiences to experience artists in a closer, more personal setting than many larger Chicago venues.
 What is the concert experience like at City Winery Chicago?
City Winery Chicago offers an intimate concert experience where guests can enjoy live performances in a seated venue with table service. Unlike larger concert halls, the space is designed to bring audiences closer to the music, allowing guests to enjoy dinner, wine, and cocktails while watching the show. The venue’s acoustics and smaller capacity make it a favorite for fans looking to experience artists in a more personal setting.
 Do shows frequently sell out?
Because City Winery Chicago is an intimate venue with limited seating, many concerts and comedy shows sell out in advance. Popular touring artists, tribute performances, and special events often fill up quickly. Guests interested in attending a show are encouraged to check the event calendar regularly and purchase tickets early to secure their preferred seating.

City Winery Chicago brings a fresh take to brunch shows in Chicago, where every weekend delivers a new kind of experience. From soulful brunch performances and high-energy tribute shows to lively drag brunches and special events, the lineup keeps things fresh while live music sets the tone alongside great food and drinks. Beyond brunch, explore a full calendar of rock concerts, R&B performances, jazz and blues shows, and comedy events.
Browse upcoming Chicago brunch shows and plan your next outing. Whether you're looking for Chicago brunch with live music or something a little different, City Winery Chicago has you covered in the West Loop near Fulton Market.
