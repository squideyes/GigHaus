EVENT VENUE IN THE HEART OF THE GORGE
Live Nights–amplified at the Ruins ​
​
The Ruins is more than just a beautiful venue—it’s a gathering space for joy, movement, electric energy and unforgettable music. From local legends to touring acts, we welcome performers who bring heart, soul, and something to dance to. Our historic walls echo best with genres like funk, rock, blues, swing, folk, Americana, soul, and anything with a rhythm that gets people on their feet. While we cast a wide musical net, we tend to steer clear of modern country, hip-hop, hardcore, or metal.
​
If you’re interested in playing here, we’d love to hear from you. Take a look at our upcoming shows to get a sense of the vibe, then send us your info through the form on our Event inquiries Page.

FREQUENTLY ASKED PERFORMER QUESTIONS
How do I book a show at The Ruins?
Start by filling out our booking request form on this page. We review every submission and do our best to respond quickly—especially if your sound aligns with our vibe and community. 
What kinds of acts do you typically host?
We host a wide range: from indie rock and Americana to jazz, folk, experimental, and global music. We prioritize original music, high-energy live sets, and artists who bring something unique to the Gorge. Local and touring acts are both welcome.
What’s the venue capacity?
The outdoor courtyard can accommodate up to 400 standing guests and 220 seated. The indoor space (The Back Room) is more intimate, best suited for shows around 100–120 guests.
What kind of sound and tech support do you offer?
We provide a full PA system, sound technician, lighting, and backline basics for most shows. If you have specific tech needs, just let us know when you submit your inquiry and we’ll confirm what’s available.
Do you pay musicians or split ticket sales?
We offer a range of compensation models depending on the show—door splits, guarantees, and occasionally ticketed series. Let us know your rates and expectations, and we’ll talk logistics when booking.
Can we sell our own merch at the show?
Yes, absolutely! We provide a space for merch tables and encourage artists to sell directly to fans. Keep 100% of your merch revenue—no venue cut.
Is there food or drink available for show attendees?
Yes. The Ruins bar serves local beer, wine, cider, and non-alcoholic options. On select nights, we also partner with food carts or caterers. Let us know if you'd like to coordinate food for your show.
Where is The Ruins located? Where can we stay and what should we do while we're here?
The Ruins is situated in downtown Hood River within walking distance of shops, restaurants, nightlife, and multiple hotels, we’d be happy to refer you to. 
Our neighbors at Lightwell Hotel have 69 rooms and suites, making it a perfect home base for the whole wedding to stay in downtown Hood River, plus an on-site adventure planning lounge, restaurants and a spa. 
The Ruins also offers onsite parking spaces. Street parking and shuttle or bus options are available upon request.
Can we check out the space or do a walkthrough before the show?
Yes! Once a show is confirmed, we’re happy to schedule a site visit or walkthrough so you and your team can get a feel for the space, load-in logistics, and soundcheck flow.
