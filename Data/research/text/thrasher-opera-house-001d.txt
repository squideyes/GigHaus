The Thrasher Opera House
Our mission is to promote the arts and the power of live performance, to provide a community gathering place, and to preserve the historic Thrasher Opera House.
As you visit us at Thrasher Opera House, please take a moment to acknowledge the original people of this land, including the Myaamia (Miami), Očhéthi Šakówiŋ (Sioux Nation), and Hoocąk (Ho-Chunk).

The mission of the Thrasher Opera House Corporation is to promote the arts, provide a community gathering place, and preserve historic Thrasher Opera House. This is done in cooperation with area educational, artistic, and civic organizations, as supported by individual and corporate donations, and utilizing the skills and contributions of community volunteers.
