City Winery Atlanta is a premier live music venue offering an intimate setting for unforgettable performances. Enjoy live music nearly every night, from rock, R&B, and jazz concerts to stand-up comedy shows and live podcast recordings. Over the years, the venue has welcomed legendary artists including Prince, Robert Glasper, Taj Mahal, Kindred The Family Soul, Lyfe Jennings, 10000 Maniacs, and many more to its stage. As a leading Atlanta concert venue, we pair world-class live entertainment with dinner service and award-winning wines for a true dinner and show experience. Browse our Atlanta concert calendar to discover upcoming concerts, comedy events, and special performances throughout the month. Whether planning a date night or searching for live music or concerts in Atlanta, City Winery Atlanta delivers exceptional performances in a seated venue inside Ponce City Market in Atlanta's Old Fourth Ward.
 Plan Your Visit
Directions, Parking & Dining at City Winery Atlanta
City Winery Atlanta is located at 650 North Ave NE inside Ponce City Market, one of Atlanta's most iconic mixed-use destinations in the Old Fourth Ward neighborhood. Whether you're coming for live music, a comedy show, or dinner and a show, getting here is easy.
Parking: Parking is available in the Ponce City Market parking deck, accessible from North Avenue. This parking is complimentary for our Vinofile members and can be validated at the concierge desk inside the venue. Additional street parking and paid lots can be found throughout the surrounding Old Fourth Ward neighborhood.
Public Transit: City Winery Atlanta is easily accessible via public transportation. Take the MARTA Red or Gold Line to the North Avenue station, which is just a short walk from Ponce City Market and the venue.
Rideshare: Uber, Lyft, and other rideshare services frequently drop off and pick up guests directly in front of the venue, making it convenient to arrive or leave after a show.
 FAQ
 Where is City Winery Atlanta located?
City Winery Atlanta is located at 650 North Ave NE inside Ponce City Market, one of Atlanta's most beloved destinations in the Old Fourth Ward neighborhood. The venue sits within a historic mixed-use landmark surrounded by restaurants, shops, rooftop attractions, and nightlife. Many guests visit City Winery for a full evening experience, combining dinner, wine, and live music in one location.
 What types of concerts and shows happen at City Winery Atlanta?
City Winery Atlanta hosts a diverse lineup of live music, comedy shows, podcast recordings, brunch performances, and special events throughout the year. Our calendar regularly features national touring artists, singer-songwriters, jazz musicians, tribute concerts, and curated local performances. Because of the venue's intimate concert setting, audiences are able to experience performances up close in a seated environment.
 Can I order food and wine during the show?
Yes. City Winery Atlanta is known for our dinner and a show experience, allowing guests to enjoy full dinner service and award-winning wines during most performances. Guests can order food and drinks directly from their table while watching the show, or arrive early to enjoy the restaurant or wine bar before the performance begins.
 Is seating reserved at City Winery Atlanta concerts?
Most shows feature reserved seating so guests can relax and enjoy the performance with food and wine service throughout the evening.
 What time do doors open for shows?
Door times can vary depending on the event, but guests are typically able to enter the venue 60 to 90 minutes before showtime. Arriving early is recommended if you plan to order dinner or drinks before the performance begins.
 What makes City Winery Atlanta different from other Atlanta concert venues?
City Winery Atlanta offers a unique concert and dining experience that combines live music, award-winning wines, and chef-driven cuisine under one roof. Unlike many traditional music venues, guests can enjoy a full meal and wine service while watching the performance. The venue's intimate concert hall also allows audiences to experience artists in a closer, more personal setting than many larger Atlanta venues.
 What is the concert experience like at City Winery Atlanta?
City Winery Atlanta offers an intimate concert experience where guests can enjoy live performances in a seated venue with table service. Unlike larger concert halls, the space is designed to bring audiences closer to the music, allowing guests to enjoy dinner, wine, and cocktails while watching the show. The venue's acoustics and smaller capacity make it a favorite for fans looking to experience artists in a more personal setting.
 Do shows frequently sell out?
Because City Winery Atlanta is an intimate venue with limited seating, many concerts and comedy shows sell out in advance. Popular touring artists, tribute performances, and special events often fill up quickly. Guests interested in attending a show are encouraged to check the event calendar regularly and purchase tickets early to secure their preferred seating.

City Winery Atlanta takes Atlanta brunch seriously — and in a city that has elevated brunch culture to an art form, that's saying something. Tucked inside Ponce City Market in the heart of Old Fourth Ward, our Atlanta brunch events bring live music, a thoughtfully crafted menu, and an award-winning wine and cocktail program together in a setting that feels as vibrant and alive as Atlanta itself. Atlanta brunch-goers know the difference between a spot that just serves food on a Sunday and one that actually creates an experience — and City Winery is firmly in the latter category. From the moment you walk in to the last note of the afternoon set, every detail is designed to make your Atlanta Sunday the best one yet. Beyond brunch, explore our full Atlanta events calendar including Atlanta rock, Atlanta R&B, Atlanta jazz, and Atlanta comedy, all paired with dinner service and award-winning wines. Atlanta has no shortage of brunch options — but only one of them puts you this close to live music inside Ponce City Market. Browse upcoming Atlanta brunch events at City Winery and secure your spot in Old Fourth Ward.
