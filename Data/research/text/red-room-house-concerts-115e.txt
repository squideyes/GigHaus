About Red Room House Concerts 
Red Room House Concerts are private, invitation-only events where music lovers enjoy performances by talented musicians in a smoke-free listening room setting. Red Room House Concerts is not a business. A donation is requested, 100% of which goes directly to the musicians we host in our Cleveland, OH home.
Performances generally consist of two 45-minute sets, with a short break in between. You’ll have the opportunity to talk with the musicians and purchase their CDs and other merchandise. Seating is limited and reservations are required.
Reservations
Request a reservation by e-mailing rsvp@redroomhouseconcerts.com. Include your name, as well as the names of anyone else coming with you. Larger groups should make separate reservations. We’ll send you an e-mail to confirm your seats. Directions and other details will be supplied via e-mail, a few days before the house concert. If your plans change and you are unable to attend, please let us know.
Stay In Touch
E-mail is our primary method of announcing upcoming shows. Sign up for show invites and reminders through our mailing list. Please send a message to info@redroomhouseconcerts.com. We’ll never share your e-mail address.
Questions?
If you have any questions, please send a message to info@redroomhouseconcerts.com. Thank you.
