City Winery Pittsburgh is a premier live music venue offering an intimate setting for unforgettable performances. Enjoy live music nearly every night, from rock, R&B, and jazz concerts to stand-up comedy shows and live podcast recordings. Over the years, the venue has welcomed legendary artists to its stage, all within the vibrant surroundings of Pittsburgh's historic Strip District. As a leading Pittsburgh concert venue, we pair world-class live entertainment with dinner service and award-winning wines for a true dinner and show experience. Browse our Pittsburgh concert calendar to discover upcoming concerts, comedy events, and special performances throughout the month. Whether planning a date night or searching for live music or concerts in Pittsburgh, City Winery Pittsburgh delivers exceptional performances in an intimate seated venue inside the Strip District Terminal.
 Plan Your Visit
Directions, Parking & Dining at City Winery Pittsburgh
City Winery Pittsburgh is located at 1627 Smallman St, Pittsburgh, PA 15222, inside the historic Strip District Terminal — one of Pittsburgh's most exciting dining and entertainment destinations. Whether you're coming for live music, a comedy show, or dinner and a show, getting here is easy.
Parking: There is paid parking available behind the building. Street parking on Smallman St can also be found, often free after 6:30 PM. After 4:00 PM on weekdays and all day on weekends, additional public parking is available in the 1600 Smallman Garage across from the Terminal at 16th Street.
Public Transit: The 54, 86, 87, 88, and 91 Port Authority buses travel through the Strip District with stops on Liberty and/or Penn Avenue, a short walk from the venue. Visit the Strip District Terminal for more information.
Rideshare: Uber, Lyft, and other rideshare services frequently drop off and pick up guests directly in front of the venue, making it easy to arrive or leave after a show.
 FAQ
 Where is City Winery Pittsburgh located?
City Winery Pittsburgh is located at 1627 Smallman St inside the Strip District Terminal, one of Pittsburgh's most popular dining and entertainment destinations. The venue sits in the heart of the historic Strip District, surrounded by restaurants, bars, and nightlife. Many guests visit City Winery for a full evening experience, combining dinner, wine, and live music in one location.
 What types of concerts and shows happen at City Winery Pittsburgh?
City Winery Pittsburgh hosts a diverse lineup of live music, comedy shows, podcast recordings, brunch performances, and special events throughout the year. Our calendar regularly features national touring artists, singer-songwriters, jazz musicians, tribute concerts, and curated local performances. Because of the venue's intimate concert setting, audiences are able to experience performances up close in a seated environment.
 Can I order food and wine during the show?
Yes. City Winery Pittsburgh is known for our dinner and a show experience, allowing guests to enjoy full dinner service and award-winning wines during most performances. Guests can order food and drinks directly from their table while watching the show, or arrive early to enjoy the restaurant or wine bar before the performance begins.
 Is seating reserved at City Winery concerts?
Most shows feature reserved seating so guests can relax and enjoy the performance with food and wine service throughout the evening.
 What time do doors open for shows?
Door times can vary depending on the event, but guests are typically able to enter the venue 60 to 90 minutes before showtime. Arriving early is recommended if you plan to order dinner or drinks before the performance begins.
 What makes City Winery different from other Pittsburgh concert venues?
City Winery Pittsburgh offers a unique concert and dining experience that combines live music, award-winning wines, and chef-driven cuisine under one roof. Unlike many traditional music venues, guests can enjoy a full meal and wine service while watching the performance. The venue's intimate concert hall also allows audiences to experience artists in a closer, more personal setting than many larger Pittsburgh venues.
 What is the concert experience like at City Winery Pittsburgh?
City Winery Pittsburgh offers an intimate concert experience where guests can enjoy live performances in a seated venue with table service. Unlike larger concert halls, the space is designed to bring audiences closer to the music, allowing guests to enjoy dinner, wine, and cocktails while watching the show. The venue's acoustics and smaller capacity make it a favorite for fans looking to experience artists in a more personal setting.
 Do shows frequently sell out?
Because City Winery Pittsburgh is an intimate venue with limited seating, many concerts and comedy shows sell out in advance. Popular touring artists, tribute performances, and special events often fill up quickly. Guests interested in attending a show are encouraged to check the event calendar regularly and purchase tickets early to secure their preferred seating.

Enjoy chef driven cuisine, award winning City Winery wines, and handcrafted cocktails served directly to your table during live music, comedy shows, and special events. Prefer daytime plans? Join us for brunch and a show. Reserve your favorite wine in advance and we will have your bottle waiting at your table when you arrive, perfectly paired and ready to pour. We will also save your selection for future shows so every visit feels effortless.
