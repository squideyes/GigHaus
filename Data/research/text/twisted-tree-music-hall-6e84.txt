Indoor Concert Venue in Jacksonville Illinois 
The Main Building at Twisted Tree is a 7,000 square foot indoor venue featuring the Twisted Tree Stage, a 384 square foot professional performance space designed for live concerts, corporate events, and private rentals. With full sound and lighting, ample room for seating or standing audiences, and modern amenities, this stage brings the excitement of live music indoors. The layout is versatile enough to host intimate shows as well as large gatherings, making it a cornerstone of entertainment in Jacksonville Illinois. 
Why Go to Events at the Twisted Tree Stage 
The Twisted Tree Stage delivers the energy of a full concert with the comfort of an indoor setting. Guests enjoy professional sound, clear sightlines, and a spacious floor plan that can adapt to seated or standing arrangements. Unlike outdoor shows that depend on weather, the Twisted Tree Stage provides year-round reliability without sacrificing atmosphere.
Artists value the professional production setup, complete with lighting, sound, and backstage space. Guests appreciate the comfort of indoor seating, climate control, and convenient access to amenities. Together, this makes every event feel polished and memorable.
The Main Building can be rented on its own for indoor events or as part of the full Twisted Tree property. This flexibility gives organizers the ability to tailor the venue to the scale and style of their event. 
The Twisted Tree Indoor Stage Experience 
Professional Setup 
The Twisted Tree Stage measures 24 feet by 16 feet, providing 384 square feet of performance space. It is equipped with professional sound and lighting systems that meet the needs of touring acts, local performers, and private events. 
Spacious Layout 
The 7,000 square foot building offers plenty of room for audiences with flexible seating or standing arrangements. Whether you want rows of chairs for a corporate presentation or an open floor for a high-energy concert, the space adapts to your vision. Televisions throughout the venue display the stage! 
Events Your Way 
With climate control, ample space, and indoor amenities, the Twisted Tree Stage is the perfect year-round venue. Guests can enjoy the excitement of live music without worrying about weather conditions, making it a dependable choice for both organizers and attendees.

The Saloon. Live Music Event Stage & Bar In Jacksonville Illinois 
Guests love attending shows here because the atmosphere is relaxed yet professional. The venue includes ample seating, a full bar for refreshments, and convenient amenities like bathrooms and a green room for performers. It is a space where you can settle in comfortably, enjoy great sound, and feel part of the performance.
Performers appreciate the Saloon because it allows them to connect with audiences on a deeper level. Whether it is an acoustic set, a songwriter showcase, or a community gathering, the intimate layout keeps the focus on the artist and the music.
The Saloon can be rented on its own for smaller events or as part of the full Twisted Tree property for larger multi-space gatherings. This flexibility makes it a one-of-a-kind destination for acoustic performances and private events in Jacksonville Illinois. 
Why Come to Events at The Saloon? 
The Treehouse is not just another outdoor stage. It is an experience that combines the thrill of live music with the wide-open energy of a festival atmosphere. At more than 800 square feet, this stage was built to showcase everything from high-energy touring acts to local favorites that bring the community together.
Guests love the Treehouse because it offers freedom of choice in how they enjoy a show. Some gather close to the stage for an up-front concert experience. Others spread out across the expansive grassy lawn with chairs and blankets, creating a relaxed and social environment. For those who prefer the comfort of being indoors, the main building opens directly into the outdoor stage area, allowing the crowd inside to stay connected to the energy outside.
Every event at the Treehouse feels larger than life. As the sun sets and the lights rise, the atmosphere shifts into something unforgettable. The open air, the professional sound, and the natural backdrop of the property all come together to create a setting that cannot be found anywhere else in Jacksonville or Central Illinois. 
The Saloon Music & Event Experience 
Intimate Atmosphere 
The Saloon creates a personal connection between performers and guests. Every seat feels close to the stage, making it easy to enjoy acoustic sets, open mic nights, and smaller shows where the focus stays on the music. The layout keeps the sound balanced and the atmosphere warm, turning each performance into an experience that feels one of a kind. 
Comfort and Amenities 
With 6,000 square feet of space, the Saloon offers ample seating, a full bar, bathrooms, and a green room for performers. These features ensure that both artists and audiences have everything they need for a smooth and enjoyable event. It is a professional setup designed for smaller gatherings without losing comfort or quality. 
Flexible Use 
The Saloon can be rented as a standalone venue or as part of the entire Twisted Tree property. This flexibility allows organizers to host private events, community functions, or acoustic performances in a space that adapts to their needs. Whether it is an intimate concert or a small group celebration, the Saloon provides the right balance of space and character.
