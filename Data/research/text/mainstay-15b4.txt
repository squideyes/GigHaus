Home of Musical Magic! 
The Mainstay, founded in 1997, is a nonprofit live music venue dedicated to the performing arts, serving the Eastern Shore of Maryland, and readily accessible to our neighbors from Baltimore, Annapolis, Washington D.C., Wilmington, and Philadelphia. In its keystone location in the harbor town of Rock Hall, Maryland, the Mainstay programs an impressive year-round, weekly calendar of accomplished local, regional, national, and international performing artists.

SUPPORT THE MAINSTAY
Attending live music performances is not just a recreational choice, especially when you have confidence in the music venue’s commitment to superior talent and appealing variety. These events are the golden threads that enrich the fabric of our lives and linger in our memories.
They help us maneuver through the stress and tedium that invariably comes our way in these challenging times. They provide the opportunities to connect with others on common ground, to feel safe and strong.
Can you imagine if circumstances hit the mute button and turned this special music off? The Mainstay has been part of people’s stories around here for decades. The generous support of our friends and visitors has kept the music playing even through difficult times. Please help us to meet these challenging times with good will, a strong back, and a stable financial footing.
Please consider a meaningful donation to our organization (“meaningful” is different for everyone). Donate online by clicking here or send us your check to P.O. Box 238, Rock Hall, MD 21661.

The Charlie Byrd Society
Jazz guitar legend Charlie Byrd’s performances at the Mainstay helped establish our tradition of musical excellence. To honor Charlie’s legacy and support the Mainstay’s commitment to spectacular music and service to the community, we invite you to join the Charlie Byrd Society with an annual pledge. Membership information is available upon request.

OTHER WAYS TO SUPPORT THE MAINSTAY
Donate Your Time
The Mainstay depends on its volunteers. If you are interested in learning more about the various ongoing or occasional roles you could play in making this place run smoothly, please contact us.
Donate Your Baking Skills
All our live shows offer homemade goodies, from pies to cookies to snack mixes. If you have a specialty item or simply enjoy baking, we would love to put your goodies on our show schedule.
Donate Some Furnishings
As curators of one of the world’s most impressive collections of used dining room chairs, we have come to realize that some items eventually face retirement. Please call us if you have sets of matching (preferably waterproof) chairs, oriental rugs of various sizes, folding metal chairs and tables, or conversation-starting floor or table lamps. Obviously, these should be in good condition. 
The Mainstay Folk Music Fund
BREAKING NEWS! FOLK MUSIC FANS, THIS ONE’S FOR YOU!
Calling all folk music lovers! We’re excited to announce the creation of The Mainstay Folk Music Fund, a wonderful new initiative launched by two of our longtime supporters and friends, Becky Byrd and Debra Woodruff Capper!
This special fund will be dedicated exclusively to supporting upcoming folk music concerts, including performances by Tom Chapin, Cathy Fink, Mike Agranoff, and other outstanding artists who keep the rich tradition of folk music alive.
And here’s where YOU come in! Whether you can contribute a little or a lot, every dollar makes a difference! Your generosity helps us bring the very best folk music talent to The Mainstay while keeping ticket prices as affordable as possible.
What better way to celebrate the spirit of folk music than by coming together as a community to keep it thriving?
Every gift counts, every supporter matters, and every song is worth sharing!
Join Becky and Debra in helping us make The Mainstay an even greater home for folk music!

Sue Matthews and Robert Redd 
For nearly two decades, Sue Matthews and Robert Redd have been bringing their popular “Songbook Series” concerts to The Mainstay, celebrating the timeless music of the Great American Songbook. Now, they have finally decided to record some of their favorites.
Joined by an outstanding group of musicians, James King on bass, Charlie Young on saxophone, and Lenny Robinson on drums, Sue and Robert recorded their new CD, “Perfect Match,” this summer at the award winning Tonal Park studio in Takoma Park, Maryland.
On Saturday, November 14, the entire group comes to The Mainstay to bring that recording to life on our stage.
The evening will feature selections from “Perfect Match,” including such enduring standards as “Skylark,” “P.S. I Love You,” “Over the Rainbow,” and “As Long As I Live,” along with many more favorites from one of the richest eras in American popular music.
As audiences have come to expect from their Songbook Series concerts, Sue and Robert will do more than simply perform the songs. Throughout the evening, they’ll share stories about the singers who made them famous and the remarkable composers and lyricists who created them.
It promises to be a wonderful evening of great songs, great musicianship and the stories behind the music, presented by two longtime Mainstay favorites and some very special musical friends.

Ronn McFarlane 
On Sunday, November 15, Lutenist Ronn McFarlane, a Mainstay audience favorite, will perform on lute, works by English, Italian, French, and German composers from the 16th and 17th centuries. These works feature John Dowland, Francesco da Milano, Hans Newsidler, Robert Ballard, and – of course -- that all-time favorite and most prolific composer: Anonymous.
GRAMMY-nominated lutenist, Ronn McFarlane strives to bring the lute - the most popular instrument of the Renaissance - into today’s musical mainstream and make it accessible to a wider audience. Since taking up the lute in 1978, Ronn has made his mark in music as the founder of Ayreheart, a founding member of the Baltimore Consort, touring 49 of the 50 United States, Canada, England, Scotland, Netherlands, Switzerland, Germany, and Austria, and as a guest artist with Apollo’s Fire, The Bach Sinfonia, The Catacoustic Consort, The Folger Consort, Houston Grand Opera, The Oregon Symphony, The Portland Baroque Orchestra, and The Indianapolis Baroque Orchestra. Born in West Virginia, Ronn grew up in Maryland. At thirteen, upon hearing “Wipeout” by the Surfaris, he fell madly in love with music and taught himself to play on a “cranky sixteen-dollar steel string guitar.” Ronn kept at it, playing blues and rock music on the electric guitar while studying classical guitar. He graduated with honors from Shenandoah Conservatory and continued guitar studies at Peabody Conservatory before turning his full attention and energy to the lute. McFarlane was a faculty member of the Peabody Conservatory from 1984 to 1995, teaching lute and lute-related subjects. In 1996, Mr. McFarlane was awarded an honorary Doctorate of Music from Shenandoah Conservatory for his achievements in bringing the lute and its music to the world. He has over 40 recordings on the Dorian/Sono Luminus label, including solo albums, lute duets, flute & lute duets, viola da gamba & lute duets, lute songs, the complete lute music of Vivaldi, a collection of Elizabethan lute music and poetry, and recordings with the Baltimore Consort. Ronn has composed new music for the lute, building on the tradition of the lutenist/composers of past centuries. His original compositions are the focus of his solo CD, Indigo Road, which received a GRAMMY Award Nomination. In 2010 Ronn founded Ayreheart, an ensemble brought together to perform new compositions as well as early music. Ayreheart’s first CD release, One Morning, consists of all- original music by Ronn McFarlane. Ayreheart’s 2016 release, Barley Moon, blends folk music and art music from Renaissance and Medieval England, Scotland, and Wales. Ronn's solo album, The Celtic Lute (2018), features his arrangements of traditional Scottish and Irish music from the 17th and 18th centuries. Fermi’s Paradox (2020), A Star in the East (2021), and And So Flows the River (2023) with Carolyn Surrick, viola da gamba, feature an eclectic blend of Renaissance, Baroque, original music, hymns, and folk tunes from Ireland, Scotland, England, and Sweden.

Joe Holt Presents Doug James
The Final Special Friday Show of the Season
Joe Holt closes out this season’s popular Special Friday series with a very special guest, award-winning singer and songwriter Doug James.
As always, Joe will be at the Mainstay piano, bringing the remarkable musicianship, spontaneity and good humor that have made these Friday evenings such audience favorites. This time, he welcomes a songwriter whose music has been heard around the world.
Doug James has written songs recorded by Michael Bolton, Dionne Warwick, Cher, Joe Cocker, Chaka Khan, Barry Manilow, Laura Branigan, Kelly Clarkson, The Spinners and many others. His credits include the Billboard #1 hit and BMI Song of the Year, “How Am I Supposed to Live Without You.” He was named Performer of the Year by the Rehoboth Beach Jazz Festival and, in October 2026, was inducted into the Delaware Rock and Roll Society Hall of Fame.
Two gifted musicians, plenty of songs and stories, and Joe Holt at the piano. Join us for one last Special Friday of the season.
