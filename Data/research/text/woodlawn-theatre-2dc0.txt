Birmingham’s LOCAL 
music venue
We are a 250 cap space for live music and private events in the heart of historic downtown Woodlawn. We host all kinds of genres and events - music from hip hop and R&B to indie rock and americana; events from corporate training sessions and panel discussions to birthday parties and weddings. Check out our show calendar, sign up for our newsletter, and join the Woodlawn Theatre community. We’ll see you soon. 
OUR MISSION
To be a part of the restorative process in Woodlawn by reviving a forgotten space where we host memorable events for all of our neighbors.

✺ Frequently asked questions ✺
 How much is it?
$250 for the 2027 Golden Ticket
 Can I literally come to every show?
Yes, please and thank you. 
 Are VIP upgrades included? 
No - typically upsells for shows are a part of the band’s revenue. If you wish to reserve special seating for a show, you will need to purchase separately to support the band(s). 
 What if a show sells out?
When a show reaches 80% of capacity sold, we will email our Golden Ticket holders and request an RSVP to attend. Don’t worry, we will always have a spot for you if you forget. This just helps us plan for seating and staffing. 
 Can I Bring a friend?
Golden Tickets are non-transferable, so only the original purchaser can use them for entry. Your friend will need their own ticket, or better yet, Golden Ticket. 
 Do the bands get the money?
No. Golden Tickets are meant to be a way for you to support the venue directly while upgrading your experience at shows. Golden tickets fit within our house complimentary ticket allotment and do not affect band settlements. 
 How do I renew for next year?
Each year we will put Golden Tickets on sale at the end of the year for the following year. These make a great holiday gift for the music lover or arts supporter in your life. 
 What if I lose my Golden Ticket?
Not to worry, simply request a new one and use your ID for entry in the meantime.

Give your event
the stage it deserves
Host corporate events, fundraisers, weddings and private parties for up to 250 guests—with a professional stage, sound system, 18-foot screen and full-service bar.
150 seated · 250 standing · Full day rentals from $2,000

Flexible for every occasion
What are you planning?
From polished presentations to milestone celebrations, Woodlawn Theatre can be configured for your guest count, program and production needs.
Weddings
Host your ceremony or reception with a stage, dance floor, full-service bar and private backstage suite with its own entrance and restroom.
Corporate events
Bring presentations, awards ceremonies and company celebrations to life with flexible seating, a professional stage, sound system and 18-foot screen.
private parties
Celebrate birthdays, anniversaries and other milestones in a stylish, approachable setting with room to gather, dance and entertain.
Fundraisers and Galas
Create a memorable experience for your supporters with flexible seating, built-in production, bar service and support for ticketed events. 
Have something different in mind?
We also offer flexible off-peak rentals for meetings, workshops, rehearsals, screenings and other gatherings.

Capacity and venue features
Not too big, not too small… Just right. 
Woodlawn Theatre works to fill a gap in the event space options in Birmingham - with a 250 person standing room capacity and a 150 person seated capacity, the Theatre offers an accommodating size for your event. Not too big, not too small. Just right.

Fair and transparent pricing $-$$$
Our room rental rates are built on what you actually need, not a one-size-fits-all package. Don’t need an A/V tech? Don’t pay for one. Would you like a valet service? We can add that in. Our staff will work with you to determine what’s necessary for your event’s success while helping your event budget go further. *Base rate is $2,000/day. Events less than 3 hours can be billed at $300/hr. Contact us for a customized quote.

Hospitality and Amenities for every occasion. 
We pride ourselves on our staff’s excellent hospitality - in fact, it’s one of our core values! And when it comes to amenities, we have what you need! A stage with full PA system, an 18’ motorized screen with projector, a full service bar, a dressing room with private entrance and bathroom, and so much more. Located on 1st Ave North, Woodlawn Theatre is less than 2 miles from the Birmingham Shuttlesworth Airport. It may just be the closest event space to the Birmingham Airport. For Birmingham natives, we are a 5-minute drive from Avondale and 10 minutes from downtown, and located across the street from Woodrow Hall and Cornerstone Elementary.

 How many guests can woodlawn theatre accommodate?
Woodlawn Theatre can be configured many different ways. 
Seated at tables: 75-100
Seated in rows: 100-150
Standing room only: 250
 What does venue rental include?
Use of full venue
Tables and chairs (you may supplement if additional are needed)
Access to A/V equipment (additional charge for hiring an A/V tech if needed)
 What is the starting price for rentals?
$2,000 for a full day.
$300/hr for rentals shorter than 4 hrs
 Can we bring our own caterer?
Yes
 How does bar service work?
We can build a custom bar package with you based on your needs. We have a liquor license and bar staff available as needed. 
 Is parking available?
Yes. There is free street parking on 1st Ave N, as well as 2 municipal lots within walking distance. 
Behind the venue there is private parking for hosts and staff. 
You can hire a valet or shuttle service if needed. 
 Are tables and chairs included?
Yes - 
150 padded black folding chairs
6 X green leather barstools
4 X bistro sized tables
6 X pop up high boys
8 X 6ft folding tables
4 X 5ft folding tables
 Can the stage, projector, and sound system be used?
Yes - for an additional fee, we can add an A/V tech to the event to run sound, lights, projector, etc. 
 How far is the venue from downtown Birmingham and the airport?
4 miles (10 min) from downtown. 
Less than 2 miles (5 min) from the airport.
