Willingham Theater
We want every citizen and visitor of Yadkin County to be moved, inspired, informed, and challenged by the transformational power of the art they consume. But art capable of inspiring, educating, and exploring is not limited to pen to paper, or paint to canvas.
The staple of our theater arts program is the Willingham Theater.
Throughout history, creatives have documented, questioned, and reconstructed society based on the power of their music and stories.
Our mission is to transform and enrich the lives of every citizen, and visitor, of Yadkin County through a broad variety of arts programs, including live theater.
The staple of our theater arts program is the Willingham Theater.
About the Theater
The Willingham Theater hosted its first performance in December 2012, just two short years after the Yadkin Cultural Arts Center opened its doors to the citizens of Yadkin County. While many might dismiss the power of a small city theater, we like to prove that we can indeed bring the big city excitement and experience to our small town. Our state-of-the-art theater seats 193 patrons and showcases a variety of engaging performances ranging from music, dance, drama, comedy, and even film. In addition to ample seating, the Willingham Theater has a remarkable state-of-the-art-technology.

About Us
The Yadkin Arts Council
The Yadkin Arts Council is a nonprofit 501(C)3 organization dedicated to enriching the lives of Yadkin County citizens through the arts. The Yadkin Arts Council has been a creative beacon in the community since 1975 and has shared space all over Yadkinville, including in the Yadkinville High School auditorium, and even a retired freezer locker! Finally, we found our permanent home when the Yadkin Cultural Arts Center opened its doors in September 2011. Click HERE to learn More About the History of the Yadkin Arts Council. 
“Transforming lives in our community through the arts by offering opportunities to encounter, create, and participate.”
Yadkin Cultural Arts Center
While we started small in those shared spaces, we have expanded rapidly in our new space, bringing big city excitement to our small town!
The Yadkin Cultural Arts Center is the home for our Yadkin Arts Council, and also features the 193-seat Willingham Theater, the YARD resident artist studios, The Willingham Performing Arts Academy, the Center Bistro, the Welborn Gallery, the Spark Costume Shop, meeting space and classrooms, and the versatile Front Yard space.
Yadkin Arts Cultural Center Facilities
Yadkin Arts Council
The Arts Council is governed by a 20-member Board. It receives grassroots funding from the North Carolina Arts Council, a division of the Office of Cultural Resources. The Yadkin Arts Council allocates these funds for arts experience and education programs in the schools. 
Willingham Theater
This state-of-the-art facility seats 193 patrons for live play productions as well as film showings throughout the year. It is also a space of learning, engagement, and expression for our students who join us for our summer camps each year. >> Learn More
Resident Artist Studios
Sometimes it can be difficult to create in your home due to a lack of space or inability to dip into that creative mindset. Because of this, we provide resident art studios which can be rented so you can dedicate a space for your creative work. In addition, patrons can arrange to visit the studios to meet with artists and watch them work. >> Learn More
The Center Bistro
The Center Bistro provides a convenient place to grab breakfast or lunch throughout the week, with ample seating inside as well as a dining patio on the Yadkin Cultural Art Center’s outdoor plaza. The menu has something for every taste, from breakfast items, deli sandwiches and wraps, to soups and salads. You’ll also find the Red Wall Gallery in the bistro, which features work by our Artist Members and other local artists. >> Learn More
Welborn Gallery
The Welborn Gallery is located just outside the Willingham Theater in the arts center and allows us to expose both residents and visitors to a variety of 2-dimensional and 3-dimensional works of art from local and regional artists alike. >> Learn More
Private Rental Space
Meeting Space, Classrooms + Front Yard Space
Among the many facilities available in our arts center, we also maintain private rental space for meetings, bridal showers, or even a dinner party! Whatever you need space for, we can accommodate it. We also have multiple classrooms for our creative workshops and summer art camps. The beautiful Front Yard space provides the comfort and intimacy of your living room while allowing guests plenty of space to socialize and celebrate.

Artist Memberships
Join a growing community with a small-town spirit
As a member artist of the Yadkin Arts Council, you will reap the rewards of membership throughout the year, from exclusive sneak peeks of upcoming events and classes to our quarterly luncheons & gatherings, and ongoing Artist Membership newsletters. The Yadkin Arts Council welcomes all who share a love and passion for the arts to join and participate in our growing community, where art and creativity are accessible to all.
You can enjoy the charm and calmness of the small town of Yadkinville while networking with regional artists, attending exhibitions, showing your work, and partaking in the Art Council’s classes, productions, and events. *Yadkin Arts Council artist members do not need to be a resident of Yadkin County, all are welcome to join!

“ACTORS, ADVANCE!” Studio @ WPAA
Mondays – 4:30-6pm
August 24th – November 16th
8th grade through 12th grade
Instructor – Elijah Golden
$10 per class – $120 for full semester
A weekly acting studio class for 8th-12th grade students with a focus on: acting skills, improv techniques, monologues & scene work (from all genres of theatre), audition prep, and more. If you’re looking to strengthen your acting prowess, this is the class for you! Whether you’re interested in community theatre, regional / professional theatre, performing arts colleges, pursuing acting as a career, or haven’t graced a stage a day in your life, this class will expand your skillset and prepare you for your future endeavors. Build confidence in your performance, strengthen bonds and friendships with fellow teen actors, and advance your skills by joining the “Actors, Advance! Studio” @ WPAA.
“SONGS & SCENES”
Wednesdays – 4:30-6pm
August 26th – November 18th
5th grade through 8th grade
Instructor – Charlie Kluttz
$15 per class – $180 for full semester
A weekly vocal performance and acting class for 5th through 8th grade students with a focus on: strengthening vocal technique, acting through song, group songs & scene work, audition prep, and music theory / musical literacy (aka sight-reading). This class explores dynamic group songs (and the scenes that surround them) from the American musical theatre repertoire. The semester will culminate with a showcase of all songs and scenes students have learned. Additional semester projects include: duet and solo songs & scenes, weekly instructional warm-ups that focus on technique, and improvisational exercises.
“SHAKE IT UP!” with Mackenzie
Thursdays – 4:45-5:45pm
August 27th – November 19th
Kindergarten through 5th grade
Instructor – Mackenzie Houston
$10 per class – $120 for full semester
A weekly music theatre dance class for Kindergarten through 5th grade students with a focus on: strengthening dance fundamentals, “across the floor” movement, combinations, and dance routines. Students will “shake it up” each week while exploring different dance combos set to a wide variety of beloved musical theatre songs. This class focuses on re-enforcing important basic dance & movement fundamentals and techniques. The semester will culminate in a class showcase to demonstrate what students have been working on. Putting the “fun” in fundamentals is exactly what this class is about!
“ADULT ACTING EXPLORATION”
Thursdays – 7-8pm (first 3 Thursdays of each month)
August 27th – November 19th
Adults (18+)
Instructor – Elijah Golden
$10 per class – $120 for full semester
A weekly adult acting class focusing on acting fundamentals and skill-building. Whether you’ve never even considered stepping onto a stage, or you’ve been acting for decades, this is the class for you! This low-stakes, low-pressure class will be shaped around what you want to get out of it. Whether it’s learning a monologue or two for your repertoire, building confidence in your scenework, delving into various acting techniques, or, even just shaking off the nerves of performing in front of others, this class is all about giving adults the space to dip their toes into the world of performance, and explore the many aspects of acting in a stress free, supportive environment.
“IMPROV NIGHT(s) @ WPAA”
Thursday – 7-9pm (last Thursday each month: 7/30, 8/27, 9/24, 10/29)
Adults (18+)
Instructor – Elijah Golden
$10 per session (or free for those enrolled in “Adult Acting Exploration”)
A once-a-month acting improv night for adults at WPAA! This low-pressure monthly workshop will explore: basic improv techniques, improv games, improvising scenes, and more. This low-stakes, low-pressure monthly workshop is all about cutting loose, breaking free, and having fun! Bring your friends and your creativity and see what unfolds on the stage. Perfect for a date night or celebratory occasion with friends!
*** You do not need to be enrolled in “Adult Acting Exploration” to attend “Improv Night” ***
“A BROADWAY BUFFET” CABARET
PERFORMANCE DATES TBD (mid-November)
(One interest meeting in October, then 2 rehearsals before performance in mid-November)
Ages 16+
A one-night-only musical theatre cabaret for ages 16+. Similar to a “miscast” or “broadway backwards” style cabaret, participants will select and prepare their own musical theatre selections to perform solo, or in small groups, in a one night cabaret in November @ WPAA (or on the Willingham Theater Stage). Piano accompaniment or karaoke backing tracks will be provided. Participants will register in the early fall, have one “interest meeting” in October, then 2 rehearsals before the performance in mid-November. Small-group numbers are encouraged. The full cast will learn one combined full-ensemble song to perform at the end of the cabaret. Register via our google form to reserve your spot / show your interest, then stay tuned for more info!

Meet the WPAA Team
Our Artistic Director
CHARLIE KLUTTZ
Charlie Kluttz was born and raised in Concord, NC and moved to Winston-Salem, NC in 2010 to study voice at the University of North Carolina School of the Arts. He obtained his Bachelors in Music (Classical Vocal Performance) and worked extensively with community theaters in the area while at UNCSA. Kluttz currently serves as a Music Director, Scenic Painter, and Scenic Designer for the Yadkin Arts Council in Yadkinville, NC. Kluttz also serves as the primary group and private lesson instructor for the Willingham Performing Arts Academy. Additionally, he is the current Music Theatre instructor for the Acting Out! program which operates under the UNCSA School of Drama.
We hope to see you this fall at the Willingham Performing Arts Academy!
ELIJAH GOLDEN
Eli Golden is very excited to be teaching “Actors, Advance!” and “Adult Acting Exploration” this fall at WPAA! He’s excited to help students learn how to get more out of their theatrical experiences and help students foster a drive to explore deeper as actors. He looks forward to providing a safe space for performers of all ages and skill levels to ask questions and try new things.
Eli Golden is a Theatre Artist from Yadkinville, NC. He obtained a BA in Theatre Arts with a concentration in Performance from Appalachian State University. Calling himself “The Jack Of All Stages,” Eli has experience on all aspects of the theatrical process, having worked extensively both onstage and backstage. He is so thankful to be able to work in the theatre that raised him, and give back to the community that got him this far. 
MACKENZIE WARDEN HOUSTON
Mackenzie Houston has been dancing her whole life and has a deep love for music, theatre, and the performing arts! She has been performing at the Yadkin Arts Council since 2016 and has participated in numerous shows, classes, and productions through the Willingham Performing Arts Academy. 
Mackenzie is also a third-grade teacher and a graduate of High Point University, where she earned her degree in Elementary Education. She believes the arts are a wonderful way for children to build confidence, express themselves, and discover something they love. 
 She is so excited to share her passion for dance and theatre with her students and, most importantly, to help grow their own love and appreciation for the performing arts!
