City Winery Nashville is a premier live music venue offering an intimate setting for unforgettable performances. Enjoy live music nearly every night, from country, Americana, and blues concerts to stand-up comedy shows and live podcast recordings. Over the years, the venue has welcomed legendary artists including Keb' Mo', Rodney Crowell, Shawn Colvin, Emmylou Harris, Lyle Lovett, Allison Moorer, and many more to its stage. As a leading Nashville concert venue, we pair world-class live entertainment with dinner service and award-winning wines for a true dinner and show experience. Browse our Nashville concert calendar to discover upcoming concerts, comedy events, and special performances throughout the month. Whether planning a date night or searching for live music or concerts in Nashville, City Winery Nashville delivers exceptional performances in a seated venue in the Wedgewood-Houston neighborhood near Downtown Nashville.
 Plan Your Visit
Directions, Parking & Dining at City Winery Nashville
City Winery Nashville is located at 609 Lafayette Street, Nashville TN in Nashville's Wedgewood-Houston neighborhood, one of the city's most exciting and fast-growing arts and entertainment districts. Whether you're coming for live music in Nashville, a Nashville comedy show, or a dinner and a show in Nashville, getting here is easy.
Parking: Street parking is available in the surrounding Wedgewood-Houston neighborhood Nashville, and there are surface lots nearby. We recommend arriving early to secure a spot, especially on busy Nashville concert nights.
Public Transit: City Winery Nashville is accessible via WeGo Public Transit. The WeGo bus network serves the broader Wedgewood-Houston area, connecting guests from across Nashville and surrounding areas to our Nashville live music venue.
Rideshare: Uber, Lyft, and other rideshare services frequently drop off and pick up guests directly in front of the venue, making it easy to get to City Winery Nashville and depart after a show — particularly recommended on high-traffic evenings near Downtown Nashville.
 FAQ
 Where is City Winery Nashville located?
City Winery Nashville is located at 609 Lafayette Street in Nashville's Wedgewood-Houston neighborhood, just minutes from Downtown Nashville. The venue is easily accessible by rideshare, and street parking is available in the surrounding area.
 What types of concerts and shows happen at City Winery Nashville?
City Winery Nashville hosts a wide variety of live entertainment nearly every night, including country, Americana, blues, rock, and R&B concerts, as well as stand-up comedy shows, live podcast recordings, and special events. Browse our Nashville concert calendar to see what's coming up.
 Can I order food and wine during the show?
Yes! City Winery Nashville offers full dinner service and award-winning wines throughout the performance, making every show a true dinner and a show experience in Nashville. Our menu features seasonal dishes crafted to complement our wine program.
 Is seating reserved at City Winery Nashville concerts?
Yes, City Winery Nashville is a fully seated venue with reserved seating. Every guest has an assigned seat, ensuring a comfortable and intimate concert experience from any spot in the room.
 What time do doors open for shows at City Winery Nashville?
Doors typically open one hour before showtime, giving guests plenty of time to settle in, explore our wine and dinner menu, and enjoy the atmosphere before the performance begins. Check the individual event listing on our Nashville events calendar for exact door times.
 What makes City Winery Nashville different from other Nashville concert venues?
Unlike most Nashville concert venues, City Winery Nashville combines world-class live music with an intimate seated setting, full dinner service, and an award-winning winery — all under one roof. It's not just a show, it's a complete evening out in Nashville.
 What is the concert experience like at City Winery Nashville?
The concert experience at City Winery Nashville is intimate, relaxed, and upscale. Guests enjoy reserved seating, table service with food and wine, and performances from national touring artists and local Nashville talent in a venue that feels personal and connected — far from the noise and crowds of larger Nashville music venues.
 Do shows at City Winery Nashville frequently sell out?
Yes, many shows at City Winery Nashville sell out quickly, especially performances by well-known national artists and popular Nashville acts. We recommend purchasing tickets in advance through our Nashville concert calendar to secure your spot.
 Is City Winery Nashville an all-ages venue?
City Winery Nashville is primarily a 21+ venue. However, select shows may be open to guests under 21 when accompanied by a parent or guardian. We recommend checking the specific event listing on our Nashville events calendar for age requirements before purchasing tickets.
 Does City Winery Nashville have private event or buyout options?
Yes, City Winery Nashville offers private event and venue buyout options for corporate events, private concerts, celebrations, and more. Our Wedgewood-Houston location provides a stunning and unique setting for private events in Nashville. Contact us to learn more about private event packages.

City Winery Nashville has redefined what a Nashville brunch can be — and if you've never spent a Sunday morning in SoBro with a glass of award-winning wine in hand and live music filling the room, you're missing one of Music City's best kept secrets. Our Nashville brunch events bring together everything that makes this city great — incredible live performances, a chef-driven menu, and a convivial atmosphere that makes every weekend feel like a celebration. Whether you're brunching with friends, planning a special occasion, or just looking for a Nashville Sunday experience that goes well beyond the ordinary eggs and mimosas, City Winery delivers a setting and a vibe that no other brunch spot in Nashville can match.
