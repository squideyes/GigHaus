VENUE 
What is MilkBoy Philly? 
MilkBoy Philly is a restaurant, bar, cafe and live-music venue all in one, located in Center City Philadelphia at 11th and Chestnut Sts. — the brainchild of music industry’s Tommy Joyner and Jamie Lokoff of MilkBoy Recording Studio.
MilkBoy Philly offers an upstairs music venue that hosts an impressive lineup of local and nationally recognized musicians, two full bars and a stepped up food and beverage program. MilkBoy Philly does triple time with early AM crowds opening weekdays at 7 a.m. offering the full drink and food menu thanks to neighboring hospitals, as well as a happy hour following and the late night scene.
MilkBoy Philly’s food menu reflects a dedication to quality product and a devotion to the Philadelphia dining and bar scene with options for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and every snack in between. General Manager Anna Reed and her team of some of Philly’s most skilled bartenders offer an array of creative signature cocktails & classics. Ever-rotating craft beer on tap, fun drink specials, your favorite canned brews, AND home of the famous MilkBoy Espresso Martini.

WHAT ABOUT
MILKBOY THE STUDIO? 
The predecessor and flagship MilkBoy the Studio was originally founded 30 years ago and is now internationally recognized as a premier recording studio for musical artists ranging from Erykah Badu to Lil Uzi Vert, Miley Cyrus to The Roots. Every live show, every late night drink, every dish from the kitchen at MilkBoy Philadelphia– it is all inspired by MilkBoy’s roots in music and the thriving music scene that got it all started.

FAQ 
 Do I have to be 21 to see a show at MilkBoy?
MilkBoy Philly is a 21+ bar, restaurant & venue (with valid ID!). Anyone under the age of 21 is permitted only if they are accompanied by a parent or legal guardian for the duration of their stay at MilkBoy. Parents must remain with their children at all times & underage persons are not permitted near either of our bar areas. 
 Are refunds or exchanges available if I can’t make the show I bought tickets for?
Ticket purchases are non-refundable. Ticket insurance is available at checkout. 
 Is your venue ADA accessible and is seating available?
MilkBoy is not a seated venue, and the venue is located on the 2nd floor. There is no elevator access to the 2nd floor, and concertgoers must be able to walk up a flight of stairs to access MilkBoy shows. Chairs are available upon request. Please feel free to contact us at milkboytickets@gmail.com if you’d like more information and we will try to be as accommodating as possible. 
 Can I go to MilkBoy without buying show tickets/paying a cover?
Of course! There is almost never a cover to hang out downstairs. 
 Where can I park?
There are parking lots and a garage nearby on Sansom street, or metered street parking. 
 Can I take pictures at your venue?
Cameras without detachable lenses are permitted- so take ‘em, post ‘em, and tag us @milkboyphilly! Those who wish to take professional photos must receive permission from the band/artist or venue. 
 Can I host a private party at MilkBoy?
Yes! Email us at milkboy.events@gmail.com or call us at (215)925-6455. Our upstairs venue space is beautiful, totally private, has a large bar & full PA system! Our rates are low and our hospitality is generous and fun. We will give you the complete run-down in person, you’ll see that hosting your event at MilkBoy will give your guests something to talk about all year long. 
 I’m in a band. How can I get booked at MilkBoy?
Most of our booking is done by Rising Sun Presents; send your information to booking@milkboyphilly.com and we’ll check you out! 
 How do I get a job at MilkBoy?
We do this the old fashioned way. If you’d like to join our family we prefer if you bring your resume in person and ask one of our staff if we’re hiring and they’ll point you in the right direction. 
 What kind of food do you serve?
The meat and produce used in our kitchens are largely sourced from local outlets, and much of it is organic. We’ve got a variety of menu options to satisfy everyone, including many vegetarian items. View our menu here. 
 Where can I find information on upcoming shows?
You can checkout our concert calendar here. You can also sign-up for our newsletter and follow us on social media for the latest updates on upcoming shows! 
 How do I buy tickets?
You can checkout our concert calendar here. You can also sign-up for our newsletter and follow us on social media for the latest updates on upcoming shows! 
 I just ordered tickets online. Now what?
Our box office located upstairs will scan or lookup your ticket through our ticketing platform to verify your purchase. 
 Wait, I thought MilkBoy was a recording studio?
MilkBoy started as a recording studio in north Philly in 1994. Since then we’ve opened two coffee shops and our downtown bar, restaurant and music-venue. This location includes our 200 capacity upstairs venue, and downstairs bar/restaurant offering Philly faves such as the MilkBoy Burger and Espresso Martini. The recording studio is located in Philadelphia, PA, at 413 N 7th Street.

Book A Private Event 
Looking for a fun place to host your party, happy hour, private show, or get together? We offer room rentals, food and beverage packages, a state of the art sound system, and have a great, accommodating staff. For more information, please contact us at events@milkboyphilly.com or call us at (215) 925-6455. We will work with you to offer a comprehensive package and make your event something that your guests talk about all year.
