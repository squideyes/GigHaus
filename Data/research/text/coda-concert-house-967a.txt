Nothing will prepare you for what awaits as you step through the nondescript front door in the heart of America - Joplin, Missouri. Theater lights warm a stage that hosts world-class talent that NEVER plays in this type of setting - a BEYOND up-close & personal listening room!
If you are a music lover, we would be honored to have you join us. For obvious reasons, be sure to act fast when a concert is announced. 
Please send us an email to: info@codaconcerthouse.com, for an invitation, booking inquiries, or more details about an event. 
The Coda Concert House... where songs are listened to with the heart.
