Welcome to Studio 84 House Concerts 
Get ready for an unforgettable musical experience right in the heart of Middleboro, Massachusetts. At Studio 84 House Concerts, where we bring talented artists to our cozy venue for intimate performances that will leave you talking about what the hell just happened. 
It all started on 2/22/14, with the late great Greg Trooper. 
Some previous artists to play our space: 
Greg Tropper, Sarah Borges, Ward Hayden and the Outliers, Amy Black, Chris Trapper, Kevin Gordon, Matt Nakoa, Heather Aubrey Lloyd, Jay Psaros, Grain Thief, Say Darling, Irish Mythen, Peter Mulvey, Tim Gearan, Daniel Champagne, Robinson Treacher, Liz Longley, Ian Flannigan, Abby Vail, Brian Dunne, Danielle Miraglia, Matt Borrello, Sparrow Blue, The Dredsox, Eric Schwartz, Hayley Reardon, Seth Glier, Lisa Bastoni, Monica Rizzio, CA McNeill, Sal Baglio and Allen Estes, Mike Montali, Grace Morrison and Rachel Laven, Vance Gilbert, Mark Erelli, and more..... 
While we're not the only house concert around, we love what we do and are always looking for new folks to join us. 
Indoor shows have a limited seating of 45 people, and our shows are BYOB. 
All money, suggested donations, go to the artists, as we're not a business. 100% goes to the artists we book.
