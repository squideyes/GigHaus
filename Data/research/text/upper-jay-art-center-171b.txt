The Upper Jay Art Center (UJAC) was founded by Scott and Byron Renderer in 2005 as a
place to enjoy music, theater, and visual art. 
​
In 2008, it incorporated as a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization and, with a wink and a nod the brothers, upholsterers by trade, named the performance space the Recovery Lounge. Over the past decade, the Lounge has become an essential gathering place in the Ausable River Valley, bringing an eclectic array of professional musicians to this rural corner of the northeast Adirondacks throughout the year and staging plays by classic and contemporary playwrights, acted by an ever expanding troupe of local talent. 
UJAC also hosts play and poetry readings, performance art, dance, and art exhibits. All events are affordable and many are free. Guests can expect a relaxed environment with warm acoustics, comfortable vintage furniture, and a diverse audience of all ages.
In 2022, UJAC’s founding Artistic Director, Scott Renderer, stepped down, passing the baton to a new generation of artistic leadership. Gabrielle Schutz, an interdisciplinary theater artist, is UJAC’s new Artistic Director, with plans to offer a fresh perspective while maintaining the legacy of the Upper Jay Art Center’s Recovery Lounge. 
​
UJAC is located in a former Ford assembly plant and showroom on Route 9N at the heart of Upper Jay. Built in 1920, the building was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2013.

Artist Opportunities
Did you know about these exciting ways for artists to engage 
with Upper Jay Art Center and Recovery Lounge?
Our ongoing new work series focuses on bringing new plays to life by engaging playwrights in collaboration with a local cast and director/dramaturg to hear their work rehearsed and read aloud for our amazing North Country audience. The goal of the series is to introduce our community to exciting emerging and mid-career artists whose work they might otherwise not discover, and offer playwrights space and resources to further the development of a work-in-progress.
​
Email info@upperjayartcenter.org to submit a script for consideration or inquire about the program.
PLAY READINGS
CASTING AND PRODUCTION
Casting - We produce several theatre productions and play readings a year, and we're always seeking to work with new local and guest performers. If you're interested in working with us on a future production, feel free to reach out and share your interest and experience with us at info@upperjayartcenter.org. 
​
Production - We're always looking for helping hands backstage for our ambitious productions (with sound, lighting, etc.). Have a technical theatre skill set to share with us? Don't have experience but open to mentorship on new skills? Email info@upperjayartcenter.org.
Collab Lab is a gathering of local artists (of many different disciplines but primarily related to live performance) who are interested in creating community while developing new work. Each meeting is treated as a rotating workshop of sorts, taking turns helping one another test material and receive supportive feedback/collaboration. We plan as needed to support whoever has an idea that needs eyes/ears/hands to move forward. Meet new potential collaborators! Say that crazy idea out loud and get feedback! Eat snacks and talk about art making! 
If you're interested, please reach out to info@upperjayartcenter.org to inquire about meeting times/details. 
COLLAB LAB
CO-PRESENTATIONS
While most of our theatre and interdisciplinary productions are developed and produced in-house, we're open to receiving submissions from guest artists/companies to explore possible co-presentations of in-development, completed, and touring productions. Email info@upperjayartcenter.org and share your work with us!

Thank you for your interest in the Recovery Lounge. Since its founding in 2005, the Upper Jay Art Center, a non-profit organization (EIN 56-2665332), has been embraced by a generous community of donors committed to supporting our efforts to present a diverse selection of musical acts and performance art and to produce quality theater. 
Please consider joining this group by making a gift to the Upper Jay Art Center. Whatever you can give will be greatly appreciated. Our work is only possible because of donors like you. Thank you!

Need Advice on Giving?
Amounts: Donations of all amounts are meaningful to us and help us fulfill our mission, but the categories above are some common giving amounts and some ideas on how they push us forward in serving our community.
​
Frequency: We have a schedule of two fundraising appeals per year (one in the Spring and one at the end of the year), but accept and appreciate donations whenever. Sustained, consistent, support with annual giving is really what allows us to think broadly, and create ambitious programming.
