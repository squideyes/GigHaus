Who We Are
Five Points Music is a nonprofit organization dedicated to exploring the power of sound in all its glory. We produce engaging concerts, workshops, and events that not only celebrate diverse musical talents but also serve as a platform to develop and promote our core community programs.
Our Mission & Vision
Our aim is simple yet profound: we want audiences of every ability to enjoy the magic of live music without barriers. By combining live events, outreach initiatives, and strategic partnerships, we’re able to support three key areas:
Hearing Loss Advocacy
We work to create a more sound-friendly world by managing safe decibel levels, offering free hearing protection for all age groups, and providing hearing screenings at festivals, concerts, and community gatherings.
Accessibility Initiatives
Our goal is to expand ADA access and inclusivity—both at our own shows and in partnership with other venues, organizers, and artists. When it comes to live music, we believe no one should be left on the sidelines.
Music Therapy
We fund and provide access to free music therapy sessions for clients of all ages and various special needs. By showcasing the incredible benefits of music therapy, our events help educate the public and break down financial barriers for families who need these services most.
Why It Matters
Music can heal, connect, and inspire. By ensuring that everyone—especially those with hearing challenges or special needs—can participate fully, we’re creating stronger, more vibrant communities. Public art plays a central role in how we reach our audiences, reminding us that sound isn’t just heard; it’s felt.
Join Us
Whether you attend one of our concerts, volunteer, donate, or partner with us on an event, you become part of our mission to bring the joy of music to everyone. Together, we can make sure that the power of sound resonates throughout our communities, touching lives and transforming futures—one note at a time.
WE ARE HAVING A UNIVERSAL IMPACT ON OUR WORLD AND WE YOU TO MAKE IT POSSIBLE. HELP SAVE THE SANCTUARY.

Donate Cryptocurrency to 5 Points Music Sanctuary
Making a cryptocurrency donation to support 5 Points Music Sanctuary enables us to continue our mission to celebrate the magic of sound in all its glory. Through our vibrant live events and concerts, we bring together audiences from near and far, uniting them in the sweet embrace of music. But our purpose goes beyond the stage. We’re committed to making a difference in the world through music therapy, hearing loss advocacy, and enhancing accessibility. Our journey began in January 2017, when we set out with a simple but profound mission: “To pursue the power of sound in all its glory.”
Our story is deeply rooted in a remarkable journey of hope and inspiration. Our Founder and Director, Tyler Godsey, experienced the transformative gift of sound through his two children, born deaf. At the tender age of 11 months, they received the incredible gift of cochlear implants, allowing them to discover the beauty of music and sound. This gift serves as the heart and soul of 5 Points Music, driving everything we do.
Support 5 Points Music Sanctuary by making a charitable gift in cryptocurrency. We accept donations in Bitcoin, Ethereum, and over 100 additional leading cryptocurrencies. 
How can I donate cryptocurrency to 5 Points Music Sanctuary?
Donating crypto is safe, easy, and fast. Using the donation form on this page, making a charitable gift is a quick three-step process:
Select your preferred crypto and enter your gift amount
Type in your email and basic info (or choose to give anonymously)
Make your gift! 
Upon making a donation, you can elect to receive an automatically generated tax receipt that will be sent to the email address you provide after a certain amount of confirmations of the transaction on the blockchain. This confirmation of your donation will help you close the loop when calculating your taxes.
Crypto charitable donations are processed quickly and safely with The Giving Block and Gemini Trust Exchange. 
Why does 5 Points Music Sanctuary accept cryptocurrency?
Crypto Philanthropy is a new and thrilling fundraising avenue for nonprofit organizations like 5 Points Music Sanctuary. With more than 300 million crypto users worldwide and the value of many cryptocurrencies rising, the crypto community is in a position to make a wide-reaching impact through philanthropy. Donating crypto is also one of the most tax-efficient ways for individuals to support causes that matter to them. We are excited about what the future holds in the crypto and blockchain space, and believe that we’ve just scratched the surface of its true fundraising potential.
