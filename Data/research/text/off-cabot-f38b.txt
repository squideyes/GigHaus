Off Cabot
Welcome to Off Cabot 
 Comedy, music, and more in an intimate performance space.
Off Cabot at 9 Wallis Street is an intimate performance venue located in Beverly, MA, just a few blocks from The Cabot. The Cabot, in partnership with John Tobin Presents, opened this lounge in 2022 as a complement to its 850 seat venue. The venue has a capacity of 125 seated and 150 standing guests, showcasing an eclectic mix of live entertainment. A hotspot for comedians on the North Shore and the Greater Boston region, you can also catch regional and national musicians, burlesque shows, magic performances, drag, live podcasts, storytelling events, and much more.
