 School of Music 
Carry on the tradition and make music a companion for life!
Sign Up Now
Jam Sessions
Meet our Instructors
If you believe that music, poetry and storytelling are essential forms of human expression that must be taught and passed forward to the next generation, you’re in the right place. Rooted in folk tradition we learn and play in small, friendly groups, master a repertoire of enduring American roots songs, and write some new classics too. With a focus on learning to play by ear rather than reading sheets of music, we hope playing music will become your go to social pass time because when you learn to play an instrument, you have a companion for life.
“The School of Music at Caffe Lena is filled with the joy of people learning to play music together, as a social experience. My kids are learning songs written about the richness of everyday life; and their histories provide wonderful educational content. Caffe Lena has become a foundational piece of our homeschooling week.”
– C. Castle – parent & student
The School of Music is generously supported by
Stewart’s Shops and the Dake Family Fund
of The Community Foundation for the Greater Capital Region
Youth Classes
Caffè Lena is a great place to learn, play, make friends and have fun–all through music! Whether it be our After School Folk Ensemble, Folk Club Kids, Music with Miss Deb, or Summer Camps, children from 18 months to teens can find a home in a music community rooted in the Folk Tradition.
With our extensive instrument library and available scholarships, every child has the opportunity to learn and grow in a supportive and inclusive environment under the guidance of teachers who are experienced educators and active, working American roots musicians.
Browse Classes
ADULT CLASSES
Looking to learn a new instrument or sharpen your skills? Caffè Lena School of Music offers a variety of group and ensemble classes for all levels! Whether you’re interested in guitar, mandolin, upright bass, banjo, we’ve got you covered. Explore our ensemble classes in Irish Music, Bluegrass, and more, and connect with fellow musicians in a relaxed and welcoming environment.
Browse Classes
ONLINE CLASSES
Some of the American roots world’s best teachers, musicians from around the world have been coming together to teach banjo, fiddle, guitar, songwriting, percussive dance, voice and more! Each session consists of four weekly classes on ZOOM. These archives are now available for purchase separately. New classes and teachers are being added all the time!
Browse Archive
ARCHIVE LIBRARY
Musicians from around the world have been coming together to learn banjo, fiddle, guitar, songwriting, percussive dance, and voice online with some of the folk world’s most beloved teachers. Each session consists of four weekly classes on ZOOM. Tech support provided for ease of joining the class. Each class is recorded and archived in a library that is yours to revisit “forever and a day”. The archives are also available for purchase separately. New classes and teachers are being added all the time.

The Joys of Jamming – Stephen C. Butler
I was very lucky to grow up in a time when people sang together in groups, with or without musical instruments. I believe it wasn’t until the mid-1980s when lightweight stereo headsets started covering both our ears that popular music became an individual pursuit. Our Folk Ensemble Classes and Camps are an exciting and unique way of teaching real world music skills.
From the first session, students experience the power of making music together and hear how interdependent each note and strum is as they learn to play.
Explore Folk music string instruments:
Try Violin/Fiddle, Guitar, Ukulele, Banjo, Mandolin, and Bass
Learn music fundamentals:
Develop strong and steady sense of rhythm
Learn how to listen and hear musically
Explore singing with your own authentic voice
Learning valuable life skills:
Working in a group setting
Listening
Creative contribution and compromise
Being part of a team and playing your part
Building friendships
Full scholarships, including instruments, are available for anyone in need.
