Our Mission
The mission of The Muse Performance Space is to share uplifting experiences through authentic music and visual arts in a welcoming and beautiful environment. We offer a warm and intimate space to connect artists with audiences and mentors with students.
The Muse is a 501(c)3 nonprofit corporation, so all donations are tax-deductible.

Perform at the Muse
Performances at the Muse are an enriching experience for artists and guests alike! Created and run by professional musicians, we want the performing artist to have the best possible experience with the fewest hassles. To that end, we have invested in a beautiful 6 ft. Yamaha grand piano, Mark bass amp, Fender Blues tube amp, Catalina Club house drum kit equipped with Aquarian Modern Vintage heads, Soundcraft Expressions Si 1 mixing board, JBL mains, Yamaha monitors, a variety of professional microphones, and more. Livestreams and recording are available options
 For booking contact: Museperformancebooking@gmail.com
 For rentals contact: museperformancerentals@gmail.com
