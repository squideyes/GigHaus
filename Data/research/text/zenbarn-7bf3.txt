About: Zenbarn
A Stage Artist's Seek Out
Founded in 2016 by Noah, Ari, and Marlena Fishman, Zenbarn was created to bring neighbors together. From Guptil Farm dairy barn, to Golden Horn East, to Tanglewoods Restaurant & Bar, this building has long served the community.
What began as a local experiment has grown into a respected stop on national touring routes
Known for high-quality sound, exceptional hospitality, and a crowd that genuinely listens and appreciates music, Zenbarn offers something increasingly rare: an intimate space where connection matters.
Our stage has welcomed legendary artists across generations and genres — from folk icons Peter Rowan and Ramblin’ Jack Elliott, to members of Wu-Tang Clan, to reggae legends like Black Uhuru.
Members of Phish, Twiddle, and other iconic Vermont bands frequent the venue and sit in regularly, creating spontaneous moments that only happen in rooms built on musical trust and community.
Zenbarn carries that legacy forward — now as one of the most dynamic small live music venues in the country and a premier Vermont barn wedding destination.
The “Zen” in Zenbarn reflects our belief in music, mindfulness, and meaningful connection.
A Family of Vermont Businesses
The Fishmans are community-based entrepreneurs at heart with a group of interrelated Vermont businesses.
In addition to Zenbarn, the family operates:
Vermont Patients Alliance — a vertically integrated cannabis company
Zenbarn Farms — a retail dispensary in Waterbury
Stowe’s Remarkable Things (formerly Stowe Craft Gallery), owned by Susan and Stephen Fishman in the heart of Stowe
Across all ventures, a common thread runs strong:
Quality. Community connection. Sustainability. Vermont pride.

Give your team a holiday party they will actually look forward to. Zenbarn turns a restored Vermont barn into a warm, candlelit celebration with a farm-to-table menu, a full bar of Vermont craft beer and cocktails, and the option of a live band on a real concert stage.

Keep the team together overnight

Event Planning FAQ
How many guests can Zenbarn host?
Up to 200 seated guests, with tables and seating set up to fit your event.
Do you provide catering?
Yes. All food and drink is handled by our in-house team, with custom farm-to-table menus and a full bar. Outside food and beverages are not permitted.
Can we have live music or a DJ?
Yes. Zenbarn has a professional stage and sound system, and we can book a band for you or work with your own DJ or performers.
Do you have AV for presentations?
Yes. Zenbarn offers full AV for presentations, speeches and performances, backed by the same professional sound and lighting we use for concerts.
Is there lodging on site?
Yes. The loft above the venue has two private suites that sleep up to eight. Nearby options include the Fairfield Inn & Suites Waterbury-Stowe, Best Western Plus Waterbury-Stowe and the Stagecoach Inn.
How far in advance should we book?
As early as you can, especially for December holiday parties and summer weekends, which fill up first.
Do you host events year-round?
Yes. Events take place inside the restored barn year-round, and the field with views of Mount Hunger is available for outdoor events in the warmer months.
How far is Zenbarn from Stowe and Burlington?
Zenbarn is about 15 minutes from Stowe, 20 minutes from Montpelier and 35 minutes from Burlington, just off I-89 at Exit 10.
Is Zenbarn accessible?
Yes. Zenbarn is wheelchair accessible. Contact us about any special accommodations.
Is parking available?
Yes. Free on-site parking, with attendants for larger events.
How do we book an event?
Start your inquiry using the button on this page or call (802) 244-8134. Our events team will follow up with menu options, layout ideas and pricing.

10 years. Thousands of gatherings. One incredible community.
Ten years ago, we started Zenbarn with a simple idea: bring our neighbors together in an old dairy barn next to where Ari and Noah grew up.
What has transpired since has exceeded anything we could have imagined.
Together, we’ve built a community around music, food, drink, wellness, celebration, connection, and belonging — one that has deeply enriched our lives and, we hope, the lives of the people around us.
Over the past decade, Zenbarn has hosted thousands of concerts, weddings, mitzvahs, birthday parties, memorials, meetings, fundraisers, celebrations, and late nights on the dance floor.
People have fallen in love here — and then gotten married here.
We’ve celebrated milestones together. We’ve experienced heartbreak together. We’ve endured tragedies, weathered hard times, and somehow kept bouncing back stronger.
It hasn’t always been easy. It was never meant to be.
Zenbarn exists because of the people who have walked through these doors, played on this stage, worked behind the bar, danced until closing time, celebrated their families here, supported our crazy ideas, and helped turn an old Vermont dairy barn into something much bigger than a building.
So what else is left to do but throw one hell of a party?
Join us as we celebrate 10 YEARS OF ZENBARN — our biggest party of the year and a celebration of this incredible community and everything we have built together.
WHAT TO EXPECT
DJs. Live music. Art. Food & drink specials. Giveaways. Surprises. Old friends. New friends. Dancing. Celebrating.
Basically, everything we love about Zenbarn packed into one big night.
What more could you ask for?
Come early. Stay late. Bring your people.

VIP EXPERIENCE
Want to celebrate in style?
Our special 10-Year Anniversary VIP Ticket includes access to an exclusive VIP lounge with heavy appetizers served throughout the night, plus a special place to hang and celebrate this milestone together.
We can’t wait to raise a glass, hit the dance floor, tell some stories, make some new ones, and celebrate the past 10 years with all of you.
Thank you for building this with us.
Here’s to the next 10!

Matt Lorenz, The Suitcase Junket, brings his one-man-band blues, folk, and stomp to Zenbarn for the record release of To Go On. Armed with a guitar, banjo, homemade percussion, and a battered suitcase kick drum, he turns a solo show into a full-on roots-music party. Don’t miss it! $15 advance / $20 day of show. All ages.
