UP CLOSE & PERSONAL
Watching a live performance at Alvas Showroom is always a unique experience due to its great atmosphere and intimate staging. Set up like a small amphitheater, the Showroom only seats about 99 people. Being so close to the artists, the viewer literally feels like a participant in the live performance.
At Alvas Showroom guests instantly feel as if they’re partying with friends and are usually joined by the performers as well. Bring your own food and drink, no alcohol! Coffee, tea, and purified water is provided. There is also plenty of free parking!
Alvas Showroom has an excellent sound system and great lighting, contributing to a memorable live performing arts experience.
Seeing the artists so up close and in such a relaxed and intimate venue gives guests the opportunity not only to hear and see the performers but also to get to know them.
Many artists have said that Alvas Showroom is their favorite place to perform in the LA area.

 FAQJairo Camacho2026-04-16T11:26:51-07:00
At Alvas Showroom, we give a F.A.Q. about your questions.
Here are the most common ones:
Q: Is there assigned seating?
A: Most seats are General Admission and are first come, first serve. We do have table seating that can be reserved (only after purchasing your admission) by submitting a reserved seating request. Check it out!
Q: Is there wheelchair access?
A: We have 2 wheelchair accessible spaces as well as wheelchair accessible bathrooms. Wheelchair accessible spaces are also first come, first served, so please send an email to booking@alvas.com with your request to confirm availability for special wheelchair spaces.
Q: Do you need to provide a printed email confirmation for entry?
A: No physical printout necessary. Purchaser’s name will be on a list at the door. Simply provide the purchaser’s name at the door for entry.
Q: Can I buy a admission for other people?
A: Yes. The other people will simply provide the purchaser’s name at the door for entry.
Q: Can admission be purchased at the venue and is there a service fee?
A: Advanced admission purchases can only be done on our website. In-person admission purchases can only be done at the front desk on the night of the show IF seats are still available. There is no service fee for in-person admission purchases. We always recommend purchasing admission in advance on our website to guarantee admission. No refunds or exchanges.
Q: When do doors open and shows start/stop?
A: Because we allow the artist to decide that, we cannot give a definite answer. Generally, doors open approximately 30 minutes before showtime, and shows are approximately 1 to 2 hours long.
Q: Is there alcohol?
A: NO ALCOHOL is allowed or sold at our venue.
Q: Are there drinks and food sold?
A: We offer complimentary coffee, tea, and hot cocoa. We also sell bottled water for $1 with free refills from our filtered water dispenser. NO ALCOHOL is allowed or sold at our venue.
Q: Are you allowed to bring your own food and drink?
A: No food is sold, but you are allowed to bring your own food and non-alcoholic beverages. NO ALCOHOL is allowed in our venue. Please be prepared to show the staff your food and drink at the door. You will be asked to dispose of any alcoholic beverages if you attempt to bring them inside.
Q: Are there tables to eat food that is brought?
A: Yes, we have tables for nearly half the seating at our venue. Tables are given to first come, first served.
Q: Is there parking available?
A: There is plenty of free parking. Please observe the 2 HOUR LIMIT parking signs. Unlimited parking is allowed after 6:00pm.
Q: Is audio or video recording allowed?
A: Audio or video recording is NOT allowed. Please refrain from using flash photography.
Q: Is there a dress code?
A: Yes, you must be legally dressed. It’s a Penal Code.
Q: Is the venue all ages?
A: We are catering to all ages. Please refrain from using obscenities or lewdness.
Q: Is the venue air conditioned?
A: Yes, the Showroom is air conditioned.
Q: How many people does the venue seat?
A: The venue seats 99 people.

SHOWTIMES
Showroom performances are available 7 days a week. Showtime is normally 8 PM and Sunday showtime is normally 4 PM. The timeframe for our gigs is 6 hours. 3 hours for load in, setup, and sound check, 2 hours for performance, 1 hour break down, load out. Extra hours are available and need to be booked ahead of time at $50 an hour. These extra hours can be used for almost anything including but not limited to rehearsal, sound check, setup, social meet and greet, etc.
GUEST LIST
We need to know the number of guests in advance of public ticket sales . We will set the number of tickets available online based on your guest totals. All heads are counted. The guest list names will be accepted from the Artist only. The list can be updated at anytime as long as seats are available.
PAYMENT POLICY IN REGARDS TO GUEST LIST
If the Artist has guests, Alvas deducts 10% of the ticket price per guest from the payment to the Artist. If Alvas has guests, Alvas adds 90% of the ticket price per guest to the Artist.
ALL AGES VENUE
As we are catering to all ages, please refrain from using obscenities or lewdness. There is no alcohol sold or allowed on the premises.
LOAD IN AND PARKING
Please load in through the front door. There is plenty of free parking, please observe the 2 HOUR LIMIT parking signs. Unlimited parking is allowed after 6:00pm.
SOUND
Alvas LLC uses our own sound engineer. Any other arrangement must be approved in writing by Alvas LLC.
BACKLINE
Please consider using our extensive backline. It is available for approximately 1/2 the price of local backline companies and is setup on the stage per your stage plot. If you are interested, let us know, and we will check availability and give you a quote. For special request, please email us at booking@alvas.com or call (310) 833-3281.
View our current backline gear and pricing here.
PIANO USAGE
For a $20 setup fee (per piano), you have available to you 2 Steinway pianos. A 1921 America B and a 1985 Hamburg C. Let us know if you will be using a piano and which one you prefer. A piano tuning before a performance has a fee of $75 per piano tuned.

PERMIT FOR ARTIST TO RECORD THEMSELVES
Unauthorized audio and video recording is strictly prohibited. If the Artist would like to record, pre-approval must be obtained in writing and the Artist must hire the recording service from a third party vendor.
Video recording:
– $150 -1 camera | $250 -2 cameras | $300 -3 cameras
Audio only recording:
– $100
(includes audio feed from our sound board)
DIGITAL PROJECTOR USAGE – $30 per show.
We do not provide AV cables or adapters. Please come in and test your operating systems, AV connectors and adapters BEFORE the date of your show. Call Art Valdez, our Sound Engineer, at (310) 833-3281 and schedule an appointment.
HOSPITALITY
We have a green room upstairs for the artists. You may bring food and non-alcoholic drinks into the green room. Complimentary bottled water, coffee and tea are available upon request. We do not provide lodging at this time.
TICKETS
All tickets are sold through Alvas LLC, which accepts credit cards online, https://alvasshowroom.com or walk-up sales, both cash and credit card.
PAYMENT EXAMPLE
First $300 of ticket sales goes to the house. Remaining balance of ticket sales is split 90/10 to the artist/house. In the event that ticket sales do not cover the first $300, you may be asked to pay the difference. For example:
$3,000 – Ticket Sales
$300 – House
—————–-
$2700 – Ticket sales to split
The Artist gets 90% of $2700 = $2430
Alvas LLC gets 10% of $2700 = $270
If you decide to use the projector, have the piano tuned, video recorded, use backline, etc. there will be additional charges. You may be required to pay these prior to the show.
PAYMENT (US Citizens)
All monies owed to the performer will be mailed via check to the name and address on your Form W-9 (MUST have the current date next to the signature). A copy of the Payment Record will be emailed to you within 72 hours of your performance informing you of the check amount. USPS is expected to deliver your check within 5-14 business days after it is mailed. For any other payment arrangements, delays and fees may apply.
PAYMENT (International Artists)
All monies owed to the performer will be sent via wire transfer to the name and address on your Form W8-BEN (MUST have the current date next to the signature). A copy of the Payment Record will be emailed to you within 72 hours of your performance informing you of the wire transfer amount. Our office will contact you for the required wire transfer information. For any other payment arrangements, delays and fees may apply.
PERFORMANCE CANCELLATION POLICY
If Alvas Showroom cancels, the Artist will receive 2.9% of total tickets sold, 30 cents per ticket, and $100 administrative cost.
If the Artist cancels, Alvas Showroom will receive 2.9% of total tickets sold, 30 cents per ticket, and $100 administrative cost.

Encore Saxophone Quartet returns to Alva’s Showroom for a highly anticipated encore performance, bringing audiences another opportunity to experience one of Los Angeles’ premier award-winning ensembles.
Internationally acclaimed artistry
Celebrated around the world as “masters of the saxophone,” the quartet captivates listeners with dazzling virtuosity, refined musicality, and “some of the most delicate sounds imaginable.”
An unforgettable musical journey
From the timeless elegance of Hollywood legends and the Great American Songbook to vibrant modern jazz works composed especially for them, Encore Saxophone Quartet offers a program rich in color, style, and imagination. Their innovative repertoire reveals the full expressive power of the saxophone quartet and promises an engaging concert experience for every listener.
Performer Names/Instruments:
Doug Masek – Soprano Saxophone
Ken Foerch – Alto Saxophone
David Brennan – Tenor Saxophone
Damon Zick – Baritone Saxophone
