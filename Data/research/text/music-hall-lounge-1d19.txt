A Gathering Space for Everyone
The Music Hall is a community-supported nonprofit organization that is committed to presenting the very best in arts and cultural events.
At The Lounge
With comedy, literary, music, and so much more, there’s something for everyone on our smaller, intimate stage!
 View Series
Cinema
The Music Hall has been screening movies for more than 120 years. From the major studios to the independents, to the unique Telluride by the Sea every September and the Academy Award Qualifying festival, New Hampshire Film Festival every October, The Music Hall delivers the very best in Cinema.
 View Series
Clubs
Do you love movies, opera, books, or exploring the latest hot new spot in town? We’ve got you covered. Our clubs and talks series dive deeper into your interests with expert hosts and discussion leaders.
 View Series
Comedy
We all love to laugh! Come see giggle-inducing acts from seasoned pros from Comedy Central and up-and-coming fresh faces from YouTube. Chuckle at stand-up and improv in both of our venues.
 View Series
Literary
We love books. We love authors. See literary greats and up-and-coming authors, and hear about their latest works, craft, and writing process. Insightful Q&As explore the intricacies of writing and publishing.
 View Series
LiveStream
Live performances in the comfort of your home! These virtual events feature talented artists from around the world.
 View Series
Member Events
Exclusive events programmed just for our Music Hall Members. From our clubs, wine tastings, expert talks with staff, and special gratitude events to show our appreciation, our member events celebrate all of our biggest supporters.
 View Series
Music
Music is in our name and in our DNA. We bring you the best concerts, from legends to soon-to-be stars. Whether the show is in our 900-seat Historic Theater or in our intimate, contemporary Lounge with just over 100 seats, you’re sure to have an amazing experience.
 View Series
Special Events
These special events help us do more in the community, from fundraisers that fund our outreach programs, like our Emerging Artist Benefit Concert, to behind-the-scenes tours of our Historic Theater.
 View Series
Talks & Education
Whether it is a child’s first live theater experience or a talk about the future of driverless cars, The Music Hall is committed to what is coming next in education and innovation.
 View Series
Theater & Dance
We were built for vaudeville and we’ve been doing it for over 140 years. From musicals to comedy, The Music Hall delivers exceptional theater.
 View Series
