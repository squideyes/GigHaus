​
Please email bookingatmuddycreek@gmail.com with the following information for a price quote on a venue rental:
1. Date requested for your event.
2. Times for your event, in a 4 hour format (ex. 5pm to 9pm).
3. How many people you expect.
4. Age range of patrons.
5. What is the nature of the event?
6. Any special needs you may need for your event.
​
7. Which location - Sparta or Winston Salem
​
8. Dinner and/or Appetizers?
