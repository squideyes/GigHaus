Soul night
Settle into your Thursday night groove with Scenic City Soul Night, a weekly series at The Woodshop led by Chattanooga’s own Bryant “Bass Hitter” Russell and his band, Bass Hitter & The Prophets. Inspired by ’70s Motown, jazz, and smooth soul, this funky, rotating ensemble features guest vocalists each week and delivers a sensual, cozy soundtrack perfect for unwinding or date night. EVERY FRIDAY 8PM
Forever Bluegrass Fridays
Every Friday night in St. Elmo, the Woodshop Listening Room comes alive with Forever Bluegrass, a Chattanooga tradition curated by Cindy Pinion and the Pinion family. Rooted in the legacy of Boxcar Pinion’s festival and carried forward with heart, Forever Bluegrass gathers some of the region’s finest pickers and singers for a night of driving banjo, soulful fiddle, and harmony-rich storytelling. Different bands each week. Seating is limited, and these nights fill fast — so don’t wait. We are a full time music venue and there can be occasional shows early on Fridays from 6pm - 8pm - separate tickets apply. Consult the calendar below.

 ABOUT
WHAT IS THE WOODSHOP ?
The Woodshop Listening Room is an intimate ticketed concert venue and dinner-and-a-show room in St. Elmo, Chattanooga, operated by Jhett Black and Callie Sioux. With great sound, close-up performances, BBQ, cocktails, and weekly roots programming, The Woodshop is built around a full night out—come for dinner, settle in for the show, and experience live music the way it’s meant to be: up close and personal.
Since opening as a bar in the midst of 2020’s shuttering, we’ve developed into a venue-style neighborhood bar that curates a homelike feeling for anyone who walks inside. Live music is in our DNA, but we’re also known for our cozy seasonal food and craft drinks menu. Between finger-licking BBQ and arguably the best local craft beer and spirits we can find, what we’ve created is a surprisingly wide selection of goods that keeps your heart warm and happy. We promise you’ll remember coming here.Open Tuesday-Thursday, 5-10pm Friday 5-11pm
Saturday 5pm - 10pm
21+ after 9pm
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: WHERE IS THE WOODSHOP LOCATED?
On the southernmost end of Chattanooga, in the historic neighborhood of St Elmo. Address: 5500 St. Elmo Avenue, Chattanooga, TN 37409
Q: WHERE DO I PARK?
There is free street parking available along the streets next to and around our bar. Please note, as a neighborhood bar, we are located among many private homes and neighborhoods, so we ask that our guests be mindful and courteous of residents around us when parking.
Q: DO YOU HAVE GIFT CARDS?
This is in the works!
Q: DO YOU ALLOW KIDS?
We do! Kids and others under 21 are welcome until 9pm. After 9, we transition to a 21+ only policy depending on the concert schedule.
We ask that all kids are accompanied by a parent or guardian at all times and refrain from wandering around, running, rough play, and touching any music and sound equipment.
Q: WHAT KINDS OF DRINKS DO YOU HAVE?
We have a carefully curated variety of beer and wine offerings as well as non-alcoholic options. Additionally, we have a high shelf and cocktail liquor menu.
Q: DO YOU SERVE FOOD?
We currently have family-recipe delicious BBQ! Dry rubbed, hickory smoked and finger-licking good. Food is available every Tuesday - Saturday from 5 -9pm.
Q: CAN I BRING IN OUTSIDE FOOD AND DRINK?
No. Outside food and drink are prohibited. We sustain our business by selling food and drink. Folks are encouraged to support us as we curate the best vibe for fellow patrons and performing artists.
Q: WHAT TIME DOES TUESDAY BLUESDAY START?
Doors open at 5pm, tickets start at $15, and music starts at 6pm! Tuesday Bluesday is hosted by award winning songwriter, bluesman and owner of The Woodshop, Jhett Black. Every Tuesday new touring, national, and local blues and roots artists are featured. This show is also recorded live for WUTC 88.1 monthly blues program. Enjoyment of the show encouraged!
Q: HOW DO I PURCHASE TICKETS FOR FOREVER BLUEGRASS FRIDAYS?
There is a $10 cover at the door, cash is preferred. We start charging a cover at 8pm. Reserved 2- and 4-person tables are available through the ticket page while they last. Parties larger than 8 cannot be guaranteed seating together.
Q: WHAT TIME DOES SOUL NIGHT START?
Doors open at 8pm, tickets start at $15 (or you can reserve a table), music starts at 8:30pm! Scenic City soul night is hosted by Bryant Bass-Hitter with featured singers and performers each week. 
Q: I WANT TO RENT THE WOODSHOP FOR AN EVENT. IS THAT POSSIBLE?
Yes! Our space is perfect for small gatherings of all different types. Please fill out this CONTACT FORM and we will respond as quickly as we can. 
Q: HOW CAN I PURCHASE TICKETS FOR A SHOW?
Shows that have presale tickets can be purchased HERE.
Q: I AM DISABLED, WHAT ACCOMMODATIONS DO YOU PROVIDE?
We truly want everyone and anyone to have a seamless experience at The Woodshop. Our main entrance is ground level, and our building is ADA certified. If you need more accommodation or accessibility, our staff is more than happy to help. 
Q: WHAT IS YOUR REFUND POLICY?
All sales are final. Absolutely no refunds – no exceptions. If a show is cancelled or postponed there will be specific instructions given to receive a refund or credit via the email you used to purchase tickets. 
Q: CAN I BUY TICKETS AT THE SHOW?
If a show has not sold out, tickets can be purchased at the door. Weekly shows have tickets available at the door.
Q: DO I HAVE TO BUY A TICKET TO COME IN?
For our ticketed concerts yes, you do. For our weekly shows (Tuesday Bluesday, Thursday Night Soul, Forever Bluegrass Friday) you are welcome to enjoy a beer or dinner out in the beer garden without paying a cover till 8pm (in the event a second show begins at 8pm - in that case you will have to purchase a ticket). Otherwise, yes, buying a ticket is necessary to enjoy the music we bring in from regional to national and international professional musicians!
Q: WHAT CAN I BRING INTO THE BAR?
We want everyone to have a great time, but there are some things you simply can’t bring inside. For those who need a list, here ya go:
No Outside Food or Beverage
No Illegal Drugs
No GoPros (or any unwarranted professional photo/video equipment)
No Selfie Sticks
No Mace
No Hula Hoops
No Glow Sticks, Glitter, Feathers, or Confetti!!!! 
No Backpacks
No Prejudices, No Bad Attitudes
Q: WHAT ARE YOUR POLICIES REGARDING PHOTO/VIDEO/AUDIO?
Only those with approved photo and video passes can bring professional equipment to a record our entertainment.
Q: HOW CAN I STAY UP-TO-DATE ON THE WOODSHOP LISTENING ROOM’S SHOWS?
Follow us on socials @thewoodshoppresents on Instagram; @thewoodshopchatt on Facebook) to hear about anything new or upcoming.
Q: I THINK I LEFT SOMETHING AT YOUR VENUE. WHAT DO I DO?
Contact us during regular business hours at 423-803-6165 or stop by anytime between 5-10pm to check if we found your items. 
Q: ARE YOU GUYS HIRING?
We are always open to reviewing a resume and getting to know you. Drop us a note on the Contact Page.
