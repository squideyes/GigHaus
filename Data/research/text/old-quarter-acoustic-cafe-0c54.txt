2ND ANNUAL OLD QUARTER SONGWRITER FESTIVAL
The Old Quarter and ARTS Downtown
proudly presents the 2nd Annual
Old Quarter Songwriter Festival!
Nov. 12-14, 2026
Old Quarter Songwriter Festival
Get ready for three unforgettable days of storytelling, soul, and song! From November 12th to 14th, the heart of Downton Galveston will come alive with the voices of songwriters from near and far, sharing original music in its purest form.
The Old Quarter Songwriter Festival celebrates the craft of songwriting in an intimate listening-room setting where lyrics take center stage. Whether you're a longtime fan of the Texas folk tradition or discovering the magic of songwriter showcases for the first time, this weekend promises heartfelt performances, acoustic brilliance, and a gathering of kindred spirits.
Join us as we honor the voices behind the music — raw, real, and rooted in truth. This is more than just a music festival; it’s a tribute to the stories that connect us all.

lineup 
 Few artists have shaped the sound of Texas songwriting like Jimmie Dale Gilmore and Butch Hancock. As founding members of the legendary Flatlanders, alongside Joe Ely, they helped redefine Americana with a blend of West Texas folk, country, blues, and poetic storytelling. Hancock's vividly crafted songs and Gilmore's unmistakable voice have influenced generations of songwriters while earning them enduring respect as two of the most important figures in American roots music. Together, they deliver an unforgettable evening of timeless songs, masterful musicianship, and the stories behind a lifetime of music. 
 Susan Gibson is one of Texas' most celebrated singer-songwriters, known for her heartfelt storytelling, warm stage presence, and beautifully crafted songs. She first gained national recognition after writing "Wide Open Spaces," the hit that launched The Chicks into superstardom, but her own catalog reveals an artist of remarkable depth, wit, and authenticity. Whether delivering poignant ballads or humorous observations on everyday life, Gibson's performances create an intimate connection with audiences, making her a beloved favorite on stages across Texas and beyond. 
 Amy LaVere and Will Sexton are two of Americana music's most compelling artists, blending exceptional songwriting with world-class musicianship. Memphis-based LaVere is celebrated for her captivating voice, evocative storytelling, and distinctive upright bass playing, while Texas native Sexton is an acclaimed guitarist, songwriter, producer, and session musician whose career has spanned more than three decades. Together, their rich harmonies, masterful performances, and undeniable chemistry create an intimate concert experience that moves effortlessly between roots, folk, blues, country, and rock. Their performances are heartfelt, soulful, and unforgettable, showcasing two master craftsmen at the height of their artistry. 
 Mississippi-born and Texas-forged, Jason Eady has earned a reputation as one of Americana's most respected singer-songwriters. Known for his rich baritone voice, masterful storytelling, and honest, roots-driven songwriting, Eady blends country, folk, and blues into songs that are both timeless and deeply personal. With acclaimed albums and years of touring behind him, he's become a favorite among fans who appreciate authentic songwriting and unforgettable live performances. 
 Mississippi-born singer-songwriter Charlie Mars has spent more than two decades crafting soulful, literate songs that blend Americana, folk, rock, and country into a sound all his own. Known for his warm, expressive voice and deeply personal songwriting, Mars has earned critical acclaim with albums including Like a Bird, Like a Plane, Blackberry Light, and Money. Whether performing on festival stages or in intimate listening rooms, Charlie Mars captivates audiences with heartfelt storytelling, memorable melodies, and the authenticity of a true songwriter's songwriter. 
Midnight River Choir is a Texas band known for their soulful blend of Americana, Texas groove, blues, and rock. Formed in New Braunfels, the band’s story began on a late-night float trip down the Guadalupe River, where their harmonies inspired the name Midnight River Choir. 
With raw energy, powerful vocals, and a sound rooted in friendship and storytelling, Midnight River Choir has built a reputation for unforgettable live performances. Their music carries the spirit of Texas — blending gritty roots, heartfelt songwriting, and a back-porch sense of community that connects with audiences wherever they play. 
Sarah Golden is a Houston, Texas singer-songwriter known for her soulful vocals, heartfelt storytelling, and deeply personal approach to folk music. With a career rooted in the Texas songwriter tradition, Golden has captivated audiences with her honest lyrics and powerful live performances. She gained national attention as a contestant on NBC’s The Voice and has continued to build a reputation as a compelling voice in the folk and Americana community. 
Her music blends acoustic folk, contemporary songwriting, and thoughtful social commentary, creating songs that connect through vulnerability, resilience, and real-life experiences. A longtime presence in the Houston music scene, Sarah Golden continues to inspire listeners with her authentic voice and commitment to meaningful songwriting. 
Heather Little is a celebrated Texas singer-songwriter known for her deeply personal storytelling, poetic lyrics, and timeless Americana sound. A true songwriter’s songwriter, Little has penned songs recorded by artists including Miranda Lambert and Sunny Sweeney, earning recognition for her honest portrayals of life, love, and resilience. 
Raised in Texas, Heather’s music blends country, folk, and roots influences with a voice that carries both vulnerability and strength. Her acclaimed albums, including Wings Like These and By Now, showcase her gift for crafting songs that feel intimate, lived-in, and unforgettable. Her live album recorded at the Old Quarter Acoustic Cafe captures the connection between her songs and the listening room tradition she represents. 
Hudson Mueller has always been something of an outlaw philosopher. Like his spirit guide Willie Nelson, he follows his musical instincts wherever they lead – even when those paths aren't particularly convenient or commercially strategic. On his stunning debut solo album Welcome to Earth, Mueller delivers a collection of songs that showcase his gifts as a storyteller while expanding the sonic possibilities of the Americana genre.
"I've spent the last 30+ years writing songs and playing in bands," Mueller reflects. "I don't have any big hits from a commercial standpoint. Not much money or fame to speak of. Willie hasn't recorded any of my songs yet. But I've had some bonafide adventure, and I've forged some incredible friendships through music. It's the closest thing I've found to real magic."
The magic is evident throughout Welcome to Earth, especially in the lead single "Money vs. Fame," which takes aim at the corrupting influence of success in the music industry. With its cutting lyrics ("Fame is just a prison with bars of solid gold / Money can be useful but it's poison to the soul") and innovative blend of folk storytelling with modern production techniques, the track serves as both a mission statement and a warning shot across the bow of an industry increasingly focused on metrics over meaning.
The Austin-born, Houston-based songwriter first cut his teeth with folk favorites The Hudsons (AMA Best Folk Band 2007, 2009) before relocating to New York City in 2009 and forming The Gold Magnolias, quickly becoming one of the city's most exciting southern soul bands. But Welcome to Earth represents a return to Mueller's storytelling roots, albeit with a sonic palette expanded by his years in the city's diverse music scene.
Juliet McConkey is a singer- songwriter originally from Nelson County, Virginia, in the heart of the Blue Ridge mountains. Now based in Austin, Texas, Juliet has been writing and performing music for well over a decade. Her folk-driven, country leaning compositions have attracted listeners and audiences irrespective of genre boundaries. With a voice as singular as they come, her songwriting possesses a deep understanding of nature and interpersonal relationships informed by her rural upbringing. 
Following a successful crowdfunding campaign, Juliet released her debut record in August of 2020, Disappearing Girl. Frequently asked questions
1. What time do we open?
Songwriter nights (Open Mic) start at 7pm. Hours for show nights may vary check event or tickets for exact times.
2. What time do the shows start?
Shows usually start at 8:30 pm. When there is an opener or an early show -- 6:30 pm or 7:30 pm. Always check the start time on the tickets you purchase.
3. Do we serve food?
We do not serve food.
4. Do we accept credit cards?
Yes
5. Do we take reservations? Where can I buy tickets?
Out of respect for the artists, we have had too many “no shows” and no longer accept unpaid reservations. You can buy your tickets on-line at: Old Quarter Tickets on Prekindle, pre-purchase your tickets at the Old Quarter Acoustic Cafe or buy at the door if the show is not sold out. Please do not purchase your tickets anywhere but the Old Quarter's Prekindle page or at the Old Quarter. We've had customers purchase tickets from people posing to have tickets on social media sites, and they are usually a scam. Don't fall for it!
6. Do we serve liquor?
No, we only serve beer and wine. You can bring your own bottle, but you will be charged a corkage fee of $15, and $2 for set-ups.
7. How do I book my band?
See Booking page. Please include samples of your music, a short bio about you and/or your band, and any links to live performances. Please remember Old Quarter Acoustic Cafe only hosts original music, no cover or tribute bands.
8. What is Listening Room etiquette?
The Old Quarter is considered a “Listening Room”. During the artist performance we ask that you refrain from loud conversations and phone calls. Please refrain from yelling out to the artist between songs and during (no one likes a heckler!) and silence your cell phone. We ask this out of respect for the artist and other patrons watching the show. You may take your drink outside to smoke, use the phone or talk with your BFF.
9. Do you refund shows?
No. There are absolutely no refunds or exchanges on tickets unless the venue or artist cancels the event. In the event a show has been canceled, you will only be refunded the price of ticket (plus tax), but not the service fees. We use a 3rd party service, and the service fee is their charge. Buy at your own risk!
10. Do we have waiting lists for sold out shows?
Yes. Please go to our Contact Us page, and submit your request to be placed on a waiting list.
11. Do you allow minors?
Yes. NO MINOR WILL BE SERVED ALCOHOL, even with parent, guardian or legal spouse present. This is up to the Old Quarter's discretion and we do not allow it. PERIOD.
