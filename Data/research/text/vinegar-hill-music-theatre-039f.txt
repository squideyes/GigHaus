Historic. Intimate. Vibrant.
Vinegar Hill Music Theatre is a place where people from near and far come together to socialize, celebrate, and connect over a shared love of music and live entertainment. Conveniently located off I-95 in Arundel, ME (close to Biddeford, Kennebunkport, Portland, and Portsmouth), our late-1800s barn, with its exposed wooden structure, exudes rustic New England charm and provides the perfect setting for the one-of-a-kind experiences we aim to foster. (Translation: our barn is really sweet, you should totally come check it out). From intimate acoustic sessions to electrifying performances, our stage is a melting pot of musical talent, nurturing emerging artists and showcasing established musicians alike. 
But we want Vinegar Hill to be more than just a place to listen to music; our goal is to make it an integral part of the local community. When you come to an event at Vinegar Hill, you’re not just attending a show; you’re investing in the power of music and the arts to uplift, inspire, and create a sense of purpose and belonging. Together, we’re building a place where everyone feels welcome, where connections are forged, and where memories are made.
So whether you’re a community member looking for a night out or a curious traveler seeking a taste of local culture, join us at Vinegar Hill—where the music is lively, the vibes are infectious, and everyone is welcome to dance to their own beat!

MEET THE VINEGAR HILL TEAM 
Emily Haranas, Managing Partner 
 emily@vinhillmusic.com 
Emily, who grew up in the high deserts of New Mexico, inherited her love of music from her parents (one of the most enduring memories of her childhood is arriving home to find her dad listening to music so loud it made the windows in their house shake). When her dad died in 2010, Emily also inherited his extensive record collection, which she and her husband Pete add to on a weekly basis. Vinegar Hill is Emily taking a chance on a dream with beloved friends in a place that she knows her dad would have loved. When she’s not working on helping Vinegar Hill grow to meet its potential, Emily spends time with her two daughters, Ella and Nia, teaches barre classes, attends as many live music performances as she can fit into her chaotic schedule, and tries to figure out how to utilize her newly-minted MA in Mindfulness. 
Peter Haranas, Partner 
Pete is Emily’s favorite assistant. When he’s not lending a helping hand, he prefers to spend his time watching the Celtics, going to sparsely attended concerts, and discussing, in great detail, matters of no consequence. 
Craig Cameron, Partner 
From cranking vinyl on a Big Bird record player while wearing footie pajamas, to receiving his first guitar as a gift, to seeing hundreds of live concerts all over the world, to performing music at venues all over New England, Craig’s love affair with music has reverberated throughout his life. He is honored to be working alongside his fiance and partner Michele (who he first met in a music venue!), and his lifelong friends Emily and Peter, as stewards of the experiences created here at Vinegar Hill Music Theatre. He can be found at Vinegar Hill most days, usually trying to do too many things at once, and happily spending time with his family. 
Michele Bundy, Partner 
Kennebunkport native Michele Bundy returned home in 2015 to start her own business, The Fifth Om. As Founder and Director of a holistic day spa in downtown Kennebunk, creating unique and fulfilling experiences is a central part of her calling in life. She brings her aesthetic expertise to the leadership team at Vinegar Hill Music Theatre. Michele is no stranger to musical performance – she played trumpet and trombone throughout her time in RSU 21 until graduating from KHS. She is currently considering Craig‘s offer to form a family band with their two children, Mason and Eve. 
Jeff Beam, Talent Buyer 
 booking@vinhillmusic.com 
Jeff Beam brings nearly 7 years of booking and venue experience from his time at One Longfellow Square in Portland, where he served first as programming director and venue manager and finally as executive director.
Jeff is passionate about curating a live music programming schedule and helping to deliver an exemplary venue and concert experience for all parties involved: “When you work at a music venue, your job is to foster an environment where you can help create positive and memorable experiences for everybody present. There’s nothing better than watching happy patrons leave the venue positively glowing after hearing their favorite artist, or after discovering their new favorite artist. We want musicians to be eager to return and to tell their fellow artist friends about the great experience they had at Vinegar Hill. Every show is a new opportunity for us to achieve these goals, and we aim to succeed each and every night.”
When he’s not at Vinegar Hill, Jeff can be found performing or recording music, or hanging at home with his cat, Wilma. Jeff prefers to live by his favorite motto: Trust the Process 
Cassy Collins, Show Manager 
 cassy@vinhillmusic.com 
Cassy Collins has spent a good part of her career around live music. After double majoring in Business and Fine Arts at Franklin Pierce University, she hit the road, traveling around the country and working at some pretty big musical events. She eventually traded life on the road for life in Maine, settling here in 2016.
Cassy loves live music, traveling, and finding her next adventure. When she’s not at the barn, she’s probably hanging out with her cat, Bobo, who she will happily tell you all about. 
Sophie Raskin, Guest Services and Box Office Coordinator 
 sophie@vinhillmusic.com 
Sophie Raskin is originally from Tulsa, Oklahoma, and came to Maine after graduating from the University of Vermont, where she majored in Music Business and Technology. After working in recording studios and venues, she eventually landed in Portland and became a part of the local music scene.
When she’s not at Vinegar Hill, you can probably find Sophie at a show, working one, working on her music zine Backline, or playing music herself. Basically, music takes up a good chunk of her time, and she wouldn’t have it any other way. 
Meredith Thomas, Production & Artist Relations Lead 
 production@vinhillmusic.com 
Meredith Thomas serves as Production & Artist Relations Lead, overseeing show advancing, production logistics, and artist communications. She coordinates across performers, management, and venue teams, ensuring seamless execution from advance planning to showtime. 
Joe Thomas, Supervising Audio Engineer 
Joe Thomas serves as Supervising Audio Engineer, overseeing live sound, lighting, and technical production. With extensive experience in live entertainment, Joe leads technical operations, coordinates engineering teams, and maintains the high production standards behind every performance.
Both musicians themselves, Joe and Meredith share a lifelong love of live music, the New England coast, and raising their three beautiful children together. 
Jack Murray, Technical Director 
Jack Murray, with his company Ocean AVL, was entrusted with the audio, video, and lighting installation during Vinegar Hill Music Theatre’s inception back in 2016, and he has been with us ever since. As the Technical Director, Jack is tasked with ensuring that all systems are running smoothly and that the artists’ equipment needs are taken care of, both prior to their arrival and during their on-stage performances. A Maine native and resident of Portland, Jack has been a part of the Southern Maine music scene for two decades. While he enjoys the fast-paced environment that operating an entertainment production company entails, he’s most happy behind the audio console mixing shows at Vinegar Hill. 
Ashley Kalloch, Marketing & Ticketing Coordinator 
 ashley@vinhillmusic.com 
Ashley helps bring Vinegar Hill’s shows to life online. From updating the website to working directly with artists and connecting with you on social media, she keeps everyone in the loop on the latest shows and events.
When she’s not working at Vinegar Hill, Ashley can usually be found in her home pottery studio, browsing vintage vinyl shops with her husband, or out adventuring with her pups, Zoe and Tilly.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS 
Where is Vinegar Hill Music Theatre located?
Our address is 53 Old Post Rd, Arundel, ME 04046. We’re located right off of Route 1 in between Biddeford and Kennebunk.
 How do I purchase tickets?
Online is the easiest way! Click on the “CALENDAR” tab, select the performance you’re interested in and the seats you’d like (if reserved seating), and enter in your payment info! We no longer have a physical box office, but if you run into any issues, please give us a call at 207-985-5552 and we can assist in your purchase.
What if I want to purchase tickets in person?
At this time we do not have a physical box office open, but if you give us a phone call, we’d be happy to help you purchase tickets over the phone! We’re usually available T – Th, 9am – 5pm at 207-985-5552. If someone doesn’t answer, please leave a voicemail and we’ll get back to you ASAP!
If I purchase tickets from you in person, will I avoid the $6.50 processing fee?
Unfortunately, no. Our ticketing fees help cover the cost of our ticketing platform (which we use regardless of how the tickets are purchased) and the credit card processing fees that we are charged every time we accept a credit card payment. Additionally, there are the costs incurred through staffing the very kind person who assists you on the phone and/or in person.
Do I need to print my tickets?
Not necessarily! You’ll be sent a confirmation email after purchasing your ticket, containing a few different forms of tickets. There’s a PDF version you are more than welcome to print out or pull up on your phone upon arrival, as well as the option to add the tickets to your Apple or Google Wallet. If you are unable to find your ticket before the show, we can look you up by the name of the ticket purchaser at the door. 
What’s your refund policy?
All ticket purchases are final and tickets may not be exchanged or refunded. It is your responsibility to make sure that you purchase tickets for the correct performance date, time, and location.
Can I transfer tickets?
You can! Once you purchase tickets, they are yours to do whatever you want with! As an extra layer of protection, you are more than welcome to call up our office and we can swap out the name of the ticket holder on the backend. We like to have an accurate email and phone number of the ticket holder in case we end up needing to communicate anything about the show. 
Can you explain the $6.50 processing fee? 
As we move into the 2026 season, Vinegar Hill Music Theatre will continue to implement a uniform ticketing fee of $6.50 per ticket — a rate we’ve kept steady since last season. This fee helps us better align with the actual costs associated with maintaining and operating our venue, while allowing us to keep base ticket prices as accessible as possible.
We remain committed to presenting a diverse lineup of performances and to supporting our community through free live music events and by partnering with local non-profits to host their fundraisers in our space. Your continued support helps make all of this possible.
Thank you for being a part of the Vinegar Hill community. We can’t wait to welcome you to the theatre this season!
How do I get in touch with someone?
Easy! Give our office a call Monday – Friday, 12pm – 5pm at 207-985-5552 or send an email to info@vinhillmusic.com.
Are there age restrictions?
The majority of our shows are ALL AGES, and if the performer requests an age restriction, we’ll be sure to clearly mark that on the show’s landing page. All comedy shows are generally 18+, but you are welcome to contact our office to confirm.
Do you serve alcohol?
We do! We have a full indoor and outdoor bar. We encourage ticket holders to show up two hours before the show to take full advantage of our outdoor patio area – enjoy a beverage and hang out with us!
What are your food options?
While we have no in-house food options, we will be (trying to) book a food truck for every performance on our calendar. To see the updated list of food trucks for the season, click on “FOOD TRUCKS” under the “CALENDAR” menu tab to see our full lineup. No outside food or beverage is allowed in the garden patio or venue. 
How does parking work?
Please park in our back lot and enter through the Old Post Rd entrance. Our front seashell lot is reserved for drop offs and handicap parking only. In the event of a full lot, parking attendants will direct traffic to our designated overflow parking areas.
Do you have handicap seats?
We do! We have 4 handicap seats with companion seats available for purchase for every show. If you need additional accommodations, please give us a call before the show at 207-985-5552.
Can I bring chairs to a General Admission show?
You cannot. No camping, folding, or beach chairs of any kind are permitted inside our building per fire codes. For our GA shows, we will have our balcony seating and some assorted cocktail tables and chairs available, first come, first serve. Once these seats are gone, they’re gone, so make sure to arrive early to make sure you secure your spot! Again, those who require handicap accessible seating can contact us prior to the show.
How many seats in your venue?
250 seats (for a seated show). Our seats are removable, so we do our best to present a good mix of seated and standing shows.
What happens if a show is cancelled or rescheduled?
All ticket sales are final. If a show is cancelled and no reschedule date is announced, tickets will be refunded.
If a performance is rescheduled, tickets will automatically be honored for the new date. Ticket holders may request a refund within 3 days of the rescheduling announcement. After that 3-day period, all tickets are non-refundable.
Rescheduling announcements will be sent via email to the email address provided at the time of purchase. Ticket holders are responsible for providing a valid email address and checking their email, including spam or junk folders, for important updates. Vinegar Hill Music Theatre is not responsible for missed communications due to an incorrect or outdated email address, email delivery issues, or messages filtered as spam or junk.
What are your venue’s seating options?
We have two venue configurations depending on what the artist prefers. 
Reserved Seated: 240 theatre seats with 10 cocktail table seats. We typically offer Premium and General seating options. Premium includes the first five rows (A-E), our five cocktail tables in the back (10 seats total), and our three balcony boxes (10 seats in each box = 30 seats total). General seating includes rows F – M. We also have ample patio seating in our garden bar (without a view of the stage).
General Admission (GA): For most general admission shows, the floor is open for standing room, with the majority of theatre seats removed to create space. However, all 30 theatre seats in the balcony remain available (unless otherwise noted). We also set up approximately 10 cocktail tables with bar stools around the perimeter of the room. All seating and table options are available on a first-come, first-served basis, so we recommend arriving when doors open if you’d like to secure a spot. If you require accessible seating or additional accommodations, please contact our office — we’re happy to help.
What’s your re-entry policy?
Re-entry is allowed. Our ushers have a list of all ticket holders, we just ask that you show your ID upon re-entry.
What’s your smoking policy?
We have a designated smoking area, which our ushers will happily point out to you upon request. NO SMOKING is allowed in our front seashell parking lot.
Can I purchase a gift card?
You can! Gift cards can be purchased here and are good to use, forever.
Do you rent out your venue for private events?
We do! Vinegar Hill is the perfect venue for weddings, corporate events, reunions, fundraisers – you name it! You can find all the information about wedding rentals on our WEDDINGS tab and about private rental on our PRIVATE RENTALS tab.
I’m a band, can I play at your venue?
Maybe! Reach out to booking@vinhillmusic.com with the details and we’ll let you know!

Explore the depths of Americana “one trippy tune at a time” with New York’s Slambovian Circus of Dreams. Voted “Best Band” by NY’s Chronogram Magazine for their rootsy, rockin’ psychedelia and fantastic stories and songs, the Slambovians’ deep connection with audiences and exciting live shows are legendary. Woodstock-tinged Americana blends a cool mix of southern rock, Celtic and British folk combined with solid songwriting – a slice of Americana pie that is irresistible! Influences of Dylan, Bowie, Syd Barrett, Tom Petty, and The Band are there, yet the Slambovians’ musicianship and critically acclaimed songwriting stand on their own. Joziah Longo (resident shaman of Slambovia), leads with vocals described as “soothing and bewitching as a snake oil tonic.” Longtime bandmates Tink Lloyd (accordion, cello, mandolin, flute) and guitarist/ mandolin wizard Sharkey McEwen bring their own magic to the Slambovian brew with “a little help from their friends” – RJ McCarty (keys, bass, sax), Bob Torsello (bass), and Matthew Abourezk (drums).
The band’s last release, “A Very Unusual Head” is considered to be “their finest work to date” (Rock’n’Reel Magazine, UK).
“The Cool End of Americana” – Maverick Magazine
“They take the stage by storm!” – NY Times

“Great songs and a whole lotta heart” – Meg Griffin, SiriusXM
