Watch the music grow.
Roots Music Project is a non-profit community of music fans and music makers. We believe in the power of music to make the world a better place. 
We provide performance opportunities, education, artist development, community building, and empowerment.

Why perform at RMP?
Boulder's premier mid-size venue
​
5 million music fans live on the Front Range of Colorado!
Our Location: 4747 Pearl Street, Ste. V3A, Boulder CO 80301 (about a 30 minute drive from Denver).
​
250 capacity standing, 150 capacity standing / seated.
​
QSC Powered Sound System, Over-the-top Marketing Support, 16x12 Stage, Full Bar, Plenty of Parking (including tour bus parking), backline available.
​
Relaxed radius clause policy that plays well with Denver and Ft. Collins.
​
Additional Services available: Merch table support, graphics, social media support, video production, live sound recording, street team marketing.
​
Large Green Room(s): Tea, Coffee, Light snacks, non-alcoholic drinks, bar comps, wifi, and work area.
"Fantastic intimate venue- great sound warm atmosphere. Great artists and nice people." - Teresa
Venue Specs
Roots Music Project fits 250 Attendees Standing, and 130 Attendees Seated.
​
AUDIO SPECS: QSC L-series Wedge Mains + Subwoofers, 6 QSC L-series Monitors, Midas M32R digital console, DL32 Stage Box, 8 in / 4 out digital snake, 16 in / 8 out XLR/TRS snake, microphones and DIs.
​
We can accommodate Large Bands on our stage. 16 x 12 feet.
​
LIGHTING SPECS: Luminair Pro 3 - lighting console, DMXking eDMX1 Pro Ethernet DMX converter, Chauvet and Rockville lighting arrays, Fog Machine, Haze Machine.

Conveniently Located in Boulder, Colorado
Centrally located at 4747 Pearl Street, Boulder, CO 80301, our venue sits on the vibrant East Pearl corridor, one of Boulder's most accessible and energetic neighborhoods. We're easy to get to from across the area, with ample on-site parking your guests will genuinely appreciate.
Whether you're coming from downtown Boulder, North Boulder, or just outside the city, Roots Music Project is your go-to event space in Colorado.
FAQs
​
How large is the event space at Roots Music Project? 
Our venue comfortably accommodates up to 350 guests, depending on your event layout (cocktail-style, seated, or performance-focused). Contact us for a walkthrough and to discuss your specific setup.
​
What types of events can I book at Roots Music Project? 
We host a wide range of private events, including birthday parties, corporate gatherings, album release parties, weddings, student recitals, team-building off-sites, baby showers, and private social gatherings.
​
What is the rental price for your Boulder event space? 
Event rentals start at $2,000. Pricing varies based on the type of event, duration, and services needed. Reach out to us for a custom quote.
​
Is there parking at your Pearl Street venue? 
Yes, plenty of it. Accessible parking is one of the things guests consistently love about our venue. Easy in, easy out.
​
Can I bring my own food or catering? 
Yes, we are open to outside catering arrangements. We also have plenty of space for food truck catering. Contact us to discuss your specific needs, and we'll work with you.
​
Do you have a bar? 
Yes! Roots Music Project has a full bar available as part of your private event rental.
​
Is there a stage or sound system? 
Absolutely. We have a dedicated performance stage and a professional sound system — one of the things that makes us a uniquely qualified venue in Boulder for music-related events and performances.
​
How far in advance should I book? 
Our rental schedule fills up quickly, especially on weekends. We recommend reaching out at least 4–8 weeks in advance for weekend dates and as soon as possible for holiday periods.
​
Can you accommodate corporate meetings or team events? 
Absolutely, Roots Music Project is a great option for a corporate event space, including team off-sites, client events, workshops, and company celebrations.
​
Where exactly are you located in Boulder? 
We're at 4747 Pearl Street, Boulder, CO 80301, on the East Pearl Street corridor, easily accessible from anywhere in Boulder or the surrounding area.
