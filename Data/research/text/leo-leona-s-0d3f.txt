It all started with Leo & Leona!
Just off Highway 33 near La Crosse, Leo & Leona's Tavern and Dance Hall in Newburg Corners has welcomed visitors since 1951. This historic southwest Wisconsin roadhouse offers a lively atmosphere, live concerts, solo performances, and special events. Visit Leo And Leona's to enjoy a memorable night of music and dancing.
