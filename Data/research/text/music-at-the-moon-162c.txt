Gary Good Presents...
​Music at the Moon 
Since the Magnolia Moon was once an amphitheater, we decided to bring back the music! About once a month we host "Music at the Moon" ~ an intimate up close and personal way to experience world class music. ​ We are also available to host your own special concert with your invited guests. We handle the food, the music... and you just sit back and enjoy! 
​If you want to get an email about each upcoming concert, please fill out the form below and we'll add you to our list! 
And to make reservations for a concert call or text Gary at 405.209.4279 or email at [email protected].
NEWS FLASH!
Want to host your own concert? Do you have space for 40+ people? If so, we can bring you the same artists we have at "The Moon" to your venue. You bring the crowd, we'll do the rest! Message or call for more info.

​THE MAGNOLIA MOON
Special Events
Plan Your Next Event @ The Moon!
The Magnolia Moon at Guthrie Retreat is a unique space that can be used in a variety of ways. For an intimate evening with up to 60 guests we have an incredible indoor space. For events with up to 350 people, there's over an acre of gardens with a Koi Pond and beautiful waterfall as well as an antique drink cart, area for a band or DJ with dancing just below. We also have overnight accommodations for up to 24. We can arrange accommodations for more within walking distance at one of our partnering bed and breakfasts. Our place is very difficult to describe, and is best seen in person. Please feel free to call or text with any questions or to set up an appointment to tour The Guthrie Retreat!
The Perfect "Surprise"!
The Magnolia Moon at Guthrie Retreat is considered by most who see it as a "hidden treasure" which makes it the perfect place to surprise a special person or group. We will help you create the scene and put all of the pieces together. 
Family Reunions & Holiday Retreats
Dinner Parties with Music
When a family gets together for a reunion, what better way to recant old memories than to be together on over an acre of beautiful gardens along with space to meet, sleep, cook-out and play? And for that special holiday gathering we will help you create your theme. 
Create a very intimate dinner with 10 to 20 guests, or have up to 60 guests along with live music or entertainment to fit the theme. You can also treat your guests to an "up close and personal" concert. Choose between a wide range of musicians and entertainers. The possibilities are endless at Guthrie Retreat!
