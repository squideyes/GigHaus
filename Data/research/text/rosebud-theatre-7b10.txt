 Fall Concert Series 
About the Series
The Roseburg Fall Concert Series brings twelve nights of touring talent to The Rosebud Theatre, an intimate, mostly all-ages music venue in the heart of downtown Roseburg. Indie favorites, outlaw country, bluegrass, blues, a Halloween weekend double header and a Tom Petty tribute to close it out. Touring artists tell us The Rosebud is their favorite room in Oregon, and once you’re inside, you’ll see why. It’s intimate and comfortable, every spot is close to the stage, and the sound is dialed in for every show.
Come for the music. Stay for the wine country, the river and a downtown full of great places to eat.

Easy to get to
Right off I-5. About an hour from Eugene, about 90 minutes from Medford, and under three hours from Portland.
Up close
The Rosebud holds about 100 people. No nosebleeds, no watching the band on a screen.
Easy on the wallet
Tickets run $15 to $25, and presale saves you a few bucks.
Walkable
Dinner, drinks and the show are all within a few blocks of each other downtown. Parking is untimed and free after 5pm.
Wine country next door
The Umpqua Valley is home to more than 20 wineries, most within a short drive of town.

Where to Stay
Roseburg sits right off I-5, with lodging just minutes from downtown.
Bell Sister Flats · 620 SE Main St. Stay right in the heart of downtown, about a block from The Rosebud. Two spacious one-bedroom flats sit on the second floor of a historic building that was once Minnie and Mineta Bell’s hat and dress shop in the early 1900s. Full kitchens, fireplaces and city views make it an easy home base. Walk to dinner, walk to the show, walk home.
Near the river: The Lodge at Roseburg and Riverfront Inn, both on Harvard Avenue, a quick drive from the theatre.
Off I-5 at Garden Valley: Hampton Inn & Suites, Comfort Inn and other familiar names, about five minutes from downtown.
Walk to the show: Vacation rentals in the historic neighborhoods around downtown put you within walking distance of dinner and the theatre.
Make it a resort night: Seven Feathers Casino Resort in Canyonville is about 25 minutes south

Where to Eat and Drink
Plenty of good options are within a few blocks of the theatre, so you can park once and walk.
Alexander's Greek Cuisine · 643 SE Jackson St. Practically next door. Gyros, hummus and spanakopita.
Brix Grill & Chill · 527 SE Jackson St. Creative American dishes and cocktails, breakfast through dinner.
True Kitchen + Bar · 629 SE Main St. Gastropub plates, craft cocktails and a deep list of Umpqua Valley wines.
Dino's Ristorante Italiano · 404 SE Jackson St. Family-owned Italian, made in house.
Old Soul Pizza · Downtown. Wood-fired pizza with local wine and rotating taps.
McMenamins Roseburg Station · Pub fare and house brews in Roseburg's historic train depot.

Things to Do While You're Here
Taste the Umpqua Valley. Make an afternoon of wine tasting before the show. Abacela, Melrose Vineyards, Reustle Prayer Rock Vineyards and HillCrest Winery & Distillery are all a short drive from downtown.
Chase waterfalls. Head east on the North Umpqua Highway (OR-138) for fall color, river views and easy waterfall hikes like Susan Creek Falls and Toketee Falls.
Saturday morning market. Start your Saturday at the Umpqua Valley Farmers Market. 9am - 1pm in downtown Roseburg.
Dig for records. Browse the stacks and get your afternoon or evening caffeine fix at Reverie Record Shop, another spot to grab Rosebud tickets. 
Wildlife Safari. The drive-through wildlife park in nearby Winston is a favorite for families.
Riverfront and parks. Walk or bike the trails along the South Umpqua River and through Stewart Park.
Local history. The Douglas County Museum covers the history and natural history of the Umpqua Valley.

FAQs
What should i expect when attending a show? 
If you have purchased a presale ticket, the doors open one hour prior to the show starting. Your ticket should state the time doors open. We will have your name on our list at the door if you purchased your presale ticket online. Online presale ticket sales end before door time on the day of the show. If online sales have ended and the show is not stated as sold out, we will have tickets available for sale at door time, until we sell out. 
If you need to purchase a ticket for the show, they will be available at the door when doors open until we reach our capacity. 
For All Ages shows - we offer a 50% discount for kids 12 and under. 
You should bring your valid ID with you to the show if you are over 21+ and intend to drink alcohol. Your ID will be checked at the door. 
We have limited seating available downstairs as well as limited seating upstairs in our Mezzanine for tonight's show, and plenty of open room. Seating is first-come-first-serve. If you require a chair, please let a staff member know. We have added some additional table seating downstairs.
You are welcome to bring in outside food. There are several downtown restaurants to order food and pick up to bring in and enjoy. We are limited to a selection of frozen, made-to-order foods at the venue. Thanks to Experience Roseburg, here is a list of downtown restaurants: 
https://bit.ly/downtownroseburg-restaurants to choose from.
We have a full bar with a limited selection of beers, local wines, spirits, and non-alcoholic beverages.
What are your hours?
We have events on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday each week unless otherwise stated. Occasionally we have events Sunday-Wednesday, which will be stated on our Event page. Refer to our Event page for detailed information and online ticket links. We have a mixture of All Ages and 21+ events. The standard door time for All Ages events is 6pm and 7pm for 21+ events. Please refer to the specific event listing for event-specific information. We do not have box office hours outside of these hours. 
Do you take reservations?
Currently we do not take general reservations, but we do have a limited number of premium presale ticket options to reserve a selection of our mezzanine seating. These tickets do cost more than our general admission and are available through the online ticket purchase links on our Events page. 
Is there seating?
The amount of seating available on the main floor depends on the genre of each event. We do have limited seating available on the ground floor and limited seating available upstairs on the mezzanine. Seating is first come, first served. Your ticket does not guarantee you a seat. If you require a seat and none are available when you arrive, please let a staff person know and we will do our best to accommodate you. 
Do you have food?
We do not have an on-site kitchen, but you are welcome to bring in outside food. There are several downtown restaurants to order food and pick up to bring in and enjoy. We are limited to a selection of frozen, made-to-order foods at the venue. Thanks to Experience Roseburg, here is a list of downtown restaurants: https://bit.ly/downtownroseburg-restaurants 
Do you have a bar?
We have a full bar on the main floor with a selection of canned beer, tap beer, wine, cider, hard seltzers, and spirits. We have a selection of non-alcoholic beverage options as well. 
Where can I get tickets? Can I purchase a ticket at the door?
You can find online presale Eventbrite ticket links through our Event page listings. These online presale tickets are subject to Eventbrite service fees. The week of each show, you can also purchase physical presale tickets with cash at Reverie Record Shop (642 SE Jackson Street) during the open hours. 
Be sure to subscribe to our weekly newsletter for limited subscriber-only presale ticket promotions! 
We are working on an option to sell physical presale tickets at The Rosebud during our open hours - stay tuned for more information on that. 
We will sell tickets at the door at The Rosebud when doors open for the show unless the show has already sold out. We are an intimate 100 capacity venue and can fill up fast, so if you want to guarantee a spot at the show, it is best to purchase an advance ticket. We keep our social media profiles updated to alert you if a show is close to sell out. Follow us on Facebook or Instagram to stay up to date. 
Are there age requirements? 
We have a variety of All Ages and 21+ events. Please refer to our Event page for details per show. 
What if I’m interested in booking? 
For booking inquiries, please send links to music and dates of interest to: booking@therosebudtheatre.com. Thank you for contacting us. We appreciate your submissions. We receive many inquiries about performing at The Rosebud. Please be patient. Please no DMs. 
Do you rent The Rosebud out for private events? 
We do not have a current pricing structure for private event rentals, but we are working on one. For the remainder of 2025 we will not be renting the venue out for private events on Thursdays, Fridays, or Saturdays during our regular show schedule. 
I still have questions! 
If you have other questions, please email us at info@therosebudtheatre.com and we do our best to get you a prompt response. We currently do not have a phone number, but monitor email consistently. You can also DM us on our Facebook page with your question.
